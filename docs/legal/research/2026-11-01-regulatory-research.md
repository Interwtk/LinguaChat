# LinguaChat: Regulatory research memo (for lawyer review)

- **Access date for all sources:** 2026-11-01
- **Status:** Desk research only. This is **not legal advice**. Every row is input for review by qualified counsel in each jurisdiction.
- **Product assumptions:** Vite/React frontend on Vercel; FastAPI backend; optional Supabase Auth (email and password); learning progress stored mainly in browser `localStorage`; local rule-based tutor with no generative AI provider active (one may be added later). The operator is based in Chile and has no legal entity yet. Planned: a beta for adults 18+, freemium subscription sold internationally, US and Chile/LatAm first, EU/EEA and Japan under consideration. The product teaches EN↔ES.
- **Source policy:** Primary or official sources where they could be retrieved. **[SEC]** marks a secondary source (law firm, news, tracker), used only to locate or corroborate a primary one, or where the official site could not be fetched. "NOT VERIFIED" means the claim could not be confirmed against a primary source in this session.
- **Legend:** Blocker = "Yes" means likely needed before charging users in that market. "Cond." means conditional on the facts noted.
