# F90 — Presupposed assumptions, premise challenge, clarification and independent communicative transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. This is not a shipped presupposition evaluator, a CEFR award, a reproduced production defect, proof of human-learning gains or a universal pragmatic norm. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 requires genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 separate selected from actually rendered instruction and tutor-origin answer cues; F47/F48 protect noncompensatory CEFR evidence; F49 accessibility; F50 soft-interest rotation; F53 register/variety; F54 conversational repair; F71 discourse referents; F73 pragmatic inference; F74 source attribution; F79 shared understanding; F81 negation scope; F86 evidence/uncertainty; F89 contrastive correction. **Distinct construct:** independently recognizing when a question, definite description, repeated-action expression or follow-up **takes an unverified proposition for granted**, deciding whether the source warrants that assumption, and responding without accidentally accepting a false premise. Understanding the literal question, correcting its focused word, or expressing generic uncertainty does not alone demonstrate this. Only apply when a **real versioned CEFR descriptor/can-do and source-authored communicative task** license this language/pragmatics outcome; otherwise `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

## Controlled setup

Freeze **what the source explicitly asserts**, **what the utterance presupposes**, **what the context licenses**, **what remains unknown**, **the speaker's commitments**, **whether the assumption is disputed**, and **the action requested of the learner**. Score these separately. A source-authored scenario may intentionally contain a false premise; the correct independent response can be a respectful correction, an explicit refusal of the premise or a clarification, not a fabricated answer. Do not make the learner verify facts outside the supplied evidence.

Presupposition and its challenge may be conveyed differently by target-language morphology, particles, definiteness, word order, intonation, discourse conventions and varieties. Validate natural realizations rather than imposing English triggers or an English-only politeness rubric. A pragmatic implication is not automatically an entailment or a fact.

Use deterministic pseudonymous multi-session histories with injected seed/clock, immutable session-qualified event IDs, frozen source/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, and trusted chronology of **actually rendered** tutor/interlocutor messages. ## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Question assumes an event that never happened.** The source-authored record states that no appointment was booked. An interlocutor asks, “Why did you cancel the appointment?” Learner A invents a cancellation reason; learner B independently responds that there was no appointment to cancel and asks whether the person means a different booking. In a later genuinely different context, a delivery was booked and **was** canceled; both must recognize that the same question is now warranted. Reject a rigid rule that every “why did you” question is false.
- **B — Repeated action and unsupported prior state.** Source notes show that a learner has never used a particular account. “Have you stopped using that account?” presupposes previous use. Learner A answers “yes” and inadvertently confirms prior use; learner B says “I haven't used that account before.” In a second scenario, documented prior use makes “I stopped last month” appropriate. The target-language rubric must accept natural equivalent denials and repairs, not only a memorized English sentence.
- **C — Definite description and referent ambiguity.** A team has two pending reports and no approved final report. A colleague asks “Where is the approved report?” Learner A invents a location; learner B clarifies that no report is approved and offers the pending versions. Later, one report becomes approved under a versioned source update. The learner must track **which** proposition is now supported without treating a later update as retroactive proof that the earlier response was wrong. When the source does not say whether approval occurred, asking for verification is correct.
- **D — Attribution, negation and socially sensitive repair.** A source-authored customer asks “When did your colleague stop ignoring my messages?” but the available correspondence does not establish that any messages were ignored. Learner A endorses the accusation while trying to be polite; learner B acknowledges the concern without accepting the disputed premise, asks for the relevant dates, and offers to investigate. A separate scenario contains documented unanswered messages and requires a source-faithful acknowledgment. Validate target-language/variety and context-sensitive repair strategies; do not require confrontation, victim-blaming, or a culturally uniform directness level.
- **E — Coached immediate accuracy versus delayed independent reversal.** In comparable initial sessions, `ASSERTED_VS_ASSUMED_PAIR` yields high immediate scores with visible answer-bearing contrasts; `DIALOGUE_CLARIFICATION_RECAST` yields slower, less polished first responses. Later **separate real sessions** present unfamiliar speakers, changed evidence status, novel presupposition forms and open addressee outcomes. The dialogue condition produces better first-try unaided premise challenges and appropriate acceptance when warranted. Compare or disclose source complexity, language variety, modality, feedback, dose, topic familiarity, accessibility and prior baseline. Lower confidence in the immediate winner, keep sampling alternatives, and record the **actually rendered** next method rather than declaring a “dialogue learner.”
- **F — Soft interests, missing follow-up, inaccessible cue and replay disorder.** A learner challenges an unwarranted assumption in familiar gaming dialogue but accepts a false premise in a neutral neighborhood schedule and an unfamiliar workplace handoff. A spoken-only stress cue is unavailable to a caption user; a delayed probe is eligible and offered but never rendered. Two real sessions reuse an offline local event ID; an answer-bearing tutor premise label rendered before the learner's response arrives in telemetry afterward; an offline event carries a conflicting source/rubric version. Deduplicate only genuine session-qualified replays, quarantine unresolved versions, reconstruct trusted render chronology and preserve identical assistance labels, review debt, context rotation and reversible next-method rationale under receipt-order permutations. Written success cannot certify listening/speaking.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-valid CEFR applicability.** A–F require a real versioned descriptor/can-do, blueprint/prerequisites, target language/varieties, source-authored premise and context, task outcome, validated rubric/evaluator, assistance policy, threshold, required skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Missing mapping, unsupported presupposition interpretation or an unlicensed objective returns `UNSUPPORTED`/`NOT_APPLICABLE`, never invented proficiency.
2. **Asserted versus assumed content.** A/B/C/D separately identify the explicit question/request, its background assumption, and the facts actually licensed by the source. A fluent answer that quietly adopts a false premise fails; merely detecting a trigger without identifying its contextual assumption also fails.
3. **Evidence-sensitive acceptance, denial and unknown.** A/B/C distinguish false, true and unknown premise status. Accept an assumption when documented, challenge it when contradicted and clarify when evidence is insufficient. Do not overgeneralize from English surface markers or mark all presuppositions as false.
4. **Attribution, referent and conversational outcome.** C/D preserve whose claim it is, which report/person/event is involved and what action the addressee needs. A source-faithful repair or clarification succeeds only if it avoids invented facts and enables the intended communication; confident but unfounded answers fail.
5. **First independent move and actual cue exposure.** D/F score the first learner-authored premise interpretation using trusted render chronology. An answer-bearing tutor annotation or completed denial rendered before that move makes it assisted; selected-but-never-rendered help does not. Legitimate source-authored question wording is task stimulus, not tutor coaching.
6. **Delayed genuinely unseen independent transfer.** C/E/F require distinct real sessions, novel question forms, contexts, source evidence and speakers, first unaided premise response and observable recipient outcome. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Same-session restatement, answer copying, missing follow-up and coached repair never prove durable independent mastery.
7. **Comparable methods, accessibility and soft interests.** D/E/F match or disclose task difficulty, source ambiguity, modality, variety/register, practice dose, feedback, topic familiarity and accessibility. Protect the shared CEFR objective, noncompensatory receptive/productive skills, due reviews and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation. Interests only alter example probabilities.
8. **Reversible adaptation and receipt-order invariance.** E/F record feasible options, selected/assigned/**actually rendered** method, later independent outcomes, uncertainty, confounds, exploration and next-choice rationale. Sparse or conflicted evidence uses `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. Session-qualified deduplication, frozen versions and trusted render chronology yield invariant assistance, premise verdicts, review debt and policy decisions under receipt-order permutations; unresolved conflicts are quarantined.
9. **Assertion-level audit without efficacy inflation.** A–F emit exact source/task/rubric/evaluator IDs, frozen asserted/presupposed propositions and evidence status, first/final learner-authored moves, rendered cues, addressee outcome, delayed/unseen denominators, confounds, replay permutations and `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED` per assertion. Documentation CI is not an executable evaluator PASS, CEFR award or proof of improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, deterministic clock/seed, real source-resolved CEFR-linked tasks, validated language/variety-aware rubrics, versioned assertions and assumptions, trusted render chronology, first unaided interpretation/repair, observable addressee outcomes, completed delayed genuinely unseen transfer with denominators, comparable method/topic/modality/access exposure, protected skills/reviews, receipt-order permutations, reversible next-method traces and assertion-level verdicts. Missing source mapping, reliable premise interpretation, accessible modality, completed independent follow-up or trustworthy chronology remains `UNSUPPORTED`/unknown.

Any claim that adaptation **improves durable independent premise-aware communication** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, delayed unseen tasks, multilingual/variety and accessibility validation, human adjudication, privacy/subgroup-harm review and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor teaches learners to answer what is genuinely known—and to question what a question merely assumes.**
