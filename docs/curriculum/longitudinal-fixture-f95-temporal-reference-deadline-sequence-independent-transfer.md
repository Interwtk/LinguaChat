## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Relative date anchored to the wrong message.** A source-authored invitation sent on a verified Tuesday says "Let's meet tomorrow morning"; a forwarded copy arrives Thursday without a new proposal. Learner A tells the recipient that the meeting is Friday, anchoring "tomorrow" to the forwarding date. Learner B anchors the quoted proposal to Tuesday, explains that it referred to Wednesday, and asks whether that proposed meeting is still valid. A second genuine Thursday message saying "tomorrow" correctly refers to Friday. Later unseen transfer changes speaker, forwarding lag and target-language date conventions; reward fidelity and clarification, not memorized weekday substitution.
- **B — Sequence, simultaneity and duration.** A verified service notice says "Check in before the briefing; the safety video plays during check-in; the tour starts after the briefing and lasts about 45 minutes." Learner A says the video starts after check-in and the tour starts during the briefing; learner B preserves the order, overlap and approximate duration without inventing a tour end time. A second notice changes the video to "after check-in" and B updates accordingly. An unseen transit notice reverses two steps and tests first unaided instructions to another person.
- **C — Deadline attaches to the wrong action.** A source-authored organizer message says "Register by Friday; payment is due the following Wednesday; bring the signed form when you arrive." Learner A tells attendees all three tasks must be completed Friday. Learner B assigns each deadline to its actual action and requests clarification if the local expression "by Friday" is ambiguous about inclusion. A later correction moves payment to Thursday without moving registration. Unseen transfer changes the domain to a neutral appointment or volunteer event and checks recipient action, not memorized dates.
- **D — Proposed, confirmed, canceled and completed are not interchangeable.** An accessible message thread contains "We might start at 9," then "Confirmed: start at 10," then "Today's session is canceled; a new time has not been set." Learner A announces that the session happened at 10. Learner B communicates cancellation and that no replacement time is confirmed. A transcript of the final cancellation is missing from one modality; caption-dependent learners are not penalized until a verified accessible source is available. A later unseen task contains a new proposal after cancellation and tests whether B avoids calling it confirmed.
- **E — Immediate coached fluency loses to delayed independent transfer.** In comparable early sessions, ANCHOR_AND_REFERENCE_CONTRAST produces polished immediate answers after visible resolved-date examples; EVENT_SEQUENCE_TIMELINE produces slower but unaided first attempts. Across separate later real sessions, with unfamiliar notices, changed reference anchors, reversed event order, and recipient-facing decisions, the timeline condition yields stronger first-attempt independent temporal fidelity and fewer fabricated confirmations. Match or disclose baseline ability, source difficulty, practice dose, feedback, register, language variety, topic familiarity, accessibility and calendar complexity. Prefer delayed unseen independent evidence over coached immediate correctness; retain exploration and record the **actually rendered** next method, never a permanent "timeline learner" label.
- **F — Interest overfit, time-zone confound and event replay.** A learner handles relative dates in preferred gaming invitations but misreads a neutral clinic appointment and an unfamiliar public-event schedule. One source says "tomorrow at 8" without the speaker's locale; another supplies a verified time-zone conversion with a daylight-saving transition. An eligible delayed probe is offered but never rendered. Two offline sessions reuse the same local event ID; a tutor-rendered calendar answer arrives in telemetry after the learner reply; a later message carries a conflicting source-version hash. Deduplicate only true session-qualified replays, quarantine version conflicts and reconstruct trusted render chronology. Preserve assistance labels, unknown time-zone state, review debt, soft-interest rotation and reversible next-method rationale under receipt-order permutations. Do not infer language weakness from missing locale or unavailable required listening.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-valid CEFR applicability.** A–F require real versioned can-do, blueprint/prerequisites, target language/variety, source-authored task/provenance, recipient purpose, validated rubric/evaluator, assistance policy, threshold, required skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Missing mapping or unsupported language-specific temporal analysis yields UNSUPPORTED/NOT_APPLICABLE, never invented proficiency.
2. **Reference anchor and ambiguity.** A/F preserve speaker/source/utterance times and the actual anchor for relative expressions. A forwarded quote does not silently reset "tomorrow"; an original new message may. Missing locale or ambiguous "next Friday" permits bounded clarification, not a fabricated exact timestamp.
3. **Sequence, overlap and duration.** B distinguishes before, after and during, and distinguishes approximate duration from a precise clock-time endpoint. Source changes must reverse the interpretation when appropriate; surface fluency cannot compensate for incorrect recipient instructions.
4. **Deadline scope, boundaries and revision.** C/D attach each deadline to its own action, preserve proposed/confirmed/canceled/completed distinctions, and honor verified corrections without applying them to unrelated events. Inclusive/exclusive boundary scoring must be validated for the target language/variety, not assumed from English.
5. **First independent move and rendered cues.** A/D/F evaluate the first learner-authored recipient-facing temporal statement or clarification against trusted render chronology. A resolved calendar date, completed timeline or answer-bearing model rendered beforehand makes the affected move assisted; selected-but-never-rendered teaching does not. Separate source-authored stimuli from interlocutor coaching.
6. **Delayed genuinely unseen independent transfer.** A/B/C/D/E/F require separate real sessions, novel anchors, domains, orderings, revisions and recipient decisions, first unaided responses and observable outcomes. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; assisted revisions, copied models and missing follow-up do not establish durable mastery.
7. **Comparable methods, accessibility and soft interests.** D/E/F match or disclose date arithmetic, locale/time-zone difficulty, source clarity, language/variety, topic familiarity, feedback, practice dose, modality and accessibility. Preserve shared CEFR objectives, noncompensatory receptive/productive evidence, due reviews and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar context rotation. Missing captions and ambiguous time zones are not language errors.
8. **Reversible adaptation and receipt-order invariance.** E/F log feasible alternatives, assigned/selected/**actually rendered** instruction, later independent outcomes, uncertainty, confounds, exploration and next-choice rationale. Sparse/conflicted evidence falls back to BALANCED_DEFAULT. Session-qualified replay deduplication, frozen versions and trusted render chronology produce identical assistance labels, temporal verdicts, review debt and next method across receipt permutations; quarantine unresolved conflicts.
9. **Assertion-level audit without efficacy inflation.** A–F emit exact source/task/rubric/evaluator IDs, frozen anchors/event/deadline/correction fields, first/final learner-authored utterances, rendered cues, recipient outcomes, delayed/unseen denominators, confounds, replay permutations and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion. Documentation CI is not executable evaluator PASS, CEFR award or proof of improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, deterministic seed/clock, source-resolved CEFR-linked temporal tasks, target-language/variety-validated reference/deadline rubrics, frozen source and correction versions, trusted cue render chronology, first unaided recipient-facing responses, observable recipient outcomes, completed delayed genuinely unseen transfer with denominators, comparable method/topic/modality/access exposure, protected skills/reviews, receipt-order permutations, reversible next-method traces and assertion-level verdicts. Missing valid source, accessible channel, unambiguous scoring, completed independent follow-up or trusted chronology remains UNSUPPORTED/unknown.

Any claim that adaptation **improves durable independent temporal communication** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, delayed unseen tasks, multilingual/variety and accessibility validation, human adjudication, privacy/subgroup-harm review and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor helps learners communicate what is scheduled, when it applies, and what is still uncertain—without inventing dates or treating plans as completed facts.**
