**Contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F33 requires genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish selected from actually rendered support; F47/F48 protect noncompensatory CEFR evidence; F49 protects accessibility; F50 protects soft-interest breadth; F53 protects language variety/register; F54 tests conversational repair; F76 tests information-gap questions; F89 tests correction fidelity; F100 tests cross-construct transfer; F103 tests critical-detail confirmation; F104 controls confounded instructional comparisons. **Distinct construct:** can a tutor use a learner's *optional, contextual metacognitive signals* (confidence, uncertainty recognition, error detection and help requests) to select reversible instruction **without confusing self-report with mastery**, coercing a confidence disclosure, rewarding unnecessary hints, or penalizing legitimate clarification—and can the learner later monitor and repair language use **independently in a genuinely unseen interaction**? ## Source-linked setup, privacy and protected invariants

Resolve a real versioned CEFR descriptor/can-do, level blueprint/prerequisites, source-authored task and permitted answer space, target language/variety/register, source/recipient roles, task's communicative stakes, authorized clarification protocol, rubric/evaluator version, accessible modality, assistance policy, first-unaided independence threshold, required receptive/productive skills, due reviews, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer and exit gate. Declare the task-specific uncertainty that a proficient speaker may reasonably identify: an ambiguous reference, incomplete source, unfamiliar form, potential grammatical/semantic error, or a truly unknown fact. **Do not invent a universal CEFR requirement to rate confidence, narrate thinking, self-correct aloud or always ask a question.** If the real task lacks an assessable monitoring/repair opportunity, report NOT_APPLICABLE; if the source or rubric cannot adjudicate the action, report UNSUPPORTED.

Optional learner self-ratings are **weak, context-sensitive observations**, not tests of honesty, personality, intelligence or fixed “learning style.” A missing rating, “prefer not to say,” silence, low confidence, a request for accessibility support or a valid question about missing source information cannot automatically lower mastery. Keep raw subjective rating separate from task-grounded first-unaided quality, justified uncertainty, source-valid clarification, spontaneous learner-authored repair, tutor prompting, retries and completed independent transfer. Do not pressure learners to reveal sensitive personal beliefs, disabilities, anxiety, health or private information to unlock a lesson. Avoid diagnostic or psychological labels.

Eligible reversible instruction may include BALANCED_DEFAULT, CALIBRATION_WITH_FEEDBACK, INDEPENDENT_CHECK_THEN_FEEDBACK, CONTRASTIVE_ERROR_NOTICING, JUSTIFIED_CLARIFICATION_PRACTICE, GRADUATED_HINT_FADING, and SOURCE_VALID_OTHER **only when implemented and evaluable**. Vary explanation, supported listening/reading/writing/speaking/free-production mix, scaffolding, pacing, repetition/review spacing, ordering and examples. Preserve identical CEFR objective, language meaning, independence rubric, required skill coverage, review debt, unseen-transfer requirement and exit gate. Do not ask “are you visual or auditory?” or assign a permanent modality category. Interest personalization is a separate soft probability layer: retain preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts.

Freeze deterministic seed/clock and session-qualified event IDs. For each eligible event log source/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, language/variety/register, baseline independent ability, task difficulty, topic familiarity, modality/accessibility, optional rating offer and consent, actual learner rating (or unknown), first learner-authored move, independently adjudicated semantic quality, source-authorized clarification, spontaneous or prompted repair, error type, hint/model exposure and **actual rendered cue chronology**, selected/assigned/rendered method, retries, completion, later delayed independent retrieval and genuinely unseen transfer, and offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators. Latency is usable only when reliably available and contextually valid; never fabricate it. If a confidence rating was requested **after** feedback, mark it post-feedback rather than treating it as a pre-answer forecast. No telemetry receipt-order trick may relabel a coached reply independent.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Confident but wrong, uncertain but competent.** Two learners complete a source-authored request to distinguish a tentative invitation from a confirmed commitment. Learner A voluntarily predicts “certain,” then independently reports the proposed time as confirmed; learner B declines the rating but independently preserves “proposed, not yet agreed.” In a matched task where the source explicitly confirms the time, the expected response reverses. A second source task genuinely lacks confirmation: B's appropriate clarification is not a wrong answer or hint dependence. Later unseen interactions change the source wording, interlocutor, topic and timing. Verdicts must follow source-grounded independent communication, not self-reported confidence or rating participation.

**B — Spontaneous repair versus answer-bearing tutor cue.** In a first unaided message a learner writes a wrong quantity, notices the mismatch against the accessible source without any tutor prompt, and sends a learner-authored correction before the recipient acts. Another learner corrects only after the tutor displays the right quantity; a third sees a selected hint that never renders. A fourth asks the legitimate interlocutor to repeat an indistinct number under the frozen source protocol. Grade first-attempt accuracy, autonomous repair, assisted repair and source-valid clarification as **different evidence**. A later telemetry packet reporting the displayed hint must not change the trusted cue-before-reply chronology. No assisted correction becomes independent first-attempt mastery.

**C — Help-seeking is neither always weakness nor always strength.** A source-authored multi-step instruction omits a destination; the learner appropriately asks which location is intended. In a matched complete-information world the destination is explicit, and a repeated request for the already supplied answer adds no information. In a third world an inaccessible listening stimulus is not repaired by an equivalent transcript; the learner's accommodation request cannot be treated as a linguistic failure or a discretionary “hint.” A learner who repeatedly asks the tutor to write the full response must have assisted outcomes separated from a peer who asks a source-valid interlocutor question. Later transfer varies information gaps and task stakes without rewarding compulsive questioning.

**D — Immediate confidence coaching versus delayed autonomous monitoring.** Across multiple real sessions, CALIBRATION_WITH_FEEDBACK produces high post-feedback confidence and fast coached corrections, while INDEPENDENT_CHECK_THEN_FEEDBACK initially produces slower first responses and some self-repair. On later distinct sessions with source-authored unseen tasks, the second method has stronger **observed** unaided error detection and recipient-safe repair, with comparable baseline, difficulty, practice dose, cue exposure, topic familiarity, language variety and accessibility. Some follow-ups are offered but not rendered, and others are rendered but not completed; missingness is reported, not imputed as success or failure. If comparability or real follow-up is inadequate, retain BALANCED_DEFAULT and uncertainty rather than declaring a winner or a causal gain.

**E — Topic familiarity, register and error-type drift.** A learner confidently monitors grammar in favorite-game chats but misses the same communicative distinction in neutral service messages and unfamiliar community exchanges. Another uses a legitimate regional variant the original rubric mistakenly rejects; a corrected source-approved rubric changes the grade, not the learner's ability. A learner becomes independently reliable with temporal references but newly struggles with indirect requests; do not generalize “well calibrated” across objectives. Matched controls rotate preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics at comparable difficulty, preserve variety/register, and update only capability-local, reversible instructional hypotheses. A popular topic is not proof that an instructional method works.

**F — Offline replay, feedback leakage, abstention and changing evidence.** Two genuine offline sessions reuse a local event ID; one replay duplicates a completed event; a confidence rating is captured only after feedback; a tutor's correct model rendered before a learner response but its telemetry arrives afterward; another rating is absent because the learner declined. A rubric revision changes an accepted regional form; an eligible delayed probe remains uncompleted; a later genuinely unseen first-unaided repair contradicts an earlier weak preference for worked examples. Deduplicate only session-qualified replays, quarantine incompatible versions, preserve actual cue order and missing follow-up, and revise method choice cautiously. Permuting network receipt order cannot change independence, mastery, review debt, or the next-method verdict. The learner's choice not to self-rate is not a defect.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real source and optional monitoring boundary.** A–F require a versioned CEFR can-do, source-authored task, authorized source/recipient, rubric/evaluator, language/variety/register, accessible channel, task-valid uncertainty and repair protocol, assistance policy, first-unaided threshold, protected skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. No task-valid monitoring opportunity is NOT_APPLICABLE; absent source mapping is UNSUPPORTED, not a newly invented CEFR milestone.

2. **Self-report cannot grant or deny mastery.** A/D/E/F preserve optional pre-answer ratings separately from post-feedback ratings, missingness, first-unaided semantic quality and later independent outcomes. Confidence cannot substitute for correct source-faithful communication; refusal or absence of a rating cannot reduce a mastery score, prevent progression or be interpreted as a fixed learner trait.

3. **Independent detection and repair have provenance.** B/F distinguish a wrong initial answer followed by spontaneous learner-authored correction from a correct first attempt and from tutor-prompted repair. Score all three separately with trusted actually rendered chronology. Selected-but-unrendered help does not contaminate evidence; an answer-bearing tutor cue before the assessed reply makes the affected outcome assisted, even when telemetry arrives late.

4. **Clarification is judged by information need and authority.** A/C distinguish justified questions to a source-authorized interlocutor from tutor requests for a completed answer and from repetitive questions where the source already supplies the needed detail. Legitimate interlocutor replies under a frozen protocol are task stimuli, not automatically tutor scaffolds. Unknown source facts cannot be confidently fabricated; neither can every question be rewarded as superior learning.

5. **Autonomy without coercion or inaccessible demands.** B/C/F preserve accessible accommodation, privacy, age-appropriate participation, voluntary ratings and nonpunitive uncertainty. Missing speech/audio or unreliable timing cannot be used as failure. No forced introspection, disclosure, accessibility withholding, inaccessible modality exploration, timed confidence gate or psychological classification is permitted.

6. **Delayed and genuinely unseen independent monitoring transfer.** A–F require meaningful time/intervening material across distinct real sessions, changed source-authored task surface and context, first-unaided learner-authored detection/clarification/repair and independently adjudicated communicative outcome. Replayed wording, coached repair, same-session rephrasing, ratings alone and missing follow-up cannot establish durable retention or transfer. Preserve eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators.

7. **Protected CEFR breadth and soft interests.** C/D/E preserve required listening, reading, writing, speaking and free production as applicable to the actual blueprint, plus due reviews, prerequisites and exit gates. Compare or disclose topic familiarity, task difficulty, source authority, language variety/register, modality/accessibility, practice dose and cue exposure. Interests only softly weight examples; adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts remain sampled. Never substitute a text proxy for required auditory evidence without source authorization.

8. **Reversible uncertainty-aware instruction and event invariance.** D/E/F log feasible candidate methods, selected/assigned/actually rendered action, observed first-unaided and delayed unseen outcomes, missingness, confidence in the hypothesis, exploration constraint, next-action rationale and safe fallback. Later independent contradictory evidence can reverse a weak preference; sparse or confounded data revert to BALANCED_DEFAULT. Session-qualified replay, version quarantine and trusted event-time cue chronology yield identical independent-evidence, review-debt and next-method decisions under receipt permutations.

9. **Assertion-level audit and bounded claims.** A–F output source/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, source-valid answer/uncertainty, rating provenance and consent if present, first learner-authored response, spontaneous versus prompted repair, legitimate partner exchange, rendered cue chronology, independent delayed/unseen outcomes, follow-up denominators, topic/modality controls, next-method trace and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion. Documentation CI or synthetic PASS does not prove shipped metacognitive coaching, human retention gains, causal instructional effectiveness or a production defect.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, frozen real CEFR-linked source tasks with adjudicable uncertainty/repair, multilingual/variety-aware rubrics, deterministic seed/clock, real session-qualified events, trustworthy rendered cue and partner chronology, optional rating provenance, independent first attempts and autonomous repairs, delayed genuinely unseen independent transfer, comparable method/topic/modality conditions, missing-follow-up denominators, protected skill/review/exit requirements, replay/version permutations and assertion-level verdicts. Missing mapping, ambiguous authority, inaccessible stimulus, invalid rating chronology or absent independent follow-up remains UNSUPPORTED/unknown.

A stronger claim that these instructional choices **improve durable autonomous language learning** requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence with credible balanced comparator or ethically reviewed experimental design, human adjudication, multilingual/accessibility validation, missing-data and subgroup-harm review, privacy protections and ongoing monitoring. **A strong tutor helps learners notice what they know and do not know, repair mistakes without dependence, and ask for clarification when the information truly warrants it—while judging mastery by independent communication, not confidence theater.**
