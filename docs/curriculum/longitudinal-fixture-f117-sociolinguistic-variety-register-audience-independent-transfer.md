# F117 — Sociolinguistic variety, audience/register fit and independent transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD, real versioned CEFR-linked tasks, source-validated acceptable language varieties/registers and longitudinal first-unaided outcomes. This does **not** ship dialect identification, accent classification, register scoring, cultural profiling, CEFR mastery or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 protects unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish actual assistance exposure from assigned help and tutor-origin faults; F49 protects accessible construct validity; F50 keeps interest personalization nonexclusive; F63 addresses first-language instructional bridges; F68 metacognitive calibration; F104 method confounds; F107 unreliable feedback; F109 code-switching and borrowing; F110 nonverbal cue uncertainty; F111 ASR noise. **Distinct construct:** when a source-authored task requires communication to a particular audience in a validated target-language variety and register, can the learner select **linguistically valid, audience-appropriate forms**, recognize alternatives, and adapt across new recipients without the tutor confusing dialect, accent, social identity, formality or unfamiliar expressions with low proficiency? A language variety is not a learner type; the task's communicative requirements, not prestige stereotypes, govern evaluation.

## Source, audience and evidence contract

For each case resolve the **real versioned** CEFR descriptor/can-do, level blueprint/prerequisites, target language, source-authorized region/variety when relevant, actual communicative purpose, recipient relationship and stated preferences, medium (spoken/written), formality and politeness constraints, source-authored message/stimulus, permitted variants, rubric/evaluator, first-unaided threshold, protected reading/listening/writing/speaking/free-production evidence, assistance/accommodation policy, due reviews, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer and exit gate. **Do not invent a universal formal-register requirement** where the source can-do only requires intelligible everyday communication. Source truth may specify a formal letter, an informal peer conversation or a recipient's explicitly requested form; an evaluator cannot silently add prestige-dialect, native-accent or honorific demands.

Freeze an **audience-and-variety ledger**: source/task/descriptor/rubric/evaluator hashes; language and source-approved variety set; recipient role and stated preferences; channel, communicative stakes and relevant social context explicitly supplied by the source; accepted lexical, grammatical and pragmatic alternatives with evidence provenance; what counts as meaning preserved, audience fit, recoverable misunderstanding or source-valid clarification; first learner-authored attempt, recipient response/action and actual tutor feedback/model/hint exposure; later delayed and unseen probes. Record unknowns explicitly. Avoid inferring nationality, class, ethnicity, gender, disability, migration history or native-speaker status from speech, names, pronouns, vocabulary, accent, latency or device. Do not store biometric voiceprints or sensitive demographic predictions. If a variety is not validated for the source task, mark that scoring claim **UNSUPPORTED**, not automatically incorrect.

Task-local labels: SOURCE_VALID_TASK, SOURCE_REGISTER_REQUIRED, REGISTER_NOT_REQUIRED, VARIETY_AUTHORIZED, VARIETY_UNVALIDATED, RECIPIENT_PREFERENCE_EXPLICIT, AUDIENCE_UNDERDETERMINED, FORM_VALID_IN_VARIETY, FORM_UNVALIDATED, MEANING_PRESERVED, MEANING_CHANGED, REGISTER_FIT, REGISTER_MISMATCH_SOURCE_VALID, POLITENESS_STRATEGY_VALID, CLARIFICATION_SOURCE_VALID, RECIPIENT_ACTION_VALID, RECIPIENT_ACTION_UNKNOWN, DIALECT_PRESTIGE_BIAS_RISK, ACCENT_NOT_PROFICIENCY, FIRST_UNAIDED, ACTUALLY_RENDERED_HELP, ASSISTED, DELAYED_UNAIDED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_UNAIDED_TRANSFER, TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED, VARIETY_METHOD_CONFOUNDED, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, VERSION_CONFLICT_QUARANTINED, NOT_APPLICABLE and UNSUPPORTED. These describe task observations, **not** fixed learner profiles.

Vary only actually implemented, accessible and source-validated explanation style, scaffolds, pacing, modality mix, practice order, review spacing and contexts. Preserve the same CEFR objectives, rubric meaning, independence thresholds, protected skill families, due reviews and exit gates. Estimate method utility from **later independent retention/transfer** with uncertainty and deliberate alternative sampling, not short-term coached politeness or self-reported learning styles. ## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Valid regional alternative is not an error.** A source-authored, informal scheduling task permits multiple validated target-language varieties. Learner A independently writes a regionally natural and recipient-intelligible expression; learner B writes a different equally valid form. Both convey the same source-required date, action and level of courtesy. An evaluator that prefers one prestige variety falsely marks A incorrect. The expected result accepts both for the shared can-do, records variety evidence, and does not impose an unannounced register threshold. In a controlled counterfactual, the source task explicitly requires a named variety for a localized audience; only then can an appropriate source-validated form constraint apply, without generalizing that constraint to the learner's proficiency elsewhere.

**B — Same grammar, different recipient stakes.** A learner writes “Send me the report today” to a close teammate in a source-approved informal chat and to a formal external client whose task specifies a polite request. The semantic request remains recognizable in both, but the second can be source-validly marked a **register mismatch** if the frozen rubric requires appropriate professional tone. The tutor must teach an alternative without claiming the learner cannot form requests at all. Swap only the recipient/audience constraint while holding can-do, difficulty and communicative action stable. If the recipient relationship was never provided, “Please clarify who this is for” can be valid; invented authority, gender or cultural hierarchy cannot drive the score.

**C — Politeness is not one rigid phrase.** A source-authored task requires respectfully declining an invitation while preserving a relationship. Two independently produced responses use different validated strategies: a brief direct decline with appreciation, and a longer indirect decline with an explanation. Both meet the source's communicative and register demands. A third learner copies a tutor-supplied ideal phrase after it was actually rendered; communicative success is creditable but **not** first-unaided productive mastery. A fourth uses a grammatical but semantically misleading softener that the recipient reasonably reads as tentative acceptance; evaluate the recipient-facing consequence separately from grammaticality. Never treat verbosity, excessive apology or a particular national etiquette stereotype as universal evidence of competence.

**D — Accent, ASR and accessibility do not define register.** Two learners deliver source-valid spoken requests with distinct accents; an ASR transcript mangles a courtesy marker for one while independently verified audio/authorized transcription supports the intended wording. Score source-valid spoken language from reliable evidence and attribute ASR uncertainty to the channel per F111. A learner using an approved text or synthesized-voice route can demonstrate the source-valid communicative construct where equivalence is prevalidated; do not silently credit phonological production if the task actually requires it. If reliable audio, access equivalence or source-approved variety coverage is absent, the affected claim remains UNSUPPORTED rather than a learner failure. No accent-based inference of class, nationality, intelligence or politeness.

**E — Method adaptation must survive new audiences.** Fictional learner A shows strong immediate assisted register matching after memorizing model phrases, but weak delayed independent adaptation to an unfamiliar recipient. Learner A later improves first-unaided transfer after SOURCE_VALID_AUDIENCE_ROLEPLAY; learner B shows the reverse pattern and benefits from CONTRASTIVE_REGISTER_EXPLANATION. Compare source difficulty, objective, baseline, variety, audience familiarity, task stakes, modality/access route, hint exposure, topic and attrition. With sufficient comparable longitudinal evidence, log different **actually delivered** next methods with confidence bounds; continue sampling alternatives and preserve all protected skills and contexts. If roleplay always occurs with familiar peers and contrastive explanation always with unfamiliar clients, mark VARIETY_METHOD_CONFOUNDED or audience confounding instead of claiming method superiority. Later contradictory outcomes must reduce confidence and permit reversal.

**F — Source changes, delayed events and unknown audience.** An offline learner answers a source-approved informal message; a later task revision changes the audience to a formal institution. Events upload out of order, with one submission replayed twice and an answer-bearing model phrase displayed only **after** the original first attempt. Score the original attempt against its original source-effective audience/rubric, deduplicate retries by session-qualified immutable event IDs and preserve actual render chronology. A different session reuses the same local task ID but targets a different recipient; never merge its evidence. If the recipient's preference is unknown, do not infer a required honorific or mark a regional form wrong; ask for clarification or use a neutral source-valid fallback. Quarantine irreconcilable source/version conflicts rather than rewriting past mastery or manufacturing an adaptation signal.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-authorized sociolinguistic construct.** A–F resolve real versioned CEFR can-do, blueprint, source task, audience/recipient, register requirement **if any**, validated variety alternatives, rubric/evaluator, assistance policy, accommodations, independent threshold, protected skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. No source-valid register requirement means REGISTER_NOT_REQUIRED, not an invented politeness gate.
2. **Variety validity without prestige bias.** A/C/D accept equivalent source-validated forms across permitted varieties; unsupported or unfamiliar forms trigger VARIETY_UNVALIDATED/UNSUPPORTED and review, not automatic incorrectness. Accent, vocabulary and names do not create demographic labels or native-speaker preferences.
3. **Meaning, register and recipient outcome separated.** B/C distinguish semantic intent, grammatical form, source-required audience fit and independently adjudicated recipient action. A register mismatch cannot erase otherwise demonstrated grammatical knowledge; a polished polite phrase cannot compensate for a changed critical commitment.
4. **Ambiguity and recipient authority.** B/F honor explicit source-authored audience facts and preferences; underdetermined relationships permit source-valid clarification or neutral fallback. No invented hierarchy, culture, gender, class or fixed national etiquette rule may determine correctness.
5. **Actual assistance and chronology.** C/F preserve first unaided production, model/hint exposure actually rendered before the answer, later rewrites, retries and source-effective rubric versions. Unseen, queued or post-answer help cannot retroactively contaminate independent evidence.
6. **Accessible and privacy-respecting adjudication.** D/F validate spoken/written construct equivalence, permitted modalities, accent-independent evaluation, ASR attribution, data minimization, consent and retention. Unknown channel quality or access equivalence produces bounded UNSUPPORTED, not proficiency or disability inference.
7. **Delayed independent retrieval and unseen recipient transfer.** A–F require meaningfully separated sessions and genuinely unseen source-authored audiences, situations and expressions, without answer-bearing models. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators; copied etiquette scripts or near-identical recipient prompts do not pass unseen transfer.
8. **Reversible method selection and soft topic rotation.** E/F log feasible, assigned and actually delivered methods, uncertainty, baseline, variety, audience, task difficulty, modality/access, help exposure, topics and missingness. Preserve alternative sampling, required receptive/productive skills, due reviews and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar contexts. Replaying events or swapping a prestige-variety label cannot manufacture a method winner.
9. **Exact-head reproducibility and bounded claims.** A–F emit deterministic seed/clock, named engine HEAD and runnable command, real descriptor/source/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, validated variety alternatives, audience facts, exposure/event ledger, first and assisted responses, recipient adjudication, delayed/unseen denominators, confound/replay/source-version permutations, and **PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion**. A documentation-only CI pass is not an executable register evaluator, a reproduced production defect or proof of real learner improvement.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires an exact engine HEAD, reproducible command and deterministic clock; real versioned CEFR-linked tasks; independently source-validated audience and variety/register alternatives; recipient-facing adjudication; consent-appropriate exposure chronology; first-unaided evidence; meaningful delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen audience transfer; construct-valid accessible routes; comparable or explicitly noncomparable methods and contexts; protected skill/review/exit obligations; complete eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators; event/source-version permutations and per-assertion verdicts. Without source-valid audience or variety coverage, reliable evidence, consent or independent follow-up, affected claims remain **UNSUPPORTED/unknown**, while independently established communicative evidence is retained.

A stronger claim that audience-aware adaptive instruction **improves durable independent communication** requires a separately reviewed, multilingual/multivariety and accessibility-validated real-learner longitudinal study with a credible comparator, attrition and subgroup-harm analysis, privacy review and reproducible independent outcomes. **A strong tutor teaches learners to adapt to people and situations without telling them that their valid language variety is inferior.**
