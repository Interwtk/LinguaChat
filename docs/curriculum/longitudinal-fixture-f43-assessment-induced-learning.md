# F43 — Assessment-induced learning cannot be misattributed to the teaching method

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a reproduced defect, shipped adaptive tutor, causal estimate or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F24 guards against biased method assignment; F37 separates assigned from delivered instruction; F39 accounts for external instruction; F41 guards against selective reporting; F42 preserves learner agency. F43 isolates **assessment as an instructional exposure**: retrieval attempts, answer-bearing feedback, worked corrections and repeated or previewed test items can themselves change later performance. The tutor must not automatically credit that change to the preceding explanation or activity. A valid independent assessment may still establish mastery; **validity of an observed can-do is separate from attribution of why it improved**.

## Controlled setup

Resolve `goal-1` to one **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, supported skill, frozen rubric, prerequisites and independent mastery/exit gate. Compare feasible A (`concise_explanation`), B (`worked_example`) and the shared balanced comparator. Hold objective, opportunity, task demand, difficulty calibration, context novelty, due reviews and supported receptive/productive opportunities constant where feasible. Use an injected clock/seed, versioned `assessment_exposure_policy` and an immutable synthetic ledger across distinct sessions and meaningful actual delays.

Predeclare each **assessment occasion** and whether it is a diagnostic pretest, low-stakes retrieval practice, assisted formative check, feedback-bearing correction, independent mastery probe, reserved transfer holdout or unavailable probe. Record stable learner/objective/skill/session/run/event/probe/item-family IDs; rubric and policy versions; instruction assignment and verified delivery; actual assessment delivery and first-attempt response; whether hints, correctness cues, model answers or worked solutions appeared **before, during or after** the response; item reuse, semantic overlap and answer exposure; feedback timing/content; retrieval attempts and repetitions; actual elapsed time and intervening teaching/practice; independent delayed retrieval and unseen-context transfer; baseline and eligible/completed/missing follow-up denominators; due-review, skill and context debt; event versus upload time; and data-use/holdout lineage. Unknown exposure remains **unknown**, never assumed absent.

Separate four ledgers: (1) instruction assigned and delivered, (2) assessment/retrieval/feedback **delivered**, (3) valid independent mastery observations and (4) uncertain instruction-effect hypotheses. Declare `ASSESSMENT_AS_PRACTICE`, `FEEDBACK_EXPOSED`, `ITEM_FAMILIARITY`, `UNEQUAL_PROBE_DOSAGE`, `PRETEST_CARRYOVER`, `HOLDOUT_CONTAMINATED`, `EXPOSURE_UNKNOWN`, `NOT_COMPARABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. No synthetic counterfactual or unsupported audio/speaking route may be invented.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — Retrieval practice masquerades as an explanation win.** Learners receiving A take several unassisted formative retrieval quizzes; B learners receive fewer opportunities. A later performs better on a genuinely independent delayed probe. Preserve the valid delayed result, but expose unequal prior test attempts as `UNEQUAL_PROBE_DOSAGE`; do not conclude that A's explanation caused the difference. Rebalance opportunities or withhold comparative attribution.
- **B — Feedback-bearing probe is also a lesson.** An initial diagnostic answer is wrong; the quiz then shows the correct form, a model response and corrective explanation. The learner succeeds later on a new independent item. The later response may count as independent evidence if truly unaided and calibrated, but the earlier assessment delivered instruction. Record feedback timing and source; do not credit the entire improvement to A or treat the corrected diagnostic response as first-attempt mastery.
- **C — Reused test item creates familiarity.** A learner repeatedly sees the same prompt, paraphrases with near-identical answer structure and finally answers it fluently; unfamiliar-context free production remains weak. Flag `ITEM_FAMILIARITY` and `HOLDOUT_CONTAMINATED` for reused/leaked items; do not promote recognition or rehearsed reproduction to independent transfer or silently relax the shared can-do gate.
- **D — Pretesting changes subsequent learning.** One learner gets a demanding pretest before B and another receives B without pretesting. The pretested learner later shows stronger retrieval. Keep the pretest as a potential learning exposure, including unsuccessful retrieval and any feedback; the two histories are not a clean B-versus-comparator contrast. Do not invent a pretest-free counterfactual.
- **E — Comparable assessment schedules, revisable inference.** Two synthetic longitudinal histories have matched eligible/delivered quiz opportunities, feedback timing, item-family novelty, review debt and later unaided retrieval/transfer, with opposite objective-specific method patterns across later sessions. Permit only an uncertainty-aware, reversible instructional preference consistent with the actual evidence; keep nonzero feasible alternative sampling and the balanced comparator. Do not claim that synthetic matching establishes human causal efficacy.
- **F — Offline replay and hidden answer exposure.** A stale offline quiz arrives twice after a newer probe; one answer-bearing correction was shown locally but its upload is late; a supposedly reserved transfer item had already appeared in a practice card. Deduplicate by stable event IDs and preserve trusted occurrence versus upload order. Quarantine unknown exposure chronology and leaked holdouts, retain uncontaminated independent evidence, and make classifications invariant to replay order.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Separate delivered exposure ledgers.** For A–F report versioned can-do/rubric/skill, eligible/assigned/verified-delivered instruction, all actually delivered tests/retrieval attempts/feedback, valid independent outcomes, follow-up denominators and uncertainty. A displayed or scheduled quiz is not a delivered attempt; a correction is not a clean assessment.
2. **Retrieval practice is a competing explanation.** In A, surface quiz frequency, spacing, opportunity and first-attempt independence by method. Mark unequal assessment dose `UNEQUAL_PROBE_DOSAGE`/`NOT_COMPARABLE`; preserve valid mastery while withholding a superiority claim for A.
3. **Feedback is timestamped instructional exposure.** In B, distinguish first response from correction and later probe; record when answer-bearing support became visible. Corrected/assisted answers cannot become unaided first attempts, and later valid independent success does not prove which preceding exposure caused it.
4. **Novelty and holdout integrity.** In C, preserve item-family reuse, answer leakage and semantic overlap. Rehearsed items cannot certify unseen transfer; any compromised holdout is `HOLDOUT_CONTAMINATED`, not silently replaced by an easier equivalent.
5. **Pretest and carryover provenance.** In D, log pretest attempts, feedback, timing and downstream exposures before comparison. Do not infer identical instruction conditions or causal method effects when assessment histories differ.
6. **Revisable objective-specific hypotheses.** In E, only comparable verified exposures and genuinely later independent unfamiliar probes may adjust a learner/objective method hypothesis with explicit uncertainty. Continue feasible alternative sampling, due reviews and balanced skills; no fixed visual/auditory/reading learner label.
7. **Replay-safe assessment lineage.** In F, stable IDs, immutable first-attempt/feedback records, trusted event versus upload order, original policy/rubric versions and data-use history prevent duplicated exposure, backdated independence or reused holdouts. Ambiguous chronology is `EXPOSURE_UNKNOWN`, not presumed clean.
8. **Shared CEFR and interests remain protected.** Across A–F retain the same can-do, prerequisites, frozen rubric, independent exit gate, required productive/receptive opportunities, due reviews and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation. Interests remain a soft context layer, not an assessment-difficulty or teaching-method gate. Unsupported modalities stay `UNAVAILABLE`.
9. **Conservative release and attribution.** Without comparable instruction **and** assessment exposure, calibrated genuinely independent delayed retrieval and unfamiliar transfer, adequate follow-up and a credible balanced comparator, report instructional superiority `UNSUPPORTED`/`NOT_COMPARABLE`. Do not erase legitimate mastery evidence, invent missing feedback history or claim improved human learning from synthetic PASS.

## Required evidence and release gate

For A–F publish the **exact tested engine HEAD**, executable command, deterministic clock/seed, full immutable synthetic ledger, supported versioned CEFR can-do and frozen rubric, versioned assessment policy, all offered versus delivered instructional options, all delivered assessment/retrieval/feedback exposures, item reuse and answer-bearing chronology, first attempts and assistance, actual delayed and novel-context independent probes, eligible/completed/missing follow-up denominators, baseline/comparator opportunity balance, event and holdout data-use lineage, replay invariance, review/skill/context coverage, uncertainty and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion.

An eventual claim of durable instructional benefit requires separately reviewed longitudinal learner evidence with assessment dose and feedback accounted for, a credible balanced comparison, sufficiently delayed independent retrieval and unfamiliar transfer, calibrated uncertainty, heterogeneity and an ethically sound pilot. **A test can both measure and teach**; passing this documentation fixture would prove only a software acceptance invariant, not the effectiveness of a tutor.
