**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F27 guards latency observation bias; F33 protects genuinely unseen transfer; F37 records actually delivered instruction; F38 preserves tutor-origin attribution; F47/F48 preserve noncompensatory source-defined CEFR mastery; F49 protects accessibility equivalence; F50 protects topic breadth; F51 distinguishes spontaneous from prompted self-repair; F53 preserves register and legitimate varieties; F54 tests repair after breakdown; F58 protects learner initiative; F64 distinguishes fluency and accuracy; F66 tests interlocutor variation; F71 tests discourse cohesion; F73 tests pragmatic inference; F76 tests information-seeking initiative. **F77 isolates source-required management of the conversational floor:** noticing when a partner is continuing or yielding, distinguishing a listening acknowledgment from a request to take over, entering at an appropriate opportunity, keeping or relinquishing a turn when necessary, and repairing an actual overlap or missed cue independently. Correct sentences, fast replies, repeated “yes,” or a polished transcript alone do not establish these interactive abilities. ## Controlled setup

Resolve a **real, versioned** CEFR descriptor/can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, task, interlocutor and relationship, communicative goal, source-defined interaction moves, permitted varieties and norms, evaluator/rubric versions, actual channel (synchronous audio/video, asynchronous text, or simulated dialogue), supported modalities, assistance rules, independent threshold, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen interaction transfer, due reviews and level exit gate. Freeze the interlocutor's scripted turns and intended opportunities **before** comparing instruction. Label each opportunity using source-validated semantics: `CONTINUATION`, `YIELD`, `ACKNOWLEDGMENT`, `CLARIFICATION_REQUEST`, `OVERLAP`, `UNRESOLVED` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`; allow multiple defensible readings when validated by the rubric. A pause, punctuation, transcript boundary, response delay or language variety cannot alone determine floor ownership. For text-only channels, assess only what is observable in that channel; mark unmeasured acoustic/prosodic cues `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE`.

Predeclare reversible instruction candidates `CUE_FUNCTION_CONTRAST`, `BACKCHANNEL_VS_TAKEOVER_PRACTICE`, `OVERLAP_REPAIR_ROLEPLAY`, `CONTEXTUAL_TURN_ENTRY`, `OPEN_UNSEEN_INTERACTION` and `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. They may vary supported listening/reading/writing/speaking mix, explanation depth, example order, scaffolding, feedback timing, pacing, review spacing and interlocutor scenarios while preserving the same CEFR goal, source-defined floor opportunities, difficulty and independent standard. A tutor-provided “your turn now,” ready-made entry phrase, highlighted pause, caption identifying a yield, or prefilled repair sentence is **assistance** for the affected move. An ordinary source-permitted interlocutor's continuation, acknowledgment, objection or explicit request is **task interaction**, not automatically coaching; log any departure from the frozen protocol. Preserve feasible alternative sampling and a balanced skill mix. Do not infer permanent “fast,” “shy,” “auditory” or “social” learner types.

Use deterministic pseudonymous multi-session histories with injected seed/clock and immutable session-qualified event IDs. Record can-do, task, source, language variety, rubric and evaluator versions; interlocutor role, channel, cue transcript/audio provenance and source-valid alternative interpretations; opportunity ID and timing **only when directly captured and clock-calibrated**; first learner-authored entry, acknowledgment, clarification, interruption, continuation, relinquishment or repair; independent communicative outcome; selected/assigned/**actually rendered** instruction; exact tutor cues, hints, captions, models and their trusted display chronology; retries, scaffold dependence, optional reliable response timing, topic familiarity and access accommodation; delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen interlocutor/context/cue-pattern transfer; and eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators. Never infer speech perception from typed text, hesitation from asynchronous timestamps, fluency from words per minute alone, or social competence from compliance with a single conversational norm.

## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — An acknowledgment is not a turn handoff.** In a source-defined collaborative exchange, the interlocutor says a short acknowledgment while continuing an explanation. Learner A treats it as permission to launch a new topic; learner B gives a valid listening response and waits for a source-validated yield, then enters clearly. An alternative language variety permits a different acknowledgment form. Keep acknowledgment function, genuine yield, timing and eventual task completion separate; do not reward counting backchannels or penalize source-valid quiet listening. If the frozen task genuinely invites immediate entry, do not label it an interruption.
- **B — Polished text hides missed interactive opportunities.** Learner A produces grammatically accurate answers in a typed simulation but repeatedly misses source-required invitations to clarify or contribute in a separate actual supported audio interaction. Learner B uses simpler language to take a valid turn, ask for repetition after an overlap and complete the same goal. A third learner only has text access. Distinguish language form, conversational move and communicative outcome; text-only success cannot certify audio turn recognition, while a source-permitted accessible alternative can establish its **validated equivalent construct**, not imaginary listening evidence.
- **C — Tutor cue exposure makes initiative assisted.** Before the interlocutor finishes, the tutor flashes “wait”; later it highlights “now respond” and displays a model interruption repair. Learner A repeats the model perfectly. In a meaningfully delayed session with a new interlocutor and a different source-equivalent yield/overlap pattern, learner B independently enters, negotiates a missed cue and resumes the shared goal without prompts. Preserve first move, actual displayed cues, coached revisions and later independent result. A hidden or merely assigned hint must not contaminate evidence, while a rendered answer-bearing cue must.
- **D — Early apparent advantage reverses under unseen transfer.** Matched early independent probes suggest `CUE_FUNCTION_CONTRAST` helps learner A more than `OVERLAP_REPAIR_ROLEPLAY`, while learner B shows the reverse. Later delayed exchanges with different speakers, unfamiliar but equivalent cue patterns and the same communicative burden reverse A's advantage. Match or disclose accent/variety, interlocutor pace, pause structure, audio quality, topic, task dose, accessibility, feedback exposure and evaluator versions. Reduce confidence, sample alternatives and log the **actually rendered** revised next instruction, not merely an internal policy assignment.
- **E — Favorite topics, timing proxies and access confounds.** Learner A appears to take turns quickly in favorite-game roleplays but misses entry opportunities in neutral and unfamiliar service scenarios. A network-delayed learner has longer message timestamps despite successful independent turn selection. Another uses an authorized caption/accommodation that supplies words but does not identify who owns the floor; a different caption explicitly highlights “your turn,” contaminating the target. Rotate favorite, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts; protect due reviews and required skill families. Never penalize disability, connection speed, speech rate or an unfamiliar accent as a substitute for source-defined interaction quality.
- **F — Reordered cues, edited audio and incomplete follow-up.** Two offline sessions reuse a local turn ID; a tutor's “your turn” overlay was rendered before the learner response but uploaded afterward; an interlocutor audio clip changes under the same local response ID; a transcript omits a backchannel; an offered delayed exchange is never completed; and a rubric revision changes whether a floor-entry move is required. Reconstruct from immutable session-qualified IDs, media/cue hashes, trusted render chronology and frozen rubric versions. Deduplicate replays, quarantine irreconcilable audio/transcript mismatches, preserve missing follow-up as unknown and require identical independent/assisted labels, skill debt and next-choice traces under receipt-order permutations.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-resolved interaction construct.** For A–F emit real versioned CEFR can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, goal, interlocutor, task, channel, source-valid floor opportunities and alternate norms, rubric, assistance rules, independent threshold, delayed/unseen probes, due reviews and exit gate. Missing validated source or invented turn-taking obligations yields `UNSUPPORTED`.
2. **Function beats speed and surface polish.** In A/B/E distinguish first independent acknowledgment, yield recognition, entry/continuation/relinquishment and outcome from grammatical accuracy, word count, reaction speed or transcript punctuation. A fast interruption cannot compensate for a source-required missed move; legitimate alternate norms remain eligible.
3. **Backchannels, handoffs and repairs are distinct.** In A/B/F classify source-supported `ACKNOWLEDGMENT`, `CONTINUATION`, `YIELD`, `OVERLAP` and `UNRESOLVED` without treating every pause or “yes” as a handoff. Credit an independently initiated source-valid overlap repair or clarification only when it actually addresses the interaction, not a memorized apology unrelated to the cue.
4. **Observed modality and accessibility boundaries.** In B/E report actual supported channel, audio/prosody provenance, timing reliability and source-validated accommodations. Asynchronous text latency is not hesitation; written dialogue cannot establish unmeasured listening/speaking, and a caption that reveals the correct floor move is tutoring rather than a neutral accommodation.
5. **Interlocutor task cues versus tutor coaching.** In A/C/F preserve exact first learner-authored move and trusted actual render chronology of prompts, overlays, transcripts, model phrases and interlocutor cues. Ordinary source-permitted dialogue is not automatically a hint; displayed answer-bearing floor signals are assisted, regardless of whether the learner typed the response unaided afterward.
6. **Independent delayed unseen turn management.** In C/D/F require meaningfully separate sessions, intervening time/practice, new source-equivalent cue patterns and interlocutors, and eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Immediate replay, rehearsed pauses, transcript-only reproduction and missing follow-up cannot establish durable independent interactive competence.
7. **Comparable methods and soft-interest breadth.** In D/E match or disclose cue ambiguity, pace, variety, interlocutor cooperativeness, channel, topic familiarity, accessibility, practice dose and exposure. Keep CEFR objectives, noncompensatory receptive/productive evidence, due reviews and favorite/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar rotation; interests never remove required interaction contexts.
8. **Reversible policy and replay invariance.** In C/D/E/F emit feasible options, selected/assigned/actually rendered method, delayed independent outcomes, uncertainty, contradictory evidence, exploration and next choice. Sparse or confounded evidence falls back to `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. In F deduplicate stable events, validate media/cue hashes and rubric versions, reconstruct trusted delivery chronology and assert identical independent labels, follow-up missingness, skill/review debt and next-choice rationale across arrival-order permutations; unresolved provenance fails closed.
9. **Assertion-level audit without efficacy inflation.** For A–F emit source/rubric/evaluator IDs, channel and opportunity truth, first/final independent/assisted moves, actual rendered cues, source-defined goal outcome, delayed/unseen denominators, topic/interlocutor/access/timing confounds, replay equivalence, policy uncertainty and `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED` per assertion. Documentation CI does not establish a shipped audio evaluator, CEFR certification or learning gains.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command; deterministic multi-session seed/clock; real source-resolved CEFR interaction tasks with validated language-/variety-aware rubrics; frozen interlocutor cues and allowable alternatives; captured channel and trusted actual tutor cue delivery; learner-authored first moves; calibrated timing only when available; independently completed delayed unseen interactions with follow-up denominators; comparable instruction, context and accessibility exposure; protected CEFR skill/review obligations; replay-order permutations; reversible next-instruction traces; and assertion-level verdicts. Missing source mapping, actual audio, cue adjudication, timing provenance, rubric equivalence or follow-up remains `UNSUPPORTED`/unknown, never invented proficiency.

A claim that instruction **improves durable independent turn-taking and conversational repair** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, independently completed delayed unseen exchanges, multilingual/variety and accessibility evaluator validation, human adjudication, privacy and subgroup-harm review, and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor teaches the learner to notice and manage real interaction independently—not merely to respond quickly when the screen says “your turn.”**
