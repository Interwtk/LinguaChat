# F124 — Wayfinding map, legend, route constraints and independent communicative transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run on a named exact engine HEAD against real versioned CEFR-linked tasks, independently source-validated multilingual and accessible wayfinding materials, and delayed first-unaided transfer. This does **not** ship a map reader, live navigation/routing service, accessible-route certification, geolocation tracker, CEFR mastery or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F33 protects genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish delivered assistance and tutor-origin mistakes; F49 protects accessibility construct validity; F50 keeps interests nonexclusive; F72 covers task goals/constraints; F82/F95 cover time references; F84/F114 cover perspective, landmarks and visual deixis; F103 covers consequential confirmation; F104 isolates method from difficulty; F107 covers unreliable tutor feedback; F118 covers conditions and negation; F120 covers source certainty; F121 covers structured-document binding; F122 covers chart scales/legends; F123 covers abstract flowchart decisions. **Distinct construct:** can the learner independently **understand, ask for, give or verify a source-supported route in a mapped environment** by binding origin/destination, map orientation, symbols, connected paths, constraints and relevant landmarks to a communicative instruction? Unlike F84/F114 this requires **map-to-world topology and source-qualified route constraints**; unlike F123 this is a **spatial network** rather than an abstract procedural decision graph. It is not an immutable visual-learning type, spatial IQ, real-world safety qualification or timed navigation exam. Use CEFR- and age-appropriate language; never invent advanced geometry or transport expertise as a prerequisite.

## Source-authorized wayfinding and evidence contract

For every case resolve the **real versioned** CEFR descriptor/can-do, level blueprint/prerequisites, target language and approved varieties, recipient/purpose, fictional source-authored map and effective version, authoritative origin/destination and route network, symbol/legend definitions, orientation reference, permitted routes and restrictions, independently validated target-language readings, source-valid instructions/clarifications, rubric/evaluator, first-unaided threshold, protected listening/reading/writing/speaking/free-production evidence, accommodation policy, due reviews, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer and exit gate. Record source-qualified node/path/landmark/entrance IDs; directed versus bidirectional edges; crossing and connection semantics; north arrow versus viewport rotation; distance units and scale when actually supplied; stairs, lifts, closures and other explicit access constraints; route availability and temporal validity. A path that merely crosses another line on a drawing is not connected unless the authoritative source says so. An icon's appearance is not an authoritative legend. Never infer a route, accessibility status, opening hours or travel time from a stylized map alone.

Freeze a **wayfinding ledger**: descriptor/blueprint/task/source/map/rubric/evaluator hashes, source authority/effective version, target language/variety, authoritative graph and legend, origin/destination and requested route constraints, map orientation, scale/unit provenance, source-valid route alternatives and uncertainty; original map and **actually rendered** crop/rotation/zoom/translation/OCR, screen-reader linearization, audio/tactile or other validated equivalent; learner's first authored route explanation/question and source-qualified bindings; tutor arrows, highlights, example routes, hints, feedback and translations with actual render timestamps; retries and channel reliability; feasible, assigned and **actually delivered** instructional choice; delayed/unseen overlap; event identity and missingness. Use fictional campuses, parks, libraries or museums, never live personal location, credentials, emergency evacuation or real-world medical/safety-critical routing.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Orientation versus viewport rotation.** A fictional museum map has a validated north arrow and entrance at node E, information desk at node I, and café at node C. The authoritative map says E → north corridor → I; the café lies east of I. Asked “How do I get from the entrance to the information desk?”, learner A says “go right” after treating screen-up as north on a rotated phone; learner B says “follow the north corridor” and identifies the source orientation. Counterfactual: rotate only the **rendered viewport** 90 degrees while preserving the source graph and north arrow; the source-relative route does not change. In a separate authoritative source update, change the entrance and corridor graph: the correct instruction may change. Accept egocentric “left/right” only when the speaker's starting facing direction is explicitly defined; otherwise credit source-valid cardinal/landmark language or a clarification. Do not infer a visual deficit from a corrupted orientation cue.

**B — Crossing lines are not necessarily connected paths.** A fictional park map has two walkways crossing visually at X, but the authoritative network specifies an overpass without a junction. To go from entrance A to garden G, the valid path uses A → bridge B → garden G. Learner A says “turn at X” because two strokes cross; learner B follows the connected path via B. Counterfactual: an independently source-validated revision adds a real junction at X; the shorter X route becomes eligible. Merely changing line thickness or zoom cannot create connectivity. If the source does not specify whether a crossing connects, accept a targeted question rather than confident invention. Route directions must bind to actual source-qualified path IDs, not just a fluent sentence.

**C — Legend, access restrictions and alternatives.** A fictional campus map marks stairs with symbol S, lift with symbol L, and a temporarily closed passage with symbol C; an explicit source legend defines each. A learner asked for a step-free route from the entrance to Room 2 follows S and produces a fluent but constraint-violating answer; another uses the validated L route. Counterfactual: the source legend swaps two symbols while preserving their drawn locations, or the effective closure version changes: the expected route must follow the **authoritative** meaning/version, not the icon's familiar appearance. A step-free route is not automatically wheelchair accessible or safe without independent source validation of relevant requirements. If the map offers no source-supported compliant route, the valid answer is bounded uncertainty/clarification, not a fabricated detour.

**D — Distance, scale and warranted precision.** A fictional town-center map provides a validated scale of 1 cm = 100 m and a labeled 300 m walking path; another stylized inset has **no scale**. Learner A estimates “two minutes” from visual line length on the inset and reports it as fact; learner B says “The labeled route is 300 meters; I cannot tell the walking time from this map.” Counterfactual: change the source-authoritative route length to 450 m and update the label; the valid distance statement changes. Rotating or enlarging the same map does not change actual route length. Do not penalize learners for lacking speed calculations outside the CEFR task; only test source-supported communicative comparisons, approximation and uncertainty.

**E — Rendering, translation, version and assistance chronology.** A fictional library-floor map v1 routes visitors through Corridor A; v2 closes A and marks Corridor B as the current route. A learner's answer to v1 is scored against v1, not retroactively against v2. On a phone, the legend is cropped; OCR reads “L” as “S”; a translated sign reverses “do not enter”; and an accessible linear route description omits the closure. Preserve the source artifact **and actual delivered rendering**, quarantine affected route-attribution claims as RENDER_CROP, OCR_LEGEND_CONFLICT, TRANSLATED_DIRECTION_CONFLICT or unvalidated accessibility, and retain any independently valid partial language evidence. The learner submits at 10:00; a tutor route highlight queued at 09:59 but **rendered at 10:01** does not contaminate the first attempt, while a later copied route is assisted. Deduplicate offline replays by immutable session-qualified event ID; identical local corridor labels in v1/v2 are not globally identical. Accessible text/audio directions must be independently validated for orientation, node sequence, restrictions and version, not assumed equivalent because they are fluent.

**F — Reversible teaching, confounds and genuinely unseen transfer.** Fictional learner A follows highlighted paths immediately but fails delayed first-unaided landmark instructions. After **actually delivered** LEGEND_AND_LANDMARK_CHECK on comparable tasks, A later independently explains a route on a new source-authored park map with a different topology, start orientation, landmarks, legend and communicative recipient. Learner B instead improves after CONNECTIVITY_WALKTHROUGH_WITH_FADING. Compare baseline proficiency, CEFR objective, target language/variety, map complexity (nodes, crossings, alternatives, scale, symbol density), route constraints, topic familiarity, accessible route, help exposure, review interval and follow-up opportunity. If A always sees two-path maps and B dense transit networks, mark METHOD_MAP_COMPLEXITY_CONFOUNDED and infer **no method winner**. Recoloring or mirroring the same network, replacing landmark names while preserving the answer path, or repeating the same rehearsed route is not unseen transfer. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-ups; missing is neither success nor failure. With repeated comparable later outcomes, A and B may receive different **actually delivered** next instruction; contradictory evidence lowers confidence and permits reversal. Preserve due reviews, productive/receptive balance, alternative exploration and nonexclusive interest-topic rotation.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real CEFR and map-source linkage.** A–F resolve real versioned descriptor/blueprint/prerequisites, source-authorized map/network/legend/effective version, target language/variety, rubric/evaluator, first-unaided threshold, accommodations, protected skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. No invented geometry or navigation prerequisite.
2. **Origin, orientation and route topology.** A/B bind origin, destination, north/reference frame, connected path IDs and crossing semantics. Viewport rotation or cosmetic drawing changes cannot alter source-relative routes; authoritative graph edits can.
3. **Legend, restrictions and route feasibility.** B/C bind source-validated symbols, closures, directionality and explicit step-free or other constraints. A fluent but restricted or disconnected route is not correct; unverified accessibility is **unknown**, never certified.
4. **Scale, uncertainty and version validity.** C/D/E preserve effective source versions, distance units, supported approximation and source-valid clarification. No inferred travel time from an unscaled sketch, retroactive penalty after a map update, or fabricated route through an unknown connection.
5. **Actual first-unaided provenance and replay.** E records source-qualified route/landmark bindings, first authored answer, retries and help **actually rendered before** the attempt. Queued, failed or post-answer highlights are not retroactive help; copied routes remain assisted; replay cannot double count.
6. **Multilingual, accessible and delivered-stimulus validity.** A/C/E independently validate translated directional/negative signs, legend, rotation, crop, OCR and accessible route descriptions. Conflicting or inaccessible stimuli quarantine affected claims; valid equivalent communicative evidence remains, without falsely asserting unaided visual-map decoding.
7. **Delayed retention and genuinely unseen route communication.** A–F require meaningful time/intervening learning, new source-authored networks with noncosmetic topology, constraints, legends, origin/recipient or distractor changes and no answer-bearing help. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators.
8. **Reversible method choice and soft interests.** F records feasible/assigned/**actually delivered** methods, baseline/task comparability, confounds, uncertainty, exploration, contradictory evidence, protected listening/reading/writing/speaking/free production, due reviews and exit gates. Interests influence example likelihood but never gate objectives or exclude adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics; no fixed learner-style label.
9. **Exact-head deterministic reproducibility and bounded claims.** A–F emit deterministic seed/clock, named engine HEAD and runnable command, descriptor/source/network/legend/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, authoritative and actually rendered accessible artifacts, first/assisted answers, help/event ledger, counterfactuals, delayed/unseen denominators and **PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion**. Docs-only CI is not an executable map evaluator, a reproducible product defect or evidence of improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and reproducible command; real versioned CEFR-linked tasks; independently validated multilingual source maps, legends, topology and accessible equivalents; source-effective and actually delivered stimuli; first-unaided evidence; meaningful delayed independent retrieval and genuinely unseen route-to-communication transfer; comparable or explicitly confounded instructional alternatives; protected skill/review/exit obligations; eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; and orientation/crop/OCR/translation/topology/legend/closure/scale/version/replay counterfactuals with per-assertion verdicts. Missing source validation, unreliable display, unavailable accommodation, ambiguous connection or absent follow-up makes the affected claim **UNSUPPORTED/unknown** while preserving independently established partial language evidence.

Any stronger claim that adaptive wayfinding instruction **improves durable independent communication** requires a separately reviewed, privacy-appropriate, multilingual, multivariety and accessibility-validated real-learner longitudinal study with credible comparison, attrition and subgroup-harm analysis and reproducible delayed outcomes. **A strong tutor teaches a learner to communicate a route supported by the actual map, not simply to repeat a highlighted path.**

**Boundary:** curricular acceptance documentation only. No Pre-A1 edits, A1–C2 release, engine, Auth, cloud, infrastructure, production, Supabase, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge changes. Integration/watchdog alone aggregates owner actions.
