# F219 — Metacognitive confidence, self-explanation, calibration and independent transfer

**Status:** design-only synthetic *cross-cutting* longitudinal curriculum acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until an independently reviewed executable exact-head harness tests the histories and actual instructional decisions. Not a shipped confidence estimator, psychological diagnosis, validated learner survey, new CEFR can-do, changed mastery threshold, or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F213 covers learner agency and deferral; F214 covers uncertainty-aware diagnostic selection; F210 covers hint dose and feedback timing; F211 covers difficulty calibration; F218 covers cross-objective error attribution. **Distinct question in F219:** can a tutor use *optional, properly timed judgments of knowing and learner-generated explanations* as fallible signals to choose better instruction, while separating self-reported confidence from independently demonstrated language ability, avoiding pressure to disclose confidence, and checking later unaided retrieval and unseen transfer? A learner's feeling of fluency is not the same as mastery, and an articulate explanation after a worked example may be entirely tutor-assisted.

## Fixed objective, measurement and provenance boundaries

Bind every synthetic history to an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, task-specific reviewed meaning/function rubric, target language and accepted varieties, prerequisites, skill and accessible channel, context family, difficulty, exposure/assistance record, due-review and skill coverage debt, and **unchanged independent mastery/exit standards**. The illustrative utterances are not released lessons. Do not invent a generic “confidence mastery” criterion, penalize someone for declining a self-rating, infer anxiety, intelligence or learning style from hesitation, or treat an explanation about a grammatical rule as evidence of independently using it in communication.

A confidence judgment, if voluntarily supplied, needs a clear **referent** (the particular task or independently performing that objective later), bounded scale with documented anchors, timing **before versus after** feedback/model/answer exposure, language and accessibility of the question, whether the learner saw their answer, and explicit missing/declined status. A post-feedback rating is not interchangeable with a pre-feedback prediction. Calibration is a comparison between predictions and **eligible observed independent outcomes**, over enough comparable opportunities to be informative; never convert an unobserved or assisted outcome into a zero or a success. No fixed universal numerical confidence threshold or population-calibration claim is assumed.

Freeze a pre-decision ledger with pseudonymous learner, stable learner/session/run/task/event IDs, trusted event chronology and learning-day provenance, objective/rubric/task/policy/channel versions, actual prompt and answer visibility, first unaided response and reviewed semantic quality, optional pre-feedback confidence and its referent, any self-explanation **before or after** answer-bearing exposure, hints/models/recasts/transcripts, retries, actual instructional option offered versus rendered, later distinct-session delayed unaided retrieval, unseen transfer, uncertainty, comparable opportunity counts and missingness. Record due/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators and skill/context coverage. Deduplicate event replays; quarantine untrusted chronology. Latency is weak contextual telemetry only when actually available and never a confidence or mastery proxy.

**Decision sequence:** fixed objective → valid accessible task actually delivered → first unaided performance and optional pre-feedback confidence → reviewed error/meaning and explanation provenance → candidate mismatch or gap with competing hypotheses → eligible instructional options and uncertainty → option actually rendered → later delayed independent retrieval and unfamiliar transfer → updated method hypothesis and next actually delivered choice. Self-explanation may be a useful *teaching activity*; a coached, corrected or copied explanation is not clean independent production. A learner may opt out of self-ratings or metacognitive prompts and still receive the balanced accessible curriculum and full mastery opportunities.

**Oracle labels:** OBJECTIVE_FIXED, RUBRIC_REVIEWED, CONFIDENCE_OPTIONAL, CONFIDENCE_REFERENT_FIXED, PREFEEDBACK_JUDGMENT, POSTFEEDBACK_JUDGMENT, JUDGMENT_DECLINED, SELF_REPORT_NOT_MASTERY, FIRST_UNAIDED, EXPLANATION_UNAIDED, EXPLANATION_MODEL_EXPOSED, EXPLANATION_NOT_LANGUAGE_TRANSFER, CALIBRATION_OPPORTUNITY_ELIGIBLE, CALIBRATION_INSUFFICIENT, OVERCONFIDENCE_HYPOTHESIS, UNDERCONFIDENCE_HYPOTHESIS, CONTRADICTORY_EVIDENCE, ACCESSIBLE_FALLBACK, LATENCY_NOT_CONFIDENCE, CLEAN_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_TRANSFER, SKILL_EVIDENCE_SEPARATE, INTEREST_METHOD_SEPARATE, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, HYPOTHESIS_REVISED, BALANCED_FALLBACK, NOT_COMPARABLE and UNSUPPORTED. Labels apply to observations, not permanent identities.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories and matched controls

**A — A confident wrong first attempt must not become either mastery or a fixed learner label.** In a valid independent writing task for an existing past-event can-do, the learner voluntarily predicts high confidence *before feedback* and writes “Yesterday I go to the market.” The meaning-reviewed first attempt misses a required past-time form. The tutor later renders a brief correction; the learner repeats “Yesterday I went to the market” with high confidence. **Expected:** retain the high pre-feedback judgment alongside the first-attempt error; classify the corrected repeat as assisted, not mastery. “Possible overconfidence on this task” is a tentative calibration observation, not a stable personality or language-style label. Offer an eligible explanation, retrieval or contrastive option and later test a genuinely new past event unaided. Matched control: the first unaided response is already valid at the same difficulty, with the same high pre-feedback rating; the policy cannot produce the same error-based remediation merely because confidence was high. A single rating is insufficient for a learner-wide calibration claim.

**B — Low confidence with strong independent performance should not force easier content.** Across two valid, comparable independent information-request tasks, a learner voluntarily predicts low success before each attempt yet produces meaning-appropriate, independently reviewed requests. A later distinct-session unseen request is also correct without assistance. Unsafe policy treats low confidence as low CEFR competence, locks challenging activities and repeatedly supplies models. **Expected:** preserve strong independent evidence and tentative *underconfidence for these opportunities* separately; do not downgrade mastery or withhold required productive practice. The tutor may offer an optional, nonjudgmental reflection or appropriately challenging independent task while maintaining normal CEFR and review standards. Matched control: replace the delayed unaided success with an actual independent meaning-critical failure; uncertainty and next instructional choice must be revisable. Neither case warrants an inferred clinical state.

**C — A polished self-explanation after a model is assisted learning activity, not transfer.** Session 1: a learner gives an incomplete unaided explanation of why “she doesn't” is used. The tutor renders a worked rule, an answer-bearing example and a leading “tell me why” prompt; the learner reproduces a polished rule explanation. Session 2 on the same day repeats the same explanation. Session 3 after a meaningful interval independently writes a new third-person sentence in an unfamiliar context, with no displayed rule or model. **Expected:** mark the session-1/2 explanation as EXPLANATION_MODEL_EXPOSED, preserve the original first attempt, and judge session 3 under its fixed independent production rubric. Do not award communicative mastery from the verbal rule or same-day echo. Matched control: session 3 shows the rule card before responding; the superficially identical correct sentence is now assisted and cannot supply clean delayed transfer. A fluent explanation of grammar may coexist with a later independent production error.

**D — Feedback timing changes what a confidence number means.** Two otherwise matched histories contain the same first unaided answer and later independent outcome. In one, the learner reports confidence before seeing the tutor's answer; in the other, a “How sure are you?” widget appears only *after* the correct answer, translation and congratulatory feedback have rendered. Unsafe policy pools the two ratings into one calibration series and attributes a later success to “confidence training.” **Expected:** the post-feedback judgment is retained as a different observation, not a pre-attempt prediction, and is excluded from the same calibration denominator. Preserve exact offered-versus-rendered feedback timestamps and answer visibility. Matched control: feedback was queued but never rendered before the rating; if trusted delivery chronology confirms no exposure, the rating can be considered pre-feedback. Missing render order is UNKNOWN, not presumed clean. No synthetic history proves the causal value of a confidence prompt.

**E — Optional reflection, accessibility and interests cannot change evidence standards.** Two matched learners have the same reviewed independent first attempts and later transfer; one declines all confidence questions and uses slow assistive input, while the other submits ratings using a fast interface. The unsafe tutor marks missing ratings as poor motivation, interprets slow typing as low certainty, and only offers the favorite gaming topic. **Expected:** JUDGMENT_DECLINED is neither failure nor negative confidence; LATENCY_NOT_CONFIDENCE and ACCESSIBLE_FALLBACK preserve equivalent instructional opportunities and mastery criteria. An accessible short reflection or no-reflection path is allowed without punitive extra turns. Matched interest-only control swaps gaming for cooking: only topic probabilities may change, while CEFR objective, difficulty, confidence evidence eligibility, teaching-method estimate, required listening/reading/writing/speaking coverage and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar context rotation remain auditable. Text reflection cannot fabricate oral/listening evidence.

**F — Conflicting calibration and transfer evidence must change delivered instruction, not merely a dashboard.** A learner supplies optional pre-feedback confidence on four distinct, valid, comparable opportunities, with clean delayed unseen outcomes: (1) high confidence + independent failure, (2) low confidence + independent success, (3) high confidence + independent success, (4) low confidence + independent failure. A fifth event is an assisted correct repeat uploaded twice; a sixth alleged pre-feedback judgment lacks trusted answer-visibility chronology. The upload order is 4, 2, duplicate 5, 1, 3, 6. Two plausible next teaching options are a brief independent prediction-and-check reflection and a neutral retrieval-first exercise without confidence prompts. **Expected:** deduplicate event 5, quarantine event 6, keep all four contradictory eligible pairs, avoid a permanent over/underconfidence label, and use calibrated uncertainty and later independent retention/transfer to select or deliberately explore an eligible option **actually rendered**. Record due/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators, comparable opportunity and modality/context coverage, and any declined prompts. Matched control changes later independent outcomes under matched task, difficulty, feedback, interval and exposure while holding self-reports fixed; the method hypothesis and delivered choice must be capable of changing, or justify a documented balanced fallback. If comparability or exposure cannot be established, label NOT_COMPARABLE and do not assert one metacognitive intervention works better.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed CEFR and evidence:** A–F bind existing versioned can-dos, task-specific meaning rubrics, prerequisites, supported modalities and unchanged mastery/exit standards. No new can-do, Pre-A1 modification, A1–C2 release, or B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval resolution.
2. **Optional, timed and interpretable judgments:** Every rating records a defined referent, anchors, voluntary/declined status, prompt language/accessibility, pre/post-feedback timing and actual answer visibility. A missing or declined rating is not failure, low confidence or low motivation.
3. **Confidence does not equal performance:** A and B preserve first unaided outcomes independently of self-report. One mismatch cannot create a permanent learner identity, CEFR downgrade, graduation credit or certainty about metacognition.
4. **Self-explanation provenance:** C records the first explanation, actual rendered model/rule exposure, leading prompts, same-day repetition and later distinct-session independent production. Coached rule recall cannot silently certify communicative use or another skill.
5. **Calibration eligibility and denominators:** D separates pre-feedback predictions from post-answer ratings, preserves offered/rendered chronology, and reports eligible observed independent outcomes versus missing/assisted/untrusted cases. Do not compute or claim calibration from incompatible denominators or insufficient observations.
6. **Access, fairness and no psychologizing:** E preserves equivalent accessible opportunities for people declining reflection or using assistive technology; device/network latency does not become confidence, intelligence, anxiety or competence evidence. Supported skill-family evidence remains separate.
7. **Interest-method separation:** Matched E changes only soft topic weights, not objective, rubric, difficulty, calibration denominator, method confidence, mastery criteria or balanced context/skill coverage; adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts remain eligible.
8. **Longitudinal reversibility and actual delivery:** F supplies stable event IDs, trusted chronology, deduplication, unknown exposure quarantine, contradictory clean delayed/transfer outcomes, uncertainty, alternatives, coverage debt and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing counts. Matched opposite outcomes must permit a revised hypothesis or justified balanced exploration with the **next option actually rendered**.
9. **Executable proof and efficacy boundary:** Publish machine-readable A–F histories and matched controls, deterministic seed/clock, exact objective/rubric/task/policy/channel versions, independently reviewed language/measurement oracles, pre/post-feedback exposure, first-attempt, confidence and explanation provenance, per-assertion PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED, and an independently reviewed executable exact-head harness. Synthetic PASS proves invariants only. Any claim that confidence reflection or self-explanation improves durable human learning requires a separately reviewed longitudinal pilot against a balanced accessible baseline, voluntary/missingness analyses, comparable opportunities, delayed unaided retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer, subgroup fairness and no hidden loss in other required skills. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** F219 makes **metacognitive self-report and coached explanation versus independently demonstrated, durable language ability** falsifiable. A strong tutor may invite reflection and revise its teaching without confusing feelings of knowing, post-answer confidence or copied explanations with actual independent transfer. **UNSUPPORTED** until independently executed and reviewed; no real-world efficacy claim is made.
