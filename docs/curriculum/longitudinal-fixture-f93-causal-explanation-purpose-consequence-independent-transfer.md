**Contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F33 requires genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish selected from actually rendered instruction and tutor-origin cues; F47/F48 protect noncompensatory CEFR evidence; F49 accessibility; F50 soft-interest rotation; F53 variety/register; F54 repair; F72 goal/constraint tracking; F73 pragmatic inference; F75 argumentation; F81 negation scope; F82 event order; F83 conditional commitments; F86 evidence/uncertainty; F90 presuppositions; F92 reported-speech attribution. **Distinct construct:** independently identifying and communicating whether a source presents an event as a **cause**, an intended **purpose**, a **consequence**, a **mere sequence/coincidence**, or an **unverified causal claim**, and choosing language that preserves that relationship for a real recipient decision. Temporal ordering alone does not prove causation; a plausible explanation cannot override the source. This fixture tests linguistic comprehension and faithful communication, not advanced domain-specific causal analysis. Explicitly distinguish “X happened after Y,” “X happened because of Y,” “X was done in order to Y,” and “X led to Y” when the source warrants different relations. Do not require a learner to infer an unreported cause.

Ambiguous “since” may be temporal or causal, depending on context. Accessible transcripts must preserve hedges, contrast, negation and speaker identity. ## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Sequence promoted to cause.** The source-authored notice says “The lights went out after the alarm sounded. The cause is still unknown.” Learner A tells a colleague “The alarm caused the outage”; learner B independently reports the sequence and unknown cause. A contrasting verified maintenance notice explicitly states “A damaged cable caused the outage”; B now reports the stated cause instead of reflexively hedging. In an unseen context the order of two events reverses, but no cause is given; the learner must not infer a new cause from order.
- **B — Purpose confused with outcome.** A team message says “We opened the window to cool the room, but the temperature did not fall.” Learner A reports “Opening the window cooled the room”; learner B preserves the intended purpose and the failed result. A new source says “The fan cooled the room, although nobody turned it on for that purpose”; the learner separates consequence from intention. Score communicative fidelity, not physics expertise.
- **C — Conditional prediction misreported as established cause.** A schedule reads “If the bridge closes, the bus may be delayed. The bridge is currently open.” Learner A announces “The bridge closure delayed the bus”; learner B reports the conditional risk and current status. Later a timestamped verified update confirms closure and delay but does not establish that closure caused the delay; the learner updates both facts without automatically asserting causation. A source explicitly confirming the causal link may license stronger wording.
- **D — Negated reason, source conflict and accessible repair.** A speaker says “We did not cancel because of the rain; the venue lost power.” A caption omits “not,” producing an opposite causal claim. Learner A repeats the caption as verified; learner B flags conflicting source evidence and asks for an accessible confirmed transcript before notifying guests. After a timestamped source-authorized correction, B preserves the correction and does not retroactively mark the earlier inaccessible turn as learner failure. Target-language scope and punctuation may affect readings; rubric versions must capture alternatives.
- **E — Coached immediate winner reverses at delayed independent follow-up.** In comparable early sessions, RELATION_CONTRAST_PAIRS produces polished immediate “because/so” sentences after visible models; EVENT_RELATION_MAPS produces slower, less fluent first unaided distinctions. Across separate later sessions with new speakers, ambiguous temporal markers, purpose/result reversals and actionable recipient decisions, the mapping condition yields better first-attempt independent source fidelity and fewer invented causes. Match or disclose baseline, source complexity, topic/domain familiarity, practice dose, feedback, variety, modality and accessibility. Downweight coached immediate correctness, sample feasible alternatives and log the **actually rendered** next method; never classify the learner as a permanent “visual/causal” type.
- **F — Interest overfit, missing follow-up and out-of-order telemetry.** A learner faithfully explains causes in familiar game narratives but turns a neutral transit sequence and unfamiliar workplace announcement into unsupported causes. A listening source lacks accessible captions; a delayed probe is eligible and offered but never rendered. Two genuine sessions reuse a local offline event ID; an answer-bearing tutor relation diagram rendered before a learner reply is received in telemetry afterward; another event conflicts on frozen source/rubric hashes. Deduplicate only genuine session-qualified replays, quarantine unresolved versions and reconstruct trusted render chronology. Assistance, first-independent verdict, review debt, context rotation and reversible next-method rationale remain invariant under receipt-order permutations. Written success cannot certify required listening or speaking.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-valid CEFR applicability.** A–F require a real versioned can-do, blueprint/prerequisites, language/variety, source-authored task and provenance, recipient goal, validated rubric/evaluator, assistance policy, threshold, required skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Missing mapping or unsupported language-specific distinction yields UNSUPPORTED/NOT_APPLICABLE, never invented proficiency.
2. **Cause versus sequence and evidence.** A/C reject promoting chronology, co-occurrence or a conditional possibility into established causation. Preserve source-confirmed causal relations when they are actually asserted; uncertainty must be calibrated, not indiscriminate.
3. **Purpose versus actual consequence.** B preserves intention, result and their possible mismatch. A grammatical “so” or “because” sentence fails if it changes an unrealized purpose into an achieved outcome or reverses who acted and what followed.
4. **Direction, negation, scope and source updates.** C/D preserve relation direction, negated reasons, conditions, speaker/source attribution and timestamped correction state. Conflicting captions trigger qualification or clarification, not confident false reporting; later verified updates do not rewrite earlier evidence.
5. **First independent move and actually rendered cues.** D/F score the first learner-authored explanation or clarification against trusted render chronology. A completed causal sentence, highlighted relation or answer-bearing map rendered beforehand makes that move assisted; selected-but-never-rendered guidance does not. Distinguish source-authored task stimulus from interlocutor coaching.
6. **Delayed genuinely unseen independent transfer.** A/B/E/F require separate real sessions, novel source structures, causal/purpose/result contrasts, new contexts and recipient decisions, first unaided responses and observable outcomes. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; missing follow-up, copied models or assisted correction never prove durable mastery.
7. **Comparable methods, accessibility and soft interests.** D/E/F match or disclose task difficulty, source certainty, domain knowledge, topic familiarity, feedback, dose, variety/register, modality and accessibility. Preserve shared CEFR objectives, noncompensatory receptive/productive skill evidence, due reviews and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar context rotation. Domain expertise must not masquerade as language mastery.
8. **Reversible adaptation and receipt-order invariance.** E/F log feasible alternatives, assigned/selected/**actually rendered** instruction, later independent outcomes, uncertainty, confounds, exploration and next-choice rationale. Sparse/conflicted evidence falls back to BALANCED_DEFAULT. Session-qualified deduplication, frozen versions and trusted render chronology yield identical assistance labels, relation verdicts, review debt and next method across receipt permutations; quarantine unresolved conflicts.
9. **Assertion-level audit without efficacy inflation.** A–F emit exact source/task/rubric/evaluator IDs, frozen events and relation types/directions, first/final learner-authored explanations, rendered cues, recipient outcomes, delayed/unseen denominators, confounds, replay permutations and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion. Documentation CI is not executable evaluator PASS, CEFR award or proof of improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, deterministic seed/clock, real source-resolved CEFR-linked tasks, target-language/variety-validated relation rubrics, frozen causal/purpose/consequence/sequence/condition fields, trustworthy cue render chronology, first unaided explanation or clarification, observable recipient outcome, completed delayed genuinely unseen transfer with denominators, comparable method/topic/modality/access exposure, protected skills/reviews, receipt-order permutations, reversible next-method traces and assertion-level verdicts. Missing source validity, reliable relation interpretation, accessible channel, completed independent follow-up or trusted chronology remains UNSUPPORTED/unknown.

Any claim that adaptation **improves durable independent causal communication** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, delayed unseen tasks, multilingual/variety and accessibility validation, human adjudication, privacy/subgroup-harm review and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor teaches learners to explain what the source actually supports—without mistaking “after,” “for,” “if,” and “because” for the same relationship.**
