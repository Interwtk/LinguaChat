# F172 — Lexical stress, word-class contrasts and independent communicative transfer

**Status:** design-only synthetic longitudinal curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable exact-head assertions bind independently reviewed target-language/variety-specific source-world and acoustic oracles to existing versioned CEFR communicative can-dos, actually rendered accessible audio, and later first-unaided unseen recipient-facing transfer. This is **not** a shipped lexical-stress recognizer, a speech-scoring feature, a universal noun/verb pronunciation rule, a new CEFR exit gate, proof of outcome-responsive teaching or evidence of improved human retention. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F89 addresses *discourse contrastive focus*, F110 paralinguistic cue boundaries, F111/F112 ASR/TTS reliability, F117 accent/variety and register, F131 segmental phonemic contrasts, F170 connected-speech boundaries/reductions, and F171 utterance-level intonation/question force. **Distinct construct:** can the learner use a **reviewed, actually audible word-level stress pattern**, together with legitimate lexical and syntactic context, to identify the intended word or word class and communicate an appropriate meaning or clarification to an unfamiliar recipient? In English, selected noun/verb pairs such as *REcord/reCORD*, *PREsent/preSENT*, *OBject/obJECT* and *PERmit/perMIT* can illustrate this **only for validated words, pronunciations and varieties**. Stress can co-occur with vowel quality, duration and reduction; do not falsely attribute all acoustic differences to pitch or stress alone. The same pattern is not universal across words, languages or accents. A printed uppercase syllable, dictionary transcription or grammatically disambiguating text-only answer is not independent acoustic perception.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Same printed word, independently audible contrast.** In a library-club activity, a reviewer validates two separate recordings of the isolated word *record*: a noun realization and a verb realization in the selected English variety. The learner hears each **without text, stress marks or prior model** and independently identifies the intended meaning, then responds to a new short audio instruction with the correct cataloging or recording action. The source oracle explicitly records stress and any co-occurring vowel differences. If either delivered recording lacks a reliable contrast, the item is SIGNAL_UNUSABLE; guessing from the task's alternating sequence is not audio discrimination.

**B — Grammar can rescue communication but cannot prove acoustic skill.** The printed instructions **"Please record the interview"** and **"The record is in the folder"** strongly distinguish verb and noun by syntax. A learner who selects the correct meaning from these written sentences may show reading/grammar competence, **not** independent listening discrimination of lexical stress. A reviewed audio-only contrast followed by a different recipient-facing spoken request is needed to support the listening claim. Correct meaning in a real dialogue can still count toward its **existing communicative can-do** when the rubric allows contextual comprehension; do not conflate that with an isolated stress subscore.

**C — Variety, co-occurring vowel changes and bad synthesis.** A reviewer validates the local realizations of *present* and *permit* and documents where vowel quality, reduction and timing accompany prominence. A TTS voice renders one candidate with effectively identical stress, while an ASR transcript erases pronunciation distinctions and a subtitle adds uppercase emphasis. Quarantine TTS_STRESS_DRIFT, ASR_STRESS_ERASURE and CAPTION_STRESS_LEAKAGE. Accept **"Do you mean the gift or that someone will present?"** if the actually heard signal and context cannot decide. Never penalize a legitimate regional pronunciation because it differs from a single reference voice.

**D — Production and a real recipient information gap.** The learner must ask a new club volunteer about a *permit* (noun) or request that a volunteer *permit* an action (verb). Record the learner's first unaided utterance and whether a **blinded, unfamiliar recipient** understands the intended action, using an independently reviewed rubric and feasible speaking modality. A listener who succeeds solely because the sentence frame makes the word class obvious supplies **communicative success**, but not isolated proof of stress production. ASR's favorable transcript alone cannot establish intelligibility; do not impose native-like pronunciation or speaking as an unavailable universal requirement.

**E — First authorship, hint timing and replay.** At 09:02 the learner hears a validated unseen *object* contrast and independently asks **"Is it the thing, or do you mean to object to the plan?"**; preserve FIRST_UNAIDED_LISTENING and TARGETED_LEXICAL_CLARIFICATION. At 09:03 a stress-marked written model and replay actually render; at 09:04 the learner repeats the model. Mark 09:04 ASSISTED, not an upgrade of the 09:02 answer. A help option merely offered but never rendered cannot contaminate 09:02. Deduplicate a retried 09:04 event by stable session-qualified ID. A screen-reader text route is legitimate accessibility evidence, but its construct label must remain accurate.

**F — Longitudinal reversible choice and new speaker.** Learner A quickly copies written stress labels yet fails delayed first-unaided responses to unseen voices. Matched **actually rendered** WORD_CLASS_MEANING_TRIANGULATION sessions are followed by improved later independent recipient responses in neutral and unfamiliar topics; cautiously increase that option's probability while continuing acoustic alternatives. Learner B initially benefits from REVIEWED_AUDIO_CONTRAST_PAIRS, then fails a new variety because a memorized pitch rule overgeneralizes; reduce confidence and sample context-plus-acoustics again. If one method used a familiar clean voice and the other an unfamiliar noisy voice, or one showed a written stress mark, **no causal method winner** follows. Replaying a trained word or merely swapping a game topic is not unseen auditory transfer. Missing follow-up is missing, not a zero or a gain.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Versioned CEFR and reviewed lexical contrast.** A–F require a real relevant can-do, valid target-language/variety lexemes, independent acoustic/source oracle, accessible actual delivery, first-unaided rubric, due review, delayed/unseen probe and unchanged exit gates; otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED.
2. **Audible lexical identification.** A requires a demonstrably delivered contrast with no answer-bearing text and an independently authored meaning/recipient action; source labels or ordered-pair guessing cannot substitute for listening.
3. **Syntax is not acoustic evidence.** B distinguishes legitimate contextual communicative success from isolated lexical-stress perception; grammar-only text cannot earn FIRST_UNAIDED_LISTENING.
4. **Variation, synthesis and uncertainty.** C audits co-occurring vowel/timing cues, legitimate varieties, TTS/ASR/caption drift and unusable signals; accepts targeted clarification instead of inventing an acoustic contrast or a universal stress rule.
5. **Independent speaking without accent policing.** D requires feasible real first-unaided production and independent recipient understanding, separates grammar-cued success from stress-specific evidence and rejects ASR-only/native-accent gates.
6. **Actual delivery, help chronology and event identity.** E preserves the earliest learner-authored move, marks only genuinely prior rendered assistance as contaminating, records replay/model exposure and deduplicates retries; accessibility may measure a different construct.
7. **Matched reversible instructional adaptation.** F compares only actually rendered feasible methods with matched lexeme, objective, variety, speaker, signal, syntax, topic, help, prior exposure, spacing and follow-up. Sparse/confounded/contradictory evidence yields no winner; regression reduces confidence and restores exploration.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer, balanced skills and soft interests.** Require a later eligible distinct session with a genuinely new reviewed talker/lexeme or materially new acoustic and communicative realization, first-unaided recipient meaning/clarification, and explicit missingness. Same clip, labeled imitation, text-only topic swap or immediate assisted repair is not delayed unseen listening mastery. Keep shared CEFR standards, required modalities and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation.
9. **Honest efficacy and implementation boundary.** Documentation CI does not demonstrate executable acoustic QA, shipped adaptive teaching or improved retention. Require exact-head executable assertions against delivered assets, observed longitudinal outcome-driven instructional choices and independently reviewed later unaided transfer against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, level enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic design/probe histories, not observed learner outcomes or executable QA. Keep lexical/variety validation, actual acoustic delivery, first-authorship provenance, reversible instructional hypotheses and UNSUPPORTED gates until independent implementation and longitudinal evidence exist.
