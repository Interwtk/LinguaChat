# F78 — Common-ground verification, false consensus and independent conversational alignment

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a shipped common-ground tracker, conversation-understanding evaluator, CEFR certification, reproduced production defect or evidence of human learning gains. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 protects genuinely unseen transfer; F37 records delivered instruction; F38 separates tutor-origin content from learner evidence; F47/F48 preserve source-defined noncompensatory mastery; F49 protects accessibility equivalence; F50 protects topic breadth; F53 preserves legitimate pragmatic/register varieties; F54 addresses breakdown repair; F58 protects initiative; F66 varies interlocutors; F71 addresses referent/discourse cohesion; F72 tracks goal/constraints; F73 addresses implicit intent; F74 guards information fidelity; F76 guards information-seeking; F77 addresses floor management. **F78 isolates conversational common ground:** detecting whether two interlocutors actually share the same understanding of a source-required proposition, referent or plan, and independently establishing, testing or repairing that shared understanding when warranted. A grammatically coherent, polite, fast or superficially agreeable exchange may conceal materially different interpretations. Conversely, explicit confirmation is not mandatory in every exchange: use the source-defined communicative task and context, not an invented universal confirmation quota.

## Controlled setup

Resolve a **real, versioned CEFR-linked descriptor/can-do**, blueprint, prerequisites, language and legitimate varieties, task and communicative goal, interlocutor roles and relationship, source-required shared information, stakes of misunderstanding, permitted implicit and explicit confirmation practices, supported channel, evaluator/rubric version, assistance limits, independent threshold, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer, due reviews and level exit gate. Freeze interlocutor private beliefs and task truth **before** instruction comparisons; the learner may not be given omniscient access to those beliefs. Annotate each source-required proposition with `KNOWN_SHARED`, `LEARNER_ONLY`, `PARTNER_ONLY`, `APPARENT_AGREEMENT`, `CONFIRMED_SHARED`, `DISAGREEMENT_DISCOVERED`, `AMBIGUOUS` or `UNOBSERVABLE`, including source-validated alternate readings. These are adjudicated **task-state** labels, not direct claims about real human mental states. If the source objective does not require alignment, do not invent a shared-belief mastery gate.

Predeclare reversible feasible methods `CONTRAST_APPARENT_VS_VERIFIED_AGREEMENT`, `MEANING_CHECK_PRACTICE`, `PARTNER_PERSPECTIVE_ROLEPLAY`, `MINIMAL_CLARIFICATION_REHEARSAL`, `OPEN_UNSEEN_ALIGNMENT` and `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. They may vary supported listening/reading/writing/speaking/free-production mix, explanation, activity ordering, scaffolding, pacing, feedback and review spacing while preserving the same source-defined CEFR objective, independent standards and opportunity burden. A tutor-supplied interpretation, ready-made confirmation question, highlighted discrepancy or model restatement is **assistance** for the affected move; a frozen source-permitted interlocutor reply is normal task interaction unless it crosses into coaching. Preserve alternative sampling and balanced abilities. Never infer a permanent “agreeable,” “visual,” “auditory,” “cautious” or other learner type.

For deterministic pseudonymous multi-session histories with injected clock/seed and immutable session-qualified event IDs, record objective/task/rubric/evaluator and language-variety versions; frozen truth and interlocutor private information; actual supported modality; first learner-authored assertion, check, paraphrase, clarification, acceptance and repair; which proposition each move targets; interlocutor reply and whether alignment is independently **observable from the scripted state**; source-defined outcome; selected/assigned/**actually rendered** method; exact tutor hints, highlighted discrepancies and answer-bearing models with trusted display chronology; retries and scaffold dependence; first-attempt quality; optional **reliable** hesitation/latency; topic familiarity, interlocutor and accessibility confounds; independent delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen partner/context/ambiguity transfer; and eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators. Do not infer private belief from an unvalidated “okay,” a checkmark, speech speed or a machine-generated summary. Separate what the learner communicated from what the partner understood and what the source task requires.

Evidence labels: `SOURCE_VALID_COMMON_GROUND`, `APPARENT_CONSENSUS_UNVERIFIED`, `MATERIAL_MISALIGNMENT`, `MEANING_CHECK_INDEPENDENT`, `VALID_IMPLICIT_ALIGNMENT`, `UNNECESSARY_CONFIRMATION`, `PARTNER_PERSPECTIVE_CHECK`, `DISCREPANCY_DISCOVERED`, `DISCREPANCY_REPAIRED_INDEPENDENTLY`, `FIRST_INDEPENDENT_ALIGNMENT_MOVE`, `TUTOR_INTERPRETATION_EXPOSED`, `ASSISTED_ALIGNMENT`, `INTERLOCUTOR_TASK_REPLY`, `INTERLOCUTOR_COACHING_LEAK`, `INDEPENDENT_DELAYED_ALIGNMENT`, `INDEPENDENT_UNSEEN_PARTNER_TRANSFER`, `TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED`, `ACCESSIBILITY_CONFOUNDED`, `FOLLOWUP_MISSING`, `EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED`, `TASK_STATE_VERSION_MISMATCH`, `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. ## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — “Okay” conceals a different plan.** A source-defined collaborative task requires agreement about which entrance to use. Partner privately interprets “main entrance” as the north gate; learner means the east lobby. Partner says “okay,” so learner A closes the exchange without checking and the simulated meeting fails. Learner B independently restates the precise location, hears the discrepancy and repairs it. Another source-valid task permits a uniquely identified entrance, making a second explicit confirmation unnecessary. Preserve true alignment and task outcome separately from politeness, grammar and confirmation count.
- **B — A perfect restatement was supplied by the tutor.** Tutor renders “Ask whether they mean Tuesday the 12th or Thursday the 14th” before learner A repeats it verbatim. Learner A is successful **with assistance**. In a later distinct session learner B independently detects a different date ambiguity, asks a new source-valid clarification and completes the task with an unfamiliar interlocutor. A suggestion only assigned internally but never rendered must not contaminate the earlier independent attempt; a rendered answer-bearing overlay must.
- **C — Polite acknowledgment versus verified understanding.** Across source-validated language varieties and interlocutor relationships, a partner's acknowledgment can mean “I heard you,” “I agree,” or a courteous deferral. Learner A treats every acknowledgment as a binding commitment; learner B uses a context-appropriate follow-up where the source task requires confirmed agreement. An alternative culturally legitimate indirect alignment practice also earns credit when the frozen interlocutor response and task outcome demonstrate the same source-defined construct. Never impose one English-centric confirmation phrase or penalize valid brevity.
- **D — Short-term success reverses on independent transfer.** Matched practice initially favors `MEANING_CHECK_PRACTICE` for learner A and `PARTNER_PERSPECTIVE_ROLEPLAY` for learner B. Delayed independent exchanges with new partners and genuinely unseen ambiguity patterns reverse A's advantage. Match or disclose topic familiarity, language variety, partner cooperativeness, ambiguity burden, assistance exposure, practice dose, evaluator version and accessibility. Lower confidence, sample a plausible alternative and log the **actually rendered** revised instruction; do not declare either learner a permanent “confirmation learner.”
- **E — Interest and modality confounds.** A learner accurately aligns plans in favorite-game scenarios but assumes shared knowledge in neutral healthcare-administration and unfamiliar community-service simulations of equivalent linguistic difficulty. A screen-reader user uses a source-validated text alternative to an audio-only task; that alternative can establish the **validated equivalent construct**, not invented acoustic comprehension. Another caption explicitly identifies the hidden misunderstanding and is therefore tutoring, not neutral accessibility support. Rotate favorite, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts; retain due reviews and required receptive/productive evidence. Do not infer hesitation from asynchronous network delays.
- **F — Replayed agreement and mutable state.** Two offline sessions reuse a local message ID; the partner's private-state script is revised under the same local run ID; a tutor “you both mean the north gate” overlay was rendered before the learner response but uploaded afterward; a later partner reply is missing; and a rubric revision changes whether explicit confirmation is required. Use immutable session-qualified IDs, frozen task-state/rubric hashes and trusted render chronology. Deduplicate genuine replays, quarantine incompatible state versions, preserve missing partner replies and follow-ups as unknown, and require the same independence labels, review debt and next-choice rationale across receipt-order permutations.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-defined construct, not invented social etiquette.** For A–F emit a real versioned CEFR can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, goal, task truth, relevant propositions, interlocutor state and allowable norms, channel, rubric, assistance rules, independent threshold, delayed/unseen probes, due reviews and exit gate. Missing source validation or a manufactured confirmation obligation yields `UNSUPPORTED`.
2. **Shared understanding is not apparent agreement.** In A/C distinguish partner acknowledgment, learner assertion, source-required proposition alignment, discovered mismatch and independent task outcome. A pleasant exchange with incompatible private task states cannot earn `CONFIRMED_SHARED`; source-valid implicit alignment may earn credit without a stock confirmation phrase.
3. **Proportionate checks and repairs.** In A/C/E reward learner-initiated meaning checks, partner-perspective checks and independently completed repairs only where source-relevant. Repetitive or irrelevant “do you understand?” loops do not replace a source-required proposition check, and unnecessary explicit checks must not be universally penalized or rewarded.
4. **Tutor-origin and interlocutor-origin provenance.** In B/F retain first learner-authored move, exact rendered hint/model/discrepancy cues and trusted delivery order. A displayed answer-bearing interpretation makes that move assisted; an unseen assigned hint does not. Ordinary frozen partner replies remain task interaction, but deviations that directly teach the correct check are `INTERLOCUTOR_COACHING_LEAK`.
5. **Language variety, channel and accessibility equivalence.** In C/E distinguish legitimate indirectness, politeness conventions and permitted paraphrases from unverified consensus; record actual channel and source-validated accommodations. Text evidence cannot certify unmeasured listening or speech; an accessibility overlay that reveals the target discrepancy is assistance. Timing is diagnostic only if reliably captured.
6. **Durable independent unseen alignment.** In B/D/F require meaningfully separate sessions, intervening time/practice, new interlocutor and unseen ambiguity realization, first unaided alignment move and completed source-defined outcome. A rehearsed partner response, immediate replay, assisted restatement or missing follow-up cannot establish delayed independent transfer; report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators.
7. **Method comparison independent of topic.** In D/E match or disclose task-state ambiguity, partner cooperativeness, source-variety expectations, topic familiarity, modality/accessibility, practice dose and feedback exposure. Preserve identical CEFR goals, noncompensatory skill evidence, review obligations and favorite/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar rotation. Interests remain soft, never exclusionary.
8. **Reversible adaptive choice and replay invariance.** In B/D/E/F emit feasible options, selected/assigned/actually rendered instruction, later independent outcomes, uncertainty, contradictory evidence, exploration and next choice. Sparse/confounded evidence defaults to `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. Deduplicate stable events, validate state/rubric hashes and trusted cue chronology, and assert identical independent/assisted labels, follow-up missingness, skill debt and rationale across upload-order permutations; irreconcilable provenance fails closed.
9. **Assertion-level evidence without efficacy inflation.** For A–F emit source/task/evaluator IDs, truth-state and alternative-norm adjudication, first/final learner moves, rendered cues, alignment and goal outcomes, independent delayed/unseen denominators, confounds, replay-equivalence and `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED` per assertion. Documentation CI is not a shipped evaluator, CEFR certification or proof of human-learning gains.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command; deterministic seed/clock and multi-session histories; real source-resolved CEFR tasks and validated multilingual/variety-aware rubrics; frozen partner-private task truth and source-valid interpretations; captured actual cue delivery and learner first moves; independently completed delayed unseen partner/context/ambiguity probes with denominators; comparable method/topic/access exposure; protected skill/review obligations; replay-order permutations; reversible next-instruction traces; and assertion-level verdicts. Without source mapping, frozen state, rubric validation, cue provenance, equivalent modality evidence or follow-up, mark `UNSUPPORTED`/unknown, not competence.

Any claim that adapting instruction **improves durable independent shared-understanding management** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, meaningfully delayed unseen partner exchanges, multilingual and accessibility evaluator validation, human adjudication, privacy/subgroup-harm review and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor prepares the learner to discover when an apparent agreement hides different meanings—and resolve it independently without needing the tutor to reveal the answer.**
