**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. ## Fixed CEFR objectives and independently reviewed sociopragmatic oracles

Bind only **existing versioned** CEFR interaction, mediation, writing, listening, reading or production can-dos that genuinely require audience awareness, formality, interpersonal tone or register adjustment. Preserve can-do identity, prerequisites, target language and variety, rubric, thresholds, accessibility, first independent evidence, delayed/unseen probes, receptive/productive balance and mastery/exit gates. If a can-do does not require register, mark NOT_APPLICABLE rather than adding a new graduation criterion. All situations are fictional low-stakes clubs, libraries, workshops and study groups; exclude legal, medical, emergency and employment decisions.

A frozen, independently reviewed **audience-and-intent oracle** records: who speaks to whom; the recipient's **explicitly given** role and relationship, not guessed identity; channel (chat, email, conversation); purpose (request, refusal, correction, update, thanks); factual commitments and uncertainty; urgency; local variety and permissible alternatives; tone constraints explicitly supplied in the scenario; and which choices might change the underlying meaning. Review multiple legitimate register realizations per variety. Do not equate “formal” with “better”, “informal” with “wrong”, indirectness with respect, or native-speaker conformity with competence. Do not infer culture, class, age, gender, disability, ethnicity or preferred honorific from names, accents, photos or personal profiles. If relationship, local conventions or requested address is missing, allow neutral wording or a tactful clarification.

A **recipient-facing equivalence oracle** accepts diverse natural formulations that preserve the same request, deadline, commitment, source and degree of certainty while appropriately addressing the specified audience. Reject semantic drift (request becomes order, tentative answer becomes promise, apology becomes admission of blame, optional request becomes mandatory deadline), invented relationship, unsupported social assumptions and rigid keyword-only scoring. A user-chosen respectful form of address takes precedence where appropriate; dialect, accent, grammatical variation and accessibility needs are not automatic errors. Sociopragmatic feedback must describe the **contextual effect** of a choice rather than moralize a learner's identity.

An **actual-delivery oracle** logs exact reviewed text/audio hashes, target variety, the channel and audience cues **actually displayed or heard**, subtitles/captions, example replies, register labels, suggested rewrites, hint cards, translations, model answers and feedback exposed before each attempt. Offered but unopened help is not delivered help. An answer-bearing “formal version” visible before production makes that attempt assisted. Failed audio cannot earn independent listening; a legitimate accessible text route measures its actual reading/mediation construct and must not be relabeled listening or speaking.

A **first-authorship/provenance ledger** records pseudonymous learner, fixed can-do/rubric/oracle versions, session-qualified stable event IDs and replay deduplication; first eligible unaided response, context, medium and recipient; exact actually delivered method/help; semantic fidelity and appropriateness as **separate** judgments; first error and assisted repair; due/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed probes, elapsed time and distinct-session provenance. Optional hesitation/latency is weak evidence, never fabricated or a standalone mastery gate. Preserve the initial independent response even after an assisted rewrite.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Same intent, two audiences, no single golden phrase.** In a fictional study group, a learner needs a copy of a schedule. To a close classmate, “Can you send me the schedule when you get a chance?” may fit. To a coordinator with whom they have no established relationship, “Could you please send me the schedule when you have a moment?” may fit. Both preserve a non-urgent request; an appropriately worded direct request may also be acceptable in either context. Reject the rule “all coordinators require indirect questions” or “friends must use slang.” Score contextual fit independently from grammatical and semantic fidelity, with reviewed alternatives.

**B — Polite rewriting must not invent an obligation.** A club note says “Could you share the draft if you have time this week? No rush.” A learner rewrites for a new committee contact: “You must send the final draft by Friday.” Reject MEANING_DRIFT and UNWARRANTED_COMMITMENT: the rewrite invents a requirement, a finalized artifact and a deadline. Accept “If you have time this week, could you share the draft? There is no rush,” or another locally reviewed equivalent. Adding “please” to the incorrect version does not fix the meaning.

**C — Unknown relationship: neutral fallback or clarification.** A learner is asked to message “Alex” about a workshop time; the fictional prompt supplies no role, relationship, address preference or local variety. Reject claims that a first name determines status, culture or required honorific. Accept “Hello, could you confirm the workshop time?” or a tactful question about preferred address when genuinely relevant. If a later message explicitly specifies a preferred form, revise the next message without treating the earlier neutral wording as an identity defect. Do not manufacture a cultural preference.

**D — Context-sensitive tone without penalizing variation.** In a fictional rehearsal chat, a peer says, “I may be ten minutes late; I'll update you.” A learner replies “Thanks for the heads-up; let us know when you can.” Accept a suitable acknowledgement. “You have guaranteed you will arrive in ten minutes” is rejected because it changes uncertainty, not because of register. A reviewed variety-appropriate concise acknowledgement, accessible text response or equivalent non-native wording can also pass. Do not require slang, emojis, a specific accent or a single cultural politeness formula.

**E — Delivered example, modality and first authorship.** At 09:02 a listening task explaining the recipient relationship fails playback; no audio is heard and no independent listening evidence exists. At 09:03 an accessible text alternative appears **with** a visible example headed “Suggested courteous message: Could you please confirm the time?” At 09:04 the learner repeats that sentence correctly. Mark ACTUAL_STIMULUS_RENDERED, ANSWER_EXPOSURE and ASSISTED on the measured reading/production route, not first-unaided listening or independent production. A hidden optional example never opened is not exposure. At a later real session an unseen, reviewed recipient scenario without a model can provide an independent first attempt. Deduplicate event replays; neither a midnight boundary nor immediate paraphrase is sufficient delay.

**F — Longitudinal adaptation, reversal and soft interests.** Learner A preserves meaning but repeatedly sends an overly commanding register in first-unaided new-recipient tasks. Across comparable sessions, **actually delivered** SAME_INTENT_MULTIPLE_AUDIENCES, MEANING_PRESERVING_REWRITE and BALANCED_DEFAULT are alternated with matched support. Later independent unseen messages may cautiously shift probabilities toward the method associated with stronger delayed transfer, while alternatives remain sampled. Learner B initially succeeds after formality contrast cards but later overgeneralizes that every unfamiliar recipient needs the longest possible indirect request; lower confidence, reverse or soften that preference and sample NEUTRAL_AND_CLARIFY_PRACTICE. If one method had clearer audience cues, easier topics, model answers, different dialect expectations, extra help or shorter delays, declare **no causal winner**. Assisted repeats, copied scripts and missing follow-up cannot prove retention. Interest in music may change example frequency, never become an exclusive topic filter or displace neutral/unfamiliar recipients and required modalities.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed CEFR and independently reviewed oracles.** A–F use only relevant existing versioned can-dos and frozen target-language/variety-specific audience, intent, recipient and delivery oracles. Preserve all mastery and evidence standards; otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED, never a new CEFR gate.
2. **Audience fit without cultural stereotyping.** A allows multiple reviewed ways to express the same intent, uses only supplied audience cues and rejects “one correct formal phrase” or fixed politeness hierarchies.
3. **Meaning before cosmetic politeness.** B rejects invented commands, deadlines, finality and commitments even when the surface wording sounds courteous; a valid rewrite preserves the original communicative commitments.
4. **Missing context and respectful repair.** C accepts neutral language or justified clarification when the relationship is unknown, then adapts to newly supplied explicit address preferences without inferred identity labels.
5. **Contextual variation and uncertainty.** D accepts multiple locally reviewed, accessible acknowledgements, preserves tentative meaning and never penalizes dialect, accent or missing slang/emojis as such.
6. **Actual exposure, accessibility and first authorship.** E counts only cues and models actually delivered, treats failed playback as unavailable listening, retains the true measured construct, marks answer-bearing examples assisted and deduplicates stable events.
7. **Matched, uncertain, reversible adaptation.** F changes method probabilities only from comparable actually delivered options and later independent outcomes; contradictory, sparse or confounded evidence cannot name a causal winner and must preserve exploration.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer and soft interests.** Require genuinely distinct sessions and independently reviewed new audiences, intents, channels and neutral/unfamiliar topics, first-unaided recipient-facing messages or justified clarification, and explicit missing follow-up. Preserve receptive/productive balance and all fixed objectives.
9. **Evidence and protected scope.** Synthetic prose or generic CI cannot prove executable sociopragmatic QA, shipped adaptive instruction or improved human retention. Require exact-head executable stimulus/provenance assertions, observed policy-decision changes and ethically reviewed delayed independent transfer against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, Pre-A1 changes, A1–C2 enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic acceptance histories, not observed learners or executable QA. Audience-aware expression must preserve intent and permit locally reviewed alternatives; adaptation remains reversible and interests nonexclusive. Remain UNSUPPORTED until implementation and independently verified learning evidence exist.
