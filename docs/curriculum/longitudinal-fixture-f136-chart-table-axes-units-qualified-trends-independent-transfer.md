## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Axis baseline and qualified trend language.** Fictional bar chart: labeled attendance values for Week 1 = **90** and Week 2 = **100**, vertical axis begins at **85**, with tick marks at 85, 90, 95, 100. Learner A says attendance “became six times higher” after judging the drawn bar heights; learner B independently says it “rose from 90 to 100, an increase of 10 attendees,” and avoids claiming a sixfold increase. Counterfactual: redraw the **same values** with a zero baseline; the underlying numerical and linguistic oracle must remain unchanged even though visual impression differs. Another source with cropped tick labels is **UNSUPPORTED** for magnitude unless an accessible validated table or narration supplies the values. No inference about attendance *causes* is licensed.

**B — Units, denominators and locale-sensitive number language.** Fictional paired tables report Town A = **60 total books borrowed** and Town B = **45 total books borrowed** in one week, but a separate table gives A = **3 books per visitor** and B = **5 books per visitor**. Learner A collapses totals and rates and says A leads on both measures; learner B correctly says A has the larger total while B has the higher per-visitor rate. Counterfactual: change B's total to **65** without changing per-visitor rate; only the total comparison flips. A separate independently reviewed Spanish-language stimulus may use `1,5` for a decimal and `1.500` for a thousands-formatted quantity, but the task must declare locale and independently validate formatting; an ambiguous `1,500` without locale is not silently parsed. Never require unit conversion or percentage arithmetic outside validated prerequisites.

**C — Table headers, category alignment and missingness.** Fictional table columns are `Monday`, `Tuesday`, `Wednesday`; rows are `Library` = 12, 15, **not recorded**, and `Museum` = 8, 10, 11. Learner A reads the Museum's Wednesday value as the Library's and then describes the Library as falling to zero; learner B independently reports the Museum's increase from 8 to 11 and says the Library's Wednesday count is **unknown**, not zero. Counterfactual: source explicitly changes the Library's Wednesday cell to **0**; now “zero” is supported. Sorting the rows or switching from a table to an accessible labeled line chart must not alter category identity. Suppressed or missing values must not be guessed from adjacent periods.

**D — Legend, period boundaries and warranted claims.** Fictional two-series chart covers **January–March**: Bus rides = 20, 25, 25; Train rides = 30, 28, 35. Legend identifies each line by both color **and** distinct line pattern, with an equivalent accessible table. Learner A swaps the series and says “train rides fell every month”; learner B independently says “bus rides rose then stayed level; train rides fell in February and rose in March.” Counterfactual: swap legend labels but leave plotted lines unchanged; the source-grounded series descriptions must switch accordingly, not follow remembered colors. “Train will keep rising in April” is an **unsupported forecast**; “bus rides caused train rides to fall” is an **unsupported causal claim**. If a title says `per week` but the chart labels are monthly totals, quarantine the affected comparison pending source resolution rather than choosing one silently.

**E — Actual delivery, accessibility, assistance chronology and replay.** A structured source labels a vertical axis `visitors (thousands)`, but the **delivered** cropped image omits `thousands`; a caption says `visitors`; a screen reader reads values without row/column headers; a color-only legend is inaccessible; a TTS narration says “fifteen” where the table has “fifty”; or an automatic translation turns `per person` into `in total`. Freeze authoritative and delivered evidence. A contradictory or inaccessible unit/series relation is **UNSUPPORTED** for that construct, not a learner failure. A first independent explanation authored at 10:00 remains FIRST_UNAIDED if a tutor's highlighted answer was queued at 09:59 but first **rendered** at 10:01; a subsequent copied explanation is ASSISTED. Duplicate immutable session-qualified event replay cannot manufacture another first attempt or delayed retrieval. Accessible equivalents must preserve values, unit, category, series, scale and caveats; otherwise record non-equivalence rather than a fictitious comparable modality.

**F — Reversible instruction, matched complexity and unseen audience transfer.** Fictional learner A produces a fluent immediate summary after a highlighted legend but later confuses rate versus total without help. After **actually delivered** CONTRASTIVE_UNIT_PAIRS and LABEL_FIRST_SOURCE_SCAN with fading prompts on comparable datasets, A later independently explains a **new** chart to an unfamiliar recipient and explicitly qualifies missing values. Fictional learner B instead improves delayed spoken and written comparisons after TABLE_TO_SENTENCE and CHART_TO_SENTENCE, with the same validated objective and evidence standard. Freeze baseline, numeric burden, series count, scale, legend complexity, category count, language variety, modality/accessibility, topic familiarity, help exposure, review interval and offered follow-up. If A practices a two-cell table with integers and B a logarithmic three-series chart with percentages, mark METHOD_SCALE_CONFOUNDED / METHOD_NUMERACY_CONFOUNDED / METHOD_SERIES_COUNT_CONFOUNDED and infer **no method winner**. Recoloring the same chart or renaming identical categories does **not** create genuinely unseen transfer; require a different dataset structure, unit/denominator relationship, audience or caveat demanding fresh interpretation. Track eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-ups; missing is neither success nor failure. Repeated comparable delayed first-unaided results may justify an **observed change in subsequent delivered instruction**, with calibrated uncertainty, continued alternative sampling and reversal after contrary evidence. Music, sports or travel interests may bias examples, never exclude adjacent, neutral or unfamiliar contexts or required CEFR skills.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real CEFR, source and prerequisite linkage.** A–F resolve versioned descriptor/blueprint/prerequisites, independently reviewed language/variety/register quantitative expressions, dataset/source-effective version, chart/table specification, rubric/evaluator, first-unaided threshold, accommodations, protected skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Advanced chart mathematics is not silently assigned to beginners.
2. **Axis, scale and source-value fidelity.** A binds axis/title/ticks/baseline to the correct values; a truncated visual baseline must not change the supported magnitude or authorize an exaggerated multiplicative claim. A source with unreadable axes and no equivalent data remains UNSUPPORTED.
3. **Units, rates, totals, locale and denominators.** B preserves units, absolute versus per-unit quantities and source-declared locale. Changing totals or denominators updates only affected claims; unvalidated decimal separators or implicit conversions cannot silently determine the oracle.
4. **Table/legend alignment, periods and missingness.** C/D bind row, column, category, series and period correctly, distinguish missing from zero, preserve plateau versus increase/decrease and reject fabricated extrapolation. Counterfactual row reorder, legend swap and zero/missing substitution must change only the appropriate evidence.
5. **Warranted communicative claims and independently reviewed language.** A–D accept licensed target-language/variety/register phrasings, distinguish source-supported comparisons from forecast or causal overreach, and separate intelligible wording from the construct-specific evidence threshold. Source conflicts and unreviewed code-switch/translation are UNSUPPORTED for affected judgments, not automatic learner errors.
6. **Actual delivery, accessibility, authorship, help chronology and replay.** E checks source-effective versus actually delivered values, units, labels, legends, captions, TTS, translation, crop/zoom, accessible tables and screen-reader order. Answer-bearing help **actually exposed before** first authored response contaminates independence; queued-but-not-rendered help does not. Replay is idempotent and inaccessible/conflicting channels quarantine only affected constructs.
7. **Delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen communicative transfer.** A–F require distinct real learning opportunities, new data relationships and recipients, first-unaided interpretation, targeted clarification or free production, and complete eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Recoloring or renaming an already learned chart does not count as unseen transfer.
8. **Reversible adaptation, matched difficulty and soft interests.** F records feasible/assigned/**actually delivered** methods, comparable graph/numeric/language complexity, uncertainty, alternative sampling, contrary outcomes and **observed change in subsequent instruction**. Interest contexts remain probabilistic; neutral/unfamiliar contexts, balanced modalities, due reviews, CEFR objectives and exit gates remain protected.
9. **Exact-head deterministic reproducibility and bounded claims.** A–F emit deterministic seed/clock, named exact engine HEAD and runnable command, descriptor/dataset/source/chart/table/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, delivered accessible artifacts, first/help/event chronology, independently reviewed language oracle, baseline/unit/denominator/locale/legend/missing/crop/caption/TTS/replay/source-version counterfactuals, confounds, delayed/unseen denominators and **PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion**. Documentation-only CI cannot establish a shipped chart-language evaluator, reproducible runtime defect or improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and reproducible command; real versioned CEFR-linked objectives; independently reviewed language/variety-specific data-description expressions; source-effective structured values and chart/table rendering; accessible authoritative and **actually delivered** stimuli; independently attributable first responses and actual answer-bearing help chronology; delayed first-unaided retrieval and genuinely unseen dataset/recipient transfer; comparable or explicitly confounded instructional methods; protected skill coverage, due reviews and exit obligations; complete eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; and per-assertion verdicts for baseline/scale/units/rates/locale/legend/period/missingness/accessibility/translation/caption/replay/source-version counterfactuals. Unvalidated number formats, inaccessible labels, contradictory delivered channels and missing follow-ups remain **UNSUPPORTED/unknown**, not learner failure.

A stronger claim that adaptive data-language instruction **improves durable independent communication** requires a separately reviewed, privacy-appropriate, multilingual and accessibility-validated real-learner longitudinal study with credible comparisons, attrition/subgroup-harm analysis and reproducible delayed outcomes. **A strong tutor helps learners describe what a table or chart actually supports, qualify what it does not, and explain the result independently in a new context.**
