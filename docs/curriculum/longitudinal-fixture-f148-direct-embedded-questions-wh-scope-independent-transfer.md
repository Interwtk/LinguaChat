## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Direct versus embedded question structure and meaning.** In a reviewed standard-English travel helpdesk dialogue, the learner needs a station location. 'Where is the station?' is a direct wh-question. 'Could you tell me where the station is?' embeds the location question inside a polite information request. Learner A produces 'Could you tell me where is the station?' in a context where the reviewed variety expects an embedded interrogative without that direct inversion; learner B independently produces an acceptable natural request and receives the location. Counterfactual: a reporter quotes 'Where is the station?' verbatim after 'She asked:'; the quotation is not automatically an embedded-clause error. A separately reviewed English variety or prosodic echo context may allow a different form; do not globally mark it incorrect. Grading must distinguish meaning, naturalness and the target register.

**B — Matrix yes/no question versus intended embedded information.** A customer asks a clerk 'Do you know when the next bus leaves?' The clerk says 'Yes' and stops. That can literally answer a knowledge question but often fails the customer's practical request for departure time. Learner A insists that 'Yes' always completes the exchange; learner B independently supplies the departure time if it is verified, or says 'I don't have the schedule; please check the display' if it is not. Counterfactual: in a survey explicitly measuring who knows the timetable, a yes/no response is the actual intended answer. 'Can you tell me whether the 7:30 bus stops here?' embeds a **polar** question, not a request for a departure time. Do not hallucinate the timetable or penalize justified uncertainty.

**C — Wh-variable, presupposition and multipart scope.** A meeting organizer asks 'Which room did the workshop move to?' but the reviewed source says no room change occurred. Learner A invents a new room; learner B independently challenges the presupposition: 'It hasn't moved—do you mean the original room?' Counterfactual: a reviewed update identifies Room 12 as the new location, licensing that answer. A separate request 'Who approved which change?' may require a reviewed pairing of people to changes, not a single unpaired name. When multiple pairings are genuinely unresolved, credit a recipient-oriented clarification instead of forcing a unique assignment. Do not conflate 'why', 'whether', 'who' and 'where' merely because the answer mentions the same event.

**D — Cross-language and register variation.** A reviewed English email asks 'Could you explain why the deadline changed?' and a Spanish colleague asks '¿Me puedes explicar por qué cambió la fecha límite?' Both may fulfill the same recipient information gap while using language-specific structures. A learner who translates every embedded question with English inversion, or imposes English auxiliary 'do' on another language, must not be credited for an unnatural target-language construction. Counterfactual: a casual spoken English echo 'You said where?' can be a valid repair question rather than a malformed formal embedded question. Japanese indirect questions may use a language-specific interrogative-plus-complement construction; require independently reviewed natural realizations and register, not a word-by-word English template. Accept equivalent respectful and direct alternatives where the communicative situation allows them; never treat formality as intelligence or a fixed learner trait.

**E — Actually delivered evidence, accessibility, authorship and replay.** Authoritative audio asks 'Do you know **whether** the clinic is open?' but ASR substitutes 'where'; captions omit 'whether'; translation inserts a known opening time; TTS prosody masks an echo quotation; or a visual answer card reveals the location before the learner replies. Compare authoritative against **actually rendered** material. Quarantine only the affected interrogative construct when the learner lacked evidence; a reviewed accessible transcript may support equivalent **reading** but cannot certify unheard listening. At 10:00 a learner independently asks 'Do you mean whether it is open, or where it is?'; a scaffold queued at 09:59 but first rendered at 10:01 cannot retroactively assist that response. A revision at 10:02 after exposure is ASSISTED. Autocomplete silently correcting 'where is' to 'where it is' is assistance, not first-unaided syntactic evidence. Replayed event IDs cannot manufacture extra first attempts, sessions or delayed success.

**F — Longitudinal method comparison, independent transfer and interests.** Fictional learner A excels at immediate highlighted clause-boundary recognition after CLAUSE_BOUNDARY_HIGHLIGHT_THEN_FADE but fails later unaided booking inquiries. Comparable actually delivered INFORMATION_GAP_ROLEPLAY_THEN_FADE trials precede independent delayed messages to a **new** recipient about a **new** uncertainty, with no displayed models. Learner B benefits from DIRECT_VS_EMBEDDED_RECIPIENT_CONTRAST on later first-unaided service questions but subsequently regresses, so the hypothesis must lose confidence and alternative sampling must resume. If one method receives short familiar 'where' questions and the other receives complex multi-wh questions, unfamiliar words, a different register or more help, mark the relevant METHOD_*_CONFOUNDED and infer **no winner**. Merely changing a station name in the same template is not unseen transfer. Log eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing separately; missing is not failure. Only repeated comparable later independent outcomes can justify an **actually delivered** instructional change, preserving shared objectives, all required modalities and balanced fallback. A travel enthusiast may see transport questions more often, but neutral, adjacent and unfamiliar healthcare/work/community topics and every CEFR objective remain reachable.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real CEFR-linked objective and reviewed oracle.** A–F bind existing versioned can-dos, prerequisites, recipient information gaps, target language/variety/register, source-world/question oracle, accessible stimuli, rubric, first-unaided threshold, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and unchanged exit gate. Never invent a question-specific CEFR descriptor.
2. **Direct versus embedded interrogative boundaries.** A distinguishes standard direct and embedded constructions while allowing independently reviewed quotation, echo, variety and register exceptions; no universal English-inversion rule across languages.
3. **Matrix intent versus embedded content.** B distinguishes literal knowledge/ability polar questions from contextually intended information requests, and embedded whether/if questions from wh-questions; a recipient-relevant answer or justified inability is credited without fabricated facts.
4. **Wh-variable, scope and unsupported presupposition.** C preserves the asked variable and multi-wh mappings, challenges unsupported changes and credits clarification when pairings or antecedents remain genuinely ambiguous.
5. **Language-specific naturalness and accessibility.** D accepts independently reviewed natural constructions and equivalent recipient outcomes without penalizing legitimate variety or requiring English auxiliary patterns in other languages; register remains contextual, not a learner label.
6. **Rendered assistance, authorship and idempotence.** E reconciles authoritative versus delivered ASR/TTS/captions/translation/visuals/screen-reader/autocomplete, quarantines only affected constructs, orders learner authorship against **rendered** help, and deduplicates session-qualified events. Accessible reading does not certify unheard listening.
7. **Confound-aware, uncertain, reversible adaptation.** F compares feasible accessible **delivered** methods under matched query type, clause depth, pragmatic intent, vocabulary, register, modality, topic, prior help, interval and follow-up. Sparse, contradictory or confounded evidence selects no winner; balanced fallback and exploration persist.
8. **Delayed independent transfer and topic/skill balance.** Assisted model-copy edits, same-session drills and name-only swaps cannot earn delayed/unseen mastery. Require a distinct eligible later session, first-unaided recipient-successful production, genuinely new information gap/recipient/structure, reviewed oracle and explicit missingness. Preserve listening, reading, writing, speaking and free-production coverage, plus neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar context rotation.
9. **Evidence honesty and release boundary.** A documentation or CI pass does not prove executable engine support, delivered outcome-driven method adaptation or improved human retention. Require exact-head executable tests tied to real objectives, longitudinal independent delayed/transfer comparison with a balanced baseline and later ethically reviewed learner evidence before any efficacy claim.
