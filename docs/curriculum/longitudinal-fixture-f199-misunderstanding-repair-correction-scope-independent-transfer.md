Not a new CEFR objective, universal apology rubric, conversation-management product, shipped adaptive tutor, or proof of human learning improvement. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F189 distinguishes attribution and uncertainty; F191 tests relevant answers; F192 cancellable inferences; F193 recipient knowledge; F194 proposals versus decisions; F196 feedback and approval scope; F198 combines claims in recipient-facing updates. **Distinct construct here:** after the learner or an interlocutor has communicated a *specific mistaken understanding*, can the learner recognize credible corrective evidence, explicitly repair the *materially wrong shared understanding* for the right audience, preserve unaffected facts and uncertainty, and continue the interaction appropriately? Merely restating the newest source or producing a correct answer in isolation does not demonstrate conversational repair.

## Fixed objective, correction and audience oracles

Bind only applicable **existing versioned** CEFR can-dos for clarification, correcting information, responding to misunderstanding, conversation maintenance, mediation or an equivalent task. Freeze objective ID and version, prerequisites, level-appropriate language burden and meaningful-turn ceiling, target language/variety, accessible channel, rubric/threshold, source and feedback versions, first-unaided/delayed standards, receptive/productive balance and exit gates. A1 repair may be a short plain correction where supported; multi-party responsibility tracing is NOT_APPLICABLE if beyond an existing objective. No extra graduation gate. Preserve the unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix disagreement, 20-meaningful-turn ceiling and closed B2 gate.

An independently reviewed **correction oracle** freezes the original utterance, speaker, addressee, actual delivery, time and scope; the specific mistaken proposition; later correction, its authority and evidence; what remains true/unknown; and what the recipient needs to understand or do next. Label claims as SOURCE_STATED, LEARNER_PREVIOUSLY_ASSERTED, MISUNDERSTANDING_DETECTED, CREDIBLE_CORRECTION, CORRECTION_DISPUTED, PROPOSAL_NOT_DECISION, REPORTED_NOT_VERIFIED, CORRECTED_FOR_RECIPIENT, CORRECTION_NOT_DELIVERED, UNAFFECTED_FACT, UNKNOWN or SUPERSEDED_WITH_PROVENANCE. Do not treat every newer statement as authoritative: a disputed change requires appropriately scoped uncertainty or clarification. An acknowledgment confirms receipt only when actually evidenced, not comprehension or agreement.

A separate **pragmatic repair oracle**, reviewed for language, register, relationship and variety, judges whether the response identifies the material correction, is appropriately clear and polite, avoids falsely assigning blame, and provides the minimum next-step information required by the addressee. Accept natural concise alternatives, including a brief apology when appropriate; never make a formulaic apology, elaborate explanation or culturally narrow politeness phrase a universal mastery gate. A repair to one recipient does not automatically repair misinformation already sent to others.

The **actual-delivery/first-authorship ledger** stores pseudonymous learner, immutable session-qualified event and replay IDs, versioned objective/source/oracle/rubric, actual rendered source turns and correction, addressee/channel, any message actually sent/acknowledged, first eligible unaided repair, subsequent assisted rewrite, hints/model answers/complete correction tables actually rendered, optional reliable latency, recurring error type, modality/accessibility route, instructional choice/dose/confidence, and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed probes. An offered but unopened hint is not exposure. A failed audio channel earns no listening evidence; accessible text may support a separately valid reading route. Duplicate sends, replayed scenarios and a calendar midnight crossing do not manufacture independent attempts or delayed retention.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Explicitly repair a previously announced false confirmation.** Maya proposes Friday for a library meeting; Theo replies “Friday works for me.” The learner tells Nora, “The meeting is confirmed for Friday.” The organizer later says to the learner, “We have not confirmed a day yet.” The learner must correct Nora's understanding: accept “I was wrong to say Friday was confirmed. It's only been proposed; we're still waiting for the organizer's decision.” Reject silently changing a private note, saying “Friday was cancelled” or repeating “Theo can attend” without retracting the confirmation. If the organizer later explicitly confirms Friday, the correction must follow the actual chronological evidence rather than permanently mark Friday impossible.

**B — A correction's audience is not automatically everyone.** A room-change notice says Room 5, but the learner earlier sent Room 3 to Nora and Theo. The learner subsequently corrects Nora only. Accept an explicit Room 5 correction to Theo when prompted to ensure both recipients have the update; do not assume Theo learned from Nora. If Theo later acknowledges the corrected message, receipt for Theo becomes supported, not automatic agreement or attendance. A visible message to a group containing both recipients may support delivery to that group only if the channel's actual-delivery evidence warrants it.

**C — Repair a numerical overclaim without discarding the valid bound.** The organizer says “At least twelve people registered.” The learner tells Maya “Exactly twelve registered.” A later organizer reminder clarifies that twelve is a minimum and the final count is not yet known. Accept “Correction: I said exactly twelve, but we only know at least twelve registered; the total could be higher.” Reject “No one knows whether twelve registered” and “Twenty registered” without evidence. If a later verified total is fourteen, update the total, not the prior source's historical meaning.

**D — Disputed correction requires scoped clarification, not automatic override.** An authorized coordinator explicitly confirms a Saturday workshop. A participant later messages “Actually, it's Sunday, I think,” without evidence of organizer approval. The learner must answer an attendee who asks which date is confirmed. Accept “The coordinator confirmed Saturday; someone suggested Sunday, but I haven't seen an official change. I'll check with the coordinator.” Reject “Sunday is confirmed” and an unjustified categorical accusation that the participant lied. If a later verified organizer update changes the date, repair the attendee-facing information accordingly.

**E — Assisted rewrite, failed audio and replay cannot manufacture independent repair.** At 09:01 the learner independently receives A's correction in text and replies to Nora “Friday is cancelled”; preserve this first-unaided error. At 09:02 the tutor renders a complete model repair; at 09:03 the learner copies it. Record ANSWER_EXPOSURE and ASSISTED_REPAIR, not independent repair mastery. A 09:04 replay of the same exchange is deduplicated. In an audio variant the correction fails to play but captions render: no listening evidence; a separately bound reading route may be valid. A hint merely offered but not opened does not count as delivered. A later transfer probe must use an unseen correction structure and real delay, not the same dates and people renamed.

**F — Longitudinal method comparison, counterevidence and soft interests.** Learner A recognizes wrong claims in isolation but repeatedly fails to notify the person who received the false information. Across comparable real sessions, RECIPIENT_CHECK, ORIGINAL_VS_CORRECTION_CONTRAST and BALANCED_DEFAULT are actually delivered and alternated. Tentatively prefer a method only if later first-unaided unseen repairs improve with matched source authority, audience count, help, language burden, channel, topic and delay. Learner B initially improves after model repair scripts but later mechanically apologizes while omitting the corrected fact; this counterevidence lowers confidence and restores balanced sampling or a concise one-sentence repair. If the preferred method received easier corrections, fewer recipients, a familiar gaming context, more answer-bearing help or shorter delays, mark confounding and **no causal winner**. Missing follow-up stays unknown. Gaming may softly weight an example; adjacent clubs, neutral libraries and unfamiliar workshops remain in rotation, without skipping required CEFR can-dos or modalities.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **No invented CEFR repair gate.** A–F bind applicable existing versioned can-dos and independently reviewed language/variety-specific source, correction and pragmatic oracles; preserve prerequisite, turn ceiling, difficulty, threshold, skill and exit standards, else NOT_APPLICABLE/UNSUPPORTED.
2. **Material misunderstanding actually repaired.** A retracts the wrong confirmation for the recipient and preserves the distinction between a proposal, a cancellation and a decision; merely knowing the new answer privately is insufficient.
3. **Recipient scope and delivery.** B tracks which recipient was misinformed, which correction was actually sent or received and what remains unknown; never infer universal dissemination from one repair.
4. **Minimal correction without epistemic inflation.** C preserves a lower bound, retracts only unsupported exactness and updates with later verified evidence; do not turn an overclaim into total ignorance.
5. **Authority, conflict and justified uncertainty.** D refuses an unverified overwrite, avoids falsely claiming the participant lied, and seeks targeted confirmation when needed.
6. **First authorship and modality integrity.** E distinguishes first-unaided errors, rendered model answers, assisted copying, unopened hints, failed audio, valid captions routes and event replay; no phantom listening or duplicate mastery.
7. **Reversible, matched adaptation.** F compares actually delivered options using later independent repair; audience, authority, source difficulty, help, channel, topic and delay confounds lower confidence and prevent causal claims.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer and nonexclusive interests.** Require new sessions, genuinely unseen misunderstanding/correction/recipient structures, independent unaided responses, explicit missing follow-up and balanced receptive/productive coverage. Rotate preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts; never skip a can-do for interests.
9. **Honest evidence and protected scope.** Synthetic histories and generic CI are not executable repair QA, a shipped tutor or proof of improved human retention. Require exact-head executable delivery/provenance/response checks, observed reversible instructional choices and ethically reviewed delayed independent outcomes against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge, Pre-A1 changes, A1–C2 enablement or weakened B2 boundaries.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic design histories, not observed learner behavior or executable tests. They specify how to recognize, communicate and verify correction of a real misunderstanding without inflating evidence or redefining CEFR mastery. Remain UNSUPPORTED until independently implemented and verified.
