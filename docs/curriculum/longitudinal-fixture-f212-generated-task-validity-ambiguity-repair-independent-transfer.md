# F212 — Generated-task validity, ambiguous prompts, repair and independent transfer

**Status:** design-only, synthetic *cross-cutting* longitudinal curriculum acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until an independently reviewed executable exact-head harness demonstrates the required preflight, exposure, repair and assessment behavior. No generated-task validator, shipped adaptive tutor, new CEFR can-do, learner diagnosis, graduation gate or human-learning gain is claimed. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 addresses prior item exposure, F50 soft interest-topic boundaries, F203 clarification selection, F208 metamorphic policy invariants and F211 independently reviewed task-demand calibration. **Distinct question in F212:** if an adaptive tutor varies or generates the *actual task prompt* for a fixed CEFR objective, can it prove that the prompt is answerable, semantically coherent, fair to the required skill, correctly scored and actually presented—then repair or withdraw an invalid task **without converting tutor-authored defects into learner failure or fabricated independent success**?

## Fixed objective, prompt validity and evidence contract

Bind every history to an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, prerequisites, target language/variety, communicative intent, independent meaning-critical rubric, task family, required productive/receptive skill, supported modality, accessibility accommodations, review obligations and unchanged mastery/exit standards. Illustrative English below is not released curriculum. Preserve the unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix mismatch as **UNSUPPORTED**; no generated recognition item substitutes for required independent production.

Each candidate task has a versioned, independently reviewed **task validity oracle**, separate from the learner's score: (1) all referenced facts/roles/times/quantities and information sources are present or an explicit clarification path is valid; (2) instructions and success conditions are mutually consistent; (3) the required language act is possible without hidden private facts or unsupported capabilities; (4) the meaning-critical rubric accepts semantically equivalent correct responses and flags genuinely different meanings; (5) distractors and context changes do not alter the fixed objective or silently change difficulty; (6) accessibility and actually rendered modality permit the intended response; (7) answer-bearing examples, transcripts and previous exposures are identified; and (8) an invalid or ambiguous task has an auditable quarantine/repair path. An AI generator's self-rating is not independent validation.

Record a deterministic **candidate-to-delivery ledger**: pseudonymous learner, session/run/event IDs, trusted occurrence versus upload order, objective/prerequisite/rubric/validator/policy versions, generation template and seed if available, candidate prompt and source facts, independent validity verdict and reasons, reviewed difficulty vector, topic-family/interest weights, modality supported/planned/rendered, accessibility state, candidate selection and uncertainty, prompt version actually rendered, visible answer-bearing content, first unaided response, semantic evaluation and assessor version, help/clarification, retries, withdrawal/repair, completed-run state, later independent delayed and unseen probes, and next **actually delivered** instructional choice. A draft prompt never rendered is not an opportunity. A repaired prompt is a **new version**, not a retroactive edit to the learner's observed task. Unknown exposure chronology is UNKNOWN, not independent evidence.

Oracle labels: OBJECTIVE_FIXED, CANDIDATE_VALIDATED, TASK_VALID, TASK_INVALID, AMBIGUOUS_BUT_CLARIFIABLE, INCONSISTENT_CONSTRAINTS, MISSING_SOURCE_FACT, RUBRIC_INCOMPLETE, EQUIVALENT_ANSWER_ACCEPTED, PROMPT_RENDERED, PROMPT_NOT_RENDERED, ACCESS_FAILURE, MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE, ANSWER_EXPOSED, FIRST_UNAIDED, LEARNER_ERROR_CONFIRMED, TUTOR_DEFECT_QUARANTINED, REPAIR_NEW_VERSION, NO_RETROACTIVE_RESCORE, CLEAN_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_INDEPENDENT_TRANSFER, TOPIC_ROTATION_PRESERVED, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, NOT_COMPARABLE and UNSUPPORTED. ## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — An impossible deadline is a tutor defect, not a grammar failure.** A generated scheduling task says, “Send the final agenda before Friday, but only after the approval that arrives next Sunday,” and simultaneously requires the learner to promise on-time completion. The independently reviewed facts make the unconditional promise impossible. The learner writes, “I can send it after approval, but not before Friday.” An unsafe evaluator calls this incorrect for not promising Friday. The expected validator rejects the inconsistent prompt or accepts the learner's principled clarification; it does not assign a CEFR failure or silently remove the Sunday condition. In a paired valid control, approval arrives Thursday and the deadline remains Friday: the learner must communicate the feasible condition accurately. A task defect and a genuine meaning-critical learner error remain distinguishable.

**B — Soft interest substitution must preserve the communicative problem.** The fixed can-do is to ask for a missing location before attending an event. A neutral workplace prompt names an event but omits the room; the correct independent response is a clarification request. A game-themed candidate accidentally includes “Room 204” in a visible sidebar, making the same question answerable directly; a second candidate uses game jargon that presupposes inaccessible knowledge. The expected tutor logs the changed information state or prerequisite burden, rejects unreviewed equivalence, and selects a valid neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar alternative. Changing only interest weights in a *genuinely equivalent* paired control changes topic sampling, not the objective, rubric, method confidence or mastery. Do not attribute easier performance to a better instructional method.

**C — Ambiguity can be the intended skill, but not a hidden answer key.** A task says, “Maya told Nora she had approved the poster,” without resolving the pronoun. If the fixed objective is recognizing ambiguity and requesting clarification, “Who approved it?” is a valid independent response. If the fixed objective instead requires accurately reporting *which person* approved, the prompt lacks a determinate source fact and cannot fairly demand one named answer. The validator must distinguish intentional, rubric-supported ambiguity from accidental under-specification. A matched unambiguous prompt names Maya as the approver; incorrect attribution there is assessable. Do not penalize an appropriate clarification as lack of fluency.

**D — A rendered transcript cannot rescue an unavailable listening assessment.** A generated listening task references an audio message and asks the learner to extract a changed meeting time. In one branch the audio never renders; in another the tutor reveals the full transcript before the learner answers. Neither branch can earn clean listening evidence. The tutor may offer a valid accessible reading practice task and keep listening coverage debt open; it must not treat the transcript response as independent listening transfer. In a paired control with independently verified accessible audio actually played, an unaided answer can be assessed under the supported listening rubric. A planned but unrendered prompt or audio does not count as a failed attempt.

**E — A narrow generated answer key must not override the meaning rubric.** A valid request task asks the learner to politely ask a colleague for a document by Friday. The generated answer key contains only “Could you send me the document by Friday, please?” The learner independently writes “Would you mind sending me the document no later than Friday?” The reviewed semantic/pragmatic rubric accepts the equivalent intent, deadline and politeness; a literal-string evaluator's rejection is an evaluator/rubric defect to quarantine and investigate, not a learner error. A control response changes “by Friday” to “after Friday” and must fail the meaning-critical deadline requirement. No automated regrading of historical records is authorized without a validated, versioned, auditable procedure.

**F — Repair, replay and follow-up cannot launder invalid opportunities.** A generated task v1 with missing source facts is shown, answered and later withdrawn. A corrected v2 is created; the learner then completes a new independent attempt. Offline sync uploads v2 before v1, duplicates both events, and includes a planned v3 that was never rendered. The tutor preserves version-specific occurrence/exposure chronology, deduplicates session-qualified event identities and quarantines v1's invalid score. It may use clean v2 practice evidence but cannot claim an independent delayed transfer from a same-session rewrite of v1 or from a previously exposed answer. Only a genuinely later, unfamiliar, unaided and valid probe can supply that evidence. A paired control with unknown exposure order stays UNKNOWN; a missing follow-up is neither success nor failure.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **No CEFR or release drift.** Resolve each history to a real versioned can-do, semantic rubric, prerequisites, modality, meaningful-turn ceiling where applicable, due reviews and unchanged independent mastery/exit gates. Invalid tasks never invent a can-do, waive an unmet skill or resolve the unsupported B2 Arc 6 boundary.
2. **Validate before scoring, independently.** Publish deterministic seed/clock, exact prompt/source facts, task/rubric/validator versions and an independently reviewed validity oracle. A generator's own “looks good” judgment is not proof. Mark unvalidated candidates UNSUPPORTED; invalid or unsatisfiable candidates cannot become learner failures.
3. **Distinguish genuine language errors from task defects.** A separates an impossible deadline from a learner's communication; the feasible paired control tests the same intent. A wrong answer to a valid task can fail, but a principled response to inconsistent instructions cannot be penalized by an invented key.
4. **Interests cannot change the information state unnoticed.** B checks whether topic substitution exposes an answer, hides prerequisites or changes independently reviewed demand. Only validated equivalent substitutions preserve method evidence; adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts remain eligible and sampled longitudinally.
5. **Clarification is evaluated against the intended objective.** C accepts appropriate uncertainty management when ambiguity is the target, and rejects a purported single-answer assessment with missing facts. Do not silently swap objectives or infer missing source facts.
6. **Actual modality and answer exposure govern credit.** D distinguishes candidate, rendered prompt, playable audio, visible transcript, access failure and unaided response. Failed audio cannot yield listening mastery, and reading fallback cannot erase required listening/speaking coverage.
7. **Semantic equivalence beats brittle answer matching.** E accepts independently reviewed meaning-equivalent responses while preserving meaning-critical deadline errors. Evaluator disagreement is logged with version and reason; historical scores are not silently rewritten.
8. **Repair is versioned and provenance-safe.** F preserves v1/v2 identities, actual render/exposure order, withdrawals, deduplication and unknown chronology. A corrected same-session task is not clean delayed retrieval; later independent transfer needs a valid, unseen, completed probe.
9. **Independent executable proof precedes implementation claims.** Publish machine-readable A–F histories and paired controls, expected per-event validity/score/quarantine and **next actually delivered** task, exact objective/rubric/validator/policy versions, seed/clock, rendered exposures, replay permutations, delayed/unseen outcomes, due/offered/rendered/completed denominators and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED in an independently reviewed exact-head harness. Even a passing harness establishes only software behavior. Claim improved human learning only after an ethically reviewed longitudinal comparison with a balanced baseline, independently reviewed comparable tasks, unaided delayed retention, unseen transfer, attrition analysis and no hidden CEFR skill loss. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge, Pre-A1 changes or A1–C2 release.

**Fixture verdict:** F212 makes **adaptive/generated task validity and the fair treatment of tutor-authored defects** falsifiable at the point of actual delivery, assessment and later independent transfer. A flexible tutor may change examples and challenge without shifting the learner's goalposts or scoring its own mistakes as learner deficits. **UNSUPPORTED** until executed and independently reviewed; synthetic scenarios do not prove human learning benefit.
