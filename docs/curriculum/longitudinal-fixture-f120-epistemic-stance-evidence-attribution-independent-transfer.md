**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run on a named exact engine HEAD against real versioned CEFR-linked tasks, independently source-validated language/variety interpretations and delayed first-unaided transfer. This does **not** ship a truth verifier, misinformation detector, epistemic-state model, CEFR mastery or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F33 protects genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 protect actual rather than queued help and tutor-origin errors; F49 protects accessibility; F50 preserves nonexclusive interest rotation; F68 concerns metacognitive calibration; F104 isolates method from difficulty; F107 concerns unreliable tutor feedback; F110 ambiguous paralinguistic cues; F113 speaker attribution; F117 register and variety; F118 conditional/scope meaning; F119 learner-initiated clarification. **Distinct construct:** can a learner communicate **who claims to know what, with which degree of commitment, on what available basis, and what still needs confirmation**, preserving the source-authored meaning in an independent new exchange? This is language-use evidence, not an assessment of whether an external claim is objectively true, and not a diagnosis of learner credulity or personality.

## Source-validated communicative contract and evidence ledger

For every case resolve the **real versioned** CEFR descriptor/can-do, level blueprint and prerequisites, target language and approved varieties, source-authored exchange or text, speaker roles and information access, communicative purpose, evidence available **within the task**, source-validated certainty/attribution alternatives, register, rubric/evaluator, first-unaided threshold, protected listening/reading/writing/speaking/free-production requirements, accommodation and assistance policy, due reviews, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer and exit gate. A beginner's source-valid “I think” versus “I know” distinction must not acquire an invented advanced evidentiality prerequisite. Where a language grammatically encodes evidentiality or uncertainty differently, use independent language-specific validation rather than literal English translations. Some contexts permit a concise direct answer; do not require hedging on every turn.

Freeze an **epistemic-communication ledger** with descriptor/task/blueprint/rubric/evaluator hashes; source-effective version and authorized language/variety readings; exact source wording and speaker/recipient; evidence available to each participant; provenance of direct observation, attributed report, inference and unverified claim **inside the task**; whether the task itself confirms or contradicts the proposition; source-authorized certainty and reporting constructions; first learner-authored interpretation/utterance and commitment level; the actual partner response; tutor hints, models, translations and corrective feedback **actually rendered before** the learner attempt; accessible modality route and channel reliability; retries; delayed/unseen probe family overlap; assigned versus actually delivered instructional choice; confidence and missingness. Minimize personal data. Never infer real-world truth, learner political belief, medical status, nationality, trustworthiness or psychological traits from a synthetic dialogue.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — “Someone said” is not “it is confirmed.”** In a source-authored workplace message, speaker A says “I heard that the meeting moved to Friday”; speaker B says “The coordinator confirmed Thursday.” Learner A's first unaided recap says “The meeting is definitely Friday”; learner B says “A heard Friday, but B reports a confirmed Thursday; I should verify the current schedule.” The task rubric credits accurate attribution and calibrated uncertainty, not a guess about the actual real-world schedule. A counterfactual changes B's line to “I also heard Friday” without authoritative confirmation: the acceptable certainty must change. If the source never identifies the coordinator's authority or reliable confirmation, preserve the conflict and mark any supposed final schedule UNSUPPORTED. A learner who merely repeats both claims without resolving the task's requested communicative action receives partial attribution evidence, not automatic completion.

**B — Tentative plans versus commitments.** In a source-validated invitation exchange, “I might come” does not promise attendance; “I'll be there” makes a stronger commitment. Learner A independently replies to “I might come” with “Great, I'll reserve your seat as confirmed”; learner B says “Let me know once you decide.” A separate case has a clear, confirmed acceptance, where an appropriately direct acknowledgment is better than needless hedging. Do not confuse grammatical correctness with accurate commitment strength. A counterfactual substitutes a source-approved target-language modal or discourse marker with equivalent intent; adjudicate by language-specific pragmatic meaning, not English word matching. If the utterance is genuinely ambiguous in its context, a source-valid confirmation question can pass without assuming a commitment.

**C — Firsthand, inference and quotation must stay separate.** A fictional source-authorized learner describes a wet street: “I saw wet pavement; I think it rained.” Learner A reports “They saw rain falling”; learner B says “They saw wet pavement and inferred that it rained.” A quoted statement “The shop owner said the delivery arrived” must not be rewritten as “I personally checked the delivery.” In a language with grammatical reportative/evidential forms, require validated equivalents. A source-valid concise summary can omit irrelevant details but must retain consequential uncertainty and attribution. The evaluator cannot penalize legitimate dialect/register variants (F117) or demand detailed evidence markers absent from the actual CEFR can-do.

**D — Communication-channel and feedback noise.** A source-authored listening task includes “probably” in the authoritative, actually rendered audio; the provided transcript accidentally omits it. A learner hears “probably” and correctly responds with uncertainty. Mark CHANNEL_UNRELIABLE and quarantine affected listening-vs-reading comparisons rather than penalizing the learner. In an ASR variant, an accurate spoken “I think the train is late” is transcribed as “The train is late”; do not score overconfidence without reliable speech evidence (F111). A tutor's confidently wrong correction is not independent ground truth (F107). An authorized accessible text path may prove the same attribution construct when the descriptor permits it, but never masquerades as unaided listening or speaking evidence.

**E — Method adaptation requires delayed, unaided calibration.** Fictional learner A excels on immediate model-assisted certainty worksheets but later overstates reported claims in unfamiliar first-unaided conversations. After **actually delivered** ATTRIBUTED_REPORT_REPHRASING, A preserves sources and uncertainty on delayed unseen messages. Learner B improves after COMMITMENT_GRADIENT_ROLEPLAY instead. Compare objective, baseline, source/target language and variety, task stakes, epistemic complexity, topic familiarity, accessibility, modality, assistance actually rendered, partner feedback, review interval, follow-up availability and attrition. If report rephrasing always uses simple explicit “heard” cases while roleplay uses subtle culturally dependent markers, flag EPISTEMIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED and do not infer superiority. With enough comparable later evidence, the next delivered method may differ between A and B with confidence bounds; contradictory evidence must reduce confidence and permit reversal. Deliberately sample alternatives and maintain balanced receptive/productive and unfamiliar-topic coverage.

**F — Provenance, chronology and genuinely new transfer.** A learner submits an unaided recap before a tutor hint is queued; the hint appears **after** the answer. The answer stays unaided. An offline event is uploaded twice: deduplicate using immutable session-qualified event identity. A later source revision changes “the manager expects a decision” to “the manager confirmed a decision”: score earlier attempts against the original effective source and later probes against the new version, never rewriting the historical attempt. A second speaker's quotation cannot silently become the first speaker's firsthand evidence. A delayed task that merely renames the speakers while retaining the same rumor, confirmation sequence and correct response is not unseen transfer. If a learner asks for confirmation but the partner never responds, retain independently authored clarification evidence and mark successful resolution FOLLOWUP_MISSING, not success or failure.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real CEFR/task linkage and appropriate burden.** A–F resolve a real versioned descriptor, blueprint, source-authored roles/utterances, validated language/variety readings, evaluator/rubric, first-unaided threshold, accommodation and assistance policy, protected skill families, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. No invented universal evidentiality grammar or advanced prerequisite.
2. **Attribution and evidential basis.** A/C/F distinguish direct observation, attributed reports, inference and unattributed rumor **as represented in the task**. A source-valid paraphrase preserves consequential speaker and evidence provenance; an unsupported conversion of hearsay to direct fact fails that construct.
3. **Commitment and certainty calibration.** A/B/C distinguish tentative, confirmed, denied and unresolved commitments. Both unjustified overstatement and meaning-changing understatement are evaluated; direct responses pass where the source warrants certainty. Source-approved alternatives and valid clarification are not penalized.
4. **Conflicts remain bounded.** A/B/F keep conflicting reports, missing authority and unanswered questions unresolved until actual source-supported clarification. The evaluator does not independently declare a synthetic claim true or authorize an external real-world action.
5. **First-unaided versus exposed help.** D/E/F log the first authored answer, retries and tutor model/translation/correction exposure **actually rendered before** the answer. Queued or post-answer help cannot retroactively turn an unaided response into assisted evidence; copied corrections cannot earn independent mastery.
6. **Multilingual and accessible construct validity.** B/C/D accept source-approved target-language varieties and pragmatic equivalents, isolate ASR/TTS/transcript contradictions, preserve legitimate accommodations and protected modalities. Unreliable audio/transcript or unvalidated marker interpretation produces affected UNSUPPORTED verdicts, not a fabricated learner deficit.
7. **Delayed independent retention and genuinely unseen transfer.** A–F require meaningful separated learning opportunities and new source-authored speakers, evidential structures, commitment situations and contexts without answer-bearing hints. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; cosmetic substitutions and immediate coached repeats do not qualify.
8. **Reversible adaptive method and soft interest rotation.** E/F record feasible, assigned and **actually delivered** methods, equivalent or explicitly confounded tasks, baseline, rendered support, uncertainty, exploration, independent outcomes, missingness and topic diversity. Preserve protected listening/reading/writing/speaking/free production, due reviews, exit gates and neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar contexts; never assign a permanent learning-style or skepticism label.
9. **Exact-head reproducibility and bounded claims.** A–F must emit deterministic seed/clock, named engine HEAD, runnable command, descriptor/source/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, source-approved interpretations, first and assisted responses, render/event ledger, counterfactuals, delayed/unseen denominators and **PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED for each assertion**. Documentation CI alone is not an executable evaluator, reproducible product defect, or evidence of actual learning gains.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and reproducible command; real versioned CEFR-linked tasks and independently validated target-language/variety stance interpretations; source-effective speaker/evidence/commitment provenance; actual rendered stimuli and tutor assistance chronology; accessible construct-valid modalities; first-unaided evidence; meaningful delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen communicative transfer; comparable or transparently confounded instructional choices; protected skill/review/exit obligations; complete eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; replay/version/channel permutations; and per-assertion verdicts. If source validity, speaker provenance, trustworthy channel, authority or follow-up is missing, affected claims remain **UNSUPPORTED/unknown** while valid partial communication evidence is retained.

A stronger claim that adaptive epistemic communication instruction **improves durable independent learning** requires a separately reviewed multilingual, multivariety, accessibility-validated real-learner longitudinal study with credible comparison, attrition and subgroup-harm analysis, privacy review and reproducible independent outcomes. **A strong tutor teaches learners to express what is known, reported, inferred and still uncertain—without manufacturing certainty, facts or mastery.**
