**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. A within-turn repair is relevant only when the source task actually requires interpreting or producing it. If no source-valid task or adjudicable repair boundary exists, return NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED rather than inventing a speech norm or answer.

Represent each turn as a **versioned sequence of communicative moves**: fragment onset, abandonment/restart marker where justified, corrected span, scope of the correction, subsequent confirmation/clarification, turn end and recipient-relevant outcome. Preserve both the **original trace** and the **best source-supported final intended meaning**; a correction may replace a word, a number, an entire proposition, or nothing at all. Do not assume that the last recognized token always wins. A genuine uncertainty statement (e.g. “maybe Friday”) remains uncertain after a repair. A later authorized source update is a new event, not retroactive proof that an earlier independent response was wrong.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Corrected detail within one turn, not two commitments.** A source-authored voice message says, “The meeting is on Thursday—sorry, Friday at two.” Learner A independently reports **Friday at two**; learner B lists both days as confirmed; learner C requests clarification because the source audio is truncated immediately after “sorry”. In a matched task, the speaker instead says “Thursday or Friday; we haven't decided”, which does **not** license a Friday-only commitment. Judge the actual repair scope and accessible source evidence, not a hard-coded “last date wins” rule.

**B — Negation and condition after a restart.** An organizer says, “You can enter now—no, wait, you **can't** enter until the supervisor approves.” One learner relays permission to enter; another preserves the revised prohibition and approval condition. A matched source says, “You can enter now; no, wait, I meant you can enter through the side door,” correcting the route, **not** the permission. Score the source-supported final speech act, condition and recipient action separately. A single restart marker does not imply global negation.

**C — Benign hesitation, stutter and legitimate uncertainty.** In a validated variety-specific exchange, “I, I sent the form yesterday” communicates a completed action despite repetition; “I, uh, think I sent it” communicates uncertainty; “I sent—actually I **drafted** it” corrects completion status. A fluent but inaccurate learner collapses all three into “sent”; another preserves the distinct commitments. A speaker with a stutter or culturally typical filler is not automatically wrong, less capable or less independent. Where the source cannot resolve intent, a polite clarification is legitimate; latency is not a mastery gate.

**D — ASR normalization and construct-preserving access.** A consented listening item includes “Fif—sorry, **fifty** units”; ASR deletes the repair and outputs “fifteen units,” while another ASR version outputs “fifty units.” The source-authorized audio independently supports fifty. A learner who correctly hears fifty must not be marked wrong by the normalized transcript. A separate learner receives a text transcript because audio is inaccessible; this can support reading or instructional practice but cannot silently certify a source-required listening construct unless the source has validated an equivalent route. If original audio or approved alternative is unavailable, mark the listening adjudication UNSUPPORTED rather than guessing.

**E — Modeled repetition versus durable spontaneous repair.** In comparable separate sessions, HEAR_REPAIR_AND_RESTATE yields impressive immediate answers after a tutor plays and explains a complete repair, while SELF_REPAIR_ROLEPLAY yields slower, sometimes incorrect unaided first attempts. At later genuinely unseen independent tasks with new speakers, dates, numbers and interlocutor purposes, the roleplay condition yields more source-valid spontaneous repairs and correct recipient actions. Another learner shows the reverse pattern. Compare or disclose baseline, difficulty, feedback, modality, register/variety, accessibility, dose, topic familiarity and actual rendered cues. Revise the capability-local hypothesis with uncertainty; do not declare a permanent “auditory” or “speaking” learner.

**F — Offline replay, late cues and a real subsequent correction.** Two genuine offline sessions reuse a local event ID; one upload is a true replay; a tutor's completed correction is rendered before the learner's second answer but arrives on the server later; a speaker's separate **later** authorized message changes the meeting from Friday to Saturday; an evaluator version changes an accepted regional filler. A learner's first unaided Friday interpretation was correct **at that time**, while Saturday becomes correct after the later update. Deduplicate only session-qualified identical replays, reconstruct trusted event/render order, preserve task-world effective times and quarantine unresolved source/rubric versions. Receipt permutations must yield the same first-unaided evidence, assisted labels, source-valid outcomes, review debt and reversible next-method rationale.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **CEFR and source applicability.** A–F require actual versioned CEFR can-do, blueprint/prerequisites, source-authored task and answer space, target language/variety/register, speech-versus-text construct, validated rubric/evaluator, accessibility, assistance threshold, protected skills, reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Absent source-valid repair evidence is NOT_APPLICABLE/UNSUPPORTED, not fabricated mastery.
2. **Local repair scope and final intent.** A/B/C preserve original fragments while determining whether a repair replaces a date, number, route, proposition or nothing. Abandoned fragments are not automatically independent commitments. An unresolved repair boundary remains UNKNOWN/UNSUPPORTED; “last token wins” is not a valid universal rule.
3. **Meaning, conditions and recipient outcome.** A/B/C distinguish confirmed versus provisional details, permission versus prohibition, completed versus drafted actions, and legitimate uncertainty. Require the source-defined recipient to take the correct action or request clarification when warranted; fluent delivery alone does not pass.
4. **Disfluency is not deficient proficiency.** C/E do not treat repetitions, stutters, fillers, nonnative accent or unmeasured pauses as errors without a source-validated communicative reason. Evaluate language-specific pragmatic meaning and accessibility, not arbitrary English speech timing or a stereotype about fluent speakers.
5. **Actual stimulus, ASR and construct equivalence.** D/F distinguish original source speech, ASR hypotheses, normalization artifacts, actual delivered media and source-approved accessible alternatives. Unverified transcripts cannot overrule audio; text-only performance does not become listening/speaking mastery; unavailable access is not learner failure.
6. **First independent response and rendered tutor chronology.** D/E/F separate learner-authored first attempts, genuine self-correction, model-dependent repetition, retries, legitimate interlocutor stimuli and answer-bearing tutor cues actually rendered before response. Assigned-but-unrendered hints do not contaminate independence; later packet receipt cannot erase earlier exposure.
7. **Time-separated unseen independent transfer.** A–F require meaningful intervening time/material, new source-authored utterance surfaces and contexts, first-unaided interpretation or production, and recipient-relevant outcomes. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed and unseen probes; same-session echo, coached correction and missing follow-up cannot prove durable retention.
8. **Reversible methods, balanced skills/interests and replay invariance.** E/F log feasible, selected, assigned and actually rendered instructional choices, baseline evidence, confounds, later independent outcomes, uncertainty, exploration and next-choice rationale. Keep required receptive/productive CEFR evidence, review debt, prerequisites and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar contexts. True replay deduplication and receipt reordering must not change decisions; genuine later source updates can.
9. **Assertion-level audit and bounded claims.** A–F emit source/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, repair-span annotations and authority, trusted utterance/cue chronology, ASR/access uncertainty, first and assisted moves, final recipient outcome, delayed/unseen denominators, replay permutations, skill/review coverage and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED **for each assertion**. Documentation CI and synthetic PASS do not demonstrate shipped speech handling, causal learning gains, CEFR mastery or a real production defect.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and reproducible command, deterministic clock/seed, real versioned CEFR-linked source utterances and independently adjudicated repair boundaries, language/variety-aware rubric, consent-respecting trustworthy audio or source-validated accessible equivalents, actual cue-render chronology, first unaided and assisted response separation, recipient outcomes, genuinely unseen delayed independent transfer, comparable method/context/difficulty/access exposure, protected skill/review/exit requirements, eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators, receipt/version permutations and assertion-level verdicts. Without adjudicable source speech, valid access, trustworthy chronology or independent follow-up, the corresponding claim remains UNSUPPORTED/unknown.

A stronger claim that adapting repair instruction **improves durable independent communication** requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, multilingual/variety and accessibility adjudication, attrition and subgroup-harm analysis, privacy safeguards and ethically reviewed human evaluation. **A strong tutor helps learners understand and correct what a speaker ultimately means, without mistaking natural speech disfluency for lack of language ability.**
