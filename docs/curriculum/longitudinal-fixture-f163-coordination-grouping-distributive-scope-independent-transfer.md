## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Shared action is not two independent actions.** Fictional club message: **“Ana and Ben carried a box to the room.”** It establishes that Ana and Ben participated in carrying a box; it does **not** establish that each carried a separate box or that two boxes arrived. A recipient summary **“Ana brought one box and Ben brought another”** fails. **“Ana and Ben carried a box together”** is acceptable only if the reviewed context licenses joint carriage; otherwise **“Ana and Ben carried a box; the message does not say how they divided the work”** preserves the evidence. Counterfactual: **“Ana carried one box, and Ben carried another”** explicitly supports two separate boxes. Do not infer who physically held which side of a box.

**B — Explicit per-member versus shared totals.** Fictional classroom instructions: **“Each of the four students needs a pen and a notebook.”** A reviewed source establishes four pens and four notebooks as the minimum individual allocation (one of each per student), assuming no reusable sharing permitted in that task. A handoff requesting only **one pen and one notebook for the whole group** fails. Counterfactual: **“The four students need one shared poster and one marker for their group”** establishes one group-level poster and marker, not four of each. **“The students need pens and notebooks”** alone does not license exact counts. If the source has not stated whether resources may be shared, request clarification rather than inventing a distribution rule.

**C — Modifier attachment changes who qualifies.** Fictional club note: **“Invite the teachers and students with badges.”** Without additional context, **“with badges”** may modify only students or both teachers and students. A confident handoff **“Every teacher must have a badge”** fails; **“Do teachers also need badges, or only students?”** succeeds. Counterfactual: **“Invite all teachers, plus only students who have badges”** explicitly resolves the scope. A translation that silently resolves an ambiguous English attachment cannot be used as evidence of the learner's interpretation of the original.

**D — Coordinated tasks do not allocate roles.** Fictional study-group message: **“Mira and Leo will present the results and take notes.”** This does not necessarily specify whether both do both tasks, one presents while the other takes notes, or the pair shares duties. A message to a substitute assigning Mira to present and Leo to take notes as an established fact fails. **“Who is presenting, and who is taking notes?”** is appropriate. Counterfactual: the organizer adds **“Mira will present; Leo will take notes.”** A later handoff must preserve the explicit assignment and source revision, not retroactively claim it was contained in the earlier sentence.

**E — Delivered list boundaries, authorship and stable events.** Original fictional source:
**“Bring: (1) two red folders; (2) blue pens and notebooks for the helpers.”**
The reviewed oracle says **two** modifies red folders only; the number of blue pens/notebooks is unspecified. An ASR stream drops **“and”**, captions collapse item (1)/(2), translation incorrectly applies **two** to every item, or screen-reader order interleaves the list. Quarantine construct-changing delivery instead of penalizing a learner for a grouping distinction they could not access. At 10:00 a learner independently asks **“How many pens and notebooks?”**; at 10:01 the tutor renders **“two folders only; other quantities unknown”**; at 10:02 the learner copies that statement. Preserve the 10:00 FIRST_UNAIDED clarification, mark the 10:02 statement ASSISTED, and deduplicate replays by stable session-qualified IDs. Queued but never rendered help cannot contaminate earlier authorship. Corrected text does not certify unheard listening.

**F — Outcome-responsive method comparison without overfitting.** Fictional learner A gets immediate correct answers with MODIFIER_SCOPE_HIGHLIGHT_WITH_FADE but later, unaided, repeatedly assigns badge requirements to the wrong group in unseen community and study contexts. Several matched, actually delivered RECIPIENT_ASSIGNMENT_CLARIFICATION_ROLEPLAY opportunities precede better delayed first-unaided handoffs with different group sizes, attachment structures and recipients. Repeated comparable evidence may cautiously favor clarification practice; keep alternatives sampled and reassess if later retention declines. Learner B initially succeeds with TWO_COLUMN_GROUP_VS_EACH_CONTRAST, then independently confuses shared group materials with per-person supplies; lower confidence, bring review forward and explore alternatives. If the contrast condition uses two people and explicit **each** while the roleplay condition uses six people and ambiguous modifiers, infer **no method winner**. Merely changing a music-club example into a gaming-club example with the same list template is not unseen transfer. Missing follow-up is MISSING, not failure. Neutral and unfamiliar topics remain eligible.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Existing CEFR objective and independently reviewed oracle.** A–F require an existing versioned can-do, prerequisites, source/recipient/information gap, source-world/grouping oracle, language/variety/register, actually delivered accessible task, evaluator/rubric, first-unaided threshold, due review, delayed/unseen probes and unchanged exit gate. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED.
2. **Joint versus separate participation.** A rejects fabricated per-person box counts or individual task completions; accepts only a source-licensed collective claim, or an explicit statement that distribution is unknown.
3. **Per-member versus group totals.** B preserves **each** and explicitly shared quantities, rejects unsupported exact counts and asks when sharing/quantity rules are absent.
4. **Modifier attachment and clarification.** C distinguishes ambiguous conjunct scope from explicitly restricted groups. Targeted clarification is success under ambiguity; confident unsupported filtering fails.
5. **Task assignment and source revisions.** D does not invent which person did which task. Later explicit allocation supersedes uncertainty for subsequent handoffs without rewriting the earlier source.
6. **Actual delivery, first authorship and deduplication.** E compares original versus rendered ASR/captions/translation/screen-reader grouping, quarantines construct-changing differences, records actual help exposure, protects pre-help authorship and deduplicates stable events. Accessible reading cannot certify unheard listening.
7. **Controlled, uncertain, reversible adaptation.** F compares feasible actually delivered methods with matched group size, conjunct/attachment load, counts, language, modality, source completeness, topic, help, spacing and follow-up. Sparse, selective, contradictory or confounded evidence yields no winner; delayed regression lowers confidence and reopens exploration.
8. **Delayed first-unaided genuinely unseen transfer and soft interests.** Hinted grouping, copied lists, same-session repetitions and topic-only swaps cannot earn delayed/unseen mastery. Require a later distinct eligible session, changed recipient/context **and changed grouping/attachment structure**, independently reviewed oracle, first-unaided actionable handoff or justified clarification and explicit missingness. Preserve shared objectives, receptive/productive balance and neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar topic rotation.
9. **Honest implementation and efficacy boundary.** Passing documentation CI does not ship a grouping evaluator or adaptive tutor or demonstrate improved human retention. Require exact-head executable tests against real objectives, evidence of delivered method changes and later independent transfer versus a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, level enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic design/probe histories, not observed learner outcomes or executable QA. Preserve grouping scope, ambiguity, accessible delivery and explicit UNSUPPORTED gates until independent runtime and human-learning evidence exists.
