## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Quote versus paraphrase and invented wording.** The verified source records Nora saying, “I might arrive after six.” Learner A writes, in quotation marks, “I will arrive at seven,” falsely upgrading uncertainty and inventing exact wording. Learner B reports, “Nora said she might arrive after six,” preserving attribution and possibility. When asked for an exact quote, B reproduces the recorded words rather than presenting the paraphrase as verbatim. An unseen source changes “might” to “will not”; the learner must follow the actual source, not the practiced template.
- **B — Nested attribution and endorsement leakage.** A source-authored handoff states, “Sam said that Jo thinks the office is closed, but Sam has not checked.” Learner A tells a visitor, “The office is closed,” collapsing Jo's belief and Sam's report into verified fact. Learner B independently reports that **Sam says Jo believes** it is closed, but Sam has not confirmed it, and recommends checking before travel. A contrasting verified closure notice should be reported as confirmed, not automatically hedged. Preserve who knows, says, doubts or merely relays each proposition.
- **C — Shifted person, place and time anchors.** On Monday in Room A, Amira tells Ben, “I'll bring it here tomorrow.” On Wednesday in Room B, the learner must report to Cara that Amira said on Monday she would bring the item to Room A on Tuesday, without interpreting “here” as Room B or “tomorrow” as Thursday. A second versioned task specifies the object and destination only later; the learner must not fabricate missing information in the earlier report. Test natural target-language choices rather than requiring one English backshift pattern.
- **D — Contradictory transcript, accessible evidence and correction.** A caption reads “Don't send the draft,” while a verified accessible written message says “Do send the draft.” Without reliable provenance, learner A confidently quotes one as certain; learner B flags the conflict, names each source and requests confirmation. After an authorized, timestamped clarification from the original speaker, B relays the corrected instruction with the correction's time and does not retroactively rewrite the earlier ambiguous evidence. A missing speaker label or audio-only distinction cannot be counted as failed written comprehension.
- **E — Coached immediate winner reverses at independent follow-up.** In comparable early sessions, SOURCE_TO_REPORT_PAIRS yields polished immediate reports after visible completed examples; SPEAKER_CHAIN_MAPPING yields slower, imperfect first unaided reports. On distinct later sessions with unfamiliar speakers, nested sources, different registers and actionable recipient decisions, the mapping condition yields better first-attempt independent attribution and less false endorsement. Match or disclose baseline, source complexity, modality, dose, feedback, topic familiarity, variety and accessibility. Reduce confidence in the coached immediate winner, sample alternatives and record the **actually rendered** next method, not a permanent “reporting learner” type.
- **F — Interest overfit, missing follow-up and out-of-order telemetry.** A learner reports gaming dialogue accurately but falsely attributes a neutral community announcement and an unfamiliar clinic schedule. The listening source is inaccessible to a caption user; a delayed probe is eligible and offered but never rendered. Two separate real sessions reuse a local offline event ID; an answer-bearing tutor paraphrase rendered before the learner's response is received by telemetry afterward; one offline event has a conflicting source/rubric hash. Deduplicate only genuine session-qualified replays, quarantine unresolved versions and reconstruct trusted render chronology. Assistance, first-independent verdict, review debt, context rotation and reversible next-method rationale must be invariant under receipt-order permutations. Written success cannot certify required listening or speaking.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-valid CEFR applicability.** A–F require a real versioned can-do, level blueprint/prerequisites, language/variety, verified source utterance and provenance, addressee and purpose, rubric/evaluator, assistance policy, independent threshold, required skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Missing mapping or unsupported language-specific reporting analysis is UNSUPPORTED/NOT_APPLICABLE, not inferred proficiency.
2. **Quotation versus paraphrase fidelity.** A/D distinguish verifiable verbatim quotation from semantic paraphrase and from uncertain transcription. Reject invented exact words, altered certainty, silent transcript repair and plausible but source-inconsistent reporting. Accept a faithful, natural paraphrase without demanding word-for-word identity.
3. **Nested attribution and reporter endorsement.** B/E preserve every speaker/source link and separate the original person's belief from the reporter's belief or verified facts. False promotion of hearsay into fact fails even if the surface sentence is grammatical. Independently identify when direct verification supersedes hearsay.
4. **Perspective, polarity, modality and speech act.** A/C/D preserve who speaks to whom, person/place/time anchors, negation, uncertainty, obligations and the intended request/instruction. Do not require universal English backshift. A report that changes a warning into permission or shifts “tomorrow” to the report date fails the task-defined recipient outcome.
5. **First independent report and actually rendered cues.** A/D/F score the first learner-authored report using trusted render chronology. A tutor's completed paraphrase, answer-bearing speaker map or highlighted reporting transformation rendered beforehand makes the affected move assisted; selected-but-not-rendered instruction does not. Distinguish authentic source content from interlocutor coaching.
6. **Delayed genuinely unseen independent transfer.** A/B/E/F require separate real sessions, novel speakers, attribution chains, source modalities, perspectives and recipient decisions, first unaided report and observable communication outcome. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Same-session copying, assisted corrections and absent follow-up do not establish durable mastery.
7. **Comparable methods, accessibility and soft interests.** D/E/F match or disclose source reliability, complexity, language/variety, register, practice dose, feedback, modality, accessibility and topic familiarity. Preserve the shared CEFR objective, noncompensatory receptive/productive skill evidence, due reviews and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation. Interests change sampling probabilities only.
8. **Reversible adaptation and receipt-order invariance.** E/F record feasible alternatives, assigned/selected/**actually rendered** instruction, independent later outcomes, uncertainty, confounds, exploration and next-choice rationale. Sparse/conflicted evidence falls back to BALANCED_DEFAULT. Session-qualified deduplication, frozen versions and trusted render chronology produce identical assistance labels, reporting verdicts, review debt and next method across receipt permutations; quarantine unresolved conflicts.
9. **Assertion-level audit without efficacy inflation.** A–F emit exact source/task/rubric/evaluator IDs, frozen original and reported propositions, source-chain and deictic mappings, quote/paraphrase flags, first/final learner-authored reports, rendered cues, recipient outcomes, delayed/unseen denominators, confounds, replay permutations and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion. Documentation CI is not executable evaluator PASS, CEFR award or proof of improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, deterministic seed/clock, real source-resolved CEFR-linked reporting tasks, validated target-language/variety-specific quotation and attribution rubrics, versioned source chains and corrections, trusted cue render chronology, first unaided reports, observable recipient outcomes, completed delayed genuinely unseen transfer with denominators, comparable method/topic/modality/access exposure, protected skills/reviews, receipt-order permutations, reversible next-method traces and assertion-level verdicts. Missing reliable source, language-specific rubric, accessible channel, completed independent follow-up or trustworthy chronology remains UNSUPPORTED/unknown.

Any claim that adaptation **improves durable independent reporting** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, delayed unseen tasks, multilingual/variety and accessibility validation, human adjudication, privacy/subgroup-harm review and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor teaches the learner to relay what was actually said, by whom and with what certainty—without turning someone else's words into fact.**
