## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Abandoned numeric fragment versus explicit final quantity.** A library helper says, in independently reviewed actually delivered audio, “Bring fif— ... sorry, **fifteen** folders.” The source-world specifies exactly fifteen folders; the truncated “fif—” has **no recoverable standalone numerical value**. A learner independently tells a new volunteer to bring fifteen, or asks “Did you say fifteen?” if the correction was obscured. A transcript that silently converts “fif—” to “fifty” invents an unsupported first quantity; a transcript that highlights **fifteen** before the attempt leaks the answer. Do not grade an audio task using the text-only reconstruction.

**B — Restart that changes the location and action.** A fictional event organizer says, “Put the chairs in—actually, put them **outside** by the notice board.” The source-world verifies that “in—” is abandoned and the final actionable destination is outside. A learner must direct a new helper to the notice board, not infer “inside” from the abandoned fragment. If the recording cuts off before “outside,” the final action is **not recoverable**; targeted clarification earns appropriate communicative credit, not a forced location guess. Independently validate that actual TTS/rendered audio retains the restart and correction.

**C — Hesitation does not create a booking or promise.** A study-group speaker says, “We could, um, reserve the hall—no, let's **ask if it's available first**.” The learner independently distinguishes an unconfirmed possibility from the final **availability inquiry**. “Um” is not an affirmative commitment, and “reserve” in an abandoned proposal does not authorize a booking. A caption, menu or ASR output that edits away the “no” or labels the room “reserved” cannot support unaided listening or confirmed action. The source-world preserves the difference between asking, proposing, confirming and executing.

**D — First-unaided learner self-repair and unfamiliar recipient.** In a fictional club meeting, the learner tells an unfamiliar volunteer, “Meet at fi—sorry, **six**,” with no written answer, rehearsal or prior model. A blinded human listener records the first understood time or asks a sensible clarification. A final correct recipient action supports the original interaction can-do even if the learner hesitates; do not add a native-like fluency gate. If the listener only succeeds because a visible calendar already shows six, report context-supported communication rather than isolated auditory repair production. ASR-cleaned punctuation is not a human-recipient verdict.

**E — First-authorship, replay and accessibility chronology.** At 09:02 an unfamiliar audio instruction renders without answer-bearing captions; the learner first asks “Was that fifteen or fifty?” Record FIRST_UNAIDED_LISTENING and TARGETED_REPAIR_CLARIFICATION, not a wrong guess. At 09:03 an edited transcript displaying the corrected number and a segmented replay **actually render**; at 09:04 the learner answers fifteen. The later answer is ASSISTED and ANSWER_EXPOSURE; it cannot retroactively upgrade 09:02. A help button merely offered, but never rendered, is not exposure. Deduplicate a retried 09:04 event by stable session-qualified ID. An accessible transcript-first route is legitimate but must report its measured construct honestly.

**F — Longitudinal adaptation with contradictory later outcomes.** Learner A answers clean written transcripts correctly but fails later independent real-speech repair tasks. Across matched sessions, actually delivered FINAL_INTENT_RECIPIENT_ROLEPLAY is followed by improved first-unaided delayed transfer to new voices, repair scopes and neutral/unfamiliar topics. Cautiously increase its selection probability while still exploring listening alternatives. Learner B improves immediately after SLOW_TO_NATURAL_REPAIR_FADING but later misreads novel abandoned commitments; lower confidence and sample clarification practice. If one method used a clearer voice, shorter utterance, more hints, familiar topic, edited captions or more follow-up opportunities, claim **no causal winner**. A repeated audio clip, assisted repeat, new calendar day or topic swap alone is not unseen transfer. Missing delayed follow-up is missing, not failure or gain.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed CEFR objective and independent oracles.** A–F require an existing relevant versioned can-do, reviewed source-world/final-intent oracle, language/variety and delivered acoustic review, unchanged rubric, due review, delayed and unseen probes. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED; no new level gates.
2. **Abandoned fragment is not an invented answer.** A must preserve the explicit final quantity without completing “fif—” into a speculative number, and must distinguish first unaided acoustic interpretation from edited transcript leakage.
3. **Correction scope and signal availability.** B must apply the correction to the final recipient destination, validate the **actually delivered** restart, and accept targeted clarification when the final location is clipped or acoustically ambiguous.
4. **Proposal, hesitation and commitment boundaries.** C must not transform a filler or abandoned proposal into a reservation, promise or executed action; preserve source-world state and quarantine ASR/TTS repair drift.
5. **Independent recipient-facing production without fluency penalty.** D must capture first-unaided learner speech and unfamiliar recipient action/repair, separate contextual success from acoustic attribution and avoid accent/disability/native-fluency gates.
6. **First authorship, actual help and deduplication.** E must retain the earliest learner-authored response, contaminate only attempts following **rendered** help/answer exposure, report accessible text as its actual construct and deduplicate replayed event IDs.
7. **Matched, uncertainty-aware and reversible adaptation.** F may update instructional probabilities only from comparable **actually delivered** options and eligible later independent outcomes. Confounds, sparse data or contradictory outcomes prevent a declared winner; regression restores exploration.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer, balance and interests.** Require an eligible later distinct session with genuinely new reviewed speaker, repair realization and context, first-unaided recipient meaning/clarification and explicit follow-up missingness. Preserve listening, speaking, reading and writing balance and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar rotation; interests never alter objective or exit standards.
9. **Evidence boundary and protected scope.** This documentation fixture and generic CI are not executable audio QA, shipped adaptive instruction or proof of human retention. Require exact-head executable delivered-asset assertions, longitudinal outcome-driven method changes and independently reviewed delayed unaided transfer against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, A1–C2 enablement or auto-merge.
