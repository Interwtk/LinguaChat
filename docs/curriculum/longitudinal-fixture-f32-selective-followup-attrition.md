# F32 — Selective delayed follow-up cannot masquerade as instructional-method efficacy

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named engine HEAD. This is not a reproduced defect, a shipped adaptive-tutor capability, a causal estimate, or evidence of improved learning. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F24 guards against biased *assignment* of teaching options, F30 against assessment-calibration drift, and F31 against time-varying learner state. F32 addresses a different problem: **selection into observed delayed outcomes**. When learners who struggle, disengage, lose access, or receive extra help are less likely to complete a delayed independent probe, the successful *completers* are not necessarily representative of everyone offered a teaching method. A high score among observed returners must not become a method-efficacy claim or a claim of durable mastery for missing learners.

## Controlled setup

Resolve `goal-1` to one existing versioned CEFR can-do, one supported skill and a genuine independent delayed-retrieval and unseen-transfer route. Define two eligible instructional options: `concise_explanation` (A) and `worked_example` (B). Keep the required objective, prerequisites, assessment instrument/rubric, independent-evidence threshold, task demand, due reviews and coverage requirements constant. Interest sampling remains a separate soft context layer.

Inject a deterministic clock and seed. Construct an append-only synthetic ledger with stable learner, objective, skill, session, exposure, invitation, probe and event IDs; actual occurrence and upload times; method eligibility, offer, exposure and completion; **predeclared follow-up eligibility and due windows**; probe invitations, delivery/receipt where actually recorded, independent starts and completions; first-attempt/assistance provenance; actual retrieval delay and intervening practice; item/rubric versions and unseen transfer; observed access or scheduling barriers; explicit opt-out/withdrawal where recorded; and missingness reasons with provenance. Record assignment and follow-up probabilities **only if they were actually logged**. Never infer a learner's motivation, ability, fatigue or reason for nonreturn from silence alone.

Use a versioned *follow-up observation policy* that distinguishes `NOT_DUE`, `NOT_INVITED`, `INVITED_UNOBSERVED`, `INTERRUPTED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `OBSERVED_ASSISTED`, `OBSERVED_INDEPENDENT` and `UNKNOWN_PROVENANCE` as applicable. An explicitly observed wrong independent attempt is failure evidence for that attempt; a missing attempt is **not** automatically wrong, correct or a mastery pass. Preserve independently valid observations for the CEFR can-do while marking cross-method efficacy `SELECTION_BIASED`, `NOT_COMPARABLE` or `UNSUPPORTED` when attrition makes the contrast unreliable. Do not assume missing-at-random or invent response weights, bounds, counterfactuals or a universal minimum follow-up rate.

## Synthetic histories

- **A — Survivorship reversal.** Both methods are offered to comparable learners. After A, strong and struggling learners complete delayed probes; after B, struggling learners disproportionately do not return while successful learners complete. B's *completer-only* delayed accuracy exceeds A's. Report offer-to-independent-follow-up denominators, missingness imbalance and uncertainty; B must not win on the observed average alone.
- **B — Different access, not different ability.** Learners assigned B have documented device/offline access barriers when follow-ups are due, while A learners have reliable access. Preserve each observed independent result and distinguish delivery failure from a wrong response. Do not infer low skill or poor B efficacy from the absence of a probe; do not infer B superiority by excluding the missing learners.
- **C — Assisted returners.** A follow-up is completed independently; B follow-ups are more often completed after reminders that include examples, hints or answer exposure. B's apparent completion and correctness rates rise. Count assisted B responses separately and never reclassify them as delayed independent retrieval or unseen transfer.
- **D — Comparable follow-up controls.** Both options have matched eligible populations, instrument calibration, actual delay windows, access opportunity, invitation/receipt provenance and high but not necessarily complete independent follow-up. Compare only demonstrably comparable independent anchor observations; show both completion denominators and sensitivity to the remaining missingness. A provisional uncertainty-aware hypothesis may be possible, not automatic causal identification.
- **E — Contradictory nonreturn and return.** Early observed returners favor B but B has substantially more unobserved follow-ups; later balanced opportunities produce delayed independent evidence favoring A, with a contradictory outlier. The method hypothesis must remain revisable, avoid overconfidence from early selected returners and continue eligible alternative sampling.
- **F — Offline and duplicated follow-ups.** An offline independent attempt arrives after a newer upload; an invitation and its completion are duplicated; another follow-up has no reliable learner/exposure linkage and one due date has not yet arrived. Deduplicate stable IDs, order by actual event time and preserve unknown provenance. Do not double count completed probes, prematurely call `NOT_DUE` a dropout, or assign orphaned evidence to a method.

## Acceptance assertions

1. **Explicit denominator accounting.** For each option expose eligible, offered, exposed, due, invited, received, independently attempted, independently completed, assisted, interrupted, withdrawn and unobserved counts with mutually interpretable status definitions. Never substitute the number of completers for the number eligible for follow-up.
2. **No completer-only winner.** In A, the higher B score among observed returners cannot establish better method efficacy. Report attrition and independent follow-up distributions by option, uncertainty and `SELECTION_BIASED` or `UNSUPPORTED` as warranted.
3. **Missing is not a score.** In B, unreceived, undelivered, inaccessible, opted-out and otherwise missing probes cannot be imputed as success, failure or mastery. An observed incorrect unaided attempt remains an observed incorrect attempt.
4. **Assistance provenance survives follow-up.** In C, hints, examples, copied repairs and reminders containing instructional content remain visible; assisted correctness cannot masquerade as independent delayed retrieval or unseen-context transfer.
5. **Comparable anchors remain usable.** In D, retain genuinely independent, calibrated and delay-matched anchor outcomes with per-option sample sizes, completion rates, remaining exclusions and confidence. Do not claim randomization, ignorable missingness or causal efficacy without supporting design evidence.
6. **Hypotheses remain reversible.** In E, later comparable delayed independent outcomes can revise a provisional objective-and-skill-specific teaching hypothesis. Missing early outcomes do not create permanent learner labels; eligible exploration and balanced coverage continue.
7. **Idempotent event-time replay.** In F, deduplicate stable invitation/probe/event IDs, use occurrence rather than upload order, respect due windows, and quarantine orphaned or ambiguous records. Replaying the same ledger in different arrival orders yields identical denominators, comparability classifications and decisions.
8. **Unchanged CEFR and interest boundaries.** No missingness policy may lower can-do/mastery gates, erase overdue reviews, omit required productive skills or treat unavailable modalities as completed. Changing interest weights alone may vary examples but not follow-up status, selection classification or required neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar context rotation.
9. **Conservative uncertainty-aware fallback.** If independent follow-up is too selective, sparse or unverifiable for a method comparison, return `SELECTION_BIASED`/`UNSUPPORTED`, preserve valid individual evidence and use the shared balanced curriculum with alternative sampling. Do not fabricate attrition corrections or assert durable independent improvement.

## Required evidence and release gate

For A–F report the **exact tested engine HEAD**, executable command, deterministic seed/clock, versioned policy, CEFR objective and supported skill, complete synthetic ledger, method eligibility/exposure and follow-up due/invitation/receipt/completion denominators, actual retrieval delays, assessment calibration, first-attempt/assistance provenance, independent retrieval and unseen transfer, missingness reasons and provenance, selection/comparability decisions with confidence, due-review and skill-coverage debt, and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED for every assertion. Include a completer-only reversal, an access-barrier case, assisted returners, matched follow-up controls, a later hypothesis reversal and offline idempotent replay.

If the engine lacks reliable follow-up status, exposure linkage or independent-outcome provenance, report **UNSUPPORTED** rather than infer method efficacy from completers. A deterministic PASS would prove a software invariant only. Claims that personalized teaching improves durable independent learning require separately reviewed longitudinal learner evidence versus a balanced baseline, comparable opportunities and assessments, independently measured delayed retrieval and unseen transfer, explicit attrition analysis, calibrated uncertainty and unchanged CEFR coverage.
