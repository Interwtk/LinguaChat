## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Gapped clause and incorrect role recovery.** The source-authored order record says “Mina ordered tea, and Leo coffee.” Learner A claims Leo **served** coffee; learner B independently reconstructs “Leo ordered coffee,” preserving the shared predicate and the distinct agent/item roles. A later unseen source says “Mina served tea, and Leo coffee”: the same surface pattern now licenses **served**, not **ordered**. Reject a memorized expansion that ignores the actual antecedent.
- **B — Fragment with two plausible antecedents.** The conversation first asks “Can you attend the review or send the notes?” and receives “On Thursday.” Neither the task nor the calendar establishes whether Thursday modifies attendance or sending. Learner A chooses one without evidence; learner B asks “Do you mean attend on Thursday or send the notes then?” After a versioned follow-up explicitly identifies sending, the learner should recover that action. A second task with only one available action should be answered without unnecessary clarification.
- **C — Response ellipsis with polarity and speaker commitment.** The source says “Ari hasn't submitted the form.” Bea replies “Neither have I.” Learner A reports Bea submitted; learner B independently reports that neither Ari nor Bea has submitted. In a contrasting dialogue “Ari submitted. So did Bea,” both submitted. In a separate question “Who has not submitted?” the learner must not treat an omitted predicate as permission to reverse negation or switch speakers. Validate target-language-specific agreement, polarity particles and natural fragment forms.
- **D — Deictic fragment, inaccessible antecedent and safe repair.** A staff member says “Use the smaller one,” but the task supplies two unlabelled containers of equal apparent size in text, while the spoken channel includes a demonstrative gesture absent from captions. Learner A guesses a container; learner B asks for an accessible label or measurement. Once a source-authored written size comparison is supplied, the learner selects correctly without a tutor answer cue. A missing gesture cannot be scored as failed text comprehension or as evidence of oral mastery; the task must be accessible and the reference licensed.
- **E — Coached immediate winner reverses at independent follow-up.** In comparable early sessions, ELLIPSIS_EXPANSION_PAIRS produces polished immediate expansions after visible models; DIALOGUE_FRAGMENT_REPAIR produces slower, imperfect first attempts. On genuinely separate later sessions with unfamiliar speakers, altered predicates, novel omission types and recipient outcomes, the dialogue condition yields stronger first-try unaided recovery and appropriate ambiguity questions. Match or disclose baseline, task complexity, source completeness, feedback, dose, modality, register, variety, topic familiarity and accessibility. Decrease confidence in the coached immediate winner, keep exploring alternatives and log the **actually rendered** next method rather than assigning an “ellipsis learner” identity.
- **F — Familiar-topic overfit, absent follow-up and replay disorder.** A learner expands fragments correctly in familiar game-chat but fails a neutral community schedule and an unfamiliar workplace handoff. A listening-only antecedent is unavailable to a caption user; a delayed probe is eligible and offered but never rendered. Two real sessions reuse an offline local event ID; an answer-bearing tutor expansion rendered before the learner response arrives in telemetry afterward; an offline record has a conflicting source/rubric hash. Deduplicate only genuine session-qualified replays, quarantine unresolved versions and reconstruct trusted render chronology. Assistance, independent evidence, review debt, context rotation and reversible next-method rationale must be invariant under receipt-order permutations. Written success never certifies required listening/speaking.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-valid CEFR applicability.** A–F require a real versioned can-do, blueprint/prerequisites, target language/varieties, source-authored discourse and licensed omission, recipient purpose/outcome, validated rubric/evaluator, assistance policy, threshold, required skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Missing mapping or unsupported language-specific analysis yields UNSUPPORTED/NOT_APPLICABLE, never invented proficiency.
2. **Overt and omitted information separately scored.** A/C preserve overt words and speaker roles while recovering only antecedent-licensed missing material. A fluent expansion with the wrong predicate, agent or item fails even if grammatically plausible; correct copied wording without discourse recovery is insufficient.
3. **Ambiguity-sensitive recovery versus clarification.** B/D distinguish uniquely licensed, multiply plausible and unavailable antecedents. Independently recover when supported; ask a task-appropriate question when genuinely ambiguous; do not reward fabricated certainty or indiscriminate clarification when the source resolves the omission.
4. **Polarity, scope, attribution and recipient outcome.** A/C/D preserve negation, quantifier/scope where present, speaker commitments and task-defined recipient action. Reject wrong-agent, reversed-polarity and invented-container outcomes. Later source updates are versioned and never retroactively relabel an earlier response.
5. **First independent move and actual cue exposure.** D/F score the first learner-authored recovery or clarification using trusted render chronology. A tutor expansion, highlighted antecedent or completed reply rendered before it makes the affected move assisted; selected-but-never-rendered instruction does not. Distinguish legitimate task stimulus from answer-bearing interlocutor coaching.
6. **Delayed genuinely unseen independent transfer.** A/E/F require distinct real sessions, novel omission forms, antecedents, speakers and contexts, first unaided response and observable addressee outcome. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Same-session restatement, copied models, missing follow-up and coached correction never prove durable mastery.
7. **Comparable methods, accessibility and soft interests.** D/E/F match or disclose ambiguity, difficulty, modality, variety/register, feedback, practice dose, source completeness, topic familiarity and accessibility. Preserve the same CEFR objective, noncompensatory receptive/productive skill evidence, due reviews and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation. Interests adjust probabilities, not eligibility.
8. **Reversible adaptation and receipt-order invariance.** E/F record feasible alternatives, selected/assigned/**actually rendered** instruction, independent later outcomes, uncertainty, confounds, exploration and next-choice rationale. Sparse or conflicted evidence uses BALANCED_DEFAULT. Session-qualified deduplication, frozen versions and trusted render chronology yield identical assistance labels, omission verdicts, review debt and next choice across receipt permutations; quarantine unresolved conflicts.
9. **Assertion-level audit without efficacy inflation.** A–F emit exact source/task/rubric/evaluator IDs, frozen overt/omitted/alternative propositions, first/final learner-authored moves, rendered cues, recipient outcomes, delayed/unseen denominators, confounds, replay permutations and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion. Documentation CI is not executable evaluator PASS, CEFR award or proof of improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, deterministic seed/clock, real source-resolved CEFR-linked discourse tasks, validated target-language/variety-specific omission rubrics, versioned antecedents and ambiguity, trusted cue render chronology, first unaided recovery/clarification, observable recipient outcomes, completed delayed genuinely unseen transfer with denominators, comparable method/topic/modality/access exposure, protected skills/reviews, receipt-order permutations, reversible next-method traces and assertion-level verdicts. Missing source mapping, reliable antecedent, accessible channel, completed independent follow-up or trustworthy chronology remains UNSUPPORTED/unknown.

Any claim that adaptation **improves durable independent understanding of omitted meaning** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, delayed unseen tasks, multilingual/variety and accessibility validation, human adjudication, privacy/subgroup-harm review and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor teaches learners to recover what context supports—and to ask when it does not.**
