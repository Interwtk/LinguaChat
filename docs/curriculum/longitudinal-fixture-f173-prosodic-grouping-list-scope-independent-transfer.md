## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Spoken negation boundary with opposite recipient actions.** A reviewed event-desk source-world distinguishes **“No, passes are required”** (rejecting a preceding suggestion; passes required) from **“No passes are required”** (passes not required). Deliver independently reviewed audio without punctuation, subtitles, a visible script or an answer-bearing prior model. The learner independently advises a new visitor whether to bring a pass. The source-world includes the preceding question and verifies that any prosodic cues are actually audible. If the recording cannot distinguish the two utterances, accept **“Do you mean we need passes, or that we don't?”** and mark ambiguity; do not infer a comma from the intended transcript.

**B — List grouping cannot be manufactured by punctuation.** A fictional café offers either **(soup or salad) plus bread** or **soup or (salad plus bread)**. The spoken request “soup or salad and bread” can be underdetermined; source-world menu rules, real phrasing and an independent listener review determine whether a particular recording disambiguates it. If not, the learner asks **“Does bread come with either choice, or only with the salad?”** A punctuation-inserted ASR transcript or a preselected menu graphic may give the correct action through text/context, but cannot earn unaided auditory grouping evidence. Do not claim every pause yields one fixed Boolean scope.

**C — Relative-clause grouping and variety.** In a fictional club, the distinction between **“The volunteers who registered early received badges”** (potentially restrictive) and **“The volunteers, who registered early, received badges”** (potentially nonrestrictive) changes which volunteers are asserted to have registered early. Independently review source-world membership and whether selected speakers actually communicate the intended interpretation through their spoken phrasing. Punctuation, syntax and context may still dominate; legitimate varieties and delivery differences must be preserved. A TTS take that omits the intended boundary or an ASR transcript that invents commas is quarantined rather than graded as acoustic evidence.

**D — Recipient-facing speaking and repair.** A learner gives a new volunteer a spoken instruction grouping two task choices, with no on-screen written punctuation or rehearsal. A blinded unfamiliar listener must either execute the intended grouping or ask a sensible clarification. Record the learner's first unaided utterance, delivered signal and listener response. If the listener understands only because a visible menu supplies the parse, count contextual communicative success where the existing rubric permits, **not** isolated prosodic-production mastery. Do not require native-like pause timing or treat ASR punctuation as a human-listener judgment.

**E — Help chronology, accessibility and replay.** At 09:02 an unfamiliar audio instruction plays with no caption; the learner first asks **“Is the bread included with both choices?”** Record FIRST_UNAIDED_LISTENING and TARGETED_GROUPING_CLARIFICATION. At 09:03 a punctuation-rich transcript and segmented replay actually render; at 09:04 the learner repeats the correct grouping. Mark the later response ASSISTED and ANSWER_EXPOSURE, not an upgrade of 09:02. A help control offered but never rendered cannot contaminate the first attempt. Deduplicate a retried 09:04 event by stable session-qualified ID. Screen-reader or text-first access is valid, but report the actually measured construct.

**F — Longitudinal outcome-responsive choice with a new voice.** Learner A copies printed commas accurately but fails later unaided instructions from new talkers. Across matched, actually rendered sessions, CONTEXT_AND_GROUPING_TRIANGULATION precedes improved delayed independent recipient actions in neutral and unfamiliar settings; cautiously raise its probability while retaining alternative listening practice. Learner B initially improves after REVIEWED_PHRASE_GROUP_LISTENING but later fails novel list scopes, so reduce confidence and explore clarification roleplay. If one method used a clearer voice, easier syntax, more help, familiar topics or more follow-up opportunities, infer **no causal winner**. A trained clip, immediate assisted repeat, calendar-day rollover or mere topic swap does not prove delayed unseen transfer; missing follow-up is missing, not failure or gain.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed CEFR and reviewed source-world.** A–F require an existing relevant can-do, reviewed source-world/grouping oracle, valid language/variety and actual audio, first-unaided rubric, due review, delayed/unseen probe and unchanged thresholds. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED.
2. **Audio-dependent polarity and action.** A requires a delivered, discriminable grouping and independently authored recipient advice without answer-bearing punctuation; if acoustic distinction is unsupported, targeted clarification wins over forced polarity guessing.
3. **Underdetermined list scope.** B separates genuinely audible grouping from context/menu/ASR punctuation and accepts accurate clarification when “or/and” attachment remains ambiguous; do not invent a universal pause rule.
4. **Clause attachment and legitimate varieties.** C checks source-world quantification, reviewed actual phrase cues, TTS boundary drift, ASR punctuation invention and valid pronunciation variation; no text-only claim of acoustic success.
5. **Independent recipient-facing production.** D requires feasible first-unaided spoken grouping, blinded unfamiliar listener action/repair and honest attribution of contextual cues; no ASR-only or native-accent graduation gate.
6. **First authorship, actual help and event identity.** E preserves the earliest learner-authored move, marks only prior **rendered** help as contamination, tracks transcript/caption accessibility as a distinct construct and deduplicates retries.
7. **Matched, reversible adaptation.** F compares only actually delivered feasible options under matched objective, syntax, source-world ambiguity, voice/signal, context, help, spacing and follow-up. Sparse, confounded or contradictory outcomes yield no winner; regression lowers confidence and restores exploration.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer, balance and soft interests.** Require a later eligible distinct session with genuinely new reviewed voice and grouping/context realization, first-unaided recipient meaning or clarification and explicit missingness. Preserve required receptive/productive skills and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation; interest preferences never change can-do or exit evidence.
9. **Implementation and efficacy boundary.** A documentation commit or passing generic CI is not executable acoustic QA, shipped longitudinal adaptation or proof of human retention. Require exact-head executable assertions on delivered assets, outcome-driven instructional changes and independently reviewed delayed unaided transfer against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, level enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic acceptance histories, not observed learner outcomes or executable QA.
