## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Clean meaning versus masked color.** Reviewed clean utterance: “Bring the **green** folder to the desk.” Actually rendered degraded audio masks the color with background chatter; independent signal reviewers cannot recover it. A learner who asks “Which color?” shows calibrated clarification; a confident green answer based on a visible green folder is not unaided listening. Clean replay later supports an **assisted** answer only. In a separate matched intelligible version, correct unaided green recognition is valid.

**B — Lost negation.** Source-world: “The meeting is **not** on Friday,” but actual phone-channel playback drops `not`. The learner asks “Did you say it is or isn't on Friday?” and does not schedule anyone yet. ASR/transcript restoration of the clean source negation is answer leakage. If a different reviewed asset preserves audible negation, unaided interpretation may count; grade the **delivered signal**, not the script.

**C — Narrowband numeric ambiguity.** A fictional librarian says “**fourteen**,” but a reverberant low-bandwidth recording makes fourteen versus forty unresolved for independent reviewers. The learner requests the digits before sending a volunteer to a shelf. A confident forty cannot be counted as language incompetence when acoustic evidence is insufficient. On-screen digits before the first attempt leak the answer; higher volume does not necessarily restore missing phonetic information.

**D — Communicative repair without false audio mastery.** In a fictional volunteer call, the learner independently says “I heard east entrance, but the last word was covered. Could you repeat the entrance?” A new interlocutor turn then **actually renders** “east entrance by the garden.” The learner relays the now-confirmed location to an uninformed recipient. A valid existing spoken-interaction can-do may credit this clarification; the confirmed later meaning is **not** unaided comprehension of the obscured first audio.

**E — First authorship, failed playback and accessible routes.** At 09:02 noisy audio renders; the first unaided learner response is “Was that fourteen or forty?” Preserve FIRST_UNAIDED_LISTENING and TARGETED_CLARIFICATION. At 09:03 noise-reduced audio and answer-bearing caption **actually render**; at 09:04 the learner says fourteen. The later answer is ASSISTED and ANSWER_EXPOSURE, never a retroactive rewrite of 09:02. A visible but unused help button is not exposure. Deduplicate a retried 09:04 event by session-qualified ID. If 09:02 playback failed, record SIGNAL_UNUSABLE, not a scored first attempt. Transcript-first accessibility remains valid under its **actual** measured construct.

**F — Reversible adaptation and acoustic confounding.** Learner A handles clean audio but guesses on acoustically recoverable unfamiliar noisy clips. Across matched sessions, actually delivered CLEAN_TO_NOISY_GRADUAL_FADING and TARGETED_MISSING_WORD_CLARIFICATION are compared with BALANCED_DEFAULT. Improved later first-unaided recipient decisions/clarifications on **new voices, channels and neutral/unfamiliar contexts** cautiously raise one method's probability, retaining uncertainty and alternative sampling. Learner B initially improves on rehearsed enhanced clips but later invents negation on unseen clipped audio; lower confidence and explore MEANING_BEFORE_ACTION_CHECK. If one method used clearer signal, better device, captions, louder playback, more hints, familiar topics or more follow-up opportunities, declare **no causal winner**. Same waveform with a different masker, a calendar boundary, assisted re-answer or missing follow-up is not durable unseen transfer.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed CEFR and independent oracles:** A–F bind existing versioned can-dos, reviewed source-world and **actually rendered** signal, unchanged rubrics and due/delayed/unseen probes; otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED. No hearing test or new gate.
2. **Recoverability before grading:** A separates clean truth from what was audible; masked content is not learner incompetence and contextual guessing cannot prove unaided acoustic mastery.
3. **Negation and non-invention:** B rejects inferred/ASR-restored negation as proof, credits targeted confirmation and blocks unjustified recipient scheduling.
4. **Channel-specific distinctions:** C validates actual transformed signal and independent alternative interpretations; digits clarification is valid, louder volume is not assumed to restore missing data.
5. **Repair versus recognition:** D credits recipient-facing clarification under the correct existing interaction objective, separating later confirmed meaning from first unaided degraded-audio recognition.
6. **Chronology, rendered help, playback and accessibility:** E preserves first authorship, marks only actually rendered help, deduplicates retries, excludes playback failures and reports accessible routes honestly.
7. **Matched reversible adaptation:** F updates probabilities only for comparable **actually delivered** methods and eligible later independent outcomes; confounds, sparse or contradictory data prevent causal-winner claims.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer and soft interests:** Require a genuinely later distinct session, new reviewed voice/channel/utterance and first-unaided recipient action/clarification; missing follow-up is missing. Preserve skill balance and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar contexts without changing CEFR standards.
9. **Evidence and protected scope:** Generic CI and this synthetic fixture do not prove executable acoustic QA, shipped adaptation or human retention. Require exact-head executable source/signal/delivery assertions, outcome-driven longitudinal changes and independently reviewed delayed unaided transfer against balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, A1–C2 enablement or auto-merge.
