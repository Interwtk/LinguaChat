# F47 — Cross-level CEFR progression cannot inherit lower-level mastery as higher-level mastery

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a reproduced defect, released level, validated adaptive tutor, causal effect or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. The existing B1 blueprint defines B1 give_an_opinion as stating an opinion with a reason; the B2 blueprint defines B2 develop_and_defend_opinion as stating an opinion with reasons **and sustaining it under a counter-argument**, explicitly depending on B1 give_an_opinion. The B1 compare_options_with_reasons → B2 weigh_advantages_and_disadvantages relationship provides a second boundary. F47 isolates **false cross-level evidence promotion**: common vocabulary, topic, learner identity, or communicative intent must not cause B1 performance to satisfy a distinct B2 rubric. Prior-level success may inform readiness and scaffolding, not waive the target-level independent evidence gate.

## Controlled setup

Resolve the two pairs against **actual versioned B1 and B2 blueprint can-do identifiers, prerequisites, evidence contracts and rubrics**, rather than matching bare intent strings or UI labels. Freeze both levels' evaluators, exit rules, support classifications and task demands. Use a deterministic clock/seed, versioned cross_level_evidence_boundary_policy, immutable synthetic events across genuinely distinct learning sessions and a balanced comparator. This is a *design harness*; do not open B1 or B2, or claim either is currently accessible to learners.

For the opinion pair, make B1 demand a reasoned stance, while B2 independently requires sustaining/defending the stance in response to a meaningful counterargument. For the comparison pair, B1 requires comparison and justified choice; B2 independently requires weighing advantages and disadvantages with relevant trade-offs. These descriptions identify **different evidence targets**, not permission to invent new thresholds. Bind every assertion to the frozen source rubrics and versioned CEFR mappings before execution; if a required target cannot be resolved, mark it UNSUPPORTED.

Record stable learner, level-qualified can-do, rubric, prerequisite, episode/run/session/event/probe IDs; objective and evaluator versions; task demand, item novelty and context family; offered/assigned/**verified-delivered** instruction; first-attempt independent quality; hints/models/retries; recognized versus free-produced responses; actual elapsed delayed retrieval and unfamiliar-context transfer; evidence source and its original level; learner choice and interests; eligible/completed/missing follow-up; trusted occurrence/upload times and data-use lineage. Preserve an explicit separation between **prerequisite readiness**, **target-level mastery evidence**, **uncertain objective-specific method hypothesis**, and **level release/exit authorization**. Missing modality or evidence stays unknown, not failed or passed.

Predeclare CROSS_LEVEL_PROMOTION_BLOCKED, PREREQUISITE_ONLY, TARGET_RUBRIC_UNMET, ASSISTED_TARGET, RUBRIC_VERSION_MISMATCH, WRONG_LEVEL_ALIAS, TRANSFER_NOT_DEMONSTRATED, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, FOLLOWUP_UNKNOWN and UNSUPPORTED. Never infer a learner type or an unseen counterfactual.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — B1 mastery misread as B2 defense.** The learner has valid, independent B1 give_an_opinion evidence and later recalls the same reasoned opinion fluently. No counterargument was posed. The B2 screen shares the word “opinion” and the policy attempts to promote the B1 result. Preserve B1 mastery/readiness, but emit PREREQUISITE_ONLY and CROSS_LEVEL_PROMOTION_BLOCKED for B2; no B2 independent defense or exit credit.
- **B — Strong prerequisite, legitimate acceleration of support.** Several distinct valid B1 opinion occasions and delayed transfer support readiness. On a new B2 defense task, the tutor may offer a briefer explanation and fewer *optional* models, then sample alternatives. The B2 learner fails to defend the stance independently. Preserve adaptive support decisions but not B2 mastery; provide recovery and due review without downgrading already valid B1 evidence.
- **C — Assisted B2 answer copied into independent evidence.** A learner produces a strong-looking counterargument response after viewing a worked example and retrying. A later independent B1 opinion remains valid. Mark the B2 occasion ASSISTED_TARGET; no copied model, retry or B1 performance can satisfy B2 independent production or delayed transfer. Retain useful practice and future probe eligibility.
- **D — Method works for B1, not necessarily B2.** Comparable later unaided B1 outcomes favor concise explanation for giving an opinion; B2 defense probes after that same method are sparse, assisted or missing. The policy tries to copy B1 method confidence and mastery to B2. Preserve the B1-specific uncertain hypothesis, mark B2 FOLLOWUP_UNKNOWN/UNSUPPORTED, use balanced feasible exploration and collect genuinely independent B2 outcomes before making a B2-specific ranking.
- **E — Similar language, distinct trade-off demand.** A B1 compare_options_with_reasons response selects an option with two qualities and a reason; a B2 weigh_advantages_and_disadvantages task demands weighing competing benefits, costs and trade-offs. Reused topic, phrases and item family must not convert B1 comparison evidence into B2 trade-off evidence. If an actual B2 response independently meets its own frozen rubric, retain it separately without retroactively changing the B1 score or weakening B2 thresholds.
- **F — Alias collision, offline replay and rubric drift.** Two levels share an unqualified intent alias; an offline B1 event arrives after a newer B2 attempt, a valid B2 event is uploaded twice, and an older rubric version is replayed after a revision. Route by original level-qualified objective and rubric, never by display label, latest UI level or arrival order. Deduplicate by stable ID, quarantine mismatched/contaminated evidence and preserve legitimate independent B1 and B2 outcomes. Two replay permutations must produce the same separate ledgers, review debt and provisional teaching decisions.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Level-qualified provenance.** For A–F output the exact versioned B1 and B2 can-do IDs, CEFR mapping, prerequisites, frozen rubrics, evaluator versions, actual instructional delivery, task demands, source-level evidence and separate mastery/teaching/availability states. Unqualified intent strings are insufficient identity.
2. **No automatic upward promotion.** In A, independent B1 opinion/retrieval, even when excellent, can satisfy a verified prerequisite but cannot satisfy B2 counterargument defense, unfamiliar B2 transfer, delayed B2 retrieval or level exit without target-specific evidence.
3. **Readiness can affect teaching, not the standard.** In B, strong prior-level evidence may justify bounded, reversible changes in explanation, scaffold, pacing or review ordering; the B2 can-do, independent gates, rubric and due-review obligations stay unchanged. B2 failure does not erase valid B1 mastery.
4. **Assistance is not independent cross-level credit.** In C, model exposure, retries and tutor-provided defense cannot become independent B2 evidence through task relabeling or later B1 success. First unaided and assisted outcomes remain separately attributable.
5. **Method hypotheses remain capability-scoped.** In D, evidence that a teaching option predicts later independent B1 retention does not prove it is superior for B2. Withhold high-confidence B2 method ranking until comparable B2-specific delayed unaided retrieval and unfamiliar transfer exist; keep balanced alternatives.
6. **Demand and rubric distinctions survive shared language.** In E, common topic, item family, lexical overlap or communicative purpose cannot collapse B1 comparison into B2 weighing, nor B1 stance into B2 defense. Credit actual target-level independent performance only against the target's unchanged versioned evaluator; record unknowns rather than fabricating outcomes.
7. **Replay, namespace and version integrity.** In F, trusted event/upload chronology, stable IDs, source-level namespace, item/model exposure, rubric version and completed-run provenance yield identical B1/B2 ledgers and review/decision outputs under duplicate or reordered replay. Unsupported migration between rubric versions must be quarantined, not silently rescored.
8. **Shared curriculum, interests and accessibility.** Across A–F retain fixed CEFR prerequisites/objectives/exit gates, receptive/productive opportunities, due reviews, exploration and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation. Interests affect context probabilities only; learner preference does not imply mastery. Unsupported listening/speaking remains UNAVAILABLE; no phantom modality evidence or fixed learning-style classification.
9. **Conservative release and evidence separation.** Without resolved real level-qualified can-dos, comparable verified instruction, target-specific independent first attempts, actual delayed retrieval, unseen-context transfer, valid rubric versions and complete follow-up denominators, report higher-level mastery/method superiority UNSUPPORTED while retaining narrower valid lower-level observations. A synthetic PASS proves only a software invariant, not human efficacy or permission to release levels.

## Required evidence and release gate

For A–F publish the **exact tested engine HEAD**, executable command, injected clock/seed, immutable ledger, frozen source blueprint/rubric/evaluator versions and explicit prerequisite mapping, full first-attempt and assistance provenance, source-versus-target-level evidence, verified delivery, real delayed independent retrieval and unseen transfer, follow-up eligible/completed/missing counts, both replay orders, reason codes, review/skill/context coverage and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED for each assertion. Include a credible balanced teaching comparator for method-benefit claims; do not invent B2 evidence from B1, assignment probabilities, outcomes, confidence percentages or unimplemented modalities.

Any claim of durable learner-specific benefit requires separately reviewed longitudinal evidence against a balanced baseline, with genuinely later independent target-level retrieval/transfer and ethically reviewed human evaluation. **A prerequisite can prepare a learner for the next level; it cannot certify the next level.**

**Boundary:** curriculum acceptance documentation only. No engine, Auth, cloud, infrastructure, production, Supabase, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge changes. Integration/watchdog remains the sole owner-action aggregator.
