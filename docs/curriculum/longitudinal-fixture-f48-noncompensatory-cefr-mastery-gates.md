# F48 — CEFR graduation cannot compensate a missing required capability with unrelated strength

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a reproduced defect, released CEFR level, implemented adaptive policy, demonstrated mastery or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F47 blocks upward promotion of lower-level evidence to higher-level objectives. F48 isolates **noncompensatory exit gates within a level**: strong performance, engagement, method preference or apparent fluency in one required capability cannot erase an unfulfilled required can-do, an overdue independent retrieval, a missing skill-family opportunity or a failed prerequisite. Adaptation may alter instruction and sequence; it cannot turn a weighted average, XP total, or model-assisted answer into the fixed shared CEFR graduation rule.

## Controlled setup

Resolve a real versioned CEFR level and its **actual source blueprint**: required can-do IDs, prerequisites, rubric/evaluator versions, evidence requirements, independent retrieval/transfer rules, due-review semantics, coverage requirements and exit predicate. Prefer an existing B1 or B2 blueprint for inspection, but do not open the level or invent a universal count, weight or passing threshold. If the blueprint does not define a purported mandatory gate, do not create one for this fixture: record `UNSUPPORTED` and request an explicit curricular decision. Keep level and source-policy versions frozen for comparisons.

Use an injected clock and seed, immutable synthetic events from distinct learning sessions, fixed task difficulty and item novelty, an unchanged independent evaluator, and a balanced instructional comparator. Represent each required objective as a **separate** ledger with `met`, `unmet`, `pending`, `invalid` or `unknown` according to the source contract; represent level exit as a separately derived decision. Do not infer that every CEFR skill is currently implemented: unavailable listening or speaking must be `UNAVAILABLE`, not passed or silently omitted. Distinguish source-mandated gates from desirable balance diagnostics.

Record stable learner, level-qualified objective, prerequisite, session/run/event/probe, assessment-item and rubric IDs; trusted occurrence/upload timestamps; first unaided quality; hint/model/retry exposure; actual delivered instructional option; supported skill and context; independent production/recognition; due review and actual elapsed delay; unseen-context transfer; eligibility/completion/missing follow-up; completion-only provenance; learner agency/interest; and policy/version lineage. Keep **instructional-method hypothesis**, **objective mastery**, **review debt**, **skill coverage** and **level-exit eligibility** independent. Unknown evidence must not become either success or failure by arithmetic.

Predeclare `REQUIRED_OBJECTIVE_UNMET`, `NONCOMPENSATORY_EXIT_BLOCKED`, `REVIEW_DUE`, `ASSISTED_ONLY`, `PREREQUISITE_PENDING`, `COVERAGE_UNKNOWN`, `FOLLOWUP_UNKNOWN`, `RUBRIC_MISMATCH`, `EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED`, `UNAVAILABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. An optional diagnostic aggregate may be displayed only alongside, never in place of, every source-mandated gate.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — One excellent capability hides a required gap.** A learner produces excellent independent, novel, delayed responses for several required written objectives and earns high engagement, but one different blueprint-required can-do has no qualifying independent evidence. A weighted score would cross an apparent graduation threshold. Retain every valid success and the gap separately; block exit with `REQUIRED_OBJECTIVE_UNMET`/`NONCOMPENSATORY_EXIT_BLOCKED`. Do not invent a threshold or subtract points from unrelated objectives.
- **B — Easy recognition crowds out required production.** An adaptive tutor observes fast repeated recognition and preferentially schedules more recognition, while a source-required free-production objective remains unassessed or assisted. Even with high overall accuracy and many completed activities, preserve the required production opportunity, balanced feasible sampling and due reviews. No recognition result can stand in for independent production. If that production mode is unimplemented, mark `UNAVAILABLE` and withhold release claims rather than fabricate success.
- **C — Strong new work with an overdue review.** The learner independently passes new tasks, but a blueprint-mandated delayed retrieval/review gate for an earlier objective is now due and not completed. The learner's recent high aggregate score cannot satisfy the earlier due obligation. Keep new evidence, schedule an eligible review and report `REVIEW_DUE` or `FOLLOWUP_UNKNOWN` as appropriate; do not treat a next-day calendar rollover or same-session retry as genuine delay.
- **D — Assisted repair and legitimate independent recovery.** A required objective initially fails; a model and hints lead to a polished corrected answer. Mark the assisted event as useful practice, not independent graduation evidence. A later, genuinely unaided novel-context response and eligible delayed retrieval may satisfy the **original unchanged** rubric if all source gates are met; the earlier assistance neither grants credit nor permanently bars recovery. Preserve both occasions and their provenance.
- **E — Personalization changes order, not obligations.** Two learners share the same CEFR blueprint. One learns better after concise explanations on a familiar music topic; the other benefits from worked examples on a neutral topic. Their future lesson order, examples, scaffold fade and spacing may diverge based on comparable later independent outcomes. Both retain the identical required-objective set, evaluator, prerequisites, exit rule, receptive/productive opportunities and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar context rotation. A disliked topic or preferred modality cannot waive a can-do.
- **F — Replay, stale rubric and cross-objective collision.** An offline passing event for objective X arrives after a newer failing event for required objective Y; X is uploaded twice; a model-assisted Y response shares an unqualified label with X; and an old rubric version is replayed. Route events to source level-qualified objectives, preserve trusted chronology and completed-run boundaries, deduplicate stable IDs and quarantine unsupported rubric migrations. Two replay orders must yield the same per-objective ledgers, due-review debt, coverage and exit denial/eligibility, without copying X credit to Y.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Resolve the actual gate.** For A–F output exact source blueprint/version, required level-qualified can-dos, prerequisites, source-defined mandatory independent/transfer/review/coverage gates, frozen rubrics, evaluator versions and the derived exit predicate. Unresolved source contracts are `UNSUPPORTED`, not assumed.
2. **No cross-objective compensation.** In A, arbitrarily strong valid evidence for completed objectives, engagement or XP must not flip an unmet *source-required* objective or level exit to met. Retain narrower valid successes without weakening thresholds.
3. **Evidence form remains binding.** In B, recognition, repeated same-item performance, assisted answers or an easy format cannot replace a source-required independent productive or receptive demonstration. Track supported and unavailable modalities separately; do not award phantom evidence.
4. **Review debt survives unrelated success.** In C, due source-required delayed retrieval or transfer cannot be discharged by high performance on another objective, a calendar boundary, an immediate repeat or a missing probe. Show eligible/completed/missing denominators and real elapsed time; unknown is not automatic failure or pass.
5. **Assistance cannot be laundered; recovery remains possible.** In D, first attempts, exposure, retries and independent later occasions are distinct. Grant target credit only when subsequent actual evidence independently meets all unchanged source gates; no permanent penalty for asking for help.
6. **Instructional flexibility without curricular substitution.** In E, method, pace, order, support and context can differ under revisable learner-specific hypotheses, but both learners face identical source-required CEFR outcomes, independent standards, review obligations and exit rules. Interests remain soft weights, never topic exclusion.
7. **Deterministic provenance and version integrity.** In F, stable event IDs, trusted occurrence/upload chronology, source-qualified objective, item/model exposure, run completion and rubric version produce invariant ledgers and exit decisions across duplicate/out-of-order replay. Quarantine mismatched versions rather than silently rescore.
8. **Coverage and agency are visible.** Across A–F report per-objective and skill-family opportunities, unmet/pending/unknown/unavailable states, alternative instructional sampling, accessible options, due reviews and unfamiliar context rotation. Never coerce a modality that is unavailable or equate learner preference with evidence of mastery.
9. **Conservative exit and release claims.** If any *source-required* objective, prerequisite, independent evidence, review, transfer, valid evaluator version or mandated coverage gate is unmet or unknown, do not assert graduation. If the source rule itself cannot be resolved, report `UNSUPPORTED`; a synthetic assertion PASS is not proof of human-learning benefit or authorization to open A1–C2.

## Required evidence and release gate

For A–F publish the **exact tested engine HEAD**, executable command, injected clock/seed, immutable ledger, source blueprint and complete level-qualified required-objective matrix, rubric/evaluator/policy versions, per-objective first unaided and assistance records, verified instructional delivery, genuine delayed independent retrieval and unseen transfer, eligibility/completion/missing denominators, supported/unavailable skill coverage, both replay orders, reason codes, exit predicate and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion. Distinguish a source-mandated graduation gate from a research preference or diagnostic score; never fabricate absent modalities, outcomes, learner-level method effects or an arbitrary threshold.

Any claim that adaptive instruction improves durable learning needs separate reviewed longitudinal evidence against a balanced comparator, later independent retention and transfer, and an ethically reviewed learner pilot. **A learner may excel unevenly; the tutor should adapt support without pretending the unfinished required objectives are finished.** Documentation alone neither implements this behavior nor certifies learners.

**Boundary:** curriculum acceptance documentation only. No engine, Auth, cloud, infrastructure, production, Supabase, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge changes. Integration/watchdog remains the sole owner-action aggregator.
