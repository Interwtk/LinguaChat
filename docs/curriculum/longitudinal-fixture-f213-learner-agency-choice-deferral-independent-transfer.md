# F213 — Learner agency, negotiated practice, deferral and independent transfer

**Status:** design-only, synthetic *cross-cutting* longitudinal curriculum acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until an independently reviewed executable exact-head harness verifies choice, delivery, deferral and evidence behavior. This is **not** an implemented learner-choice policy, a permanent learning-style profile, a new CEFR can-do, a new graduation gate, proof of improved learning or permission to open A1–C2. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** \`docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md\`. F206 audits instructional selection bias; F208 tests interest/method separation; F209 handles scarce review opportunities; F210 handles requested help and scaffold exposure; F212 handles invalid generated prompts. **Distinct question in F213:** when a learner *actively chooses, refuses, defers, interrupts or renegotiates* a proposed activity, can a flexible tutor honor that agency, offer a genuinely suitable alternative and keep an auditable record of **what was actually taught and independently demonstrated**—without mistaking a preference, a skipped opportunity or a self-rating for a stable learning trait, a failure or CEFR mastery?

## Fixed standards, choice provenance and opportunity ledger

Bind each history to an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, prerequisite/blueprint mappings, target language and variety, independently reviewed meaning-critical rubric, required receptive/productive skill and supported modality, accessibility needs, due-review obligations, relevant meaningful-turn ceiling and unchanged independent mastery/exit requirements. Illustrative English utterances below are not released lessons. Preserve the unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix boundary as **UNSUPPORTED**; learner choice cannot authorize recognition-only graduation.

For each *choice opportunity*, freeze pseudonymous learner; session/run/event IDs; trusted occurrence versus upload order; policy/objective/rubric versions; pre-choice completed independent evidence and uncertainty; existing due and skill-coverage debt; proposed activity, objective, method, topic, difficulty and planned modality; eligible alternatives and accessibility constraints; whether the proposal was actually shown; exact learner utterance/action and its meaning (explicit request, decline, defer, pause, preference, accessibility need or UNKNOWN); stated time constraints; option availability; policy rationale and any bounded exploration; and the **next actually rendered** activity. A proposal that was never shown is not a refusal. A decline is not an incorrect language response. A request to avoid a modality for now is not a permanent identity or proof of inability.

Maintain a separate immutable *delivery and outcome ledger*: what task, prompt, explanation, hint, transcript and modality actually rendered or played; answer-bearing exposure; first unaided response and reviewed semantic quality; retries and assistance; completion/abandonment; due/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed probes; later independently reviewed unfamiliar transfer; and subsequent actual policy revisions. Deduplicate session-qualified event IDs under offline replay. Unknown chronology or whether a model was seen remains UNKNOWN/quarantined, never clean unaided evidence. A learner's reported confidence or preference is an **agency/engagement signal**, not an independent mastery score or causal estimate of method efficacy.

The tutor may honor a request for a different supported explanation, activity order, topic or shorter session, while retaining required objectives and offering a feasible path back to due work. A learner may pause or decline without punishment; uncompleted opportunities remain visible as missing/deferred, not failed. Repeated deferral must not erase a CEFR requirement, invent a negative ability observation or coerce the learner through an inaccessible activity. When an alternative cannot assess the required skill, offer honest practice and leave the skill gate open. With sparse or contradictory independent evidence, retain a balanced default and sample suitable alternatives; do not convert “I learn best from videos” into a fixed visual/auditory type.

Oracle labels: OBJECTIVE_FIXED, PROPOSAL_SHOWN, PROPOSAL_NOT_SHOWN, LEARNER_CHOICE_OBSERVED, LEARNER_REQUEST_AMBIGUOUS, DECLINED_NOT_FAILED, DEFERRED_WITH_REASON, REVIEW_DEBT_PRESERVED, ACCESS_NEED_RESPECTED, ALTERNATIVE_ELIGIBLE, ALTERNATIVE_ACTUALLY_RENDERED, ALTERNATIVE_NOT_RENDERED, PREFERENCE_NOT_PROFICIENCY, SELF_RATING_NOT_MASTERY, FIRST_UNAIDED, ANSWER_EXPOSED, ASSISTED_REPAIR, CLEAN_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_INDEPENDENT_TRANSFER, MODALITY_COVERAGE_OPEN, TOPIC_ROTATION_PRESERVED, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, NOT_COMPARABLE and UNSUPPORTED. These classify decisions and evidence, never fixed learner types.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — “Not now” is a deferral, not a wrong answer.** A required versioned request-writing review is due. The tutor actually displays an eligible independent open-response task; the learner says “I only have five minutes; can we do it next time?” before responding. An unsafe system records a failed attempt and clears the due review. The expected tutor records PROPOSAL_SHOWN, DECLINED_NOT_FAILED and DEFERRED_WITH_REASON, preserves review debt and offers a feasible supported short alternative or ends the session without invented evidence. A matched control where the learner actually answers the valid task with a meaning-critical error may record a real failure. A second control with a planned but never displayed task cannot be called a learner decline. No fixed countdown or diagnosis of motivation is inferred.

**B — “Teach it another way” is a request, not a learning-style diagnosis.** After two completed comparable first-unaided errors on a fixed can-do, the learner requests “Could you show one example instead of explaining the rule again?” The tutor may deliver a worked example, recording actual answer exposure and an assisted immediate repair. It cannot label the learner permanently “visual” or infer that examples outperform concise instruction from the request or copied repair. A later distinct-session, unseen unaided response is needed to update method confidence. In a paired control the learner asks for the same example but never opens it; no example exposure may be fabricated. With conflicting delayed results, the hypothesis remains revisable and alternatives stay eligible.

**C — Choosing easy recognition does not waive required production.** The fixed CEFR objective requires independently composing a polite request with a deadline. The learner selects a multiple-choice recognition drill rather than the proposed open response. The tutor may allow suitable practice and record recognition accuracy, but cannot promote that accuracy to the open-production rubric, clear productive-skill debt or claim the learner independently transferred the skill. In the paired control the learner later completes a valid unseen open request without hints and meets the reviewed rubric; only that independent evidence can contribute to the required production gate. A repeated preference for recognition does not justify permanently hiding free production.

**D — An accessibility or modality constraint requires a real alternative.** The tutor proposes an audio-based listening task, but the audio fails to play and the learner requests written instructions. The tutor offers a valid reading or writing activity, explicitly marking listening evidence unavailable and preserving listening coverage debt. The transcript cannot be relabeled as listening, nor can the learner be marked unmotivated or low-ability for declining unusable audio. A matched control with verified accessible audio actually delivered and an unaided answer may produce listening evidence under the existing rubric. A requested accommodation is not an answer-bearing hint by default; whether the actual content exposes the answer must be reviewed separately.

**E — Self-confidence and topic enthusiasm do not replace independent retention.** A learner says “I know this already” and strongly prefers game examples. Earlier immediate game-themed practice was correct only after hints; a later independently reviewed unfamiliar-context probe contains a meaning-critical error. The tutor may acknowledge confidence and choose an appropriately short diagnostic, but must retain the independent error, uncertainty and due review. It cannot mark mastery from self-rating, avoid neutral/unfamiliar contexts or infer that games are the optimal teaching method. A paired history with genuinely delayed, unfamiliar, unaided success may justify a different future practice choice without changing the fixed can-do. Change *only* interest weights in another matched control: topic sampling may change, but independent evidence and method confidence must not.

**F — Repeated learner overrides, offline replay and actual delivery remain distinguishable.** Across three sessions, the tutor proposes due practice: session 1 the learner defers before seeing the prompt; session 2 the learner requests a shorter alternative, which renders and is completed after a worked example; session 3 the learner completes a genuinely unseen, unaided delayed probe. Offline synchronization uploads session 3 first, duplicates session 2 and replays the same session-1 request under a different upload order. Expected output preserves session-qualified identities, true proposal/render/exposure chronology, exactly one assisted completion and one qualified independent delayed result, with review debt changed only by evidence allowed under the existing gate. A fourth proposed alternative that never renders counts as no delivery. In a paired control where the session-3 prompt's novelty or exposure order cannot be established, transfer stays UNKNOWN/quarantined rather than clean. The next *actually delivered* choice must reflect the verified evidence and remaining debt, not a stale proposed recommendation.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Shared CEFR standards remain non-negotiable.** Bind every history to real versioned can-dos, prerequisites, semantic rubrics, supported modalities, independent mastery and exit criteria, due reviews and any existing turn ceilings. Learner choice can change the path, not the objective or release gate; the B2 Arc 6 recognition/open-retrieval mismatch remains UNSUPPORTED.
2. **Proposals, choices and deliveries are different events.** Publish deterministic seed/clock, session-qualified event IDs, exact option set and pre-choice evidence, proposal shown/not shown, learner action, actual selected/rendered task and subsequent completed response. A planned but unshown option cannot be a refusal; a selected but unrendered option cannot be instruction.
3. **Declining or deferring is not language failure.** A keeps due review debt visible, never fabricates a wrong answer, latency, completion or proficiency penalty, and distinguishes an explicit decline from a real independently scored wrong response.
4. **Method requests are hypotheses, not learner types.** B honors a supported request and records the worked example's actual exposure; a copied repair is assisted. A stated preference alone never proves that method improves delayed independent retention, and later contrary evidence must be able to revise the hypothesis.
5. **Agency cannot launder recognition into production.** C allows honest optional practice but retains open-production obligations and independent evidence requirements. No self-selection of an easier format can silently lower a CEFR rubric or erase productive/receptive coverage.
6. **Access needs outrank fake mastery and forced compliance.** D offers only actually supported accessible alternatives, separates transcript/reading from listening, preserves unavailable skill debt and never interprets an access failure as a learner deficit.
7. **Self-report and interests are separate from learning evidence.** E records confidence and preferred topics as soft signals, keeps independent semantic errors authoritative for mastery, and samples preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts longitudinally. Equivalent topic substitutions do not change method confidence or required objectives.
8. **Replay, exposure and missingness are evidence-safe.** F deduplicates trusted event identities across upload permutations, distinguishes deferral/assisted practice/clean delayed transfer, preserves unknown exposure chronology as UNKNOWN and reports due/offered/rendered/completed/missing opportunities separately. No proposed or copied response becomes independent achievement.
9. **Executable and human-learning claims have separate gates.** Publish machine-readable A–F histories and matched controls, exact objective/rubric/policy versions, seed/clock, event permutations, per-event expected labels, next **actually rendered** activity, debt transitions and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED assertions in an independently reviewed exact-head harness. Passing synthetic checks proves only software behavior; improvement claims require ethically reviewed longitudinal comparison with a balanced baseline, comparable opportunity, unaided delayed retention, unseen transfer, attrition analysis and no hidden CEFR skill loss. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge, Pre-A1 changes or A1–C2 release.

**Fixture verdict:** F213 makes **learner agency without evidence laundering** falsifiable. A strong tutor may negotiate the next supported activity with the learner, but must still tell the truth about what was offered, delivered, declined, assisted and independently retained. **UNSUPPORTED** until executed and independently reviewed; synthetic scenarios do not prove human learning gains.
