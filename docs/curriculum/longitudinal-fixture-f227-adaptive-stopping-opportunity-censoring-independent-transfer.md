# F227 — Adaptive stopping, unequal practice opportunities and independent transfer

**Status: DESIGN-ONLY / UNSUPPORTED.** This is a synthetic longitudinal curriculum acceptance specification, not an implemented stopping policy, an executable engine test, CEFR certification, evidence of human-learning gains or authorization to experiment on learners. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable. No Auth, cloud, infrastructure, production, Supabase, paid Claude, EvoLabs, automatic merge or B2 Arc 6 policy change.

**Governing contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F206/F222 cover policy selection and method confounding; F26/F32 cover delayed opportunities and selective follow-up. **Distinct failure here:** the tutor itself changes *when an activity, review, method comparison or evaluation stops*. An apparent improvement can arise because the tutor stops measuring after early success, continues testing after early failure, or suppresses difficult probes when the session budget is nearly exhausted. These are informative, policy-created observation windows, not necessarily changes in durable independent skill.

## Fixed construct, decision-time stopping ledger and comparison boundary

Bind each scenario to an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, reviewed meaning/function rubric, required skill/channel, prerequisites, accessible delivery, independent exit standard and due delayed/unseen transfer. No new objectives, thresholds or release permissions are invented. A completed assisted practice item is not a substitute for an independent exit probe.

Before each selection **and each stopping decision**, freeze: pseudonymous learner/objective; rubric and policy versions; feasible accessible alternatives; pre-specified activity/review opportunities and stopping rationale; item-family, challenge and answer-exposure versions; stable run/session/attempt/event IDs; trusted occurrence versus arrival; first unaided result; hints/retries/scaffold; actual method, topic, channel and dose delivered; remaining session budget; learner-initiated stop versus policy-initiated stop versus technical interruption; opportunity counts **planned, due, offered, rendered, started, completed, independently eligible, pending and not observed**; and future delayed retrieval/transfer obligations. Keep the pre-decision record immutable even if late outcomes change future policy.

The denominators must reflect **all learners and eligible opportunities at the point of policy choice**, not just completed high-scoring items. A policy-selected early stop is not a pass, failure or proof of forgetting. If a learner explicitly declines, preserve agency and do not force a probe; distinguish refusal from algorithmic suppression, and retain unmet skill debt. An inaccessible modality cannot be silently counted as completed or replaced with a non-equivalent skill. A short session may legitimately end; it cannot silently graduate the can-do.

## Six deterministic synthetic histories and matched controls

**A — Stop-on-first-success inflates reported mastery.** A0 and A1 are independent first-attempt responses at the same reviewed task band, 1/1 correct in two separate sessions. The policy stops both sessions immediately and never renders the two planned unfamiliar transfer tasks or a delayed retrieval probe (planned 5, rendered/completed 2, unobserved 3). Unsafe policy reports 100% mastery and cancels the objective. **Expected:** preserve 2/2 observed first-attempt successes, 3 unobserved obligations and an UNMEASURED independent transfer/retention outcome; do not infer graduation. **Matched control:** actually offer reviewed, accessible, unfamiliar and meaningfully delayed probes under the unchanged rubric; only completed, independently eligible results can support a revised claim.

**B — Failure-contingent continuation reverses method rankings.** For the same can-do, method P ends a session at its first correct answer (observed 8/8), while method Q requires three opportunities regardless of first response (observed 16/24); both have identical pre-choice target skill and comparable initial challenge but unequal stopping policies. No comparable delayed unseen probes exist. Unsafe policy declares P superior from 100% versus 67%. **Expected:** NOT_COMPARABLE for method effectiveness; retain planned/offered/completed opportunities, first-attempt outcomes, unequal exposure and stopping reasons. **Matched control:** pre-register equivalent stopping and assessment schedules across feasible P/Q, with reviewed item families and later independent retrieval/transfer; observational contrasts still require baseline, propensity and missingness review.

**C — An early failure triggers legitimate remediation, not permanent low mastery.** C0 is a reviewed independent first-attempt error on a prerequisite. C1–C3 are appropriately scaffolded repairs with 3/3 correct; the policy pauses challenge for a brief instructional detour. C4 is a planned independent target probe not rendered because the learner's session ends. Unsafe policy either calls the detour a failure or counts its 3/3 assisted success as the target exit. **Expected:** retain C0 diagnostic evidence, credit the instruction as practice, record C4 due/unobserved and schedule a feasible future independent probe without a permanent learner-type label. **Matched control:** offer a genuinely unseen, independently reviewed target probe after a meaningful interval; assess the unchanged can-do, not the scaffolded repair items.

**D — Session-budget censoring concentrates missing difficult work.** A 12-minute accessible session has six planned tasks: four familiar recognition tasks and two independent production/transfer tasks. The policy spends the budget on the four easy tasks (4/4 correct) and stops before either productive task; the same pattern recurs in three sessions (12 familiar completions, six productive tasks unrendered). Unsafe policy reports balanced improvement from the easy denominator and clears productive skill debt. **Expected:** distinguish genuine 12/12 familiar accuracy from six unmeasured productive opportunities; preserve noncompensatory CEFR evidence and plan feasible balanced review rather than penalizing the learner for tutor scheduling. **Matched control:** reserve a feasible production/transfer opportunity before familiar review while holding accessibility, total budget and required construct fixed; compare completed independent outcomes and opportunity costs, not only scores.

**E — Interest-based stopping makes topic preference look like learning.** On favourite travel topics the tutor stops after two easy successful turns (6/6 across three sessions); in neutral/unfamiliar contexts it offers longer, harder negotiations (4/9), then incorrectly attributes the gap to a superior travel teaching method. The policy stops before an equivalent travel negotiation and before a neutral easy control. **Expected:** NOT_COMPARABLE because topic, pragmatic burden, dose and stopping rule differ; interests remain soft weights and cannot remove neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar rotation or required negotiation. **Matched control:** independently review equivalent communicative burden, challenge, item novelty, accessible channel, stopping opportunities and delayed transfer in both contexts; if equivalence cannot be established, use a neutral fallback and preserve uncertainty.

**F — Stop/restart, duplicate events and late evidence.** F0 freezes a low-confidence pre-choice policy with two accessible methods and three independent opportunities due. F1 is an independently completed first turn (stable attempt F-T1); F2 is a duplicate replay of F-T1; F3 is a policy stop after the first correct response; F4 is a learner-requested pause in a different session; F5 is a due unseen probe not rendered; F6 is a late-arriving reviewer correction to F1's rubric version; F7 is an actually offered independent probe whose outcome remains pending. Unsafe policy counts F1 twice, turns F5 into failure, treats F4 as algorithmic avoidance, retroactively rewrites F0 and marks F7 complete. **Expected:** deduplicate F-T1, retain the original and revised rubric versions with a NOT_COMPARABLE bridge pending review, keep F0 and F3 historically fixed, separate learner agency from policy stop, and preserve due/offered/rendered/completed/pending denominators. **Matched control:** render the missing accessible reviewed probe with a new stable ID and trusted answer isolation; only its observed result may change *future* hypotheses.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Shared objective and release boundary:** existing versioned CEFR can-do, rubric, prerequisites, independent exit and noncompensatory skill obligations remain unchanged. No Pre-A1 edit or A1–C2 release.
2. **Explicit stopping provenance:** log pre-choice and pre-stop alternatives, policy version, stop actor/reason, session budget and actual delivered task/method, with stable event IDs and trusted chronology.
3. **Full opportunity denominators:** planned/due/offered/rendered/started/completed/independently eligible/pending/not observed are separately auditable. Never report completed-only percentages as cohort-wide mastery.
4. **Independent evidence integrity:** assisted repairs, easy recognition, repeated first-turn success and unrendered probes cannot replace reviewed first unaided, delayed retrieval or unseen independent transfer.
5. **Method comparability:** stop-on-success versus fixed-length practice is NOT_COMPARABLE without matched task, baseline, difficulty, exposure, channel, opportunity schedule and missingness; no automatic causal claim.
6. **Learner agency and access:** learner-initiated pause, policy stop, technical failure and inaccessible task are distinct; never coerce participation or fabricate skill failures, passes or oral proficiency.
7. **Interest and skill balance:** favourite topics remain soft weights; neutral, adjacent and unfamiliar contexts plus required receptive/productive skills retain feasible opportunities and independent exit obligations.
8. **Reversible chronology:** duplicates do not inflate counts, late rubric changes retain versioned bridges, pending remains pending and new evidence can change only later decisions, not historical choice/stopping snapshots.
9. **Executable and efficacy boundary:** a future independent exact-head harness must bind reviewed CEFR source/rubric, verified item equivalence, stop actor and render chronology, opportunity denominators, matched controls and per-case PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED. Synthetic specification alone does not demonstrate shipped adaptation or human-learning gains; ethically reviewed longitudinal comparison must measure delayed unaided retention, unseen transfer, subgroup access and selective missingness.

## Independent verification and non-goals

A future machine-readable fixture and independent runner should report exact curriculum and engine HEADs, executable command, reviewed can-do/rubric/task calibration, pre-choice and pre-stop policy snapshots, actual delivered dose, stable deduplicated events, stop actor and session-budget provenance, all opportunity denominators, answer-bearing render chronology, first unaided versus assisted evidence, delayed unseen transfer, matched-control results and reviewer-signed per-assertion PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED. Until those bindings exist, **F227 is UNSUPPORTED**; a static JSON or a green generic CI run must not be called proof of pedagogical efficacy.

No real learner monitoring, policy rollout, new modality, level availability change, Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase modification, paid provider, EvoLabs work, merge or B2 Arc 6 change is authorized.

**Verdict:** a strong tutor may stop, pause or remediate for good reasons, but must not mistake the evidence it chose *not to observe* for durable independent learning.
