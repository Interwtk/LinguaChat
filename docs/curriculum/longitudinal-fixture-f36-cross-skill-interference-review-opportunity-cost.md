# F36 — Cross-skill interference and review opportunity cost must not be hidden by local adaptive gains

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. This is not a reproduced defect, implemented adaptive tutor, causal estimate or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F25 protects required skill coverage and review debt; F26 protects delayed-review opportunity; F28 prevents cross-skill extrapolation; F35 protects support fading. F36 tests a separate longitudinal question: **when a policy improves independently measured outcomes for one objective or skill, does its allocation of finite instructional time coincide with deterioration, interference or missed independent retrieval elsewhere?** Local improvement cannot establish net durable benefit if other required abilities are harmed or their evidence opportunities disappear. Conversely, an observed decline cannot be attributed causally to interference without comparable exposures and controls.

## Controlled setup

Resolve at least two **existing versioned CEFR can-dos**, with real supported skill routes, prerequisites, rubrics, mastery/exit gates and review obligations. Use a genuinely feasible receptive/productive pair (for example supported reading and written free production), not invented listening/speaking assessments. Compare A (`adaptive_focus`) with B (`balanced_review`) under the same **fixed total instructional and assessment time budget**, candidate tasks, accessibility and availability constraints, objective versions, calibrated independent anchor probes, difficulty bands and topic-context opportunities. Neither policy may silently drop a required can-do, change assessment demand, or claim an unimplemented modality.

Use a deterministic clock, seed, versioned `cross_skill_interference_policy` and append-only synthetic ledger. Capture learner, objective/version, skill, session, learning day, event/attempt/run/probe IDs, teaching option and assignment eligibility, offered/completed activities, **planned versus actual minutes**, modality and context, prerequisites, prior independent baseline, first-attempt errors, latency when trustworthy, hint/scaffold and answer exposure, intervening instruction and practice, retrieval due/offer/completion/deferral, actual delays, item-family novelty, independently scored unseen-context transfer, assessment/rubric calibration, missingness and event-time versus upload-time provenance. Track both **within-objective gain** and **cross-objective maintenance**, opportunity costs, evidence denominators and uncertainty. Predeclare how to classify `LOCAL_GAIN_WITH_MAINTENANCE`, `POSSIBLE_INTERFERENCE`, `OPPORTUNITY_DISPLACEMENT`, `NOT_COMPARABLE` and `UNKNOWN` without inventing universal effect-size or forgetting thresholds.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — Local gain, verified regression elsewhere.** A concentrates supported instructional minutes on written production and improves repeated delayed unaided writing anchors. Previously strong independent reading retrieval declines on comparable calibrated anchors after an actual delay. Preserve both signals and mark possible interference, with time allocation and uncertainty; do not report overall adaptive superiority or automatically assert writing caused the reading decline.
- **B — Displaced review is not measured forgetting.** A replaces due reading reviews with successful favorite-topic writing tasks. Reading reviews were due but not offered; there are no new reading responses. Keep the review debt and missing reading outcome explicit. Do not score a failure, declare maintained reading mastery, or credit net improvement from writing success alone.
- **C — Genuine complementary gain.** A and B have comparable opportunities and calibrated delayed independent anchors across both supported skills. A's writing improves and reading is maintained on repeated independent unfamiliar probes, with no outstanding required reviews. Preserve the valid positive evidence and allow a provisional, uncertainty-aware hypothesis, not a causal or global learning-style claim.
- **D — Task-switching and short-term fatigue.** One learner's writing drops immediately after a dense reading block but recovers on later matched, rested independent probes. Another shows persistent delayed writing difficulty after repeated switches. Distinguish transient state, ordering and fatigue from durable cross-skill interference; never convert an immediate post-switch error into proven forgetting or permanent preference.
- **E — Reversal after delayed maintenance probes.** Early immediate scores favor intensive writing focus. Later matched delayed independent reading and writing anchors reveal regression in reading and no lasting writing advantage; balanced scheduling performs better on the supported evidence. Lower confidence, revise the objective/skill-specific allocation hypothesis, restore due reviews and continue alternative sampling without weakening CEFR gates.
- **F — Unequal time, missingness and replay.** A's sessions are longer and its easiest follow-ups more often completed; B receives more difficult unfamiliar probes. An offline reading review occurred before a writing probe but uploaded afterward, one event is duplicated, and another has unknown completion or time provenance. Normalize actual time and opportunity, deduplicate IDs, order by trusted occurrence time and quarantine ambiguous records. Never infer net benefit from raw completed-only accuracy or upload order.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Joint outcome ledger.** For A–F expose per policy/objective/skill eligible, assigned, offered, attempted, completed and independently assessed counts; actual versus planned time; first attempts, assistance, valid delayed retrieval, unseen transfer, due reviews, missingness and confidence. Separate local gains from maintenance and net coverage.
2. **No local-score laundering.** In A, improved delayed independent writing does not erase verified reading regression. Report both on calibrated anchors and label the relationship `POSSIBLE_INTERFERENCE` unless an appropriate comparison supports a stronger inference; do not automatically call the policy better overall.
3. **Missing review is not forgetting or maintenance.** In B, due-but-unoffered and offered-but-uncompleted reading reviews remain debt, not negative or positive retrieval observations. The policy cannot graduate or assert preserved retention on the basis of writing results.
4. **Valid joint evidence survives.** In C, independently verified delayed and unfamiliar transfer outcomes for both skills remain attributable and reportable, including sample sizes, opportunity denominators, actual delays and uncertainty. A positive synthetic result is not proof of human-learning improvement.
5. **Transient state is distinct from durable interference.** In D, record order, elapsed time, fatigue/context indicators when observed and later rested probes. Immediate deterioration alone is not durable loss; persistent independently verified delayed deterioration remains visible rather than explained away.
6. **Reversible allocation under a shared budget.** In E, revise tentative focus and review scheduling when comparable delayed cross-skill evidence contradicts immediate scores. Preserve balanced exploration, supported receptive/productive obligations, overdue reviews and the original CEFR rubrics.
7. **Comparable opportunity and replay safety.** In F, compare like-for-like objectives, difficulty, item novelty, elapsed delay, time budget and assessment opportunity. Stable-ID deduplication and trusted event-time ordering yield invariant counts and classifications across upload orders; uncertain provenance stays `UNKNOWN`.
8. **Interest, availability and standards separation.** Topic interest may softly weight examples but cannot redirect all time away from neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar contexts, conceal debt, change required objectives or independent mastery thresholds. Unsupported listening/speaking remain `UNAVAILABLE`, never implicitly completed.
9. **Conservative net-benefit gate.** If calibrated cross-skill maintenance anchors, matched time/opportunities, reliable provenance or delayed independent evidence are insufficient, report `UNSUPPORTED`/`NOT_COMPARABLE`, preserve narrower observations and use the shared balanced curriculum with normal scaffolding and reviews. Never claim overall durable benefit from a single skill's assisted or immediate improvement.
