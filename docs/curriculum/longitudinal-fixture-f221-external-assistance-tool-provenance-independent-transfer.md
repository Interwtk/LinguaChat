Not a shipped tool-use detector, anti-cheating system, surveillance mechanism, new CEFR can-do, changed mastery threshold, or evidence of improved human learning. **Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. ## Fixed objective, tool provenance and privacy boundaries

Bind every history to an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, reviewed communicative meaning/function rubric, target language and accepted varieties, task difficulty, context family, required skill family, accessible channel, prerequisites, due-review obligations, and unchanged independent mastery/exit rules. The illustrative learner utterances below are synthetic, not published lessons. The same aid may be permissible during practice yet disqualify a particular response from being **clean independent evidence** when it supplies the answer-bearing language being evaluated. Independence is **task- and construct-specific**: a screen reader that reads instructions aloud is not automatically answer help on a writing objective; an answer-generating translator on a free-production task is. Spellcheck may matter to an orthographic subcriterion but not necessarily to a meaning-only criterion; the reviewed rubric decides. Do not impose a blanket ban on accessibility or normal communication tools.

Freeze a **pre-decision** ledger: pseudonymous learner; stable learner/session/run/task/event and content-version IDs; trusted occurrence and render chronology; objective/rubric/skill/channel versions; task instructions and explicitly permitted tools; first valid learner-originated attempt with reviewed semantic quality; tutor hints/models/recasts/transcripts and **actual exposure**; declared or instrumented in-product aid requests and outputs; aid type (lookup, meaning-bearing translation, predictive completion, generated answer, human collaboration, accessibility input, UNKNOWN), scope and timing; provenance source and confidence; edits after aid exposure; assistance eligibility for each rubric dimension; due/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-ups; delayed unaided retrieval and unseen transfer in distinct sessions; candidate teaching choices, uncertainty, and **next choice actually rendered**. Replays, pasted duplicate responses and late events cannot manufacture fresh independent attempts.

**Observability and fairness:** log only aids the product legitimately observes or the learner voluntarily discloses. An unusually fluent sentence, fast paste, accent, language background, disability, assistive technology or delayed response is **not proof** of undisclosed AI, translator use, cheating or language mastery. Unknown external assistance stays UNKNOWN; do not fingerprint, monitor the clipboard, inspect other apps, require webcam surveillance, infer a diagnosis, or pressure learners to confess. If clean evidence cannot be established, offer an accessible fresh independent probe under normal standards; do not retroactively mark an unknown response wrong. A learner can decline tool-use questions and still receive instruction and assessment opportunities.

**Decision sequence:** fixed objective and reviewed construct → task-specific permitted aids and accessible delivery → record offered versus **actually rendered** tools/models and first attempt → classify observed aid scope and unknowns without accusations → distinguish useful assisted practice from clean mastery evidence → offer a feasible independent probe in a genuinely new context when needed → later meaningful-delay unaided retrieval and unseen transfer → update uncertain instructional hypothesis and actual delivered choice. Keep reasonable tool-assisted alternatives and balanced unaided opportunities; never optimize for polished drafts, generated-text length or immediate assisted correctness. Interest personalization remains a separate soft context layer with adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts eligible.

**Oracle labels:** OBJECTIVE_FIXED, RUBRIC_REVIEWED, CONSTRUCT_SPECIFIC_AID, PERMITTED_PRACTICE, ACCESSIBILITY_NOT_ANSWER_AID, ANSWER_BEARING_EXPOSURE, LOOKUP_SCOPE_RECORDED, TRANSLATION_ASSISTED, PREDICTIVE_COMPLETION_ASSISTED, HUMAN_COLLABORATION_ASSISTED, FIRST_UNAIDED_PRESERVED, INDEPENDENCE_UNKNOWN, NO_UNOBSERVED_TOOL_INFERENCE, NO_PUNITIVE_CHEATING_LABEL, FRESH_ACCESSIBLE_PROBE, CLEAN_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_TRANSFER, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, SKILL_EVIDENCE_SEPARATE, INTEREST_METHOD_SEPARATE, DUE_REVIEW_RETAINED, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, HYPOTHESIS_REVISED, ACTUAL_NEXT_CHOICE, BALANCED_FALLBACK, NOT_COMPARABLE and UNSUPPORTED. These label observations and eligibility, never a permanent learner identity.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories with matched controls

**A — A dictionary lookup can teach without automatically erasing all independent evidence.** For an existing request-for-directions writing can-do, a learner independently writes a semantically valid request, then opens an in-product dictionary to check the meaning of an unrelated place noun before submitting a later revised sentence. Unsafe policy treats all dictionary use as cheating, marks the first valid attempt failed and blocks mastery. **Expected:** preserve the timestamped pre-lookup first attempt and its reviewed meaning score; record the lookup's exact scope and the later response as exposed to that lookup. Determine eligibility by the fixed rubric and whether the lookup supplied the tested construct, not a global “used tool” flag. Matched control: the dictionary reveals the exact required request phrase *before* the first valid submission; that answer-bearing phrase cannot count as clean independent production, even if the final sentence is correct. A separate unseen task can provide clean evidence.

**B — Full-sentence translation is legitimate practice but not an unaided production pass.** A learner writes a Spanish message, invokes an in-product translator to generate an English invitation, and copies the result into a CEFR writing activity. The tutor then asks for a new invitation unaided in a different context in a later session. Unsafe policy awards independent mastery from the copied translation and attributes the later result to the learner's “preferred translation method.” **Expected:** record translation output, exposure and copied-answer dependence; keep the assisted invitation as practice, not clean writing mastery. Score the later valid first unaided invitation independently and retain both successes and failures for future decisions. Matched control: the learner first produces the same valid English invitation *before* any translation exposure; the original response may be independently eligible under the same rubric. A tool used after a committed first attempt cannot rewrite its provenance.

**C — Predictive text and accessibility aids are not interchangeable.** On a writing task, learner X uses a screen reader for instructions and voice dictation to enter their own novel request; learner Y accepts an autocomplete suggestion that supplies the entire meaning-critical request clause. Both submit identical surface text. Unsafe policy marks X assisted and Y independent merely because X used assistive technology. **Expected:** record the actual role of each aid, distinguish access/input mediation from answer-bearing generation, and assess independent communicative meaning under the unchanged reviewed rubric. If the task explicitly assesses unaided spelling, document the affected orthographic dimension separately; do not infer listening/speaking mastery from screen reader or dictation. Matched control: X dictates after hearing an answer-bearing model; the response is now exposed and cannot be called clean for that construct. Device speed and disability are never proxies for aid dependence.

**D — Human co-writing is valuable rehearsal, not solitary transfer.** In a collaborative conversation rehearsal, a peer supplies the next question and corrects the learner's reply; the tutor records a fluent exchange. The learner later independently handles an unfamiliar request from a new interlocutor after a meaningful interval. Unsafe policy equates group success and number of dialogue turns with independent CEFR interaction mastery. **Expected:** mark which turns were authored by the learner, which were suggested/corrected by the peer, and whether the learner saw the model before responding; preserve the collaborative episode as supported practice. Assess later first unaided communicative repair and appropriateness under the fixed rubric, with skill/channel evidence kept separate. Matched control: a peer supplies the exact answer for the later probe; the superficially identical reply is now assisted and cannot certify independent transfer. Do not ban collaboration from practice.

**E — Unknown external tools must trigger neither accusations nor fabricated mastery.** A learner pastes a polished response into a valid writing task. No in-product tool exposure or reliable external provenance is available. Another learner types the identical response character by character with equivalent task conditions. Unsafe policy infers AI use from paste speed for one learner, labels cheating, downgrades CEFR and withholds review; an opposite unsafe policy assumes both responses were definitely unaided and certifies durable mastery. **Expected:** retain the observed response and semantic quality, mark external assistance provenance UNKNOWN rather than asserting use or non-use, and avoid a punitive label. Apply the same published evidence policy to comparable uncertainty; offer a fresh, accessible, unseen independent probe where needed, with no extra penalty or surveillance. Matched control: trusted in-product chronology proves the learner saw an answer-generating model before submission; now the assisted status is supported by evidence. A voluntary declaration may clarify provenance but declining is not a failure or motivation signal.

**F — Contradictory tool-assisted histories must alter the next *delivered* teaching option.** For one fixed information-request can-do, four distinct-session, reviewed, comparable outcomes exist: (1) brief dictionary-supported practice followed by clean delayed unseen success; (2) translator-supported practice followed by clean delayed unseen failure; (3) dictionary-supported practice followed by clean delayed unseen failure; (4) translator-supported practice followed by clean delayed unseen success. A fifth apparent success is a duplicate answer generated by a tool, and a sixth claimed independent outcome has unknown answer-visibility chronology. Events arrive 4, 2, duplicate 5, 1, 3, 6. A required listening review and a neutral-topic production transfer remain due; one follow-up was queued but never rendered. **Expected:** deduplicate 5, quarantine 6, retain all four contradictory comparable observations, distinguish exposure to aid during *instruction* from assistance during the later *assessment*, and keep due/coverage debt plus due/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Select a feasible next **actually rendered** option with uncertainty and deliberate balanced exploration rather than permanently classifying the learner as a “dictionary” or “translator” type. Matched control reverses later clean outcomes while holding objective, rubric, difficulty, accessible channel, exposure, delay, context and missingness fixed; the method hypothesis and delivered next choice must be capable of changing or justify a documented balanced fallback. If tool dose or probe conditions differ, mark NOT_COMPARABLE and avoid causal superiority claims. Swapping gaming for cooking changes soft topic weights only, never method confidence or required objectives.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Shared CEFR standards:** A–F use existing versioned can-dos, reviewed construct-specific meaning/function rubrics, prerequisites, skill requirements and unchanged mastery/exit rules. No Pre-A1 edit, A1–C2 release, invented objective, or resolution of B2 Arc 6 recognition/open-retrieval disagreement.
2. **Assistance is scoped and timed:** A–C record tool identity, observed versus volunteered provenance, actual output/render time, aid scope and whether answer-bearing material preceded the first valid attempt. A post-attempt lookup cannot retroactively taint the preserved first response; answer-bearing pre-attempt generation cannot silently become independent evidence.
3. **Practice remains legitimate:** B and D allow translator, AI or peer-supported learning as practice with transparent provenance. Correct assisted composition is neither a failure nor an automatic clean mastery pass; a later new-item independent probe decides transfer.
4. **Accessibility and construct fairness:** C distinguishes input/access mediation from answer generation and documents any affected rubric dimension. Neither assistive technology, accent, device latency nor transcription alone proves a different CEFR skill or weaker language ability.
5. **Unknown is not cheating or mastery:** E never infers undisclosed tool use from paste speed, polished writing or demographic/device proxies, never invents negative evidence, and never forces disclosure. An accessible fresh independent opportunity replaces surveillance or punitive certainty.
6. **Clean delayed evidence:** B–F require distinct-session, meaningfully delayed, unseen, reviewed first unaided responses without answer-bearing exposure. Same-item copying, translated drafts, coached turns, immediate repeats and untrusted chronology cannot fabricate durable retention or cross-context transfer.
7. **Interest, skill and coverage separation:** Matched F swaps interests without changing objective, difficulty, rubric, aid-eligibility standard, instructional-method estimate, review debt or modality requirements. Adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts stay eligible; text/translation practice cannot fabricate listening or speaking mastery.
8. **Reversible longitudinal decisions:** F retains stable event IDs, trusted chronology, duplicates, UNKNOWN, contradictory clean outcomes, alternatives, uncertainty, due/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators and the next option **actually rendered**. Opposite independent outcomes can revise the method hypothesis or justify balanced exploration; no permanent “tool learner” label.
9. **Executable and efficacy boundary:** Publish machine-readable A–F histories and matched controls with deterministic seed/clock, objective/rubric/task/tool/policy/channel versions, independently reviewed linguistic and provenance oracles, first-attempt/exposure chronology, per-assertion PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED and an independently reviewed exact-head executable harness. Synthetic PASS establishes invariants only. Any claim that a particular tool-assisted teaching method improves durable human learning requires a separately reviewed longitudinal pilot against an accessible balanced baseline, comparable opportunities and tool dose, delayed unaided retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer, missingness/attrition and subgroup-fairness analyses, and no hidden loss in required skills. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** F221 makes **tool-supported practice versus independently demonstrated language competence, without surveillance or unsupported accusations**, falsifiable. A strong tutor may teach with dictionaries, translators, predictive text, accessible input and human collaboration while testing independent retention honestly. **UNSUPPORTED** until independently executed and reviewed; no human-efficacy claim is made.
