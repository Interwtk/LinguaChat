# F206 — Instructional-policy selection bias, missing follow-up and independent transfer audit

**Status:** design-only synthetic longitudinal *cross-cutting* curriculum acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable, independently reviewed decision/exposure/provenance/outcome checks and ethically reviewed delayed learner evidence exist. This is **not** a new CEFR objective, mastery threshold, randomized trial, deployed personalization policy, proof of causal benefit or authorization to open A1–C2. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. Earlier fixtures test individual communicative constructs; F205 tests temporal anchoring. **Distinct question in F206:** when the tutor *chooses* a method based on prior learner performance, can we tell whether later independent improvement is due to instruction, or merely to easier tasks, familiar interests, extra help, selective follow-up or biased assignment? This fixture audits the *selection-and-observation process*, not another language skill. It does not supersede any earlier fixture or invent a graduation gate.

## Frozen objective, comparable choices and independently reviewed outcomes

For every comparison bind an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, target language/variety, prerequisites, assessment and rubric/oracle versions, task difficulty and communicative purpose, meaningful-turn ceiling if applicable, help policy, accessibility, independent first-attempt standard, delayed/unseen transfer standard, modality balance and exit thresholds. An A1 learner and a C1 learner may have different eligible methods and tasks; never pool their scores as if they faced an identical standard. An unsupported speaking/listening route is not an eligible treatment and cannot produce phantom credit.

At the *time of the decision*, record pseudonymous learner and objective version, completed prior evidence available to the policy, eligible instructional alternatives, unavailable alternatives with reasons, selected method, method intensity/dose, stated rationale, uncertainty, exploration versus exploitation, selection probability **only if genuinely known**, context/interest selection as a separate variable, task/rubric versions, accessible modality and planned follow-up. Never backfill a selection probability, pre-choice confidence or hypothetical alternative after seeing the outcome. If an assignment probability is unknown, label it UNKNOWN and do not use propensity weighting or pretend the observation was randomized.

An **actual-delivery ledger** records stable session-qualified event/replay IDs, rendered/heard explanation and hints, modality success/failure, answer-bearing examples, retries, first unaided authorship, repair exposure, task context and burden, completed-run provenance, and any later policy revision. Planned but undelivered material is not exposure. A rendered answer is exposure even if the learner ignores it; an unopened hint is not. Deduplicate retries and replays. A completed same-day practice event does not become delayed retrieval by crossing midnight.

An **outcome ledger** distinguishes immediate first-unaided quality, assisted repair, delayed first-unaided retrieval, genuinely unseen independent communicative transfer, skill family, interval and intervening material, due/offered/rendered/completed/missing probes, reason for missingness when actually known, and independently reviewed rubric judgments. Do not code missing follow-up as success, failure or zero latency. Compare like with like: task complexity, channel, topic familiarity, assistance, outcome interval, baseline proficiency, language variety and availability must be inspectable. Exploratory observational differences may guide tentative reversible hypotheses; they do **not** establish causal superiority.

Task-local audit labels: CEFR_OBJECTIVE_BOUND, CHOICE_SET_RECORDED, CHOICE_SET_MISSING, PRE_CHOICE_EVIDENCE_FROZEN, SELECTION_PROBABILITY_KNOWN, SELECTION_PROBABILITY_UNKNOWN, METHOD_ACTUALLY_DELIVERED, METHOD_NOT_DELIVERED, ANSWER_EXPOSURE, FIRST_UNAIDED_RESPONSE, ASSISTED_REPAIR, DELAYED_UNAIDED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_UNAIDED_TRANSFER, FOLLOWUP_COMPLETED, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, FOLLOWUP_REASON_KNOWN, SELECTION_BIAS_SUSPECTED, TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED, TASK_DIFFICULTY_CONFOUNDED, HELP_CONFOUNDED, CHANNEL_CONFOUNDED, DELAY_CONFOUNDED, POSITIVITY_VIOLATION, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, REVERSIBLE_POLICY_UPDATE, BALANCED_EXPLORATION, INTEREST_ROTATION_PRESERVED, NOT_APPLICABLE and UNSUPPORTED. These describe events, not permanent learner types.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Easy-task assignment creates a false winner.** The policy tends to choose CONCISE_EXPLANATION on familiar, short A2 requests and WORKED_EXAMPLE on unfamiliar, longer requests for the **same versioned can-do**. Immediate first-unaided scores are 8/10 and 5/10, respectively. A naive dashboard declares concise explanation superior. The audit instead flags TASK_DIFFICULTY_CONFOUNDED and TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED. In later genuinely matched unseen independent tasks, each method is followed by 3/6 correct first attempts; this does not prove equivalence or causal effect, but removes the basis for the earlier winner claim. Keep uncertainty and exploration. Never pool assisted successes with these independent outcomes.

**B — Selective follow-up makes retention look stronger than it is.** Ten learners receive METHOD_A; four complete a genuinely delayed unaided probe and three succeed. Six have no completed follow-up, with no documented reason. The only observed delayed rate is 3/4 **among completers**, not 3/10 or a population retention rate of 75%. METHOD_B has eight of ten completers, five successes. The unequal completion patterns and small counts preclude a method-winner conclusion. Record missingness, completion opportunity and sensitivity of any comparison; do not impute six failures or successes. A due probe that was never rendered is not a completed failure.

**C — Interests are not an instructional treatment.** A learner interested in gaming receives concise explanations with gaming examples; another receives worked examples in unfamiliar community-service contexts. The same can-do and nominal rubric do not make this a method-only comparison. Mark TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED. In later appropriately reviewed, comparable sessions, vary *context independently from method*: both concise and worked explanations occur across preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts. Preserve language burden and response standard. Never exclude an unfamiliar required objective or conclude that gaming interest is a fixed learning style.

**D — Contradictory delayed evidence reverses a provisional choice.** For one learner, several completed comparable sessions suggest METHOD_A helps first-unaided performance. The policy tentatively selects A more often but continues bounded exploration of B. Two later distinct-session, unseen unaided transfer probes fail under A while comparable B probes succeed. Lower confidence, inspect rubric/task/help differences and revise the hypothesis reversibly; do not assign a permanent A/B learner label. A single later failure is not by itself proof that B wins. The next choice must reflect the newly observed evidence rather than replaying the old recommendation.

**E — Delivery failure, assistance and duplicate events cannot inflate benefit.** The tutor selects LISTENING_CONTRAST but audio playback fails; a transcript is rendered. No listening treatment or listening mastery is credited. A separately bound reading task may produce reading evidence. The learner first answers incorrectly, sees an answer-bearing example, then copies a correct reply: retain FIRST_UNAIDED_RESPONSE plus ANSWER_EXPOSURE and ASSISTED_REPAIR, not independent mastery. A replay with the same stable event identity adds no new observation. An unopened hint is not exposure. If no suitable accessible alternative exists, use the balanced fallback and mark the comparison unsupported rather than fabricating method delivery.

**F — Exploitation must not erase coverage, exploration or unfamiliar contexts.** A learner often succeeds with reading-first, gaming-themed tasks but has due listening, free-production and unfamiliar-context objectives under the shared CEFR graph. A policy that repeatedly serves only gaming reading drills fails BALANCED_EXPLORATION and INTEREST_ROTATION_PRESERVED even if short-term accuracy rises. Choose among genuinely available accessible methods, include neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar contexts and preserve due independent productive and receptive evidence. Do not claim success for unavailable audio/speech; expose the modality coverage gap. A later balanced baseline comparison must examine independent retention and transfer as well as coverage, not just average correctness.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **No new CEFR gate.** Every outcome binds an applicable existing versioned can-do, target language/variety and independently reviewed rubric with unchanged independent mastery and exit thresholds. Cross-level or unsupported modality comparisons are NOT_APPLICABLE/UNSUPPORTED.
2. **Pre-choice decision provenance.** The eligible choice set, reasons, actual method, prior evidence and uncertainty are frozen before the result. Unknown assignment probabilities remain unknown; no post-hoc propensity or randomized-assignment claim.
3. **Selection bias is visible.** A rejects a winner conclusion from 8/10 versus 5/10 when method, task difficulty and familiarity differ; matched 3/6 versus 3/6 is descriptive, not causal proof.
4. **Missing follow-up is not a score.** B distinguishes due, offered, rendered, completed and missing delayed probes, reports completion denominators and does not silently equate completer accuracy with learner-population retention.
5. **Interests remain an independent soft layer.** C tests method and context separately, preserving preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar examples without skipping required can-dos or using interest as a fixed learner identity.
6. **Instructional hypotheses reverse with evidence.** D lowers confidence and can change later actual decisions when comparable delayed first-unaided evidence contradicts an earlier choice, while continuing alternatives rather than permanently assigning a method.
7. **Delivery, first authorship and modality are real.** E rejects phantom listening exposure, answer-copy mastery, undelivered methods, unopened-hint exposure and replay double-counting; reading fallback requires separate valid evidence.
8. **Balanced longitudinal outcome beats short-term score.** F preserves required receptive/productive and accessible modality coverage, exploration, context rotation, genuinely delayed retrieval and unseen independent transfer; no reward for easy-task loops.
9. **Evidence and release claims stay bounded.** A–F are synthetic specifications, not observed outcomes or executable QA. Before any adaptive-efficacy claim require independently reviewed exact-head executable selection/delivery/provenance/outcome checks, demonstrated reversible decisions, comparison against a balanced baseline and an ethically reviewed longitudinal learner evaluation with missingness/selection-bias analysis. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge, Pre-A1 change, A1–C2 enablement or weakened B2 recognition-versus-open-retrieval boundary.

**Fixture verdict:** F206 defines a falsifiable audit of *why a method was selected* and *which later independent evidence was actually observed*. It deliberately prevents a method-selection dashboard from confusing correlation, assisted correctness or selective follow-up with durable learning gains. **UNSUPPORTED** until separately implemented and verified.
