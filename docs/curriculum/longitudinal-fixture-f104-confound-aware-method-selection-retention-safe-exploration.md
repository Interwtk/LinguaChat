# F104 — Confound-aware instructional choice, retention-safe exploration and independent unseen transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD and independently adjudicated source-linked learning evidence. No claim of causal instructional efficacy, human-learning improvement, shipped adaptive capability, CEFR certification, or production defect. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 requires genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish selected from actually rendered support; F47/F48 preserve noncompensatory CEFR evidence; F49 accessibility; F50 soft-interest breadth; F53 language variety/register; F74 source fidelity; F100 compositional transfer; F103 critical-detail read-back. **Distinct construct:** does the curriculum/decision evaluator distinguish a learner's *apparent* benefit from an instructional method from benefit explained by task difficulty, timing, teacher support, topic familiarity, modality access or selection bias, and choose a **reversible, uncertainty-aware next instructional action** without sacrificing delayed independent retrieval, required skills or breadth? This is not a claim that a deterministic fixture estimates causal treatment effects.

## Source-linked setup, units and protected invariants

Resolve the real versioned CEFR descriptor/can-do, level blueprint/prerequisites, authorized source tasks, language/variety/register, evaluator and rubric versions, baseline independent proficiency, required receptive/productive skills, assistance policy, independent-evidence threshold, due reviews, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer and exit gate. Declare the intervention decision point **before** observing its outcome: learner, capability, learning-day/session, eligible candidate methods, reasons each is feasible, source-authored task pool, task complexity, delivery channel/accessibility, available support and planned review horizon. If a CEFR objective, valid task, outcome or method cannot be mapped, report UNSUPPORTED rather than inventing a method winner.

Instructional methods are **reversible policies**, not learner identities: BALANCED_DEFAULT; WORKED_EXAMPLE_THEN_FADE; RETRIEVAL_FIRST; LISTENING_READING_ALTERNATION; FREE_PRODUCTION_WITH_FEEDBACK; CONTRASTIVE_ERROR_REPAIR; SHORTER_SPACING_WITH_REVIEW; and SOURCE_VALID_OTHER. Use only actually implemented/evaluable options. A method can change explanation style, supported modality mix, scaffolding, pacing, repetition/review spacing, activity order and examples. **Hold fixed** the shared CEFR objective, meaning and skill coverage, rubric/evaluator, independence threshold, due reviews, unseen-transfer requirements and exit gate. No auditory/visual/reading “learning style” label or permanent assignment.

Keep **topic interest** separate from method selection. Onboarding and demonstrated interests may softly weight preferred contexts, but adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts must remain eligible and deliberately sampled. Do not equate preferred-topic fluency with a modality advantage. An accessible accommodation is an entitlement, not a randomized treatment to withhold. Never withhold required instruction, force inaccessible media, delay overdue review, or randomize a learner into a known harmful or invalid condition.

For every eligible decision log: pseudonymous learner/capability; real session-qualified event ID; source/task/rubric/evaluator hashes; clock/seed; baseline and recent independent performance; recurring error types; prior exposure, practice dose, hint dependence and time since learning; modality accessibility; task difficulty and topic familiarity; candidate feasibility/exclusions; policy version; selected/assigned/**actually rendered** method, rendered assistance chronology and legitimate interlocutor stimulus; if randomized, the logged assignment probability/propensity and constraints **at the decision time**; learner's first unaided attempt; retries and assisted outcomes separately; offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up; delayed independent retrieval; genuinely unseen independent transfer; required-skill coverage and review debt. Reliable latency may be used cautiously; absent latency is unknown, not failure. Do not infer causal effect from a propensity that was never logged or from a retrospectively invented control group.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Simpson reversal from topic and difficulty.** Across multiple distinct real sessions, WORKED_EXAMPLE_THEN_FADE is offered mostly on familiar hobby tasks and RETRIEVAL_FIRST mostly on unfamiliar public-service tasks. The pooled assisted-correctness score favors worked examples. Within comparable task-difficulty/topic strata, later first-unaided transfer favors retrieval-first or is too sparse to rank. A matched control swaps the topic allocation while holding the task-specific independent outcomes constant: a valid next-method verdict must not follow the pooled assisted winner. Rotate preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics; do not permanently suppress either method.

**B — Teacher help and actual rendering change the verdict.** The policy selects FREE_PRODUCTION_WITH_FEEDBACK, but a tutor's completed answer appears before the learner's first reply. Another selected worked example never renders because the component unmounts. A third learner receives a legitimate interlocutor response after an unaided clarification, not an answer-bearing tutor cue. Late-arriving telemetry claims the first cue was delivered afterward. Reconstruct trusted rendering and response chronology: assisted first reply stays assisted regardless of receipt order; selected-but-unrendered help does not contaminate the first attempt; valid partner stimulus follows the frozen protocol. A method with high coached correctness is not thereby preferred for independent retention.

**C — Adaptive assignment bias and missing overlap.** An adaptive policy assigns CONTRASTIVE_ERROR_REPAIR almost exclusively after repeated independent errors, while BALANCED_DEFAULT goes to high-performing learners. Raw later scores make default look superior. Record the predecision baseline, error burden, source difficulty, selection policy and **true logged** propensities. In a matched control with overlapping eligible methods, evaluate comparable delayed independent outcomes and uncertainty; in a no-overlap world, return INSUFFICIENT_COMPARABILITY/UNSUPPORTED for a causal ranking, keep a safe balanced choice and log a permissible exploration opportunity. Do not manufacture inverse-propensity weights, certainty, counterfactual outcomes or fairness guarantees.

**D — Short-term win, delayed reversal and attrition.** LISTENING_READING_ALTERNATION produces rapid assisted success, but later unaided retrieval and unseen free production are weaker than with RETRIEVAL_FIRST on comparable source tasks. Some eligible delayed probes were offered but not rendered; others were rendered but never completed. In a matched alternate history, those missing learners are precisely the ones with recent struggles. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators, time windows and plausible attrition sensitivity; missing does not mean pass, fail or no effect. Favor the better **observed** later independent evidence only when comparability is adequate, maintain due reviews and keep exploring. Never assert that the method caused a retention gain without separately reviewed real-learner evidence.

**E — Skill imbalance and accessibility protection.** A learner excels at reading and writing with WORKED_EXAMPLE_THEN_FADE but has an overdue listening objective and weak unaided speaking transfer. A caption-only text proxy cannot certify listening; unavailable audio or speech recognition cannot be counted as learner failure. The policy must preserve required receptive/productive evidence and accessible alternatives, schedule due practice, and avoid boosting the apparent preferred method by ignoring missing skill domains. In a matched control, source-authorized accommodation restores access without weakening the certified skill requirement. A temporary modality shift must not become a permanent learner category.

**F — Offline replay, rubric drift, topic overfit and safe reversal.** Two offline sessions reuse a local event ID; a late tutor cue arrives before the assessed response in trusted event time; a rubric version changes an allowed regional form; the learner performs well in favorite-game contexts but fails matched unfamiliar community tasks. A valid first independent probe on a later learning day reverses the previous weak method preference. Deduplicate only genuine session-qualified replays; quarantine incompatible source/rubric/evaluator versions; preserve event-time cue provenance and unknown follow-up; rotate contexts; apply a bounded reversible method update and continue safe alternative sampling. Permuting network receipt order must not change independence, mastery, review debt or next-method decision.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source applicability and predeclared decision point.** A–F require real versioned CEFR can-do/blueprint, authorized tasks, rubric/evaluator, language/variety/register, accessibility, baseline, assistance policy, required skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Freeze eligible candidates and feasibility before outcomes. Missing source mapping, an unimplemented method or an unadjudicable outcome is UNSUPPORTED, not a manufactured improvement.

2. **First-unaided versus assisted correctness.** A/B/D/F separate first learner-authored unaided quality from retries, scaffolds, completed tutor examples and legitimate protocol-bound interlocutor replies. Score support based on **actually rendered** chronology, not selection or late telemetry. A high assisted score cannot silently substitute for independent mastery or transfer.

3. **Confound-aware comparability and Simpson control.** A/C/D explicitly stratify or disclose baseline proficiency, error burden, task difficulty, source, practice dose, timing, topic familiarity, modality/accessibility and tutor support. A pooled ranking that reverses within comparable strata must not determine the method. Sparse or imbalanced comparisons yield uncertainty, not false precision.

4. **Assignment policy, overlap and bounded inference.** C logs decision-time feasible candidates, actual selection policy and propensity when randomized. Missing propensities, non-overlap, changing policy or unmeasured confounding prohibit a causal ranking. A deterministic synthetic counterfactual is a test of decision logic, **not** a causal estimate for people. Preserve a safe reversible default when evidence is weak.

5. **Durable independent outcomes and denominator integrity.** A/C/D/F measure later first-unaided retrieval and genuinely unseen transfer across distinct real sessions, changing context/task surface and avoiding memorized repeats. Keep eligible, offered, rendered, completed and missing denominators separate. Report review age and exposure; do not label unknown as success or failure.

6. **Noncompensatory CEFR coverage and accessibility.** D/E/F cannot trade away required listening, reading, writing, speaking, free production, due reviews or exit gates to maximize one metric. Inaccessible modalities and invalid text/audio substitutions remain confounded or UNSUPPORTED; accessible accommodations cannot be withheld for exploration.

7. **Interest breadth independent of method.** A/F preserve preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts at comparable difficulty, with source-authored topic sampling and exposure logs. Interest may influence example probability but never excludes topics, changes objective/rubric or becomes a proxy for “learning style.” A topic-only improvement must not be misreported as method effectiveness.

8. **Reversible policy, uncertainty and event-order invariance.** B/C/D/F log feasible alternatives, assigned and rendered method, observed later evidence, confounds, exploration constraints, next-choice rationale and fallback. Favor delayed independent outcomes over short-term assisted correctness when comparable; allow reversal, avoid permanent classifications and keep bounded alternative sampling. Session-qualified replay, version quarantine and trusted cue/source chronology must produce identical verdicts under receipt permutations.

9. **Assertion-level audit and bounded claims.** A–F emit source/task/rubric/evaluator IDs, policy and seed/clock, decision-time feasibility/propensities if available, baseline and task/topic/modality controls, first unaided and assisted outcomes, cue chronology, follow-up denominators, required-skill/review status, candidate uncertainty, next-method trace and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED for each assertion. Documentation CI or synthetic PASS does not establish shipped adaptation, real learner benefit, CEFR award or a reproducible production defect.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command; frozen source-linked CEFR tasks and valid multilingual/variety-aware rubrics; deterministic seed/clock; actual delivered method/cue provenance; session-qualified events; first independent learner-authored attempts; delayed and genuinely unseen independent transfer; source-valid accessibility; matched or explicitly noncomparable task/method/topic conditions; real decision-time assignment metadata; follow-up denominators; protected skills/reviews/exit gates; replay/version permutations; and assertion-level audit. Without these, mark relevant assertions UNSUPPORTED/unknown.

A stronger claim that adaptive method choice **improves durable independent learning** requires separately reviewed longitudinal real-learner evidence, credible comparator or ethically reviewed experimental design with overlap, human adjudication, multilingual and accessibility validation, missing-data analysis, privacy/subgroup-harm review and monitoring for unequal opportunities. This fixture cannot itself prove efficacy. **A strong tutor tests competing instructional hypotheses cautiously, changes its teaching when independent evidence warrants it, and never sacrifices balanced language competence to chase easy scores.**
