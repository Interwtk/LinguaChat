Fluency, answering one prominent part, repeating the question, or giving a blanket “yes” cannot establish complete source-required communication. Conversely, an accurate bounded partial answer with an explicit unresolved part may satisfy a task that does not require full resolution. If the actual descriptor/task has no multi-part answer-scope demand, mark NOT_APPLICABLE; never manufacture a compound-question graduation gate.

## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — One fluent answer leaves a mandatory question unanswered.** A source-authored organizer asks, “What time does the workshop start, and is the room wheelchair accessible?” Both slots are mandatory for the recipient's source-defined planning task. Learner A gives a fluent start time and says “All set,” although accessibility is still unknown; the recipient assumes both are resolved. Learner B independently gives the verified time and explicitly says accessibility has not been confirmed, proposing a source-permitted clarification. B is not penalized for lacking an inaccessible fact. In a separate task where only the time was requested, the same concise time answer is complete. A later unseen inquiry changes slot count, order, topic and recipient goal.

- **B — A blanket yes has ambiguous scope.** A source-authored colleague asks, “Did you send the draft, and did the manager approve it?” The source proves sending but leaves approval unknown. Learner A answers “Yes” and the recipient falsely records approval. Learner B says “I sent it; I don't know whether it was approved,” or a source-validated equivalent. In a second world both are independently confirmed, a compact response is accepted only if target-language convention and recipient interpretation make its scope unambiguous. Negative question wording and conjunction order vary in the unseen probe; scoring follows slot truth and the actual recipient's understanding, not token count.

- **C — Dependent, optional and privacy-sensitive slots.** A source-authored service exchange asks for an appointment date, preferred contact method and an optional reason for the visit. The date is needed to schedule; contact preference is required only if follow-up is requested; the reason may legitimately be declined. Learner A fabricates a reason and treats a tentative date as confirmed. Learner B states the proposed date with its conditional status, answers the applicable contact slot and politely declines the optional reason. A later task introduces a dependent follow-up question that becomes inapplicable after a valid “no.” Do not award completion for inventing private details, nor penalize a validated boundary or a correctly marked inapplicable slot.

- **D — Source-authorized answer versus tutor-completed checklist.** In a source-authored audio inquiry with two requested details, a tutor displays a filled two-row answer map before the learner responds; learner A repeats it and appears fully correct. Learner B initially omits a detail, then later independently identifies both requested slots in a new accessible source-authored dialogue, answers the known part and asks a source-valid clarification for the other. A caption error hides one question in the original audio. Score first attempt, actually rendered answer-bearing cue and modality/access confound separately; do not treat the caption omission as poor comprehension or a later assisted correction as independent mastery.

- **E — Short-term slot checklists versus delayed independent recipient fidelity.** Across matched early sessions, `SLOT_MAP_THEN_RESPOND` yields high immediate correctness after visible filled checklists, while `PARTIAL_ANSWER_REPAIR_DIALOGUE` produces slower first unaided messages. In later **distinct real sessions** with genuinely unseen question bundles, changed interlocutors and recipient decisions, repair dialogue yields better independent identification of unanswered mandatory slots for this synthetic learner. Match or disclose baseline proficiency, number and dependency of slots, source ambiguity, task stakes, practice dose, feedback, register/variety, topic familiarity, modality/accessibility and answer length. Prefer completed delayed unseen outcomes to assisted short-term completeness; log an **actually rendered** changed next method, preserve uncertainty and continue exploring alternatives.

- **F — Interest overfit, late cue, replay and source revision.** A learner correctly answers bundled questions in favorite gaming chats but leaves required slots unresolved in neutral travel coordination and unfamiliar community events. A tutor's slot-by-slot model is rendered before the first reply but its telemetry arrives afterward; a second model was selected but never rendered. Two offline sessions reuse a local event ID; an authoritative source correction changes a slot from “confirmed” to “unknown”; a rubric revision validates a concise variety-specific multi-slot answer; an eligible delayed probe is offered but never completed. Reconstruct trusted render chronology, deduplicate only session-qualified true replays, quarantine incompatible source/rubric versions, retain unknown follow-up and review debt, rotate contexts and require invariant independence/slot verdicts and reversible next-method traces under receipt-order permutations.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real task applicability and frozen scope.** A–F emit a real versioned CEFR descriptor/can-do, blueprint/prerequisites, target language/variety, source-authored inquiry, recipient goal, atomic slot graph, mandatory/optional/dependent status, source truth/uncertainty, accepted answers, rubric/evaluator, assistance policy, threshold, required skills, reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Missing real mapping, invalid slot decomposition or unsupported target-language convention yields NOT_APPLICABLE/UNSUPPORTED, never invented proficiency.

2. **Correct slot segmentation and scope.** A/B/C distinguish independently requested subquestions, conjunction/disjunction, negative wording, shared modifiers and dependent follow-ups. A blanket affirmation or one answered clause cannot silently cover other slots. A task requiring one concise answer is not expanded into an artificial completeness test; a legitimately inapplicable follow-up is not treated as omitted.

3. **Faithful answer states without fabrication.** A/B/C score each source-required slot as answered with source-supported content, explicitly unknown, awaiting clarification, validly declined, inapplicable or genuinely omitted. Preserve tentative versus confirmed states, privacy boundaries and recipient actionability. An unknown or declined slot is not magically “resolved,” but may be a fully valid communicative outcome when the frozen task allows it.

4. **Recipient comprehension, variety and repair.** A/B/C/D verify the recipient's actual interpretation, acceptable target-language response forms and source-permitted clarification. Distinguish a concise unambiguous combined answer from a pragmatically ambiguous bare particle; never penalize appropriate uncertainty, accessible alternatives or valid regional conventions.

5. **First independent move and trusted render chronology.** A/D/F score the first learner-authored recipient-facing response against what was actually rendered. A filled answer map, highlighted omitted slot, complete model or tutor-authored question breakdown rendered before the move makes the affected target assisted; selected-but-unrendered material does not. Separate legitimate task stimuli, permitted interlocutor answers and unsolicited coaching.

6. **Delayed genuinely unseen independent transfer.** A–F require distinct real sessions, new source-authored multi-part questions, changed slot order/dependencies, first unaided responses and observable recipient outcomes. Emit eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Repeated question templates, copied slot maps, assisted repairs and uncompleted follow-ups cannot prove durable mastery.

7. **Comparable methods, accessibility and interest breadth.** D/E/F match or disclose slot count/type, source uncertainty, response length, baseline proficiency, interlocutor, practice dose, feedback, target variety/register, modality/accessibility and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic exposure. Sparse/conflicted evidence falls back to `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. Session-qualified replay deduplication, frozen versions and trusted cue-render chronology must yield identical slot/assistance verdicts, review debt and next method across receipt permutations; quarantine unresolved conflicts.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, deterministic seed/clock, real source-linked CEFR multi-part inquiries, validated target-language/variety slot interpretations, frozen task/slot/rubric versions, trusted cue-render chronology, first unaided recipient-facing responses, observable recipient interpretations, completed delayed genuinely unseen transfer with denominators, comparable method/topic/modality/access exposure, protected skills/reviews, receipt-order permutations, reversible next-method traces and assertion-level verdicts. Missing source mapping, safe accessible channel, reliable chronology, adjudicable slot truth or independently completed follow-up remains UNSUPPORTED/unknown.

Any claim that adaptation **improves durable independent multi-part communication** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, multilingual/variety and accessibility validation, human adjudication, privacy/subgroup-harm review and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor helps learners answer what they know, make the unanswered parts visible, and avoid pretending a partial answer resolves everything.**
