## Fixed CEFR objectives and independently reviewed causal-discourse oracles

Bind only existing versioned CEFR reading, listening, interaction, mediation or production can-dos that genuinely require explaining reasons, intentions, consequences or uncertainty. Preserve the exact can-do, prerequisites, target language/variety, rubric, thresholds, accessible route, first-independent evidence, delayed/unseen probes, receptive/productive balance and mastery/exit gates. If irrelevant, mark NOT_APPLICABLE, not a new language-level requirement. All scenarios are fictional low-stakes club, library, rehearsal and community-workshop communication, not real medical, legal, financial, emergency or safety-critical decisions.

An independently reviewed **causal-discourse oracle**, frozen before task delivery, records the utterance and speaker/source; explicit causal, purposive, temporal, concessive or inferential markers; antecedent and consequent propositions; asserted versus inferred relations; temporal direction; negation/scope; whether an intention succeeded; relevant alternative explanations; and acceptable ambiguity. It must separately represent (1) what a speaker *claims* caused an outcome, (2) what the fictional evidence independently establishes, and (3) what a recipient can responsibly report. Neither “after” nor repeated co-occurrence establishes causation. “Because” may encode a speaker's explanation without independent proof; “so that” typically marks intended purpose rather than achieved result. Treat target-language grammatical and discourse variation as reviewed possibilities, not English-only keyword rules.

A **recipient-facing explanation oracle** accepts natural target-language/variety-appropriate summaries, questions, corrections and uncertainty, including alternative phrasing with the same semantic commitments. Distinguish “X happened after Y,” “the speaker says Y caused X,” “Y was done to try to achieve X,” “X occurred as a reported consequence of Y,” and “we cannot tell what caused X.” Reject invented causal certainty, unsupported blame, inferred completion and claims of achieved purpose without outcome evidence. Do not reward a single dialect, accent, register or fixed sentence template.

An **actual-delivery oracle** logs reviewed text/audio hashes, target language/variety, displayed captions and subtitles, playable audio actually heard, visual arrows, diagrams, causal labels, worked examples, translations, hints, feedback and answer-bearing highlights **actually exposed before each attempt**. A diagram labeling an arrow “caused” is answer exposure. An optional diagram never opened is not exposed. Failed audio cannot count as unaided listening; accessible text may validly test reading or mediation but must not be relabeled listening.

A **first-authorship/provenance ledger** records pseudonymous learner, fixed can-do/rubric/oracle versions, stable session-qualified event IDs and replay deduplication; first eligible unaided interpretation and recipient-facing explanation; exact stimulus and delivered help chronology; assigned/offered/**actually delivered** method; context family and modality; due/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed/unseen probes with elapsed time and distinct-session provenance. Optional hesitation/latency is weak evidence only, never fabricated or a standalone mastery gate. Retain the first independent error even after an assisted correction.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Sequence is not proof of cause.** A fictional workshop note says, “The lights went out just after Maya plugged in the projector.” A learner reports, “Maya's projector caused the outage.” Reject TEMPORAL_ONLY_NOT_CAUSAL: the note establishes temporal sequence, not the cause. Accept “The outage happened shortly after Maya plugged in the projector; the cause is unknown.” Do not assert that the projector is innocent either. A later independent electrician's report could change the evidence, but that report must actually be provided and reviewed.

**B — Attributed explanation is not independently verified fact.** A fictional club organizer writes, “We met in the library because the rehearsal room was closed.” A learner tells a new member, “The organizer says the room closure was why they moved to the library.” Accept SPEAKER_CAUSE_ATTRIBUTED. Reject a stronger claim that an independently confirmed building closure forced the move when no separate evidence exists. If a second message says the organizer merely *thought* the room was closed, preserve that epistemic qualification. Conversely, do not reduce an explicit reported reason to mere temporal co-occurrence.

**C — Intended purpose is not achieved outcome.** In a fictional poster-design thread: “Nia enlarged the font so that new members could read the instructions more easily.” A learner reports, “Every new member could read the instructions afterward.” Reject PURPOSE_NOT_OUTCOME: the stated purpose does not establish the actual reading outcome. Accept “Nia enlarged the font to make the instructions easier to read; we haven't checked whether it worked.” A separately observed accessibility test could establish a narrower result, but never assume universal success.

**D — Consequence and its scope.** In a fictional art club log: “The printer jammed, so the posters were delivered late; the invitations were sent on time.” A learner says, “The printer delay made all the club materials late.” Reject CONSEQUENCE_SCOPE_PRESERVED: the stated consequence concerns posters, not invitations. Accept “The log links the printer jam to late posters, while the invitations went out on time.” This is an attributed causal report, not independent proof that no other factor contributed. Do not infer that posters were never delivered or that the jam affected every printer.

**E — Actual exposure, accessible route and first authorship.** At 09:02 a listening prompt with “Nia enlarged the font so that members could read it” fails playback; no audio is heard. No unaided listening evidence is available. At 09:03 an accessible text route actually displays the sentence plus a labeled arrow “purpose: easier reading, outcome: unknown”; at 09:04 the learner correctly distinguishes intention from result. Mark ACTUALLY_RENDERED_HELP, ANSWER_EXPOSURE and ASSISTED for the measured reading/interaction route, not independent listening. An unopened optional arrow diagram would not count. At 10:15 a new independently reviewed fictional scenario delivered without answer exposure may yield a new first unaided response in its real modality. Deduplicate replayed event IDs; neither a clock boundary nor immediate paraphrase proves delayed retrieval.

**F — Longitudinal method comparison, reversal and soft interests.** Learner A repeatedly promotes “after” to “because” on independent first attempts. Across comparable real sessions, **actually delivered** TEMPORAL_VERSUS_CAUSAL_CONTRAST, SPEAKER_CLAIM_VERSUS_EVIDENCE_CONTRAST and BALANCED_DEFAULT alternate with documented help and difficulty. Repeated meaningfully delayed first-unaided explanations to new recipients, new speakers and unfamiliar/neutral contexts may cautiously shift instructional probabilities while keeping alternatives sampled. Learner B initially benefits from causal-arrow cards, but later assumes every arrow proves a verified cause; reduce confidence, reverse or soften that preference and sample ALTERNATIVE_EXPLANATION_DIALOGUE. If arrows explicitly gave answers, one method had easier language, stronger interests, extra hints, clearer audio, shorter delays or more completed follow-up, record **no causal winner**. Assisted retries, same-story paraphrases and missing probes cannot establish durable transfer. Keep independent listening, reading and production/interaction evidence; interests only softly weight example topics and never gate objectives.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed CEFR and reviewed oracles.** A–F bind only relevant existing versioned can-dos, language/variety-specific causal and recipient oracles, actual delivery and unchanged mastery standards; otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED, never a new CEFR gate.
2. **Temporal versus causal.** A rejects an unsupported causal claim from “after,” preserves the supported event sequence and permits uncertainty without asserting innocence.
3. **Speaker attribution versus verification.** B preserves the explicitly reported reason and source/qualification while distinguishing it from independently established causation.
4. **Purpose versus observed result.** C rejects conversion of an intended benefit into an achieved outcome, while accepting appropriately qualified explanations.
5. **Consequence scope.** D preserves the distinction between delayed posters and on-time invitations; no global-delay or non-delivery inference is permitted.
6. **Actual delivery, accessibility and first authorship.** E counts only exposed cues, preserves the earliest eligible unaided evidence, marks playback failure unavailable, retains the actual measured construct and deduplicates replay; assisted corrections cannot earn independent mastery.
7. **Matched, uncertain, reversible adaptation.** F changes method probabilities only using comparable actually delivered options and later independent outcomes; sparse, confounded or contradictory evidence cannot establish a causal winner and must preserve exploration.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer and soft interests.** Require real distinct sessions, independently reviewed new causal relations, speakers and contexts, first-unaided recipient-facing explanations or justified clarification, and explicit missing follow-up. Rotate adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics without skipping fixed objectives or balanced modalities.
9. **Evidence and protected scope.** Synthetic prose or generic CI cannot establish executable causal-oracle QA, shipped outcome-driven adaptation or human retention gains. Require exact-head executable stimulus/provenance assertions, observed policy-decision changes and independently reviewed delayed unaided transfer against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, Pre-A1 changes, A1–C2 enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic acceptance histories, not observed learners or executable QA. Preserve distinctions among sequence, claimed cause, purpose and observed result; keep adaptation reversible and interests nonexclusive. Remain UNSUPPORTED until implementation and independently verified learning evidence exist.
