## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Inclusive permission versus exactly one.** A fictional club supplies a reviewed notice: **“You may join the drawing workshop, the photography workshop, or both.”** Both workshops are explicitly available and have no conflicting schedules. Learner A independently tells a friend “You must choose exactly one” and fails. Learner B says “You can choose either workshop or both” and preserves the source. Counterfactual: a revised notice states **“Choose exactly one workshop”**; selecting both now fails. A third notice merely says **“Choose drawing or photography”** without specifying exclusivity; do not automatically award a categorical “both allowed” or “both forbidden” claim. Credit “Does ‘or’ mean one only, or can I choose both?” when clarification is appropriate.

**B — At least one, not both, neither.** A fictional museum volunteer rota states **“At least one of Ana and Ben must attend; both may attend.”** A learner who reports “Exactly one must attend” wrongly adds exclusivity. Counterfactual 1: **“Ana and Ben must not both attend”** excludes joint attendance but, absent an attendance minimum, allows neither; it does **not** mean “neither may attend.” Counterfactual 2: **“Neither Ana nor Ben may attend”** excludes each. Counterfactual 3: **“Both Ana and Ben must attend”** requires both. Evaluate recipient-facing messages that preserve the option set, not isolated symbol manipulation.

**C — Option availability versus a question or tentative preference.** A fictional café guest asks **“Could I have tea or coffee?”**; the menu source has not established stock. The server's reply **“Which would you prefer?”** asks for a preference, not a verified stock guarantee. A learner who tells another server “Both are definitely available and the guest ordered tea” fails. A supported handoff says “The guest asked about tea or coffee; availability and final choice still need confirmation.” Counterfactual: after the café confirms both are available, the guest says **“Tea, please”** and the server acknowledges the order; a confirmed tea choice is now source-supported. **“Maybe tea”** remains tentative, and a later explicit change to coffee supersedes the earlier choice at its effective turn.

**D — Negation grouping and source-bound ambiguity.** A fictional club note says **“You cannot bring both a camera and a tripod.”** It forbids the pair but does not prohibit either item individually. A learner who tells a friend “You cannot bring a camera or a tripod” adds a restriction. Counterfactual: **“You cannot bring either a camera or a tripod”** (with reviewed intended reading) excludes both; **“You must bring a camera and a tripod”** requires both. If a spoken “not” or an ambiguous scope is unclear, accept a targeted clarification rather than pretending the source determines the exact allowed set. Do not generalize one English/Spanish translation of “not A or B” without a reviewed reading.

**E — Accessible channels, actual exposure and chronology.** A fictional voice note says **“You may bring a pen or a pencil, or both.”** ASR substitutes **“and”** for **“or”**; captions omit **“or both”**; a translation adds **“only one”**; a screen reader skips the second option. Log each **actually delivered** channel and quarantine construct-changing corruption rather than marking the learner wrong for it. A learner independently asks “Did you say ‘or both’?” at 10:00; an answer-bearing model renders at 10:01. The first clarification remains FIRST_UNAIDED; a copied explanation at 10:02 is ASSISTED. A replayed session-qualified event is not a second independent observation or a new learning day. An accessible text reconstruction can support text-based interpretation, not unheard listening. A revised source at 10:03 does not retroactively rewrite the 10:00 oracle.

**F — Longitudinal method reversal and nonexclusive interests.** Fictional learner A answers immediately after INCLUSIVE_EXCLUSIVE_MINIMAL_PAIRS but later independently treats **“not both”** as **“neither”** in a new unfamiliar equipment handoff. Multiple matched, actually delivered ALTERNATIVE_SET_READBACK opportunities precede better delayed first-unaided recipient-correct explanations across changed option structures and recipients. Only repeated comparable observations may cautiously favor readback, while preserving exploration. Fictional learner B initially benefits from OPTION_TABLE_WITH_EXPLICIT_UNKNOWNS but later confidently asserts both options are available after merely hearing an “A or B?” question; confidence falls, review becomes due and alternatives resume. If readback receives two explicit options but the table method receives four ambiguous alternatives, or only one method renders an answer-bearing hint, declare **no winner**. Changing “music” to “gaming” while retaining identical wording and option set is not unseen transfer. Missing later probes are MISSING, not learner failure. Interests never exclude neutral/unfamiliar topics, skip objectives or change required modality balance.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Existing CEFR objective and independently reviewed option oracle.** A–F require an existing versioned communicative can-do, prerequisites, source authority/version, recipient/information gap, option-set oracle, target language/variety/register, accessible delivered stimulus, evaluator/rubric, first-unaided threshold, due review, delayed and unseen probes and unchanged exit gate. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED; no invented logic-specific CEFR requirement.
2. **Inclusive/exclusive constraints are source-bound.** A credits “one or both” only where explicitly supported, “exactly one” only where explicitly required, and treats bare conversational “or” as context-dependent when exclusivity is unresolved. Do not confuse permission with a requirement.
3. **Lower bounds, conjunction and negation preserve alternatives.** B distinguishes “at least one,” “not both,” “neither” and “both” and never upgrades “not both” to “neither,” or “at least one” to “exactly one.”
4. **Question, availability and commitment are separate.** C does not infer stock from a question, or an order from a tentative preference. Credit confirmed selection only after reviewed conversational uptake; source-effective revisions supersede earlier choices without rewriting history.
5. **Scope grouping and multilingual validity.** D preserves the reviewed conjunction/disjunction/negation grouping, accepts meaning-equivalent target-language paraphrases and warranted clarification, and never imposes one universal inclusive/exclusive translation or punctuation rule.
6. **Actually delivered accessible chronological evidence.** E reconciles source and delivered audio/ASR/captions/translations/screen-reader option structure, quarantines meaning-changing corruption, preserves first authorship before actual help, deduplicates stable events and distinguishes accessible-equivalent from different constructs. Text cannot prove unheard listening.
7. **Controlled uncertain reversible instructional adaptation.** F compares feasible actually delivered methods under matched option count, explicitness, source completeness, negation burden, modality, language, topic, help, spacing and follow-up. Sparse, contradictory, selectively observed or confounded evidence yields no winner; delayed regression reduces confidence and restores exploration.
8. **Delayed first-unaided unseen transfer and nonexclusive interests.** Hinted choice tables, copied summaries, immediate repetition and topic-only swaps cannot earn delayed/unseen mastery. Require a later distinct eligible session, new source/recipient/context **and changed alternative-set or scope structure**, reviewed oracle, first-unaided actionable handoff and explicit follow-up missingness. Preserve shared CEFR objectives, receptive/productive balance and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation.
9. **Honest implementation and efficacy boundary.** A green documentation CI is not a shipped choice-scope evaluator, an outcome-responsive tutor or proof of improved human retention. Require exact-head deterministic executable assertions against real objectives, logged delivered instructional changes, later independent transfer against a balanced baseline, and separately reviewed learner evidence before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs work, threshold weakening, level enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are fictional design/probe cases, not observed learner histories or executable QA. Preserve alternative-set ambiguity, real conversational uptake, delivered-channel fidelity, first-authorship chronology and explicit UNSUPPORTED gates until implementation and evaluation evidence exist.
