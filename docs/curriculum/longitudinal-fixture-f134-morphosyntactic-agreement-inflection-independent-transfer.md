## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Controller versus nearby noun attraction.** In an independently reviewed fictional standard-English task, “The **keys** to the cabinet **are** missing” has plural controller *keys* and plural verb *are*, despite the intervening singular *cabinet*. Learner A writes “The keys to the cabinet is missing” after following the nearest noun; learner B independently identifies the controller and says “The keys are missing.” Counterfactual: “The **key** to the cabinets **is** missing” changes the controller to singular even though the nearest noun is plural. The oracle must change with the dependency graph, not with word proximity or a simplistic token-count heuristic. A learner's understandable message and their specific agreement evidence may be scored separately under the real rubric; neither one item nor a correction copied from the tutor establishes CEFR mastery.

**B — Feature bundle across Spanish noun phrase and predicate.** In an independently validated fictional Spanish-variety task, “**Las casas blancas están abiertas**” expresses plural houses, with number and grammatical noun-class agreement on article, adjective and predicate. Learner A reproduces the article but leaves the adjective and predicate in a singular form; learner B independently produces a licensed plural phrase and correctly explains that multiple houses are open. Counterfactual: “**La casa blanca está abierta**” changes number and licensed inflections. A separate reviewed example with a masculine grammatical noun must not be used to infer a person's gender or identity. The oracle requires validated target-language and variety-specific feature dependencies; it must not overgeneralize Spanish endings to all languages or assume every word ending in -a is feminine.

**C — Inflection, suppletion and temporal meaning are separable.** Fictional English sources contrast “Every day, she **goes** home” and “Yesterday, she **went** home.” The first encodes a habitual present context with third-person singular agreement; the second is past with irregular suppletion. Learner A uses “goes” for the yesterday event after memorizing a present-tense chart; learner B independently selects the appropriate licensed form and communicates the intended time. Counterfactual: change “she” to “they” in the present habitual clause; a validated singular-they or plural-they referent uses “they **go**,” not “they goes,” while the person's identity remains unspecified. Another counterfactual changes the time adverb but keeps the original verb; the rubric must detect a source form–meaning conflict rather than reward surface matching. Do not require advanced irregular or aspectual forms before independently validated prerequisites.

**D — Language-specific absence of a feature is not learner failure.** In a reviewed Mandarin Chinese task, the independently validated source may express habitual work without English-style third-person-singular verb agreement; a learner's correct Mandarin production must not be marked wrong for lacking an English “-s.” In a separately reviewed English task, “They work” is licensed for an appropriate singular-they referent; an evaluator must not impose “they works” based on singular semantic reference. A fictional code-switched turn is evaluated only under independently validated language-boundary and borrowing rules; an unreviewed mixed utterance is UNSUPPORTED for the affected feature, not automatic evidence of low proficiency. Counterfactual: change the target language, variety or grammatical environment and re-resolve the entire dependency oracle. Grammatical category labels must never be copied across languages as if they shared identical morphology.

**E — Delivered form, accessibility, assistance chronology and replay.** A source-authorized English recording clearly distinguishes “The dogs **are** ready,” but the actually delivered caption says “The dog **is** ready”; a TTS clip truncates an inflectional ending; ASR drops a spoken final consonant; a keyboard silently autocorrects “go” to “goes”; or a screen reader announces a highlighted answer before the learner's turn. Freeze **both** authoritative and actually delivered channels. Contradictory or inaccessible morphology evidence is UNSUPPORTED for the affected construct; silently corrected learner text is ASSISTED unless an independently authored original is preserved and attributable. A first response authored at 10:00 stays FIRST_UNAIDED if a conjugation table was queued at 09:59 but first **rendered** at 10:01; a later response copied from it is ASSISTED. Replay of the same immutable session-qualified event ID cannot manufacture a second first attempt or delayed follow-up. Accessible equivalents must preserve the same feature contrast or explicitly record non-equivalence.

**F — Reversible method selection and unseen communicative transfer.** Fictional learner A immediately fills color-highlighted subject–verb blanks correctly but fails delayed first-unaided agreement when an intervening noun appears. After **actually delivered** DISTRACTOR_RESISTANCE_PRACTICE with gradually faded prompts on comparable tasks, A independently produces a new instruction to an unfamiliar recipient with correct agreement and meaning. Fictional learner B instead improves delayed oral and written form–meaning fidelity after MEANING_FIRST_NUMBER_CONTRAST with comparable feature and lexical load. Freeze baseline, real CEFR descriptor, target language/variety, controller–target distance, distractor number, irregularity, lexical familiarity, sentence complexity, modality/accessibility, topic, hint exposure, review interval and follow-up opportunity. If A sees regular verbs with adjacent controllers while B receives irregular forms and deeply embedded clauses, mark METHOD_IRREGULARITY_CONFOUNDED / METHOD_DEPENDENCY_DISTANCE_CONFOUNDED and infer **no method winner**. Renaming nouns while preserving the trained dependency graph and answer path is not unseen transfer. Track eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-ups; missing is neither success nor failure. Repeated comparable delayed unaided outcomes may justify **actually changed next instruction**, with uncertainty, alternative sampling and reversal after contrary results. Preserve balanced skills, due reviews, shared CEFR gates and nonexclusive topic rotation.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real CEFR, language and feature linkage.** A–F resolve versioned descriptor/blueprint/prerequisites, independently reviewed language/variety/register morphology and alternatives, dependency graphs, source-effective versions, rubric/evaluator, first-unaided thresholds, accommodations, protected skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Advanced agreement chains are not silently assigned to beginner objectives.
2. **Controller–target and number fidelity.** A/B identify the actual agreement controller and targets, preserve number and noun-phrase/predicate feature dependencies, and change the oracle when the controller or feature bundle changes. Nearest-noun heuristics, isolated endings and cosmetic name swaps are insufficient.
3. **Inflection and meaning alignment.** C distinguishes person, tense/aspect, habitual context and irregular/suppletive forms, preserving what the source actually entails. Contradictory temporal adverbs or pronoun/verb counterfactuals change the expected evidence; a memorized table alone does not.
4. **Language/variety validity without identity inference.** B–D accept reviewed language-specific alternatives, zero marking, singular-they agreement, regional variants and valid code-switch boundaries. English/Spanish paradigms are not universal; grammatical noun class never authorizes inference of a person's gender identity.
5. **Separate communicative intelligibility from construct-specific form evidence.** A–D distinguish independently meaningful communication from demonstrated agreement accuracy according to the validated CEFR rubric; do not award mastery for copied corrections or impose a single prestige register. Unsupported linguistic judgments stay UNSUPPORTED, not fabricated errors.
6. **Actual delivery, accessibility, authorship, help chronology and replay.** E checks authoritative versus delivered audio, ASR, TTS, captions, autocorrect, screen-reader order and morphological highlighting. Answer-bearing help **actually exposed before** first authored response contaminates independence; queued-but-not-rendered help does not. Duplicate event replay is idempotent; defective channels quarantine only affected constructs.
7. **Delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen communicative transfer.** A–F require separate real learning opportunities, changed dependency structures/communicative recipients, first-unaided interpretation or free production without answer-bearing help, and eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Renamed trained sentence skeletons do not qualify as unseen transfer.
8. **Reversible adaptation, matched difficulty and soft interests.** F records feasible/assigned/**actually delivered** methods, comparable controller distance, distractors, irregularity and lexical load, uncertainty, alternative sampling, contrary outcomes and **observed change in later instruction**. Interests only probabilistically bias examples; adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts, CEFR objectives, protected skills and due reviews remain.
9. **Exact-head deterministic reproducibility and bounded claims.** A–F emit deterministic seed/clock, named exact engine HEAD and runnable command, descriptor/source/dependency/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, source-effective and delivered artifacts, authored first/help/event chronology, independently reviewed morphology oracle, feature/language/variety/caption/ASR/TTS/autocorrect/replay/source-version counterfactuals, confounds, delayed/unseen denominators and **PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion**. Documentation-only CI cannot establish a shipped morphology evaluator, reproducible runtime curricular defect or improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and reproducible command; real versioned CEFR-linked objectives; independently reviewed language/variety-specific morphological dependencies and licensed alternatives; accessible authoritative and actually delivered stimuli; independently attributable first attempts and actual answer-bearing help chronology; delayed unaided retrieval and genuinely unseen feature-dependency transfer; comparable or explicitly confounded instructional methods; protected skill coverage, due reviews and exit obligations; complete eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; and controller/target/number/person/tense/irregularity/variety/language/ASR/TTS/caption/autocorrect/replay/source-version counterfactuals with per-assertion verdicts. Unvalidated morphology, contradictory delivered channels, inaccessible feature contrasts, absent or missing follow-ups remain **UNSUPPORTED/unknown**, not learner failure.

A stronger claim that adaptive morphology instruction **improves durable independent communication** requires a separately reviewed, privacy-appropriate, multilingual and accessibility-validated real-learner longitudinal study with credible comparisons, attrition/subgroup-harm analysis and reproducible delayed outcomes. **A strong tutor helps learners connect grammatical forms to meaning and use them independently in new situations, rather than merely copy the correct endings.**
