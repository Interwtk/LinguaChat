# F45 — Cohort-level instructional patterns cannot be assigned to an individual learner

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a reproduced defect, shipped adaptive tutor, causal estimate, learner-level treatment effect or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F24 guards assignment bias; F28 blocks cross-skill extrapolation; F32/F40 guard selective follow-up and learner-level harm; F39 separates external instruction; F41 guards selective reporting; F44 separates engagement from learning. F45 isolates **ecological inference**: a cohort, course, school or objective-level method association is not evidence that this particular learner benefits from that method. Even an apparently stable cohort result cannot supply an unobserved individual counterfactual. Aggregate data may motivate a **low-confidence, reversible option to sample**, never a fixed learner type, independent mastery decision or individualized superiority claim.

## Controlled setup

Resolve `goal-1` to one **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, genuinely supported skill, frozen rubric, prerequisites and independent mastery/exit gate. Compare feasible A (`concise_explanation`), B (`worked_example`) and the shared balanced comparator. Match actual task demand, item novelty, independent scoring, delay definition, due reviews and context coverage where feasible. Use a deterministic injected clock/seed, a versioned `ecological_inference_guardrail_policy` and an immutable synthetic ledger across real elapsed days and distinct sessions.

Record stable learner/cohort/objective/skill/session/run/event IDs; **cohort definition and membership validity period**; observed baseline readiness, opportunity, assignment mechanism and known selection probability (unknown stays unknown); offered/assigned/verified-delivered instruction and intervening external practice when actually known; first-attempt quality, hints, models, retries and support; genuinely delayed independent retrieval and unfamiliar transfer; follow-up eligible/completed/missing counts; learner and opportunity weighting; actual event versus upload times; assessment/rubric/policy versions; and data-use lineage. Cohort labels are descriptive and must not be inferred from sensitive or protected traits. No subgroup membership, demographic proxy, causal effect or personal response may be fabricated.

Maintain separate ledgers for (1) cohort-level descriptive associations, (2) individual verified instructional exposure, (3) individual independent CEFR evidence and (4) uncertain, objective-specific individual method hypotheses. Declare `ECOLOGICAL_INFERENCE_BLOCKED`, `COHORT_MIX_SHIFT`, `WITHIN_GROUP_REVERSAL`, `INDIVIDUAL_EVIDENCE_ABSENT`, `GROUP_PRIOR_ONLY`, `COHORT_NOT_COMPARABLE`, `MEMBERSHIP_STALE`, `FOLLOWUP_UNKNOWN` and `UNSUPPORTED`. A cohort-level signal can guide safe exploration only if actually eligible and uncertainty remains explicit.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — Strong cohort B advantage, no individual evidence.** Several cohorts show higher delayed independent B scores, but a newly enrolled learner has only a baseline and one assisted B practice, with no valid later retrieval or transfer. The tutor may offer B among balanced options, but must mark the learner-specific effect `INDIVIDUAL_EVIDENCE_ABSENT`/`GROUP_PRIOR_ONLY`; it cannot claim B is best for this learner, assign a permanent learning style or graduate them.
- **B — Simpson reversal from cohort composition.** In each comparable observed readiness stratum, A has equal or better delayed independent outcomes than B; pooled data favor B because B was delivered more often to learners with stronger baselines. Report stratum and pooled denominators, assignment/availability and follow-up differences. Flag `WITHIN_GROUP_REVERSAL`/`COHORT_NOT_COMPARABLE`; do not export the pooled B ranking to any individual or manufacture a propensity score.
- **C — Learner contradicts a legitimate cohort trend.** Comparable cohort-level B results favor B, but one learner repeatedly shows stronger delayed unaided retrieval and novel free production after verified A than after comparable B occasions. Preserve the descriptive cohort result, prioritize valid learner/objective evidence for a provisional personal mix, lower confidence where attribution is uncertain and keep nonzero feasible B/balanced sampling. Do not call the cohort false or claim proven B harm.
- **D — Membership changes without an ability change.** A learner switches class/cohort mid-term; the new cohort's aggregate preference favors A, while no new personal instructional or independent assessment event occurs. Changing membership or a cohort summary refresh alone must not rewrite the learner's mastery, first-attempt history or established individual evidence. A group prior, if used at all, is time/version-bounded and subordinate to the learner's evidence; `MEMBERSHIP_STALE` must block backdated assignment.
- **E — Sparse group, selective follow-up and privacy.** A small observed subgroup appears to favor B, but follow-up completion is much lower after A, and the subgroup is so small that publishing member-level slices could expose individuals. Retain internal aggregate uncertainty and eligibility/missingness accounting under appropriate privacy controls; suppress unsafe small-cell reporting, withhold personalized efficacy claims and use the balanced curriculum. Do not infer sensitive traits or treat missing probes as failures.
- **F — Offline replay and aggregate leakage into mastery.** A stale cohort-summary event arrives after a newer summary, a delayed probe is uploaded twice, a B card was displayed but not delivered, and a reused assessment item was leaked as a model answer. Deduplicate by stable event IDs and trusted occurrence time, preserve membership/version lineage, quarantine contaminated probes and prevent cohort scores from becoming individual mastery events. Two replay orders must yield identical individual CEFR evidence, hypotheses and cohort denominators.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Four-layer provenance.** For A–F separately report versioned CEFR can-do/rubric/skill, cohort descriptive evidence and membership interval, individual offered/assigned/verified-delivered instruction, valid independent mastery observations, and the learner/objective method hypothesis with uncertainty. Cohort averages cannot be silently copied into individual outcomes.
2. **Cold-start conservatism.** In A, an apparently favorable B cohort result with no learner-specific delayed independent evidence is `GROUP_PRIOR_ONLY`, not a personal treatment effect or fixed style. Preserve a feasible balanced default, alternative sampling, required due reviews and unchanged mastery gates.
3. **Within-group versus pooled audit.** In B, publish observed readiness strata, eligible/delivered/followed-up denominators and learner- versus opportunity-weighted outcomes. Expose the reversal and mark unsupported causal/individual claims `ECOLOGICAL_INFERENCE_BLOCKED`/`COHORT_NOT_COMPARABLE`; do not fabricate assignment probabilities or counterfactuals.
4. **Individual contrary evidence survives.** In C, repeated comparable learner-specific delayed unaided retrieval and unfamiliar transfer can revise an uncertain objective-specific method mix despite a different pooled ranking. Do not erase cohort observations, overstate individual causality, transfer the hypothesis to another skill or eliminate feasible exploration.
5. **Membership is not performance.** In D, cohort entry, exit or relabeling cannot create, delete or rescore a learner's first attempts, independent mastery, delivered exposure or due-review debt. Membership validity intervals and policy versions determine which descriptive context was available when a decision was made.
6. **Sparse, missing and private groups.** In E, publish safe aggregate eligibility/completion/missingness and uncertainty while suppressing disclosive small-cell detail; do not infer protected traits, impute missing results as zeros, or assert group-specific superiority without comparable follow-up and adequate evidence.
7. **Replay and contamination invariance.** In F, stable event IDs, trusted event/upload chronology, original cohort/rubric/policy versions, verified delivery and assessment-answer exposure produce identical counts and decisions under reordered replays. Duplicate or answer-contaminated probes cannot become independent transfer or group evidence.
8. **Shared CEFR and interest boundary.** Across A–F keep fixed can-do, prerequisites, rubric, independent exit gates, due reviews, required receptive/productive opportunities and neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar topic rotation. Interests remain a soft context layer; unsupported listening/speaking stays `UNAVAILABLE`, never inferred from cohort outcomes.
9. **Conservative evidence and release.** Without comparable verified personal exposures, genuinely delayed independent retrieval, unseen transfer, adequate follow-up and a credible balanced comparator, learner-level method superiority is `UNSUPPORTED` regardless of cohort significance. Retain valid narrower mastery observations; synthetic PASS is a software invariant, not human efficacy.

## Required evidence and release gate

For A–F publish the **exact tested engine HEAD**, executable command, deterministic seed/clock, immutable synthetic ledger, real supported versioned CEFR can-do and frozen rubric, cohort membership/definition/version, safe cohort and readiness-stratum denominators, assignment and actual delivery, individual first attempts/assistance, calibrated actual-delay unaided retrieval and unseen transfer, eligible/completed/missing follow-up, event/upload and data-use lineage, replay invariance, review/skill/context obligations, uncertainty/reason codes and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED for every assertion. Include a shared balanced comparator; do not invent counterfactuals, unsupported modalities, protected-group membership or absent outcomes.

Future claims of durable learner-specific benefit require separately reviewed longitudinal learner evidence against a credible balanced baseline, sufficient independent delayed retrieval and unfamiliar transfer, explicit uncertainty/heterogeneity and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A group trend can suggest a question to test, not answer what works for an individual.** A passing documentation fixture, if achieved, establishes only a specified software acceptance invariant.
