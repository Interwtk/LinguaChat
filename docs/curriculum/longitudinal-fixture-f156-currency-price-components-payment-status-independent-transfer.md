## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Currency identity, symbol and locale.** A fictional seller's reviewed bilingual label explicitly states **USD 12.50** for one ticket. Learner A independently tells a Chilean recipient '12.500 pesos chilenos'; learner B independently says 'twelve US dollars and fifty cents' or 'USD 12.50'. A forwarded cropped screenshot showing only **'$12.50'** has no verified currency: the correct communicative response requests the currency rather than assigning USD or CLP. Counterfactual: **'1,500'** with no trusted locale is ambiguous, even if the reader is accustomed to one separator convention. No conversion to CLP is warranted without a reviewed rate and timestamp. This tests language and source preservation, not FX proficiency.

**B — Per-item, per-order and incomplete totals.** A reviewed fictional ticket quote says **two tickets at USD 8 each plus one USD 3 booking fee per order, with all applicable taxes explicitly included**. The stated total is **USD 19**. Learner A independently says 'USD 11 per ticket, so USD 22'; learner B independently tells the recipient 'USD 19 for both tickets, including the one order fee'. Counterfactual: if the USD 3 fee is **per ticket**, the total is USD 22; if tax inclusion is **not stated**, do not claim USD 19 is a guaranteed tax-inclusive checkout total. The evaluated act is the correct recipient-facing explanation and clarification, not arithmetic speed.

**C — Discount conditions and charge components.** A fictional quote states **USD 50 before tax**, with **20% off the item price only if a stated voucher is valid**, and tax/other fees not supplied. A verified eligible voucher yields **USD 40 before tax/fees**, not a guaranteed final USD 40 charge. Learner A independently announces 'you will pay exactly USD 40'; learner B says 'the discounted item price is USD 40 before any unspecified tax or fees'. Counterfactual: an expired voucher leaves the stated item price at USD 50 before tax/fees. A changed source revision is effective only from its reviewed date; never combine old discount rules with new fees.

**D — Deposit, card authorization and refund status.** A fictional rental notice distinguishes a **USD 30 refundable security deposit**, a **USD 5 nonrefundable service fee**, and a card event marked **authorization pending**. Learner A independently reports 'USD 35 was permanently charged and refunded'; learner B independently says 'the notice lists a USD 30 potentially refundable deposit and USD 5 fee; the card authorization is pending, so it does not prove capture or refund'. Counterfactual: a later reviewed settlement receipt may support a captured amount, while a separate refund confirmation is required to claim a refund. A stated refundable deposit does not guarantee return regardless of conditions. Do not infer bank processing times.

**E — Recurrence, channels and chronological evidence.** A fictional subscription notice says **USD 9 per month after a seven-day introductory period**, but does not specify a verified first charge date, applicable time zone or cancellation effect. Learner A independently tells a friend 'it is USD 9 once, forever'; learner B independently states the monthly recurrence and asks for the missing timing/cancellation terms. A delivered audio says 'nine dollars'; ASR outputs 'ninety dollars', a caption drops 'per month', a translation changes '9,50' into '950', and a screen reader skips a separate mandatory-fee line. Log the **actually delivered** stimulus and quarantine construct-changing corruption instead of penalizing a learner. If the learner independently asks 'nine or ninety?' at 10:00, then an answer-bearing preview renders at 10:01, the first clarification remains unaided while later copied totals are ASSISTED. Replaying a session-qualified event does not create a new learning day. An accessible text alternative can support written price comprehension but not unheard audio discrimination.

**F — Longitudinal method choice, reversals and interest separation.** Fictional learner A immediately succeeds after PRICE_COMPONENT_HIGHLIGHTING but later independently omits per-order fees in a new unfamiliar booking handoff. Comparable, actually delivered CURRENCY_AND_RECIPIENT_READBACK opportunities precede better delayed first-unaided explanations across different source structures and recipients. Only repeated matched outcomes may cautiously increase confidence in readback, with continued sampling of alternatives. Fictional learner B initially succeeds after QUOTE_VS_PAYMENT_STATE_MINIMAL_PAIRS but later independently claims a pending authorization is a refund; confidence decreases, review becomes due and balanced alternatives resume. If one method gets simple tax-inclusive tickets while another gets conditional subscriptions with missing fees, or one reveals the total in a tooltip, mark confounding and declare **no winner**. Merely changing 'concert ticket' to 'game ticket' with identical numbers/template is not unseen transfer. A missing later probe is MISSING, not a failed learner. Interest-weighted examples never exclude neutral/unfamiliar topics or alter required objectives, evidence thresholds or skill balance.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Existing CEFR objective and reviewed source-world oracle.** A–F require an existing versioned communicative can-do, prerequisites, recipient/information gap, independently reviewed transaction source/oracle, target-language/variety/register, accessible delivered stimulus, evaluator/rubric, first-unaided threshold, due reviews, delayed and unseen probes, unchanged exit gate. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED; no invented finance-specific CEFR mastery.
2. **Currency and numeral provenance.** A preserves explicitly stated ISO currency and locale. A bare '$' or locale-ambiguous number remains UNKNOWN; neither inferred national currency nor invented exchange rate receives credit.
3. **Component and fee-basis integrity.** B distinguishes per-item from per-order fees and explicit tax inclusion. The reviewed USD 19 versus USD 22 counterfactuals are accepted only with the matching source basis; incomplete tax information prohibits a guaranteed final-total claim.
4. **Conditional discounts and source revision.** C applies discounts only when stated conditions are verified, distinguishes discounted item price from tax/fee-inclusive total, and does not silently combine superseded source revisions.
5. **Transaction-state and deposit discipline.** D distinguishes refundable deposit from nonrefundable fee, authorization from capture, pending from settled, and refundable eligibility from actual refund. A refund claim requires independently reviewed confirming evidence.
6. **Actually delivered accessible chronological evidence.** E reconciles source with rendered ASR/captions/translations/screen-reader order, quarantines meaning-changing corruption, preserves first authorship before actual help exposure, distinguishes accessible-equivalent from different construct and deduplicates immutable session-qualified events. Text cannot prove unheard listening.
7. **Controlled uncertain reversible adaptation.** F compares feasible actually delivered methods under matched complexity, source completeness, numeracy, currency familiarity, modality, register, topic, help, interval and follow-up. Sparse, contradictory, selectively observed or confounded evidence yields no winner; delayed regression reduces confidence and restores exploration.
8. **Delayed first-unaided unseen transfer and nonexclusive interests.** Assisted total calculations, immediate repeats, template-number swaps and hinted payment-state labels cannot earn delayed/unseen mastery. Require a later distinct eligible session, new source/recipient/context **and changed price-component or transaction structure**, reviewed oracle, first-unaided actionable handoff and explicit follow-up missingness. Preserve shared CEFR objectives, receptive/productive coverage and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation.
9. **Honest implementation and efficacy boundary.** A document or green CI is not a shipped price evaluator, actual outcome-sensitive teaching or proof of improved human retention. Require exact-head deterministic executable assertions against real objectives, logged delivered instructional changes and later independent transfer compared with a balanced baseline; a separately reviewed learner pilot is required for efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs work, threshold weakening, level enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are fictional design/probe cases, not collected learner observations or executable QA. Preserve all unknowns, source boundaries, payment-state distinctions, genuine independent chronology and explicit UNSUPPORTED gates until implementation and evaluation evidence exist.
