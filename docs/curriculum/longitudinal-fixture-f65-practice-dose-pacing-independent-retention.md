# F65 — Adaptive practice dose and pacing must earn durable independent retention

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a reproduced production defect, implemented dose scheduler, fatigue detector, CEFR certification, or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F26 addresses delayed-retrieval opportunity; F27 guards latency bias; F32 addresses selective follow-up; F36 guards review opportunity cost; F37 distinguishes assigned from delivered instruction; F43 isolates assessment-induced learning; F44 rejects engagement proxy optimization; F49 protects accessibility; F59 addresses desirable challenge; F64 balances fluency and accuracy. **F65 isolates dose and pace:** how much practice and how quickly it is sequenced must be revisable instructional choices, not proxies for effort, motivation, ability or mastery. Optimize meaningful later *unaided* retrieval and transfer per feasible opportunity while maintaining all source-required CEFR evidence.

## Controlled setup

Resolve an **actual versioned** CEFR can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, source-defined communicative task, modality, evaluator/rubric, language burden, assistance rules, meaningful delayed-retrieval requirement, unseen-context transfer, due reviews and exit gate. Freeze all source standards before varying instructional schedules. Do not invent universal ideal lesson minutes, attention-span thresholds, response-speed cutoffs, spacing intervals, fatigue labels or mastery shortcuts. Missing capture, evaluator or objective mapping is `UNAVAILABLE`/`UNSUPPORTED`, not inferred success.

Predeclare feasible, source-valid instructional candidates `SHORT_DISTRIBUTED_PRACTICE`, `LONGER_CONTIGUOUS_PRACTICE`, `EARLIER_BREAK_AND_RESUME`, `SLOWER_SCAFFOLD_FADE`, `FASTER_INDEPENDENT_PRODUCTION` and `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. These are **methods to test**, not permanent learner types or medical/psychological judgments. Preserve the required receptive/productive skill mix and due-review obligations; a candidate that omits an unavailable modality cannot award its CEFR credit.

For each comparable learner/objective history, record versioned objective/rubric, actual eligible and delivered task opportunities, selected versus actually rendered pacing, session-qualified immutable event IDs, trusted event occurrence versus upload time, task order, planned and observed practice exposure, active versus idle/wait time only if trustworthy, item novelty, topic familiarity, assistance/model/cue delivery chronology, first unaided semantic response, recurring error types, revisions and scaffold dependence, optional reliable latency, completed/abandoned sessions, independent delayed retrieval and unseen transfer, and follow-up eligible/offered/completed/missing denominators. Preserve learner-chosen pauses and noncompletion as observed opportunity information; never treat missing follow-up as failure or as evidence that a method works.

Predeclare evidence states `DOSE_ASSIGNED`, `DOSE_DELIVERED`, `DOSE_PARTIAL`, `ACTIVE_TIME_UNKNOWN`, `PACE_OBSERVED`, `PACE_CONFOUNDED`, `FIRST_UNAIDED`, `ASSISTED_REVISION`, `INDEPENDENT_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL`, `INDEPENDENT_UNSEEN_TRANSFER`, `FOLLOWUP_MISSING`, `COMPARISON_CONFOUNDED`, `REVIEW_DEBT`, `RUBRIC_MISMATCH`, `EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED`, `UNAVAILABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. These are task/event labels, never identity labels.

## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Massed immediate success versus distributed later recall.** Two comparable learners practice the same source-defined objective with equivalent total *delivered* independent task opportunities and target exposure. A completes a long block with many correct assisted final answers and high immediate recognition but fails a meaningful delayed unaided request. B receives shorter separated practice, shows modest immediate success and later completes an unseen request independently. Distinguish assisted correctness, true dose, delayed opportunities and transfer. B's schedule is only provisionally favored if item difficulty, feedback, topic and assessment exposure are comparable.
- **B — A slower pace is not automatically better.** Learner A has repeated meaning-critical errors on first attempts and benefits from a slower scaffold fade with independent checks. Learner B already transfers the same objective unaided and a permanently slowed sequence consumes time while leaving required unfamiliar-context production untested. Compare later independent outcomes, not completion time or tutor interaction count; adjust pace separately for each objective and reverse decisions when evidence changes.
- **C — Latency, access and idle-time confounds.** A learner uses a permitted assistive input route; another pauses because the interface is offline; a third drafts an asynchronous response with no reliable start timestamp. An analytics pipeline incorrectly treats wall-clock gaps as practice duration, hesitation and low proficiency. Mark `ACTIVE_TIME_UNKNOWN`/`PACE_CONFOUNDED`; retain any valid first-attempt content evidence without inferring attention, disability, motivation or a slow-learner type. Do not transform a text response into verified oral fluency evidence.
- **D — Breaks, noncompletion and selective follow-up.** A learner completes short practice bursts and independently returns for later probes; another is assigned a long session but stops before most activities render; a third receives a delayed probe but cannot complete it. Log assigned, rendered, attempted and completed dose and eligible/offered/completed/missing follow-up separately. A high score among only the long-session completers cannot establish that longer sessions improve learning; unobserved outcomes remain unknown.
- **E — Topic preference, review debt and a policy reversal.** An early run suggests fast game-themed practice is effective; later source-equivalent neutral and unfamiliar transfer reveals weak independent production and a due review is missed. A balanced slower alternative improves delayed unaided results after matched opportunities. Reduce confidence and revise the objective-specific pace/dose hypothesis, preserve alternative sampling, schedule due review and rotate interest, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts. Neither the preferred topic nor a faster apparent completion rate can displace required objectives or modalities.
- **F — Offline replay, cue leakage and version drift.** An assigned 20-minute block renders only three minutes of tasks; idle time is counted as practice; the same response uploads twice; two sessions reuse a local attempt ID; a hint appears before a revision but syncs afterward; local clock and time-zone changes reverse the apparent event order; and a rubric version changes. Reconstruct trusted delivered events with session-qualified IDs and source-version boundaries, quarantine uncertain cue timing and active duration, deduplicate replay, and ensure either upload order produces identical independent-evidence and next-policy decisions. Do not infer an exact dose or compare incompatible rubrics.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Frozen objectives and noncompensatory mastery.** For A–F output actual versioned CEFR can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, task, modality, evaluator/rubric, required meaning/accuracy/interaction criteria, allowed assistance, delayed and unseen transfer, due reviews and exit gate. Unknown mappings remain `UNSUPPORTED`; faster or longer practice cannot waive independent CEFR evidence.
2. **Assigned dose is not delivered dose.** In A/D/F separate candidate, selected, assigned, rendered, attempted, completed and actually experienced instructional tasks; distinguish meaningful active practice from idle/wait time when measurable. Do not award exposure or independent attempts for unrendered tasks or an uncompleted session.
3. **Independent first attempts and assistance remain distinct.** In A/B/F preserve the first unaided semantic attempt, delivered hints/models, learner-initiated repair, assisted revisions and final answers in trusted event order. Immediate assisted correctness and long streaks cannot earn delayed or independent transfer credit.
4. **No fabricated pacing measurement or learner diagnosis.** In C/F require validated event timestamps and observability before using duration, latency, breaks or speaking rate. Missing, offline, accessibility-confounded and asynchronous measurements stay unknown. Never infer a fixed learning style, laziness, disability, fatigue or ability from response speed.
5. **Dose comparisons need matched exposure and opportunity.** In A/B/D compare only source-compatible tasks with recorded prerequisite readiness, total actually delivered exposure, novelty, difficulty, topic familiarity, modality, feedback, order, assessments and opportunity denominators; otherwise mark `COMPARISON_CONFOUNDED`. Distinguish more practice from better spacing or faster pacing.
6. **Later unaided retention and transfer determine adaptation.** In A/B/E require meaningful elapsed/intervening practice and genuinely unseen source-valid independent probes, with eligible/offered/completed/missing follow-up counts. Repeated same-item recognition, immediate correction and delayed probes contaminated by fresh hints cannot establish durable gains.
7. **Reversible pacing with protected obligations.** In B/E output alternative schedules, chosen and delivered dose, independent outcome evidence, uncertainty and next reversible choice. Contradictory delayed outcomes lower confidence; preserve due reviews, source-required receptive/productive modalities and unfamiliar contexts, with balanced default under sparse data.
8. **Soft interests, learner agency and replay determinism.** In D/E/F allow learner-initiated breaks and topic interests without treating them as proficiency, while rotating adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar contexts. Replay receipt permutations with stable session-qualified event IDs, exposure chronology and frozen rubric versions must yield identical evidence ledgers and next-policy decisions; ambiguous records fail closed.
9. **Assertion-level audit and no efficacy inflation.** For A–F emit source IDs, task and rubric versions, delivered dose and pacing observability, first/final response and assistance chronology, independent delayed/unseen outcomes, eligible/offered/completed/missing denominators, review and skill debt, comparison confounds, uncertainty, replay equivalence and per-assertion `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED`. Documentation CI cannot establish that a dose scheduler exists or improves human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, seeded clock and reproducible synthetic multi-session histories, real source-resolved CEFR task/rubric versions, reliable delivered-practice and cue chronology, session-qualified immutable IDs, independent first-attempt scoring, comparable dose/pacing opportunities or explicit confounds, meaningful independently completed delayed retrieval and unseen-context transfer, missing-follow-up denominators, protected review/skill/topic coverage, reversible decision traces, receipt-order permutations and assertion-level verdicts. Unknown active time, contaminated assessment, missing modalities and incomplete follow-up remain unknown rather than being filled in.

A claim that personalized pacing/dose **improves durable independent learning** requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, matched exposure and opportunity, adequate delayed unaided outcomes, missingness and sparse-data checks, accessibility/agency safeguards and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor changes practice pace because independent learning evidence warrants it, not because a learner clicks faster or spends longer on a screen.**
