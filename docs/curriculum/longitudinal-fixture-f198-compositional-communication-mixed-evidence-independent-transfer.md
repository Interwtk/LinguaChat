# F198 — Compositional communication across mixed evidence, changing group state and independent transfer

**Status:** design-only synthetic longitudinal curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until independently reviewed target-language/variety-specific mixed-discourse oracles, verified source delivery and first-authorship provenance, executable exact-head tests, and genuinely delayed first-unaided unseen transfer bind to existing versioned CEFR can-dos. Not a new CEFR objective, composite graduation gate, event-management system, shipped adaptive tutor, or proof of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F189 tests attribution and uncertainty; F191 answer relevance; F193 information access; F194 proposal versus group decision; F195 task ownership and conditional progress; F196 feedback, revisions and version approval; F197 quantity scope, bounds and denominators. **Distinct construct:** can a learner combine *several already-taught communicative distinctions in one coherent, source-faithful recipient-facing response*, without laundering one valid fact into an unrelated claim, losing which version is current, or answering only an easy subset? Isolated success on each component does not establish independent integration. Conversely, a single mixed-task failure does not erase valid component-level evidence or create a new CEFR requirement.

## Fixed CEFR objective, compositional oracle and evidence boundaries

Bind only **existing versioned** CEFR can-dos whose real communicative tasks naturally require combining source information across turns (for example, an appropriate-level short update, clarification, interaction, mediation or message). Freeze can-do ID, prerequisite graph, level-specific language burden and turn ceiling, target language/variety, source/task/rubric versions, help policy, accessible route, first-unaided and delayed/unseen standards, thresholds, receptive/productive coverage and exit gates. A1 examples may combine only simple already-required statements; complex multi-source coordination is NOT_APPLICABLE unless an existing objective supports it. Do not turn the fixture into a compulsory multi-construct test for every learner. Preserve the unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix disagreement, 20-meaningful-turn ceiling and closed B2 gate.

An independently reviewed **mixed-discourse oracle** freezes the exact utterances, speaker/addressee/channel/timestamp, which messages and artifact versions were actually visible to each participant, reference groups and quantity units, explicit decision/approval rules (if any), source-licensed uncertainty, relevant dependencies and target-language/variety conventions. Track each claim separately as SOURCE_STATED, REPORTED_BY_SPEAKER, LOWER_OR_UPPER_BOUND, APPROXIMATE, PROPOSED, CONDITIONALLY_PROMISED, CONFIRMED, REJECTED, VERSION_APPROVED, RECEIPT_UNKNOWN, SUPERSEDED_BY_VISIBLE_UPDATE or UNKNOWN. These are *source-linked statuses*, not a generic trust score. A newer message changes only the fact and audience it actually updates. Do not invent a policy that the latest speaker always overrides an earlier authorized decision.

An independently reviewed **recipient-purpose and coverage oracle** specifies which facts the recipient needs to act or understand, what can be omitted without harm, whether one targeted clarification is warranted, and which apparently relevant statements would be misleading. Require concise, natural, communicatively adequate responses rather than a rigid checklist recitation. Preserve valid paraphrases and cultural/variety alternatives; do not award credit for dumping every source line if the answer leaves a material question unresolved. Never assume a reply was read, a proposal accepted, a draft approved, a numerical limit reached or an optional task completed without source evidence.

An **actual-delivery and first-authorship ledger** records pseudonymous learner, immutable session-qualified event IDs and replay deduplication, fixed can-do/source/oracle/rubric versions, original source turns and revisions actually rendered, channel and captions/transcripts/audio accessibility route, answer-bearing examples/hints/tables actually displayed, first eligible unaided recipient-facing response, subsequent assisted repair, error location and cross-claim dependency, optional reliable latency, topic familiarity, delivered instructional method/dose/confidence, and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed probes. A source mentioned in a fixture but never delivered cannot justify learner credit or blame. An unopened hint is not exposure; a rendered complete state table is answer-bearing help. Failed audio earns no listening evidence; captions may support a valid reading/mediation route only. Replays, trivial renamings and midnight boundaries do not create new independent attempts or delayed transfer.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — A numeric limit and an unapproved plan are separate facts.** A fictional library note says, “The reading room can hold up to twenty people.” Maya proposes Friday; Theo replies “Friday works for me”; no designated organizer confirms the date. The learner must update Nora, who asks whether the room and date are ready to announce. Accept “The room's maximum is twenty, but we haven't confirmed the Friday plan or current available places.” Reject “Twenty places are booked for Friday” and “The group approved Friday.” If a later visible organizer message confirms Friday, the learner may update the date status but still cannot infer current free seats.

**B — Claimed completion, visible correction and approval must not collapse.** A student-club poster has Room 3. Nora requests Room 5 and praises the heading. Maya says “I changed it and sent the new poster,” but no revised file is shown. The learner must brief Theo: “Nora liked the heading and asked for Room 5; Maya says she changed and sent it, but we haven't checked the new version or received approval.” Reject “Nora approved the corrected poster” and “The visible poster definitely says Room 5.” A later source-visible version 2 with Room 5 supports the revision, not approval; a later explicit approval for version 2 applies only to that version.

**C — Partial group replies, source attribution and recipient access.** An organizer writes to Maya, “Eight of twenty invitees confirmed; five declined; the rest haven't replied.” Maya privately tells Theo, “I think everyone has seen the update.” The learner is asked to tell Nora what is known. Accept “The organizer reports eight confirmations and five declines out of twenty; other replies are not recorded. Maya thinks everyone saw the update, but we have no evidence Nora received it.” Reject “Twelve declined,” “Everyone saw the update,” or treating Maya's belief as the organizer's verified claim. A later explicit acknowledgment from Nora changes Nora's receipt status only.

**D — Multi-turn state updates and justified clarification.** A book-exchange group says each participating club may bring up to two guests. Nora offers to prepare signs; Maya asks her to do so, but Nora has not accepted. Theo then confirms that the venue permits a maximum of ten guests total. The learner must tell the coordinator what remains open. Accept “There is a two-guest limit per club and a ten-guest overall cap. Nora offered to help with signs but hasn't confirmed the assignment.” If the number of participating clubs is required for a total estimate, one targeted question is justified; do not fabricate a grand total. A later “Yes, I'll make the signs” changes the task assignment only, not the guest limits.

**E — Rendered mixed-state answer, missing audio and replay cannot manufacture integration.** At 09:01 the learner independently reads A's sources and incorrectly announces “Friday confirmed; twenty booked.” Preserve both first-attempt errors. At 09:02 the tutor renders a complete two-column source/status answer map; at 09:03 the learner copies the correct summary. Record ANSWER_EXPOSURE and ASSISTED, not independent integration mastery. Replaying the same sources at 09:04 is deduplicated. In a listening variant audio fails but captions render: no listening credit; a separately bound reading route may be valid. An offered but unopened hint is not exposure. Later independent transfer needs a genuinely new session, unseen source constellation, recipient goal and semantic relationships—not the same poster with renamed people.

**F — Longitudinal method choice, cross-component counterevidence and soft interests.** Learner A accurately reports counts and approvals in isolation but repeatedly fuses them in mixed updates. Across comparable real sessions, SINGLE_CLAIM_SOURCE_TRACING, TWO_CLAIM_INTEGRATION and BALANCED_DEFAULT are actually delivered and alternated. Tentatively favor one only if later first-unaided unseen mixed updates improve without sacrificing component accuracy under matched source count, language burden, topic, help, channel and delay. Learner B initially improves with a completed state map but later copies the map mechanically, omitting the recipient's actual question; counterevidence lowers confidence and restores balanced sampling or a recipient-purpose recap. If a method received shorter sources, answer-bearing examples, familiar gaming clubs or earlier follow-up, mark confounding and **no causal winner**. Missing follow-up remains unknown. Gaming interest may softly weight one example; neutral libraries, adjacent clubs, unfamiliar workshops and required CEFR objectives/modalities remain reachable.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **No invented composite CEFR gate.** A–F bind only applicable existing versioned can-dos, independently reviewed source/recipient oracles and actual source delivery. Preserve prerequisites, level burden, turn ceilings, evaluator meaning, thresholds, skill balance and exit gates; otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE/UNSUPPORTED.
2. **Cross-claim integrity.** A preserves capacity versus availability and proposal versus decision simultaneously; a valid numeric statement cannot license a false date confirmation.
3. **Revision, status and version separation.** B distinguishes praise, request, claimed change, source-visible version and version-specific approval; new evidence updates only supported claims.
4. **Source, group and audience scope.** C preserves exact source attribution, confirmed/declined/unanswered denominators and recipient-access uncertainty; a speaker's belief does not become a verified group fact.
5. **Dependencies without invented commitments.** D preserves per-club and global caps, offer versus acceptance and justified missing-denominator questions; do not infer task completion or total guests.
6. **Actual delivery and first authorship.** E distinguishes original independent errors, rendered answer maps, assisted copying, unopened hints, failed audio, valid captions routes and replay; no phantom listening or duplicate independent credit.
7. **Matched reversible adaptation.** F changes uncertain instructional hypotheses only after comparable actually delivered methods and stronger later first-unaided integration; component-load, topic, help, channel, visibility or delay confounds lower confidence.
8. **Delayed unseen integration and nonexclusive interests.** Require genuinely distinct sessions, independently reviewed new multi-source/recipient structures, unaided responses, explicit missing follow-up, no same-script leakage, balanced receptive/productive coverage and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation. Never skip a required can-do for interests.
9. **Honest evidence and protected scope.** Synthetic histories and generic CI are not executable mixed-discourse QA, a shipped adaptive tutor or evidence of improved human learning. Require exact-head executable source/delivery/response/provenance checks, observed reversible decisions and ethically reviewed delayed independent transfer against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge, Pre-A1 changes, A1–C2 enablement or weakened B2 boundaries.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic acceptance histories, not observed learners or executable tests. The goal is source-faithful communication when multiple ordinary facts interact, without replacing component-level CEFR evidence or falsely claiming adaptive efficacy. Remain UNSUPPORTED until implementation and independent verification.
