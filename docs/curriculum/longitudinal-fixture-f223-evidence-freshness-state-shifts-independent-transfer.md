# F223 — Evidence freshness, learner-state shifts and reversible instructional hypotheses

**Status: DESIGN-ONLY / UNSUPPORTED.** Synthetic longitudinal curriculum acceptance fixture, not a shipped adaptive tutor, CEFR certification, proof of learning gains or authorization for a live experiment. Pre-A1 remains frozen and A1–C2 unavailable. No Auth, cloud, infrastructure, production, Supabase, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge changes.

**Governing contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F220 covers session interruption, F221 external assistance, F222 method-selection confounding. **Distinct question:** can a tutor revise its instructional hypothesis when the learner, task, measurement conditions or evidence change over time, without fossilizing a once-useful method or treating elapsed time and context changes as proof of forgetting?

## Fixed standard and temporal evidence ledger

Bind every synthetic history to an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, target language and accepted varieties, reviewed meaning/function rubric, prerequisites, objective difficulty, required receptive/productive skills, supported accessible channels, independent mastery/exit criteria and due-review obligations. Illustrative contexts are not new objectives. Text-only practice cannot certify speaking or listening. This fixture does not resolve the B2 Arc 6 recognition/open-retrieval disagreement.

Freeze a **pre-decision** record: pseudonymous learner; objective, task, rubric, content, policy and channel versions; stable session/run/event/attempt IDs; trusted occurrence and answer-bearing render chronology distinct from arrival time; first valid unaided attempt and reviewed semantic quality; retries, hints, models, transcripts and tools actually exposed; error type; latency only when reliably measured; earlier independent delayed retrieval and unseen transfer; prior method/dose; due/offered/rendered/completed/missing counts; feasible accessible alternatives, learner choice, topic family, reviewed difficulty; selection reason, uncertainty and **next method actually rendered**. Do not rewrite an earlier decision with later-arriving observations.

**Evidence freshness is not mastery expiry.** Time since a comparable clean probe may increase uncertainty or motivate a fresh assessment. It must not manufacture a failed attempt, revoke a valid historical independent achievement or prove ability loss. Only new valid evidence under unchanged CEFR standards can change the current review need and instructional hypothesis. A changed task or rubric is not silently the same instrument.

Instructional choices are driven by comparable reviewed learning outcomes; interests only softly reweight examples. Neither interests nor device reliability create permanent learner types. Retain neutral, adjacent and unfamiliar topics, skill debt, and sampling of feasible alternatives. Missing, duplicate, untrusted and assisted observations cannot create false independent improvement or decline.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories and matched controls

**A — A previously useful scaffold may cease to be the best supported option.** Sessions A0/A1/A2 contain three distinct-session, reviewed delayed unaided probes following worked-example instruction, with recurring independent repair errors. Scaffolds remain reasonable. A3/A4/A5 later contain three meaningfully delayed, unseen, unaided successes following feasible retrieval-first/free-production practice, without answer-bearing exposure. Unsafe policy freezes a permanent “worked-example learner” label. **Expected:** preserve early and later evidence, competing hypotheses and uncertainty; select a justified *actually rendered* lower-scaffold opportunity or balanced alternative without claiming observational causality. **Matched control:** change A3–A5 into model-assisted repeats with identical surface correctness and dates. Those repeats cannot establish a new independent method winner; an unseen clean probe remains due.

**B — Absence is not an incorrect response.** After clean independent performance at B0, the learner is absent for 45 elapsed days; three reviews become due but none is offered, rendered or completed. Unsafe policy inserts three failures, downgrades CEFR and declares the previous method ineffective. **Expected:** due=3, offered=0, rendered=0, completed=0, missing=3; retain historical evidence, mark current retention UNMEASURED, and offer an accessible re-entry probe. No universal 45-day forgetting rule. **Matched control:** a valid, actually rendered, independently reviewed, meaningfully delayed unseen re-entry probe at B1 fails. This new evidence may increase review/support and uncertainty, but the absent days still contain no failed attempts.

**C — Rubric revision is not invisible learner regression.** C0/C1 use reviewed task/rubric v1 to assess the meaning/function of an information request. At C2 a reviewed v2 changes a meaning-critical requirement or difficulty. Identical surface language now receives a different score. Unsafe policy pools v1 and v2, attributes the lower score to forgetting and switches method. **Expected:** preserve raw answers and immutable versions, emit NOT_COMPARABLE without independently reviewed construct/difficulty equivalence or a new v2 baseline; do not silently retroactively rescore. **Matched control:** an independent reviewer establishes that v2 changed presentation only and signs an equivalent-construct, equivalent-difficulty scoring bridge. Comparability may be cautiously restored; no causal winner is implied.

**D — Device reliability is not language ability.** D0/D1 contain clean, reviewed listening outcomes with functioning audio. D2 has failed playback, unknown transcript exposure and unreliable latency; a speaking probe is offered but never rendered. Unsafe policy treats delay and missing audio as language failure, selects text forever and marks oral debt complete. **Expected:** quarantine unreliable latency, mark affected outcomes UNKNOWN/NOT_RENDERED, retain required listening/speaking debt and choose feasible accessible practice or a later valid channel-specific probe. **Matched control:** audio works, delivery is learner-compatible and a distinct-session, genuinely rendered independent listening/speaking probe supplies reviewed language evidence. Only that result, not the device change, can inform skill mastery.

**E — Interest drift does not imply method-effect drift.** A learner formerly prefers gaming contexts and later demonstrates more interest in cooking. The soft topic sampler adjusts probabilities but retains gaming, cooking, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts for the same reviewed can-do and difficulty. Unsafe policy invents a new auditory/visual type or excludes neutral topics. **Expected:** interests may change, while objective, rubric, exit criteria, skill debt and method-confidence evidence stay unchanged absent new comparable outcomes. **Matched control:** add repeated, reviewed, meaningfully delayed unseen independent results under feasible alternative methods, holding the interest shift constant. Only the new evidence may revise the instructional hypothesis after comparability checks.

**F — Late arrivals, replayed successes and contradictory outcomes.** F0 pre-choice snapshot has two feasible methods, due listening review and low-confidence balanced policy. F1 commits a valid independent transfer success with stable ID F-T1; F2 replays F-T1; F3 claims success with UNKNOWN answer visibility; F4 arrives late but occurred *before* F1, containing a separate valid independent failure; F5 queues a listening review that is never rendered. Unsafe policy counts the replay, credits UNKNOWN, calls queued review complete, rewrites the earlier F1 choice with F4 and locks a method. **Expected:** reconstruct trusted occurrence/render chronology, deduplicate F-T1, quarantine F3, retain F4 only for subsequent decisions, preserve listening debt and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; record a feasible **actually rendered** F6 activity with its contemporaneous choice snapshot. Sparse contradictory evidence warrants a documented balanced fallback. **Matched control:** reverse only the later independently reviewed clean transfer result while preserving objective, difficulty, aid exposure, delay, accessible channel, opportunity and event identities. A later method hypothesis must be revisable or justify the same balanced fallback. A topic-interest swap cannot alter method confidence.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Objective/release invariance:** A–F preserve existing versioned CEFR objectives, reviewed rubrics, prerequisites, mastery/exit standards and skill coverage; no Pre-A1 edit, A1–C2 release or invented can-do.
2. **Time is uncertainty, not fabricated failure:** B's 45-day gap and three missing reviews do not create failures, automatic CEFR downgrades or a deterministic forgetting score.
3. **Clean evidence and provenance:** A/B/F distinguish first valid unaided attempts, meaningful delayed unseen transfer, answer-bearing exposure, assisted repeats, trusted occurrence versus arrival and stable event IDs.
4. **Versioned comparability:** C refuses to pool changed constructs, difficulty or rubrics without independently reviewed equivalence; historical raw outcomes remain auditable.
5. **Channel/accessibility fairness:** D separates device failure and unreliable latency from language errors; unavailable channels cannot earn oral/listening mastery or create permanent labels.
6. **Interest-method separation:** E updates soft context weights without changing method confidence absent independent evidence; adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts and all required objectives remain eligible.
7. **Contradiction and missingness:** F deduplicates replays, quarantines UNKNOWN, preserves due/offered/rendered/completed/missing counts, never backdates a decision and never counts queued work as delivered.
8. **Reversible delivered adaptation:** A–F preserve competing uncertain hypotheses and accessible feasible alternatives; matched controls permit a changed next **actually rendered** method or a justified balanced fallback, not a post-hoc invented recommendation.
9. **Executable/efficacy boundary:** machine-readable A–F histories and matched controls must include deterministic seed/clock, versioned source bindings, independently reviewed linguistic and comparability oracles, and per-assertion PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED, executed by an independent exact-head harness. Synthetic invariant PASS cannot establish improved human learning; any efficacy claim additionally requires an ethically reviewed longitudinal comparison with delayed unaided retrieval, unseen transfer, missingness and accessible subgroup checks, without hidden required-skill regression.

## Independent verification requirements

A future harness must report exact curriculum and engine HEAD, executable command, resolved objective/task/rubric/channel versions, trusted event and answer-render chronology, pre-choice feasible candidates and actual delivered dose, reviewed first-attempt/delayed/transfer eligibility, version-comparability verdict, due/offered/rendered/completed/missing counts, before/after uncertain hypotheses, matched-control result, per-assertion PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED and independent reviewer signoff. Until that exists, **all F223 acceptance outcomes are UNSUPPORTED**.

**Verdict:** a strong tutor should change its teaching when valid new learning evidence arrives, not when time passes, rubrics change, interests shift, devices fail or late events replay. Adaptation remains uncertain, reversible, skill-balanced and grounded in independent durable transfer rather than assisted correctness.
