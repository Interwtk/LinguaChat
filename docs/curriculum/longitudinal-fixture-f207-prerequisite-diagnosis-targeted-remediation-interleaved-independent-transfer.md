# F207 — Prerequisite diagnosis, targeted remediation and interleaved independent transfer

**Status:** design-only synthetic longitudinal *cross-cutting* curriculum acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until independently reviewed language/variety-specific diagnostic and response oracles, exact-head executable provenance/decision checks, and genuinely delayed independent learner evidence exist. This is not a new CEFR objective, prerequisite gate, released feature, medical/cognitive diagnosis, proof of causal improvement or permission to open A1–C2. Pre-A1 stays frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F206 audits *selection bias and incomplete outcomes* in method comparisons. **Distinct question in F207:** when the learner makes a recurring communicative error, can the tutor distinguish a plausible *underlying prerequisite gap* from a superficial error, offer a bounded targeted repair, and later verify independent performance in **mixed, unseen communicative contexts** without confusing copied corrections or drill familiarity with mastery? Error categories are provisional task hypotheses, never permanent learner types.

## Fixed objective, competing hypotheses and reviewed diagnostic oracles

Bind an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, prerequisites already defined by the shared curriculum, target language/variety, communicative intent, baseline skill family, rubric/oracle versions, task difficulty, help and accessibility policy, meaningful-turn ceiling where applicable, independent first-attempt standard, delayed retrieval and unseen transfer standard, and unchanged mastery/exit thresholds. Example English utterances below are *illustrative only*: they do not assert that a particular A1/B1/C1 can-do or prerequisite is currently implemented. Unsupported level, modality or linguistic diagnosis is NOT_APPLICABLE/UNSUPPORTED. Preserve the unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix boundary; do not silently resolve it.

The **diagnostic oracle** must preserve the learner's intended meaning, actual utterance, prompt, available cues, source channel, error position, and independently reviewed set of plausible explanations. For example, "She doesn't likes coffee" may involve do-support agreement, verb-form selection after an auxiliary, memorized chunks or a slip; the surface string **alone cannot prove which cause**. Use short, level-appropriate **non-answer-bearing contrast probes** that distinguish hypotheses while preserving the communicative objective. A correct probe is evidence about that probe, not an automatic root-cause certificate. Ambiguous, sparse, inconsistent or inaccessible observations leave the cause UNKNOWN and use balanced instruction.

At decision time, freeze the objective/prerequisite versions, prior first-unaided evidence, competing hypotheses and uncertainty, chosen diagnostic/repair option, eligible alternatives, reasons for excluding unavailable options, planned intensity, topic family and expected later probes. An **actual-delivery ledger** records stable session-qualified event IDs, prompt and context, rendered/heard examples, hint and model exposure, attempted answers, first authorship, retries, repair, accessibility and channel failures, and completion provenance. A proposed contrast not delivered cannot diagnose anything. A displayed answer is exposure even if the learner does not copy it; an unopened hint is not. Deduplicate replays and stale duplicate writes.

The **outcome ledger** separates (a) first-unaided performance before help, (b) assisted repair and near-copy, (c) unaided performance on a different *untrained* realization, (d) genuinely delayed unaided retrieval in a distinct learning session with intervening material, and (e) **interleaved independent transfer** where the learner must choose the right construction among other plausible constructions rather than simply repeat a blocked drill. Record due/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-ups and genuine uncertainty. A calendar-day boundary, paraphrased same script, answer-bearing feedback or topic-only substitution does not establish delayed unseen transfer.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — One surface error does not identify a prerequisite.** In an English communicative task, the learner independently writes "She doesn't likes coffee." A naive tutor permanently labels them "bad at present simple" and assigns ten identical worksheets. The reviewed oracle instead records competing possibilities (auxiliary construction, verb form after auxiliary, memorized phrase, slip). Two short *non-answer-bearing* probes ask for equivalent negative and affirmative meanings with different subjects. If results are inconsistent or too sparse, mark CAUSE_UNDETERMINED and retain BALANCED_DEFAULT; no permanent type or automatic downgrade. A later example that explicitly supplies "doesn't like" is answer exposure and cannot retroactively make the first response correct.

**B — Target the diagnosed distinction, then fade support.** Across distinct completed sessions, first-unaided probes consistently show the learner can form affirmative third-person statements ("She likes coffee") but selects an inflected verb after "doesn't" in several new negative sentences. A reviewed oracle may now provisionally favor the auxiliary-plus-base-form distinction. Deliver a bounded AUXILIARY_FORM_CONTRAST and GUIDED_SELF_CORRECTION without rewriting the whole CEFR goal. An immediately copied "She doesn't like coffee" is ASSISTED_REPAIR, not mastery. On a later unseen task the learner independently asks about a colleague's preferences using a different verb and subject; record that response under the existing can-do and rubric. If the probe was shown in advance, it is not unseen.

**C — Blocked drill fluency is not interleaved communication.** The learner gets 9/10 on a blocked exercise where every item is a negative sentence after the same worked example. In a mixed, unseen exchange requiring an affirmative statement, a question, a negative reply and a polite clarification, they repeatedly apply the negative pattern in the wrong place. Record BLOCKED_DRILL_SUCCESS and independent INTERLEAVED_UNAIDED_CHOICE failure separately; do not promote the can-do or declare a durable repair. Rebalance activity ordering and introduce mixed contextual selection, then require a genuinely delayed independent mixed exchange. Never count same-day ten retries as ten retention observations.

**D — A plausible diagnosis can be wrong and must reverse.** The tutor initially suspects a tense-form gap because the learner writes "I goed yesterday." Later independent contrasts show correct selection of past versus present in unfamiliar narratives but inconsistent recall of a few irregular past forms. Lower confidence in the broad tense hypothesis; consider a narrow lexical-form hypothesis while preserving narrative sequencing and meaning standards. A learner who succeeds on a supplied "went" model but fails an unseen independent account still has no delayed mastery. When contradictory evidence appears again, reopen alternatives rather than freezing a "grammar learner" identity. No single error proves a cognitive condition.

**E — Access, assistance and incomplete follow-up cannot fabricate remediation benefit.** The tutor chooses a listening contrast, but playback fails and only the written transcript renders. Do not mark listening instruction delivered or award listening evidence; a separately bound reading diagnostic may be valid. The learner initially answers incorrectly, opens a hint that contains the answer, then repeats it correctly. Retain FIRST_UNAIDED_RESPONSE, CONTRAST_ANSWER_EXPOSED and ASSISTED_REPAIR. A duplicate event replay is deduplicated. A delayed probe was due but never rendered; mark FOLLOWUP_MISSING, not success or failure. If no accessible diagnostic is available, use the supported balanced fallback and label the intended route UNSUPPORTED.

**F — Avoid remediation loops and topic overfitting.** A learner interested in games receives repeated gaming-themed negative-sentence drills and becomes fast on familiar scripts. Meanwhile required listening, free production, unfamiliar-context transfer and another due can-do are being crowded out. Flag OVER_REMEDIATION_DETECTED and REQUIRED_OBJECTIVE_PRESERVED; schedule balanced, accessible opportunities with preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts. Sample both targeted and balanced instruction in appropriately matched tasks. Only later unaided, delayed and unseen mixed communication can support a tentative benefit. If neutral-context transfer fails despite gaming drill success, reduce confidence and adapt reversibly; interest does not become a prerequisite, a method or an exclusive topic filter.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Existing CEFR and prerequisite standards are immutable.** A–F bind existing versioned can-dos, relevant already-defined prerequisites, reviewed language/variety oracles, independent mastery/exit standards, help policy and skill balance. No new graduation gate, unauthorized level opening or silent B2 resolution.
2. **Surface error is not a diagnosis.** A records multiple plausible explanations, uses non-leaking contrast probes where supported, and preserves UNKNOWN when evidence cannot distinguish causes. Never assign a fixed learning style, permanent deficit or medical label.
3. **Remediation is targeted, bounded and actually delivered.** B requires reviewed repeated first-unaided evidence, a logged eligible choice and real contrast exposure; a copied correction does not become independent mastery.
4. **Mixed independent choice outranks blocked-drill success.** C rejects promotion based on 9/10 repetitive practice when a later unseen mixed communicative exchange fails. Interleaving changes the construction-selection demand, not merely the nouns.
5. **Diagnostic hypotheses are reversible.** D lowers confidence and revises the next actual teaching decision when independent evidence supports a narrower or different cause, without changing shared objectives or asserting causal superiority.
6. **Provenance, assistance and accessibility are honest.** E distinguishes failed audio, valid separate reading evidence, unopened versus answer-bearing help, first authorship, duplicate replay and unrendered follow-up; no phantom modality credit or manufactured retention.
7. **No over-remediation or interest lock-in.** F preserves other due objectives, productive/receptive balance, accessible modalities, deliberate alternative sampling and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar context rotation.
8. **Delayed transfer is genuinely independent and comparable.** Across histories, require distinct completed learning sessions, intervening material, unseen mixed communicative tasks, stable event identity, explicit missingness and review of topic, difficulty, channel, help and delay confounds. A midnight crossing or renamed script is insufficient.
9. **Evidence and release claims remain bounded.** Synthetic histories are specifications, not executable tests, shipped diagnosis or observed human gains. Require independently reviewed exact-head executable diagnostic/choice/delivery/first-authorship/interleaving checks, demonstrated reversible decisions, balanced-baseline comparison and ethically reviewed longitudinal independent learner outcomes before adaptive-efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge, Pre-A1 changes or A1–C2 release.

**Fixture verdict:** F207 makes prerequisite diagnosis and remediation falsifiable without reducing language standards. Its central protection is that *getting a practiced pattern right* is not the same as independently choosing and using it later in real communication. **UNSUPPORTED** until implemented and independently verified.
