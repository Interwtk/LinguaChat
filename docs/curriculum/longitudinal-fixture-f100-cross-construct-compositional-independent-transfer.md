# F100 — Cross-construct composition, communicative integrity and independent unseen transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a shipped mixed-dialogue evaluator, CEFR certification, reproduced production defect, proof of improved real-learner learning, or a new universal graduation requirement. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 requires genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish selected from actually rendered help and tutor-origin errors; F47/F48 protect noncompensatory CEFR evidence; F49 accessibility; F50 soft interests; F53 language variety/register; F54 repair; F71/F72/F74/F78/F80 protect reference, goal, source and recipient state; F81–F89 cover scope, time, contingencies, modality, authority, certainty, comparisons, imagined worlds and correction; F90–F99 separately specify presupposition, ellipsis, attribution, causality, quantities, time, figurative language, refusals, negative questions and multi-part answer scope. **Distinct integration question:** when a **real versioned CEFR-linked communicative task itself combines two or more independently specified meaning obligations**, can a learner independently preserve their *joint meaning* for the recipient, including interactions that make individually fluent component answers collectively misleading? Passing isolated F90–F99 probes does not establish this; failing a contrived combination that the actual descriptor/task does not demand must not block progression. This fixture tests **composition of source-required obligations**, not an invented higher-level language construct, a demand to mention every detail, or a generic reasoning/IQ test.

## Controlled setup and adjudication

Resolve the real versioned CEFR descriptor/can-do, level blueprint and prerequisites, target language/variety/register, source-authored task and recipient goal, authorized source versions, accessible channel, rubric/evaluator, assistance policy, independent threshold, required receptive/productive skills, due reviews, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer and level exit gate. Freeze a **task-specific composition graph** before instruction: each source-required atomic proposition or communicative action; whether it is mandatory, optional, conditional, legitimately unknown/declinable or not applicable; source-authoritative truth, time and speaker; dependencies between propositions; accepted variety-specific realizations; and the recipient decision each combination licenses. Mark explicit **interaction edges** such as “negative question about a tentative plan,” “reported quotation with a quantifier exception,” or “partial answer that must not imply confirmation.” Compare the joint recipient interpretation with source truth, not with token overlap or the length of a reply. A short, accurate, culturally appropriate clarification or partial answer may be fully valid when the frozen task allows it.

Predeclare reversible feasible teaching methods `COMPONENT_CONTRAST_THEN_COMPOSE`, `WHOLE_DIALOGUE_THEN_DECOMPOSE`, `RECIPIENT_MISUNDERSTANDING_REPAIR`, `SOURCE_STATE_RECONSTRUCTION`, `UNSEEN_COMPOSITIONAL_ROLEPLAY` and `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. Vary only implemented/evaluable listening, reading, writing, speaking, free production, explanation depth, scaffolding, pace, activity order, repetition/review spacing and examples. Hold **identical** source-required can-do, composition graph, task burden, accepted meanings, rubric, independence threshold, required skills, reviews and exit gate across candidates. Sample alternatives under uncertainty rather than assigning permanent learner styles. Interests softly affect examples but preserve preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts; do not choose only familiar or easy combinations.

Use deterministic pseudonymous histories across **distinct real learning sessions**, injected clock/seed, immutable session-qualified event IDs and frozen source/task/graph/rubric/evaluator hashes. Record source chronology, speaker/recipient permissions, first learner-authored recipient-facing response, proposition-by-proposition and **interaction-edge** entailment, actual recipient interpretation and task outcome, first-attempt quality, retries, hints/models/scaffold dependence, recurring error types, calibrated latency only when reliable, accessibility/modality, topic familiarity, practice dose, selected/assigned/**actually rendered** instruction and trusted render chronology. A tutor's answer-bearing composition map, completed multi-constraint reply, highlighted missing edge, or recipient paraphrase supplied before the assessed move is assistance **only when actually rendered**; a selected but never rendered hint is not. Source-authored facts and normal task-protocol clarification are legitimate stimuli; an interlocutor that reveals the target answer outside the protocol is coaching. Track eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed and genuinely unseen transfer denominators separately. Do not infer unobserved speaking/listening, fabricate recipient understanding, count an assisted correction as independent, or treat unavailable modalities and inaccurate captions as learner deficits.

Evidence labels: `SOURCE_VALID_COMPOSITE_TASK`, `COMPOSITION_GRAPH_FROZEN`, `SOURCE_REQUIRED_EDGES`, `ATOMIC_MEANINGS_PRESERVED`, `INTERACTION_SCOPE_PRESERVED`, `JOINT_RECIPIENT_INTERPRETATION_FAITHFUL`, `ATOMIC_PASS_JOINT_FAIL`, `VALID_BOUNDED_PARTIAL_ANSWER`, `UNKNOWN_OR_REFUSAL_PRESERVED`, `SOURCE_VERSION_PRESERVED`, `FIRST_INDEPENDENT_COMPOSITE_MOVE`, `TUTOR_COMPOSITION_CUE_RENDERED`, `ASSISTED_COMPOSITE_MOVE`, `INTERLOCUTOR_TASK_STIMULUS`, `INTERLOCUTOR_COACHING_LEAK`, `INDEPENDENT_DELAYED_COMPOSITE_RETRIEVAL`, `INDEPENDENT_UNSEEN_RECOMBINATION_TRANSFER`, `TASK_COMPLEXITY_CONFOUNDED`, `TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED`, `ACCESSIBILITY_CONFOUNDED`, `FOLLOWUP_MISSING`, `EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED`, `VERSION_CONFLICT_QUARANTINED`, `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. These describe bounded task evidence, not learner identities or stable traits.

## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Negative question + false presupposition + uncertain time (F90/F95/F98).** A source-authored organizer asks, “Isn't the workshop still on Thursday?” The frozen authoritative notice states **the workshop has not yet been scheduled**; Thursday was only an abandoned suggestion. Learner A separately recognizes that “isn't” asks about the event and that Thursday is a day, then replies “Yes, Thursday,” leading the recipient to travel unnecessarily. Learner B independently responds “There isn't a confirmed workshop date; Thursday was only suggested,” or a validated concise equivalent. In a different source world Thursday is confirmed and an unambiguous affirmative is correct. The joint graph requires proposition status, date attribution and question-polarity meaning; do not award A joint integrity from isolated token-level successes. Later unseen transfer uses a new event, a changed question form and a new recipient.

- **B — Multi-part inquiry + conditional offer + privacy boundary (F83/F97/F99).** A source-authored community organizer asks, “Can you volunteer on Saturday, and may we share your phone number?” The learner's authorized information is: Saturday is unavailable; Sunday **might** be possible pending confirmation; phone sharing is declined. Learner A fluently says “I can help Sunday” and “sure” without specifying which question “sure” answers; the recipient schedules Sunday and shares a number. Learner B independently declines Saturday, describes Sunday as tentative, and declines sharing contact details. A later source-authored task makes a second question optional and a short refusal sufficient; do not impose exhaustive slot completion or disclosure where the task does not require it. Score scope, consent/commitment and recipient action together, with culturally valid refusal forms accepted.

- **C — Quotation + quantity exception + changed source (F80/F92/F94).** A versioned notice first says “All four sessions are online.” An authoritative later correction says “Three sessions are online; the Friday session is in person.” A coordinator quotes the old notice while asking for a current summary. Learner A correctly attributes the original quotation but reports that **all** sessions remain online; the recipient misses Friday's venue. Learner B independently says the earlier notice said all were online but the corrected plan has a Friday exception, and does not invent the other dates. A control task explicitly asks for the historical quote, not the current plan: accurately reporting the superseded statement with attribution is valid. Later unseen transfer changes the quantifier, exception and speaker without repeating the answer.

- **D — Idiom + causal inference + respectful clarification (F54/F93/F96).** A source-authored speaker says, “That announcement really opened doors; attendance rose afterward, but we don't know why.” Learner A correctly paraphrases the idiom as “created opportunities” yet asserts the announcement **caused** the attendance increase. Learner B independently preserves the figurative meaning and the causal uncertainty, or asks a source-permitted clarification. In a literal-world control about a building's doors, a literal interpretation is correct. Another source world provides valid causal evidence, so appropriately stronger causal language is accepted. Score source-grounded interpretation and communicative appropriateness, not blanket preference for hedging or literalism.

- **E — Component drill wins immediately; composition practice wins later.** In matched early sessions for the **same source-required graph and CEFR can-do**, `COMPONENT_CONTRAST_THEN_COMPOSE` yields many correct isolated recognition items and quick model-assisted combined answers. `RECIPIENT_MISUNDERSTANDING_REPAIR` initially produces slower unaided attempts and visible errors. In later **distinct real sessions**, genuinely unseen tasks recombine two or three obligations with new surface wording, recipients and decisions; the synthetic learner taught via repair independently preserves more interaction edges and makes fewer consequential recipient-state errors. Match or disclose baseline proficiency, number and dependency of obligations, source ambiguity, response burden, target variety/register, feedback, practice dose, modality/accessibility and topic familiarity. Prefer completed delayed unseen outcomes to immediate coached component accuracy, log the **actually rendered** changed next method, reduce confidence when evidence reverses and keep sampling feasible alternatives. The fixture does not assert that repair is universally superior.

- **F — Topic overfit, cue-order reversal, version conflict and missing follow-up.** A learner handles favorite-game dialogues with two interacting constraints but fails a neutral travel update and an unfamiliar civic task of equivalent graph difficulty. A tutor's complete composite answer was rendered before the first response but its telemetry arrives later; another scaffold was selected but never displayed. Two offline sessions reuse the same local event ID; a source correction changes an exception from confirmed to unknown; a rubric revision validates a concise regional response; an eligible delayed probe is offered but never completed. An inaccessible audio cue contains a required negative particle missing from captions. Reconstruct trusted render chronology, deduplicate only true session-qualified replays, quarantine incompatible source/rubric/graph versions, mark uncompleted follow-up unknown and inaccessible input confounded, preserve review debt and topic breadth, and demand identical independence/edge/next-method verdicts under receipt-order permutations.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real CEFR and task applicability, not a fabricated mixed-skill gate.** A–F emit a real versioned descriptor/can-do, blueprint/prerequisites, source-authored task, target language/variety, recipient goal, frozen composition graph with source-required atomic obligations and interaction edges, rubric/evaluator, assistance policy, independent threshold, required skills, reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. A task without source-required interacting obligations yields `NOT_APPLICABLE`; missing mapping or unverifiable source semantics yields `UNSUPPORTED`, never invented proficiency.

2. **Atomic success cannot mask joint semantic failure.** A/B/C/D contrast independently correct component recognition with an incorrect combined message. Verify the graph's required interaction edges and the recipient's source-defined interpretation. A fluent sentence, summed component score or answer length cannot compensate for a materially false joint implication; neither may an optional detail become a new mandatory obligation.

3. **Scope, truth, attribution and source-version fidelity.** A/B/C preserve negative-question proposition, tentative versus confirmed status, consent/commitment, quoted versus current information, quantity exceptions and legitimate unknowns. Correctness depends on the frozen source world and recipient task: the same utterance can be valid in one world and misleading in another. Do not invent facts, confirmations, dates, causal links or disclosures.

4. **Meaning-equivalent answers, bounded partials and repair.** B/C/D accept concise source-valid answers, target-language varieties/registers, privacy boundaries and source-permitted clarification. A partial answer is not automatically wrong or complete: evaluate the actual mandatory/dependent slots and recipient actionability. Separate an appropriate repair from an unsupported assertion, and figurative interpretation from unsupported causal certainty.

5. **First independent move and delivered-cue provenance.** A/D/F evaluate the first learner-authored recipient-facing response against trusted **actually rendered** tutor/interlocutor chronology. A worked combined reply, highlighted interaction edge, answer-key translation or explicit missing-slot correction rendered beforehand makes affected evidence assisted; assigned-but-unrendered content does not. Separate legitimate source-authored task material from answer-leaking coaching. Late telemetry cannot turn assistance into independence.

6. **Delayed independent recombination, not repeated isolated templates.** A–F require distinct real learning sessions, meaningful delay, genuinely unseen source-authored **recombinations** of required obligations, first unaided responses and observable source-defined recipient outcomes. Change surface form, source truth, dependency or interlocutor while preserving calibrated task burden; avoid simply repeating the same compound prompt with new nouns. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; uncompleted probes and assisted repairs do not establish durable joint mastery.

7. **Comparable methods, accessibility and topic breadth.** D/E/F match or disclose graph size/edge complexity, source uncertainty, baseline proficiency, feedback, dose, response length, variety/register, modality/accessibility and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic exposure. Protect noncompensatory CEFR receptive/productive requirements, due reviews and exit gates. Inaccessible audio/captions, unavailable speaking recognition and off-task general reasoning burdens are confounds, not learner failures.

8. **Reversible outcome-based adaptation and replay invariance.** E/F emit feasible candidates, selected/assigned/**actually rendered** instruction, later independent joint outcomes, uncertainty/confounds, exploration and next-choice rationale. Stronger completed delayed outcomes can reverse an immediate-score preference without permanently labeling the learner; sparse/conflicted evidence falls back to `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. Session-qualified deduplication, frozen graph/source/rubric versions and trusted cue chronology must yield receipt-order-invariant atomic/edge verdicts, assistance, review debt and next choice; unresolved conflicts fail closed.

9. **Assertion-level audit and bounded claims.** A–F emit exact source/task/graph/rubric/evaluator IDs, atomic/interaction-edge expected meanings, source-version truth, first/final learner-authored messages, rendered cues, recipient interpretation/outcome, delayed/unseen denominators, confounds, replay permutations and `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED` per assertion. Documentation CI is not executable mixed-dialogue evaluation, a CEFR award or evidence of improved human retention.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, deterministic seed/clock, real source-linked CEFR tasks that actually require compositional meaning, validated multilingual/variety-aware rubrics, frozen source/task/graph versions, trusted rendered-cue chronology, first unaided recipient-facing responses, observable recipient interpretations, completed delayed genuinely unseen **recombination** transfer with denominators, comparable method/topic/modality/accessibility exposure, protected required skills/reviews, receipt-order permutations, reversible next-method traces and assertion-level verdicts. Missing real mapping, source authority, reliable chronology, accessible modality, valid composition adjudication or completed follow-up remains `UNSUPPORTED`/unknown.

Any claim that instructional adaptation **improves durable independent compositional communication** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, multilingual/variety and accessibility validation, human adjudication, privacy/subgroup-harm review and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor helps a learner preserve the whole meaning when several ordinary language demands interact, not merely pass each isolated drill.**

**Boundary:** curricular acceptance documentation only. No Pre-A1 edits, A1–C2 release, engine, Auth, cloud, infrastructure, production, Supabase, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge changes. Integration/watchdog alone aggregates owner actions.
