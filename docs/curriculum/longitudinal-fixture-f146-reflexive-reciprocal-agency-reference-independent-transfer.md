## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Self-directed action versus reciprocal relation.** In a reviewed English incident report with two named people, 'Maya and Omar photographed themselves' licenses the self-directed reading; it does not establish that Maya photographed Omar or vice versa. 'Maya and Omar photographed each other' instead licenses reciprocal relations. Learner A writes 'They photographed each other' when the source explicitly says each took their own photo; learner B independently writes 'Each took a photo of themselves.' Counterfactual: a separate reviewed source says each photographed the other, so B must now preserve reciprocity. If the source pronoun antecedent is unresolved, credit 'Who does themselves refer to?' rather than guessing.

**B — Emphatic agent versus solitude and beneficiary.** A coordinator reports 'Lina assembled the display herself, with two colleagues holding the panels.' Emphatic 'herself' foregrounds Lina's personal involvement; it does not entail she worked alone or acted on her own body. Learner A says 'Lina worked completely alone'; learner B independently says 'Lina assembled it herself while colleagues helped hold the panels.' Counterfactual: 'Lina assembled it by herself, without help' licenses a solo-action claim. Separately, 'Lina made herself lunch' identifies a beneficiary, which is not equivalent to 'Lina made lunch herself'; assess that role distinction when it matters to the recipient.

**C — Group reciprocity, scope and completion.** A team lead writes 'The four mentors agreed to message each other about schedule changes.' Learner A reports that all twelve directed messages were sent and received. Learner B independently preserves the commitment and asks who still needs confirmation. Agreement is not completion; reciprocal wording alone does not establish all-to-all pairwise coverage. Counterfactual: a reviewed delivery log explicitly records every directed mentor-to-mentor exchange, licensing a stronger report. 'Each mentor messaged themselves a reminder' is self-directed, not communication among mentors. Where the intended network is underspecified, recipient-relevant clarification is correct.

**D — Ambiguous antecedents and language-specific forms.** A reviewed English message says 'Rina told Jo that she should introduce herself.' Without additional context, the intended introducer may remain unresolved. Learner A confidently invents Jo; learner B asks 'Do you mean Rina or Jo should introduce herself?' A revised source naming the subject licenses a determinate report. A Spanish dialogue 'Ana y Luis se escribieron' may license reciprocity in its reviewed context, but the token 'se' alone does not certify that interpretation or successful delivery. Other languages may encode self-reference, reciprocity and emphasis differently; independently review each construction, register and natural paraphrase rather than enforcing an English template.

**E — Rendered evidence, accessibility, authorship and replay.** Authoritative audio says 'They introduced themselves, not each other.' ASR deletes 'not', captions substitute 'each other' for 'themselves', translation collapses the distinction, a screen reader reads an unlabeled diagram, or autocomplete inserts the answer. Compare authoritative against **actually delivered** stimuli; if the delivered material cannot support the distinction, quarantine **only the affected construct** as UNSUPPORTED, not the learner's whole competence. At 10:00 the learner independently asks 'Did they introduce themselves or one another?'; a model queued at 09:59 but first rendered at 10:01 cannot retroactively assist that response. A correction at 10:02 after exposure is ASSISTED. Reviewed accessible text may test equivalent meaning, but text-only performance does not certify unobserved listening. Replayed session-qualified event IDs cannot create additional independent attempts, sessions or delayed successes.

**F — Longitudinal methods, confounds, delayed retention and unseen transfer.** Fictional learner A succeeds immediately after PARTICIPANT_ROLE_MAPPING_THEN_FADE but repeatedly confuses self/other roles in delayed first-unaided recipient messages. Comparable **actually delivered** SELF_VS_OTHER_MINIMAL_EVENT_PAIR and REFLEXIVE_VS_RECIPROCAL_DIALOGUE trials precede a genuinely new unaided incident report with different participants, recipient, purpose and construction. Learner B instead improves delayed independent emphasis-versus-solo distinctions after EMPHATIC_VS_SOLO_CONTEXT_CONTRAST and LEARNER_GENERATED_STATUS_REPORT. If one method uses two familiar people and common 'themselves' while another uses four participants, uncommon verbs and formal audience, flag METHOD_PARTICIPANT_COUNT_CONFOUNDED, METHOD_PRONOUN_FREQUENCY_CONFOUNDED and METHOD_REGISTER_CONFOUNDED: infer **no winner**. Swapping names in the same self-portrait drill is not unseen transfer. Match baseline, role complexity, lexical familiarity, variety, modality, help, topic, delay and follow-up. Record eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing separately; missing is neither success nor failure. Only repeated comparable later **first-unaided** outcomes justify a logged change in **actually delivered** instruction, retaining uncertainty, alternatives and reversibility after contradictory evidence. Interest in games, music or sports can bias contexts but cannot suppress neutral/unfamiliar topics or CEFR obligations.
