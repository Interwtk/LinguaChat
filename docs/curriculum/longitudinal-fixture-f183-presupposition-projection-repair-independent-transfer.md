An independently reviewed **utterance-and-common-ground oracle** fixed before delivery records exact target-language wording, speaker and addressee, prior turns, temporal and physical context, reviewed presupposition triggers and their possible scopes, what is directly asserted or asked, candidate background assumptions, independently established facts, legitimate accommodation, explicit challenges, alternative readings and unresolved uncertainty. Reviewers must distinguish (1) what a speaker presupposes, (2) what the shared conversation has accepted and (3) what the task actually establishes as true. A learner need not challenge every harmless conversational assumption; accommodation can be appropriate when supported by context. Do not grade a disputed premise as fact solely because a question contains it. Factive, change-of-state, iterative, definite-description and focus constructions require language/variety-specific review; no English-only heuristic may silently govern another language.

An independently reviewed **recipient-repair oracle** stores proportionate, natural responses: direct answer where a premise is established, targeted clarification where unresolved, polite correction of a false premise, or cautious paraphrase preserving the speaker's assumption without endorsing it. Accept multiple pragmatically suitable phrasings and registers. Do not demand a single culturally privileged refusal or make confidence, accent, social identity or speed proxies for correctness.

An **actual-delivery oracle** records reviewed text/audio hashes, language/variety, audible intonation, punctuation, scene cues, speaker roles, translations, transcripts, captions, hints, exemplars, answer-bearing highlights and repairs **actually displayed or heard** before each attempt. An unclicked hint is not exposure. Audio playback failure cannot earn unaided listening evidence; an accessible text alternative may validly test reading/interaction but cannot be relabeled listening.

A **first-authorship/provenance ledger** retains pseudonymous learner and versioned objective/rubric/oracle, stable session-qualified event IDs and replay deduplication; first eligible unaided interpretation and recipient-facing reply; exact delivered stimuli and assumptions; chronological actual help/answer exposure; accessibility construct; instructional method assigned/offered/**actually delivered**; and delayed/unseen probes due, offered, rendered, completed or missing, with real elapsed time and distinct-session provenance. Optional hesitation/latency is weak evidence only, never fabricated or a mastery gate. Assisted repairs never overwrite original independent errors.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Loaded question with false premise.** At a fictional club meeting, an organizer asks a new volunteer, "When did you stop borrowing the rehearsal room?" The reviewed prior record explicitly says the volunteer has **never** borrowed that room. A learner reports, "They used to borrow it and then stopped." Reject QUESTION_PREMISE_NOT_FACT and the unsupported invented history. Accept "I haven't borrowed it" or "I think there may be a mix-up; I never used that room." In a separate reviewed context where prior borrowing and cessation are established, a date answer may be appropriate. Do not treat every "when did you stop" construction identically across languages.

**B — Iterative expression without verified history.** In a fictional workshop message, "Nora returned the blue key again" is reported by a speaker whose earlier key-return history is absent. The utterance presents a return and invites a prior similar event, but the task's independent record has not verified that earlier event. A learner tells the organizer, "The log proves Nora returned it twice." Reject ITERATIVE_PRIOR_EVENT_UNVERIFIED: a speaker's presupposition is not a verified log. Accept "Nora says she returned it again; the earlier return isn't in our log" or a targeted request for confirmation. In a different scenario with two documented returns, ordinary accommodation is valid; never require gratuitous doubt.

**C — Factive content and uncertain source.** A fictional message reads, "Maya regrets sending the draft invitation." In the independently reviewed context, the speaker uses the ordinary factive reading and a prior turn confirms that Maya sent the draft. A learner says, "Maya may send it later, but hasn't sent it yet." Reject the contradiction; accept that the draft was sent and Maya now regrets doing so, without inventing the recipient or consequences. Contrast with "Maya worries she might send the wrong draft," which does not establish that she sent anything. A report that someone *claims* Maya regrets sending it preserves source uncertainty; do not silently convert hearsay into verified fact.

**D — Projection and conditional scope are not mechanical.** Two reviewed fictional exchanges are delivered separately. First: "Mina doesn't regret sharing the poster" in a context that supports the ordinary presupposition that she shared it; the learner must distinguish the denial of regret from a denial of sharing. Second: "If Mina shares the poster, she may regret it" with no established sharing; the learner reports "She already shared it." Reject CONDITIONAL_NOT_ASSERTION. The oracle must record legitimate scope, metalinguistic negation, accommodation and variety-specific alternatives; no universal "every trigger always projects" rule. A well-justified clarification is valid when the context is underdetermined.

**E — Actual delivery, accessibility and first authorship.** At 09:02 a listening prompt with "When did you stop borrowing the room?" fails to play; no audio is heard. Do not grade unaided listening or invent an error. At 09:03 an accessible text route renders the question alongside a visible caption "this question wrongly assumes earlier borrowing"; at 09:04 the learner correctly challenges the premise. Mark ACTUALLY_RENDERED_HELP, ANSWER_EXPOSURE and ASSISTED for the reading/interaction route, not independent listening. An optional unclicked caption would not count as exposure. At 10:15 a genuinely new reviewed and successfully rendered scenario with no exposed answer can yield a new first unaided attempt in its actual modality. Deduplicate replayed event IDs; preserve chronology and elapsed time.

**F — Matched longitudinal adaptation with reversal.** Learner A initially accepts unsupported premises in independent new exchanges, then receives alternating, **actually delivered** ASSERTED_VERSUS_ASSUMED_CONTRAST, QUESTION_PREMISE_REPAIR_ROLEPLAY and BALANCED_DEFAULT across comparable sessions. Only repeated, meaningfully delayed first-unaided responses to unseen speakers and neutral/unfamiliar topics, with the same CEFR objective and comparable premise difficulty, may cautiously favor a method; continue sampling alternatives. Learner B improves after COMMON_GROUND_RECONSTRUCTION_WITH_FADE but later begins challenging even well-established harmless assumptions. Lower confidence, reverse or soften the preference, and explore balanced accommodation-versus-challenge practice. If a method included explicit premise labels, simpler prompts, extra hints, familiar topics, shorter delays or better follow-up, record **no causal winner**. Same-dialogue paraphrases, assisted retries, midnight-only session boundaries and missing follow-up cannot demonstrate durable transfer. Maintain listening, reading and productive interaction balance.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed CEFR and reviewed oracles.** A–F require existing relevant versioned can-dos, independent common-ground and recipient-repair review, actual delivery and unchanged mastery standards; otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED, never a new CEFR gate.
2. **False question premises.** A rejects invented earlier borrowing, accepts proportionate correction and permits direct answers only where the premise is independently supported.
3. **Iterative assumptions versus established events.** B distinguishes the speaker's "again" presupposition from verified history and permits context-justified accommodation without fabricated logs.
4. **Factive versus prospective meanings.** C separates confirmed past sending, the speaker's attitude, and merely possible future action; source uncertainty must not be laundered into fact.
5. **Projection, scope and variation.** D distinguishes negated attitude from negated event and conditional possibility from occurrence, while requiring reviewed construction- and variety-specific alternatives.
6. **Actual delivery, first authorship and accessibility.** E counts only exposed cues, retains earliest eligible unaided evidence, records playback failure as unavailable, keeps constructs distinct and deduplicates replays; assisted repair never earns independent mastery.
7. **Matched, uncertain, reversible adaptation.** F updates instructional probabilities only with comparable actually delivered methods and later independent outcomes; sparse, confounded or contradictory evidence cannot declare a causal winner and must preserve exploration.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer and soft interests.** Require distinct real sessions, independently reviewed new speakers and contexts, first-unaided recipient-facing correction, accommodation or clarification, and explicit missing follow-up. Rotate adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics without skipping fixed objectives or balanced modalities.
9. **Evidence and protected scope.** Synthetic prose or generic CI cannot establish executable presupposition-oracle QA, shipped outcome-driven adaptation or human retention gains. Require exact-head executable delivered-stimulus/first-authorship assertions, observed policy-decision changes and independently reviewed delayed unaided transfer against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, Pre-A1 changes, A1–C2 enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic acceptance histories, not observed learners or executable QA. Preserve the distinction between assertion, assumed common ground and independently verified fact, allow calibrated accommodation and correction, and keep instruction reversible. Remain UNSUPPORTED until implementation and independently verified learning evidence exist.
