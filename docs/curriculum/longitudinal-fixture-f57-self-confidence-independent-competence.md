# F57 — Learner-reported confidence must not substitute for independent language competence

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a reproduced defect, deployed confidence intervention, psychological assessment, validated CEFR judgment or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F46 concerns the *tutor's confidence in an instructional-method forecast*. F57 concerns the *learner's optional self-reported confidence about performing a specific communicative task*. These are separate from observed CEFR competence, actual help exposure, willingness to participate, perceived safety, satisfaction and the tutor's instructional hypothesis. The tutor may respond sensitively to confidence reports, but cannot award or deny mastery on self-belief, tone, accent, speed, hesitation or willingness to disclose a feeling. A confident wrong response remains wrong under the applicable rubric; a hesitant correct unaided response retains its valid independent evidence.

## Controlled setup

First resolve a real versioned CEFR can-do, blueprint, supported modality, task, evaluator/rubric, permissible support, required independent production/reception, delayed retrieval, unseen-context transfer, review obligations and level-exit gates. Freeze these across comparisons. Confidence prompts are optional and clearly task-specific (e.g. `How sure are you that you can arrange this appointment without hints?`); do not ask for a global ability identity, a permanent learning style or sensitive mental-health information. No universal confidence scale, cutoff or calibration threshold is invented. If a source-validated task-specific response scale exists, record its exact version and anchors; otherwise confidence remains qualitative/unknown.

Use deterministic pseudonymous synthetic histories, an injected clock/seed, distinct real learning sessions, stable session-qualified event IDs and immutable event streams. Capture the **timing** of each voluntary confidence report (before task, after first attempt, after hints, after feedback or after seeing an answer), question/scale version, missing/declined state, and whether the learner could see their score. Separately record actual first-attempt semantic quality and rubric result; modality; cue, hint, model, transcript and peer exposure; retries; independent versus assisted performance; communicative outcome; later unaided retrieval and novel-context transfer; task difficulty and context familiarity; legitimate accessibility support; and assignment versus verified delivery of any confidence-oriented teaching intervention. Optional latency is weak and context-sensitive, never a proxy for confidence, disability or mastery. Minimize personal transcripts; do not infer anxiety, personality, intelligence or protected traits.

Predeclare `CONFIDENCE_REPORTED_PRE_ATTEMPT`, `CONFIDENCE_REPORTED_POST_FEEDBACK`, `CONFIDENCE_DECLINED`, `CONFIDENCE_UNKNOWN`, `CONFIDENCE_COMPETENCE_DISAGREEMENT`, `INDEPENDENT_COMPETENCE_OBSERVED`, `ASSISTED_ONLY`, `CONFIDENCE_PROMPT_CONTAMINATION`, `DELAYED_PROBE_PENDING`, `FOLLOWUP_UNKNOWN`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `RUBRIC_MISMATCH`, `EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED` and `UNSUPPORTED`. These are provenance labels, not clinical categories or fixed learner labels.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — High confidence, weak independent performance.** A learner confidently predicts they can write a source-defined request unaided. The first response omits a meaning-critical condition; after a worked example and two retries, the final message is correct. Retain the confidence report and assisted practice benefit, but do not certify independent mastery from confidence or the repaired final response. Schedule the source-required independent probe rather than punitive or shaming feedback.
- **B — Low confidence, strong independent transfer.** A learner says they are unsure, yet independently handles an unfamiliar rescheduling dialogue with source-valid clarification and later reproduces the capability unaided after a meaningful delay. Preserve correct first-attempt and later evidence despite modest self-rating, natural pauses, accent variation and a preference not to speak about feelings. Offer supportive practice choices without requiring confidence to increase before CEFR credit.
- **C — Confidence rises without durable competence.** Repeated praise, easy familiar examples and immediate model exposure increase reported certainty and short-term assisted accuracy. On later comparable unseen-context unaided probes, the learner cannot perform the required objective. Keep satisfaction, confidence change, assisted success and independent retention as separate series; do not optimize praise or certainty as a substitute for learning.
- **D — Competence grows before confidence, then reports reverse.** For one learner, a brief self-explanation and calibrated feedback initially improve later independent transfer even though confidence remains low; subsequent counterbalanced role-play produces better delayed independent outcomes while confidence rises under both methods. Update the provisional instructional choice using independent results, with uncertainty and alternative sampling, rather than choosing the intervention that produces the largest confidence gain. An opposite learner history may support a different method for the same can-do.
- **E — Topic, disclosure and accessibility confound apparent calibration.** A learner reports high certainty for familiar gaming examples and declines confidence questions in unfamiliar neutral/administrative contexts; another uses an approved accessible response mode and takes longer without worse independent performance. Compare only source-equivalent task demands; report selection/missingness by topic and prompt timing. Do not infer low confidence from refusal, treat accessibility latency as a deficit, turn interests into an exclusive filter or certify an unavailable oral modality using text.
- **F — Late self-report, answer exposure and replay.** An offline pre-task confidence response is uploaded after the task; a different post-feedback rating arrives first, a model answer was displayed between them, one event is duplicated, a new session reuses the local turn ID, and a later rubric version changes. Reconstruct trusted event-time and actual delivery/exposure, not upload order; preserve the immutable pre-task report and the distinct post-feedback report. Deduplicate within session-qualified IDs, quarantine uncertain chronology, and never backdate a post-answer rating as an independent prediction or silently rescore an old rubric.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-defined evidence remains authoritative.** For A–F report versioned CEFR can-do, blueprint, task, modality, evaluator/rubric, allowed assistance, independent evidence, delayed retrieval, transfer, review and exit obligations. Confidence never changes the assessed construct or mastery threshold; missing source rules or unsupported modality produce `UNSUPPORTED`/`UNAVAILABLE`.
2. **Confidence and competence are different observations.** In A and B, identical confidence levels need not imply identical independent performance, and different confidence levels cannot overwrite equally valid unaided evidence. Preserve the first attempt, semantic quality, task result and actual support.
3. **Report timing and contamination matter.** A rating after a hint, answer reveal, feedback or score display is not an uncontaminated pre-task forecast. In C and F, distinguish voluntary pre-attempt, post-attempt and post-feedback reports and explicitly mark chronology/exposure uncertainty.
4. **Durable independent outcomes outrank certainty gains.** In C and D, evaluate later unaided retrieval and unseen-context transfer on source-comparable tasks; do not treat confidence increase, immediate assisted accuracy, engagement or a fluent coached final response as proof of retention. Missing later probes are `FOLLOWUP_UNKNOWN`, not successes or failures.
5. **Adaptive teaching is provisional and reversible.** In D, log available, selected and verified-delivered instructional choices, task difficulty, uncertainty, first-attempt outcomes and later independent results. Contrary evidence must permit a method reversal and nonzero feasible exploration; never assign a permanent confident/unconfident learner type.
6. **Disclosure is optional and non-punitive.** In B/E, a declined or absent self-report remains `CONFIDENCE_DECLINED`/`CONFIDENCE_UNKNOWN`, not inferred low confidence, low ability or a CEFR failure. The learner can continue with ordinary tutoring and valid independent assessment.
7. **Interest, accessibility and modality boundaries remain intact.** In E, retain adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts and all required can-dos; counterbalance topic familiarity separately from teaching method. Honor supported accommodations without assuming they are hints, and never award unobserved listening/speaking competence.
8. **Replay is deterministic without retroactive rewriting.** In F, receipt-order permutations, duplicate uploads, reused local IDs and rubric-version mismatches converge on the same confidence timeline, first-attempt provenance, independent evidence and due-review ledger. Quarantine uncertain exposure; no later rating or outcome may rewrite an earlier report.
9. **No release or efficacy inflation.** For every history emit an auditable ledger of source IDs, self-report question/scale/time/decline, support and answer exposure, first/final attempts, independent versus assisted outcomes, eligible/completed/missing delayed probes, unseen transfer, topic/modality coverage, provisional method choice/uncertainty and `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED` per assertion. A reviewed document or green documentation QA is not an executable longitudinal test, a validated confidence measure, a shipped tutor or proof of human-learning improvement.

## Required evidence and release gate

To claim an executable fixture PASS, publish the exact tested engine HEAD, command, injected seed/clock, immutable event streams and replay permutations, actual source-resolved CEFR blueprint/task/evaluator/rubric, confidence prompt and scale versions, pre/post-report timestamps, delivered answer/hint exposure, first-attempt and later independent results, delayed and unseen-context probe eligibility/completion/missingness, method alternatives and decision changes, interest/context and skill balance, and per-assertion verdicts. Where confidence scale validity, chronology, source requirements, modality or follow-up is unknown, say so; never fabricate a calibrated confidence score, psychological trait, causal gain or independent performance.

Any future claim that confidence-sensitive instruction improves durable independent learning requires separately reviewed longitudinal learner evidence, a credible balanced comparator, sufficient unaided delayed retrieval and unseen-context transfer, subgroup harm checks and an ethically reviewed pilot. **The tutor should help learners become independently capable, not merely feel capable or appear confident.**
