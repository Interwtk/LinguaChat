## Fixed CEFR objectives and independently reviewed temporal oracles

Bind only **existing versioned CEFR** listening, reading, spoken-interaction, mediation or written-production can-dos that genuinely require temporal event interpretation or accurate event-status reporting. Preserve the exact can-do, prerequisites, language/variety, rubric, thresholds, accessible route, first-independent evidence, due/delayed/unseen probes, balanced receptive/productive practice and mastery/exit gates. If the construct is not required by a can-do, mark NOT_APPLICABLE; do not invent a level gate. All examples concern fictional, low-stakes school clubs, rehearsals, libraries and community workshops, never real medical, legal, financial, emergency or safety-critical instructions.

An independently reviewed **event-timeline oracle**, frozen before delivery, stores the original utterance time, each event's participants and reference time, explicit temporal connectives, tense/aspect or equivalent constructions, event intervals, asserted starts/ends, possible overlap, habitual versus episodic readings, completion evidence, source reliability, underspecified order and legitimate alternative readings. Store a **partial order** when the language does not establish a total sequence; never fill gaps with a plausible but unspoken story. The oracle separately distinguishes (1) the event as described by the speaker, (2) evidence independently established in the fictional scenario and (3) what a recipient may safely report. Language-specific grammatical aspect is not a universal guarantee of real-world completion; contextual readings, dialect variation and reported-speech shifts require review.

An independently reviewed **recipient-timeline oracle** accepts accurate retellings, corrections, cautious uncertainty and targeted clarification, including multiple natural target-language/variety-appropriate phrasings. It must distinguish "started", "was doing", "usually did", "had done", "plans to do" and "finished" when the scenario actually supports those differences. It must not privilege one accent, register, speed, cultural style or phrasing over semantically equivalent answers.

An **actual-delivery oracle** records exact reviewed text/audio hashes, target language/variety, spoken pauses and prosody where actually heard, punctuation, original-message timestamps, visual timelines, subtitles, transcripts, translations, hints, worked examples, answer-bearing highlights and every cue **actually exposed before each attempt**. A displayed timeline that orders the events is assistance, not independent inference; an unopened optional timeline is not exposure. Failed audio playback cannot earn unaided listening evidence. An accessible text alternative may validly measure reading or interaction, but must not be relabeled listening.

A **first-authorship/provenance ledger** records pseudonymous learner, fixed objective/rubric/oracle versions, stable session-qualified event IDs and replay deduplication; first eligible unaided interpretation and recipient-facing event report; exact delivered stimuli and timeline; chronological actual help, model answers and accessibility route; assigned/offered/**actually delivered** instructional method; and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed and unseen probes, with real elapsed time and distinct learning-session provenance. Optional latency/hesitation is weak evidence only, never fabricated or used as a mastery gate. An assisted correction cannot overwrite the first independent error.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Earlier past event versus later past event.** In a fictional club message: "By the time Maya arrived, Theo had already locked the storage room." A learner tells the coordinator, "Maya arrived and then Theo locked it." The reviewed oracle places Theo's locking **before** Maya's arrival; reject the reversed order. Accept "Theo had locked the room before Maya got there." Do not infer how long before, who gave permission, or that the room remains locked now. In a separate reviewed utterance, "Theo locked the room when Maya arrived" may allow different timing interpretations; do not transfer A's ordering without evidence.

**B — Overlapping activity is not completed work.** In a fictional rehearsal log: "When the lights went out, Nia was painting the sign." A learner reports, "Nia finished painting before the outage." Reject EVENT_COMPLETION_UNVERIFIED. The utterance supports an ongoing painting activity overlapping the outage, not its finish. Accept "Nia was painting when the lights went out; we don't know if she finished." Contrast with "Nia had finished painting before the lights went out," where completion before the outage is asserted. The oracle must not assume that an interruption always stops the activity permanently.

**C — Habitual past does not assert a current routine.** A former club member says, "I used to open the library room on Tuesdays." The learner updates a fictional current roster to say, "They open it every Tuesday now." Reject HABIT_NOT_CURRENT_FACT: a prior habit is not evidence of a current schedule. Accept "They regularly opened it on Tuesdays in the past; the present schedule is unconfirmed." The language/variety reviewer must record legitimate contextual exceptions and equivalent habitual constructions; do not equate every simple past with habituality.

**D — Deadline or plan is not proof of delivery.** In a fictional workshop thread, an organizer writes on Monday, "I'll send the agenda by Thursday." On Wednesday, with no later confirmation, the learner reports, "The agenda has been sent." Reject DEADLINE_NOT_COMPLETION. Accept "The organizer plans to send it no later than Thursday; we haven't confirmed delivery." In a separately reviewed follow-up, "I sent the agenda yesterday" may support a report that the speaker **says** it was sent, but independent receipt is still a different fact. Do not convert a future commitment, elapsed deadline or message timestamp into observed completion.

**E — Actual exposure, accessibility and first authorship.** At 09:02 an audio prompt containing "Nia was painting when the lights went out" fails to play; no audio is heard. Do not score an unaided listening attempt. At 09:03 an accessible reading route actually displays the sentence plus a labeled diagram "painting in progress at outage"; at 09:04 the learner correctly reports the overlap. Mark ACTUALLY_RENDERED_HELP, ANSWER_EXPOSURE and ASSISTED for the observed reading/interaction route, not independent listening. An optional unopened diagram would not count as exposure. At 10:15 a genuinely new, independently reviewed scenario, successfully rendered without answer exposure, may yield a first unaided response in its actual modality. Deduplicate replayed event IDs, retain event order and do not infer a meaningful retention interval from a clock or midnight boundary alone.

**F — Longitudinal method comparison, reversal and topic separation.** Learner A repeatedly confuses ongoing and completed events on independent first attempts. Across comparable real sessions, **actually delivered** ONGOING_VERSUS_COMPLETED_CONTRAST, EVENT_CARDS_ORDER_WITH_FADE and BALANCED_DEFAULT alternate with logged help and difficulty. Only repeated, meaningfully delayed first-unaided retellings of unseen events, new speakers and neutral/unfamiliar topics may cautiously favor a method while keeping alternatives sampled. Learner B initially improves with event cards but later starts overordering events whose sequence is genuinely unknown; lower confidence, reverse or soften the preference, and explore PARTIAL_TIMELINE_RECONSTRUCTION. If one method showed a labeled timeline, easier temporal connectives, shorter delays, extra hints, familiar interests, clearer audio or more completed follow-up, record **no causal winner**. Assisted retries, same-story paraphrases, missing probes and same-day surface changes cannot prove durable transfer. Maintain independent listening, reading and productive/interactive evidence; interests only softly bias examples and never filter required objectives.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed CEFR and independently reviewed event oracles.** A–F bind existing relevant versioned can-dos, target-language/variety-specific event and recipient oracles, actual delivery and unchanged mastery standards; otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED, never a new gate.
2. **Past-event order.** A rejects the reversed locking/arrival sequence, accepts the supported before-relation and forbids invented exact timing or present lock status.
3. **Ongoing versus finished.** B distinguishes activity overlapping an interruption from established completion; contrastive perfective/pluperfect interpretations require independently reviewed wording and context.
4. **Habitual versus current.** C rejects turning a past repeated routine into a verified present assignment and allows context-justified alternative readings rather than English-only heuristics.
5. **Deadline versus actual completion.** D distinguishes future intention, due time, claimed sending and verified receipt, and preserves source/time anchors.
6. **Actual delivery, accessibility and first authorship.** E counts only exposed cues, retains earliest eligible unaided evidence, labels playback failure unavailable, preserves the measured construct and deduplicates event replays; assisted correction cannot earn independent mastery.
7. **Matched, uncertain and reversible adaptation.** F changes instructional probabilities only on comparable actually delivered options plus later independent outcomes; sparse, confounded or contradictory data cannot establish a causal winner and must preserve exploration.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer and soft interests.** Require distinct real sessions, independently reviewed new event timelines, speakers and contexts, first-unaided recipient-facing event reports or justified clarifications, and explicit missing follow-up. Rotate adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics without skipping fixed objectives or balanced modalities.
9. **Evidence and protected scope.** Synthetic prose or generic CI cannot establish executable temporal-oracle QA, shipped outcome-driven adaptation or human retention gains. Require exact-head executable stimulus/timeline/first-authorship assertions, observed policy-decision changes and independently reviewed delayed unaided transfer against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, Pre-A1 changes, A1–C2 enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic acceptance histories, not observed learners or executable QA. Preserve supported event order, ongoingness, habituality, plans and uncertainty without invented completion; keep instruction reversible and interests nonexclusive.
