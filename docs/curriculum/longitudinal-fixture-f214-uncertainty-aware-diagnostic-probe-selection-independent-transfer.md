# F214 — Uncertainty-aware diagnostic probe selection, opportunity cost and independent transfer

**Status:** design-only synthetic *cross-cutting* longitudinal curriculum acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until an independently reviewed executable exact-head harness checks the decisions, exposures, evidence and later outcomes. This is **not** a shipped diagnostic scheduler, an inferred learning style, a new CEFR can-do, an additional mastery gate, a causal method comparison or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F43 treats assessment itself as a learning exposure; F206 audits biased instructional selection; F207 tests *how to interpret* prerequisite contrasts and subsequent remediation; F209 handles scarce review opportunities; F211 calibrates task difficulty; F213 protects learner choice. **Distinct question in F214:** *when several plausible explanations for uncertain performance remain, should the tutor ask a short diagnostic question, continue ordinary teaching, schedule delayed retrieval or abstain—and can it select an actually useful, feasible probe without sacrificing required learning time, contaminating the answer, or pretending that a probe proves its own hypothesis?* The target is **diagnostic decision quality and opportunity cost**, not another linguistic can-do.

## Fixed standard and pre-decision evidence

Resolve each history to an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, target language/variety, reviewed semantic rubric, prerequisites and skill family, supported and accessible modality, independent production/reception requirements, due-review and coverage debt, meaningful-turn ceiling where applicable, and unchanged mastery/exit rules. English examples below are illustrative, not released lessons. The unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix boundary remains **UNSUPPORTED**; a diagnostic recognition item cannot substitute for independent productive evidence.

At the **decision boundary**, freeze pseudonymous learner, objective and rubric versions; trusted completed-session provenance; first-unaided attempts and meaning-critical errors; actually delivered prior instruction, feedback and item exposure; plausible *task-level* hypotheses and uncertainty (including UNKNOWN); currently due reviews and coverage debt; available session budget and learner choice; eligible supported probe/teaching/review options and why any are unavailable; intended diagnostic distinction; predicted answer patterns **declared before observing the probe**; independently reviewed task validity and language burden; expected cost in turns and displaced opportunities; topic/context family and soft interest weights; and selection reason. A guessed information-gain number, post-hoc prediction or undocumented selection probability must never be presented as measured.

Maintain an immutable **offer-to-outcome ledger**: offered, displayed, rendered/heard and completed probe versions; stable learner/session/run/event IDs; trusted occurrence versus upload order; item-family novelty and answer-bearing exposure; first independent answer and reviewed meaning; hints, retries, transcript, models, corrections and feedback; accessibility/channel failures; completed/abandoned state; actual follow-up interval and intervening material; delayed first-unaided retrieval; genuinely unseen independent transfer; due/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; subsequent actual teaching choice; and coverage debt transitions. A diagnostic attempt can itself be retrieval practice or instruction; log that exposure separately from a tentative claim that an earlier teaching method worked. A planned probe that never renders yields no diagnostic observation. Missing/ambiguous data are UNKNOWN, not zero or a failure.

A useful probe distinguishes *at least two predeclared, plausible alternatives* under an independently reviewed rubric while being brief, valid, accessible and non-answer-bearing for the intended first attempt. If a safe contrast is unavailable or the learner declines, continue the balanced curriculum or feasible due review and retain uncertainty. Do not force endless tests to optimize model confidence, use latency alone as ability, or select easy recognition merely because it is quick. Interest changes may alter validated equivalent contexts, not the diagnostic target, rubric, method belief or required skill. Preserve adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics.

Oracle labels: OBJECTIVE_FIXED, PRE_DECISION_HYPOTHESES_FROZEN, HYPOTHESES_NOT_DISCRIMINATED, VALID_DISCRIMINATING_PROBE, PROBE_NOT_ELIGIBLE, PROBE_OFFERED, PROBE_NOT_RENDERED, PROBE_ACTUALLY_RENDERED, FIRST_UNAIDED, ANSWER_EXPOSED, ASSISTED_DIAGNOSTIC, PROBE_AS_PRACTICE, OPPORTUNITY_COST_RECORDED, DUE_REVIEW_PRESERVED, LEARNER_DECLINED_NOT_FAILED, ACCESS_UNAVAILABLE, CAUSE_UNDETERMINED, HYPOTHESIS_REVISED, BALANCED_FALLBACK, INTEREST_METHOD_SEPARATE, CLEAN_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_INDEPENDENT_TRANSFER, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, NOT_COMPARABLE and UNSUPPORTED. Labels apply to events and tentative explanations, not permanent learner identities.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories with matched controls

**A — A contrast must actually distinguish hypotheses.** A learner independently writes “She doesn't likes coffee.” Two predeclared plausible explanations are (H1) uncertainty about the verb form after `doesn't` and (H2) a one-off slip despite general command. The tutor proposes a second *identical* negative sentence as its “diagnostic.” That item offers little distinction and rehearses the answer pattern. Expected: HYPOTHESES_NOT_DISCRIMINATED; no confident diagnosis or forced remedial loop. A matched, independently reviewed control uses short, varied affirmative/negative contrasts and an unseen verb, without showing the target answer, and records whether results meaningfully favor one explanation. Even then, a single probe cannot certify the cause or mastery. If a worked example appears first, label ANSWER_EXPOSED and do not call the response clean first-unaided.

**B — Sparse latency and a channel fault do not justify a probe cascade.** A learner previously answered accurately, but one later response arrives after 90 seconds because the app was offline; no trustworthy on-task latency exists. The tutor's proposed series of five timed quizzes assumes the delay proves a grammar deficit. Expected: CAUSE_UNDETERMINED, reject the latency-based diagnosis, preserve accessible balanced practice and optionally offer a short untimed independent check if other evidence warrants it. Matched control: several completed, comparable first-unaided meaning errors across distinct sessions may justify a bounded contrast. No invented timing, attention diagnosis, permanent “slow learner” label or automatic difficulty downgrade.

**C — Information has a cost when review and skill coverage are due.** A learner has five minutes, a valid delayed open-production review due and unresolved uncertainty between two explanations of an earlier low-stakes error. One optional diagnostic would consume the entire session; the review has a defined independent evidence obligation. Expected: record both eligible opportunities and cost, prioritize the feasible due independent review or offer a genuine learner choice, leave the diagnostic hypothesis uncertain and reschedule rather than silently clearing it. If the learner declines the review, record deferral, not failure. Matched control: with a longer available session and no urgent due debt, a brief validated contrast may be selected. No universal numeric information-gain threshold or fabricated time estimate is asserted.

**D — The diagnostic test teaches and can contaminate a later comparison.** The tutor presents a valid first-unaided contrast, then displays the correct answer and a worked explanation. The learner immediately passes a paraphrased version and later passes a truly unseen delayed task. Expected: preserve the first independent result, mark the feedback and repeated diagnostic as PROBE_AS_PRACTICE, treat the immediate paraphrase as potentially contaminated, and accept only properly reviewed independent later evidence for its own valid purpose. Do **not** attribute the later gain solely to the earlier teaching method, because the probe and feedback also taught. In a paired control without feedback and with matched opportunity, keep comparisons descriptive unless assignment and follow-up are credibly comparable.

**E — Interests cannot decide what is being diagnosed.** The fixed objective is independently requesting missing information. A game-themed prompt accidentally provides the missing room number on a visible panel, while a neutral event prompt does not. A tutor that treats the game version as an equivalent diagnostic would falsely infer the learner needs less clarification practice. Expected: independently reject the non-equivalent candidate, use a valid equivalent context or neutral fallback, preserve the same can-do and rubric, and continue sampling preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts. In a matched pair where *only* interest weights change and both prompts are independently validated as equivalent, the topic mix may change but the diagnosis, uncertainty and method preference must not. No topic-exclusive filter.

**F — Offline ordering, unknown exposure and changing evidence must not lock the policy.** In session 1, a probe is offered but not rendered. In session 2, a valid probe renders and the learner answers unaided incorrectly, then sees a model. In session 3, an unfamiliar, independently reviewed delayed probe is completed without hints and succeeds. The upload order is session 3, duplicate session 2, session 1; a second upload has a missing exposure timestamp for a different supposedly unseen item. Expected: deduplicate stable event IDs; never count session 1 as a completed diagnostic or session 2's copied repair as mastery; preserve the valid session-3 result without claiming that the probe caused it; quarantine unknown novelty/exposure for the other item. The next **actually delivered** instructional choice may revise a tentative hypothesis using valid independent evidence, while retaining uncertainty, due debt and alternatives. In the paired control session 3 fails: the same policy must be able to revise in the opposite direction, not replay a stale recommendation.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Frozen CEFR and skill obligations.** Every history binds an existing versioned can-do, prerequisites, independently reviewed rubric, target language/variety, supported skill and modality, independent exit criteria, due reviews and meaningful-turn ceilings where relevant. No recognition-to-production substitution, B2 Arc 6 shortcut or A1–C2 release.
2. **Predeclared competing explanations.** A records plausible alternatives and their predicted diagnostic distinctions *before* a probe result; an identical rehearsed prompt does not automatically distinguish causes. Unsupported hypotheses remain UNKNOWN. No fixed learning-style, disability or cognitive diagnosis.
3. **Eligible, valid and actually rendered probes.** Publish candidate, reviewed semantic validity, cost, access constraints, option set, selection rationale and actual render/exposure events. Unrendered, inaccessible, answer-bearing or ambiguous probes cannot create clean first-unaided diagnostic evidence.
4. **Weak telemetry cannot manufacture deficits.** B rejects offline/network latency as a reliable hesitation score, distinguishes genuine repeated semantic errors from one noisy observation, and chooses bounded checks or balanced fallback without timed mastery gates.
5. **Diagnostic opportunity cost and agency are auditable.** C records what due review or skill opportunity a diagnostic would displace, respects short-session constraints and declines, preserves debt, and never turns a skipped test into failure or completed evidence.
6. **Testing itself can be teaching.** D separates instruction, diagnostic retrieval, answer-bearing feedback, assisted repairs, item familiarity, delayed independent outcomes and attribution. Later valid mastery is not proof that the original teaching method caused improvement.
7. **Soft interest context remains independent.** E requires validated equivalent information state, difficulty and rubric for topic substitutions, preserves context rotation, and proves that changing only interest weights cannot change diagnostic belief or fixed standards.
8. **Replay and contradiction revise rather than freeze.** F is invariant to upload permutations and duplicates, quarantines unknown exposure chronology, separates offered/rendered/completed/missing probes, and shows opposite subsequent **actual** decisions under genuinely opposite later independent evidence.
9. **Executable proof and human-learning efficacy are different claims.** Publish machine-readable A–F traces and matched controls, exact objective/rubric/policy/probe versions, deterministic clock/seed, predeclared predictions, task validity reviews, option/cost/coverage ledgers, per-event oracle labels, follow-up denominators, replay permutations and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED outputs in an independently reviewed exact-head harness. Synthetic PASS proves only the specified software invariant. Claims of improved durable learning require a separately ethically reviewed longitudinal comparison against a balanced baseline with comparable assessment exposure, delayed unaided retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer, missingness and heterogeneity analysis, and no hidden CEFR skill loss. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge, Pre-A1 modification or A1–C2 enablement.

**Fixture verdict:** F214 makes **whether, when and how the tutor should diagnose under uncertainty** falsifiable. Good tutoring sometimes means asking a discriminating question, and sometimes means teaching, reviewing or accepting uncertainty instead. **UNSUPPORTED** until independently executed and reviewed; neither these synthetic histories nor future synthetic PASS establish real-world learning benefit.
