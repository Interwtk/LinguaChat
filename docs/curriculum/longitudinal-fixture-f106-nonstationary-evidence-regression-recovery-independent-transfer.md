# F106 — Nonstationary learner evidence, reversible regression/recovery support and independent unseen transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD with versioned CEFR-linked source tasks, trustworthy learner-event provenance and independently adjudicated longitudinal evidence. This is not shipped learner-state inference, a validated regression detector, a production defect, a CEFR award or proof of human learning gains. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 requires genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish assigned from actually rendered assistance; F47/F48 preserve noncompensatory CEFR evidence; F49 accessibility; F50 soft-interest breadth; F53 language variety/register; F80 source/rubric version boundaries; F100 cross-construct communication; F104 addresses confounded method comparisons; F105 separates self-ratings and autonomous repair from mastery. **Distinct construct:** when independent learner performance **changes over time**, can a tutor tell a credible capability-local regression or recovery from one-off noise, changed task conditions, missing follow-up or evaluator drift, and change its teaching **proportionately, reversibly and with uncertainty**? F104 compares instructional methods under confounding; F106 evaluates **temporal change and recovery decisions** without assuming stable learner ability or permanent “learning styles.”

No compulsory self-rating or modality identity questionnaire. Interest preferences are a separate soft layer: sample preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — A single poor session is not a permanent regression.** A learner has repeated independent success preserving “proposed, not confirmed” across reading and conversation. One later attempt in a noisy or interrupted session incorrectly states a meeting is confirmed; channel conditions are recorded as uncertain, not inferred as fatigue or illness. Another learner makes the same error in **multiple comparable unaided source-authored sessions**, including a delayed unseen context. A matched source world actually confirms the meeting and must reverse the answer key. The first history calls for uncertainty and a balanced probe, not permanent downgrade; the replicated source-grounded gap warrants proportionate targeted practice while preserving previous independently earned evidence.

**B — Real recovery versus a scaffolded illusion.** A learner repeatedly confuses past and ongoing events in source-valid narratives. TARGETED_ERROR_CONTRAST plus GRADED_SCAFFOLD_THEN_FADE produces correct immediate replies only after a tutor displays the tense/aspect cue; another learner independently distinguishes the meanings before help. At later **distinct sessions**, new source-authored contexts, new lexical items and different speakers test first-unaided choices, then delayed unseen production. The tutor may record assisted progress but cannot close the gap or restore independent mastery until completed unaided delayed/transfer evidence supports it. A hint selected but never rendered must not contaminate the response; a late-arriving record of a cue actually rendered earlier must.

**C — Task and evaluator changes can mimic a slump.** A versioned task pool changes from short explicit notices to multi-clause indirect requests, and an evaluator update mistakenly rejects an authorized regional variety. Raw accuracy falls, although matched unchanged-rubric anchor tasks remain independently correct. A second learner fails both old and new comparable source tasks on the same semantic objective. The first history is task-difficulty/evaluator drift and requires recalibration or quarantine, not capability regression; the second may support a local gap after sufficient independent evidence. A corrected rubric changes evaluation provenance, not the learner's underlying action. No incompatible score series may be silently joined.

**D — Recovery in one context does not prove broad transfer.** A learner improves at distinguishing conditional promises in preferred gaming conversations, then repeatedly turns “I could help on Monday” into a confirmed commitment in neutral service messages and unfamiliar community contexts. Another learner succeeds unaided across all four context strata and an unseen recipient role. Match difficulty, language variety/register, access and task outcome; preserve preferred-topic engagement but require adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar sampling. Update only the conditional-commitment capability hypothesis, not a global “bad at speaking” or “learns visually” label. Do not let interest filtering remove required objectives or contexts.

**E — Recovery pacing, review debt and unequal opportunities.** Two learners have similar initial errors. One receives repeated accessible delayed retrieval opportunities and completes new unaided probes; the other is offered a probe that never renders, another that renders but is not completed, and a third during an unavailable speech channel. The first shows durable recovery on independent new tasks; the second has **unknown** delayed status, not failure or success. A temporary increase in practice/review spacing may be selected, but review debt and required listening/speaking/free production cannot be silently waived or replaced by text proxies. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators and channel/accessibility limitations.

**F — Nonstationary evidence, offline replay and safe reversal.** Two genuine offline sessions reuse a local event ID; a third event is a true replay; network packets deliver tutor-cue telemetry out of order; a source correction supersedes a cached task; and a rubric migration changes what is admissible. A weak early preference for worked examples is contradicted by later completed unaided retrieval and unseen transfer under a different method. Deduplicate only session-qualified true replays, reconstruct trusted source/cue chronology, quarantine incompatible source/rubric versions and retain uncertainty around missing follow-up. The same semantic evidence in any receipt order must produce the same capability-local regression/recovery label, protected review debt, eligible-method set and next-action rationale. Later contradictory independent evidence must permit reversal of a weak instructional hypothesis without erasing valid history.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real CEFR source and bounded applicability.** A–F require versioned CEFR descriptor/can-do, blueprint/prerequisites, real source-authored task and answer space, language/variety/register, evaluator/rubric, accessible channel, independence and assistance rules, protected skills, reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Without repeated assessable capability evidence, regression/recovery is UNKNOWN/UNSUPPORTED, never an invented CEFR sublevel or forced remediation requirement.

2. **Time-aware, capability-local evidence.** A/B/C distinguish first unaided outcomes, later assisted correctness, autonomous repair, repeated independent failure, independently demonstrated recovery and cross-capability evidence. One noisy trial cannot permanently revoke prior mastery; repeated credible source-linked failure cannot be ignored merely because an old mastery flag exists. Any change is scoped to the evidenced objective, with timestamped rationale and uncertainty.

3. **Comparable tasks and measurement breaks.** A/C/D compare or explicitly disclose task difficulty, source-authorized meaning, topic familiarity, language variety/register, modality/accessibility, practice dose and evaluator/rubric versions. Changed task pool or rubric creates a comparability break; regrade only where provenance permits, quarantine conflicts and never relabel changed scoring as a learner regression.

4. **Rendered help versus independent recovery.** B/F grade first learner-authored unaided responses using trusted actual tutor-cue chronology. Assigned-but-unrendered support is not assistance; a rendered answer-bearing cue before the response makes that response assisted regardless of packet receipt order. Immediate coached accuracy is not recovered independent mastery.

5. **Proportionate, reversible support.** A/B/E/F record predecision eligible methods, selected/assigned/actually rendered support, strength and uncertainty of evidence, safe fallback, exploration quota and next-action rationale. Sparse or confounded observations prefer BALANCED_DEFAULT or a diagnostic independent probe; credible recurring errors can prompt targeted, fadeable practice. Later independent recovery or contradictory evidence can reverse weak preferences without fixed style labels or irreversible learner downgrades.

6. **Delayed and genuinely unseen recovery transfer.** A–F require meaningful delay/intervening material across distinct real sessions, new source-authored surface/context, first unaided learner production/comprehension, independent adjudication and recipient-relevant outcomes. Same-session repetition, coached correction, reused templates, optional confidence ratings or absent follow-up cannot establish durable recovery. Record eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators.

7. **CEFR breadth, access and soft interests.** D/E preserve shared objectives, noncompensatory required listening/reading/writing/speaking/free production as applicable, prerequisites, due reviews and exit gates. Accessibility accommodations must not be misclassified as tutor hints or linguistic deficits; missing/unavailable audio or speech cannot silently pass auditory/speaking evidence. Preferred topics increase probability only; adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts remain sampled.

8. **Temporal provenance and receipt-order invariance.** C/E/F freeze source/rubric/evaluator versions, session-qualified IDs, trusted cue/source event order, review due dates, and missingness. Permuting receipt order or replaying an identical event cannot change independence, regression/recovery classification, review debt or next-method decision. Incompatible source/correction/evaluator evidence fails closed as UNSUPPORTED until adjudicated.

9. **Assertion-level audit and bounded claims.** A–F output source/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, comparable observation windows, task and topic controls, first-unaided/assisted/delayed/unseen outcomes, follow-up denominators, protected skills, candidate/rendered methods, uncertainty, temporal change and replay permutations, plus PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED for each assertion. Synthetic or documentation QA PASS does not prove shipped longitudinal inference, human recovery, causal instructional improvement or a production defect.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and reproducible command, frozen CEFR-linked source tasks and valid adjudication, multiple actual time-separated sessions, trustworthy first-unaided/rendered-support provenance, matched anchor tasks or explicit comparability breaks, independently completed delayed genuinely unseen transfer, protected skill/review/exit requirements, accessible channels, source/rubric migrations, follow-up denominators, deterministic replay/receipt permutations and assertion-level verdicts. Missing task provenance, inadequate temporal coverage, invalid comparators, unrendered follow-up, accessibility failures or unresolved version conflicts remain UNSUPPORTED/unknown.

A claim that nonstationary adaptation **improves durable independent learning** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence with credible balanced comparator or ethically reviewed experiment, independent human adjudication, multilingual/variety/accessibility validation, missing-data and subgroup-harm analysis, privacy safeguards and ongoing monitoring. **A strong tutor neither panics after one bad lesson nor ignores a recurring gap; it tests whether learning is durable, helps the learner recover independently, and changes course when better evidence arrives.**
