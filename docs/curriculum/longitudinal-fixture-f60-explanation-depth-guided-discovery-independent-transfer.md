# F60 — Explanation depth and guided discovery must be judged by later independent transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a reproduced defect, shipped explanation selector, a validated cognitive-load metric, CEFR certification, or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F35 addresses scaffold fading/recovery; F37 distinguishes assigned from delivered instruction; F56 tests feedback timing; F59 calibrates challenge. F60 isolates **explanation strategy**: the tutor may provisionally choose concise rules, a worked example, contrastive examples, a guided question sequence, or a longer explicit explanation, but must judge each by what the learner can **later produce independently**. Explanation length, apparent insight, stated preference, confidence and immediate assisted correctness are not mastery. No permanent “discovery learner,” “grammar learner,” or modality-based learning-style category is allowed.

## Controlled setup

Resolve an **actual versioned** CEFR can-do, blueprint, prerequisite graph, language/skill and modality, target meaning/form/use, evaluator/rubric, permissible aids, independent mastery evidence, delayed retrieval, unseen-context transfer, due reviews and exit gates. Hold the required communicative construct and independent threshold fixed. Predeclare which explanation variants are source-valid for the same target, which presuppose knowledge not yet taught, and which are not comparable because they change language load, exposure to answers or practice opportunity. Separate **explanation strategy** from scaffold amount, task difficulty, feedback timing, topic familiarity, practice quantity and accessibility presentation. If a modality or evaluator is unavailable, record `UNAVAILABLE`; a written paraphrase does not prove oral competence. No invented universal explanation length or response-time threshold.

Use deterministic pseudonymous synthetic learner histories with injected seed/clock, immutable session-qualified event IDs and distinct real learning sessions. Capture objective/rubric versions; eligible strategies; selection, **actual delivery and exposure** (including partial/failed renders); language of explanation; number and specificity of examples; prompts that reveal target answers; first unaided semantic attempt; errors and repairs; hints/models/retries; optional measured latency; learner opt-out or request for a clearer explanation; independent production; later meaningfully delayed retrieval; and unseen-context transfer. Record the count of **eligible, offered, completed and missing** follow-up probes. Distinguish self-generated explanation from tutor-supplied rule and note when the learner simply repeats the worked example. Respect source-permitted bilingual clarification and accessibility without equating either to inability. Keep interest-topic weighting as a separate soft policy with neutral, adjacent and unfamiliar rotation. Avoid inferring intelligence, disability, personality, effort or fixed learning styles from verbosity, pauses, self-report or mistakes.

Predeclare `EXPLANATION_SELECTED`, `EXPLANATION_DELIVERED`, `EXPLANATION_ASSIGNED_NOT_DELIVERED`, `WORKED_ANSWER_EXPOSED`, `LEARNER_GENERATED_RULE`, `TUTOR_SUPPLIED_RULE`, `ASSISTED_PRACTICE_ONLY`, `INDEPENDENT_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL`, `INDEPENDENT_UNSEEN_TRANSFER`, `STRATEGY_COMPARISON_CONFOUNDED`, `FOLLOWUP_UNKNOWN`, `RUBRIC_MISMATCH`, `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE`, `EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED`, `UNAVAILABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. These are evidence states, not learner identities.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — Short rule versus worked example, opposite later outcomes.** On the same versioned can-do and matched task, learner A makes more independent progress after a concise form/meaning rule and learner B after a source-valid worked example. Both can reproduce an example immediately; only delayed, unaided, previously unseen tasks distinguish their useful strategies. The tutor may provisionally diverge for A and B **without** labeling either learner as a type, relaxing the common standard, or treating example imitation as independent transfer.
- **B — Guided discovery that merely supplies the answer.** A tutor asks apparently open questions but supplies each critical word and the final rule before the learner responds. Another learner generates a plausible rule before any revealing cue, then applies it in a genuinely new context. Equal final answers and equally positive learner feedback must not erase authorship/exposure differences. A guided question that encodes the answer is assistance, not independent discovery.
- **C — Long explanation creates immediate fluency but later interference.** An elaborate explanation produces high coached accuracy and a convincing verbal summary, yet subsequent independent retrieval confuses two neighboring forms. A shorter contrastive explanation followed by independent attempts yields stronger delayed discrimination for the same target. Record repeated error *meaning* and cue dependence; adapt explanation detail provisionally and retain future alternative trials. Do not diagnose cognitive overload from elapsed time or message length alone.
- **D — Explanation-language, prerequisite and accessibility confounds.** A learner needs a missing prerequisite, another uses a permitted bilingual clarification, and another requests an accessible text presentation instead of a failed audio render. A comparison falsely credits the longer explanation because it alone teaches the prerequisite or is actually readable. Repair prerequisite/presentation validity first; do not classify language choice or accommodation as a weak learner trait. If source equivalence or the required modality cannot be established, return `UNSUPPORTED`/`UNAVAILABLE` rather than selecting a winning strategy.
- **E — Topic familiarity and later policy reversal.** Interest-weighted music examples initially make guided discovery look superior to neutral worked examples; counterbalanced topic, item novelty, order and explanation exposure remove the advantage. After additional independent delayed probes, the learner's earlier apparent explanation preference reverses. Lower confidence, update the per-objective hypothesis, sample viable alternatives, rotate neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar topics, and protect due reviews and receptive/productive skill coverage.
- **F — Offline exposure, duplicate events and changed rubric.** A worked example is assigned but not rendered; a different hint is actually displayed before a retry but its event syncs late; one independent first attempt is uploaded twice; a new session reuses a local event ID; and a later evaluator version changes acceptable responses. Reconstruct trusted occurrence/**delivery** chronology with session-qualified IDs and immutable source versions. Preserve original first-attempt independence when warranted, mark only cue-exposed revisions assisted, quarantine unknown exposure and reject unsupported cross-rubric comparisons. Receipt order must not change the policy ledger.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-defined target and standards remain fixed.** For A–F emit actual versioned can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, modality, evaluator/rubric, permissible supports, independent mastery requirement, delayed retrieval, unseen transfer, due review and exit gate. Changing explanation cannot alter CEFR objectives or required independent evidence. Missing source mapping is `UNSUPPORTED`; missing modality/evaluator is `UNAVAILABLE`.
2. **Compare explanations, not mixed interventions.** In A/C/D/E, explicitly separate explanation content/depth from scaffold quantity, feedback timing, practice count, task difficulty, prerequisite teaching, language burden, topic familiarity, item exposure and order. If they cannot be held comparable or reported, label the causal comparison confounded instead of declaring a winning method.
3. **First independent attempt survives later coaching.** In A/B, distinguish rule or example *supplied by the tutor* from language or generalization *generated by the learner before exposure*. Record semantic quality and later transfer. A polished answer after a revealing question is assisted practice, not proof of unaided mastery.
4. **Assigned instruction is not experienced instruction.** In B/F, preserve selected, attempted, displayed/heard and actually exposed explanation variants with trusted timing. Unrendered text, unplayed audio and missing telemetry cannot be counted as delivered; uncertain exposure is quarantined.
5. **Delayed and unseen transfer determine value.** In A/C, immediate assisted accuracy, confidence and verbal explanation alone cannot establish method superiority. Require meaningful intervening time/material, independent later probes, unseen contexts, and honest eligible/offered/completed/missing denominators; a calendar rollover alone is not a delay.
6. **No permanent learner-style inference.** In A/E, represent strategy hypotheses by objective and context with uncertainty; contradictory delayed evidence can reverse a provisional choice. Preserve feasible exploration, balanced skills and review obligations; never assign a visual/auditory/discovery/grammar “type.”
7. **Accessibility, agency and interests stay separate.** In D/E, honor source-valid accommodations, requests for clarification, pauses and safe topic alternatives without lowering required competence. Interest weights are soft and cannot exclude neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar contexts or required can-dos. A text substitute does not certify unsupported speech/listening.
8. **Replay and rubric changes preserve provenance.** In F, receipt-order permutations, duplicate events, reused local IDs, late hints, incomplete probes and rubric changes converge on identical first-attempt, independent/assisted, review and policy ledgers. Keep source versions immutable and never fabricate cross-version equivalence.
9. **No documentation-to-efficacy inflation.** For each history output source/rubric IDs, strategy candidates, actual delivery, first/final attempts, error categories, cue exposure, independent delayed/transfer probes and missingness, topic/skill balance, provisional hypothesis with uncertainty and per-assertion `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED`. A green documentation CI is not an executable longitudinal evaluator, a shipped tutor or proof of improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, deterministic seed/clock, immutable synthetic events with replay permutations, source-resolved can-do/task/rubric versions, construct-equivalent explanation comparisons, delivered content and cue chronology, first-attempt semantic outcomes, independently completed delayed retrieval and unseen transfer with missingness denominators, balanced topic/skill coverage, reversible policy decisions and assertion-level verdicts. Unmeasured strategy exposure, unavailable modalities, uncertain equivalence and missing probes remain explicitly unknown or unsupported.

Any claim that personalized explanation strategies improve **durable independent retention and transfer** requires separately reviewed longitudinal real-learner evidence against a credible balanced comparator, sufficient genuinely delayed unaided outcomes, checks for harm to learners with sparse observations and accessibility needs, and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor explains in the way that helps this learner learn independently over time, then changes its mind when the evidence changes.**
