# F119 — Conversational repair, clarification initiative and independent communicative transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD, real versioned CEFR-linked tasks, source-validated multilingual repair opportunities and longitudinal first-unaided outcomes. This does **not** ship a dialogue-repair detector, conversational agent, CEFR mastery, reliable emotion/intent inference or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 protects genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish actually rendered tutor help from queued help and tutor-origin errors; F49 protects accessible construct validity; F50 protects nonexclusive interest rotation; F104 separates method from task difficulty; F107 handles unreliable feedback; F110 handles ambiguous nonverbal cues; F111/F112 separate ASR/TTS noise from learner evidence; F113 protects speaker attribution; F114 protects referent grounding; F117 protects variety/register; F118 protects scope and conditional meaning. **Distinct construct:** can a learner **notice a consequential conversational breakdown, initiate an appropriate clarification or self-repair, incorporate the partner's actual answer, and independently reach an accurate shared understanding**, without the tutor supplying the repair or treating polite but uninformative exchanges as mastery? This is a communicative strategy construct, not a fixed personality trait or a mandate to ask clarifying questions on every turn.

## Source-validated repair opportunity and evidence contract

For each case resolve the **real versioned** CEFR descriptor/can-do, level blueprint and prerequisites, target language and source-approved varieties, source-authored dialogue and partner role, communicative goal, the information actually available to each speaker, specific ambiguity or breakdown, source-valid repair strategies, register constraints, task rubric/evaluator, first-unaided threshold, protected listening/reading/writing/speaking/free-production evidence, assistance/accommodation policy, due reviews, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer and exit gate. Do not add a higher-level pragmatic prerequisite to a beginner descriptor: a simple “Could you repeat the time?” may be sufficient where the source task warrants it. If the partner's statement is already clear, a correct direct response may be better than unnecessary clarification. Do not infer partner feelings, consent, identity or intent beyond source evidence.

Freeze a **repair-opportunity ledger**: task/descriptor/blueprint/rubric/evaluator hashes; dialogue/source-effective version; language/variety and speaker turns; actual stimulus and render provenance; who possesses the missing information; ambiguity type and stakes; source-authorized clarification, confirmation, self-repair and direct-answer paths; first learner-authored turn; whether the learner noticed a breakdown, sought a discriminating detail and integrated the reply; partner answer provenance; tutor hint/model/translation exposure **actually rendered before the learner turn**; accommodation route; retries; review interval; delayed/unseen task-family overlap; confidence and missingness. Record only supported events, with privacy minimization; do not infer learner anxiety, disability or cultural identity from repair frequency.

Task-local labels: SOURCE_VALID_TASK, REPAIR_OPPORTUNITY_VALIDATED, NO_REPAIR_NEEDED, INFORMATION_GAP_REAL, INFORMATION_GAP_UNRESOLVED, LEARNER_INITIATED_CLARIFICATION, LEARNER_INITIATED_SELF_REPAIR, PARTNER_INITIATED_REPAIR, TUTOR_PROMPTED_REPAIR, DISCRIMINATING_QUESTION, NONDISCRIMINATING_QUESTION, CONFIRMATION_SOURCE_VALID, ANSWER_INTEGRATED, UNSUPPORTED_ASSUMPTION, REGISTER_SOURCE_VALID, FIRST_UNAIDED, ACTUALLY_RENDERED_HELP, ASSISTED, DELAYED_UNAIDED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_UNAIDED_TRANSFER, ASR_TTS_CHANNEL_CONFLICT, REPAIR_METHOD_CONFOUNDED, TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, VERSION_CONFLICT_QUARANTINED, NOT_APPLICABLE and UNSUPPORTED. Vary only actually implemented, accessible, source-validated explanation, scaffold, modality mix, pacing, ordering, examples and review spacing. Preserve shared CEFR objective, evaluator meaning, independent thresholds, protected skill families, due reviews and exit gates. Compare **later first-unaided retention and unseen transfer**, with uncertainty, deliberate alternative sampling and equivalent task opportunity—not assisted success or a “learning style.” Interest topics remain soft probabilities; retain adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Learner asks for the missing discriminating detail.** A source-authored scheduling dialogue says “We can meet on Thursday” but has two possible Thursday dates in context. Learner A independently asks “Do you mean the 12th or the 19th?”; learner B immediately confirms the 12th without evidence; learner C asks “Can you explain?” without identifying which detail is missing. Score A as a source-valid discriminating clarification, B as an unsupported assumption and C as an attempted but nondiscriminating repair, without automatically treating C as zero communicative competence. A partner's subsequent explicit “the 19th” must change the final agreed date; simply asking a question without using the reply does not complete the task. A counterfactual source dialogue explicitly gives the date: a direct correct response passes and gratuitous questions cannot inflate mastery.

**B — Self-repair is different from tutor-provided repair.** Learner A independently says “I booked Tuesday—sorry, I mean Thursday” before any tutor intervention and then accurately confirms Thursday with the partner. Learner B makes the same correction only after a tutor displays “Say Thursday instead.” Learner C reads a pre-rendered model verbatim. Preserve A's first-turn error and self-initiated correction as separate evidence, mark B/C assisted, and do not silently overwrite the original first attempt with the polished final answer. A source task may validly credit an autonomous self-repair as communicative strategy evidence while **not** converting the originally incorrect lexical/grammar attempt into first-try independent correctness.

**C — Appropriate repair varies by register and context.** In source-approved casual chat, “Huh? Which one?” can be an acceptable clarification; in a formal customer exchange, a more suitable source-validated request may be “Could you confirm which invoice you mean?” Do not reject a legitimate regional or register variant merely for deviating from a single preferred phrase (F117). A learner who asks an elaborate polite question about the wrong invoice number has not resolved the information gap. If the source has no validated register expectation, mark that dimension UNSUPPORTED rather than penalizing a regional variety. Preserve the same can-do and comparable semantic burden across interest-based topic substitutions.

**D — Technical noise is not a learner's pragmatic deficit.** In a listening task, TTS says “fifteen” while the authoritative transcript says “fifty”; the learner asks the partner to repeat the number. The repair attempt is observable, but the listening-error attribution is ASR_TTS_CHANNEL_CONFLICT and affected proficiency claims are UNSUPPORTED until reliable stimulus provenance is established. In an ASR variant the learner speaks an accurate clarification that is transcribed as a different request: do not mark the learner unable to clarify without trustworthy speech evidence (F111). Accessibility-preserving text routes may support the same **repair-strategy** can-do when source-authorized, but cannot be misreported as independent listening/speaking mastery. Missing latency or a long pause never proves unwillingness to clarify.

**E — Adaptive method choice requires delayed independent repair.** Fictional learner A completes model-assisted clarification drills yet later assumes dates without checking; after actually delivered INFORMATION_GAP_ROLEPLAY, A independently asks discriminating questions and incorporates answers in delayed unfamiliar conversations. Learner B shows the reverse pattern and benefits from CLARIFICATION_FORM_MINIMAL_PAIRS. Compare source-validated opportunities, can-do, baseline, language/variety, partner responsiveness, stakes, difficulty, modality/access route, topic familiarity, assistance exposure, spacing and missingness. With enough comparable outcomes the next **actually delivered** instructional choice may differ by learner, with uncertainty and alternative sampling. If roleplay always contains obvious two-choice dates while minimal pairs contain subtle pronoun ambiguity, flag REPAIR_METHOD_CONFOUNDED; do not announce a winner. Contradictory delayed evidence must lower confidence and permit reversal. Balance receptive and productive skills and unfamiliar contexts.

**F — Turn ordering, offline replay and source revision.** A learner asks for clarification before a tutor hint is queued, but the hint renders only after the learner's turn: the learner's first clarification remains unaided. A delayed offline event is uploaded twice: deduplicate by session-qualified immutable event ID. A partner's answer is revised from “Gate B” to “Gate C” with a new source-effective version; the earlier first attempt remains scored against its contemporaneous source, and later transfer uses the new version without rewriting history. A partner's answer from another speaker/session cannot silently resolve the current dialogue. If the partner never replies, the learner's independently initiated appropriate question is retained as strategy evidence, but successful answer integration and completion are **missing**, not inferred. A “new” transfer task that only changes names while recycling the same ambiguity and answer sequence is not genuinely unseen.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real source/CEFR linkage and opportunity validity.** A–F resolve versioned CEFR can-do, blueprint, source dialogue/roles, language-specific repair strategies, rubric/evaluator, accommodation/assistance policy, independent threshold, protected skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. No invented high-level pragmatic prerequisite or universal register norm.
2. **Detect the information gap without over-clarifying.** A distinguishes validated ambiguity from NO_REPAIR_NEEDED; both a source-valid discriminating clarification and a source-valid direct answer can pass in their proper contexts. Generic questions and confident assumptions do not automatically resolve the gap.
3. **Partner response integration and shared understanding.** A/C/F require source-supported incorporation of the **actual** partner answer and accurate final confirmation. A correct question without a response does not earn completed dialogue evidence; a fabricated answer is not independent transfer.
4. **Learner agency versus tutor assistance.** B/F distinguish learner-initiated self-repair and clarification from partner-initiated or tutor-prompted corrections, including pre/post-rendered help, copied models, retries and chronology. Never rewrite first-attempt quality when a later turn is improved.
5. **Multilingual variety, register and construct validity.** C/D accept source-approved target-language repair equivalents and legitimate varieties, preserve accessible paths and protected modalities, and quarantine unreliable ASR/TTS stimuli. Do not infer proficiency, anxiety or intent from latency alone.
6. **Meaningful delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen transfer.** A–F require separated learning sessions and independently authored new partner roles, information gaps, ambiguity structures and contexts, without answer-bearing hints. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; cosmetic topic changes do not pass.
7. **Reversible instruction adaptation with balanced skill coverage.** E/F log feasible, assigned and actually delivered methods, comparable opportunity/difficulty, baseline, assistance, uncertainty, exploration, delayed outcome, topic diversity and protected review/exit obligations. Sparse or confounded evidence falls back to balanced instruction; no fixed learning-type label.
8. **Event, source and speaker provenance.** D/F deduplicate replayed events, bind turns to the right speaker and source-effective version, distinguish queued from rendered help and quarantine irreconcilable conflicts. Missing partner turns and follow-ups remain missing, never synthetic successes or failures.
9. **Exact-head reproducibility and bounded claims.** A–F emit deterministic seed/clock, named engine HEAD and runnable command, descriptor/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, source-approved repair paths, first and assisted turns, render/event ledger, delayed/unseen denominators, counterfactuals and **PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion**. Documentation CI alone is not an executable repair evaluator, a reproducible product defect or proof of real-learner gains.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD, reproducible command and deterministic clock; real versioned CEFR-linked tasks; independently source-validated multilingual repair opportunities and register alternatives; reliable partner/source/stimulus provenance; actual exposure and first-unaided chronology; accessible construct-valid routes; accurate partner-answer integration; meaningful delayed independent retrieval and genuinely unseen communicative transfer; comparable or explicitly noncomparable method choices; preserved protected skill/review/exit obligations; eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; replay/version/confound permutations; and per-assertion verdicts. Without a validated information gap, trustworthy partner response, reliable channel or independent follow-up, affected claims remain **UNSUPPORTED/unknown** while valid partial strategy evidence is retained.

A stronger claim that adaptive repair instruction **improves durable independent communication** requires a separately reviewed multilingual, multivariety, accessibility-validated real-learner longitudinal study with credible comparator, attrition/subgroup-harm analysis, privacy review and reproducible independent outcomes. **A strong tutor helps the learner recover shared meaning autonomously; it does not manufacture agreement, mastery or certainty.**
