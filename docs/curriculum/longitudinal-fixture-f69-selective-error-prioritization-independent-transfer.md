# F69 — Selective error prioritization must improve independent communicative transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a reproduced production defect, implemented error-triage policy, CEFR certification, or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F38 guards tutor-origin error attribution; F51 spontaneous versus prompted repair; F53 register appropriateness; F54 authentic clarification; F56 feedback timing and interruption; F59 desirable difficulty; F60 explanation depth; F64 fluency versus accuracy; F68 metacognitive help-seeking. **F69 isolates *which* errors to address and how much corrective information to present, not *when* to interrupt.** A strong tutor must not bury a learner under corrections, ignore recurring form errors that the source rubric requires, or mistake a flawless revision after answer-bearing feedback for independent competence.

## Controlled setup

Resolve a **real, versioned** CEFR can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, task and communicative function, skill/modality, evaluator/rubric, semantic and pragmatic demands, permitted assistance, independent evidence, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer, due reviews and level exit gate. Freeze those demands before comparing treatments. An apparently minor form error may be consequential when it changes meaning, politeness, reference or a source-required grammatical distinction; do not impose universal grammar-versus-meaning rankings, error-count cutoffs or a single correction dose across objectives.

Predeclare feasible reversible feedback-focus candidates `MEANING_FIRST`, `FORM_FIRST`, `PRAGMATICS_FIRST`, `SINGLE_PRIORITY_ERROR`, `MULTI_ERROR_DIAGNOSTIC` and `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. Select only options that preserve safety, source-valid task demands, accessibility and adequate chances to practise all required error families. The policy may choose what to explain and rehearse, but **must not lower independent scoring requirements** or suppress necessary corrections to manufacture success. For high-stakes misunderstanding, do not withhold an essential correction merely to preserve an experiment.

For comparable learner/objective histories, retain source/rubric versions, first unaided response and meaning, all observed error candidates and their severity/source, recurring error-family history, whether the error came from the learner, tutor prompt, transcription or evaluator, candidate/assigned/rendered feedback focus, actual delivered wording and answer-bearing content, response revisions and retries, independent versus supported provenance, task and feedback dose, time/attention burden when observed, modality/accessibility, context familiarity, due reviews, delayed independent retrieval, unseen transfer, and eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators. Use immutable session-qualified event IDs, content hashes and trusted event occurrence/delivery order, not receipt order. Missing voice/audio or latency is unknown, never a negative learner trait.

Observation labels: `SOURCE_REQUIRED_ERROR`, `MEANING_BREAKDOWN`, `FORM_ERROR`, `PRAGMATIC_MISMATCH`, `NONCRITICAL_VARIANT`, `TUTOR_ORIGIN_ERROR`, `EVALUATOR_UNCERTAIN`, `FIRST_ATTEMPT_INDEPENDENT`, `FEEDBACK_ASSIGNED`, `FEEDBACK_RENDERED`, `ANSWER_BEARING_FEEDBACK`, `ASSISTED_REVISION`, `INDEPENDENT_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL`, `INDEPENDENT_UNSEEN_TRANSFER`, `ERROR_FAMILY_RECURRED`, `FOLLOWUP_MISSING`, `TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED`, `ACCESSIBILITY_CONFOUNDED`, `EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED`, `RUBRIC_MISMATCH`, `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. Labels describe evidence, not permanent learner identities.

## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — More corrections do not automatically teach more.** On matched source-equivalent requests, learner A receives a long multi-error explanation after each attempt, then produces a polished assisted revision but misses the intended request independently in a new context days later. Learner B receives one source-relevant meaning repair plus independent retry opportunities, and later makes the request successfully without help. Record first-attempt quality, actual correction exposure, equivalent practice opportunity and delayed transfer; do not infer the one-error policy wins from immediate revision quality, fewer corrections or learner satisfaction alone. If the long explanation is the only available safe response, do not force an invalid comparison.
- **B — Recurrent form errors can be mastery-critical.** A learner communicates the gist correctly but repeatedly confuses a source-required past-versus-present distinction. A `MEANING_FIRST` policy marks the gist as understood yet does not award independent mastery. A `FORM_FIRST` policy targets the required distinction, and a later unaided, unfamiliar probe tests whether it transferred. Conversely, a harmless spelling variant outside the source-defined construct must not become a mandatory gate. Freeze the actual can-do and evaluator before either decision.
- **C — Pragmatic harm and tutor-origin errors need different treatment.** A learner's utterance is grammatical but too direct for a source-required polite refusal; a purely grammar-focused correction misses the communicative failure. In another session the tutor supplies an unnatural model, which the learner echoes; the error must be attributed to the tutor/model rather than counted as a recurring independent learner error. Source-valid register and meaning are assessed without stereotyping accents, cultures or personalities.
- **D — A useful correction focus can reverse by objective and time.** Matched histories initially suggest `SINGLE_PRIORITY_ERROR` yields stronger delayed independent requests than `MULTI_ERROR_DIAGNOSTIC`, while narrative sequencing benefits from a broader review. Later unseen probes contradict the first advantage. Emit objective-specific uncertain hypotheses, preserve exploration, and actually reverse the next feasible choice; do not classify the learner as someone who “only learns from brief feedback.”
- **E — Interests, modality and accessibility can masquerade as feedback effects.** Targeted feedback is paired with favourite sports dialogues while broad feedback is paired with unfamiliar public-service requests; another learner uses an accessible text entry method that changes apparent correction burden. Match or disclose context, objective, difficulty, modality, exposure and feedback opportunity before attributing retention to error focus. Preserve adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics, due reviews, required productive/receptive balance, and explicit `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE` when oral assessment cannot run.
- **F — Offline corrections, missing probes and rubric drift.** A feedback card is assigned but never rendered; a model answer is delivered before a revision but uploads afterward; two sessions reuse a local event ID; a delayed probe is offered but not completed; an evaluator revision changes which form error is material. Reconstruct delivered feedback from trusted session-qualified events, deduplicate replay, keep missing follow-up separate from failure, and reject incomparable rubric versions. Reversing upload order must not change independence labels, error priorities or the next policy decision; unresolved chronology fails closed.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Frozen, noncompensatory construct.** For A–F emit actual versioned can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, source task, skill/modality, evaluator, semantic/pragmatic demands, permitted help, independent evidence, delayed and unseen probes, reviews and exit gate. Feedback selection never waives source-required meaning, form or register. Unresolved mappings remain `UNSUPPORTED`.
2. **Error taxonomy with accountable provenance.** In B/C/F distinguish learner-produced meaning, form and pragmatic errors from tutor-origin models, transcription uncertainty, evaluator uncertainty and source-irrelevant variants. Recurrence counts only comparable independent learner attempts; never silently turn a model-induced error into a learner deficit.
3. **First-attempt versus corrected success.** In A/B/C/F retain first unaided semantic/pragmatic/form evidence separately from feedback, revisions, hints and model copies. A flawless answer after an answer-bearing correction remains assisted for that target; genuine source-permitted clarification is evaluated under the frozen rubric rather than universally penalized.
4. **Priority choice respects communicative consequence.** In A/B/C distinguish an error that blocks meaning, one that violates source-required grammatical contrast, a context-required pragmatic failure and a noncritical variant. Select among feasible focus candidates with an auditable rationale; no universal priority order, correction quota or unreviewed cultural assumption substitutes for the source rubric.
5. **Independent delayed and unseen outcome.** In A/B/D/F compare policies using source-equivalent independent delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen transfer after meaningful intervening practice/time. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators. Immediate corrected accuracy, lower error count or reduced feedback volume alone cannot establish durable benefit.
6. **Comparable exposure and protected access.** In A/D/E match or disclose practice dose, correction content and burden, opportunity, objective, topic familiarity, difficulty, modality and accessibility. All required language skills, review obligations and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar contexts remain available; unassessed speaking/listening must not become text-proxy success.
7. **Delivered-feedback chronology and replay invariance.** In C/F trace candidates, assigned versus actually rendered focus, exact cue hashes, answer-bearing content, trusted event ordering, session-qualified IDs and offline replay deduplication. Receipt-order permutations yield identical evidence and next-choice traces; unknown cue delivery or rubric drift is not silently passed.
8. **Reversible, uncertain learner-specific adaptation.** In A/D/E/F emit feasible alternatives, actual chosen/rendered focus, error-family evidence, objective-specific later outcomes, uncertainty, exploration, contradictory evidence and the next reversible choice. Missing or confounded evidence triggers a balanced source-safe default, not a fixed learner type; interests affect contexts only.
9. **Assertion-level verdicts and truthful release gate.** For A–F emit source/rubric IDs, error provenance/severity, first/final outcomes, correction delivery chronology, assisted/independent labels, delayed/unseen probes and denominators, confounds, modality/topic/review debt, replay equivalence and per-assertion `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED`. Documentation QA does not mean an executable tutor test passed or that real learners improved.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command; deterministic seeded multi-session histories and controlled clock; real source-resolved CEFR tasks and rubrics; first unaided scoring; attributable error candidates; assigned and rendered feedback with delivery hashes; support-sensitive revisions; matched opportunities and correction burden; delayed independent and genuinely unseen probes; follow-up denominators; replay-order permutations; reversible policy decisions; protected required skill/review/topic coverage; and assertion-level verdicts. Missing evaluator/source mappings, event delivery, error attribution, modality or follow-up must be marked `UNSUPPORTED` or unknown, not fabricated.

A claim that adaptive error prioritization **improves durable independent retention or transfer** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, sufficiently delayed and unseen independent outcomes, privacy/accessibility review, subgroup harm checks and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor chooses the correction that helps the learner do more unaided later, without hiding the mistakes that the language objective actually requires them to overcome.**
