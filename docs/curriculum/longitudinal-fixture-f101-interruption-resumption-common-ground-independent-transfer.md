# F101 — Interruption, conversation resumption, common-ground continuity and independent unseen transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a shipped dialogue-memory feature, a promise of cross-session personal-data retention, CEFR certification, a reproduced production defect, or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 requires genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish selected from actually rendered support; F47/F48 preserve noncompensatory CEFR evidence; F49 accessibility; F50 soft-interest breadth; F53 language variety/register; F54 repair; F71 discourse reference; F72 goal constraints; F78 shared-ground confirmation; F80 source/version attribution; F89 correction; F95 temporal anchoring; F100 compositional recipient integrity. **Distinct construct:** when a **real versioned CEFR-linked communicative task** requires returning to an unfinished exchange after an intervening topic, interruption, changed interlocutor or authorized later resumption, can the learner independently recover **only the still-valid shared commitments, referents and unresolved questions**, distinguish them from withdrawn or unknown states, and continue without inventing memory or agreement? F71/F78 test reference/common-ground locally; F100 tests simultaneous obligations. F101 tests **continuity across a source-defined interruption boundary and re-entry decision**. A task with no legitimate continuity demand is `NOT_APPLICABLE`, never a new universal graduation gate or a generic memory-span/IQ test.

## Controlled setup and adjudication

Resolve the real versioned CEFR descriptor/can-do, level blueprint and prerequisites, target language/variety/register, source-authored exchange, recipient goal, authorized source versions, accessible modality, validated rubric/evaluator, assistance policy, independent threshold, required receptive/productive skills, due reviews, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer and exit gate. Freeze a **bounded conversation-state ledger** from the source-authored dialogue before teaching: speaker/recipient identity, current topic, grounded referents, propositions and their source authority, confirmed/tentative/withdrawn/unknown commitments, unanswered questions, deadlines and valid next actions. Mark which state survives each **explicitly authored interruption boundary**, which information becomes stale or superseded, what the recipient can reasonably know, and which short recap or clarification is sufficient. Distinguish a genuine topic switch, deliberate pause, message forwarded later, device/session discontinuity and source correction; do not assume they have the same permissions or retained context. A learner may validly say they do not know or ask for a recap. Never fabricate persistent memory when the task does not supply authorized information.

Predeclare feasible reversible methods `STATE_LEDGER_THEN_RESUME`, `RECIPIENT_RECAP_VERIFY`, `INTERRUPTION_ROLEPLAY`, `CONTRAST_STALE_AND_CURRENT`, `QUESTION_BEFORE_COMMITMENT` and `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. Vary only actually implemented/evaluable listening, reading, writing, speaking, free production, explanation style, scaffold, pacing, repetition/review spacing, ordering and examples. Hold the **same** CEFR can-do, authorized state and interruption, source-required re-entry burden, recipient goal, rubric, independence threshold, skills, reviews and exit gate across candidates. Do not equate a learner's temporary preference for recaps with a fixed learning style. Preserve uncertainty, sample feasible alternatives and reverse method choices when later independent outcomes warrant it. Interests softly weight examples while retaining preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts.

Use deterministic pseudonymous histories across **distinct real learning sessions**, injected clock/seed, immutable session-qualified event IDs, frozen source/task/ledger/rubric/evaluator hashes, trusted source chronology and **actually rendered** tutor/interlocutor chronology. Record the first learner-authored recipient-facing move **after re-entry**, whether a prior cue revealed the answer, valid state carried forward, invalid/stale state discarded, legitimate clarification, recipient interpretation/action, first-attempt quality, retries, hint/model/scaffold dependence, recurring error type, calibrated latency only when reliable, topic familiarity, dose, modality/accessibility, independent delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen transfer. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators separately. An answer-bearing tutor recap, completed state ledger or explicit missing-commitment cue **actually rendered** before the assessed move is assistance; assigned-but-unrendered help is not. A source-authored interlocutor recap is legitimate task material **only if the frozen protocol includes it**, and must not be compared as equal burden with a no-recap condition. Late telemetry cannot retroactively make coached performance independent. Do not treat absent or inaccessible source history as a learner failure.

## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Topic interruption must not turn a suggestion into a booking.** A source-authored exchange says: “Tuesday could work, but I need to check the room.” The conversation turns to an unrelated lunch question for several turns. On return, the recipient asks, “So should I tell everyone it's Tuesday?” Learner A separately recognizes “Tuesday” and the earlier suggestion but replies “Yes, it's booked.” Learner B independently says Tuesday is only tentative and the room still needs confirmation, or uses a validated concise clarification. A control world explicitly confirms the room before the interruption; a firm confirmation is then correct. Later unseen transfer changes event, interruption content, recipient and status wording. Score the re-entry recipient action, not recall of a keyword.

- **B — Ambiguous referent after topic switching.** A source-authored organizer discusses a **red** accessibility form and a **blue** registration form; only the blue one was submitted. An unrelated topic intervenes. The recipient returns with “Was that one sent?” where “that one” is genuinely ambiguous in the authorized source context. Learner A confidently answers “Yes, the red one” from recency or a familiar topic. Learner B independently asks which form is meant, or identifies the blue form **only when the task's source context disambiguates it**. A matched control adds an explicit “blue registration form” anchor, making direct confirmation correct. Do not reward indiscriminate clarification when the referent is already clear, or penalize a culturally natural clarification when it is not.

- **C — Correction during interruption invalidates old common ground.** The first authoritative source message states “The Friday session is online.” A later authorized correction, delivered while participants discuss another topic, says “Friday is now in person.” On resumption, learner A fluently restates the old online plan; learner B independently distinguishes the earlier plan from the corrected current venue. A historical-report control explicitly asks “What did the original notice say?” and accepts the old claim **with attribution**. If the correction was never accessible to the learner, classify missing-source/accessibility confounding rather than hallucinating an obligation to know it. Unseen transfer changes correction direction, source authority and time.

- **D — A discontinuity cannot license invented memory or private-state carryover.** A source-authored exchange is resumed in a new conversation with **no authorized earlier transcript**, no user-approved retained facts and a recipient who does not know prior commitments. Learner A invents “As we agreed yesterday, I will send your address,” claiming continuity. Learner B independently says they lack the earlier details and requests a safe recap, without exposing private information. A second source world provides an explicit, authorized, accessible prior summary: the learner may then use only its supported facts. This is a **curricular communication task**, not an instruction to implement app storage, upload progress or retain sensitive personal data.

- **E — Immediate recall versus later independent re-entry.** In matched early sessions for the same source-required interruption ledger and CEFR can-do, `STATE_LEDGER_THEN_RESUME` yields high immediate accuracy after a tutor-completed ledger; `INTERRUPTION_ROLEPLAY` initially produces slower unaided attempts and occasional repairs. In **later distinct real sessions**, genuinely unseen exchanges change the interruption location, unresolved commitment, referents and recipient decision. The synthetic learner taught through roleplay independently preserves more current state and makes fewer consequential stale-action errors. Match or disclose baseline proficiency, interruption length, state-graph complexity, source ambiguity, feedback, practice dose, language variety/register, modality/accessibility and topic familiarity. Prefer completed delayed unaided outcomes over coached immediate recall, log the **actually rendered** changed next method and keep exploring; no universal superiority of roleplay is claimed.

- **F — Interest overfit, late tutor recap, event replay and unavailable source.** A learner resumes favorite-game dialogues accurately but confuses neutral travel and unfamiliar community exchanges of equivalent state complexity. A tutor's full recap was rendered before the first re-entry move but telemetry arrives later; another recap was selected but never displayed. Two offline sessions reuse the same local event ID; an authoritative correction arrives out of order; a rubric revision accepts a concise regional clarification; an eligible delayed probe is offered but never completed. In an inaccessible audio source, a negation that withdraws an earlier offer is missing from captions. Reconstruct trusted render/source chronology, deduplicate only true session-qualified replays, quarantine incompatible source/ledger/rubric versions, preserve review debt and unknown follow-up, avoid scoring inaccessible negation as a learner error, rotate topic contexts and demand invariant re-entry/assistance/next-method verdicts under receipt-order permutations.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real CEFR task and interruption applicability.** A–F emit a real versioned descriptor/can-do, blueprint/prerequisites, target language/variety, source-authored dialogue, explicit interruption/resumption boundary, recipient goal, frozen bounded state ledger and source authority, rubric/evaluator, assistance policy, independent threshold, required skills, reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. No source-required continuity demand means `NOT_APPLICABLE`; missing mapping or inaccessible/unverifiable state means `UNSUPPORTED`, not invented mastery.

2. **Correct continuity versus false carryover.** A/C/D compare what the recipient can legitimately carry forward across a specified boundary. Tentative commitments remain tentative; superseded plans do not silently survive; unavailable or unauthorized history cannot become remembered agreement. A false continuity claim cannot pass because the sentence is fluent or repeats the right noun.

3. **Referents, unknowns and bounded clarification.** B/D accept independently accurate disambiguation, culturally appropriate clarification and safe acknowledgment of missing information. A valid direct answer must not be penalized merely for avoiding an unnecessary recap; an ambiguous reference cannot be scored as confirmed. Preserve recipient permissions and privacy boundaries.

4. **Source correction, chronology and recipient action.** A/C/F distinguish old from current source-authoritative state, historical reporting from present instructions, and available from inaccessible corrections. Score source-defined recipient interpretation and next action. Never infer consent, booking, venue, obligation or disclosure from silence, a previous tentative offer or an unrelated turn.

5. **First independent re-entry and delivered-cue provenance.** A/B/E/F evaluate the first learner-authored recipient-facing re-entry move against trusted **actually rendered** tutor/interlocutor chronology. An answer-bearing recap, filled ledger, highlighted stale fact or explicit model delivered beforehand makes the affected evidence assisted; selected-but-unrendered cues do not. Source-authored recap is legitimate only under a predeclared equivalent task protocol. Late receipt of render telemetry cannot promote coached answers to independence.

6. **Delayed genuinely unseen independent interruption transfer.** A–F require distinct real learning sessions, meaningful delay, new source-authored exchanges with changed interruption placement, status changes/referents/recipient decisions, first unaided responses and observable source-defined outcomes. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Replaying the same conversation, copying a tutor recap, assisted repairs or uncompleted follow-ups do not establish durable continuity mastery.

7. **Comparable teaching, accessibility and interest breadth.** C/E/F match or disclose state-ledger size, interruption length, ambiguity, source availability, baseline proficiency, feedback, dose, response burden, variety/register, modality/accessibility and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic exposure. Protect shared CEFR objectives, noncompensatory receptive/productive skills, due reviews and exit gates. Inaccurate captions, missing context, unavailable speech recognition and off-task memory-load differences are confounds, not learner deficits.

8. **Reversible outcome-based method selection and replay invariance.** E/F log feasible candidates, selected/assigned/**actually rendered** method, completed later independent re-entry outcomes, uncertainty/confounds, exploration and next-choice rationale. Stronger delayed outcomes may reverse immediate-score preferences without permanent style labels; sparse or conflicting evidence falls back to `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. Session-qualified replay deduplication, frozen source/ledger/rubric versions and trusted cue chronology must yield identical state, independence, review-debt and next-method verdicts across receipt permutations; unresolved conflicts fail closed.

9. **Assertion-level audit and bounded claims.** A–F emit exact source/task/ledger/rubric/evaluator IDs, interruption boundary and accessible context, current/stale/unknown expected state, first/final learner-authored messages, actually rendered cues, recipient interpretation/action, delayed/unseen denominators, confounds, replay permutations and `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED` per assertion. Documentation CI is not executable dialogue evaluation, a CEFR award, verified app memory or proof of improved human retention.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, deterministic seed/clock, real source-linked CEFR tasks requiring interruption/resumption, validated multilingual/variety-aware state and recipient rubrics, frozen source/task/ledger versions, accessible authorized source history, trusted actually rendered cue chronology, first unaided re-entry responses, source-defined recipient interpretations/actions, completed delayed genuinely unseen interruption transfer with denominators, comparable method/topic/modality/accessibility exposure, protected required skills/reviews, receipt-order permutations, reversible next-method traces and assertion-level verdicts. Missing real mapping, accessible prior context, trustworthy chronology, valid recipient-state adjudication or completed follow-up remains `UNSUPPORTED`/unknown.

Any claim that adaptation **improves durable independent conversation resumption** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, multilingual/variety and accessibility validation, human adjudication, privacy/subgroup-harm review and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor helps learners re-enter a conversation with the right shared facts, admit what is unknown, and avoid turning yesterday's possibility into today's false promise.**
