# F71 — Discourse cohesion and referent tracking must transfer to independent multi-turn communication

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not an implemented discourse policy, a reproduced production defect, a validated multilingual evaluator, CEFR certification or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F23 protects topic/method attribution; F33 guards unseen-item exposure; F38 attributes tutor-origin errors; F49 preserves accessibility construct equivalence; F50 protects topic breadth; F53 guards legitimate register and varieties; F54 tests clarification/repair; F58 tests learner initiative; F62 protects independent production; F66 varies interlocutors; F67 tests learner-generated examples; F70 tests contextual lexical selection. **F71 isolates cross-turn discourse cohesion:** accurate isolated sentences or fluent connector use do not establish that a learner can independently maintain an intelligible referent, temporal sequence and communicative through-line across turns or paragraphs in an unfamiliar task. Conversely, a source-valid elliptical, zero-anaphoric, discourse-marked or noncanonical organization must not be rejected merely for diverging from an English-centric template.

## Controlled setup

Resolve a **real, versioned** CEFR can-do, curriculum blueprint, prerequisites, task genre and communicative purpose, required oral/written interaction or production modality, source-valid discourse and cohesion construct, language-specific reference conventions, legitimate varieties and register, evaluator/rubric, assistance limits, independent evidence, meaningful delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer, due reviews and level exit gate. Freeze the construct before treatments. Do not invent a universal connector quota, pronoun frequency, explicit-subject requirement, paragraph count or English word-order rule. If the source objective only tests sentence-level performance, do not manufacture a discourse-level graduation gate.

Predeclare feasible reversible instructional candidates `REFERENT_MAP`, `MODELLED_RECOUNT`, `SEQUENCE_REORDER`, `CONTRASTIVE_COHESION`, `UNSCAFFOLDED_DIALOGUE` and `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. Each option must preserve the same objective and independent-evidence standard. A planning scaffold can support practice without establishing independent mastery. Retain exploration and skill balance rather than assigning a fixed narrative/visual/verbal learner type. Interest personalization changes context sampling only, with adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics preserved.

For comparable learner/objective histories record source/evaluator versions; discourse purpose and genre; participants/entities, referent introductions, subsequent references and ambiguity; temporal and causal relations where required; task, interlocutor and topic familiarity; model/template and story exposure; selected, assigned and **actually rendered** method; exact content and feedback hashes; first unaided multi-turn or multi-paragraph production; tutor-generated recap, rephrasing, answer-bearing prompts, hints, retries and repairs with trusted delivery chronology; independent delayed and genuinely unseen discourse transfer; modality/accessibility and opportunity/dose; and eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators. Use immutable session-qualified event IDs and trusted occurrence/delivery order, never upload order. Do not infer oral fluency or turn-taking from a transcript when audio interaction was unavailable.

## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Correct sentences, unclear referents.** A learner produces grammatically acceptable isolated sentences but in a new multi-person service exchange repeatedly uses an ambiguous reference, so the interlocutor cannot determine who receives an item. A second learner initially produces shorter, less polished sentences after referent-map practice yet later independently introduces and tracks entities accurately in an unfamiliar exchange. Compare source-equivalent task demand, entity load, topic, model exposure, assistance, practice dose and delayed unseen communication; neither isolated grammar nor polished surface form proves discourse transfer.
- **B — Connectors do not guarantee coherent meaning.** A learner inserts several fluent linking expressions while describing events in a contradictory order; another uses fewer overt markers but communicates a source-valid sequence with clear referents and purpose. Apply the frozen language- and genre-specific evaluator: distinguish surface connector counts from interpretable temporal/causal structure. Do not penalize source-valid ellipsis, zero subjects, discourse particles, implicit links or varieties solely because an English template expects explicit pronouns or connectives.
- **C — Tutor summaries can manufacture apparent coherence.** In a conversation the tutor supplies a full recap identifying each participant and event, after which the learner repeats a coherent account. Another learner independently notices a referent ambiguity and resolves it with a source-permitted clarification that does not provide the answer. Preserve first production, actual recap and hint delivery, learner-initiated repair and the task's interactional rubric. The first learner's post-recap account is assisted; the second's clarification is evaluated on the actual construct rather than automatically credited or rejected.
- **D — Useful discourse instruction changes with objective and evidence.** Early controlled delayed probes favor sequence-reordering for event narratives; referent mapping later helps with multi-party dialogues. Still later independent unfamiliar tasks reverse the first pattern. Emit objective-specific uncertain hypotheses and actually choose/render a different feasible method after contradictory evidence. Do not freeze a learner into a permanent “diagram learner,” “story learner” or modality type.
- **E — Favorite contexts and access routes can fake gains.** Modelled recounts occur only in familiar music stories while unsupervised dialogues occur in unfamiliar public-service tasks. A text-accessible route lacks audio turn-taking evidence, and an oral interaction objective is not evaluable. Match or disclose entity load, genre, topic familiarity, exposure, accessibility and opportunity before attributing method gains. Preserve required skills, due reviews and topic rotation; mark oral/interactive evidence `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE` rather than converting text success into spoken competence.
- **F — Offline replay, changing references and missing follow-up.** A referent map is assigned but never renders; a tutor recap is delivered before a revision but uploads later; two offline sessions reuse a local turn ID; a story template keeps an item ID but changes the entities; a delayed transfer is offered but never completed; and a revised evaluator newly permits a source-valid omitted subject. Rebuild the ledger using session-qualified IDs, rendered content hashes, trusted cue order and versioned source/evaluator semantics. Deduplicate replays, quarantine unresolved delivery chronology, and distinguish missing follow-up from failed learning. Reversing upload order must not alter independence, referent-error attribution or the next instructional choice.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Frozen source-defined discourse construct.** For A–F emit the actual versioned can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, task/genre, modality, reference and sequencing requirements, legitimate language/variety realizations, evaluator, assistance policy, delayed/unseen probes, due reviews and exit gate. Missing source mapping is `UNSUPPORTED`; a generic “use three connectors” or English-pronoun rule cannot replace the source rubric.
2. **Cross-turn intelligibility is assessed beyond sentence accuracy.** In A/B distinguish accurate isolated sentences and connector counts from coherent multi-turn meaning, entity introduction, resolvable references and source-required temporal/causal relations. Emit the concrete referent ambiguity or semantic contradiction, not an unexplained stylistic score.
3. **Language-specific cohesion variants remain valid.** In B/C/F accept source-valid ellipsis, zero anaphora, discourse particles, indirect links, registers and varieties when the intended relation remains recoverable. Reject genuinely ambiguous or meaning-changing reference under the frozen task rubric, not because of a single model wording. Rubric updates require explicit version-aware re-evaluation, not silent drift.
4. **First independent discourse is distinct from tutor-mediated repair.** In A/C/F preserve first unaided multi-turn production, actual tutor recaps, modelled entity links, answer-bearing cues, clarification content and revisions. A learner's repetition of the tutor's coherent summary is supported practice, not independent mastery; permitted non-answer-bearing clarification is assessed by the interactional task.
5. **Delayed unseen discourse transfer governs adaptation.** In A/D/F require meaningful intervening time/practice and source-equivalent independent discourse in genuinely unseen entity configurations, interlocutor exchanges or narrative contexts. Record eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; immediate retelling, recycled story templates and missing follow-up cannot prove durable benefit.
6. **Method comparisons control discourse burden and context.** In A/D/E match or disclose entity count, ambiguity burden, temporal complexity, genre, topic familiarity, instruction dose, prior model exposure, modality and accessibility. Protect listening/speaking/reading/writing, free production, due reviews and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topics. No phantom spoken interaction from text-only evidence.
7. **Rendered events and offline replay are invariant.** In C/F emit selected vs assigned vs actually rendered instruction, exact entity/story/model hashes, immutable session-qualified event IDs, trusted cue delivery order and source/evaluator versions. Replays and upload-order permutations yield identical first-independent evidence, contamination labels, discourse-error attribution and next-choice trace; unresolved order or rubric equivalence fails closed.
8. **Reversible, uncertain objective-specific instructional choices.** In A/D/E/F emit feasible methods, chosen and rendered method, independent delayed outcomes, uncertainty, contrary evidence, exploration and next choice. Sparse or confounded histories fall back to a balanced source-safe policy; do not create fixed learner types or let interest preferences change mastery requirements.
9. **Assertion-level audit without efficacy inflation.** For A–F emit source/rubric IDs, genre and referent demands, first/final discourse evidence, legitimate alternatives, rendered content and cue provenance, delayed/unseen outcomes and denominators, context/modality/review debt, replay equivalence, next-choice rationale and per-assertion `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED`. Documentation QA is not an executable tutor evaluation or proof of improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command; deterministic seeded multi-session histories with controlled clock; real source-resolved CEFR discourse tasks and language-aware evaluators; rendered turn/paragraph content hashes and referent annotations; first unaided meaning and coherence evaluation; trusted tutor-cue chronology; controlled or disclosed topic, genre and discourse load; independent delayed and genuinely unseen multi-turn transfer with follow-up denominators; protected skill, review and topic breadth; replay-order permutations; reversible policy changes; and assertion-level verdicts. Missing source mapping, referent resolution, event chronology, modality or follow-up remains `UNSUPPORTED`/unknown, not invented competence.

A claim that adaptive discourse instruction **improves durable independent communication** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, sufficiently delayed unseen independent tasks, multilingual evaluator validation, privacy/accessibility review, subgroup harm checks and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor helps learners sustain meaning across unfamiliar turns and texts, not just produce polished isolated sentences or copy the tutor's summary.**
