# F37 — Assigned teaching method is not proof of delivered or experienced instruction

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. This is not a reproduced defect, shipped adaptive tutor, causal estimate or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F20 protects unobserved alternatives, F24 addresses biased *assignment*, F32 addresses selective *follow-up*, and F35 tracks scaffold visibility. F37 isolates another failure mode: **the tutor selects instructional option A, but the learner actually receives B, sees only part of A, skips it, or receives no verifiable instruction at all**. A selection log cannot be promoted to exposure evidence. Equally, comparing only learners who completed an option creates post-assignment selection bias. Preserve both the original assignment and what was independently verified as delivered/experienced.

## Controlled setup

Resolve `goal-1` to an **existing versioned CEFR can-do** and actually supported skill route, rubric, prerequisite and independent mastery/exit gate. Compare eligible option A (`concise_explanation`) with B (`worked_example`) and a shared balanced baseline. If either option is unavailable, declare the actual supported substitute rather than invent a listening, speaking or audio experience. Hold objective, evaluator, task demand, probe difficulty, novelty, review opportunities and interest/context rotation constant. Use a deterministic clock, seed, versioned `instruction_delivery_policy`, a predeclared eligibility/assignment rule and an append-only synthetic event ledger across real learning sessions and days.

Record stable learner, objective/version, skill, session, run, event, assignment, instructional asset/version, render, interaction, probe and item-family IDs; candidate options, assignment probability if known, assignment reason and policy version; **assigned, attempted-to-render, confirmed-rendered, available, actually interacted-with and verified-experienced option** as separate fields; substitution reason and route; content visibility, foreground/interaction evidence where trustworthy, start/end/active-time provenance, skips, interruptions, partial exposure, hints/model answers, offline occurrence versus upload times, retries, first-attempt quality, later calibrated unaided probes, actual delay, intervening practice, unseen-context transfer, review debt, context family, modality availability and missingness. A render event alone does not prove attention or comprehension; unknown experience remains unknown. Do not collect raw conversation text or fabricate a universal minimum exposure duration.

Predeclare conservative classifications `ASSIGNED_ONLY`, `DELIVERED_CONFIRMED`, `PARTIAL_OR_INTERRUPTED`, `SUBSTITUTED`, `EXPERIENCE_UNKNOWN`, `NO_VERIFIED_EXPOSURE` and `NOT_COMPARABLE`. Keep an **assignment-based** descriptive cohort (all eligible assignments, including skips) separate from **verified-exposure** diagnostics. Neither completed-only nor exposure-only differences justify causal method ranking without accounting for post-assignment selection and comparable follow-up.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — Assignment without exposure.** A is assigned, but the lesson fails to render and the learner later completes an independent probe after unrelated practice. Keep the assignment in its denominator and preserve the valid probe as a learner observation; do **not** attribute the outcome to A or count A as delivered.
- **B — Substitution and availability.** A is assigned but a supported accessibility/availability fallback actually delivers B; a second case has an unavailable listening route and no feasible substitute. Record A as the assigned option and B as the delivered option in the first case, with the reason and construct preserved; mark the second as no verified exposure. Never manufacture listening/speaking achievement.
- **C — Partial exposure and answer-bearing cue.** A's explanation appears but is skipped after one line; B's worked example is fully shown and includes a model answer that is visible during an immediate task. A later clean, delayed unfamiliar probe is independently completed. Preserve the valid later probe and each exposure's visibility/dose; do not treat immediate model-assisted correctness as independent mastery or infer that full B was intrinsically superior to skipped A.
- **D — Completion-conditioned reversal.** A is assigned to many eligible occasions but is frequently interrupted; B has fewer assignments yet more completions among highly prepared learners. Completed-only delayed success favors B while matched assignment-based opportunities do not support that conclusion. Report assignment, delivery, partial, completion, follow-up and baseline-readiness denominators; no naive winner or invented counterfactual.
- **E — Verified alternative and adaptive reversal.** Early verified, comparable A exposures are followed by clean delayed independent transfer favoring A. Later balanced assignments with **confirmed actual B delivery**, matched opportunities and new delayed probes favor B. Allow a provisional, uncertainty-aware reversal while retaining nonzero exploration and original CEFR gates. An assigned-but-never-delivered B in a control history cannot drive this reversal.
- **F — Offline replay and ambiguous engagement.** An offline B delivery occurs before a later probe but uploads afterward; a duplicate delivery event replays; a conflicting assignment event arrives late; one render has no trustworthy visibility or active-time signal; another instruction is delivered only after the probe. Deduplicate stable IDs, order by trusted occurrence time and preserve the original assignment versus actual delivery. Quarantine ambiguous or contradictory provenance, never backdate the later instruction or infer experience from a render alone.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Full delivery funnel.** For A–F expose per policy/option/objective/skill eligible, assigned, attempted render, confirmed delivered, partially experienced, substituted, skipped, interrupted, experience unknown, completed and independently assessed counts, plus observed timing, assistance and uncertainty. Unknown is not zero exposure or verified exposure.
2. **No assignment-as-exposure laundering.** In A, an assignment without verified delivery cannot be credited as the instructional antecedent of later retrieval/transfer. Preserve the independent probe as an observation with uncertain or different teaching attribution, not a false failure.
3. **Substitution is explicit and construct-safe.** In B, distinguish original assignment from actual supported delivered method, version and reason. Unsupported modalities remain `UNAVAILABLE`; accessibility aids that preserve the tested construct are not automatically disqualifying, but answer-bearing help must be recorded.
4. **Partial/assisted work is not durable mastery.** In C, partial exposure and visible model answers remain attributable, with their dose/visibility limitations. Later genuinely delayed, independent unfamiliar probes may remain valid, but their causal attribution is not invented.
5. **Post-assignment selection is visible.** In D, never rank methods from completed-only success when completion, readiness or follow-up differ. Report assignment-based and verified-exposure diagnostics separately, with selection/missingness warnings and no made-up outcomes for skipped sessions.
6. **Outcome-based reversal requires actual comparable instruction.** In E, later matched, independently valid outcomes after verified B delivery can revise a tentative hypothesis; B assignments without delivery cannot. Continue alternative sampling, report uncertainty and preserve review/skill coverage.
7. **Idempotent event chronology.** In F, stable-ID replay and trusted occurrence-time ordering yield invariant assignment/delivery counts and probe lineage across upload orders. Contradictory assignment or unknown experience is `UNKNOWN` until resolved; instruction occurring after a probe cannot explain that probe.
8. **CEFR, interests and availability are independent.** Every history preserves the same versioned can-do, evaluator, prerequisites, delayed independent evidence gates, due reviews and supported receptive/productive obligations. Soft topic interests vary examples only; neutral, adjacent and unfamiliar contexts remain reachable, and no unavailable modality is silently credited.
9. **Conservative comparison and release gate.** If assignment-to-delivery fidelity, meaningful exposure provenance, matched independent follow-up, item novelty or delayed retrieval is insufficient, report `UNSUPPORTED`/`NOT_COMPARABLE`, retain narrower observations and use the balanced curriculum with normal support/reviews. Neither high delivery rate nor high immediate assisted correctness proves durable learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

For A–F publish the **exact tested engine HEAD**, executable command, deterministic seed/clock, policy/asset versions, full synthetic event ledger, real objective/skill mapping and rubric, assignment mechanism and candidate set, original assignment and verified actual option, substitutions and delivery funnel, support/visibility/time provenance, calibrated independent delayed and unseen probes, opportunity and missingness denominators, review/skill/context coverage, replay invariance, uncertainty/reason codes and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED for every assertion. Include a fixed balanced comparator with equivalent feasible instruction and assessment opportunities. Distinguish assignment-based descriptive results from verified-exposure associations; do not claim causal efficacy from either without defensible identification.

A deterministic fixture PASS proves only a software acceptance invariant. A claim that adaptation improves durable independent learning requires separately reviewed longitudinal learner evidence, comparable balanced baseline, verified delayed retrieval and unfamiliar transfer, uncertainty calibration and unchanged CEFR coverage.
