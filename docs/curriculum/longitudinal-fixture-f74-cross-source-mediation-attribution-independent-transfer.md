# F74 — Cross-source mediation, attribution and independent information transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a shipped mediation evaluator, factuality checker, translation certifier, reproduced production defect, CEFR certification or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 protects genuinely unseen transfer; F38 protects tutor-origin attribution; F47/F48 preserve level-qualified, noncompensatory CEFR evidence; F49 protects accessibility construct equivalence; F50 protects topic breadth; F54 tests clarification after communication breakdown; F62 distinguishes recognition from independent production; F70 tests contextual lexical selection; F71 tests discourse cohesion; F72 tests goal/constraint management; F73 tests implicit intent. **F74 isolates source-faithful mediation of information for another audience:** fluent retelling, plausible invented detail, or a polished tutor-written summary is not independent evidence that the learner can select, connect, qualify and communicate what the actual source material supports. This construct applies **only** to a real, versioned CEFR-linked task whose source rubric actually requires relaying, summarizing, explaining or mediating information. Do not impose an advanced multi-source synthesis gate on a simpler source-defined can-do, assume every CEFR level has the same mediation burden, or treat factual-world knowledge as a substitute for language-task evidence.

## Controlled setup

Resolve a **real, versioned** CEFR can-do and mediation descriptor where applicable, curriculum blueprint, prerequisite graph, source materials and permissions, intended recipient and communicative purpose, source language(s), target language/variety, source-defined mediation operation, information-selection burden, task complexity, relevant reading/listening/writing/speaking modalities actually available, rubric/evaluator, allowable paraphrase and simplification, permitted clarification and aids, independent evidence, meaningfully delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer, due reviews and level exit gate. Freeze the source packet and audience needs before instruction comparisons. Distinguish direct quotation, faithful paraphrase, reasonable inference, unsupported addition, uncertainty, contradiction and omitted source-critical information; require no universal number of facts, sources, citations, turns or words. If the source or rubric cannot adjudicate an inference, report **UNSUPPORTED**, not a fabricated factuality verdict.

Predeclare reversible instructional candidates SOURCE_HIGHLIGHT_AND_MAP, AUDIENCE_FIRST_REFORMULATION, CONTRASTIVE_SOURCE_CHECK, GUIDED_SYNTHESIS_THEN_FADE, OPEN_UNSEEN_MEDIATION and BALANCED_DEFAULT. Candidates may vary explanation, modality mix **only when actually supported**, scaffold fading, practice ordering, pace, examples and review spacing while keeping the same source-defined task, language objective, mediation burden and independent mastery gate. A tutor-supplied summary, highlighted answer span, translation, source ranking, prefilled comparison or recipient-ready message is **assistance** for the affected target. Source texts presented as part of a source-defined open-material mediation task are not automatically coaching; the frozen rubric must say what consultation is permitted. Never equate a reading comprehension task with an unaided oral mediation claim.

Use deterministic pseudonymous multi-session histories with injected seed/clock and immutable session-qualified event IDs. ## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Fluent relay invents a missing condition.** In a source-defined mediation task, a learner relays two short public-service notices to a recipient. One notice gives an opening time; neither gives a booking deadline. Learner A fluently adds a plausible deadline and omits a source-required access condition. Learner B uses simpler but source-faithful language, preserves the access condition and states that the deadline is not provided. Score target-language intelligibility, information selection, provenance, uncertainty and task-relevant omissions separately. Do not award independent source fidelity for eloquence or penalize a legitimate simplification outside the rubric's essential information.
- **B — Conflicting sources require qualified synthesis, not forced agreement.** Two versioned notices disagree about a venue or time; a later update has explicit provenance but its precedence is not always established. One learner silently chooses the convenient claim and states it as certain; another identifies the discrepancy, attributes the alternatives and communicates a source-valid next step or clarification. When the frozen packet establishes which update supersedes the earlier notice, credit correct precedence; when it does not, credit appropriate uncertainty rather than inventing an authoritative answer. Do not infer truth solely from recency, document formatting or tutor preference.
- **C — Tutor-made summary masquerades as learner mediation.** A learner initially overlooks a required condition, then sees a tutor-generated comparison table, translated answer-bearing passage and recipient-ready message. Their polished revision copies those facts accurately. A distinct-session unseen source packet later tests whether the learner can independently select, qualify and relay new information under the same source-defined burden. Preserve first attempt, actual cue delivery, final assisted output, permitted original-source consultation and later independent performance separately. Copying the tutor's synthesis is useful practice, not independent mediation mastery.
- **D — Method advantage changes with audience and delay.** For a source-equivalent objective, learner A initially performs better on later independent relay after SOURCE_HIGHLIGHT_AND_MAP; learner B initially benefits more from AUDIENCE_FIRST_REFORMULATION. A subsequent unfamiliar audience and new source packet reverse A's apparent advantage. Match or disclose source length/density, contradiction count, language, topic, audience knowledge, output channel, exposure, practice dose, support, opportunity and evaluator versions. Emit uncertainty, counterevidence, exploration and a changed **actually rendered** next feasible instruction; never permanently classify learners as 'visual summarizers' or 'audience-first types'.
- **E — Interests and modality cannot silently alter the mediation gate.** A favourite gaming-news packet is repeatedly paired with guided mapping, while unfamiliar civic notices are paired with open reformulation; another learner has text access but unavailable audio comprehension, and a third uses source-permitted accessible captions. Disentangle familiarity, source difficulty, prior-world knowledge, language proficiency, accessibility, instructional choice and task construct. Keep adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar packets in rotation, protect due reviews and receptive/productive obligations, and mark an unmeasured oral/listening requirement MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE. An approved access accommodation that does not reveal the answer is not automatically tutoring.
- **F — Stale source, late model and offline replay.** A source notice changes after task assignment; the displayed source version differs from a cached packet ID; a tutor's translated summary is delivered before a learner revision but syncs later; two offline sessions reuse a local turn ID; a delayed unseen probe is offered but not completed; and a rubric revision changes which omission is material. Rebuild from source/content hashes, actual rendered packet, trusted cue delivery, immutable session-qualified IDs and frozen rubric versions. Deduplicate events, quarantine unresolved source/rubric mismatch and ambiguous chronology, preserve missing follow-up as unknown, and require identical evidence and next-choice traces across receipt-order permutations.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-resolved mediation construct.** For A–F emit the real versioned CEFR-linked can-do/descriptor where applicable, blueprint, prerequisite, task, source packet and audience, source-defined mediation demand, skill/modality, rubric, permitted consultation, independent standard, delayed/unseen probes, reviews and exit gate. Missing mappings or unvalidated multilingual equivalence yield UNSUPPORTED, not an invented universal mediation threshold.
2. **Fidelity is distinct from fluent wording.** In A/B identify each material source-supported claim, contradiction, unsupported addition, omission, attribution and uncertainty with the relevant source spans/versions. Fluent or grammatically polished output cannot compensate for source-required information being distorted; valid source-faithful paraphrase need not reproduce one model sentence.
3. **Conflicts and uncertainty remain source-grounded.** In B/F preserve competing claims, timestamps, source provenance, explicit supersession if actually provided and the audience's information needs. Credit justified qualification, clarification or precedence; reject unsupported certainty and arbitrary 'latest always wins' rules. Unresolvable source truth remains unknown.
4. **Original-source consultation is not tutor synthesis.** In C/F distinguish source-defined open-material consultation from tutor-supplied highlights, translations, selected facts, comparison tables and ready-made messages. Preserve first unaided selection/output, answer-bearing cue delivery and final revisions; an assisted synthesis cannot become independent evidence through later upload or rewording.
5. **Delayed unseen independent mediation is decisive.** In A/C/D/F require meaningfully separate sessions and genuinely new source packets, recipient needs or information configurations with source-equivalent burden, plus independently completed delayed retrieval/transfer. Emit eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators. Same-packet repetition, immediate coached accuracy and absent probes cannot establish durable independent mediation.
6. **Comparison protects source, audience and modality equivalence.** In D/E match or disclose packet density, contradictions, task difficulty, audience familiarity, language direction, modality, accessibility, topic knowledge, practice dose, prior exposure and opportunity. Preserve required CEFR skills, review debt and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topics; no text-only proxy may certify unavailable speaking/listening.
7. **Reversible teaching from outcomes, not learner labels.** In C/D/E emit feasible candidates, selected/assigned/actually rendered instruction, first attempts, later independent outcomes, uncertainty, contrary evidence, alternative sampling and next choice. Confounded or sparse data trigger BALANCED_DEFAULT; interest weighting changes example probabilities only, never required objectives, source fidelity or mastery thresholds.
8. **Source/cue chronology and replay invariance.** In F verify immutable session-qualified IDs, packet/source/content/rubric versions, displayed source hashes, trusted tutor-model delivery, deduplicated offline events and explicit migration policy. Arrival-order permutations must preserve source attribution, independent/assisted labels, omissions, follow-up missingness, review debt and next-choice rationale; unresolved source or evaluator mismatch fails closed.
9. **Auditable assertions without efficacy inflation.** For A–F emit source/rubric IDs, audience/task requirements, claim-to-span attribution, first/final independent/assisted outputs, actual cue and source exposure, delayed/unseen denominators, confounds, skill/topic/review debt, replay equivalence, method uncertainty and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion. Documentation CI is not a shipped mediation evaluator, a human-learning study or permission to release levels.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command; deterministic multi-session seed/clock; real source-resolved CEFR mediation tasks and language-aware validated rubrics; versioned multi-source packets with attributable and conflicting claims; recipient/audience needs; learner-authored first output and allowed consultation; exact tutor cue/source render chronology; genuinely delayed unseen independent mediation with follow-up denominators; comparable instructional exposure or explicit confounds; accessible modality boundaries; topic/skill/review protection; replay-order permutations; reversible policy decisions and assertion-level verdicts. Missing source validation, unsupported translation equivalence, cue chronology, source precedence, modality or follow-up remains UNSUPPORTED/unknown rather than fabricated competence.

A claim that personalized mediation instruction **improves durable independent communication** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, multilingual evaluator validation and human adjudication of source fidelity/uncertainty, meaningful delayed unseen tasks, privacy/accessibility and subgroup-harm review, and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor teaches learners to carry another speaker's meaning accurately and independently—not to repeat the tutor's polished but possibly unsupported synthesis.**
