# F70 — Contextual lexical selection and collocation must transfer to independent communication

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not an implemented lexical policy, reproduced production defect, validated lexical evaluator, CEFR certification or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F23 protects topic/method attribution; F33 guards unseen item exposure; F38 attributes tutor-origin errors; F50 preserves soft-interest breadth; F53 guards legitimate varieties and register; F59 calibrates challenge; F61 tests interleaving; F62 protects independent production; F63 distinguishes first-language bridges; F67 tests learner-generated examples; F69 prioritizes feedback. **F70 isolates contextual lexical choice:** recognizing or recalling a word in isolation does not establish that the learner can independently select a meaning-appropriate word, combination or paraphrase in a new communicative context. Conversely, a source-valid synonym, dialectal variant or effective paraphrase must not fail solely because it differs from a memorized phrase.

## Controlled setup

Resolve a **real, versioned** CEFR can-do, curriculum blueprint, prerequisites, communicative task/function, skill/modality, lexical meaning and source-required phraseology, semantic and pragmatic evaluator/rubric, allowed paraphrase and varieties, assistance limits, independent evidence, meaningful delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen contextual transfer, due reviews and level exit gate. Freeze those demands before treatments. No universal collocation-frequency threshold, native-speaker-only phrase list, single canonical answer, fixed vocabulary quota or requirement to use one synonym may replace the source rubric. A collocation matters to mastery only insofar as the source-defined construct actually requires natural or meaning-appropriate selection.

Predeclare feasible, reversible instructional candidates `ISOLATED_RECALL`, `CONTEXTUAL_CONTRAST`, `COLLOCATION_IN_CONTEXT`, `PARAPHRASE_AND_REPAIR`, `SPACED_CONTEXT_ROTATION` and `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. A method can help prepare a learner without itself constituting independent mastery. Keep enough opportunities for required comprehension and free production, and retain alternative methods rather than locking a learner into a supposed vocabulary-learning style. Interest-based contexts remain a separate soft sampling layer.

For comparable learner/objective histories record source and evaluator versions; actual target sense, communicative intent and source-valid alternatives; task and topic familiarity; item, phrase, collocation, semantic frame and generator/template exposure; selected, assigned and **actually delivered** instructional method; exact rendered prompt/model/feedback content hashes; first unaided learner-produced meaning and word/phrase selection; source-valid paraphrases, errors and their provenance; hints, translation, answer-bearing cues, retries and revisions with trusted delivery chronology; practice opportunity/dose and modality/accessibility; independent delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen-context transfer; and eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators. Use immutable session-qualified event IDs, content hashes and trusted occurrence/delivery order, not upload order. If speech, listening or latency is not reliably observed, mark it unknown; a transcript alone is not assessed oral proficiency.

Observation labels: `ISOLATED_RECALL_ONLY`, `CONTEXTUAL_SENSE_VALID`, `CONTEXTUAL_SENSE_MISMATCH`, `SOURCE_REQUIRED_COLLOCATION`, `SOURCE_VALID_VARIANT`, `SOURCE_VALID_PARAPHRASE`, `TUTOR_MODEL_CONTAMINATION`, `INDEPENDENT_FIRST_ATTEMPT`, `ANSWER_BEARING_CUE_DELIVERED`, `ASSISTED_REVISION`, `INDEPENDENT_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL`, `INDEPENDENT_UNSEEN_CONTEXT_TRANSFER`, `LEXICAL_TEMPLATE_REUSED`, `TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED`, `ACCESSIBILITY_CONFOUNDED`, `FOLLOWUP_MISSING`, `EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED`, `RUBRIC_MISMATCH`, `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. These are evidence labels, never permanent learner attributes.

## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Isolated recall is not contextual choice.** A learner correctly recognizes and translates a target word on repeated flashcards, then chooses its wrong sense in an unfamiliar service request and cannot complete the intended meaning independently. Another learner has slower immediate recall after contextual-contrast practice but later independently selects a source-valid expression for the same communicative function. Compare source-equivalent meaning, item exposure, practice dose, assistance and delayed unseen performance; neither flashcard speed nor immediate contextual success alone establishes method superiority.
- **B — Phraseology can matter without enforcing one memorized answer.** For a source-defined task where a conventional word combination affects naturalness or meaning, a learner recalls each component but produces an unsuitable combination in free writing. A different learner uses an acceptable source-valid collocation or paraphrase that the tutor's example list omitted. Apply the frozen evaluator and permitted varieties: do not award source-required phraseological competence from isolated word recognition, but do not reject semantically and pragmatically valid alternatives simply because they differ from the template. If the source construct does not require a particular collocation, do not manufacture that gate.
- **C — A lexical repair after a model is not independent retrieval.** A tutor presents a near-complete target phrase; the learner repeats it flawlessly. Another learner independently notices a sense mismatch, asks for a source-permitted non-answer-bearing clarification and reformulates the request without receiving target wording. Retain the first attempt, actual cue delivery, who initiated the repair and exact semantic effect. The first revision remains assisted; the second is evaluated under the actual interactional rubric rather than universally rewarded or penalized.
- **D — The useful lexical method reverses across objectives and time.** Early comparable delayed probes favor contextual contrast for an ambiguous verb, while phrase-in-context rehearsal better supports a source-required routine request. Later independent unseen probes contradict the first advantage. Emit objective-specific uncertain hypotheses, explore feasible alternatives and actually change the next chosen and rendered method; never label the learner a permanent “contextual vocabulary learner.”
- **E — Interests, context difficulty and modality can fake a lexical gain.** Collocation rehearsal appears only in favorite gaming dialogues, while isolated recall is tested in unfamiliar public-service situations; a learner using an accessible text input route has different observed response timing; an oral lexical objective cannot be assessed because audio evaluation is unavailable. Match or disclose context familiarity, task demand, exposure, opportunity, modality and accessibility before attributing retention to method. Rotate favorite, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts, protect due reviews and all required skills, and mark oral evidence `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE` instead of substituting text success.
- **F — Offline replay, answer exposure and changing lexical rubrics.** A contrast card is assigned but never renders; an answer-bearing example is delivered before a revision but uploads later; two sessions reuse a local attempt ID; a phrase generator reuses an item ID with changed wording; a delayed transfer probe is offered but not completed; and an evaluator update newly permits a previously rejected paraphrase. Reconstruct immutable session-qualified rendered events and versioned source/evaluator semantics, deduplicate replays, keep missing follow-up separate from failure, and quarantine unresolved delivery order or rubric drift. Reversing upload order must not change independence, lexical-choice labels or the next method decision.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Frozen source-valid lexical construct.** For A–F emit the actual versioned can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, task, modality, target sense, source-required phraseology, accepted paraphrases/varieties, semantic/pragmatic rubric, assistance, delayed/unseen probes, due reviews and exit gate. Missing source mapping is `UNSUPPORTED`; neither an arbitrary word count nor template match can substitute for source-defined mastery.
2. **Sense and collocation are evaluated in context.** In A/B distinguish isolated form recognition from correct meaning selection, required combination and communicative success. Emit the context and meaning conditions that make a choice acceptable or unacceptable; a memorized translation alone earns no unseen productive credit.
3. **Valid alternatives are not false negatives.** In B/C/F recognize source-valid paraphrases, legitimate varieties and task-appropriate lexical choices even when they differ from the tutor's model. Reject a genuinely meaning-changing or source-required phraseological error under the frozen rubric, not on personal taste or a native-speaker stereotype. Rubric changes require version-aware re-evaluation, never silent score drift.
4. **First unaided evidence is separated from assisted repair.** In A/C/F preserve the first semantic attempt, source-permitted clarification, translation/model/hint content, actual delivery order, answer leakage and revisions. A repeated model answer is supported practice, not independent mastery; a legitimate non-answer-bearing clarification is judged by the source task.
5. **Durable independent contextual transfer governs selection.** In A/D/F require meaningful intervening time/practice, source-equivalent independent delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen semantic contexts or phrase combinations, with eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Immediate word recall, rehearsed phrase reproduction and missing follow-up cannot prove lasting benefit.
6. **Methods are compared without context or access confounds.** In A/D/E match or disclose target sense, lexical burden, exposure, difficulty, topic familiarity, instruction dose, modality, accessibility and independent opportunities. Protect required listening/speaking/reading/writing, free production, due reviews and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar contexts; no phantom oral evidence from text.
7. **Rendered content and replay invariance.** In C/F emit selected versus assigned versus rendered instruction, versioned item/template IDs, actual content hashes, trusted cue delivery chronology and immutable session-qualified IDs. Offline duplicates and receipt-order permutations must produce the same independent ledger, lexical-error attribution and next-choice trace; unknown delivery or rubric equivalence fails closed.
8. **Uncertain, reversible learner-specific adaptation.** In A/D/E/F emit feasible options, chosen and actually rendered method, objective-specific delayed outcomes, uncertainty, contradictory evidence, exploration and the next reversible choice. Sparse or confounded evidence selects a balanced source-safe fallback, not a permanent vocabulary learner type; interest weights affect contexts only.
9. **Assertion-level audit without efficacy inflation.** For A–F emit source/rubric IDs, sense/phraseology requirements, valid alternatives, first/final meaning, exposure and support provenance, delayed/unseen results and denominators, confounds, modality/topic/review debt, replay equivalence, next-choice rationale and per-assertion `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED`. Passing documentation QA does not establish an executable lexical tutor or better human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command; deterministic seeded multi-session histories with controlled clock; real source-resolved CEFR tasks, senses, permitted phraseology/varieties and evaluators; rendered lexical content and delivery hashes; first unaided semantic scoring; actual hint/model chronology; controlled or disclosed practice exposure and context familiarity; independent delayed and genuinely unseen contextual transfer with follow-up denominators; protected required skills, reviews and topic breadth; offline replay-order permutations; reversible policy decisions; and assertion-level verdicts. Unavailable source mappings, semantic evaluation, event chronology, modality or follow-up remain `UNSUPPORTED`/unknown, never invented evidence.

A claim that contextual lexical instruction **improves durable independent communication** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, sufficiently delayed and unseen independent tasks, privacy and accessibility review, subgroup harm checks and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor teaches learners to choose words that convey the intended meaning in new situations, not merely to recognize words or reproduce its own examples.**
