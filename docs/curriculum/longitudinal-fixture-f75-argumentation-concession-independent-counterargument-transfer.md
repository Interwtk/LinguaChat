# F75 — Reasoned stance, concession and independent counterargument transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a shipped argument evaluator, debate coach, factual-truth arbiter, CEFR certification, reproduced production defect or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 guards genuinely unseen transfer; F38 tracks tutor-origin content; F47/F48 preserve source-defined noncompensatory CEFR evidence; F50 protects topic breadth; F53 protects register and legitimate language variation; F54 tests clarification after breakdown; F58 protects learner initiative; F62 distinguishes recognition from independent production; F68 tests calibrated uncertainty; F69 prioritizes error feedback; F71 protects discourse cohesion; F72 tests communicative goals and constraints; F73 tests implicit intent; F74 protects cross-source mediation. **F75 isolates independent argumentation under a relevant source-defined communicative task:** stating a preference, reproducing the tutor's persuasive paragraph, or sounding confident does not establish that a learner can articulate a position, connect reasons to it, engage with a relevant objection, make a warranted concession and respond independently. This construct is **only** a gate when the real, versioned CEFR-linked task and rubric require some such discourse move. Do not impose formal debate, evidence citations, advanced rhetoric or a universal number of reasons on elementary or unrelated can-dos. Judge *language-task fulfillment*, not agreement with the tutor's opinions or a universal philosophy of argument.

## Controlled setup

Resolve a **real, versioned** CEFR can-do/descriptor, curriculum blueprint, prerequisites, communicative purpose, task and source materials, interlocutor/audience relationship, target language and permitted varieties, actual interaction channel and supported modalities, argumentation burden specified by the source rubric, acceptable positions and legitimate alternative structures, evidence/knowledge assumptions, evaluator version, permitted questions and aids, independent standard, meaningful delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer, due reviews and level exit gate. Freeze these before method comparisons. A task may require a simple justified choice, an exchange of reasons, or a nuanced response to an objection; do not silently escalate its difficulty. Source-valid opinions may differ, and a learner must not be graded on whether they endorse a favored policy, identity or political stance. If the rubric cannot distinguish language quality from factual correctness or domain expertise, mark that judgment **UNSUPPORTED**.

Predeclare reversible instructional candidates `CLAIM_REASON_LINKING`, `OBJECTION_RESPONSE_DIALOGUE`, `CONCESSION_AND_QUALIFICATION`, `CONTRASTIVE_ARGUMENT_MODELS`, `OPEN_UNSEEN_STANCE_EXCHANGE` and `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. Vary explanation detail, supported listening/reading/writing/speaking mix, role-play, activity ordering, scaffold fading, pace and review spacing while holding fixed the source-defined objective, argument burden, permissible viewpoints and independent evidence threshold. Tutor-written theses, reasons, concessions, objections, model rebuttals, translations and response outlines are **assistance** for the affected target. An ordinary interlocutor's source-permitted objection or clarification is part of the assessment interaction, not automatically tutoring; distinguish it from an answer-bearing coaching prompt. Preserve exploration of feasible alternatives rather than assigning a permanent “debater,” “visual,” or “logical” learner type.

Use deterministic pseudonymous multi-session histories with injected clock/seed and immutable session-qualified event IDs. Record can-do, source/rubric/evaluator versions; task/topic and familiarity; source-provided evidence and its provenance; audience/role and permissible viewpoints; source-defined discourse moves; actual learner-authored initial position, reasons, objections understood, concessions, replies and final communicative outcome; semantic relevance, clarity, coherence and respectful register; *separate* factual/source fidelity where required; selected/assigned/**actually rendered** instruction; exact tutor/interlocutor utterance and hint/model exposure chronology; retries, revisions and support dependence; comparable practice dose, accessibility and actual modality; independent delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen topic/interlocutor/objection transfer; and eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators. Do not invent reasoning skill from fluent connectors, force one canonical rhetorical structure, treat disagreement as an error, or infer spoken competence from text.

Observation labels: `STANCE_SOURCE_VALID`, `REASON_RELEVANT`, `REASON_UNCONNECTED`, `OBJECTION_ADDRESSED`, `OBJECTION_IGNORED`, `WARRANTED_CONCESSION`, `UNWARRANTED_CAPITULATION`, `QUALIFIED_REPLY`, `SOURCE_FACT_DISTORTED`, `VIEWPOINT_BIAS_RISK`, `FIRST_INDEPENDENT_ARGUMENT`, `TUTOR_ARGUMENT_EXPOSED`, `ASSISTED_REBUTTAL`, `INDEPENDENT_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL`, `INDEPENDENT_UNSEEN_ARGUMENT_TRANSFER`, `TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED`, `ROLE_METHOD_CONFOUNDED`, `ACCESSIBILITY_CONFOUNDED`, `FOLLOWUP_MISSING`, `EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED`, `RUBRIC_VERSION_MISMATCH`, `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. ## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Fluent connectors hide an unsupported argument.** In a versioned source-defined discussion task, a learner speaks fluently with “firstly,” “however” and “therefore,” but the stated reason does not support their conclusion and the reply never engages the interlocutor's relevant objection. A second learner uses simpler, source-valid language to give a clear reason and respond directly. Score target-language clarity, argument relevance and the source-required discourse moves separately. Do not penalize noncanonical yet coherent ordering or reward connector count as a substitute for independent task completion.
- **B — Concession is not surrender or compulsory agreement.** An interlocutor raises a plausible concern about a learner's proposal. One learner parrots “you are right” and abandons their position after a tutor-provided model; another acknowledges the limited concern, adjusts one condition and defends the remaining proposal with a relevant reason. A third politely maintains a well-supported disagreement without a formal concession. The frozen source rubric determines which moves are necessary; a source-valid refusal to concede is not inherently incorrect, and a tutor-approved opinion is not the answer key. Track whether any content was coached before it appeared.
- **C — Polished rebuttal after tutoring is assisted.** A learner initially gives a reason but cannot answer an objection. The tutor supplies an outline, a translated counterargument and a ready-to-send reply; the learner produces a polished revision. A separate, delayed session presents a genuinely new but source-equivalent objection and asks for an independently composed response. Keep initial attempt, actual answer-bearing cue delivery, coached revision and delayed independent performance separate; do not award mastery from reproducing the tutor's rebuttal.
- **D — Instructional advantage reverses with delayed transfer.** Early matched delayed probes suggest `CLAIM_REASON_LINKING` works better for learner A and `OBJECTION_RESPONSE_DIALOGUE` for learner B. Later unseen exchanges with a different interlocutor and an equally demanding objection reverse A's apparent advantage. Compare or disclose topic familiarity, interlocutor cooperativeness, task burden, evidence packet, modality, practice dose, support and evaluator versions. Decrease confidence, sample alternatives and log an **actually rendered** changed next instruction, not just a newly assigned plan.
- **E — Favorite-topic debates and inaccessible modes create false gains.** Argument models are practiced only with a favorite entertainment topic, while open replies occur only in unfamiliar civic contexts; a learner's text route is accessible but oral interaction is not implemented; another learner uses a source-permitted access accommodation that does not supply reasons. Separate topic familiarity, background knowledge, source difficulty, instruction and accessibility. Rotate favorite, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics; protect due reviews and required receptive/productive skill coverage. Mark unsupported oral assessment `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE` rather than converting written success into speaking mastery.
- **F — Offline chronology, changed objections and evaluator drift.** An objection is rewritten under the same local item ID; a tutor's rebuttal model is displayed before the learner revision but uploaded after it; two sessions reuse a local turn ID; a delayed probe is offered but not completed; and a rubric revision changes whether a concession is mandatory. Rebuild from immutable session-qualified IDs, content hashes, actually rendered objection/model chronology and frozen rubric versions. Deduplicate replay, preserve missing follow-up as unknown, quarantine irreconcilable rubric/chronology differences and demand identical independent/assisted labels and next-choice traces across receipt-order permutations.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-resolved argument construct.** For A–F emit the actual versioned CEFR-linked can-do, blueprint, task, source materials, role/audience, argument moves actually required, permitted positions/varieties, rubric, assistance rules, independent standard, delayed/unseen probes, reviews and exit gate. Missing source mapping or an unvalidated argument rubric yields `UNSUPPORTED`; no universal debate or concession threshold is invented.
2. **Reasoning moves are not connector counts.** In A/B report the learner's first independent position, relevant reason, objection interpretation, concession/qualification and response as separate rubric-aligned observations. Surface fluency or persuasive tone cannot compensate for a source-required missing move; valid alternate ordering and simpler phrasing remain eligible.
3. **Viewpoint neutrality and source truth are separate.** In A/B/E distinguish language-task quality, argument relevance, domain factual claims and any source-required factual fidelity. Accept different defensible positions and justified disagreement; do not reward agreement with the tutor, require political alignment, penalize identity/variety, or hallucinate external truth. If factual/source adjudication is not validated, that component is `UNSUPPORTED`.
4. **Concession and interaction are contextual.** In B/D preserve the frozen task's relationship, objection, interaction channel, expected politeness/register and allowable clarification. Credit a source-valid limited concession, reasoned reply or maintained disagreement as appropriate; do not force a concession when the source task does not require one or treat an ordinary interlocutor objection as a tutor hint.
5. **First independent versus answer-bearing coaching.** In B/C/F preserve first learner-authored argument, exact rendered tutor models, translations, outlines and rebuttal suggestions, revisions and assistance provenance. A polished reply after an answer-bearing model is assisted; ordinary source-permitted clarification is assessed under the interaction rubric, and accommodations are not automatically coaching.
6. **Delayed unseen argument transfer is decisive.** In C/D/F require meaningful intervening time/practice, distinct sessions and new source-equivalent objections, interlocutors or contexts, with eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Same-template reproduction, immediate coached fluency and uncompleted follow-up do not prove durable independent argumentation.
7. **Comparable methods preserve breadth and skill obligations.** In D/E match or disclose objection difficulty, source knowledge, topic familiarity, audience, modality/accessibility, practice dose, cue exposure and independent opportunities. Preserve source-defined CEFR objectives, noncompensatory evidence, due reviews and favorite/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar rotation; never substitute text-only evidence for unmeasured oral skills.
8. **Uncertain reversible policy and replay invariance.** In C/D/E/F emit feasible methods, selected/assigned/actually rendered choices, delayed outcomes, uncertainty, counterevidence, exploration and next choice. Sparse/confounded evidence falls back to `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. In F verify immutable session-qualified IDs, objection/model hashes, trusted delivery chronology, deduplication, rubric version and identical evidence/choice traces under replay-order permutations; unresolved versions or exposure fail closed.
9. **Assertion-level audit without efficacy inflation.** For A–F emit source/rubric IDs, task burden and permitted viewpoints, first/final independent/assisted argument moves, actual objection/model exposure, delayed/unseen results and denominators, confounds, skill/topic/review debt, replay equivalence, next-choice rationale and `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED` per assertion. Documentation CI is not a shipped evaluator or proof that instruction improves learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command; deterministic multi-session seed/clock; real versioned CEFR-linked argument tasks and language-aware, viewpoint-neutral validated rubrics; source-defined reasons, objections and permissible responses; actual learner-authored first turns; trusted tutor/interlocutor cue provenance; genuinely delayed unseen independent transfer with follow-up denominators; comparable instruction/context/access exposure; protected CEFR skill and review obligations; replay-order permutations; reversible next-instruction traces; and assertion-level verdicts. Missing source mapping, evaluator validation, actual audio, exposure chronology, rubric equivalence or follow-up remains `UNSUPPORTED`/unknown, not invented competence.

A claim that argument instruction **improves durable independent communication** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, independent delayed and unseen interaction, multilingual and viewpoint-bias evaluator validation, human adjudication, privacy/accessibility and subgroup-harm review, and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor helps learners form, qualify and defend their own meaning in new exchanges—not repeat the tutor's favored position.**
