## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Identifying clause selects one recipient.** In a fictional classroom there are two assistants, one wearing blue and one wearing green. The instruction is **“Give the folder to the assistant who is wearing blue.”** The reviewed source-world identifies the blue-wearing assistant; the first-unaided recipient-facing action or paraphrase selects that assistant. Giving it to either assistant or treating the clause as optional fails. Counterfactual: the learner sees no clothing colors and cannot ask a knowledgeable person; **“Which assistant is wearing blue?”** is correct targeted clarification, not a guessed selection. A visible pointing cue that reveals the answer before response is assistance.

**B — Identifying versus supplementary information.** In a fictional club, four volunteers exist; only two registered on Monday. **“The volunteers who registered on Monday will receive a badge”** selects the two Monday registrants, not all four. In a separately reviewed written notice, **“The volunteers, who registered on Monday, will receive a badge”** can present registration as supplementary information about the already identified volunteer group; the intended group and punctuation are independently fixed. Do not infer that all four registered from punctuation alone when source-world facts conflict; flag the inconsistent notice for clarification. In audio without reliable prosody or context, do not silently score the distinction as uniquely recoverable. Accept a natural paraphrase instead of requiring the learner to name restrictive/nonrestrictive terminology.

**C — Attachment ambiguity must remain open.** A fictional note says **“I met the assistant of the teacher who won the award.”** The relative clause can plausibly attach to **teacher** or **assistant** absent further context. The learner asks **“Who won the award—the teacher or the assistant?”** rather than inventing the winner. A reviewed follow-up **“The teacher won it”** permits the learner to identify the teacher as award winner while preserving that the assistant was the person met. A confident guess before clarification fails; a corrected answer after the follow-up is a repaired, not first-unaided, response to the original incomplete information.

**D — Multi-object location and scope.** A fictional equipment room contains two boxes, one beside a lamp and one beside a chair; only the lamp-side box contains paper. **“Bring the box that is beside the lamp”** selects the lamp-side box. In a different scene, **“Bring the box beside the lamp on the shelf”** can leave unclear whether the box or lamp is on the shelf; if both interpretations remain plausible, a targeted **“Is the box on the shelf, or is the lamp?”** is acceptable. Do not score a particular attachment as certain based only on the tutor's preferred parse. The recipient's action must follow the reviewed source, not merely a grammatical label.

**E — Actual delivery, chronology, accessibility and leakage.** At 09:00 an original written prompt clearly separates **“the students who arrived early”** from other students; a generated caption omits **“who arrived early”**, and an audio rendition loses a contrastive pause required by a separately reviewed supplementary-clause task. Quarantine construct-changing renditions; do not score a learner against information never rendered. At 09:02 the learner independently asks **“All students, or only those who arrived early?”**; at 09:03 the tutor actually displays a highlighted referent diagram; at 09:04 the learner repeats the correct subset. Preserve the 09:02 FIRST_UNAIDED clarification; mark the 09:04 response ASSISTED. A diagram queued but never displayed does not retroactively contaminate 09:02. A screen-reader description that exposes the target before first response is answer exposure. Replayed event IDs cannot create extra trials. A readable transcript is evidence for reading/clarification, not unheard listening.

**F — Longitudinal method selection, reversal and genuinely unseen transfer.** Learner A correctly points to referents while a color-coded diagram is visible but fails delayed first-unaided spoken descriptions. Across several matched **actually delivered** CLAUSE_TO_RECIPIENT_ROLEPLAY opportunities, later first-unaided choices improve with new antecedent sets, different clause placements and neutral/unfamiliar contexts; cautiously increase roleplay probability while still sampling diagrams and learner-generated questions. Learner B initially succeeds after roleplay but later confuses an embedded relative clause during a new unaided transfer: lower confidence, advance review and reopen alternatives. If diagram tasks have two antecedents and one short clause while roleplay tasks have four antecedents, nested clauses and noisy audio, infer **no method winner**. Merely swapping a game club for a music club while keeping the same referents, syntactic template and answer position is **not** unseen transfer. Missing delayed probes remain MISSING, not failures or evidence of gain.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Existing objective and reviewed reference oracle.** A–F bind a versioned existing CEFR can-do, prerequisites, recipient information gap, language/variety, source-world, attachment alternatives, actually accessible delivery, independent rubric, first-unaided threshold, due review, delayed/unseen probes and unchanged exit gate; otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED.
2. **Identifying selection.** A selects the uniquely identified assistant under reviewed observable facts; missing observable facts permit targeted clarification. No confident invented selection earns independence.
3. **Supplementary versus identifying scope.** B distinguishes subset selection from separately established supplementary description only when reviewed source-world, written punctuation or actually available prosody and discourse support the distinction. Contradictory or missing context is not silently resolved.
4. **Attachment ambiguity and repair.** C–D preserve all plausible interpretations until a reviewed disambiguating source arrives; accept concise natural targeted questions and subsequent accurate repair. No fabricated unique attachment or recipient action.
5. **Communicative equivalence and modality boundaries.** A–D accept natural paraphrases, relative-pronoun alternatives and variety-appropriate clarification that preserve referents; do not require metalanguage or treat punctuation, prosody, reading and listening as interchangeable evidence.
6. **Delivery, authorship, exposure and replay.** E audits original versus actually rendered source and assistive transformations; quarantines meaning-changing omissions or shifts, preserves pre-help first authorship, marks actual models/diagrams as assistance and deduplicates stable events.
7. **Controlled, uncertain, reversible instruction.** F compares feasible **actually delivered** methods under matched antecedent load, nesting, ambiguity, language, modality, help, source, prior exposure, spacing, topic and follow-up. Sparse, selective, contradictory or confounded observations yield no winner; regression lowers confidence and restores exploration.
8. **Delayed genuinely unseen independent transfer and interest diversity.** Assisted repetition, same-session recall, unchanged referent sets and topic-only swaps do not earn delayed/unseen mastery. Require a later distinct eligible session, new referent set and changed attachment/relative-clause realization, recipient/context, reviewed oracle, first-unaided accurate selection or justified clarification and explicit missingness. Keep shared CEFR gates, skill balance and neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar topic access.
9. **Honest implementation and efficacy boundary.** Documentation CI is not executable referent QA, shipped adaptive tutoring or proof of human learning. Require exact-head executable assertions against real objectives and actual instruction changes driven by longitudinal outcomes, then independently reviewed later transfer versus a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, level enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic design/probe histories, not observed learner outcomes or executable QA. Preserve ambiguity, actual source accessibility, reversible adaptation and explicit UNSUPPORTED gates until implementation and independent longitudinal evidence exist.
