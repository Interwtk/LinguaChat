# F222 — Instructional-method selection bias, counterfactual uncertainty and independent transfer

**Status:** DESIGN-ONLY / UNSUPPORTED longitudinal curriculum acceptance fixture. This document is neither an implemented adaptive tutor nor an efficacy result. It does not authorize learner experimentation, production tracking, a CEFR release, or any change to mastery thresholds. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Governing contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F219 addresses learner confidence, F220 session constraints and interruptions, and F221 external assistance provenance. **Distinct question here:** when instructional methods are assigned under different learner states, can LinguaChat avoid falsely concluding that the method associated with higher immediate or delayed scores caused better learning? A tutor must remain useful without pretending observational data are randomized evidence.

## Shared binding and evidence ledger

Each synthetic history must bind to an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, target language and accepted varieties, reviewed communicative rubric, objective difficulty, prerequisites, required receptive/productive skills, accessible delivery constraints, independent mastery/exit criteria and review obligations. No invented can-do or B2 Arc 6 recognition/open-retrieval resolution is permitted. Example utterances are illustrative, not published lessons.

Record a **pre-decision** snapshot before every choice: stable pseudonymous learner, objective/task/rubric/skill/channel/content/policy versions; session and event IDs; trusted event and answer-bearing render chronology; first valid unaided attempt; reviewed semantic quality; hesitation only when reliably measured; retries and hints; prior proficiency evidence and due-review debt; learner choice, time budget, accessibility constraints and availability of candidate methods; **which methods were feasible**, why each was offered or excluded, selection policy/version and any deterministic exploration seed; the **method actually rendered** and its dose; topic/context and difficulty; answer exposure; later distinct-session meaningful-delay unaided retrieval and unseen transfer; and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Never replace actual delivery with a recommendation that was merely queued.

Treat method comparison as **uncertain observational evidence** unless assignment and comparability are justified. Do not equate a high conditional success rate with a causal benefit. Explicitly distinguish selection, exposure, completion and outcome denominators; preserve failures and missing follow-ups. Missing results are not failures or successes by default. Keep topic-interest selection separate from instruction-method selection.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories and matched controls

**A — Difficulty-driven method assignment (confounding by indication).** Two cohorts work toward the same existing information-request can-do. Learners with more recurring errors receive scaffolded explanations; learners already showing clean success receive direct free production. The free-production group scores higher at the next probe. Unsafe rule declares free production universally superior and removes scaffolds from learners who need them. **Expected:** retain baseline ability/error differences and method eligibility; label the naive comparison NOT_COMPARABLE; use within-learner, similarly difficult and independently scored delayed new-item evidence before revising a hypothesis. Matched control holds pre-choice errors, first-attempt quality, item difficulty, accessibility, exposure and review interval comparable across method alternatives; only then is a cautious comparison eligible, still not proof of causality.

**B — Attractive topics masquerading as method effects.** The learner practices reading with a favorite game topic and listening with unfamiliar transport announcements. Reading yields higher first-attempt scores. Unsafe rule classifies the learner as a permanent “reader” and eliminates listening. **Expected:** separate interest, topic familiarity, modality, audio quality and item difficulty; preserve listening debt and neutral/unfamiliar contexts. Matched control rotates gaming, transport and neutral contexts across both methods while preserving objective and reviewed difficulty. An interest-topic swap must not change CEFR standards or silently become evidence that one instructional method works better.

**C — Immediate correctness versus durable transfer.** An answer-bearing worked example produces 9/10 correct assisted repeats in session 1; a brief retrieval-first method produces 6/10 independent first attempts. After 10 elapsed days, a distinct-session unseen task shows stronger independent meaning transfer after retrieval-first instruction. Unsafe rule optimizes for the 9/10 assisted correctness and suppresses retrieval practice. **Expected:** distinguish assisted from clean first attempts, immediate from delayed, and same-item repeats from genuinely unseen transfer; prefer durable independent evidence when reviewed and comparable. Matched control reveals the later transfer answer before submission; that response becomes assisted and cannot support superiority. A day offset alone is not proof of meaningful delay if an intervening answer exposure occurred.

**D — Missing-not-at-random follow-up.** Method X has 8/10 completed delayed probes correct, but 20 additional due probes were never rendered because the learner was offline. Method Y has 7/10 correct with all 10 due probes rendered and completed. Unsafe rule reports 80% versus 70% as a complete learning comparison. **Expected:** preserve X due=30, offered/rendered/completed=10 and missing=20, Y due=10, completed=10; distinguish non-render from decline, interruption and failure; avoid ranking on completers alone. Matched control makes X's 20 missing probes valid independent failures or successes; conclusions must be allowed to change without silently imputing either outcome in the original history. Never penalize an individual for offline time.

**E — Self-selection, accessibility and ethically bounded exploration.** A learner requests written explanations because speech playback fails on their device. Another prefers audio for convenience. Unsafe rule treats choices as stable learning-style labels and forces a randomized audio-only challenge, or attributes their later outcomes solely to modality. **Expected:** respect feasible accessible channels and explicit choices; do not infer a diagnosis or permanent preference; sample only consent-compatible, feasible, non-harmful instructional alternatives with equivalent objectives and evidence requirements. A spoken skill objective cannot be certified from text-only practice; retain it as pending and offer an accessible valid assessment path. Matched control restores working audio with learner consent; the candidate set can expand without retroactively changing earlier evidence. Do not claim that passive observational switching is a randomized trial.

**F — Simpson reversal, duplicate events and a genuinely delivered next choice.** Two contexts differ in baseline difficulty. Method P is mostly assigned to hard tasks and Q to easy tasks. Aggregated outcomes favor Q, while reviewed comparable within-context delayed independent results favor P or are too sparse to decide. Events arrive out of order; one successful transfer is a replay and one has UNKNOWN answer-visibility chronology. A listening review is due but queued and never rendered. Unsafe rule picks Q from the pooled rate, counts the duplicate, calls UNKNOWN a pass and marks listening complete. **Expected:** reconstruct trusted chronology, deduplicate, quarantine UNKNOWN, stratify by baseline context/difficulty, retain uncertainty and missing denominators, and record a feasible **actually rendered** next activity that respects listening debt and balanced exploration. Matched control reverses the within-context independent outcomes while holding opportunity, difficulty, rubric, aid exposure and timing constant; the method hypothesis must be revisable or explicitly fall back to balanced sampling. A favorite-topic swap affects context probabilities only.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Objective invariance:** every case uses existing reviewed versioned CEFR objectives, unchanged mastery/exit criteria, prerequisites, skill coverage and valid accessibility; no Pre-A1 edit or A1–C2 activation.
2. **Pre-choice provenance:** record baseline state, candidate feasibility, selection reason, learner preference, objective/rubric versions, actual rendered method and dose before scoring outcomes. A post-hoc rationale is not a valid decision record.
3. **Comparability gate:** method contrasts require independently reviewed equivalent constructs, difficulty, exposure, opportunity, context, baseline proficiency, valid channel and meaningful delay; otherwise emit NOT_COMPARABLE/UNSUPPORTED, not a causal winner.
4. **Independent durable outcomes:** distinguish assisted correctness, first unaided response, meaningful-delay retrieval and unseen cross-context transfer; a model answer or same-item replay cannot certify independent mastery.
5. **Interest separation:** B and F rotate related, neutral and unfamiliar contexts; no topic filter may remove a CEFR objective or masquerade as a method effect.
6. **Fair feasible exploration:** E excludes inaccessible or unwanted options; no fixed visual/auditory/reading learner label, coerced modality, unsupported diagnosis or unapproved live experiment.
7. **Missingness and chronology:** D and F preserve due/offered/rendered/completed/missing counts, distinct sessions, stable IDs, trusted render order, deduplication and UNKNOWN. No denominator laundering, invented failure or invented success.
8. **Reversible delivered adaptation:** A–F retain competing hypotheses, contradictory evidence and uncertainty. Matched controls must permit a changed next **actually rendered** instructional choice or an explicitly justified balanced fallback while preserving receptive/productive skill debt.
9. **Executable and efficacy boundary:** a future machine-readable fixture with deterministic seed/clock, versioned source bindings, reviewed linguistic and causal-comparability oracles, matched controls and per-assertion PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED must be executed by an independent exact-head harness. Synthetic invariant PASS would not establish that method selection improves real learning. Any human-efficacy claim additionally requires a reviewed longitudinal protocol, ethically appropriate comparison, delayed unaided outcomes, unseen transfer, attrition/missingness analysis, accessible subgroup fairness and no hidden skill regression.

## Verification contract and non-goals

Required future harness outputs: exact curriculum and engine HEAD; executable command; source/rubric/task/policy/channel resolution; each case/control's trusted timeline; pre-choice candidate set; delivered method and dose; independent-eligibility determination; due/offered/rendered/completed/missing counts; comparability judgment; expected-versus-observed assertion results; and independent reviewer signoff. Until those exist, **all acceptance outcomes are UNSUPPORTED**. No deployment, live user assignment, Auth/cloud/infra/Supabase/production mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge or production-level tracking is authorized.

**Fixture verdict:** F222 makes a central tutor-quality failure falsifiable: LinguaChat must not mistake who received a method, which topic they saw, or who returned for follow-up for evidence that the method caused durable independent learning. Adaptive choices remain uncertain, reversible, balanced and grounded in independently reviewed CEFR evidence.
