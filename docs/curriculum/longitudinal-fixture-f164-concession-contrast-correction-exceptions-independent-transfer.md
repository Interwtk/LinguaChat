## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Concession preserves both propositions.** Fictional event message: **“Although the room is small, it seats eight people.”** **“The room is small and seats eight”** preserves both facts. **“The room is too small for eight”** contradicts the source; **“The room seats eight because it is small”** invents a cause. Counterfactual: **“The room is small, so it can seat only six”** explicitly provides a different claim. A concession does not quantify size or explain why capacity is unexpected.

**B — Contrast is not automatic causation or cancellation.** Fictional library note: **“We have chairs, but we do not have tables.”** A correct handoff preserves available chairs and missing tables. **“There are no chairs or tables”** fails; **“The chairs caused the table shortage”** fails. Counterfactual: **“We have chairs because the club donated them; however, there are no tables”** licenses the donation as the source of chairs, not the cause of missing tables. **“Nora likes mornings, whereas Leo prefers evenings”** contrasts preferences, not availability.

**C — Correction, proposal and completed event are distinct.** Fictional study-group exchange: **“Meet on Tuesday.”** The same organizer later writes **“Sorry, not Tuesday—Thursday instead.”** A recipient-facing update reports Thursday as the corrected meeting plan with source/time. **“The meeting happened Thursday”** invents completion; **“Meet Tuesday or Thursday”** retains a superseded proposal. Counterfactual: **“Could we meet Thursday instead?”** is a question, not an accepted change. An unrelated participant's preference is not automatically an authorized correction. If confirmation is missing, ask who approved the revision.

**D — Explicit exception retains its domain.** Fictional club instruction: **“All team members except Lina should bring a notebook.”** The instruction applies to members other than Lina; it does not forbid Lina from bringing one or specify what guests need. **“Nobody needs a notebook”** and **“Lina must not bring one”** fail. Counterfactual: **“All attendees except Lina should bring a notebook”** changes the relevant set. Unknown membership calls for clarification rather than guessed eligibility. F157 separately tests general quantifier scope; F164 tests a recipient-facing exception within a contrastive instruction.

**E — Actual delivery, first authorship and event identity.** Original fictional audio: **“We can use the hall, but not the stage. Actually, use the classroom instead.”** ASR drops **“not”**, captions omit **“actually”**, translation changes **“instead”** into **“as well”**, or screen-reader order reverses the correction. Quarantine construct-changing delivery rather than penalizing the learner for inaccessible distinctions. At 10:00 the learner independently asks **“Is the classroom replacing the hall?”**; at 10:01 the tutor renders an answer-bearing correction arrow; at 10:02 the learner copies it. Preserve the 10:00 FIRST_UNAIDED question; the 10:02 handoff is ASSISTED. Queued but unrendered help cannot contaminate earlier authorship; replayed stable event IDs cannot create extra sessions. Corrected text cannot certify unheard listening.

**F — Outcome-responsive methods, real unseen transfer and soft interests.** Fictional learner A sorts **but/although** examples correctly with TWO_CLAIM_CONTRAST_WITH_FADE but repeatedly invents causes in later first-unaided summaries. Several matched, actually delivered RECIPIENT_HANDOFF_ROLEPLAY opportunities precede better delayed independent handoffs with new speakers, revisions and exception domains. Repeated comparable evidence may cautiously favor handoff practice; alternatives remain sampled. Learner B improves initially with CORRECTION_TIMELINE_READBACK but later treats unaccepted proposals as confirmed: lower confidence, advance review and reopen exploration. If one method uses a simple **but** clause and another uses three turns, two negations and a revised exception, infer **no method winner**. Replacing a music club with a game club in the same sentence template is not unseen transfer. Missing follow-up is MISSING, not failure; neutral and unfamiliar topics remain eligible.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Existing CEFR objective and reviewed oracle.** A–F bind existing versioned can-dos, prerequisites, recipient/information gap, source-world/discourse oracle, language/variety/register, actually delivered accessible task, evaluator/rubric, first-unaided threshold, due review, delayed/unseen probes and unchanged exit gate. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED; no invented advanced grammar requirement.
2. **Concession preserves assertions.** A retains both claims and rejects contradictions or causal explanations not licensed by the reviewed source.
3. **Contrast preserves polarity and scope.** B keeps chairs available and tables unavailable, distinguishes preference from availability, and rejects fabricated causes from **but/whereas/however**.
4. **Correction versus proposal versus completion.** C preserves source/time/authority, does not promote questions into confirmations or plans into completed events, and accepts clarification when revision authority is uncertain.
5. **Exception-domain fidelity.** D preserves member versus attendee scope and does not turn an exception from an obligation into a prohibition; unknown membership remains unknown.
6. **Actual delivery, chronology and deduplication.** E compares original and rendered ASR/captions/translation/screen-reader order, quarantines construct-changing losses, protects pre-help authorship, logs actual answer exposure and deduplicates stable events. Accessible reading cannot certify unheard listening.
7. **Controlled uncertain reversible adaptation.** F compares feasible actually delivered methods under matched clause count, negation, correction, exception-set size, language, modality, topic, source completeness, help, spacing and follow-up. Sparse, selective, contradictory or confounded evidence yields no winner; delayed regression lowers confidence and reopens exploration.
8. **Delayed first-unaided genuinely unseen transfer and nonexclusive interests.** Copied models, assisted clause sorting, same-session repeats and topic-only substitutions cannot earn delayed/unseen mastery. Require a later distinct eligible session, changed recipient/context **and changed discourse-relation/revision structure**, reviewed oracle, first-unaided actionable handoff or clarification and explicit missingness. Preserve shared objectives, balanced modality evidence and neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar topic rotation.
9. **Honest implementation and efficacy boundary.** Passing documentation CI does not ship a discourse evaluator, adaptive tutor or proof of improved human retention. Require exact-head executable assertions against real objectives, delivered instruction changes based on longitudinal outcomes, later independent transfer versus a balanced baseline and separately reviewed learner evidence before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, level enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic design/probe histories, not observed learner outcomes or executable QA. Preserve contrast, concession, correction authority, exception scope, accessible delivery and explicit UNSUPPORTED gates until implementation and longitudinal evidence exist.
