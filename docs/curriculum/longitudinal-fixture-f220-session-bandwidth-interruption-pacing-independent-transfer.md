# F220 — Session bandwidth, interruption, pacing, recovery and independent transfer

**Status:** design-only synthetic *cross-cutting* longitudinal curriculum acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until an independently reviewed executable exact-head harness tests event histories and the **actual delivered** teaching decisions. This is not a shipped fatigue detector, cognitive diagnosis, validated pacing optimizer, new CEFR can-do, changed mastery standard, or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 remains frozen and A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F210 covers hint dose and feedback timing; F211 task challenge; F213 explicit learner choice/deferral; F214 diagnostic selection; F219 voluntary metacognitive judgments. **Distinct question in F220:** can a tutor adapt *the amount, sequence and recoverability of instruction within constrained or interrupted sessions* without confusing opportunity, connectivity, interruption, voluntary breaks or repeated exposure with language competence—and subsequently verify durable **unaided retrieval and unseen transfer** across genuinely distinct sessions?

## Fixed objective, session provenance and competing hypotheses

Every history binds existing **versioned CEFR can-do**, reviewed task-specific communicative/meaning rubric, target language and accepted varieties, prerequisite and required skill family, accessible channel, task/context difficulty, due-review obligations, independent mastery/exit rules and balanced context/skill coverage. The utterances below are synthetic illustrations, not published lessons. Session length, declared available time, pause, device events, engagement and optional self-reported energy are **scheduling context**, never an automatic competence, intelligence, disability, motivation, fatigue or psychological label. Do not infer tiredness from latency, accent, timezone, device type, accessibility tool or a missed notification. No universal optimal session duration or break interval is asserted.

Freeze a **pre-decision** ledger: pseudonymous learner; stable learner/session/run/task/event and content-version IDs; trusted occurrence and delivery chronology; learning-day and meaningful elapsed-time provenance; fixed objective/rubric/skill/channel; due review and coverage debt; declared remaining time (if volunteered), pause/resume/cancel intent and whether a session actually ended; offered/queued/**rendered** task, explanation, hint, model, audio and transcript; first valid unaided attempt and reviewed semantic quality; retries and assistance exposure; partial response and completion boundary; interruption cause when observed versus UNKNOWN; policy options, uncertainty and actual next action; distinct-session delayed unaided retrieval and unseen transfer; due/offered/rendered/completed/abandoned/missing follow-up counts. Replayed or late events cannot create extra sessions or rewrite the first attempt. Unknown or invalid delivery is not a learner failure or pass.

**Decision sequence:** fixed objective and overdue obligations → eligible accessible activities and actual time constraints → choose a feasible chunk or balanced fallback with uncertainty → log what was actually rendered and any assistance → preserve completed independent evidence and mark partial/abandoned work separately → resume without re-awarding old evidence or leaking an answer → later independent retrieval and genuinely unseen transfer → update the tentative pacing hypothesis and actually delivered next option. Pacing may change chunk size, optional explanation detail, order, spacing and support while preserving opportunities for required productive and receptive skills. Never optimize merely for streaks, time-on-app, number of completed screens or immediate assisted correctness. Interest weights change soft example/context probabilities only.

**Oracle labels:** OBJECTIVE_FIXED, RUBRIC_REVIEWED, SKILL_SEPARATE, TIME_BUDGET_VOLUNTARY, TIME_UNKNOWN, SESSION_ACTUALLY_ENDED, PAUSE_NOT_FAILURE, INTERRUPTED_UNKNOWN, PARTIAL_NOT_COMPLETED, FIRST_UNAIDED_PRESERVED, RENDERED_EXPOSURE_RECORDED, QUEUED_NOT_RENDERED, RESUME_IDEMPOTENT, REPLAY_DEDUPED, SAME_SESSION_NOT_DELAYED, MEANINGFUL_DELAY_VERIFIED, CLEAN_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_TRANSFER, LATENCY_NOT_FATIGUE, ACCESSIBLE_FALLBACK, DUE_REVIEW_RETAINED, COVERAGE_DEBT_RETAINED, INTEREST_METHOD_SEPARATE, ACTUAL_NEXT_CHOICE, HYPOTHESIS_REVISED, BALANCED_FALLBACK, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, NOT_COMPARABLE and UNSUPPORTED. These are observation and policy labels, not permanent learner types.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories with matched controls

**A — A five-minute session must not erase a due independent review.** An existing request-for-directions can-do has an overdue *independent* retrieval and a separate new explanation scheduled. The learner voluntarily indicates five minutes remain. Unsafe policy starts a long favorite-topic lesson, marks the skipped review complete and awards a streak. **Expected:** preserve the overdue obligation, select an actually deliverable short accessible independent review (or an explicitly deferred due item when no valid probe fits), and record which was offered, rendered, attempted and completed. A short independent first attempt is scored under the unchanged rubric, not an easier exit rule. Matched control: the learner has thirty minutes and identical prior evidence; a longer mixed sequence may be eligible, but due retrieval and balanced skill obligations cannot disappear. An unrendered queued review earns no credit in either history.

**B — Connection loss after a partial answer is not an incorrect first attempt.** On a valid independent writing task, the prompt renders and the learner types a partial request; connectivity fails before the submission is durably committed. A retry sends the same event twice, then the user resumes the run later. Unsafe policy records a wrong answer, increments practice attempts, and shows the full answer before the learner's first valid attempt. **Expected:** retain the partial as PARTIAL_NOT_COMPLETED with the actual interruption provenance; deduplicate retries; do not count a failed transport as a semantic error, a completed run or independent mastery. On resume, avoid exposing answer-bearing content unless the activity is explicitly converted to assisted practice; otherwise preserve a clean first-attempt opportunity. Matched control: a fully submitted, durably recorded meaning-critical error **before** disconnect remains a real first attempt and is not erased by the reconnect. Reopening the same run never creates a new learning session.

**C — Pause, midnight and repeated reopening do not manufacture delayed retrieval.** A learner finishes an unaided production task at 23:58, pauses, and resumes at 00:03 in the same ongoing learning episode. The identical prompt is reopened twenty times and answered correctly after the tutor has shown a model. Unsafe policy calls the new calendar date twenty independent delayed successes. **Expected:** preserve the original first attempt and actual model exposure; deduplicate repeated event IDs; recognize the ongoing episode and insufficient meaningful delay; do not credit assisted same-item repetitions as clean delayed retrieval. Matched control: after a genuinely distinct later learning session and meaningful intervening interval, a new valid, unmodeled task checks the same objective in a different context. Only its reviewed first unaided answer may be eligible delayed evidence, subject to normal objective standards. Calendar rollover alone is never enough.

**D — Long sessions and slow responses do not justify a fatigue diagnosis.** Two otherwise matched learners complete the same reviewed reading and free-production objectives: one uses a screen reader, dictation and long pauses; the other types quickly. Both have equivalent clean first attempts and later unseen transfer. Unsafe policy labels the slower learner “fatigued/weak,” permanently shortens all lessons, omits speaking/listening and downgrades mastery. **Expected:** no inferred fatigue, intelligence, motivation or skill weakness from duration/latency; maintain accessible pacing and the same independent rubrics, review obligations and available skill opportunities. A volunteered request for a break can change scheduling, not competence. Matched control: later independently reviewed production errors appear under equivalent valid conditions; the tutor may cautiously adapt instruction based on those *language outcomes*, not on input speed. Unavailable or failed audio remains UNKNOWN and cannot become listening evidence.

**E — A successful short chunk is not proof that chunking improves retention.** Across distinct sessions, a learner completes three short chunks with immediate model-assisted correctness and one longer independent activity with a meaning-critical error. The unsafe policy concludes “short lessons work best” from screen completion rate and shows only favorite gaming examples. **Expected:** log assistance, first attempts, comparable task difficulty, actual chunks delivered and missing delayed follow-ups; do not compare assisted short successes to unaided long failures as if they were equivalent. Keep plausible short/long or retrieval-first alternatives, balanced skill practice and neutral/unfamiliar context rotation. Matched control: both formats have comparable reviewed first unaided attempts, exposure, difficulty, intervals and valid later unseen transfer; only then may a tentative pacing hypothesis update, with uncertainty and deliberate exploration. Changing gaming to cooking alters only soft context weights, not objective, method estimate, rubric or follow-up eligibility.

**F — Interrupted and contradictory histories must change the *delivered* next plan.** For a fixed information-request can-do, a learner has four eligible distinct-session independent outcomes under two feasible pacing options, all with reviewed equivalent difficulty, context rotation and meaningful delays: short retrieval-first yields success then failure; longer explanation-plus-independent-practice yields failure then success. A fifth apparent short success is a duplicate assisted retry, and a sixth alleged long-session failure has no trusted prompt-render chronology. Events arrive in order 4, 2, duplicate 5, 1, 3, 6. Two required skill reviews remain due, and one planned follow-up was never rendered because the learner left. **Expected:** deduplicate event 5, quarantine event 6, retain contradictory eligible outcomes and due/coverage debt, distinguish not-offered from not-rendered from abandoned from missing follow-up, and choose a feasible **actually rendered** next sequence with explicit uncertainty or balanced exploration. Matched control reverses the later independently reviewed outcomes while holding objective, task difficulty, assistance, intervals, access, context and available time fixed; the next plan must be capable of changing, or provide a documented balanced fallback. If formats differ in exposure or comparability, mark NOT_COMPARABLE; no causal “best pacing” claim is allowed.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed curricular standards:** A–F bind versioned existing CEFR can-dos, reviewed meaning/function rubrics, prerequisites, skill and modality requirements, and unchanged mastery/exit thresholds. No Pre-A1 edit, A1–C2 release, invented objective, or resolution of B2 Arc 6 recognition/open-retrieval disagreement.
2. **Session constraints are not competence:** A and D treat volunteered time/break requests as planning context; absent time is UNKNOWN. Latency, accessibility, device behavior and interruption cannot become fatigue, psychological, motivation, CEFR or ability labels.
3. **Opportunity versus outcome:** A and B distinguish due, offered, queued, actually rendered, submitted, completed, abandoned and missing; only valid reviewed learner evidence can count. A skipped or interrupted task is neither automatic failure nor mastery.
4. **Resume and first-attempt integrity:** B preserves durable submitted first attempts, separately marks partial/uncommitted responses, deduplicates replay, and does not leak a model into a purported clean assessment on resume. Failed transport does not fabricate error or a new session.
5. **Meaningful delayed evidence:** C requires distinct-session, meaningfully delayed, unaided, reviewed new-item retrieval; midnight, reopen loops, copied models and same-run repetition do not count. All original exposure and chronology survive.
6. **Access, modality and balance:** D preserves equivalent accessible opportunities and unchanged rubrics; text or failed audio cannot certify oral/listening skill. Pacing adjustments do not starve required productive/receptive objectives or erase review debt.
7. **Pacing and interest are separate policies:** E controls objective, difficulty, assistance, interval and independent follow-up when comparing instructional pacing; gaming-versus-cooking affects only soft context probabilities. Adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts stay eligible.
8. **Reversible longitudinal delivery:** F preserves event IDs, trusted occurrence/render chronology, contradictory eligible outcomes, duplicates, UNKNOWN, due/offered/rendered/completed/abandoned/missing denominators, uncertainty, alternatives and the **next choice actually rendered**. Opposite clean outcomes can revise a tentative pacing hypothesis or justify balanced exploration without fixed learner labels.
9. **Executable and efficacy boundary:** Publish machine-readable A–F histories and matched controls, deterministic seed/clock, objective/rubric/task/channel/policy versions, independent language/measurement oracles, assistance and interruption exposure, per-assertion PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED and independently reviewed exact-head executable harness. Synthetic PASS establishes invariants only. Any claim of improved durable human learning needs a separately reviewed longitudinal learner pilot against a balanced accessible baseline, comparable opportunities, delayed unaided retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer, missingness/attrition and subgroup-fairness analyses, and no hidden loss in required skills. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** F220 makes **time-aware pacing and interruption recovery without fabricated competence or retention** falsifiable. A strong tutor can respect the learner's available time, resume safely, and adjust future instruction while keeping CEFR and independent learning evidence intact. **UNSUPPORTED** until independently executed and reviewed; no human-efficacy claim is made.
