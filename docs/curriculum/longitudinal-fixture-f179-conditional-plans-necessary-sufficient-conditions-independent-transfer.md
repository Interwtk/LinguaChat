Bind each eligible task to an **existing versioned CEFR reading, listening, spoken interaction, mediation or written production can-do** whose authentic communication demand includes understanding or conveying conditions, plans, alternatives or clarification. Preserve can-do/prerequisites, target language and reviewed variety, unchanged rubric/threshold, accessible route, first-independent evidence, due/delayed/unseen probes, receptive/productive balance and mastery/exit gates. Fictional clubs, libraries, workshops and community events only; no medical, legal, financial, emergency or safety-critical operational decisions.

An independently reviewed **conditional-world oracle**, fixed before delivery, records the utterance and source; proposition/antecedent/consequent; negation and modal scope; whether a condition is necessary, sufficient, both, neither or **not determinable** from the utterance; relevant time/reference world (actual, proposed, future possible, past counterfactual); condition status (met, unmet, unknown, no longer applicable); whether the action is authorized, merely permitted, proposed, prohibited, pending or completed; exceptions, alternatives and source-confirmed plan revisions. Separate **recipient-action oracle** records what a recipient may responsibly conclude or do *given only the evidence delivered to that recipient*, not hidden author knowledge. The oracle must include legitimate ambiguity and targeted clarification; do not automatically infer actual-world truth from grammatical counterfactual forms or from the speaker's apparent confidence.

An **actual-delivery oracle** records independently reviewed target-language/variety text/audio assets, speaker turns, visible condition markers, timestamps when relevant, real rendered hashes, audio playback success, captions/translations, visual flow diagrams, models and answer-bearing hints. Hidden plan-state flags, unavailable timestamps or undisplayed diagrams cannot count as learner-visible evidence. Review accent, dialect and accessibility equivalence; an accessible text route can validly measure reading/mediation but cannot be relabeled unaided listening.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — A condition does not certify a booking.** A fictional workshop coordinator says, “**If** the library confirms the room, we **can** hold the rehearsal there.” No confirmation has been delivered. The learner tells the team “The rehearsal is booked at the library.” Mark CONDITIONAL_AS_COMMITMENT and ACTION_NOT_AUTHORIZED. “We may use the library if it confirms the room; confirmation is still pending” preserves the condition. A confirmation delivered in a later session can justify a **new** updated report, not retroactive credit for the first overstatement. Do not treat “can” as evidence of a completed reservation.

**B — Only if is necessary, not automatically sufficient.** The fictional printer instructions say “You may print the posters **only if** the coordinator approves the design.” The learner reports “The coordinator approved it, so printing is definitely allowed.” Even if approval is independently verified, the sentence establishes approval as a **necessary** condition; it does not establish sufficiency or eliminate other requirements. “We cannot print without approval; let's confirm the remaining requirements” is safe. In a separate reviewed variant, “If the coordinator approves it, you may print” supplies a different sufficient permission claim within that scenario; do not collapse the two. No real authorization is inferred beyond the fictional utterance.

**C — Unless and even if retain scope.** A club message says “**Unless** the rain stops, we'll meet inside. **Even if** more chairs arrive, the upstairs room will remain closed.” With rain status unknown, the learner cannot announce an unconditional indoor meeting or claim extra chairs reopen upstairs. The reviewed interpretation preserves the default conditional meeting plan and the independently stated upstairs restriction. Where “unless” leaves what happens after rain stops unstated, accept a targeted “If it stops raining, where should we meet?” rather than fabricating an outdoor plan. A contradictory update must be confirmed before changing the recipient-facing instruction.

**D — Counterfactual explanation is not an event log.** A fictional volunteer says “**If I had checked** the calendar earlier, I **would have noticed** the overlap.” The learner writes “The volunteer checked the calendar and noticed the overlap.” That reports hypothetical consequences as completed events. “The volunteer says checking earlier would have revealed the overlap” preserves the counterfactual. Common pragmatic implications about whether checking actually happened are **defeasible**, not a deterministic truth oracle: if the recipient needs the actual history, request clarification. Compare with an independently reviewed actual-world message, “I checked the calendar and found the overlap,” without assuming the same world state.

**E — First authorship, accessibility and a confirmed update.** At 10:02 an audio message says “If the instructor agrees, we can move the workshop to Friday,” but the audio fails to play. The learner requests a transcript. Do **not** score unaided listening or infer failure to understand a condition. At 10:03 a transcript with a highlighted conditional and a model plan summary actually renders; at 10:04 the learner correctly reports “Friday is pending approval.” Record ASSISTED and ANSWER_EXPOSURE; a merely offered but unopened model is not exposure. At 11:00 a separate, actually delivered instructor approval plus explicit plan update may justify a new report “Friday is confirmed.” Keep first authorship and plan-state timestamps distinct, deduplicate replayed event IDs, and grade the accessible route under the **real** measured construct.

**F — Longitudinal reversal, matched comparisons and unseen transfer.** Learner A repeatedly understands simple “if” clauses but independently treats “only if” as “if” in messages to new recipients. Across matched sessions, actually delivered NECESSARY_VERSUS_SUFFICIENT_CONTRAST and ASK_BEFORE_ACTING_CLARIFICATION alternate with BALANCED_DEFAULT; later **first-unaided** reports on unseen club, library and neutral workshop scenarios preserve permission direction and unknown status. Only after comparable repeated delayed probes may the policy cautiously increase the supported method's probability while retaining alternatives. Learner B initially performs well after CONDITION_TO_ACTION_MAPPING but later misreports a new “unless” plan; lower confidence and explore UNLESS_AND_EVEN_IF_SCOPE_REPAIR. If one method had easier conditions, explicit answer-bearing diagrams, verified plan status, familiar topics, extra help, shorter intervals or more follow-up, record **no causal winner**. A calendar boundary, memorized paraphrase, assisted retry or absent follow-up is not delayed unseen transfer. Preserve receptive/productive balance and independent recipient action, not just recognition.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed CEFR and reviewed world/recipient oracles.** A–F bind existing versioned relevant can-dos, independent conditional-world and recipient-action review, actual-delivery provenance and unchanged mastery standards. If not relevant or not deliverable, mark NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED; no new CEFR gate.
2. **Conditional plan is not a confirmed plan.** A rejects an unconditional room booking from a pending “if” statement; later confirmation does not rewrite the original first attempt.
3. **Necessary/sufficient direction is preserved.** B rejects the invalid converse/sufficiency inference from “only if,” contrasts it with reviewed “if” permission and accepts clarification when additional conditions are unknown.
4. **Scope, exceptions and incomplete branches.** C preserves “unless” and “even if” without inventing what happens when rain stops or implying chairs override the upstairs restriction.
5. **Actual versus hypothetical worlds.** D rejects hypothetical-as-completed reports; counterfactual pragmatic implications remain defeasible unless separately established.
6. **First authorship, actual help and accessibility.** E keeps the earliest eligible unaided report/clarification, records only rendered exposure, distinguishes audio failure from learner error, deduplicates retries and dates confirmed plan updates without retroactive mastery.
7. **Matched, uncertain, reversible adaptation.** F changes probabilities only from comparable actually delivered instructional choices and eligible later independent outcomes; sparse, confounded or contradictory observations prevent causal-winner claims and retain exploration.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer and independent soft-interest rotation.** Require a genuinely later distinct session, new independently reviewed conditions/sources/recipients, first-unaided action/report or targeted clarification, and explicit missing follow-up. Rotate adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics while preserving fixed can-dos and balanced modality coverage.
9. **Evidence and protected scope.** Synthetic prose and generic CI do not establish executable conditional-world QA, shipped tutor adaptation or human retention. Require exact-head executable stimulus/world/first-authorship assertions, demonstrably outcome-driven instructional decisions and independently reviewed delayed unaided transfer against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, A1–C2 enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic acceptance histories, not observed learners or executable QA. Preserve logical direction, condition status, hypothetical worlds, justified clarification and reversible instruction. Remain UNSUPPORTED until implementation and independently verified learning evidence exist.
