**Contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F178 covers attributed source and epistemic certainty; F179 covers logical conditions and contingencies; F181 covers pronoun and ellipsis reference. **Distinct construct:** when an utterance conventionally or contextually performs an action beyond its literal grammatical form (request, offer, refusal, deferral, clarification), and when its practical force remains uncertain. A question about ability can be an indirect request, but a description of a problem does not automatically authorize an action. Polite form is not proof of consent, a promise, or task completion.

## Fixed objectives and independently reviewed pragmatic oracles

Bind only to **existing versioned CEFR listening, reading, spoken interaction, mediation or written-production can-dos** that genuinely require interpreting requests or responding appropriately in discourse. Preserve the can-do, prerequisites, target language and independently reviewed variety, unchanged rubric/threshold, accessibility route, first-independent evidence, due/delayed/unseen probes, balanced receptive/productive coverage and mastery/exit gates. If the construct is not demanded by the can-do, mark NOT_APPLICABLE; never invent a level requirement. All situations are fictional, low-stakes school-club, library, rehearsal or community-workshop exchanges; no real medical, legal, financial, emergency or safety-critical decisions.

An independently reviewed **speech-act oracle**, fixed before delivery, stores speaker/recipient roles, exact wording, prior turns, shared activity, physical and temporal context, prosody when actually heard, literal semantic content, plausible intended acts, strength of each inference and unresolved alternatives. It distinguishes a **conventional indirect request** supported by context from a **context-dependent hint** that could be merely an observation. Reviewers must document legitimate readings, including literal questions when the setting supports them; no single guessed hidden intention can become ground truth. An independently reviewed **recipient-response oracle** records appropriate clarification, acknowledgment, refusal, acceptance or action-report language, distinguishing a proposal from consent, a promise from tentative willingness, and an action requested from one actually completed. Respect target-language pragmatics, register, community variation and accessibility without imposing a universal politeness hierarchy or stereotypes about speakers.

An **actual-delivery oracle** records the reviewed language/variety, rendered text/audio hashes, interlocutor turns, audible intonation, subtitles, punctuation, scene cues, role cards, translations, exemplars, hints and answer-bearing highlights **actually exposed** before the first answer. A fictional role card that explicitly says "she is requesting the marker" is assistance, not unaided pragmatic inference. An optional unviewed hint is not exposure. An audio failure cannot be scored as unaided listening; an accessible text path may validly assess reading/interaction without being relabeled hearing.

The **first-authorship/provenance ledger** stores pseudonymous learner, objective/rubric/oracle versions, stable session-qualified event IDs and replay deduplication; earliest eligible unaided interpretation, recipient-facing response or targeted clarification; delivered stimuli and supported alternatives; ordered actual hints/models/replays/transcripts/answer exposure; accessibility route; method assigned/offered/**actually delivered**; and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed and unseen probes with elapsed time and distinct-session provenance. Optional hesitation/latency is weak, never fabricated and never a mastery gate. Assisted repair never overwrites the original unaided response.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Conventional ability question as request, not a completed action.** In a fictional library workshop, a participant beside a box of markers says to a nearby helper, "Could you pass me the blue marker, please?" The reviewed shared context supports a conventional indirect request, not a quiz about physical ability. A learner replies, "Yes, I am physically able to," and ends the exchange without offering or passing the marker. Record that the response misses the likely interactional purpose. Accept "Sure, here you go" when the roleplay includes the actual pass, or "I can't reach it; could you ask Nia?" when unable. Saying "I will pass it" alone is a promise, not evidence that the object was handed over. A separate reviewed ability-testing context may legitimately make a literal answer appropriate; do not force the workshop reading onto it.

**B — Contextual observation is not automatic permission.** During a fictional rehearsal, a speaker looks toward a window and says, "It's a little chilly in here." No request, gesture, shared rule or follow-up establishes whether the speaker wants the window closed, is making conversation or is explaining why they put on a sweater. The learner tells a teammate, "She ordered us to shut the window." Reject INTENT_OVERATTRIBUTION; the observation alone does not justify a command or completed-action report. Accept "Would you like me to close the window?" or an accurate observation without invented intent. In a separately reviewed version, "It's chilly—could you close the window?" establishes the request; the oracle must use the **delivered** contrast, not an imagined gesture.

**C — Polite deferral is not agreement or refusal.** A fictional club organizer asks, "Could you help set up chairs after class?" The recipient says, "I'll check my schedule and let you know." The learner informs a teammate, "They promised to help." Reject ACCEPTANCE_NOT_ESTABLISHED: the reply is a deferral, not a confirmed commitment, though it is also not a categorical refusal. Accept "They haven't confirmed yet" or "When will you know?" A distinct response, "Yes, I'll help," may establish a promise, but still not completed setup. "Maybe" and "I'll try" must be evaluated against independently reviewed context and variety rather than mechanically treated as guaranteed yes/no.

**D — Register-sensitive response without a single culturally privileged script.** In a fictional community-workshop email, an unfamiliar coordinator writes, "I was wondering whether you could send the agenda before Thursday." The learner treats it as a statement about the coordinator's curiosity and replies only "You were wondering." In the reviewed context it is an indirect request for an agenda. Accept several natural target-language/variety-appropriate responses: agreement with an honest time, a polite refusal, or a targeted clarification if the agenda/version is unclear. Do not require one honorific, accent, phrase or stereotype. Contrast with an explicit metalinguistic discussion of what the coordinator wondered, where a literal interpretation could be justified. Do not conflate a courteous request with proof of authority, consent or completed delivery.

**E — Delivery, accessibility, and first authorship.** At 09:02 an audio roleplay contains "Could you pass the blue marker?" but playback fails before any words are heard. Do not grade unaided listening or fabricate a comprehension error. At 09:03 accessible text renders with an answer-bearing role caption "a request for the marker"; at 09:04 the learner correctly identifies the request. Record ASSISTED and ANSWER_EXPOSURE for the observed reading/interaction route, not unaided listening. An unclicked optional caption is not exposure. At 10:15 a new reviewed, successfully delivered and unassisted request in a distinct context can create a new first attempt in its actual modality. Deduplicate repeated event IDs and preserve event order and real elapsed time.

**F — Matched longitudinal adaptation, uncertainty and reversal.** Learner A repeatedly answers conventional indirect requests as literal ability questions, then independently responds appropriately to later unseen neutral and unfamiliar requests after **actually delivered** LITERAL_VERSUS_INTENDED_ACT_CONTRAST and REQUEST_RESPONSE_ROLEPLAY alternate with BALANCED_DEFAULT in comparable sessions. Only repeated meaningfully delayed, first-unaided recipient-facing transfer may cautiously increase the probability of a supported method, while alternatives remain sampled. Learner B initially improves with ROLE_AND_CONTEXT_RECONSTRUCTION_WITH_FADE but later overinterprets ambiguous hints as orders in an unseen workshop exchange; lower confidence and explore HINT_VERSUS_REQUEST_UNCERTAINTY. If one method supplied explicit role labels, stronger intonation, easier requests, more hints, familiar interests, shorter intervals or more follow-up, record **no causal winner**. Same-dialogue paraphrases, assisted retries, a mere midnight boundary and missing follow-up are not durable transfer. Maintain reading/listening and productive/interactive balance.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed CEFR and independent review.** A–F bind existing relevant versioned can-dos, independent speech-act and recipient-response oracles, actual delivery and unchanged mastery/evidence standards; otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED, never an invented CEFR gate.
2. **Conventional indirect requests and actual action.** A recognizes the context-supported request, allows justified literal readings in a different reviewed setting and distinguishes willingness, promise and observed completion.
3. **Ambiguous hints remain ambiguous.** B forbids converting an observation into an order without delivered evidence, accepts targeted clarification and forbids retrospective attribution based on hidden intent.
4. **Deferral versus commitment.** C rejects an unconfirmed promise, distinguishes noncommitment from categorical refusal and never credits proposed future work as already completed.
5. **Register without cultural stereotyping.** D supports multiple context-appropriate responses, preserves request meaning and respects target-language/variety review without one mandatory polite formula.
6. **First authorship, actual help and accessibility.** E logs only exposed cues, retains first eligible unaided evidence, labels playback failure as unavailable, preserves the measured construct and deduplicates replays; assisted repair is not independent mastery.
7. **Matched, uncertain and reversible adaptation.** F may alter instructional probabilities only after comparable actually delivered options and later independent outcomes. Sparse, confounded or contradictory data cannot establish a causal winner and must preserve exploration.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer and soft interests.** Require genuinely later distinct sessions, independently reviewed new interlocutors, requests and ambiguous hints, first-unaided recipient responses or justified clarifications, and explicit missing follow-up. Rotate adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics while preserving fixed can-dos and balanced modalities.
9. **Evidence and protected scope.** Synthetic prose or generic CI cannot establish executable pragmatic-oracle QA, shipped outcome-driven adaptation or human retention gains. Require exact-head executable stimulus/intent/first-authorship assertions, observed policy-decision changes and independently reviewed delayed unaided transfer against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, Pre-A1 changes, A1–C2 enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic acceptance histories, not observed learners or executable QA. Preserve conventional requests, calibrated uncertainty about hints, noncommitment, appropriate recipient responses and reversible instruction. Remain UNSUPPORTED until implementation and independently verified learning evidence exist.
