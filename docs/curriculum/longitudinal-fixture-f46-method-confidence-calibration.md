# F46 — Instructional-method confidence must be calibrated to later independent evidence

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a reproduced defect, shipped adaptive tutor, validated probability forecast, causal effect or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F30 guards assessment calibration drift; F32 guards selective follow-up; F40 guards learner-level harm; F41 guards selective reporting; F45 blocks cohort-to-individual inference. F46 isolates **confidence calibration**: a tutor can correctly keep an instructional recommendation provisional in words yet internally assign unjustified certainty, overexploit a noisy winner, or report a numerical confidence that does not predict future independent outcomes. Confidence in *which method may help* is distinct from the learner's demonstrated CEFR mastery, from certainty that an event occurred, and from satisfaction with a format.

## Controlled setup

Resolve `goal-1` to an existing versioned CEFR can-do, supported skill, fixed prerequisites, frozen rubric and independent mastery/exit gate. Compare eligible A (`concise_explanation`), B (`worked_example`) and a shared balanced comparator. Keep item difficulty, novelty, actual retrieval-delay definition, follow-up opportunity, context family, review debt and receptive/productive requirements comparable wherever possible. Use an injected deterministic clock and seed, a versioned `method_confidence_calibration_policy`, an immutable synthetic ledger and actual distinct learning sessions.

Before outcomes, specify **what confidence means**: e.g., a forecast distribution or bounded probability of a *predefined* later unaided retrieval-plus-unfamiliar-transfer outcome under an eligible method, **not** a probability that the method caused learning or that a learner has a fixed style. Define forecast issuance time, target, horizon, eligibility, abstention rule, uncertainty interval or ordinal uncertainty state, minimum comparable evidence, recalibration/revision procedure and an appropriate predeclared reliability assessment. If numeric probabilities are unavailable or unjustified, output `UNQUANTIFIED` with a reason; do not invent precise percentages or universal sample thresholds.

Log stable learner/objective/skill/session/run/event/probe IDs; forecast and policy versions with trusted issue time; the then-available information set and method eligibility; known assignment mechanism/probability (unknown stays unknown); offered/assigned/**verified-delivered** instruction; first unaided attempts and support; rubric/item versions; genuinely delayed independent retrieval and unseen-context transfer; observed baseline/readiness and practice/external-instruction when known; eligible/completed/missing follow-up and its reasons; trusted event versus upload chronology; review/skill/context coverage; and whether an outcome was used for model fitting, tuning, or a later untouched evaluation. Never use an outcome to backdate or rewrite the forecast that preceded it.

Keep distinct ledgers for (1) observed independent CEFR mastery, (2) forecasted future independent outcomes and method ranking, (3) evidence strength/calibration diagnostics, and (4) causal attribution status. Predeclare `SPARSE_CONFIDENCE`, `UNQUANTIFIED`, `OVERCONFIDENT`, `MIS_CALIBRATED`, `CONTRADICTORY_EVIDENCE`, `FORECAST_LEAKAGE`, `DRIFT_RECALIBRATION_REQUIRED`, `FOLLOWUP_UNKNOWN`, `NOT_COMPARABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. Reliability requires genuinely later held-out outcomes and adequate independent units; repeated probes of one learner are correlated, not new people.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — One lucky early success.** After one verified B lesson, a learner completes one strong first attempt; the later delayed probe is not yet due. A policy reports 95% confidence that B is best. Preserve the good immediate observation, but mark the method confidence `SPARSE_CONFIDENCE`/`UNQUANTIFIED`, keep a balanced default and feasible A/B exploration, and do not promote a fixed style or durable mastery.
- **B — Assisted streak masquerades as certainty.** Many B cards end correct only after model exposure, hints and retries. The tutor treats their volume as independent replication and produces a narrow high-confidence B forecast, although novel delayed unaided production is weak. Separate assistance, clustered repeated occasions and actual independent outcomes; mark `OVERCONFIDENT` and withhold a supported method ranking without erasing legitimate practice.
- **C — Missing follow-up creates false precision.** B forecasts appear well calibrated among the few learners who return, but B has disproportionately missing later probes, and A has more complete follow-up. Report all issued forecasts and eligible/completed/missing denominators by method, learner and opportunity; mark affected comparison `FOLLOWUP_UNKNOWN`/`NOT_COMPARABLE`. Do not convert missing outcomes to zero, success or a falsely narrow interval.
- **D — Contradictory evidence widens then revises.** Several comparable later independent A occasions initially support A for one written-request objective. New verified B occasions produce stronger genuinely delayed unaided transfer, with unchanged rubric and task demand. The forecast must retain its old version, acknowledge conflict, reduce unjustified certainty, then update the uncertain objective-specific mix as evidence accumulates; keep nonzero feasible alternatives, due reviews and coverage.
- **E — Confident predictions fail under drift.** A forecast family once aligned with independent delayed outcomes, but item calibration, instructional delivery, or the learner's observed proficiency changes; recent held-out outcomes systematically diverge. Distinguish observed drift from noise, record assessment/option versions, audit temporal calibration and fall back to balanced sampling or `DRIFT_RECALIBRATION_REQUIRED` until comparable evidence returns. Do not extrapolate confidence across an unsupported skill or silently rescore old mastery.
- **F — Replay, leakage and confidence laundering.** An offline outcome arrives late and twice, an analyst recomputes yesterday's confidence using tomorrow's result, a supposed holdout was already used to choose B, and one assessment answer appeared in a tutor model. Preserve original forecast issue-time snapshots and data-use lineage; deduplicate by stable IDs, quarantine contaminated probes and mark `FORECAST_LEAKAGE`. Different replay orders must yield the same eligible calibration sample and unchanged historical forecasts.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Forecast target and provenance.** For A–F emit versioned CEFR can-do/rubric/skill, eligible methods and actual delivery, exact forecast target/horizon, issue-time information set, confidence representation, comparator, independent outcomes, missingness and uncertainty. Method-effect confidence must never be substituted for mastery or causal certainty.
2. **Sparse-data restraint.** In A, one immediate success with no due delayed independent transfer cannot produce a justified high-confidence personal method ranking; an unsupported numeric percentage becomes `UNQUANTIFIED`/`SPARSE_CONFIDENCE`. Keep balanced alternatives and unchanged mastery gates.
3. **Support and dependence are not independent replications.** In B, assisted correctness and correlated same-item/same-session retries cannot narrow method uncertainty as if they were independent delayed outcomes. Report support exposure and actual independent opportunity denominators.
4. **Forecast reliability is evaluated prospectively.** Across A–F retain predictions as issued **before** their outcomes, with prespecified target and horizon. Where enough comparable untouched independent outcomes exist, assess forecast reliability (e.g., grouped predicted versus observed frequencies or a proper scoring rule) with clustering/uncertainty and coverage; otherwise mark calibration `UNSUPPORTED`. No invented calibration percentage, threshold, significance or power.
5. **Missingness prevents false precision.** In C, include all eligible issued forecasts, completed/missing later probes and option-specific opportunity; report conditional-on-observed diagnostics as such, not population-wide calibration. Missingness cannot silently improve the method ranking.
6. **Confidence responds to contradiction and drift.** In D/E, valid contrary independent retrieval/transfer must trigger uncertainty review, preserve historical predictions and permit reversal. Rubric, delivery, baseline or environment drift cannot be hidden behind a stale high-confidence label; fall back when comparisons are invalid.
7. **Replay and holdout integrity.** In F, immutable forecast snapshots, event-ID deduplication, trusted occurrence/upload chronology, policy/rubric versions, outcome-use lineage and answer-exposure quarantine yield invariant forecasts-as-issued, calibration denominators and mastery under replay permutations. A reused tuning outcome is not untouched validation.
8. **CEFR, interests, agency and accessibility.** Across A–F keep fixed objectives/prerequisites/rubrics/independent exit, due reviews, receptive/productive balance, alternative method sampling and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation. Interest preference or learner choice is not confidence in efficacy; unsupported listening/speaking remains `UNAVAILABLE`, and no sensitive trait inference is required.
9. **Conservative release and evidence separation.** If forecast targets, temporal holdouts, comparable verified exposures, sufficiently delayed unaided retrieval, unfamiliar transfer, follow-up coverage or reliable uncertainty estimates are absent, report method confidence/calibration `UNSUPPORTED` or `UNQUANTIFIED`; preserve narrower valid can-do observations and normal support. A synthetic PASS cannot establish human-learning gains.

## Required evidence and release gate

For A–F publish the **exact tested engine HEAD**, executable command, deterministic clock/seed, immutable synthetic ledger, real supported versioned CEFR can-do and frozen rubric, complete forecast issue-time snapshots and target/horizon definitions, policy versions, eligibility/assignment/verified delivery, first-attempt and assistance history, actual delayed independent retrieval and unseen-context transfer, eligible/completed/missing follow-up, learner/occasion dependence handling, prespecified prospective calibration procedure and uncertainty, leakage/drift diagnostics, replay invariance, review/skill/context coverage and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion. A credible balanced comparator is required for claims about method superiority; never fabricate an unobserved counterfactual, holdout, assignment probability, independent outcome or calibrated percentage.

Any later claim that personalized teaching improves durable independent learning requires separately reviewed longitudinal learner evidence with a credible balanced baseline, sufficiently delayed unaided retrieval and unfamiliar-context transfer, calibration assessed on genuinely later untouched outcomes, heterogeneous learner outcomes and an ethically sound pilot. **Confidence should track evidence quality, not the number of clicks or the tutor's certainty of tone.** Documentation acceptance, if achieved, proves only a specified software invariant.
