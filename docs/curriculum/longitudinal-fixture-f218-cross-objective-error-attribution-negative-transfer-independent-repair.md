# F218 — Cross-objective error attribution, interference, prerequisite repair and independent transfer

**Status:** design-only synthetic *cross-cutting* longitudinal curriculum acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until an independently reviewed, executable exact-head harness exercises the histories and actual teaching decisions. Not a shipped diagnostic engine, proof of an identified cognitive cause, new CEFR can-do, mastery change, learner-type classification or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F214 concerns when to ask an uncertain diagnostic question; F215 concerns language bridging/code-switching; F211 concerns task difficulty; F212 concerns invalid tasks; F217 concerns pragmatic dialogue. **Distinct question in F218:** when similar errors recur across objectives, modalities, contexts and days, can the tutor distinguish a plausible *shared teachable gap* from superficial string similarity, interference, isolated slips, task defects or missing evidence—then choose a targeted, reversible intervention and verify **independent repair and transfer** without silently granting mastery in neighboring CEFR can-dos?

## Fixed objective graph, rubric and causal humility

Every history binds existing, versioned CEFR can-do IDs, prerequisite edges and allowed review dependencies, objective-specific meaning-critical rubric, target language and reviewed accepted varieties, skill family and valid channel, difficulty/context equivalence, assistance/exposure provenance, and unchanged independent mastery and exit standards. The examples below are **synthetic illustrative utterances, not released lessons**. Do not invent or modify the underlying can-do graph, promote an unverified inferred prerequisite to a mandatory gate, change the B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval boundary, or turn a reading result into a speaking result.

Before any diagnosis or teaching choice, record pseudonymous learner, stable learner/session/run/item/event IDs, trusted occurrence chronology and learning-day provenance, objective/rubric/prerequisite/policy/task versions, task intent and validity, actual modality and accessibility, first unaided response, error location and independently reviewed meaning/function, candidate error families with confidence, observed language history **only when legitimately known**, actual hint/model/translation/recast exposure, later retries, and due/available review and skill coverage debt. Record offered versus actually rendered explanations and examples; never treat a queued but undelivered model as exposure. Separate surface similarity from reviewed evidence of a common underlying operation.

The evidence sequence is **objective → task actually rendered → first unaided response → reviewed task-specific error/UNKNOWN → candidate shared gap and competing explanations → diagnostic or teaching option actually delivered → exposed assistance → later distinct-session unaided retrieval → unseen cross-context and cross-objective transfer → updated hypothesis, uncertainty and next delivered choice**. Report due/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators. Deduplicate by stable event identity; out-of-order uploads cannot rewrite first-attempt or exposure history. Missing, corrupt, ambiguous, inaccessible or unreviewed evidence is UNKNOWN, neither mastery nor failure. Latency alone is not an error cause.

An apparent common misconception is a **revisable hypothesis**, not a permanent learner label. A tutor may select a brief contrast, worked example, independent production, accessible listening or a prerequisite review when evidence supports it; it must preserve alternatives and test independent transfer. It cannot infer native language from a name, nationality, accent, device locale or a single phrase; cannot claim that cross-language influence is proven by a matching phrase; and cannot automatically penalize legitimate varieties or task-appropriate alternatives. Interest weights only affect soft topic sampling, never which CEFR objective exists, which errors count, or the proof of independent learning.

**Oracle labels:** OBJECTIVES_VERSIONED, PREREQUISITES_VERSIONED, RUBRIC_MEANING_REVIEWED, FIRST_UNAIDED, SURFACE_MATCH_NOT_CAUSE, CANDIDATE_SHARED_GAP, ALTERNATIVE_CAUSE_OPEN, CROSS_OBJECTIVE_NOT_AUTO_CREDITED, ISOLATED_SLIP_POSSIBLE, TASK_INVALID_UNKNOWN, CHANNEL_INVALID_UNKNOWN, LANGUAGE_HISTORY_NOT_INFERRED, VARIANT_ACCEPTED, HINT_OR_MODEL_EXPOSED, TARGETED_REPAIR_DELIVERED, REPAIR_ASSISTED_ONLY, CLEAN_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_CROSS_CONTEXT_TRANSFER, CROSS_OBJECTIVE_PROBE_REQUIRED, PREREQUISITE_DEBT_RETAINED, COVERAGE_DEBT_RETAINED, INTEREST_METHOD_SEPARATE, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, HYPOTHESIS_REVISED, BALANCED_FALLBACK, NOT_COMPARABLE and UNSUPPORTED. Labels describe observations and decisions, never fixed learner identities.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories and matched controls

**A — Repeated similar strings do not automatically prove one misconception.** A learner produces “I have 20 years” on a self-introduction writing task and later “I have 19 years” in a different written self-description; both are independently produced and meaning-reviewed as age-expression errors under their fixed tasks. A third task has “I have two sisters,” which is valid possession. Unsafe policy groups every occurrence of “I have” as wrong, assumes the learner's native language and awards a shared-gap diagnosis with certainty. **Expected:** preserve two independently reviewed age-expression observations as a *candidate* reusable gap, accept valid possession, retain competing explanations (translation, limited exposure, memorized pattern) and select a reviewed contrast or unaided diagnostic as actually delivered. Matched control: one age-expression error is a copied tutor example or an invalid prompt; it cannot strengthen the independent pattern. A later unseen age expression in a different context, unaided and after meaningful delay, is required to test repair; it cannot automatically award the distinct possession objective.

**B — One spelling pattern can mask different causes and skills.** In one valid written past-event task the learner independently writes “goed”; in another they write “walked,” correctly; a third speech task yields an uncertain ASR transcript “goed” without reviewable audio. Unsafe policy declares all past-tense forms unlearned, downgrades oral ability and drills only the favorite topic. **Expected:** a reviewed irregular-past error may motivate targeted practice, but correct regular-past evidence remains valid; the uncertain ASR transcript is CHANNEL_INVALID_UNKNOWN for oral scoring, not a third independent grammatical failure. Matched control: independently reviewed audio actually contains an irregular-past error in a comparable oral task; it may inform a speaking-specific hypothesis, but written and spoken mastery obligations stay distinct. Neither history proves a single cognitive cause or allows silent CEFR exit.

**C — A prerequisite may explain difficulty, but must not become an invented gate.** A learner independently fails a valid multi-clause request task requiring a time reference, while passing the same request function with a simpler time phrase. A versioned prerequisite map lists time-reference comprehension as relevant; the first failure could also reflect task complexity or an ambiguous instruction. **Expected:** retain the original can-do and prerequisite versions, examine validity and comparable opportunities, then offer an eligible targeted time-reference review or short diagnostic *without* rewriting the curriculum graph or declaring all higher objectives locked. Matched control: the time reference is explicitly supplied and understood, but the learner still fails the request function; the shared prerequisite hypothesis must weaken and the tutor may switch toward request formulation. Later success in the prerequisite does not automatically satisfy the advanced request can-do; independently assess both where required.

**D — A correct assisted repair is not proof of the root cause or durable transfer.** Session 1: first unaided attempt “She don't like coffee” on a valid third-person present-tense task. The tutor renders a worked example and the learner repeats “She doesn't like coffee.” Session 2 on the same learning day repeats the identical example correctly. Session 3, after a meaningful interval, independently produces “My brother doesn't work on Fridays” in a new neutral context; session 4 independently fails a different unseen third-person task. **Expected:** preserve first-attempt error, model exposure and assisted repairs; same-day repeated items do not create delayed retrieval. Record both later clean outcomes with uncertainty and review debt; do not assert the worked example caused the improvement. Matched control: replace session 3 with a model-exposed response, leaving only the later independent failure; the hypothesis must become more cautious and the next actual teaching choice may change. A future unfamiliar production probe is needed before declaring stable transfer.

**E — Legitimate variants, invalid prompts and access faults must not manufacture an error cluster.** Two reviewed, meaning-equivalent requests use different acceptable target-language varieties; a third task's instructions contradict one another; a fourth listening task never plays audio and shows a transcript instead. Unsafe policy clusters all deviations from its preferred exact answer as one learner misconception, marks listening failed and selects permanent remedial drills. **Expected:** VARIANT_ACCEPTED for reviewed alternatives, TASK_INVALID_UNKNOWN for contradictory instructions, CHANNEL_INVALID_UNKNOWN for absent audio, no punitive misconception count and visible listening/coverage debt. Matched control: replace only the invalid task with a valid independently reviewed meaning-critical error; only that new eligible observation may inform a tentative gap. Interest weights (gaming versus cooking) may alter context frequency but cannot change rubric, error attribution, evidence eligibility, required modalities or neutral/unfamiliar rotation.

**F — Contradictory longitudinal outcomes must reverse or temper the *delivered* intervention.** Two plausible teaching options target a candidate shared gap: a short contrastive explanation and independent retrieval with minimal hints. Across distinct sessions, matched tasks and accessible channels, the learner has two clean delayed unseen successes after contrastive explanations, then two clean delayed failures under a changed context; a separate assisted success is uploaded twice. The event arrival order is 4, 2, duplicate assisted event, 1, 3; a claimed fifth success has no trusted prompt/exposure chronology. **Expected:** EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED; quarantine the untrusted claim; count eligible successes and failures without converting assisted repeats into mastery. Reassess the shared-gap and method hypotheses with uncertainty, preserve coverage and due/missing denominators, and log an eligible next option **actually rendered** (including justified balanced exploration). Matched control reverses the independently reviewed later outcomes while preserving task difficulty, assistance, review intervals and exposure; the decision policy must be capable of changing its next delivered intervention or explaining a calibrated balanced fallback. If comparable opportunity and exposure cannot be established, label NOT_COMPARABLE rather than claim causal superiority.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Stable CEFR and prerequisite boundaries:** A–F bind existing versioned objectives, prerequisite edges, independently reviewed task rubrics, supported skills and modalities, meaningful-turn ceilings where relevant, and unchanged mastery/exit criteria. No invented can-do or mandatory gate; Pre-A1, A1–C2 and the B2 Arc 6 boundary remain unchanged.
2. **Error identity is not error cause:** A separates valid possession from two meaning-reviewed age-expression errors; surface strings, demographics, device locale and unverified first-language assumptions cannot become a fixed cognitive diagnosis. Competing explanations and uncertainty remain explicit.
3. **Cross-skill evidence is not pooled blindly:** B distinguishes written irregular-past evidence, valid regular-past evidence, uncertain ASR and reviewed oral performance. A result in one skill never silently certifies another.
4. **Prerequisite intervention without curricular shortcuts:** C uses only versioned prerequisite relations, distinguishes task complexity/validity from missing knowledge, preserves the original can-do and requires independent evidence before claiming prerequisite or advanced-objective mastery.
5. **Assistance and delayed independent repair:** D preserves first attempt, actually rendered example, assisted repetition, same-day non-independent practice, distinct-session delayed unaided success, contradictory unseen failure and subsequent review debt. Neither copied correctness nor one successful later item proves durable mastery or causal method benefit.
6. **Validity, variants and accessibility:** E accepts reviewed legitimate varieties, quarantines contradictory tasks and absent audio, preserves accessible teaching and modality-specific coverage, and never converts UNKNOWN into a learner failure or pass.
7. **Interest-method and context independence:** Matched E changes only soft topic weights; fixed objective, rubric, difficulty, error attribution, method confidence and skill evidence remain invariant. Preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts stay eligible.
8. **Replay, denominators and reversible delivery:** F publishes stable event IDs, trusted chronology, duplicate/unknown handling, due/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-ups, eligible cross-objective probes, alternatives, uncertainty, and the **actually delivered** next instructional action. Contradictory clean outcomes can revise a hypothesis without permanent learner labels.
9. **Executable and human-efficacy boundaries:** Publish machine-readable A–F histories plus matched controls, deterministic seed/clock, exact objective/rubric/prerequisite/task/policy/channel versions, independently reviewed meaning/error oracles, per-event exposure/skill/coverage ledgers, next delivered choice, and per-assertion PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED from an independently reviewed exact-head executable harness. Synthetic PASS can establish software invariants only. A claim of improved durable human learning requires a separately reviewed longitudinal pilot against a balanced accessible baseline, comparable opportunities, delayed unaided retrieval, unseen cross-objective transfer, missingness/fairness analyses and no hidden loss in other required skills. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** F218 makes **cross-objective error clustering versus evidence-grounded, reversible targeted repair** falsifiable. A strong tutor can notice patterns and choose useful explanations without inventing causes, punishing technical failures, bypassing CEFR obligations or mistaking copied corrections for independent learning. **UNSUPPORTED** until independently executed and reviewed; no real-world efficacy claim is made.
