# F83 — Conditional commitments, contingency revision and independent communicative transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a shipped conditional-language evaluator, CEFR certification, reproduced production defect or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F31 protects against transient learner state masquerading as method effects; F33 requires genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish assigned versus actually delivered instruction and tutor-origin cues; F47/F48 preserve source-defined noncompensatory mastery; F49 protects accessibility equivalence; F50 protects topic breadth; F53 accepts valid language varieties; F72 tracks task constraints; F74 protects source fidelity; F75 covers argumentation; F80 covers authoritative asynchronous updates; F81 covers quantifier/negation/exception scope; F82 covers temporal reference, event order and aspect. **F83 isolates a different source-required communicative construct:** independently understanding, expressing and *revising what someone should do when a stated condition is or is not met*, without mistaking a conditional possibility for a confirmed fact, a prerequisite for a guarantee, or a hypothetical alternative for an event that happened. Do not invent formal-logic examination, a universal English conditional paradigm, risk-taking norms, numerical probability calculation or specific grammatical morphology as CEFR requirements. Apply only to a real versioned CEFR-linked descriptor/can-do and communicative task whose outcome requires a conditional distinction.

## Controlled setup

Resolve a **real versioned** CEFR descriptor/can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, source-defined task/outcome, target language and accepted varieties, source-valid condition readings, rubric/evaluator versions, assistance policy, independent threshold, due reviews, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer and exit gate. Freeze source-authored condition text, its authority and scope, antecedent status (`TRUE`, `FALSE`, `UNKNOWN`, `CONFLICTING`), consequence or permitted action, exceptions, modality (required/permitted/possible), actual versus hypothetical event status, and any source-valid ambiguity. Track `CONDITION_MET`, `CONDITION_NOT_MET`, `CONDITION_UNKNOWN`, `CONSEQUENCE_REQUIRED`, `CONSEQUENCE_PERMITTED`, `CONSEQUENCE_NOT_ENTAILED`, `PREREQUISITE_NOT_SUFFICIENT`, `HYPOTHETICAL_ONLY`, `REVISION_REQUIRED`, `SOURCE_AMBIGUOUS` and `NOT_APPLICABLE` as task-state annotations, **not** learner-facing terminology or universally valid logical translations. A natural-language “unless” or “only if” can have context-dependent pragmatic force; the source-authored adjudication must preserve legitimate readings rather than force one abstract formula.

Predeclare feasible reversible instruction candidates `CONDITION_OUTCOME_CONTRAST`, `BRANCHING_DIALOGUE_REHEARSAL`, `CONTEXTUAL_COUNTEREXAMPLE`, `LISTEN_AND_REPLAN`, `OPEN_UNSEEN_CONTINGENCY_TASK` and `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. Vary actually implementable and evaluable listening/reading/writing/speaking/free production, explanation, scaffolding, pacing, ordering, example context and spaced review. **Never** vary shared CEFR objectives, source-valid interpretations, source-required receptive/productive evidence, independent threshold or exit gate. A tutor-rendered completed decision tree, answer-bearing counterexample, highlighted prerequisite or scripted replan contaminates independence for the affected learner move; a policy-assigned but unrendered cue does not. Legitimate interlocutor clarification belongs to the task when allowed; an interlocutor who reveals the target decision outside the protocol is coaching. Rotate preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts, and continue sampling alternatives rather than classifying learners as fixed “logical,” “visual,” “auditory” or other types.

Use deterministic pseudonymous multi-session histories with injected seed/clock, immutable session-qualified event IDs, frozen source/task/rubric hashes and exact authored source utterances. Record condition authority, antecedent observations and changes, valid interpretations, first learner-authored decision/conditional statement, actual task outcome, selected/assigned/**actually rendered** instructional method and tutor-cue chronology; first-attempt quality, retries, hint dependence, recurring error classes, optional calibrated latency, practice dose, topic familiarity, channel, accessibility and language variety; later independent delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen transfer; eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators. Separate linguistic misunderstanding from missing facts, task-domain expertise, numeric risk judgment, device delays, inaccessible audio, ASR noise, ambiguous policy wording and changed source authority. Do not score a lucky yes/no choice, rote “if” sentence or tutor-supplied plan as independent conditional competence.

Evidence labels: `SOURCE_VALID_CONDITIONAL_TASK`, `ANTECEDENT_STATUS_RESOLVED`, `CONDITION_CONSEQUENCE_PRESERVED`, `NECESSARY_NOT_SUFFICIENT`, `HYPOTHETICAL_NOT_ASSERTED`, `CONDITION_CHANGE_REPLANNED`, `SOURCE_AMBIGUITY_RETAINED`, `FIRST_INDEPENDENT_CONDITIONAL_DECISION`, `TUTOR_BRANCH_CUE_EXPOSED`, `ASSISTED_CONDITIONAL_DECISION`, `INTERLOCUTOR_TASK_CLARIFICATION`, `INTERLOCUTOR_COACHING_LEAK`, `INDEPENDENT_DELAYED_CONDITIONAL_RETRIEVAL`, `INDEPENDENT_UNSEEN_CONDITIONAL_TRANSFER`, `DOMAIN_KNOWLEDGE_CONFOUNDED`, `TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED`, `ACCESSIBILITY_CONFOUNDED`, `FOLLOWUP_MISSING`, `EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED`, `VERSION_CONFLICT_QUARANTINED`, `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. Labels describe bounded evidence, not learner identities or permanent traits.

## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — A condition is not a promise.** A source-defined reservation task says “If the room becomes available, we **may** offer a later slot”; the room is still unconfirmed. Learner A reports “The room is available and the slot is booked,” confusing condition, possibility and fact. Learner B independently says that an offer is possible **only if** availability is confirmed and asks for confirmation. Accept meaning-equivalent target-language forms without requiring English modal verbs. Do not make an invented booking policy or domain knowledge part of the CEFR gate.
- **B — Prerequisite versus guarantee.** A source-defined application says “You can be considered only if the form is complete”; completing the form does **not** guarantee acceptance. Learner A tells another person that a complete form ensures acceptance. Learner B independently preserves the necessary-but-not-sufficient relation and communicates the correct next step. A separate source may use “if” pragmatically as an assurance; preserve that source-valid interpretation instead of imposing a universal formal-logic template. F81 quantifier/exception scope alone does not settle this prerequisite-versus-guarantee distinction.
- **C — A changed condition requires a changed plan.** A multi-turn task says to use the outdoor venue if weather permits, otherwise the indoor room. After the learner's initial correct independent plan, an authoritative update says the outdoor venue is unavailable. Learner A repeats the original plan; learner B independently revises the instructions for the intended recipient and marks what is confirmed versus pending. If the tutor rendered “Switch to the indoor room now” before the revision, the revision is assisted even if the update arrived in storage later. A permitted interlocutor asking “Is the outdoor venue still available?” is not automatically answer-bearing coaching.
- **D — Early method winner loses at delayed transfer.** On matched first unaided tasks, `BRANCHING_DIALOGUE_REHEARSAL` appears better than `CONTEXTUAL_COUNTEREXAMPLE` for a learner. After meaningful delay, new conditional tasks with unfamiliar antecedents and changed information show better independent outcomes after counterexamples, while the branching advantage disappears. Match or disclose condition complexity, source ambiguity, prerequisite knowledge, language variety, task stakes, topic familiarity, practice dose, feedback, channel, accessibility and rubric version. Lower confidence, explore alternatives and log the **actually rendered** next method; never assign a permanent “branching learner” label.
- **E — Interests and modality do not replace broad ability.** A learner correctly handles “if the quest unlocks” in a favorite game but mistakes an unconfirmed clinic appointment for a confirmed one in a neutral context and fails to revise an unfamiliar travel plan after a changed condition. Another learner independently completes accessible written conditional tasks; no speaking/listening evidence exists. Rotate favorite, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts; preserve all source-required receptive/productive mastery and due reviews. Do not mistake caption-provided answer branches, unfamiliar local practices, accessibility accommodations or speech-recognition failures for independent mastery or deficiency.
- **F — Offline replay, conflicting authority and missing follow-up.** Two offline sessions reuse a local event ID; a device clock shifts; a tutor's completed branch was actually displayed before the learner's first decision but uploaded later; two source versions disagree about whether an exception applies; a rubric revision changes accepted conditional readings; and an offered delayed unseen probe is never completed. Deduplicate only true session-qualified replays, rely on trusted source/cue provenance rather than receipt time, quarantine irreconcilable authority/rubric conflicts, preserve unknown antecedents and missing follow-up. Receipt-order permutations must yield identical independence labels, review debt and next-method rationale.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-valid communicative construct before scoring.** For A–F emit versioned CEFR can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, source task/outcome, language/variety, authoritative condition text and accepted readings, rubric/evaluator, assistance, independence threshold, delayed/unseen probes, due reviews and exit gate. Missing source mapping or invented logic/morphology/risk requirements yields `UNSUPPORTED`.
2. **Conditional possibility versus established fact.** In A/C distinguish antecedent `TRUE`/`FALSE`/`UNKNOWN` from consequence, permission and actual confirmation. Credit source-valid paraphrases that preserve conditional force and communicative outcome; reject fluent but unsupported assertions of fact. Do not infer mastery from the presence of “if.”
3. **Necessary conditions and hypothetical status.** In B/E distinguish prerequisite from guarantee, and hypothetical or counterfactual planning from events that actually occurred, only when source-required. Accept valid target-language forms without English tense or modal templates; do not demand formal symbolic logic from learners.
4. **Updates, exceptions and legitimate ambiguity.** In C/F recompute the communicative plan from the authoritative *updated* condition and source-defined exception scope, preserving unresolved/contradictory facts and valid clarification. Do not fill unknown conditions from device time, cultural assumptions, user interests or an older source version.
5. **Actually rendered cue and interlocutor provenance.** In C/E/F compare trusted tutor-cue render chronology with the first learner-authored decision/replan. A displayed answer-bearing branch, caption or replan makes that move assisted; assigned-but-unseen material does not. Separate legitimate task clarification from answer-leaking coaching.
6. **Independent delayed unseen transfer with denominators.** In C/D/F require meaningful session separation, new source conditions and outcomes, first unaided conditional decisions, a completed source-defined communicative outcome and eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Immediate rehearsals, cosmetic substitutions, copied branch plans and absent follow-up cannot prove durable independent mastery.
7. **Comparable methods and nonexclusive interests.** In D/E match or disclose condition complexity, domain knowledge, ambiguity, stakes, topic, practice dose, feedback, modality, accessibility, variety and rubric. Protect common CEFR objectives, noncompensatory receptive/productive evidence, due reviews and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic exposure; interests only softly bias example selection.
8. **Reversible adaptation and replay invariance.** In C/D/E/F emit feasible alternatives, selected/assigned/actually rendered method, later independent outcomes, uncertainty/confounds, exploration and next-choice rationale. Sparse or confounded data use `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. Deduplicate session-qualified replays; freeze authority/rubric versions; prove receipt-order invariance for assistance, conditional state, skill/review debt and next choice, quarantining conflicts.
9. **Assertion-level audit without efficacy inflation.** For A–F emit source/task/rubric/evaluator IDs, condition/status and authority annotations, first/final learner-authored decisions, actual rendered assistance, communicative outcomes, delayed/unseen denominators, confounds, replay equivalence and `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED` per assertion. Documentation CI does not establish a shipped evaluator, CEFR certification or improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, deterministic seed/clock and multi-session histories, real source-resolved CEFR-linked conditional tasks, multilingual/variety-aware adjudicated rubrics, frozen authoritative antecedents and updates, trusted rendered-cue provenance, first independent decisions and source-defined outcomes, independently completed delayed unseen transfer with denominators, comparable method/topic/modality/access exposure, protected skill/review obligations, replay-order permutations, reversible next-method traces and assertion-level verdicts. Missing source mapping, authoritative condition, validated reading, reliable modality or follow-up remains `UNSUPPORTED`/unknown, never invented proficiency.

Any claim that adaptive instruction **improves durable independent conditional communication** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, delayed unseen tasks, multilingual/variety and accessibility evaluator validation, human adjudication, privacy and subgroup-harm review and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor helps a learner communicate what follows *if* circumstances change—and what does not yet follow—rather than merely reproduce conditional sentences.**
