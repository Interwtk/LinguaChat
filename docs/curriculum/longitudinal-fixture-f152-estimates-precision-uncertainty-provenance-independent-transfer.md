## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Exact verified count versus informal estimate.** A reviewed room inventory confirms **38 chairs counted** at 09:00. An organizer separately says “We expect **about 40 guests**,” without a verified guest list. Learner A tells a colleague “There are exactly 40 guests and 40 chairs”; learner B independently says “We counted 38 chairs this morning; the organizer estimates around 40 guests, so seating may be short.” The second statement communicates the difference without asserting a guaranteed shortage or exact attendance. Counterfactual: if the organizer later supplies a verified 40-person registration count, that changes the status of registrations, not necessarily actual arrivals. Do not conflate a source's estimate with an exact inventory or infer that a count remains current forever.

**B — Forecast interval versus enforceable limit.** A courier's reviewed notice says “Estimated delivery: **three to five business days**”; a different reviewed service agreement says “Guaranteed **no later than Friday**,” with a clearly stated business-day calendar. Learner A reports that the courier guarantees arrival by day five; learner B independently tells the recipient “The courier estimates three to five business days, but that is not a guarantee. The other agreement states a Friday deadline.” Counterfactual: without a defined starting day, timezone, holiday policy or legal meaning of “guaranteed,” request clarification rather than compute a definite date or promise compensation. The interval is not proof that all days are equally likely, nor that the midpoint is the expected date.

**C — Rounded measurement versus exact decimal.** A reviewed route measurement is reported as **2.5 km rounded to the nearest 0.1 km**; the raw measured value and uncertainty instrument are not supplied. Learner A says “The distance is exactly 2.500 km”; learner B independently says “The route is approximately 2.5 km according to the reported measurement.” With the stated rounding convention alone, a conventional underlying rounded value may lie from 2.45 up to but not including 2.55 km, but this rounding bin is **not** a formal measurement-error interval or probability statement. Counterfactual: if the source merely says “2.5 km” without a rounding rule, do not invent that bin. A language error is distinguishable from an inaccessible unit conversion or arithmetic challenge; use source-provided normalized figures where appropriate.

**D — Source method, revision and incompatible uncertainty types.** An organizer estimates **80–100 attendees** from prior events at 08:00; a ticket system later reports **92 confirmed registrations** at 15:00. Learner A says “The final attendance was exactly 92, proving the estimate was accurate”; learner B independently reports “The earlier attendance estimate was 80–100, while 92 people were registered as of 15:00; actual attendance is not yet known.” Counterfactual: an updated organizer estimate of 95–110 might conflict with the registration count without either being false, because they measure different constructs. Preserve the methods and timestamps; ask whether registrations include cancellations, guests or walk-ins before claiming a contradiction. A later source does not retroactively make earlier first-authored claims dishonest if the earlier source was accurately attributed.

**E — Rendered multimodal evidence and authorship order.** Authoritative audio says “**roughly fifteen to fifty** participants may attend”; ASR renders “fifty participants,” captions omit “roughly,” a translation turns “may attend” into “will attend,” and an accessible chart labels the range “15–50” with a prewritten summary “expected attendance: 15–50.” Record the actual channel presented and its source fidelity. If a learner independently asks “Was that fifteen to fifty, and is it an estimate?” at 10:00 before an answer-bearing model renders at 10:01, the first authored move remains independent; the subsequent edited model-shaped answer is assisted. Reading a transcript is not evidence of hearing the range; an inaccessible dash or ambiguous spoken number quarantines only affected constructs. Autocomplete, screen-reader ordering, highlighted interval and previews count as exposure if they reveal the intended response. Replaying one session-qualified event never creates new delayed evidence.

**F — Longitudinal, uncertain method selection and genuine transfer.** Fictional learner A gives immediate correct hedges after EXACT_VS_ESTIMATED_MINIMAL_PAIRS but later, unaided, turns a weather forecast range into a guarantee in a different recipient handoff. Comparable actually delivered RANGE_TYPE_CONTRAST_DIALOGUE trials precede later first-unaided source-correct communication about a service estimate; the hypothesis may shift toward the dialogue method **only** with repeated comparable delayed evidence. Fictional learner B initially benefits from ROUNDING_PRECISION_REPHRASE_THEN_FADE but later invents precise bounds for an unqualified estimate; confidence falls and balanced alternatives resume. If one method always uses clearly labelled forecasts and another uses conflicting, method-unknown ranges, or one supplies a ready-made hedge, flag confounding and infer **no winner**. Changing “40 guests” to “45 guests” in the same worksheet is not unseen transfer. Track due/offered/rendered/completed/missing probes separately; missing is not failure. A learner interested in gaming may more often see player-count estimates, but neutral community, transport, workplace, health, adjacent and unfamiliar contexts remain reachable without altering the CEFR objective or rubric.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real CEFR objective and independently reviewed source oracle.** A–F bind an existing versioned can-do, recipient/purpose, reviewed source quantity/type/method/time/precision, language/variety/register, accessible stimulus, rubric, first-unaided threshold, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and unchanged exit gate. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED, not fabricated level mastery or statistical certification.
2. **Exactness and approximation integrity.** A separates 38 verified chairs from around 40 estimated guests, does not convert expectations to actual attendance, and preserves source timestamps and recipient-relevant caveats.
3. **Range-type and commitment integrity.** B distinguishes forecast intervals from guarantees or policy bounds; rejects invented probabilities, midpoint expectations, deadlines, timezone/holiday assumptions and legal commitments without an explicit reviewed source.
4. **Precision and rounding honesty.** C preserves source-supplied rounding, never upgrades 2.5 km to exact 2.500 km, and never treats a rounding bin as an instrument confidence interval. If rounding is unknown, credit qualified language rather than invented precision.
5. **Method, update and referent integrity.** D distinguishes projected attendance, confirmed registrations and actual attendance; tracks source method/time, avoids false conflict or retroactive correction, and asks for missing method/completeness details.
6. **Actually delivered accessible, chronological and idempotent evidence.** E reconciles authoritative with rendered audio/ASR/captions/translations/charts/screen readers/autocomplete, quarantines affected constructs only, orders first authorship before rendered help, and deduplicates session-qualified events. Reading cannot certify unheard listening.
7. **Controlled, uncertain, reversible adaptation.** F compares feasible actually delivered methods with matched uncertainty type, source completeness, arithmetic burden, precision, language/variety/register, modality, topic, help and follow-up. Sparse, contradictory or confounded evidence yields no winner and balanced exploration; later regression lowers confidence.
8. **Delayed first-unaided transfer and nonexclusive interests.** Assisted hedges, highlighted estimates, immediate repeats and number-only swaps cannot earn delayed/unseen mastery. Require later distinct eligible session, genuinely new source/recipient/context and uncertainty type, reviewed oracle, first-unaided recipient-correct communication and explicit missingness. Preserve shared objectives, receptive/productive coverage and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation.
9. **Honest support, efficacy and release boundary.** A documentation commit or green CI does not establish executable engine support, outcome-driven method changes or human retention improvement. Require exact-head executable assertions against real objectives, logged actually delivered policy changes and delayed independent transfer versus a balanced baseline, followed by ethically reviewed learner evidence before efficacy claims.
