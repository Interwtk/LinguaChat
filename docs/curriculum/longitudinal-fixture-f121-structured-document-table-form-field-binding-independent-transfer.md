# F121 — Structured documents, table/form binding and independent communicative transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run on a named exact engine HEAD against real versioned CEFR-linked tasks, independently source-validated multilingual/accessible layouts and delayed first-unaided transfer. This does **not** ship a table parser, OCR validator, form autofill system, accessibility conformance certificate, CEFR mastery or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 protects genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish rendered help and tutor-origin errors; F49 protects accessible construct validity; F50 preserves nonexclusive interests; F71 covers general discourse cohesion; F72 tracks task constraints; F82/F95 cover temporal meaning; F103 covers critical-detail readback; F104 isolates method from difficulty; F107 handles unreliable feedback; F112 covers TTS noise; F114 covers spatial deixis; F118 covers quantifier/conditional scope; F120 covers source attribution. **Distinct construct:** can a learner independently recover and communicate **which source-authored value belongs to which row, column, field, section, label, unit and qualification** in a structured document, and transfer that mapping to a genuinely different document without copying a tutor's highlighted answer? The construct is communicative interpretation of source-valid structure, not a general intelligence, numeracy, visual-spatial ability or reading-speed diagnosis.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Correct number, wrong row.** A fictional class timetable has rows for Tuesday and Thursday and columns for start time and room. Tuesday starts at 09:00 in Room B; Thursday starts at 10:00 in Room A. Asked independently, “Where is Thursday's class?”, learner A says “Room B” after selecting the correct *column* but wrong *row*; learner B says “Room A” and can identify the relevant binding. A counterfactual swaps the room cells while keeping headers, question, task objective and level unchanged: the expected answer must change. A learner who says only “10:00” has extracted a real value but has not answered the requested location. Do not conflate time comprehension with table binding or award mastery because the right value appeared somewhere on the page.

**B — Form labels, placeholders and empty fields.** A source-authored fictional registration form displays “Contact email (optional)”, “Emergency contact (required)” and a pale example placeholder “name@example.test” inside an empty field. Learner A treats the placeholder as submitted user data and calls the optional field mandatory; learner B correctly distinguishes label, required marker, example and actual value. In a counterfactual the source changes “optional” to “required” and supplies a genuine filled value; acceptable interpretation changes. Preserve language-specific conventions for required markers and field order, including right-to-left/vertical layouts when validated. The task assesses understanding and communication of form instructions, not actually collecting or submitting personal information.

**C — Merged headers, footnotes and units.** A fictional museum schedule has a merged “Weekend” header spanning Saturday/Sunday, separate adult/student price columns and a footnote “Student price applies with valid student ID”. Asked for the Saturday student admission price **without** an ID, a learner must not report the student number as an unconditional entitlement. A separate source-valid task asks only what the displayed student price is; there a direct price can be correct without claiming eligibility. A counterfactual moves the footnote marker to the adult column: the applicable qualification must follow the source, not a memorized rule. Distinguish currency/unit from quantity, blank from zero, and visually adjacent but structurally unrelated cells. If source authors have not validated a merged-cell interpretation, mark the affected answer UNSUPPORTED.

**D — Rendering, OCR and accessible reading order.** A source-valid table has a header row and a total row. A cropped phone screenshot removes the leftmost row labels, while OCR linearization places the total under an ordinary item. A learner using the screenshot or OCR selects the only value they could reasonably associate with the visible prompt. Record RENDER_CROP or OCR_LAYOUT_CONFLICT and quarantine affected learner-deficit claims; do not silently grade against an unseen complete source. A validated screen-reader rendering that preserves header-to-cell associations may supply construct-equivalent **reading** evidence when permitted, but must not be relabeled as unaided visual-layout skill. In another variant a translation changes “per person” to “per group”: quarantine the affected unit binding until the source-authorized representation is restored. Never assume the default visual rendering is the ground truth of what the learner actually encountered.

**E — Adaptive instruction needs comparable delayed outcomes.** Fictional learner A answers assisted highlighted-row worksheets correctly but later misbinds values in unfamiliar first-unaided timetables. After **actually delivered** ROW_COLUMN_TRACE_WITH_FADING, A independently answers delayed new timetable questions accurately. Learner B improves instead after FIELD_LABEL_PARAPHRASE on comparable documents. Compare real CEFR objective, baseline, document genre, number of rows/columns, header complexity, footnotes, target language/variety, accessibility, topic familiarity, assistance actually rendered, review interval, task stakes and attrition. If tracing is always taught on simple two-row tables and paraphrase on complex merged headers, mark METHOD_DOCUMENT_COMPLEXITY_CONFOUNDED and infer no winning method. Sufficient comparable longitudinal evidence may change the **next actually delivered** method for A versus B with uncertainty bounds; contradictory later evidence reduces confidence and permits reversal. Preserve receptive/productive skills and neutral, adjacent and unfamiliar contexts rather than teaching only the learner's favorite topic.

**F — Version, provenance, replay and unseen transfer.** A fictional event program lists Workshop X in Room 2. Learner's first unaided answer is captured against source version v1; later v2 moves it to Room 4. Keep v1 evidence scored against v1, and v2 against v2. An offline submission is uploaded twice: deduplicate by immutable session-qualified event ID; a local row ID reused in another document must not cross-bind values. A tutor highlights Room 2 **after** the first response: that first answer remains unaided, while a later copied response is assisted. A purported delayed probe with only renamed rooms but identical layout, distractors and answer positions is not genuinely unseen transfer. A truly new source-authored form with different label grouping, footnote scope and question purpose can qualify if independently completed after a meaningful interval. Missing follow-up remains FOLLOWUP_MISSING, never fabricated success or failure.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real CEFR/task and source structure linkage.** A–F resolve versioned descriptor, blueprint, prerequisites, source document and effective version, validated row/column/field/section relationships, target-language reading order, rubric/evaluator, first-unaided threshold, accommodations, protected skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Do not invent advanced data-literacy prerequisites.
2. **Value-to-label binding, not number spotting.** A/B distinguish header, row, column, field, placeholder, submitted value and question target. A value found elsewhere in the document does not earn the requested communicative answer; source-authorized equivalents do.
3. **Qualification, scope and unit preservation.** B/C require correct optional/required markers, merged-header and footnote scope, units, blank/zero/unknown distinctions and condition-dependent interpretation. No unsupported eligibility or external action follows from a price or form field.
4. **Multilingual and accessible structure validity.** B/C/D validate language/variety, reading direction and equivalent accessible representations against the source. Crop, OCR, translation and linearization conflicts quarantine affected claims rather than manufacturing learner errors or accessibility deficits.
5. **First-unaided provenance versus rendered assistance.** A/E/F bind the first learner-authored answer to the exact effective document and what was actually rendered before it. Queued, post-answer or undelivered highlighting is not retroactive assistance; copied tutor answers and retries cannot establish independent mastery.
6. **Source/version, document and event isolation.** C/D/F retain source-effective versions, stable document-qualified cell/field IDs and render hashes; deduplicate replayed events and quarantine irreconcilable conflicts. Never migrate a v2 answer key or a second document's reused ID into a v1 attempt.
7. **Delayed independent retention and genuinely unseen transfer.** A–F require meaningful separated opportunities and new source-authored documents with noncosmetic structure, genre, questions and distractors, without answer-bearing help. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; changed labels with identical answer paths do not suffice.
8. **Reversible method selection and soft interest rotation.** E/F log feasible, assigned and **actually delivered** methods, comparable baseline/complexity, language, modality, assistance, uncertainty, exploration, topic diversity and missingness. Preserve protected listening/reading/writing/speaking/free production, reviews and exit gates. No permanent “visual learner” classification or interest-exclusive examples.
9. **Exact-head reproducibility and bounded claims.** A–F emit deterministic seed/clock, named engine HEAD and runnable command, descriptor/source/document/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, source-validated bindings, original/rendered representations, first/assisted answers, render/event ledger, counterfactuals, delayed/unseen denominators and **PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED for each assertion**. Documentation CI alone is not an executable structure evaluator, reproducible product defect or proof of learning gains.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and reproducible command; real versioned CEFR-linked tasks and independently validated multilingual document structure; source-effective row/column/field/footnote/unit bindings; actual accessible stimulus and assistance chronology; first-unaided evidence; meaningful delayed independent retrieval and genuinely unseen cross-document transfer; comparable or transparently confounded instructional methods; protected skill/review/exit obligations; eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators; crop/OCR/translation/replay/version permutations; and per-assertion verdicts. Missing source structure, unreliable render, unvalidated accessible equivalence or absent follow-up leaves the affected claim **UNSUPPORTED/unknown**, while independently established partial communication evidence is retained.

A stronger claim that adaptive structured-document instruction **improves durable independent communication** requires a separately reviewed multilingual, multivariety, accessibility-validated real-learner longitudinal study with credible comparison, attrition and subgroup-harm analysis, privacy review and reproducible independent outcomes. **A strong tutor teaches the learner to read what each value actually means in context, not merely to pick a familiar-looking number or copy a highlighted cell.**

**Boundary:** curricular acceptance documentation only. No Pre-A1 edits, A1–C2 release, engine, Auth, cloud, infrastructure, production, Supabase, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge changes. Integration/watchdog alone aggregates owner actions.
