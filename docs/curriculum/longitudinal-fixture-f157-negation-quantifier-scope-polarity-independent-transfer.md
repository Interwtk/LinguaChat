Avoid legal, medical or high-stakes eligibility conclusions.

Freeze a **versioned, independently reviewed scope oracle** per stimulus: source and revision/effective time; closed/open reference set and its members when known; speaker/recipient; exact utterance and punctuation/prosody when actually delivered; whether quantification is universal, existential, lower/upper bound, exclusive or distributive; negation placement and its semantic scope; grammatical vs pragmatic implications; explicit exception set; polarity-item licensing in the named language/variety; known unknowns; alternative readings and warranted clarification. Separate logical entailment from likely conversational inference. In particular, *not all* entails a counterexample when the relevant nonempty set and ordinary universal reading are established, but **does not by itself guarantee that any member satisfied the predicate**. *Only registered participants may enter* makes registration necessary under that rule; it does not promise admission to everyone registered. A source saying *no rooms are available* does not mean no rooms exist. Do not treat an incomplete list, unknown attendance roster, or ambiguous recording as complete evidence.

Independently review natural target-language realizations. English *not everyone came*, *no one came*, *everyone didn't come* (potentially scope-ambiguous), *I didn't see anyone*, *only Ana signed* and *Ana wasn't the only one who signed* are not interchangeable. Spanish *no todos vinieron*, *nadie vino*, *no vi a nadie* (ordinary negative concord, not a double-positive), *solo Ana firmó* and *Ana no fue la única que firmó* preserve different meanings. Do not apply English double-negation heuristics to Spanish, or declare a universal cross-language rule for negative concord, focus particles or ambiguous scope. Accept natural semantically equivalent paraphrases and contextually justified clarification instead of demanding a word-for-word translation or one privileged dialect. Where prosody, discourse context or reference-set completeness changes the reading, require that evidence rather than inventing it.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Not all versus none; no fabricated positive count.** A reviewed fictional club roster has **four** registered members: Ana, Ben, Camila and Dario. The signed attendance sheet confirms Ana and Ben arrived, while Camila and Dario did not. The recipient asks, 'Did everyone arrive?' A learner who says 'Nobody arrived' fails; 'Not everyone arrived; Ana and Ben did' is supported. Counterfactual: a second notice states only **'Not everyone arrived'**, without a list or positive count. It warrants at least one nonarrival on the established ordinary reading but does **not** establish that anyone arrived. 'Some arrived' is not creditable without additional source evidence. A learner who says 'I know at least one person did not arrive, but I don't know how many came' preserves the uncertainty.

**B — Only, necessary conditions and exclusion scope.** A fictional library notice says **'Only registered students may reserve a study room; staff approval is also required.'** A learner who tells a friend 'All registered students are guaranteed a room' reverses the rule. A correct handoff says registration is required and approval is additionally needed; actual availability is not established. Counterfactual: **'Only Ana submitted the form'** entails that no other relevant person submitted it (given the reviewed reference set), while **'Ana was not the only person who submitted'** entails at least one additional submitter but does not identify that person. A missing roster prevents naming others. This case tests focus/exclusivity rather than general permission knowledge.

**C — Polarity and cross-language negative concord.** A reviewed bilingual chat has English **'I didn't see anyone in the waiting room'** and Spanish **'No vi a nadie en la sala de espera'**. Both normally report seeing no person there; the Spanish form does not assert seeing someone. A learner who mechanically interprets the two Spanish negative expressions as cancelling each other fails. Counterfactual: **'I didn't see everyone' / 'No vi a todos'** expresses a different scope, not zero sightings; a context with poor visibility does not justify a claim that no one was present. A variant with an unclear audio negative or unsupported dialect requires clarification or an appropriately bounded interpretation, not a universal grammar claim.

**D — Quantified bounds and verified exceptions.** A fictional event sheet explicitly states **'At least three volunteers are needed, no more than five may enter, and all except Leo have completed orientation'**. The reviewed roster is exactly **Ana, Ben, Camila and Leo**; three completed orientation. The learner should convey lower bound **3**, upper bound **5**, and that Leo is the one documented orientation exception. 'Exactly five volunteers must attend' and 'Nobody except Leo is allowed' are unsupported. Counterfactual: if the roster is incomplete, **'all except Leo'** cannot be used to infer the total number of oriented people, and if the entry limit is revised to **'fewer than five'**, five is no longer permitted. The communicative target is a faithful invitation or clarification, not isolated counting speed.

**E — Delivered-channel corruption and authorship chronology.** A fictional voice message says **'Not all the seats are reserved'**; ASR displays **'All the seats are reserved'**, captions omit **'not'**, and a screen reader skips **'except on Sundays'** in a separate access notice. Log each actually delivered channel and quarantine construct-changing discrepancies instead of marking a learner wrong for the corrupted prompt. A learner independently asks 'Did you say *not all* or *all*?' at 10:00. An answer-bearing transcript preview renders at 10:01; the initial clarification is FIRST_UNAIDED, while a later copied paraphrase is ASSISTED. A replayed event with the same session-qualified ID is not another independent observation or learning day. A transcript-accessible alternative can support written meaning but cannot certify unheard audio comprehension. A source revision at 10:02 must not retroactively change the 10:00 source oracle.

**F — Longitudinal method reversal and soft-interest separation.** Fictional learner A immediately answers correctly after SCOPE_CONTRAST_MINIMAL_PAIRS but later independently turns *not everyone* into *no one* in a new unfamiliar museum handoff. Several matched, actually delivered RECIPIENT_READBACK opportunities precede stronger delayed first-unaided scope-preserving messages across different recipients and structures; only repeated comparable observations may cautiously favor readback, while still sampling alternatives. Fictional learner B initially benefits from REFERENCE_SET_VISUAL_OR_TEXT_TABLE but later independently treats *only* as sufficient for admission; confidence in that method falls, review becomes due, and exploration resumes. If the table method receives a closed four-person roster but readback receives an incomplete open roster, or one method exposes the answer in a tooltip, declare **no winner**. Merely changing 'music club' to 'gaming club' while retaining identical wording, roster and answer is not unseen transfer. Missing later probes are MISSING, not learner failures. Interest-weighted topics never exclude neutral/unfamiliar examples, suppress required modalities or change CEFR thresholds.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Existing CEFR can-do and independently reviewed meaning oracle.** A–F require a versioned existing communicative objective, prerequisites, recipient/information gap, source-world/scope oracle, language/variety/register, accessible delivered stimulus, evaluator/rubric, first-unaided threshold, due review, delayed and unseen probes and unchanged exit gate. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED; do not invent logic-specific CEFR mastery.
2. **Negation and quantifier scope preservation.** A distinguishes 'not all' from 'none' and never upgrades a missing positive count to 'some arrived'. Credit only source-supported entailments; log pragmatic inferences separately.
3. **Exclusivity and necessary/sufficient distinction.** B preserves *only* and the correct reference set; registration is not an admission guarantee. Unknown additional submitters may not be named.
4. **Language-specific polarity validation.** C uses reviewed natural English/Spanish realizations, accepts valid negative concord, rejects false double-positive readings and does not universalize a dialectal or syntactic rule without evidence.
5. **Bounds, exception membership and source completeness.** D preserves at-least/at-most versus exact/fewer-than meanings, checks the exception against a reviewed roster, and does not infer a count from an incomplete reference set.
6. **Actually delivered accessible chronological evidence.** E logs source and rendered audio/ASR/caption/translation/screen-reader variants, quarantines meaning-changing corruption, preserves first authorship before actual help, deduplicates stable events and distinguishes accessible-equivalent from different constructs. Text cannot prove unheard listening.
7. **Controlled uncertain reversible instructional adaptation.** F compares feasible actually delivered methods under matched scope burden, roster completeness, modality, language, topic, help, interval and follow-up. Sparse, contradictory, selectively observed or confounded evidence yields no winner; delayed regression reduces confidence and restores exploration.
8. **Delayed first-unaided unseen transfer and nonexclusive interests.** Hinted rewrites, immediate repetition, topic-only swaps and unchanged templates cannot earn delayed/unseen mastery. Require a later distinct eligible session, new source/recipient/context **and a changed negation/quantifier or reference-set structure**, reviewed oracle, first-unaided actionable handoff and explicit follow-up missingness. Preserve shared CEFR objectives, receptive/productive balance and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation.
9. **Honest implementation and efficacy boundary.** This document or green CI is not a shipped scope evaluator, an outcome-responsive tutor or proof of improved human retention. Require exact-head deterministic executable assertions against real objectives, logged delivered instructional changes, later independent transfer against a balanced baseline, and separately reviewed learner evidence before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs work, threshold weakening, level enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are fictional design/probe cases, not collected learner observations or executable QA. Preserve ambiguity, source completeness, language-specific polarity, actual delivery chronology and explicit UNSUPPORTED gates until implementation and evaluation evidence exist.
