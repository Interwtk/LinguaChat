# F209 — Review triage under limited session budgets, forgetting signals and CEFR coverage debt

**Status:** design-only, synthetic *cross-cutting* longitudinal curriculum acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until independently reviewed executable scheduler/teaching-policy checks run against a named exact engine HEAD and real, ethically reviewed longitudinal independent-learning evidence exists. Not a new CEFR can-do, an implemented scheduling algorithm, a fixed review interval, proof of learning gains, a new exit gate or permission to open A1–C2. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F206 covers selection and missing-follow-up bias; F207 diagnoses prerequisite gaps; F208 defines paired-history metamorphic invariants. **Distinct question in F209:** when several existing objectives compete for limited *actually available* learning time, can the tutor choose, deliver and revisit practice based on uncertain **independent forgetting/transfer and coverage debt**, without turning missed opportunities, short assisted success, interest preferences or unavailable modalities into false mastery or failure? This fixture specifies observable scheduling *decisions and consequences*, not universal intervals, a psychological fatigue diagnosis or a promise that the engine currently schedules these options.

## Fixed curriculum, opportunity and evidence model

Bind each history to existing **versioned** can-do IDs and prerequisite/blueprint mappings, target language and variety, CEFR level, communicative-intent rubric, independent mastery and exit standards, due-review obligations, relevant skill families, supported modality routes, actual accessibility constraints, and any existing meaningful-turn ceiling. Do not invent level-specific content or relax graduation rules. Preserve the unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix boundary as UNSUPPORTED, not an implicit choice.

At each decision point, freeze an **opportunity ledger**: session ID and start/end or observed available duration (UNKNOWN when not measured); learner-selected time budget if supplied; actual tasks offered, rendered, attempted and completed; interrupted/abandoned tasks; existing due objectives and their last *qualified* independent evidence; unserved due-review debt; skill-family exposure debt; task burden estimates with uncertainty; eligible accessible activities; explicit channel failures; current soft interest weights and context rotation; decision seed and policy/rubric versions. A declared 10-minute budget is a planning constraint, not proof of 10 minutes of engagement, and must never be a timed mastery gate.

Use a separate immutable **outcome ledger** with session-qualified event IDs, trusted occurrence versus upload ordering, objective/rubric version, selected versus **actually delivered** method, context family, first unaided response, semantic-error severity, answer-bearing model/hint/transcript exposure, retries, completion, independent production, delayed retrieval after meaningful intervening material, independently reviewed unseen transfer, and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up. Deduplicate replay; unknown event order, missing latency or unknown novelty remains UNKNOWN. A new calendar day, extra repetitions in one sitting or a near-identical paraphrase does not independently establish forgetting, spacing or transfer.

An **eligible scheduling decision** records: the required can-do or review candidate, due/debt and independent-evidence reasons, estimated task burden, uncertainty, eligible alternative candidates, accessible modality, instructional/scaffold choice, planned topic family, expected later independent probe and why competing obligations were deferred. A subsequent event must establish actual rendering and completion; a proposed plan is not delivered instruction. The scheduler may select a balanced fallback when evidence is sparse and may split, defer or re-offer practice when opportunity is constrained. It may not erase debt, manufacture an observation, substitute recognition for required free production, or infer that a favored topic has higher pedagogical efficacy.

Define test-oracle outcomes per decision: REQUIRED_OBJECTIVE_PRESERVED, DUE_REVIEW_VISIBLE, DEFERRED_WITH_REASON, COVERAGE_DEBT_VISIBLE, OPPORTUNITY_OBSERVED, BUDGET_DECLARED_NOT_MEASURED, DELIVERED_AND_COMPLETED, ABANDONED_NOT_CREDITED, FIRST_UNAIDED, ASSISTED_REPAIR, CLEAN_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_INDEPENDENT_TRANSFER, FORGETTING_SIGNAL_REVIEWED, UNCERTAINTY_UPDATED, METHOD_ALTERNATIVES_ELIGIBLE, ACCESSIBLE_ROUTE_SELECTED, MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE, TOPIC_ROTATION_PRESERVED, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, NOT_COMPARABLE and UNSUPPORTED. Labels are evidence and decisions, never fixed learner traits.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — A short session cannot silently starve due independent review.** The learner has two existing due can-dos (one independent free-production review and one reading comprehension review), a new attractive game-themed lesson, and a declared short session budget that cannot reliably fit all three. An unsafe policy repeatedly serves the game lesson because immediate completion is likely, silently clearing the due list. The expected tutor preserves both due obligations, makes a reasoned, bounded choice of a feasible review or a smaller independent probe, logs the deferred obligation and keeps a future opportunity. It may use a game context **only if** communicative burden remains equivalent; interest alone cannot outrank required objectives. A task planned but not rendered earns no evidence. If every feasible review is inaccessible or too long, mark debt and fallback/UNSUPPORTED rather than pretending it was completed.

**B — Assisted fluency must not postpone a review after clean forgetting.** In an earlier completed session, a learner produces a correct response only after an answer-bearing model. In a later distinct completed session, an unfamiliar, independently scored communicative probe reveals a meaning-critical error on the same versioned can-do. A naive policy uses the recent copied success to push review farther away. The expected policy retains the assisted versus independent distinction, incorporates the later forgetting signal and uncertainty, and may offer a sooner or differently scaffolded *future* opportunity while keeping the later clean probe unaided. No exact universal spacing interval or mandatory method winner is asserted. A matched control with a genuinely correct delayed independent probe may justify a different review choice, but both controls keep identical CEFR standards and accessible alternatives.

**C — Ten same-session repetitions are not ten spaced retrievals.** One history contains ten correct repetitions of a familiar pattern within a single completed session, including visible hints; another contains fewer, distinct completed learning sessions with intervening material and independently reviewed unseen prompts. The first may show improved practice fluency, but cannot be counted as ten delayed independent retrieval events, cannot automatically retire a due review, and cannot be compared as if both histories had equal novelty, help or delay. A midnight crossing or a renamed item family does not manufacture a new qualified session. If an answer-bearing worked example precedes the supposedly unseen prompt, mark contamination. Require explicit first-authorship, true session provenance and independently reviewed novelty before promoting any evidence.

**D — Interrupted opportunity is not a negative learner answer.** A due review is planned for Tuesday, but playback fails before rendering; on Wednesday the learner opens a session, sees the prompt and leaves without responding; on Thursday the device reconnects and uploads the same events out of order. Preserve the three distinct states: due-but-not-offered, offered/rendered-but-uncompleted, and a genuinely completed response if one later exists. Do not fabricate failures, mastery, an observed latency, or a completed review from a task start. Deduplicate upload replays by session-qualified event identity and trusted occurrence chronology. A policy may re-offer an accessible independent opportunity and keep the debt visible. The absence of a follow-up must be disclosed when comparing instructional methods; a learner's absence is not proof of poor retention.

**E — Coverage debt must survive constrained modalities.** The learner has repeated independent reading success, but the versioned curriculum still requires productive and listening/speaking evidence. During a session, audio is unavailable and a written transcript renders instead; speech capture is unsupported. The tutor may use a separately valid reading task or an accessible supported written-production activity, but must not award listening or speaking mastery, erase their coverage debt, or silently replace the shared CEFR standard. If an objective cannot currently be assessed, mark the relevant route UNSUPPORTED and preserve the unavailable requirement for authorized future review. Continue sampling *supported* modalities and suitable alternatives; do not trap the learner indefinitely in easy recognition tasks just because their predicted score is high.

**F — Opportunity-aware reversibility without topic lock-in.** Across several matched completed sessions, concise instruction appears to work on a preferred music topic, but later genuinely delayed, independent neutral/unfamiliar transfer fails. A separate worked-example method has sparse comparable observations because its scheduled follow-ups were often never offered. The tutor must not rank methods using only completers or claim that music caused learning gains. It lowers confidence or reopens alternatives, schedules an accessible independent unfamiliar-context probe when feasible, and preserves due-review and receptive/productive debt. It deliberately keeps preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics eligible and eventually sampled; a constrained session may defer a context with a logged reason, not ban it. The next *actually delivered* choice, not merely a recommendation, must be auditable. Without comparable completed delayed outcomes, superiority remains UNKNOWN.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Immutable shared standards.** All six histories bind existing versioned CEFR can-dos, prerequisites, independently reviewed semantic rubrics, availability, due reviews and noncompensatory mastery/exit criteria. Neither session budgets nor interests change required objectives or resolve unsupported B2 blueprint boundaries.
2. **Opportunity and outcome are separate.** Log declared versus observed budget, due/offered/rendered/attempted/completed/abandoned stages, actual delivery, task burden and missingness. A proposed activity or interrupted session never creates a completed observation.
3. **Triage is accountable, not a popularity contest.** A produces a feasible, reasoned next objective or explicit constrained fallback; every deferred due can-do and skill debt remains visible. Preferred topics influence examples, not eligibility or mastery standards.
4. **Delayed independent forgetting changes the policy evidence.** B preserves the first assisted success and later independent error separately, updates uncertainty and explains the next review/scaffold decision; it cannot treat copied fluency as proof to delay all review. No fixed optimal interval is invented.
5. **Spacing and novelty require qualified provenance.** C rejects repeated same-session drills, day-boundary tricks, answer-exposed prompts and renamed scripts as multiple clean delayed transfers. Matched method comparisons require equivalent difficulty, help, context, channel and opportunity.
6. **Interruptions and missingness remain explicit.** D distinguishes not offered, rendered/uncompleted and completed; replay and upload permutations cannot change trusted evidence. Unknown latency and unanswered follow-up are neither failure nor success.
7. **Modality coverage is noncompensatory.** E preserves required productive/receptive skill debt and refuses phantom listening/speaking evidence when audio or oral capture is unavailable. A transcript can support reading only under its own valid rubric.
8. **Reversible, diverse, actually delivered instruction.** F incorporates contradictory delayed independent transfer, refuses biased completer-only method rankings, keeps alternative methods and soft topic rotation alive, and verifies the next decision against actual delivery. Sparse or incomparable evidence triggers a balanced fallback.
9. **No inflated implementation or efficacy claim.** Publish deterministic event histories, exact seed/clock, objective/rubric/policy versions, expected per-step decisions and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED results in an independently reviewed executable exact-head harness before calling scheduling implemented. Human benefit additionally requires an ethically reviewed longitudinal comparison with a feasible balanced baseline, comparable opportunity denominators, delayed independent retrieval, unseen transfer, missingness/selection analysis and no hidden loss of CEFR skill coverage. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge, Pre-A1 changes or A1–C2 release.

**Fixture verdict:** F209 makes *which obligation to teach or review next, under limited real opportunity* independently falsifiable. The target is durable, unaided communication with honest review and skill debt—not completion streaks, copied short-term correctness, favored topics or a fabricated schedule. **UNSUPPORTED** until executed and independently reviewed.
