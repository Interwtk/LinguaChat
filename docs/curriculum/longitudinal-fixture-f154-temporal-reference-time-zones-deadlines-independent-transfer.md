## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Cross-midnight recipient handoff with explicitly fixed offsets.** An independently reviewed synthetic notice fixes a remote meeting at **2026-10-26 23:30 UTC−03:00**, using a *fixed offset*, not a claim about any city's seasonal clock. Its instant is **2026-10-27 02:30 UTC** and **2026-10-27 03:30 UTC+01:00**. Learner A tells a recipient at UTC+01:00 "Monday at 03:30"; learner B independently says "Tuesday 27 October at 03:30 in your UTC+01:00 clock; the organizer's date is Monday 26 October." Counterfactual: if the notice names only "Santiago time" without a date-specific verified IANA zone conversion, do not silently substitute the fixed UTC−03:00 assumption. A correctly converted hour with the wrong date fails the actionable handoff.

**B — Relative references anchored to the original author, not reading time.** A synthetic organizer sends a timestamped Monday 26 October 2026 message saying "Let's meet tomorrow at 10:00 at the library." The recipient reads it Wednesday 28 October. Learner A independently relays "Thursday at 10:00" by anchoring "tomorrow" to reading time; learner B independently says "The organizer meant Tuesday 27 October at 10:00; that time has already passed. Ask for a new arrangement." Counterfactual: a forwarded message without a trustworthy original timestamp or a message composed across zones is ambiguous; request an anchor rather than fabricating one. "Next Friday" without an agreed convention remains clarification-worthy even when a calendar weekday can be computed.

**C — Deadline words do not supply a missing cutoff.** A reviewed workshop notice says "Send your request **by Friday**; support is available **until 17:00 on Thursday**." It does not specify whether "by Friday" means the start or end of Friday, or which time zone governs the submission. Learner A promises "Friday 23:59 in your time zone is safe"; learner B independently says "Support ends Thursday at 17:00, but the request cutoff is not precise enough here; ask the organizer for the exact date, time and zone." Counterfactual: if an authoritative amended notice explicitly says "Friday 30 October 2026, 17:00 UTC−03:00 inclusive," communicate that cutoff with its source and scope. Support hours are not automatically the submission deadline.

**D — Elapsed duration, local clock and ambiguous DST civil times.** A reviewed synthetic timetable states "The online activity lasts **90 elapsed minutes**." For a **fixed-offset** start at 23:30, the end is 01:00 on the following date, not 00:00 or 01:00 on the same date. A separate civil-time exercise offers "01:30" on a named daylight-saving fallback date in a named IANA zone but supplies no fold/offset; the oracle marks two possible instants, not one. A spring-forward gap can make a purported local time nonexistent. Learner A assumes every local hour is 60 elapsed minutes and selects an arbitrary fold; learner B independently distinguishes duration from clock display and asks for an offset/instant when a fold is unresolved. Do not pretend a named zone's actual transitions were checked unless the fixture supplies a reviewed tzdb version and date-specific transition oracle.

**E — Rendered-channel distortion and first-authorship chronology.** A source notice says "The session is **not** on Friday the **fifteenth**; it is on Monday the **nineteenth**, at 15:00 UTC." ASR omits "not"; captions substitute "fifty" for "fifteen"; a translated notification moves "tomorrow" to the viewer's date; a screen reader drops "UTC"; and a calendar preview silently converts 15:00 UTC to a recipient's local zone. Record what the learner **actually received** and which constructs remain observable. If the learner first asks "Which zone is 15:00 in?" at 10:00 and a conversion hint first renders at 10:01, preserve the first independent clarification; an answer revised after the hint is ASSISTED. A readable caption cannot certify comprehension of unheard audio. Duplicate replay of the same session-qualified event cannot create a later retrieval session. A genuinely accessible equivalent may preserve the same construct, but a changed temporal fact must be quarantined.

**F — Outcome-sensitive adaptation with matched temporal burden.** Fictional learner A immediately converts fixed offsets correctly after FIXED_OFFSET_CROSS_MIDNIGHT_HANDOFF but later independently shifts the date incorrectly when informing a new recipient. Comparable actually delivered SPEAKER_RECIPIENT_TIME_ANCHOR_DIALOGUE trials precede stronger delayed first-unaided handoffs in a new setting; confidence in that candidate can increase only after repeated matched observations, while alternatives remain sampled. Fictional learner B performs well after BY_VS_UNTIL_MINIMAL_PAIRS but later invents an unsupported deadline in an unseen request; lower confidence and resume balanced exploration. If one candidate receives a complete time-zone table and the other an ambiguous "next Friday" notice, or one renders the converted answer, flag confounding and infer **no winner**. Merely replacing "library" with "game club" in the same schedule template is not unseen transfer. Missing later probes remain missing, not wrong. A learner interested in music may more often encounter concert scheduling, but medical appointments, community services, travel, workplace, neutral and unfamiliar topics remain reachable under the same CEFR can-do and rubric.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real CEFR objective and reviewed temporal oracle.** A–F bind existing versioned communicative objectives, recipient/purpose, independently reviewed source authority, message timestamp, anchor, zone/offset/calendar/date format, interval and cutoff semantics, accessible stimuli, evaluator/rubric, first-unaided threshold, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and unchanged exit gate. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED; never invent CEFR mastery or real-world appointment confirmation.
2. **Cross-midnight exactness without fictional DST certainty.** A correctly preserves the event's instant, recipient hour **and calendar date** under explicit fixed offsets. A named civil zone requires independently reviewed date-specific rules; never equate it automatically with a fixed offset.
3. **Relative anchor and ambiguity discipline.** B anchors "tomorrow" to the trustworthy original speaker timestamp, not the time a recipient reads it; missing anchors and convention-sensitive "next Friday" yield clarification, not fabricated certainty.
4. **Deadline and availability restraint.** C distinguishes "by" from "until," support hours from submission cutoff, and a known inclusive boundary from an unspecified one. No precise time zone or guaranteed acceptance may be invented.
5. **Instant, elapsed duration and DST integrity.** D preserves date rollover for elapsed intervals and does not conflate wall-clock difference with elapsed duration. DST gap/fold examples require a reviewed IANA/tzdb oracle or stay UNKNOWN; never pick an arbitrary fold.
6. **Actually delivered, accessible, chronological, idempotent evidence.** E reconciles authoritative versus rendered audio/ASR/captions/translations/calendar previews/screen readers, quarantines changed constructs, preserves first authorship before actual help exposure, distinguishes accessible equivalent from a different construct and deduplicates immutable session-qualified events. Reading cannot certify unheard listening.
7. **Controlled, uncertain, reversible adaptation.** F compares feasible actually delivered methods under matched source completeness, temporal complexity, register, modality, topic, help, review interval and follow-up opportunity. Sparse, contradictory or confounded observations yield no winner; delayed regression reduces confidence and restores alternatives.
8. **Delayed first-unaided unseen transfer and nonexclusive interests.** Assisted time conversions, immediate repeats, same-template place swaps and hinted replies cannot earn delayed/unseen mastery. Require a later distinct eligible session, new source/recipient/context and temporal structure, reviewed oracle, first-unaided actionable communication and explicit missingness. Preserve shared CEFR objectives, receptive/productive coverage and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation.
9. **Honest implementation and efficacy boundary.** A document or green CI is not executable time interpretation, demonstrated outcome-sensitive instruction or evidence of improved human retention. Require exact-head deterministic executable assertions against real objectives, logged actually delivered instructional changes and delayed independent transfer versus a balanced baseline, followed by ethically reviewed learner evidence before efficacy claims.
