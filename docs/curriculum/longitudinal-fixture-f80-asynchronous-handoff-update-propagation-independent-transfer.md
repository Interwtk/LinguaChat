# F80 — Asynchronous handoffs, update propagation and independent communicative transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a shipped asynchronous-communication evaluator, CEFR certification, reproduced production defect or proof of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 protects genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 require actual instruction delivery and tutor-origin attribution; F47/F48 protect source-defined noncompensatory mastery; F49 protects accessibility equivalence; F50 protects topic breadth; F53 allows valid language varieties; F54 covers breakdown repair; F58 protects learner initiative; F66 varies interlocutors; F71 covers reference coherence; F72 tracks goals and constraints; F74 protects source fidelity; F77 covers turn management; F78 covers bilateral common ground; F79 isolates multiparty coordination **within a shared interaction**. **F80 isolates a different source-required construct:** independently conveying a material change across a time-separated, possibly asynchronous handoff, preserving the applicable version of a plan and its uncertainty, directing the update to the source-required audience, and recognizing when a later reply is based on superseded information. A timely acknowledgement or a copied message is not evidence that the intended recipient has the correct actionable information. Conversely, instant replies, read receipts, platform-specific notifications, universal acknowledgement demands and project-management skills are **not** invented CEFR requirements. Apply only when a real versioned CEFR-linked interaction/mediation can-do and task actually require asynchronous or time-separated information transfer.

## Controlled setup

Resolve a **real versioned** CEFR descriptor/can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, source-defined communicative task, language and varieties, participant roles and authorized information, channel constraints, allowed accommodations, source-required update content and recipients, rubric/evaluator versions, assistance rules, independent threshold, due reviews, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer and level exit gate. Freeze a versioned timeline of messages, sender/recipient visibility, delivery states, source-authoritative changes, cancellation or supersession rules and any legitimate uncertainty **before** comparing instructional methods. Label propositions `CURRENT_CONFIRMED`, `CURRENT_TENTATIVE`, `SUPERSEDED`, `CANCELLED`, `RECIPIENT_UNINFORMED`, `DELIVERED_NOT_UNDERSTOOD`, `CONFIRMED_UNDERSTOOD`, `AMBIGUOUS` or `UNOBSERVABLE`; these describe adjudicated **scripted task state**, not mind-reading or a claim about actual users. For tasks with no source-required handoff, emit `NOT_APPLICABLE` rather than manufacture obligations.

Predeclare feasible reversible instruction candidates `VERSION_CONTRAST_READING`, `AUDIENCE_UPDATE_REWRITE`, `DELAYED_HANDOFF_ROLEPLAY`, `SOURCE_AND_RECIPIENT_CHECK`, `OPEN_UNSEEN_ASYNC_TASK` and `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. These may vary implemented/evaluable reading, listening, writing, speaking and free-production mix, explanation, examples, scaffold, activity ordering, pacing and review spacing **without** changing source-defined objectives, independent standard, receptive/productive evidence or communicative burden. A tutor-supplied latest plan, highlighted missing recipient, prewritten change message, template that names the target correction, or prompted follow-up question is assistance for the affected move; an ordinary scripted recipient's clarification within task protocol is not. A scripted recipient that discloses the exact required response outside protocol is a coaching leak. Preserve alternative sampling and favorite/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation; never classify learners permanently by channel, response speed or preferred modality.

Use deterministic pseudonymous multi-session histories with injected seed/clock and immutable session-qualified event IDs. Capture frozen source/rubric/task and plan versions, authority for each change, actual visibility/delivery chronology, first learner-authored update, recipients addressed, materially preserved vs omitted constraints, recipient response or missingness, the source-defined communicative outcome, selected/assigned/**actually rendered** instruction, exact tutor cues and chronology, first-attempt quality, retries, hints/scaffold dependence, recurring error type, optional calibrated latency only when reliable, practice dose, topic familiarity, channel/accessibility constraints, later independent delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen handoff transfer, and eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators. Distinguish `SENT`, `DELIVERED`, `READ`, `ACKNOWLEDGED` and `UNDERSTOOD_FOR_TASK` where observable. Never turn a missing reply into failure or success, a read receipt into comprehension, an offline queue delay into learner hesitation, or typed message performance into spoken/listening proficiency.

Evidence labels: `SOURCE_VALID_ASYNC_HANDOFF`, `CURRENT_VERSION_IDENTIFIED`, `SUPERSEDED_PLAN_PROPAGATED`, `MATERIAL_CHANGE_PRESERVED`, `REQUIRED_RECIPIENT_REACHED`, `UNCONFIRMED_RECIPIENT_STATE`, `FALSE_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT_CONFIDENCE`, `VALID_UNCERTAINTY_RETAINED`, `FIRST_INDEPENDENT_UPDATE`, `TUTOR_UPDATE_CUE_EXPOSED`, `ASSISTED_UPDATE`, `RECIPIENT_TASK_REPLY`, `RECIPIENT_COACHING_LEAK`, `INDEPENDENT_DELAYED_HANDOFF_RETRIEVAL`, `INDEPENDENT_UNSEEN_HANDOFF_TRANSFER`, `CHANNEL_DELAY_CONFOUNDED`, `TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED`, `ACCESSIBILITY_CONFOUNDED`, `FOLLOWUP_MISSING`, `EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED`, `VERSION_CONFLICT_QUARANTINED`, `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. These are bounded observations with uncertainty, not enduring learner types.

## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Old plan forwarded after a material revision.** A source-defined appointment handoff starts at 14:00 in venue X; an authorized later update changes the venue to Y while retaining the time. Learner A forwards the older plan to the required recipient; learner B independently identifies the authoritative newer update, communicates the venue change while retaining 14:00, and completes the source-defined handoff. If the change is only tentative, a valid learner communicates the uncertainty instead of asserting a final decision. Do not invent a need to notify someone outside the task's required audience.
- **B — Delivery and agreement are not understanding.** A delayed message is marked read and receives “okay,” but the recipient's next source-defined action uses the superseded location. Learner A treats the receipt as proof of successful handoff; learner B independently detects the mismatch from a task-valid reply, clarifies the material change and achieves the goal. In a valid alternative, no reply is due and the source task does not require confirmation: preserve unknown recipient comprehension rather than penalizing an invented follow-up duty.
- **C — Tutor wrote the exact update.** Tutor actually renders “Tell Ana that the deadline moved to Friday, not Thursday; the file format stays PDF.” Learner A copies it: successful but assisted for the update. A second learner independently reconstructs a new handoff from separate source messages after a meaningfully delayed interval. A selected but never rendered tutor cue cannot contaminate independent evidence. A scripted recipient asking a normal clarification is interaction; one revealing the target wording without protocol permission is a coaching leak.
- **D — Early advantage reverses across time and context.** Early matched first unaided probes favor `AUDIENCE_UPDATE_REWRITE` over `VERSION_CONTRAST_READING` for one learner. In later unseen delayed tasks with new recipients, different source-authoritative updates and equivalent source-required communicative burden, the apparent advantage reverses. Match or disclose task difficulty, number of updates, source ambiguity, participant roles, topic familiarity, practice dose, feedback, channel, accessibility, language variety and rubric version. Reduce confidence, sample alternatives and capture the actually rendered revised instruction; never claim a permanent writing/reading learner style.
- **E — Interests and channel access cannot narrow mastery.** A learner handles favorite-game scheduling changes but misses a material constraint in a neutral service update and an unfamiliar community event. Another learner uses accessible text for a source-equivalent written handoff; the available trace cannot prove unobserved oral listening/speaking. A caption that explicitly identifies the superseded plan provides assistance. Rotate favorite, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts, retain due reviews and all source-required receptive/productive evidence, and do not infer language incompetence from disability, device availability or delayed network delivery.
- **F — Offline out-of-order updates and missing follow-up.** Two offline sessions reuse a local message ID; a delayed older update arrives after the newer authorized one; a tutor's correction was rendered before the learner's first message but uploaded afterward; a recipient reply is never received; and the task rubric version changes the required audience. Reconstruct from immutable session-qualified IDs, frozen authority/timeline/rubric hashes and trusted cue-render chronology. Deduplicate true replays, quarantine conflicting version/authority records, preserve missing replies as unknown, and require identical independence labels, review debt, recipient-state uncertainty and next-instruction rationale across receipt-order permutations.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source validity before assessment.** For A–F emit versioned CEFR can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, task, language/variety, source-authoritative change rules, required recipients, communicative goal, channel, rubric, assistance, independent threshold, delayed/unseen probes, due reviews and exit gate. Missing mapping or invented read-receipt/response-time requirements yields `UNSUPPORTED`.
2. **Correct update version and scope.** In A/F distinguish authoritative current, tentative, cancelled and superseded messages; preserve unchanged material constraints and valid uncertainty. Receipt order is not authority. Conflicting source versions are quarantined rather than guessed.
3. **Recipient-aware independent communication.** In A/B/E credit only source-required audience coverage and learner-authored updates that actually communicate the necessary changed information. Copying, message counts, generic “FYI,” platform read receipts or obligatory universal acknowledgements cannot replace the source-defined communicative outcome; concise and culturally valid alternatives remain eligible.
4. **Tutor and interlocutor provenance.** In C/F use trusted rendered-cue chronology and first learner-authored update. An answer-bearing tutor cue actually displayed before the move makes the affected evidence assisted; assigned-but-unrendered cues do not. Task-valid recipient clarification is ordinary interaction, while answer leakage outside protocol is marked.
5. **Delivery, comprehension and missingness separation.** In B/F distinguish sent, delivered, read, acknowledged and source-task-understood states when observable. A missing or delayed recipient reply stays unknown; no synthetic completion or failure. Device/network latency is not automatically hesitation.
6. **Independent delayed unseen transfer.** In C/D/F require separated sessions and meaningful delay, genuinely new update/recipient/channel-context configurations, first unaided communication and a completed source-defined outcome. Rehearsed templates, immediate replay, tutor-authored updates or absent outcome evidence cannot establish durable independence. Emit eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators.
7. **Comparable methods and broad topic exposure.** In D/E match or disclose update count, source ambiguity, role/audience, topic familiarity, practice dose, feedback, channel, variety, rubric and accessibility. Preserve common CEFR objectives, noncompensatory receptive/productive mastery, due reviews and favorite/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar contexts; interests only softly influence examples.
8. **Reversible adaptation and deterministic replay.** In C/D/E/F emit feasible candidates, selected/assigned/actually rendered method, independent later outcomes, uncertainty/confounds, exploration and next choice. Sparse/confounded histories fall back to `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. Deduplicate immutable events, enforce task/authority/rubric hashes and replay-order invariance for assistance, recipient state, missingness, skill/review debt and rationale. Irreconcilable provenance fails closed.
9. **Assertion-level audit without efficacy inflation.** For A–F emit source/task/rubric/evaluator IDs, frozen versioned update timeline, first/final learner-authored messages, actual rendered assistance, required-recipient coverage, observed outcome, delayed/unseen denominators, confounds, replay equivalence and `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED` per assertion. Documentation CI cannot prove a shipped asynchronous evaluator, CEFR certification or improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, deterministic seed/clock and multi-session histories, real source-resolved CEFR-linked handoff tasks with validated multilingual/variety-aware rubrics, frozen authority and recipient expectations, actual delivery and tutor-cue provenance, first learner-authored updates, independently completed delayed unseen handoff/context probes with denominators, comparable instructional/topic/channel/access exposure, protected skill/review obligations, replay-order permutations, reversible next-instruction traces and assertion-level verdicts. Missing source mapping, message authority, delivery chronology, equivalent modality evidence or follow-up remains `UNSUPPORTED`/unknown, never invented proficiency.

Any claim that adaptive instruction **improves durable independent handoff communication** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, delayed unseen exchanges, multilingual/variety and accessibility evaluator validation, human adjudication, privacy and subgroup-harm review and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor teaches learners to carry forward the right information when plans change, not merely to forward the latest-looking message.**
