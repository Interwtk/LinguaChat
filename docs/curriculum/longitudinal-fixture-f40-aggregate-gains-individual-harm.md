# F40 — Aggregate adaptive gains cannot hide learner-level regression or sparse-data harm

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. This is not a reproduced defect, shipped adaptive tutor, causal estimate, subgroup efficacy finding or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F24 guards against policy selection bias, F25/F36 against skill and review debt, F28 against cross-skill extrapolation, F31 against temporal-state confounding, F32 against selective follow-up, and F39 against outside-learning attribution. F40 addresses a different failure: **a method may improve pooled delayed scores while harming some eligible learners or objectives, especially where evidence is sparse, follow-up differs, or the aggregate is dominated by frequent users**. Group-level improvement cannot automatically override a learner's repeated contrary independent outcomes, and a single poor observation cannot justify a permanent learner label.

## Controlled setup

Resolve `goal-1` to an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, a genuinely supported skill, fixed prerequisites, immutable rubric and independent mastery/exit gates. Compare eligible methods A (`concise_explanation`) and B (`worked_example`) with a feasible shared balanced baseline. Keep task demand, assessment calibration, independent scoring, item novelty, actual retrieval delay, due-review opportunity and neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar context coverage comparable. Inject a deterministic clock and seed, a versioned `heterogeneous_outcome_guardrail_policy`, and an immutable synthetic event ledger spanning multiple sessions and real learning days.

Record stable learner/objective/skill/session/run/event IDs; policy and assignment versions; eligible option set, selection mechanism and known probability (never fabricate one); verified delivery and meaningful exposure; first-attempt independent quality, hints/models/retries, actual delayed retrieval, novel-context transfer, follow-up eligibility/completion, practice and external-instruction provenance where legitimately known, objective/skill review debt, assessment/rubric versions, event versus upload time, and missingness/reason codes. Report **learner-weighted and opportunity-weighted** outcomes separately: a prolific learner must not silently stand in for ten infrequent learners. Only use non-sensitive, actually observed baseline-readiness, objective, skill, accessibility/availability and opportunity strata when appropriate; never infer protected characteristics or profile a learner as a fixed type.

Predeclare `INDIVIDUAL_CONTRARY_EVIDENCE`, `HETEROGENEITY_OBSERVED`, `SPARSE_UNCERTAIN`, `AGGREGATE_ONLY`, `FOLLOWUP_IMBALANCED`, `NOT_COMPARABLE`, `NO_INDEPENDENT_HARM_ESTIMATE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. Separate (1) valid learner mastery, (2) learner/objective-specific method hypothesis, (3) pooled descriptive outcomes, and (4) evidence for any method's causal contribution. An observed lower score is not by itself proof of harm caused by instruction.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — Pooled gain, repeated individual regression.** Most comparable learners improve after verified B delivery, so pooled independent delayed retrieval favors B. One learner has repeated, sufficiently comparable B exposures followed by poorer unaided delayed retrieval and unseen transfer than their A/balanced occasions. Keep the aggregate observation, flag the learner-specific contradictory evidence and reduce confidence in B *for that learner and objective*; do not insist on B solely because it wins globally or assert causal harm without defensible identification.
- **B — Frequent-user domination.** One high-frequency learner completes many B probes and improves; several low-frequency learners complete one or two comparable A/B probes with mixed or unfavorable B outcomes. Opportunity-weighted B success is high, but learner-weighted estimates and uncertainty differ. Publish both denominators and per-learner contribution; do not count repeated correlated probes as independent people or proclaim a universal winner.
- **C — Sparse evidence and safe exploration.** A learner has one low-quality B first attempt, an assisted repair and no eligible delayed probe yet; another has two clean but conflicting delayed results. Neither case establishes B failure or A superiority. Preserve normal scaffolding, due reviews and feasible A/B sampling; do not label the learner a permanent 'worked-example nonresponder', manufacture a delayed result, or withhold required objectives.
- **D — Differential follow-up conceals risk.** B has many completed independent delayed probes among confident learners but many missed or inaccessible follow-ups among learners with low baseline readiness; A has more balanced follow-up. The completed-only aggregate favors B. Report eligible, assigned, verified-delivered, independently followed-up and missingness counts by observed readiness and option. Withhold comparative efficacy where follow-up/overlap is insufficient; missing is not failure or success.
- **E — Reversible learner-specific hypothesis with shared standard.** Earlier comparable, verified A occasions favor A for one learner's written-request objective, while another learner favors B for the same can-do. Later genuinely delayed unaided unfamiliar probes reverse the first learner's pattern. Revise that learner/objective's uncertain option probabilities, preserve nonzero eligible alternative sampling, fixed CEFR rubric and required receptive/productive coverage; never transfer either preference automatically to other skills or learners.
- **F — Replayed, stale and noncomparable outcomes.** Duplicate a delayed probe, reverse offline upload order, insert a post-probe method exposure, include a changed rubric with no common anchor and an unsupported listening route. Stable-ID replay cannot inflate B's apparent pooled gain. Exclude uncalibrated, chronologically impossible or unavailable-route comparisons with explicit reason codes while retaining narrower valid learner observations; never count phantom listening/speaking success.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Four distinct reporting layers.** For A–F report objective/version, skill, rubric, eligible/assigned/verified-delivered method, independent first attempts, assistance, calibrated actual-delay retrieval and unseen transfer, due reviews, learner-specific hypothesis/confidence, pooled descriptive outcomes and causal-attribution status separately.
2. **No aggregate override of repeated contrary evidence.** In A, the pooled B advantage cannot erase repeated valid learner/objective-specific contrary independent outcomes. Mark `INDIVIDUAL_CONTRARY_EVIDENCE`, revise confidence and choose balanced/supported alternatives as warranted, without declaring a proven treatment harm.
3. **Unit-of-analysis integrity.** In B, publish unique learners, occasions, delivered exposures, eligible/completed delayed probes and learner-weighted versus opportunity-weighted results. Repeated probes from one learner cannot be presented as independent learner replications or support an unqualified global winner.
4. **Sparse outcomes do not create fixed types.** In C, immediate assisted success, one failure and absent delayed probes cannot establish a durable method ranking or permanent learner class. Mark `SPARSE_UNCERTAIN`; preserve feasible alternative sampling, normal support and shared mastery gates.
5. **Differential follow-up is visible.** In D, show denominators and missingness by observed baseline-readiness/opportunity stratum and option. A completed-only advantage with unequal follow-up is `FOLLOWUP_IMBALANCED`/`NOT_COMPARABLE` for affected comparisons; no invented outcomes, arbitrary zero imputation or unsupported inverse-probability weights.
6. **Heterogeneity and reversal remain objective-specific.** In E, different learners may receive different provisional method mixes for the same CEFR can-do only after sufficiently comparable independent evidence; later contradictory delayed/transfer evidence can revise those choices and confidence. Maintain nonzero eligible exploration and balanced skill/review coverage.
7. **Replay, assessment and modality integrity.** In F, event-ID deduplication, trusted occurrence-time ordering, assessment-version anchors and verified actual delivery yield invariant counts across replay orders. Post-probe instruction, uncalibrated scoring and unavailable listening/speaking cannot become comparable independent evidence.
8. **Interests, privacy and CEFR remain independent.** Across A–F, soft onboarding/demonstrated interests change context probabilities only; preserve adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts, unchanged objectives/prerequisites/rubrics, due reviews and productive/receptive obligations. Do not infer protected attributes or demand sensitive personal disclosures to form groups.
9. **Conservative comparison and release gate.** Without adequate comparable verified delivery, learner/opportunity coverage, calibrated clean delayed retrieval, unseen transfer and follow-up, classify the affected method claim `UNSUPPORTED`/`NOT_COMPARABLE`, preserve narrower legitimate mastery observations and use the balanced curriculum with normal scaffolds. Neither a synthetic PASS nor an aggregate correlation proves improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

For A–F publish the **exact tested engine HEAD**, executable command, deterministic seed/clock, versioned guardrail policy, complete immutable synthetic ledger, real existing CEFR can-do/skill and frozen rubric, assignment mechanism and verified exposure, first attempts/assistance, actual independent delay and unfamiliar transfer, baseline/readiness and opportunity comparability, learner- and opportunity-weighted denominators, within-learner repeated-outcome correlation handling, observed missingness and attrition, review/skill/context obligations, replay invariance, uncertainty/reason codes and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED for all nine assertions. Include a feasible fixed balanced comparator and do not invent unobserved outcomes, protected-group membership or causal counterfactuals.

A deterministic fixture PASS establishes **only** a software acceptance invariant. Claims that adaptive instruction preserves or improves durable independent learning across learners require separately reviewed longitudinal learner evidence with a credible comparable balanced baseline, sufficient delayed independent retrieval and unfamiliar transfer, calibrated uncertainty, and a specific examination of low-data learners and potential regressions without relaxing CEFR coverage. A pooled improvement is not a guarantee for any individual.
