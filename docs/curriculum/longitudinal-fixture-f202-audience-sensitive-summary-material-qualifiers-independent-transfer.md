Not a new CEFR objective, privacy policy, summarization product, shipped adaptive tutor or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F191 addresses relevance of an answer; F193 asks who received information; F197 preserves quantifier scope; F198 integrates multiple claims; F199 repairs misinformation; F201 attributes conflicting sources. **Distinct construct:** when a learner must *compress the same source for different legitimate recipients*, can they select what matters for each recipient while preserving qualifications that change the recipient's understanding or next action? A summary may be shorter and differently ordered without becoming a different claim. This is a communicative language task, not automated truth adjudication or a universal rule that every detail must be repeated.

## Fixed objective, source and recipient-purpose oracles

Bind only applicable **existing versioned** CEFR can-dos for summarizing, reporting, explaining, relaying instructions, mediating information or equivalent language tasks. Freeze objective ID/version, prerequisites, level-appropriate linguistic burden, target language/variety, source/rubric/oracle versions, accessible channel, help policy, meaningful-turn ceiling where applicable, first-unaided and delayed/unseen standards, thresholds, receptive/productive balance and exit gates. At lower levels, a short practical relay may suffice only where an existing can-do warrants it; complex multi-document synthesis is NOT_APPLICABLE otherwise. No new graduation gate. Preserve the unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix disagreement; B2 stays unavailable.

An independently reviewed **source-proposition oracle** freezes each exact utterance, speaker, time, version, channel, actual learner delivery, proposition and provenance; whether it is confirmed, proposed, conditional, approximate, disputed, corrected, attributed or unknown; and which details are logically dependent. The evaluator may not silently resolve disputed claims or assume a message reached a recipient. A later correction changes what should be relayed only when it was actually delivered before the learner's response. Summaries cannot replace “at least” with “exactly,” a proposal with a booking, a possibility with a fact, or a deadline with an already completed action.

A separate independently reviewed **recipient-purpose oracle** specifies each recipient's legitimate question, known context, needed decision/action, permissible detail and minimum materially relevant qualifiers. Evaluate whether a concise summary supports that purpose without fabricating certainty, omitting a decision-changing condition or revealing unnecessary private detail. Different summaries of the same source can both be correct. Accept natural paraphrases and appropriate uncertainty; do not require a fixed sentence order, maximal length, or formulaic register. A privacy-sensitive scenario requires a separately reviewed disclosure policy; this fixture does not itself authorize withholding legally or operationally required information.

The **actual-delivery and first-authorship ledger** records pseudonymous learner, stable session-qualified event/replay IDs, fixed can-do/source/recipient/oracle/rubric versions, exact source turns and updates actually rendered, recipient and purpose actually shown, first eligible unaided recipient-facing summary, omissions/additions and their consequence, hints/examples/completed summaries actually rendered, assisted repairs, optional reliable latency, recurring error type, instructional choice/dose/confidence, accessible modality route, and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed probes. Offered-but-unopened help is not exposure; a rendered full summary is answer-bearing. Failed audio earns no listening credit; visible text may support a separately bound reading route. Duplicate submissions, copied revisions and crossing midnight cannot create independent mastery or delayed retention.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Short summary preserves a decision-changing condition.** A library notice says “The workshop is planned for Friday at 16:00, provided the coordinator confirms the room by Thursday noon.” Nora asks whether she can tell guests the date is final. Accept “Friday at four is the plan, but the room still needs confirmation by Thursday noon; don't announce it as final yet.” Reject “The workshop is confirmed Friday” and “The workshop is cancelled.” Omitting the organizer's name may be legitimate compression; omitting the condition is not. A later verified confirmation changes the current summary only if delivered before the response.

**B — Two recipients require different but compatible summaries.** A community-event update says “The entrance has moved to the north gate; volunteers should arrive at 08:30 for setup, visitors at 10:00; bring an ID to collect a volunteer badge.” A visitor asks where and when to arrive: accept “Use the north gate; visitors enter at ten.” A volunteer asks for setup instructions: accept “North gate, 08:30 for setup, and bring ID for your badge.” Reject telling visitors that everyone must arrive at 08:30 or telling volunteers only the visitor start time. Omitting volunteer-badge details from the visitor summary is appropriate, not loss of mastery. The task oracle must freeze which audience was actually presented.

**C — A numeric limit and uncertainty survive compression.** A club notice says “At least twelve people registered; the room holds up to twenty, but the organizer hasn't confirmed the final attendance.” Asked to update a coordinator choosing a room, accept “There are at least twelve registrations, the room's maximum is twenty, and the final count is still unknown.” Reject “Exactly twelve will attend,” “Eight seats remain” and “Twenty people confirmed.” A short summary may omit the club's poster color, but not convert minimum registration, room capacity or unknown attendance into a definitive headcount.

**D — Appropriate selectivity does not invent or disclose unnecessary details.** A student tells the event organizer “I cannot attend the setup because of a private appointment; I can still edit the program remotely by six.” The organizer asks the learner for an operational update. Accept “They can't attend setup, but can edit the program remotely by six.” Reject “They refused to help,” “They will definitely finish editing” and a fabricated medical explanation. If the organizer has a separately established, legitimate disclosure requirement, the independently reviewed recipient policy—not the tutor's guess—governs what detail is necessary. This scenario tests concise communicative relevance, not a new privacy compliance rule.

**E — Answer-bearing help, source delivery and replay do not create independent mastery.** At 09:01 the learner reads A and independently writes “Friday is confirmed.” Preserve FIRST_UNAIDED_SUMMARY and MATERIAL_QUALIFIER_OMITTED. At 09:02 the tutor renders a full accurate example; at 09:03 the learner copies it. Record ANSWER_EXPOSURE and ASSISTED_REWRITE, not independent mastery. A 09:04 duplicate submission is deduplicated. If the audio version fails but an accurate transcript renders, do not award listening credit; a separately bound reading route may count. An unopened hint is not exposure. A later independent probe needs a genuine interval and changed source, recipient purpose and qualifier structure—not renamed library rooms.

**F — Reversible adaptation, contrary evidence and interests remain separate.** Learner A writes fluent short summaries but repeatedly drops conditions needed by recipients. Across comparable completed sessions, RECIPIENT_GOAL_FIRST, MATERIAL_QUALIFIER_CHECK and BALANCED_DEFAULT are actually delivered and alternated. Tentatively favor an option only if later first-unaided unseen recipient summaries preserve decision-changing qualifications under matched complexity, audience stakes, language burden, help, channel, topic and delay. Learner B initially succeeds after a complete proposition map but later adds unsupported certainty to an unfamiliar recipient update; lower confidence, resume balanced exploration and test SHORT_VS_DISTORTED_SUMMARY_CONTRAST. An easier source, fewer qualifiers, familiar gaming context, extra hints or shorter delay is confounding, **not** evidence of a causal winner. Missing follow-up stays unknown. Gaming may softly influence one example; adjacent sports, neutral libraries and unfamiliar community events remain available. Required can-dos and modalities are never filtered out by interests.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Shared objective, unchanged standard.** A–F bind only applicable existing versioned CEFR can-dos with independently reviewed source and recipient-purpose oracles, preserving prerequisites, level burden, turn ceiling, thresholds, accessibility, skill coverage and exit gates; otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE/UNSUPPORTED.
2. **Material conditions survive compression.** A distinguishes a contingent Friday plan from confirmation or cancellation; irrelevant detail may be omitted without dropping a decision-changing qualifier.
3. **Recipient-specific relevance without contradiction.** B permits two different concise summaries of the same source, each preserving the correct recipient's arrival time and necessary instructions.
4. **Quantifiers and unknowns survive.** C preserves lower bounds, capacity limits and unresolved final attendance without inventing an exact total or remaining seats.
5. **Selective reporting without invention.** D communicates operationally relevant availability, avoids fabricated motives and unnecessary disclosure, and defers disclosure obligations to a separately reviewed policy.
6. **First authorship and modality integrity.** E distinguishes independent error, rendered complete example, assisted copying, unopened hints, failed audio, possible text route and replay deduplication; no phantom independent or listening credit.
7. **Matched reversible instructional adaptation.** F compares actually delivered options against later first-unaided recipient summaries; purpose, stakes, source complexity, help, topic, channel and delay confounds lower confidence and prevent causal-winner claims.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer and nonexclusive interests.** Require distinct genuine sessions, changed source/recipient/qualifier structures, first-unaided output, explicit missing follow-up, no script leakage, balanced receptive/productive skills and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar context rotation; never skip objectives for interests.
9. **Honest evidence and protected scope.** Synthetic histories and generic CI are not executable audience-summary QA, a shipped tutor or proof of improved human retention. Require exact-head executable delivery/provenance/response checks, observed reversible teaching decisions and ethically reviewed delayed independent outcomes against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge, Pre-A1 changes, A1–C2 enablement or weakened B2 boundaries.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic design histories, not observed learner behavior or executable tests. They specify how to adapt a summary to the recipient while retaining materially necessary qualifications and uncertainty. Remain UNSUPPORTED until independently implemented and verified.
