# F211 — Task challenge calibration, difficulty confounding and independent transfer

**Status:** design-only, synthetic *cross-cutting* longitudinal curriculum acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until an independently reviewed executable exact-head harness demonstrates the policy and evidence behavior. Human-learning gains require separate ethically reviewed longitudinal evidence. This is **not** a new CEFR can-do, an implemented difficulty engine, a universal optimal challenge band, a learner-ability diagnosis, a new graduation gate or permission to open A1–C2. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F206 audits method-selection confounding, F208 specifies paired metamorphic invariants, F209 covers limited review opportunities and F210 covers feedback timing/dose/fading. **Distinct question in F211:** when the tutor chooses *how demanding the next eligible task should be*, can it distinguish task burden and access failures from learner competence, keep challenge meaningful, and change its **next actually delivered task** using later independent retention/transfer without manipulating CEFR standards or optimizing easy short-term accuracy?

## Fixed objective, challenge and evidence contract

Every history binds an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, prerequisites, target language/variety, communicative intent, independently reviewed semantic and pragmatic rubric, supported assessment modality, task family, accessibility needs, meaningful-turn ceiling if any, due-review obligations and unchanged independent mastery/exit standards. The English utterances are illustrative, not new released content. Preserve the unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix mismatch as **UNSUPPORTED**; no fixture resolves it by silently replacing production with recognition.

Challenge is a **task property**, not a permanent student category or a score inferred from correctness. Independently review and version a task-demand vector: linguistic complexity, discourse length, number of meaning-critical constraints, response openness, context familiarity/novelty, distractors, modality burden, accessibility demands, time pressure if genuinely imposed, and any answer-bearing assistance. Store separately (a) the required CEFR rubric and (b) optional practice-demand variants. A harder-looking topic, longer sentence or unfamiliar interest is not automatically a more difficult language objective. If equivalence is unreviewed, mark tasks NOT_COMPARABLE; do not normalize away a missing skill.

Freeze a deterministic **opportunity-and-outcome ledger**: pseudonymous learner; session/run/event/item-family IDs; trusted occurrence order versus upload order; objective/task/rubric/policy versions; candidate task variants and independent demand ratings; eligibility and selection reason with uncertainty; topic-family interest weights; supported/planned/actually rendered modality; whether instructions and prompt were accessible; first unaided semantic response; error type/severity; retries; actual hint/model/transcript exposure; assistance; observed latency only when reliable and interpretable; completion/abandonment; delayed and unseen probes (due/offered/rendered/completed/missing); and subsequent **actually delivered** task choice. An unrendered recommendation, a same-session paraphrase, an uploaded replay, a transcript substituted for failed audio, or an answer-exposed retry cannot be promoted to clean transfer.

A policy may choose among eligible practice-demand variants, alternate explanation and scaffolding, or schedule an independent diagnostic probe. It must record *why* and keep alternatives open for exploration. The fixed can-do, noncompensatory modality obligations, semantic error rubric, mastery threshold and exit rules cannot be raised/lowered merely because a task was easier/harder or a learner likes the topic. No universal target success rate, frustration diagnosis, latency threshold, fixed difficulty ladder or automatic “learning-style” label is asserted.

Oracle labels: OBJECTIVE_FIXED, DEMAND_REVIEWED, DEMAND_UNKNOWN, TASK_RENDERED, FIRST_UNAIDED, MEANING_CRITICAL_ERROR, ASSISTED_REPAIR, CLEAN_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_INDEPENDENT_TRANSFER, CHALLENGE_INCREASE_WITH_REASON, CHALLENGE_REDUCE_WITH_REASON, REPROBE_WITH_REASON, ALTERNATIVES_ELIGIBLE, LATENCY_UNRELIABLE, ACCESS_FAILURE, MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE, TOPIC_ROTATION_PRESERVED, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, NOT_COMPARABLE, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED and UNSUPPORTED. These are auditable observations and decisions, not learner identities.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — An easy accuracy streak is not independent transfer.** The fixed can-do is to convey a request with a deadline and a condition. In three completed sessions, a learner answers near-identical, single-clause recognition items correctly on the first try, while the independent reviewed rubric requires an open communicative response. An unsafe tutor reports mastery and repeatedly serves only easy recognition because completion is high. The expected tutor retains the correct *recognition* observations, leaves required open-production and unfamiliar-context evidence unearned, and selects an eligible open-response practice or diagnostic task with recorded demand and uncertainty. In the paired control, genuinely new independent open responses with delayed/unseen transfer may justify increasing challenge for practice, **without changing the CEFR gate**. Same-day duplicates and changed topic labels do not count as new transfer.

**B — A sudden harder task must not masquerade as a loss of underlying competence.** A learner independently succeeds on reviewed comparable tasks in earlier completed sessions. A new prompt adds three constraints, unfamiliar discourse and a longer response requirement; the first unaided answer omits one meaning-critical condition. The tutor records the error **and** the changed demand vector; it may provide targeted instruction or select a matched diagnostic before changing its hypothesis about competence. It cannot conclude from the single incomparable item that the learner “regressed” or permanently assign an easier track. A paired history changes only the task demand, keeping objective, learner history, context familiarity and access constant: the two task outcomes cannot be pooled as equal-difficulty evidence. A later clean unfamiliar probe can revise the hypothesis in either direction.

**C — Latency and nonresponse need a valid opportunity.** A learner pauses before an independently correct open answer. In one branch, reliable on-device timing exists; in another, a backgrounded tab, device slowdown and intermittent connection make timing untrustworthy. A third branch has a prompt that never actually renders. The tutor may consider reliable latency as a weak contextual signal but must never use it alone to diagnose struggle, assign a fixed ability label, penalize mastery or force a simpler task. The nonrendered prompt is no assessment opportunity; missing responses and abandoned sessions are not failures. If the audio route is inaccessible, offer a supported accessible route and retain any unmet listening/speaking requirement as UNSUPPORTED, not “mastered through transcript.”

**D — Hard assisted repair cannot outrank clean moderate performance.** A more demanding open task yields an incorrect first attempt; a worked example then renders and the learner copies a correct answer. A moderately demanding, independently reviewed comparable task yields a correct first unaided answer and later clean retrieval. The tutor keeps assisted hard-task completion and independent moderate-task evidence distinct. It may sample a supported intermediate or hard variant for further diagnosis, but cannot use the copied hard response as proof of higher competence or automatically downgrade the learner because of one error. In a control, the hint was planned but never rendered; do not invent exposure. The next delivered choice, not merely the planned choice, must be checked.

**E — Later independent learning outranks easy immediate wins.** On comparable existing can-do practice, a low-demand sequence produces more immediate unaided successes; a cautiously increased-demand sequence produces fewer immediate successes but, among **comparable completed follow-ups**, more independently authored delayed retrieval and unfamiliar-context transfer. The tutor should update uncertainty and its next challenge-sampling rationale using these later outcomes rather than maximizing the easy streak; it must **not** assert causal superiority from synthetic histories. A paired control makes the harder sequence's follow-ups mostly missing: mark NOT_COMPARABLE, disclose due/offered/rendered/completed denominators, and avoid treating the remaining completers as the whole cohort. Continue exploration with a feasible balanced fallback when evidence is weak.

**F — Interests, modality and replay cannot manufacture calibrated difficulty.** A learner prefers games, so the tutor samples a game-themed practice task alongside adjacent community, neutral workplace and unfamiliar travel contexts. An interest-only transformation changes the probabilities of those examples but not the required can-do, independently reviewed demand ratings, method-evidence ledger, clean mastery status or candidate method eligibility. If a game task actually has easier vocabulary, that change must be logged as a **difficulty confound**, not attributed to interest or a better teaching method. One intended listening task fails to play and renders a transcript; it cannot provide listening evidence. Later offline uploads replay events out of order; deduplicate by session-qualified identity, use trusted occurrence/exposure order and quarantine unknown ordering. Preserve actual longitudinal sampling of neutral and unfamiliar topics and outstanding receptive/productive coverage debt.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **No standards drift.** Bind every history to real versioned CEFR objectives, prerequisite/semantic rubrics, task-family mappings, supported modalities, independent mastery/exit gates, due reviews and meaningful-turn ceilings. Challenge variation never invents a can-do, waives a skill or resolves the unsupported B2 Arc 6 mismatch.
2. **Task demand is explicit and independently reviewed.** Publish deterministic seed/clock, candidate variants, demand-vector components, task/rubric versions, accessibility and topic familiarity. Mark unreviewed or differently burdened comparisons NOT_COMPARABLE rather than declaring a universal difficulty rank.
3. **Easy accuracy does not replace open transfer.** A preserves valid easy recognition evidence but cannot promote it to independently required open production, delayed retrieval or unseen transfer. A later clean, completed and meaningfully distinct probe is necessary.
4. **Difficulty confounding is not learner regression.** B records the meaning-critical first-attempt error and changed task demand separately; the next diagnostic/scaffold/challenge decision is reversible and explained. A single harder item cannot permanently classify a learner.
5. **Latency, opportunity and accessibility are honest.** C distinguishes reliable measured latency, unreliable latency, prompt not rendered and abandoned attempts. None alone creates a deficit or mastery claim; failed audio/transcript routes do not silently satisfy listening or speaking.
6. **Assistance and actual delivery remain auditable.** D separates first unaided answers, rendered answer-bearing models, copied repairs and planned-but-undelivered support. Verify the next *actually delivered* task, alternatives, uncertainty and reason, not only a policy recommendation.
7. **Independent delayed transfer guides challenge.** E updates the challenge hypothesis using comparable completed delayed and unfamiliar probes while exposing selection/follow-up denominators. Missing follow-up is UNKNOWN, not failure/success; synthetic data cannot prove causal human gains.
8. **Interest, replay and skill coverage stay separate.** F allows soft context-weight changes, preserves adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic sampling and refuses to treat interest-linked vocabulary ease as method efficacy. Deduplicate event replays; quarantine unknown chronology; preserve all unmet noncompensatory CEFR modalities.
9. **Implementation and benefit remain unproven without independent checks.** Publish machine-readable A–F histories, paired transformations, expected per-step decisions and rendered task events, independently reviewed demand/semantic oracles, exact objective/rubric/policy versions and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED in an independently reviewed executable exact-head harness. Claim human learning gains only after ethically reviewed longitudinal comparison with a feasible balanced baseline, comparable difficulty/opportunity, delayed unaided retention, unseen transfer, attrition/selection analysis and no hidden loss of required CEFR skills. Do not mutate Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase, use paid Claude, touch EvoLabs, auto-merge, change Pre-A1 or release A1–C2.

**Fixture verdict:** F211 makes **task-demand calibration and the next delivered challenge** falsifiable without confusing easy completion, answer-exposed repairs, unreliable latency or topic familiarity with durable independent competence. **UNSUPPORTED** until executed and independently reviewed; even a passing software harness would not establish human learning benefit.
