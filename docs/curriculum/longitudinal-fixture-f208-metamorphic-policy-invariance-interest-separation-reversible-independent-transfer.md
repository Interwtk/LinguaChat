# F208 — Metamorphic policy invariants: interest separation, evidence integrity and reversible independent transfer

**Status:** design-only, synthetic *cross-cutting* curriculum acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until independently reviewed executable metamorphic checks run against a named exact engine HEAD and ethically reviewed longitudinal learner evidence exists. This document is not a new CEFR objective, an implemented policy, proof of causal learning gains, a new graduation requirement or permission to open A1–C2. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F206 audits selection bias, incomplete follow-up and actual delivery; F207 tests prerequisite diagnosis and bounded remediation. **Distinct question in F208:** when two histories differ *only in one controlled input*, does the tutor's **next actual instructional decision and evidence classification** change only where that input legitimately permits it? This is a metamorphic *test-oracle* specification, not another list of language exercises. It catches silent coupling between interests and methods, assistance and mastery, replay order and evidence, missingness and scores, or obsolete rubrics and graduation.

## Frozen objective and paired-history harness

Resolve one or more **existing, versioned CEFR can-dos** with language/variety, source blueprint and prerequisites, actual supported skill route, task difficulty, communicative intent, independent rubric/oracle version, help and accessibility policy, meaningful-turn ceiling if applicable, delayed retrieval and unseen-transfer standard, due-review obligations, and unchanged noncompensatory mastery/exit thresholds. An English example is illustrative, never evidence that a specific level or modality is implemented. Preserve the unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix boundary rather than silently deciding it.

For each pair, define a deterministic **base history H**, a single declared transformation **T(H)** and a frozen decision point after all eligible completed events. Keep the learner/objective version, rubric, actual task burden, availability, accessibility, initial ability evidence and sampling seed identical **except for the named transformation**. If a supposedly controlled change also changes task difficulty, answer exposure, support, follow-up opportunity or context familiarity, mark the pair NOT_COMPARABLE rather than force an invariance result.

Minimum synthetic input schema: pseudonymous learner key; objective, prerequisite and rubric versions; session-qualified immutable event and item-family IDs; trustworthy occurrence versus upload ordering; completed-run and learning-day provenance; chosen and *actually delivered* method/dose/modality; eligible alternatives and uncertainty recorded before outcomes; separate topic-family/interest weights; first unaided semantic response and error; hint/model/transcript/answer exposure; retry or independent authorship; due/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed probes; intervening material, genuine delay and independently reviewed unseen-transfer result; replay identity; supported versus unavailable modalities. Unknown timing, missing selection probabilities and unverified novelty stay UNKNOWN.

Minimum output schema per H and T(H): next selected **existing** can-do, eligible method set, actual next method/scaffold/pacing/review decision, uncertainty and exploration reason, chosen topic family, first-unaided/assisted/independent delayed/unseen-transfer evidence counts, contamination and missingness flags, due-review and receptive/productive coverage debt, objective/rubric compatibility, and a verdict for each invariant. A candidate recommendation is not proof of actual delivery: a separate subsequent event must show what rendered or played. Use versioned policy and independent scoring oracles; record exact seed/clock and complete paired diffs.

Metamorphic outcomes are **MUST_REMAIN_EQUAL**, **MAY_CHANGE_WITH_REASON**, **MUST_NOT_INCREASE**, **MUST_INVALIDATE_COMPARISON**, or **UNSUPPORTED**. These apply to specified output fields, not to a blanket identical UI: two valid contexts may have different wording. Unknown outcomes never become zero, a fabricated negative response, or a successful delayed retrieval. Do not assert fixed universal method probabilities, topic quotas, time intervals or learning-style categories.

## Six deterministic paired synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Interest-only perturbation cannot rewrite teaching evidence.** H contains repeated independent first attempts and later delayed/unseen transfer on the same versioned request can-do, with both concise and worked-example instruction actually delivered in matched tasks. T(H) changes **only** the learner's soft interest weights from music to games; the candidate contexts remain independently reviewed as equivalent in difficulty, rubric, exposure and communicative intent. The next required can-do, mastery status, method-evidence ledger, method confidence and eligible instructional choices MUST_REMAIN_EQUAL. The chosen example topic MAY_CHANGE_WITH_REASON. Across later sessions, preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts must remain eligible and actually sampled; neither interest setting becomes an exclusive filter. If the context swap changes difficulty or accessibility, mark the method comparison NOT_COMPARABLE rather than claim the invariant was tested.

**B — Adding assisted success cannot manufacture independent mastery.** H includes an incorrect first unaided response to a novel communicative request. T(H) appends two correct retries after a rendered answer-bearing worked example, with unchanged original first attempt. The first-unaided score, clean delayed retrieval and unseen-transfer counts MUST_REMAIN_EQUAL; assisted-repair and exposure counts may increase. The next teaching decision MAY_CHANGE_WITH_REASON toward a bounded scaffold or diagnostic contrast, but the can-do cannot be promoted, its CEFR gate lowered, or the worked example declared superior from the copied answers. An unopened hint in a separate control is not exposure; a rendered answer is exposure even if the learner says they ignored it.

**C — Clean contradictory delayed outcomes must update uncertainty and possibly choice.** H contains multiple comparable completed sessions tentatively favoring concise instruction for a specific can-do, while exploration of worked examples remains possible. T(H) adds two **genuinely delayed, independent, unfamiliar, completed** probes after matched actual delivery: concise is followed by repeated meaning-critical failures while worked examples are followed by correct independent responses. The tutor MUST incorporate the new evidence, lower or otherwise explicitly re-evaluate confidence in the earlier concise preference, and produce a revised reasoned next decision; it MAY_CHANGE_WITH_REASON to worked examples or a balanced exploration choice. A forced switch or causal superiority claim is not required when data remain sparse. A separate control appending only immediate assisted success MUST NOT be treated as equivalent to the delayed evidence. The CEFR objective and standards remain fixed.

**D — Event replay and upload permutation must not change the learning history.** H records a first unaided probe, a later hint and a completed session with stable session-qualified event IDs. T(H) duplicates several events, reorders their upload, and adds an offline answer exposure whose **trusted occurrence** preceded a later probe but whose upload followed it. Deduplicated counts and final classification MUST_REMAIN_EQUAL across every upload permutation of the *same occurrence history*. The later exposed probe must be contaminated in both orders; the earlier clean attempt remains clean. A conflicting or unverifiable occurrence order yields UNKNOWN/quarantine, never a fabricated clean transfer. Identical local attempt IDs from different sessions must remain distinct; an uncompleted run cannot earn completed delayed retrieval.

**E — Deleting follow-up observation cannot turn uncertainty into failure or success.** H contains an actually offered, rendered and completed delayed independent probe after meaningful intervening material. T(H) removes its completion event while retaining due and offered/rendered provenance; no learner answer is invented. The completed delayed denominator and evidence count MUST_NOT_INCREASE, and the missing-follow-up flag must appear. The tutor may adjust future review *opportunity* while preserving the original can-do, but it must not impute failure, infer maintained mastery, treat nonresponse as an instructional-method loss, or claim a higher retention rate among all eligible learners from the smaller completer set. A second control removes the offer as well: due-but-unoffered review debt must remain visible and distinct from offered-but-uncompleted.

**F — Rubric drift and unavailable modality cannot be silently compared away.** H contains valid independent reading/written-production evidence under a single versioned rubric, with required listening/speaking routes explicitly unavailable. T(H) changes the assessment rubric or objective version for later observations **without a reviewed equivalence mapping**, and separately changes a planned listening contrast to failed audio with only a written transcript delivered. The mixed-version method comparison MUST_INVALIDATE_COMPARISON or be partitioned by version; no automatic regrading, cross-version mastery carryover or phantom listening evidence. The transcript may support a separately valid reading task, not listening mastery. Preserve the existing required CEFR coverage, due reviews, topic rotation and balanced supported options; mark unimplemented routes UNSUPPORTED instead of relaxing graduation. Replays and interest-weight changes cannot repair the missing equivalence or missing audio.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-bound standards never drift.** Every pair binds real versioned can-dos, prerequisites, language/variety oracles, independent scoring, delayed/unseen standards, modality availability, due reviews and unchanged CEFR mastery/exit rules. Unknown mappings and unresolved B2 boundaries remain UNSUPPORTED, not invented.
2. **Controlled transformations are explicit.** Publish H, T, exact changed fields, unchanged controls, deterministic seed/clock, source versions, comparable task burden and a machine-checkable expected relation per output field. Reject comparisons with unacknowledged topic/difficulty/help/availability confounding.
3. **Interest is not method evidence.** A permits equivalent context selection to change but forbids an interest-only update from changing independent ability evidence, required objective, method confidence or CEFR status. Preserve actual longitudinal adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar sampling, not just eligibility.
4. **Assisted repair is not independent achievement.** B preserves the original first unaided response, distinguishes rendered versus unopened support, counts copied retries as assisted, and forbids new independent mastery or a method winner without later clean evidence.
5. **Contradictory delayed evidence reaches the next decision.** C proves that new completed, independently reviewed, comparable delayed/unseen observations alter the policy's evidence state and uncertainty/reasoning; the next choice is revisable and alternatives remain sampled. A synthetic result does not establish human causal benefit.
6. **Replay, chronology and completion are invariant.** D produces the same deduplicated evidence and decisions under upload permutations of identical trusted occurrences, preserves cross-session event identity, respects exposure chronology and excludes abandoned runs. Untrusted ordering stays UNKNOWN.
7. **Missingness and opportunity are not scores.** E reports due, offered, rendered, completed and missing denominators separately. Removing a completion cannot increase clean retrieval evidence, fabricate a negative answer or erase review debt; method comparisons disclose selective follow-up.
8. **Version and modality integrity are nonnegotiable.** F refuses unreviewed cross-rubric comparisons and failed-modality credit; a reading transcript is not listening evidence. Required CEFR skills, supported receptive/productive balance, alternative exploration and soft topic rotation remain visible.
9. **Release claims require independent proof.** A–F are design-only metamorphic specifications. Before claiming implementation, require independently reviewed exact-head executable pair generation, objective/rubric/decision/delivery/provenance assertions and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED outputs. Before claiming adaptive efficacy, additionally require an ethically reviewed longitudinal learner evaluation against a feasible balanced baseline, comparable opportunities, delayed independent retention, unseen transfer, missingness/selection-bias analysis and no hidden loss of required skills. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge, Pre-A1 changes or A1–C2 release.

**Fixture verdict:** F208 turns the adaptive-tutor vision into **paired counterfactual-input software invariants**: the teaching method may respond to real independent outcomes, the example topic may respond to interests, and neither is allowed to corrupt the other's evidence. **UNSUPPORTED** until executed and independently reviewed; a passing synthetic harness would validate software behavior, not prove improved human learning.
