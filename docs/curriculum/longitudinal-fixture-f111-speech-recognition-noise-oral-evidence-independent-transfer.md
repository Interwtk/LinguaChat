# F111 — Speech-recognition channel noise, oral-evidence attribution and independent unseen transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD with real versioned CEFR-linked oral tasks, consented/adjudicated speech evidence, actual render/exposure chronology and longitudinal first-unaided outcomes. This is **not** a shipped ASR capability, an accent detector, a reproducible production defect, CEFR speaking mastery or proof of human learning gains. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F33 protects genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish assigned versus delivered help and tutor-origin errors; F49 protects accessibility/construct equivalence; F51/F64 distinguish self-repair and fluency from accuracy; F53 protects register and language-variety appropriateness; F54 communication repair; F56 feedback timing; F104 confound-aware method selection; F107 unreliable feedback provenance; F108 within-turn disfluency; F109 code-switching; F110 paralinguistic cue boundaries. **Distinct construct:** when an oral attempt passes through microphone capture, voice-activity detection, speech recognition, normalization and evaluation, can the tutor separate **what the learner actually said** from what the pipeline guessed, and evaluate the required CEFR speaking construct without penalizing channel errors or granting phantom mastery? A transcript is a fallible observation, not an authoritative record of pronunciation, fluency, interaction or meaning.

## Source, observation and independence contract

Resolve the real versioned CEFR descriptor/can-do, level blueprint/prerequisites, oral communicative purpose, elicitation and recipient, authorized language/variety/register, task rubric and evaluator, supported audio modality, consent/retention policy, construct-equivalent accessibility route (if one exists), first-unaided threshold, permitted clarification and help, protected skill coverage, due reviews, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer and exit gate. Specify whether the assessed construct is spoken intelligibility to a defined recipient, lexical/grammatical meaning, pronunciation of a contrast, interactional repair, fluency, listening comprehension or written transcription. **Do not infer pronunciation from text, require a native-like accent, or award speaking credit for typed text or TTS playback.** If the needed audio/recipient evidence is absent or untrustworthy, return UNKNOWN/UNSUPPORTED for that construct rather than learner failure or success.

Retain a privacy-minimized, session-qualified **speech-evidence provenance trace**: source/task/CEFR/rubric/evaluator versions; elicitation and recipient; consent state and permitted retention; recording/capture availability, clipping, dropouts, overlap, device/channel and VAD segmentation when observed; audio or authorized adjudication reference; ASR model/configuration/language settings and N-best hypotheses/confidence where available; punctuation, number, language and code-switch normalization; human/source-authorized adjudication and its uncertainty; learner-authored first utterance and chronological repairs; tutor hints, model audio, captions, suggested phrases, transcripts and feedback **actually rendered** before each attempt; independent recipient outcome; delayed/unseen follow-up eligibility, offering, completion and missingness; selected method, alternative methods, scaffold dose, difficulty, topic familiarity, prior exposure and review state. Distinguish **recorded acoustic evidence**, **machine hypothesis**, **adjudicated learner production**, **recipient comprehension** and **tutor-origin suggestion**. Never reconstruct an absent recording from ASR text.

Task-local labels may include AUDIO_CAPTURE_VALID, AUDIO_MISSING, AUDIO_CLIPPED, OVERLAPPING_SPEECH, VAD_BOUNDARY_UNCERTAIN, ASR_HYPOTHESIS, ASR_SUBSTITUTION, ASR_INSERTION, ASR_DELETION, NORMALIZATION_CHANGED_MEANING, LANGUAGE_ROUTING_UNCERTAIN, ADJUDICATED_UTTERANCE, ADJUDICATION_DISAGREEMENT, SOURCE_VALID_PRONUNCIATION_CONTRAST, RECIPIENT_UNDERSTOOD, RECIPIENT_CLARIFICATION, LEARNER_SELF_REPAIR, TUTOR_PROMPTED_REPAIR, ANSWER_BEARING_TRANSCRIPT_RENDERED, FIRST_UNAIDED, ASSISTED, SPEAKING_EVIDENCE_UNSUPPORTED, ACCESS_ROUTE_DIFFERENT_CONSTRUCT, DELAYED_UNAIDED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_UNAIDED_TRANSFER, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, REPLAY_DEDUPED, VERSION_CONFLICT_QUARANTINED, BALANCED_DEFAULT and NOT_APPLICABLE. These are **task-event labels**, never stable accent, disability, ethnicity, nationality, fluency-type or learning-style labels.

Feasible reversible instructional candidates include BALANCED_DEFAULT, MEANING_FIRST_SPEAKING, TARGETED_SOUND_CONTRAST_WITH_VALID_AUDIO, INDEPENDENT_RECIPIENT_REPAIR, SHORTER_PROMPTS_WITH_UNCHANGED_OBJECTIVE, LISTEN_THEN_PRODUCE_WITH_HELP_ACCOUNTING, AUDIO_CHANNEL_TROUBLESHOOTING, SOURCE_VALID_OTHER and an authorized construct-equivalent accessibility alternative. Only supported methods may be selected; changing a microphone setting is **measurement repair**, not proof that a teaching method improved learning. Change modality mix, explanations, scaffolding, pacing, ordering, review spacing and practice dose only in ways that preserve required CEFR skills and independent evidence. Interest personalization is a separate soft topic sampler with preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — ASR substitution versus actual learner meaning.** A source-authored oral scheduling task requires “Tuesday at two” and includes an authorized, independently adjudicated recording of the learner clearly saying that phrase to a recipient who understands it. An ASR hypothesis instead outputs “Thursday at two” because of a documented recognition substitution. Another learner genuinely says “Thursday at two” in a clean recording, with an adjudicated wrong-day outcome. Both have the same raw ASR string, but only the latter has a learner-origin semantic error. The first cannot lose speaking evidence because a recognizer misheard; neither receives automatic speaking mastery solely from a corrected transcript. A third case has no reliable recording or recipient adjudication: the speaking verdict is UNSUPPORTED, not assumed correct from the target sentence.

**B — Accent, disfluency and clipping without trait inference.** A source-authored roleplay accepts multiple task-valid pronunciations/varieties that remain intelligible to its intended recipient. One learner pauses and self-repairs “four—sorry, five tickets” before any tutor prompt, and the recipient receives five; an overaggressive VAD drops “sorry, five” and ASR records four. Another recording clips the critical word entirely, so no adjudication is possible. Preserve the first learner's complete self-repair and recipient outcome; do not treat pause duration, accent, nationality, speech difference or VAD behavior as automatic incompetence. Mark the clipped case UNSUPPORTED and offer a safe re-attempt without inventing the missing word.

**C — Transcript exposure contaminates later oral evidence.** In a source-authored oral task, the learner first gives an independently intelligible response, then opens an answer-bearing transcript and repeats the tutor's suggested wording. The first attempt remains independently attributable; the repeat is assisted. In a paired history, the transcript/model audio was **assigned but never rendered** before the first response, so it must not contaminate that response. A third history exposes the answer-bearing transcript before recording: a correct later imitation cannot count as first-unaided production. Listening to tutor TTS, clicking replay or reading a caption is never by itself speaking evidence.

**D — Language routing, numbers and recipient clarification.** A source-authorized multilingual context includes a proper name and a code-switched loanword; the recognizer silently forces everything into one language and normalizes “fifteen” to “fifty” or “one five” to “15.” The original consented speech and adjudicated recipient exchange determine whether the learner communicated the source-required quantity. If the utterance remains ambiguous, an independently initiated “Did you mean fifteen or fifty?” can be valid interactional repair when the objective permits it. Do not conflate ASR language identification, punctuation restoration, digit formatting or code-switching with lexical mastery or failure. A pronunciation-specific objective still needs source-valid acoustic adjudication, not merely successful semantic clarification.

**E — Reversible adaptation with channel and topic controls.** Across comparable fictional sessions, learner A receives targeted pronunciation contrasts and initially obtains high ASR confidence on familiar music topics, but delayed independent speech with an unfamiliar recipient fails to transfer. Meaning-first recipient-repair practice initially looks less polished in ASR yet later produces better first-unaided intelligibility and unseen transfer. Learner B has the opposite delayed pattern on matched CEFR tasks. A third learner changes microphones between methods, making the apparent gain noncomparable until channel conditions are controlled or explicitly stratified. Hold objective, rubric, task difficulty, source variety, recording quality, actual help, practice dose, topic familiarity and follow-up missingness visible. Update capability-local uncertain hypotheses, sample safe alternatives, protect required oral and other CEFR skills, and rotate preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topics. Do not optimize ASR confidence, imitation quality or immediate assisted correctness as the learning goal.

**F — Offline speech packets, adjudication versions and privacy boundaries.** Two distinct real sessions reuse a local recording ID, a third packet is a true replay, and an offline first utterance arrives after the tutor has already logged feedback from a later turn. A revised ASR model changes the transcript, while an authorized adjudicator corrects a speaker attribution on a consented clip. Use session-qualified event identity, trusted capture/render chronology, effective source/adjudication versions and exact help exposure to reconstruct the original first-unaided status; deduplicate only true replays. Quarantine irreconcilable chronology or conflicting adjudication rather than inventing a canonical utterance. If consent is withdrawn or media is unavailable under retention rules, preserve only allowed minimal provenance and mark affected audio-dependent claims UNSUPPORTED; do not resurrect deleted speech or treat absent audio as a learner error.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source-valid CEFR construct and supported modality.** A–F require real versioned can-do, blueprint/prerequisites, oral purpose, target recipient, accepted variety, rubric/evaluator, supported audio and consent route, first-unaided threshold, delayed/unseen probes, reviews and exit gate. Text-only activities and unimplemented voice cannot claim oral evidence. Missing essential speech source/adjudication yields UNSUPPORTED; irrelevant acoustic fields are NOT_APPLICABLE.
2. **Learner-versus-channel error attribution.** A/B/D retain original audio/adjudication provenance separately from ASR/VAD/normalization hypotheses and recipient outcomes. Machine substitutions, insertions, deletions, segmentation, clipping and language routing do not automatically become learner errors or successes. Where attribution cannot be resolved, report uncertainty/UNSUPPORTED rather than hallucinated ground truth.
3. **Pronunciation, meaning, fluency and interaction remain distinct.** A/B/D evaluate only the source-defined construct. An intelligible authorized variety is not failed for non-native accent; a transcript cannot certify acoustic contrasts; a disfluent but communicatively successful self-repair is not automatically wrong. Pauses, latency, speech differences or nationality cannot act as universal mastery or deficit labels.
4. **First-unaided attempt and actual rendered assistance.** B/C/F preserve exact learner attempt, spontaneous self-repair, tutor-prompted repair, transcript/model-audio/feedback render chronology and recipient response. Answer-bearing content shown before a later utterance makes that utterance assisted; assigned but unrendered content does not. ASR correction after the fact never upgrades a model-assisted attempt to independent.
5. **Consent, privacy and accessibility without construct substitution.** B/D/F respect consent, minimal retention, deletion and authorized adjudication; never infer identity, disability or sensitive traits from speech. A validated construct-equivalent alternative may satisfy its authorized objective; typing or reading a transcript cannot silently fulfill a speaking/pronunciation descriptor. Unavailable or nonconsented audio is UNSUPPORTED, not learner failure.
6. **Method choice versus measurement repair.** A/E/F distinguish pedagogical method from microphone, recognizer, language-routing or evaluator version changes. Log feasible options, delivered instruction, support dose, channel quality, source/task/topic controls, uncertainty, fallback and safe alternative sampling. ASR confidence gains or immediate imitation do not establish durable learning; contradictory delayed evidence can reverse the method recommendation.
7. **Independent delayed retrieval, unseen transfer and protected skills.** A–F use source-authored new prompts/recipients/realizations with meaningful intervening time/material and first-unaided oral production or repair where required; prevent memorized transcript and tutor imitation from counting as transfer. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators and preserve listening, reading, writing, speaking/free production, due reviews and CEFR exit gates.
8. **Topic, replay, version and chronology invariance.** E/F rotate preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts without changing construct or threshold. Session-qualified deduplication, reordered receipt, model upgrades, adjudication corrections and consent-effective boundaries cannot alter historical assistance status or manufacture new oral evidence. Irreconcilable cases are quarantined.
9. **Assertion-level reproducibility and bounded claims.** A–F emit source/CEFR/rubric/evaluator versions, consent and audio-channel provenance, ASR hypotheses versus adjudicated production, recipient outcomes, actual help chronology, learner/channel error attribution, uncertainty, delayed/unseen denominators, method/topic controls, replay/version permutations and **PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion**. Documentation or synthetic CI PASS is not proof of shipped ASR, human speaking gains, CEFR mastery or a production defect.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and reproducible command; deterministic seed/clock; real versioned CEFR-linked oral tasks; consented and authorized acoustic evidence or source-valid recipient/adjudicator outcome; independently reviewed pronunciation/variety rubrics where relevant; channel/ASR/VAD/normalization versions; actual rendered help and transcript chronology; independent first attempts versus assisted repairs; delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen recipient/context transfer; comparable or explicitly noncomparable method/topic/channel conditions; protected skills/reviews/exit gates; follow-up denominators; and replay, version and consent-boundary permutations with assertion-level verdicts. Without trustworthy audio/adjudication, consent or independent follow-up, the corresponding claims remain UNSUPPORTED/unknown.

A stronger claim that speech-aware instruction **improves durable independent oral communication** requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator or ethically reviewed study, validated language-variety and accessibility adjudication, measurement-error and attrition analysis, subgroup-harm checks and consent/privacy safeguards. **A strong tutor distinguishes a learner's speech from what a recognizer guesses, and adapts to later independent communication rather than machine confidence.**

**Boundary:** curricular acceptance documentation only. No Pre-A1 edits, A1–C2 release, engine, Auth, cloud, infrastructure, production, Supabase, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge changes. Integration/watchdog alone aggregates owner actions.
