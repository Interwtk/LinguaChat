# F201 — Conflicting source claims, attribution, verification and independent conversational transfer

Not a new CEFR objective, truth-detection product, fact-checking service, shipped adaptive tutor or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F193 distinguishes who has access to information; F198 combines mixed communicative evidence; F199 repairs misunderstandings; F200 distinguishes conditional plans from execution. **Distinct construct:** when two sources disagree or a source is uncertain, can the learner communicate *who claims what, when and with what verification status*, without silently choosing a winner, repeating a rumor as fact or suppressing a genuine later correction? This assesses language-mediated attribution and recipient-facing clarification, not external adjudication of truth.

## Fixed objective, provenance and recipient oracles

Bind only applicable **existing versioned** CEFR can-dos for reporting, comparing accounts, asking for clarification, summarizing or mediating information. Freeze objective ID/version, prerequisites, level-appropriate language burden, target language/variety, source/oracle/rubric versions, turn ceiling where applicable, help policy, accessible route, first-unaided and delayed/unseen evidence standards, thresholds, receptive/productive balance and exit gates. At lower levels, a short “X says A; Y says B; let's check” may suffice if already warranted by the can-do. Complex evaluation of credibility is NOT_APPLICABLE when outside that can-do. No new graduation gate. Preserve the unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix disagreement; B2 stays closed.

An independently reviewed **source-claim oracle** freezes each exact utterance, original speaker, role/claimed authority, audience, channel, publication and event time, version, source access, actual delivery to the learner, quoted-versus-paraphrased wording, evidence of correction or retraction, and the specific proposition claimed. Record whether a report is direct observation, an attributed claim, an unverified forward, an official but possibly outdated notice, or a confirmed update. Authority is contextual and fallible; two claims can disagree without the evaluator deciding which is true. Silence, non-receipt, missing metadata and lack of contradiction do not constitute confirmation. A newer timestamp alone does not establish greater accuracy, and a forwarded message does not become independent corroboration.

A separate **recipient-communication oracle** freezes what the addressee needs now: the conflicting claims, source/time attribution, what remains unverified, whether an authoritative decision has actually been communicated, what action is safe to take and a targeted verification request if necessary. Accept concise natural paraphrases, source-appropriate uncertainty, and language/variety-specific reporting verbs and evidential markers; do not demand formulaic citations or penalize justified caution. Distinguish reporting “Maya says Room 5” from asserting “Room 5 is confirmed.” Source provenance is not personal identity profiling.

The **actual-delivery and first-authorship ledger** records pseudonymous learner, stable session-qualified event/replay IDs, fixed objective/source/oracle/rubric versions, exact messages/updates and source metadata actually rendered, available authority cues, first eligible unaided recipient-facing response, subsequent help/model/correction actually rendered, optional reliable latency, recurring error, instructional choice/dose/confidence, modality/accessibility route, and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed probes. Unopened hints are not exposure; rendered model summaries are answer-bearing. Failed audio is not listening evidence; an actually displayed transcript may support a separately bound reading route. Replays, copied corrections, duplicate events and calendar-boundary changes do not manufacture independent retention.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Conflicting reports do not create an invented winner.** At 09:00 Maya messages “The rehearsal is in Room 3.” At 09:05 Theo messages “I heard it's in Room 5.” Neither message is an official booking confirmation. Nora asks where to go. Accept “Maya says Room 3, but Theo heard Room 5. The room isn't verified; let's check the booking.” Reject “It's definitely Room 5 because Theo spoke later,” “Both rooms are booked,” and “Maya lied.” If an independently delivered verified booking later specifies Room 5, the recipient can report that update while retaining historical attribution.

**B — A forwarded rumor is not a second independent source.** Leo forwards a screenshot saying the workshop is cancelled. Priya forwards the same screenshot; both derive from one unattributed post. The organizer has not issued a notice. Accept “Two people forwarded the same unverified post; we should ask the organizer before telling everyone it's cancelled.” Reject “Two independent sources confirmed cancellation.” A later official notice may establish a new status, but forwarding count alone never does.

**C — An official but outdated notice can be superseded without erasing its history.** An official Monday notice assigns Room 2. A Tuesday official correction, actually delivered to the learner, says Room 4 replaces Room 2. A recipient still holds Monday's screenshot. Accept “The Monday notice said Room 2, but Tuesday's correction changed it to Room 4; please use the updated notice.” Reject “Room 2 was never announced” and “Both rooms are currently confirmed.” If Tuesday's correction was *not* delivered before the first response, do not score the learner as though it was known.

**D — Reported observation, inference and retraction remain separate.** A student says “I saw staff carrying chairs outside,” then speculates “Maybe the talk is outdoors.” Later they clarify “I don't know the venue.” Accept “They saw chairs outside, but the talk location is still unconfirmed.” Reject “The talk is outdoors” and “The student confirmed the talk was cancelled.” If the original source retracts an incorrect observation, update the attribution and do not preserve it as current evidence.

**E — Assistance, modality failure and replay cannot manufacture mastery.** At 10:01 the learner reads A and independently asserts “Theo confirmed Room 5.” Preserve FIRST_UNAIDED_ATTRIBUTION with an unsupported-certainty error. At 10:02 a complete who-said-what table is rendered; at 10:03 the learner copies a correct cautious summary. Record ANSWER_EXPOSURE and ASSISTED_REPAIR, not independent mastery. At 10:04 replay of the same source is deduplicated. In a listening variant the audio fails but a transcript appears: no listening credit; a separately bound reading response may count. An unopened hint does not count as help exposure. Later valid transfer needs genuine delay and a different source/conflict structure, not renamed rooms.

**F — Reversible teaching choice, contrary evidence and soft interests.** Learner A repeatedly confuses later reports with confirmed facts. Across comparable completed sessions WHO_SAID_WHAT_RECAP, SOURCE_TIME_TIMELINE and BALANCED_DEFAULT are actually delivered and alternated. Tentatively favor a method only when later first-unaided unseen recipient updates improve under matched source complexity, authority, language burden, channel, help, topic and delay. Learner B performs well immediately after a complete table but later treats forwarded rumors as corroboration; decrease confidence, restore exploration and consider ORIGINAL_VS_FORWARD_COMPARISON. An easier official notice, more hints, familiar gaming example or shorter delay is a confound, **not** causal evidence. Missing follow-up stays unknown. Gaming may softly weight an example, but adjacent sports, neutral libraries and unfamiliar community notices remain reachable; no required objective or modality is skipped.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Shared objective only.** A–F bind applicable existing versioned can-dos and independently reviewed target-language/variety source and recipient oracles, preserving prerequisites, turn limits, burden, thresholds, modality coverage and exit gates; otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE/UNSUPPORTED.
2. **Attribution without fabricated certainty.** A reports who said which room and preserves unresolved conflict; a newer message is not automatically authoritative or true.
3. **Corroboration requires independence.** B recognizes shared origin of forwards and does not treat repetition as separate confirmation.
4. **Version and delivery chronology.** C differentiates original notice and superseding correction, and never scores a correction not actually delivered to the learner.
5. **Observation, inference and retraction.** D reports direct observation without converting speculation into a verified venue; genuine retraction updates current claims.
6. **First authorship and access route.** E preserves initial error, rendered answer-bearing help, assisted copying, unopened hints, failed audio, possible reading route and replay deduplication; no phantom listening or independent credit.
7. **Matched reversible adaptation.** F compares delivered methods against later independent source-aware communication; complexity, authority, help, channel, topic and delay confounds lower confidence and prohibit causal-winner claims.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer and nonexclusive interests.** Require distinct genuine sessions, changed source structures and recipient purposes, first-unaided output, explicit missing follow-up, no script leakage, balanced receptive/productive coverage and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar rotation; never skip can-dos for interests.
9. **Honest evidence and protected scope.** Synthetic histories and generic CI are not executable source-attribution QA, a shipped tutor or human efficacy proof. Require exact-head executable delivery/provenance/response tests, observed reversible instructional decisions and ethically reviewed delayed independent outcomes against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge, Pre-A1 changes, A1–C2 enablement or weakened B2 boundaries.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic design histories, not observed learner behavior or executable tests. They specify how to report contradictory claims and verification status to a recipient without turning a rumor, forward or outdated notice into an unsupported fact. Remain UNSUPPORTED until independently implemented and verified.
