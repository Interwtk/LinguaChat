**Status:** design-only synthetic longitudinal curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable exact-head assertions bind independently reviewed discourse/referent and recipient-action oracles, target-language/variety-specific **actually delivered** stimuli, first-authorship/help/accessibility chronology and later first-unaided unseen recipient-facing transfer to existing versioned CEFR can-dos. This is not a universal coreference solver, new CEFR gate, shipped adaptive tutor or evidence of human learning gains. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F177 covers physically/temporally anchored deixis (this, here, tomorrow); F175 covers speaker attribution in overlapping turns; F178 covers reported-source certainty; F180 covers quantifier scope. **Distinct construct:** preserving discourse-internal reference and omitted material across connected utterances: which earlier entity or event a pronoun, `one`, `the other`, `did too`, `so` or omitted phrase can legitimately recover, and when the referent remains unresolved. Fluent grammar is not proof that a report identifies the correct person, item, action or degree of certainty.

## Fixed objectives and independently reviewed discourse oracles

Bind each eligible task to an **existing versioned CEFR reading, listening, spoken interaction, mediation or written-production can-do** whose authentic demand includes tracking reference across sentences or turns, relaying an action, or requesting clarification. Preserve the can-do/prerequisites, target language and independently reviewed variety, unchanged rubric/threshold, accessible route, first-independent evidence, due/delayed/unseen probes, receptive/productive balance and mastery/exit gates. If the construct is absent from a can-do, mark NOT_APPLICABLE rather than inventing a gate. Use only fictional library, club, rehearsal and community-workshop scenarios; no real medical, legal, financial, emergency or safety-critical decisions.

An independently reviewed **discourse-state oracle**, fixed before delivery, records the ordered utterances, speakers, entity/event mentions, attributes, syntactic roles, antecedent candidates, agreement and language-specific constraints, discourse salience, elided predicate and polarity, time/reference context, and candidate readings supported, contradicted or genuinely unresolved by the **delivered** context. It must distinguish grammatical compatibility from proven identity: pronoun gender or proximity alone cannot force a unique antecedent when multiple candidates remain. A separate **recipient-action oracle** specifies which identity, object, event or action may responsibly be relayed to a new recipient, and the smallest targeted clarification when evidence is insufficient. Independently review alternate readings; do not make one interpretation mandatory merely because it sounds more natural to an annotator.

An **actual-delivery oracle** records reviewed target-language/variety text/audio, real rendered hashes, speaker turns, prosody, captions, punctuation, playback success, images, referent highlights, translations, models and answer-bearing hints **actually exposed**. An answer-bearing color highlight or diagram shown before response is assistance; an unopened optional model is not exposure. Accessible reading may validly assess reading/mediation but must not be relabeled unaided listening after audio failure. Review pronoun/ellipsis differences across languages and varieties; English-specific `do too` and `one` examples must not be copied verbatim into other languages without independent linguistic review.

The **first-authorship/provenance ledger** retains pseudonymous learner, objective/rubric/oracle versions, stable session-qualified event IDs and replay deduplication; earliest eligible unaided recipient-facing report, repair or targeted clarification; delivered stimulus and alternative readings; ordered hints/models/replays/transcripts/answer exposure; accessibility route; assigned/offered/**actually delivered** method; and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed/unseen probes with real elapsed time and distinct-session provenance. Optional hesitation/latency is weak and never fabricated or a mastery gate. Assisted repair cannot overwrite the first unaided response.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Ambiguous `it` cannot be assigned by guess.** A fictional organizer says, “Maya put the poster beside the folder. It was blue.” Both the poster and folder are plausible blue objects; no gesture, prior description or other disambiguating evidence was delivered. The learner tells a teammate, “The poster was blue.” Mark AMBIGUOUS_ANTECEDENT and UNJUSTIFIED_REFERENT_ASSIGNMENT, not a categorical wrong-poster claim: the color is known, the referent is not. Accept “One of them was blue; I need to check which” or “Was the poster or the folder blue?” If a later source identifies the folder, that supports a **new** report and never retroactively validates the first guess. Do not use a hidden author intention as the oracle.

**B — `One` and `the other` preserve the set.** A fictional librarian says, “We borrowed two microphones. One stopped working; the other still works.” The learner reports “Both microphones stopped working.” Reject: exactly one of the stated pair is reported broken and the other working, but **which physical microphone** failed remains unknown. “One of the two is broken; the other works. We need to identify the broken one” is supported. In a separate message with three microphones, `the other` may need additional context: do not mechanically apply the two-item reading. The number and identity of candidates are reviewed before delivery, not inferred after the answer.

**C — Ellipsis restores a predicate, not a new action.** A fictional club message says, “Mina checked the guest list, and Theo did too.” The learner says “Theo approved the guest list.” Reject UNJUSTIFIED_ACTION_ATTRIBUTION: `did too` recovers **checked**, not approved. In a separately reviewed contrast, “Mina didn't approve the poster, but Theo did” means Theo approved it while Mina did not; a learner who reports both approved has lost polarity. A bare “Theo did” without an available prior predicate is underdetermined and merits clarification, not invented approval.

**D — Continuity across turns without fabricated referents.** A fictional planning exchange says, “Lina left the draft with Omar. He passed it to Nia. She filed it.” The reviewed version confirms Omar and Nia as the only referents for `he` and `she`, and the draft as the sole transferable document. The learner reports “Lina filed the draft.” Reject the actor attribution; Nia filed it. A contrasting delivered version introduces a second document and another compatible person before “She filed it”: if multiple plausible antecedents remain, accept a targeted “Who filed which document?” instead of imposing the first version's resolution. Review language-specific agreement and discourse context; do not assume binary gender or pronoun morphology universally resolves identity.

**E — Delivery, accessibility and first authorship.** At 09:02 an audio-only message says, “One microphone failed; the other works,” but playback fails. The learner asks for text. Do **not** grade an unaided listening failure or fabricate hearing. At 09:03 a transcript and an answer-bearing two-object diagram actually render; at 09:04 the learner correctly reports the contrast. Record ASSISTED and ANSWER_EXPOSURE for the observed reading/mediation construct, not unaided listening. An optional but unopened diagram does not count. At 10:15 a newly authored, successfully delivered unassisted text or audio task can create a **new** first attempt in its true modality. Deduplicate replayed event IDs and preserve exposure order and elapsed time.

**F — Matched longitudinal adaptation, reversal and unseen transfer.** Learner A repeatedly chooses a nearest-noun antecedent when two candidates are equally plausible, then overstates identities to new recipients. Across matched sessions, actually delivered CANDIDATE_ANTECEDENT_CONTRAST and ASK_WHEN_REFERENT_UNRESOLVED alternate with BALANCED_DEFAULT. Only repeated, meaningfully delayed, first-unaided reports on unseen library, neutral club and unfamiliar workshop dialogues may cautiously increase the probability of an effective option while still sampling alternatives. Learner B initially improves after REFERENT_CHAIN_TRACKING_WITH_FADE but later independently confuses `did too` with approval in an unseen context; decrease confidence and explore ELLIPSIS_PREDICATE_RECONSTRUCTION. If one option used fewer referents, no ambiguity, highlighted arrows, familiar interests, more hints, shorter delay, or more follow-up, record **no causal winner**. Assisted repeats, same-dialogue paraphrases, calendar-midnight-only gaps and missing follow-ups do not establish durable transfer. Keep listening/reading and productive/interactive coverage balanced.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed CEFR and reviewed oracles.** A–F bind existing versioned relevant can-dos, independent discourse-state and recipient-action review, actual delivery and unchanged mastery/evidence requirements. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED; no invented level gate.
2. **Ambiguous antecedent remains unresolved.** A rejects a confident poster-color attribution when `it` has multiple supported antecedents, accepts uncertainty/targeted clarification and forbids retroactive first-attempt credit.
3. **Partitives and set size.** B distinguishes `one` and `the other` for a reviewed two-item set, rejects both-broken, preserves unknown physical identity and refuses to force the same resolution for a three-item context.
4. **Ellipsis and polarity.** C recovers `checked` from `did too`, not `approved`, and preserves contrasting approval polarity; no predicate is hallucinated when prior discourse is unavailable.
5. **Referent continuity and legitimate ambiguity.** D preserves actor/object chains when disambiguated and accepts clarification rather than invented identity when additional compatible candidates are introduced.
6. **First authorship, actual help and accessibility.** E records the earliest eligible unaided report, only actually rendered exposure, playback failure as unavailable evidence, accurate measured construct, ordered events and replay deduplication; assisted repair is not independent mastery.
7. **Matched uncertain reversible adaptation.** F may alter method probabilities only after comparable actually delivered choices and later independent outcomes. Sparse/confounded/contradictory data prevent causal-winner claims and retain exploration.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer and soft interest rotation.** Require genuinely later distinct sessions, new independently reviewed discourse chains/recipients, first-unaided reporting or targeted clarification and explicit missing follow-up. Rotate adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts while preserving fixed can-dos and balanced modalities.
9. **Evidence and protected scope.** Synthetic prose or generic CI cannot establish executable discourse-oracle QA, shipped outcome-driven adaptation or human retention gains. Require exact-head executable stimulus/discourse/first-authorship assertions, observed policy decision changes and independently reviewed delayed unaided transfer against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, Pre-A1 changes, A1–C2 enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic acceptance histories, not observed learners or executable QA. Preserve referent identity, predicate recovery, ambiguity, justified clarification and reversible instruction. Remain UNSUPPORTED until implementation and independently verified learning evidence exist.
