# F115 — Response-latency provenance, false hesitation signals and independent transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD, real versioned CEFR-linked tasks, instrumented and consent-appropriate event chronology, independently validated latency attribution, and longitudinal first-unaided outcomes. This is **not** shipped timing-based learner diagnosis, an attention or disability classifier, CEFR mastery, a reproduced production defect, or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 protects genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish offered/assigned from actually rendered assistance and tutor-origin faults; F49 protects accessible construct fidelity; F50 protects soft interest breadth; F77 concerns conversational floor management; F101 concerns interruption/resumption; F104 requires confound-aware instructional selection; F108 concerns linguistic disfluency and self-repair; F111 concerns ASR input corruption; F112 concerns TTS output corruption. **Distinct construct:** when response time or hesitation is available as *optional* evidence, can the tutor distinguish **learner-controlled deliberation after a task is actually perceivable** from server, transport, audio, device, accessibility, interruption and recording delays—without treating slow responses as low proficiency or fast guesses as mastery? This fixture tests the provenance and admissibility of latency as one uncertain longitudinal signal, **not** a universal speed requirement or a new CEFR timing gate.

## Source, measurement and evidence contract

Resolve a **real versioned** CEFR descriptor/can-do, blueprint/prerequisites, target language and validated variety/register, source-authored communicative goal, task/recipient action, rubric/evaluator, protected listening/reading/writing/speaking/free-production evidence, independent threshold, permissible timing constraints **only if the source explicitly specifies them**, accessibility route, assistance policy, due reviews, delayed retrieval, unseen transfer and exit gate. Timing is optional auxiliary evidence: no device clock, speech pause or typing interval alone establishes confusion, comprehension, confidence, attention, impairment, dishonesty or mastery. If a task is untimed, no invented speed cutoff may fail an otherwise valid first-unaided answer.

Use a privacy-minimized, session-qualified **opportunity-to-respond timeline** with monotonic timestamps where available: task version and source-effective time; prompt server creation, network delivery, client receipt, complete render, actual visibility/audibility and audio-playback completion; foreground/background/focus changes; learner's explicitly available start or first interaction; speech capture start/stop, endpointing/VAD and ASR completion; text composition and submission *without logging raw keystrokes*; tutor hints/model/feedback actually rendered; offline queues and retry receipts; task response and recipient-facing action. Separate trusted observed intervals from inferred ones, track clock domains and uncertainty bounds, and never subtract unrelated wall-clock timestamps as if synchronized. Minimize telemetry, obtain appropriate consent, support opt-out, retention limits and accessibility accommodations; do not collect biometrics or infer sensitive traits.

Task-local labels: SOURCE_VALID_TASK, SOURCE_TIMED_CONSTRAINT, UNTIMED_TASK, OPPORTUNITY_OBSERVED, OPPORTUNITY_UNKNOWN, LEARNER_CONTROLLED_INTERVAL, SERVER_DELAY, NETWORK_DELAY, CLIENT_RENDER_DELAY, AUDIO_NOT_READY, CAPTURE_ENDPOINT_DELAY, ASR_DELAY, BACKGROUND_OR_INTERRUPTED, ACCESS_ACCOMMODATION, CLOCK_UNTRUSTED, FIRST_UNAIDED, ACTUALLY_RENDERED_HELP, ASSISTED, FIRST_ATTEMPT_VALID, FAST_GUESS_WRONG, SLOW_INDEPENDENT_VALID, LATENCY_ADMISSIBLE_AUXILIARY, LATENCY_INADMISSIBLE, RECIPIENT_ACTION_VALID, DELAYED_UNAIDED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_UNAIDED_TRANSFER, DEVICE_METHOD_CONFOUNDED, TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, REPLAY_DEDUPED, VERSION_CONFLICT_QUARANTINED, NOT_APPLICABLE and UNSUPPORTED. Labels are bounded task observations, never permanent learner types.

Feasible reversible candidates: BALANCED_DEFAULT, SOURCE_VALID_WAIT_FOR_FULL_PROMPT, THINK_THEN_PRODUCE, BRIEF_EXPLANATION_THEN_UNAIDED_PROBE, TIMED_ONLY_WHEN_SOURCE_VALID, ASYNCHRONOUS_REFLECTION, LOW_LATENCY_TURN_EXCHANGE, UNSEEN_INDEPENDENT_ROLEPLAY and SOURCE_VALID_OTHER. Change only implemented, accessible, source-validated explanation, scaffolding, pacing, modality mix, activity order, spacing and examples. Preserve shared CEFR objectives, independent evidence standards, skill balance, due reviews and exit gates. Adapt on uncertain longitudinal **later independent retention/transfer** and keep exploring alternatives; never optimize response speed or immediate coached correctness as the goal. Soft interests increase topic probability while retaining preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Slow correct deliberation versus fast unsupported guess.** Two learners independently complete an untimed source-authored clarification task after the complete prompt is rendered. Learner A waits, considers the recipient's constraints and gives a correct clarification with valid follow-through; learner B immediately guesses a fluent but incorrect commitment. The scoring must privilege source-valid independent meaning and recipient action, not penalize A's longer interval or reward B's speed. A matched source-authored time-critical simulation may assess a separately validated deadline, but must not silently impose it on untimed CEFR objectives. If first-perceivable time is unknown, latency is inadmissible.

**B — Network and media delay masquerading as hesitation.** In a listening task, the prompt is created at t=0, the page skeleton appears at t=1, audio buffers until t=12 and finishes at t=21; the learner responds independently at t=24. Another learner receives the same valid audio immediately and responds at t=14. A naive server-submit minus server-create interval makes the first learner look slower; the admissible opportunity begins no earlier than the source-valid perceivable stimulus boundary. A counterfactual swaps network speed while holding learner answers fixed: mastery and method choice must remain invariant. If complete audio delivery cannot be established, do not score a required listening construct as failed.

**C — ASR/VAD endpointing and self-repair.** A speaker independently says, “Tuesday—sorry, Thursday at two,” finishing at t=7. Voice activity detection waits five extra seconds before closing capture and ASR returns at t=16. The language evidence must evaluate the corrected intended date against source truth; the endpoint and ASR lag are not learner hesitation. Another learner uses a legitimate natural pause within an utterance; the system cannot treat it as a failure or force an early hint. A tutor phrase actually spoken before the learner's correction changes the assistance label only when exposure is established; a queued but unplayed hint does not.

**D — Accessibility, interruption and opt-out.** One learner uses an authorized screen reader and waits for a long source-valid prompt to finish; another receives an approved simplified display that preserves a separately validated reading construct; a third backgrounds the app to answer a phone call; a fourth opts out of timing telemetry. The first learner's access time and the third learner's interruption cannot be interpreted as lack of proficiency; the fourth still receives the same CEFR opportunity and evaluation. An accessibility alternative may count toward a source-required skill only if equivalence is explicitly validated. If opportunity chronology cannot be known, retain correctness evidence and mark latency unknown, never impute a punitive value.

**E — Reversible pacing based on delayed independent transfer.** Across matched fictional sessions, a fast-paced method produces high immediate assisted completion and short answer times but poor first-unaided delayed retention; a slower THINK_THEN_PRODUCE method yields better later recipient-valid independent transfer for one learner. Another learner shows the reverse after equal source-valid exposure. Compare or disclose baseline competence, prompt length, difficulty, modality, audio/render/network/device conditions, access route, help exposure, topic familiarity, attrition and task-specific time demands. The tutor records a reversible uncertain hypothesis and a changed **actually delivered** next pacing choice, samples alternatives, and protects all required skills and due reviews. If methods were delivered only on different devices or topics, report confounding rather than a causal winner.

**F — Offline clocks, duplicate events and out-of-order help.** An offline learner sees a prompt, answers independently and later receives a hint; network replay uploads the hint receipt first, then the answer twice. A second session reuses a local event ID; one device clock jumps backward and another has a timezone change; a cached audio artifact is replaced by a different duration at the same URL. Preserve immutable session-qualified event identities, trustworthy monotonic local order and actual render/exposure times with uncertainty. Deduplicate only true retries; do not label the earlier answer assisted because its packet arrived later, combine distinct sessions, calculate negative hesitation from unsynchronized clocks or reconstruct old audio from a mutable URL. Quarantine irreconcilable stimulus/version chronology; missing follow-up is missing, not failure.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Applicability and source validity.** A–F resolve real versioned CEFR descriptor/can-do, blueprint, source-authored communicative task and timing policy, validated variety, rubric/evaluator, accessibility, independent threshold, protected skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Untimed objectives cannot acquire a fabricated speed threshold; absent timing observability means LATENCY_INADMISSIBLE, not failed mastery.
2. **Opportunity-to-respond provenance.** A/B/D/F distinguish server creation, complete learner-perceivable stimulus, audio completion, foreground state, capture, response and receipt. Use compatible monotonic clocks and explicit uncertainty; incomplete prompt/audio or unsynchronized clocks prohibit precise learner-controlled latency claims.
3. **Channel, capture and interruption attribution.** B/C/D separate buffering, render, ASR, VAD endpoint, network queue, device backgrounding, assistive-technology playback and actual learner-controlled intervals. No technology-origin delay may be scored as a learner-origin linguistic error or stable weakness.
4. **Language meaning and recipient consequence.** A/C assess source-valid first-unaided meaning, self-repair, clarification and recipient-facing action independently of raw speed. Fast wrong guesses cannot outrank slower correct independent transfer; source-authorized time-critical constraints apply only to their validated task.
5. **Actual assistance chronology.** C/E/F distinguish offered, queued, assigned and **actually rendered/heard** hints, examples and feedback. Assistance before the relevant answer can invalidate independence; a later hint cannot retroactively do so, regardless of server arrival order.
6. **Accessibility, privacy and opt-out parity.** D/F enforce consent, minimization, no raw keystroke or biometric storage, accommodation and construct-valid equivalents, and opt-out without CEFR penalty. No timing-derived health, personality, attention or disability labels.
7. **Delayed independent retrieval and unseen transfer.** A–F separate first-unaided outcomes from assisted practice; require meaningfully separated sessions and genuinely unseen source-authored prompts/recipients with valid first-unaided decisions. Emit eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators and preserve protected skill/review obligations.
8. **Reversible adaptation and confound control.** B/E/F record feasible, assigned and **actually delivered** pacing/methods, timing uncertainty, baseline, source difficulty, device/channel/access, topic, exposure, missingness and alternative sampling. Counterfactual channel swaps, deduplicated retries and clock/version permutations must not change mastery or produce spurious method preferences. Interests remain a soft, nonexclusive context layer.
9. **Assertion-level reproducibility and bounded claims.** A–F emit deterministic seed/clock, exact engine HEAD, CEFR/task/source/rubric/evaluator hashes, versioned prompt/audio artifacts, opportunity/exposure event ledger, first/assisted answers, recipient decisions, delayed/unseen denominators, confound and replay permutations, and **PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion**. A documentation-only CI pass is not an executable learner-model validation or proof of real learning improvement.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and reproducible command; deterministic seed/clock; real versioned CEFR-linked source tasks; approved and observable stimulus delivery or validated accessible equivalents; monotonic opportunity and assistance chronology with clock uncertainty; independent language/recipient-action adjudication; meaningful delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen transfer; comparable or explicitly noncomparable methods, devices, channels and topics; protected skill/review/exit requirements; eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators; and event-replay/clock/version permutations with per-assertion verdicts. If timing, source truth, consent, access or independent follow-up is absent, affected latency/adaptation claims remain UNSUPPORTED/unknown, while otherwise valid language evidence is retained.

A stronger claim that latency-aware teaching **improves durable independent communication** requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal outcomes with a credible balanced comparator or ethically reviewed study, multilingual and accessibility validation, device/channel confound analysis, attrition and subgroup-harm checks, and privacy safeguards. **A strong tutor notices when a learner needs time, but never mistakes a loading spinner, screen reader, natural pause or careful thought for lack of knowledge.**

**Boundary:** curricular acceptance documentation only. No Pre-A1 edits, A1–C2 release, engine, Auth, cloud, infrastructure, production, Supabase, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge changes. Integration/watchdog alone aggregates owner actions.
