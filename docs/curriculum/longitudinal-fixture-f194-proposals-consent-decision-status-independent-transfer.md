# F194 — Proposals, agreement scope, decision status and independent communicative transfer

**Status:** design-only synthetic longitudinal curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable exact-head tests bind independently reviewed, target-language/variety-specific proposal/decision oracles, actual source delivery, first-authorship provenance, and genuinely delayed first-unaided unseen transfer to existing versioned CEFR can-dos. Not a shipped consensus detector, voting engine, legal consent model, universal meeting rule, new CEFR gate or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F79 concerns multiparty information alignment; F80 concerns asynchronous update propagation; F83/F179 concern conditional plans; F85 concerns source-authored permissions and authority; F97 concerns respectful refusal; F169/F182 concern offers and indirect requests; F189 concerns attribution; F191 concerns partial answers; F193 concerns who received information. **Distinct construct:** when an existing source-linked communicative task requires communicating a group plan, distinguish **a suggestion, personal preference, willingness, scoped agreement, conditional acceptance, objection, authorized decision, and completed action**, including whose assent applies to which version. Shared awareness of a proposal is not agreement with it; agreement by some is not a group decision without a source-established decision rule. English examples are illustrative, not universal speech-act templates.

## Fixed CEFR objective and independently reviewed decision oracles

Bind only **existing versioned** CEFR interaction, mediation, listening, reading, writing, speaking or free-production can-dos whose actual source task requires proposing, negotiating, confirming, summarizing or revising a shared decision. Freeze can-do ID, prerequisite graph, level and turn ceiling, target language/variety, source/task and rubric/evaluator versions, accessibility route, assistance policy, first-independent and delayed/unseen evidence, thresholds, required receptive/productive coverage and exit gates. For lower levels, use only simple invitation/response/confirmation moves genuinely required by the bound task; do not add committee governance, legal consent or advanced pragmatics. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE. Preserve the unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix disagreement, its 20-meaningful-turn ceiling and closed gate. Use fictional low-stakes club meetings, library displays and workshop plans; exclude medical, legal, financial, emergency, safety-critical and intimate-consent decisions.

A frozen, independently reviewed **speech-act and scope oracle** records each exact utterance, speaker, addressee, message channel, source version, referenced proposal and its dimensions (date, time, place, role, activity), explicit qualifications, response target, language/variety and surrounding turns actually available. Separately classify PROPOSED, PREFERENCE_EXPRESSED, WILLINGNESS_OFFERED, CONDITIONAL_AGREEMENT, SCOPED_AGREEMENT, OBJECTION, CLARIFICATION_REQUESTED, WITHDRAWN, REVISED, AUTHORIZED_DECISION, ACTION_COMPLETED and UNKNOWN. A bare “Thursday works for me” establishes that speaker's availability, not universal approval; “I like the poster” may endorse the design without approving the print order. “Sounds good” can be ambiguous if several options were discussed: accept a scoped recap or targeted clarification. Do not infer private intentions, honesty, personal identity or an unspoken agreement.

A separate frozen **decision-procedure oracle** specifies only source-established roles, quorum/approval requirements if any, who is entitled to decide, which participants must be consulted, the precise proposal version under consideration, whether an explicit final announcement exists, and what action (if any) is documented as complete. Where the source does **not** establish a decision procedure, label DECISION_RULE_UNKNOWN and do not invent majority, unanimity, chair authority, veto power or cultural norms. A proposal can be viable without being selected; a selected plan can remain unexecuted. A correction or withdrawn proposal does not retroactively erase earlier messages, and prior agreement does not automatically carry over to changed time/place/obligations. Distinguish source-verified authorization from an ordinary participant's claim of authorization; preserve conflicting versions as unresolved until the source resolves them.

A **recipient-action oracle** freezes the actual communicative goal: faithfully summarize the current state, seek only necessary approval or clarification, communicate the chosen version to intended recipients, or report that the action remains pending. Accept natural variety-appropriate alternatives, concise formulations and legitimate uncertainty. Avoid demanding redundant reconfirmation after an explicit source-valid final decision. This fixture tests language-mediated decision status, **not** whether the learner's preferred plan is best.

An **actual-delivery and first-authorship ledger** records pseudonymous learner, fixed can-do/task/oracle/rubric versions, immutable session-qualified event IDs with replay deduplication, exact source utterances and attachments actually rendered, audio/captions/transcripts and accessibility route actually available, tutor models/hints/feedback actually displayed and chronology, first eligible unaided recipient-facing message, later assisted revisions, error type, optional reliable latency, topic familiarity, instructional option/dose, confidence, and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed probes. A selected but unopened hint is not exposure; a displayed decision-state table or finished message is answer-bearing help. Failed audio cannot earn independent listening; readable fallback may support an actual reading/mediation route only. Preserve genuinely distinct sessions and meaningful elapsed time; replay, renaming people or crossing midnight cannot create delayed transfer.

Task-local labels: CEFR_OBJECTIVE_BOUND, SPEECH_ACT_ORACLE_REVIEWED, DECISION_PROCEDURE_REVIEWED, PROPOSAL_NOT_DECISION, PERSONAL_AVAILABILITY_ONLY, SCOPED_AGREEMENT_PRESERVED, CONDITIONAL_ACCEPTANCE_PRESERVED, RESPONSE_TARGET_AMBIGUOUS, OBJECTION_PRESERVED, DECISION_RULE_UNKNOWN, REQUIRED_APPROVAL_MISSING, AUTHORIZED_DECISION_CONFIRMED, ACTION_NOT_COMPLETED, VERSION_REVISED, OLD_APPROVAL_NOT_PORTABLE, WITHDRAWAL_PRESERVED, UNNECESSARY_CONFIRMATION, JUSTIFIED_CONFIRMATION, FIRST_UNAIDED_RESPONSE, ACTUAL_SOURCE_RENDERED, ACTUALLY_RENDERED_HELP, ANSWER_EXPOSURE, ASSISTED, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, DELAYED_UNAIDED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_UNAIDED_TRANSFER, SAME_SCRIPT_LEAKAGE, METHOD_SCOPE_CONFOUNDED, METHOD_TOPIC_CONFOUNDED, METHOD_HELP_CONFOUNDED, METHOD_ROLE_CONFOUNDED, METHOD_CHANNEL_CONFOUNDED, METHOD_DELAY_CONFOUNDED, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, NOT_APPLICABLE, UNSUPPORTED. ## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — A suggestion and personal availability are not a confirmed meeting.** Maya writes in a fictional club chat, “Could we meet on Thursday at four?” Theo replies, “Thursday works for me.” No one else has replied and no final booking is recorded. The learner must update Nora. Accept “Maya suggested Thursday at four; Theo can make it, but the meeting hasn't been confirmed” or a proportionate question to the person who can confirm it. Reject “Everyone agreed to meet Thursday” and “The room is booked.” A later source-valid organizer announcement confirming the time changes the status; do not continue calling the plan unconfirmed after that announcement is actually delivered.

**B — Agreement has a target and may be conditional.** Maya proposes “Use the blue poster and print 30 copies.” Nora replies, “The blue design looks good, but let's check the cost before printing.” Accept that Nora likes the design but has **not** unconditionally approved printing 30 copies. Reject “Nora approved the whole order.” In a second source-valid continuation, Nora says “Yes, print 30 if the coordinator confirms the budget.” Preserve the condition and do not report a completed print order. If the reply “Sounds good” appears after multiple proposals with no identifiable target, accept a targeted clarification or qualified report rather than inventing the agreed version.

**C — Decision rules must come from the task, not the tutor's assumptions.** A fictional workshop brief explicitly says its three named coordinators must approve a date before it can be announced. Maya and Theo approve Friday; Nora has not responded. The learner must report that approval is incomplete and request the missing source-required response. In a different task with **no** decision rule, two of three participants prefer Friday; the learner must not invent “majority wins” or “everyone must agree.” In a third source-authored task, the designated coordinator has explicitly approved Friday and sent the final announcement; accept a concise confirmation without needless requests for another vote. Never turn these examples into universal organizational, legal or cultural rules.

**D — Revision, objection and withdrawal do not silently inherit approval.** The group previously approved a Saturday 10:00 workshop in Room 3. Maya proposes changing it to Sunday 11:00 in Room 5; Theo says “I can do Sunday,” but Nora objects because she cannot attend. No authorized revised decision is recorded. Accept “Saturday at 10 in Room 3 was the agreed plan; Sunday in Room 5 is a proposed change with an objection.” Reject “The group approved Sunday” and “The Saturday agreement never existed.” Later Maya explicitly withdraws the Sunday proposal; a faithful update states that the proposed change was withdrawn, without assuming that the earlier booking remains valid if its reservation status is not independently established. Old approval is not automatically portable to new conditions.

**E — Rendered answer, missing audio and replay cannot earn independence.** At 09:01 the learner reads the actual discussion but no solution. At 09:02 the tutor renders a completed chart: “Theo agrees to Thursday; Nora has not approved; meeting pending.” At 09:03 the learner copies that recap. Mark ANSWER_EXPOSURE and ASSISTED; preserve any earlier eligible unaided message and do not award independent mastery. Replaying the same event at 09:04 cannot create another first attempt. In a separate listening variant, playback fails and captions alone render: award no independent listening, and score the actual reading/mediation route only when its bound rubric permits. An unopened hint does not count as exposed. Delayed independent transfer requires a genuinely new proposal/response/decision-procedure combination in a later distinct session without answer-bearing help.

**F — Longitudinal method selection, counterevidence and nonexclusive interests.** Learner A repeatedly converts a participant's preference into a final group decision. Across matched real sessions, PROPOSAL_VS_DECISION_CONTRAST, WHO_AGREED_TO_WHAT_LEDGER and BALANCED_DEFAULT are **actually delivered** and alternated. A method may be cautiously favored only if later unseen first-unaided recipient messages better preserve proposal status, agreement scope and final authorization with matched roles, procedure, language burden, topic, help, channel and delay. Learner B initially improves after ledger practice but later overcorrects by calling even an explicitly authorized final announcement “only a proposal”; new independent errors lower confidence and restore balanced sampling or decision-status contrast. If one method used a simpler group, clearer authority, favorite gaming topics, visible answers or shorter follow-up delays, mark confounding and **no causal winner**. Missing follow-up is unknown, not failed retention. Gaming interests may softly weight examples, but neutral libraries, adjacent clubs, unfamiliar workshops and all required CEFR objectives/modalities remain available.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed source-defined CEFR boundaries.** A–F bind only applicable existing versioned can-dos, independently reviewed speech-act/decision/recipient oracles and actual source delivery. Preserve prerequisites, language/variety, level-specific turn ceilings, rubrics, thresholds, required skills and exit gates; otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE/UNSUPPORTED.
2. **Proposal versus decision.** A separates a suggestion, one participant's availability, final announcement and completed booking; neither false group agreement nor invented execution earns credit.
3. **Scope and conditions of assent.** B preserves exactly which proposal dimensions were endorsed, the outstanding cost/budget condition and unresolved reply target; no unconditional approval is inferred from partial praise or ambiguous “Sounds good.”
4. **No invented decision procedure.** C follows only explicitly source-established approval roles/rules, recognizes missing required approvals, and avoids imposing majority/unanimity or redundant confirmation when the authorized final decision is already established.
5. **Revisions and objections.** D retains the original agreed version, proposed revision, objection and withdrawal chronology; earlier approval is not silently transferred to changed date/place/obligations or converted into a completed booking.
6. **Actual delivery and first authorship.** E separates rendered source, displayed answer-bearing help, unopened hints, failed audio, captions, first unaided output, assisted copying and event replay; no phantom listening or assisted independent mastery.
7. **Matched, reversible instructional adaptation.** F changes method hypotheses only after comparable **actually delivered** alternatives and later independent recipient-facing transfer. Sparse, contradictory or confounded evidence lowers confidence; reasonable alternatives remain sampled.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer and soft interests.** Require genuinely distinct sessions, independently reviewed new proposals/roles/procedures, unaided messages, explicit missing follow-up, balanced receptive/productive coverage and nonexclusive preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation. Never skip CEFR objectives due to interests.
9. **Evidence and protected scope.** Synthetic prose and generic CI are not executable negotiation QA, shipped consensus tracking or proven human-learning gains. Require exact-head executable source/decision/delivery/provenance checks, observed reversible instructional choices and ethically reviewed delayed independent transfer versus a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge, Pre-A1 changes, A1–C2 enablement or weakened B2 boundaries.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic acceptance histories, not observed learners or executable tests. A strong tutor should help the learner distinguish what was proposed, what each person actually agreed to, what the source-authorized group decided and what remains undone, then independently communicate that distinction in unfamiliar situations. Remain UNSUPPORTED until implementation and independent verification.
