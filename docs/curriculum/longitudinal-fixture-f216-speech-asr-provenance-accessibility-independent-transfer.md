# F216 — Speech/ASR evidence provenance, accessibility, accent fairness and independent transfer

**Status:** design-only synthetic *cross-cutting* longitudinal curriculum acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until an independently reviewed executable exact-head harness exercises the specified events and decisions. This is **not** a shipped speech evaluator, microphone pipeline, accent classifier, accessibility certification, new CEFR can-do, change to mastery, causal method comparison or proof of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F208 distinguishes a transcript from actual listening; F210 separates hints from unaided performance; F212 rejects invalid generated tasks and broken channels; F215 treats cross-language explanations as revisable teaching aids. **Distinct question in F216:** when speech capture, automatic speech recognition (ASR), playback, transcription, accessibility and accent variation interact, can LinguaChat distinguish **what the learner actually produced or heard** from **what a device or recognizer reported**, without awarding or denying independent speaking/listening mastery on unreliable evidence, while still adapting the next *actually delivered* instruction?

## Fixed objectives, evidence sources and eligibility

Bind each synthetic history to an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, target language and accepted variety/rubric, meaning-critical requirements, skill family (speaking versus listening versus reading/writing), independent production/reception obligations, prerequisites, supported accessible modality, review/coverage debt, meaningful-turn limits where applicable, and unchanged mastery/exit standards. English examples are illustrative only, not released lessons. The unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix boundary remains **UNSUPPORTED**. A text-only substitute cannot silently satisfy a required speaking or listening demonstration; nor may a valid accessible alternative be treated as a failure.

Freeze a **pre-decision** ledger of pseudonymous learner, task/rubric/policy/ASR/TTS versions when known, independently reviewed expected communicative intent and acceptable variants, offered and actually rendered/heard audio/text channels, user-selected accessibility settings when explicitly known, task language, item/context family, microphone permission/device/network status, capture start/end/completeness, acoustic quality or confidence only if genuinely available, ASR hypotheses with uncertainty and provenance, optional human-reviewed evidence if legitimately available, first unaided production, actual exposure to transcript/translation/model/hint, feedback/retry events, and later independent unfamiliar transfer. Never infer hearing ability, disability, ethnicity, nationality, accent identity, fluency, effort or attention from a single ASR confidence score, device locale, latency or missing microphone event. Do not require collection or retention of raw voice beyond independently authorized, minimal evidence; a synthetic fixture must not mandate a new audio-storage system.

Record offered → permission granted/denied → media actually played/captured → channel integrity → first attempt → recognizer output → uncertainty → independently reviewed evidence (if available) → feedback/exposure → retry → delayed unaided retrieval → unseen transfer → next **actually delivered** teaching option. Stable learner/session/run/event IDs and trusted occurrence order govern deduplication; upload order does not. Separate **capture absent**, **capture corrupt**, **ASR uncertain**, **linguistic error independently supported**, **human review unavailable**, **assisted repair**, and **clean independent skill evidence**. Missing evidence is UNKNOWN, not a failure or a pass. Log due/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators and skill coverage debt. A transcript shown before an answer is an answer-bearing exposure where it reveals the target, even if the microphone or audio later works.

ASR output is a **measurement**, not ground truth. Treat recognizer confidence as calibration-dependent telemetry, not a CEFR score; missing confidence is UNKNOWN. A system may request a repeat, offer a non-punitive clarification, select an eligible alternative or abstain from grading when it cannot distinguish recognizer error from learner error. An independently reviewed target-language oral performance may count under the existing rubric if truly supported and available; do not invent a human reviewer or pronounce a word correct from a text transcript alone. Compare instructional options only with comparable task difficulty, speech exposure, assistance, accessible opportunities, review intervals and independently reviewed outcomes. Interest weights affect context frequency, never pronunciation standards or the objective; rotate preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories with matched controls

**A — A recognizer's error is not automatically the learner's error.** For an existing independently requesting-information speaking can-do, a learner's first valid audio capture is independently reviewed (synthetic oracle) as the intelligible request “Could you tell me where the station is?”; the ASR hypothesis is “Could you tell me where the *statue* is?” with low or unreported confidence. An unsafe tutor grades a meaning-critical mistake and increases remedial pronunciation drills solely from the transcript. **Expected:** preserve the capture and first attempt; record ASR_HYPOTHESIS_UNCERTAIN and REVIEWED_SEMANTIC_SUCCESS under the pre-existing rubric; do not count the ASR substitution as a learner error or infer accent. Matched control: the independently reviewed actual utterance really says “statue” when the prompt requires “station”; apply the fixed semantic rubric without pretending the ASR caused the error. If independent review is unavailable, mark REVIEW_UNAVAILABLE and abstain from an unsupported speaking pass/fail; do not silently invent a reviewer.

**B — Reading a transcript is not listening to speech.** An existing listening can-do requires identifying a changed appointment time from audio. In session 1 the player is offered but never actually plays; the interface displays “The meeting is now at three” before the learner answers “three.” An unsafe policy awards listening mastery. **Expected:** AUDIO_NOT_PLAYED, TRANSCRIPT_EXPOSED, TEXT_SUBSTITUTE_NOT_LISTENING; the answer may support reading under a separately valid objective but not the fixed listening requirement. Matched control: an accessible, functioning audio rendition actually plays before any transcript, the learner answers unaided, and an independently reviewed delayed unfamiliar audio item succeeds without text exposure. Only this control is eligible for the corresponding listening evidence; replaying the identical clip with the answer visible is not unseen transfer.

**C — Missing microphone permission is neither poor speaking nor mastery.** A learner chooses not to grant microphone permission during a due speaking check; another synthetic learner grants permission but a device fault creates an empty file. Both type an excellent response to an offered accessible text alternative. **Expected:** distinguish PERMISSION_DECLINED_NOT_FAILED from CAPTURE_CORRUPT, retain the due speaking obligation, allow useful teaching/communication in text without claiming independent spoken production. Do not force inaccessible repetition or diagnose low confidence. Matched control: later the learner uses a genuinely supported accessible oral channel and produces a valid independently reviewed, unaided unfamiliar request; only then can the speaking requirement be evaluated. If no accessible oral assessment is available, retain UNKNOWN and surface the limitation rather than fabricate a CEFR exit or punish the learner.

**D — Feedback exposure must not become independent pronunciation or speaking evidence.** Session 1 captures an unaided oral attempt with an independently reviewed missing polite request form. The tutor then actually plays a model answer, shows a transcript and invites repetition; the learner repeats it accurately. **Expected:** preserve the first attempt, label MODEL_EXPOSED, TRANSCRIPT_EXPOSED and ASSISTED_RETRY, and never treat the repetition as independent oral mastery or evidence that a particular explanation works best. In a distinct later session an unfamiliar, valid oral request without model/hint may contribute CLEAN_DELAYED_ORAL_RETRIEVAL and UNSEEN_INDEPENDENT_ORAL_TRANSFER under the existing rubric. Matched control: a proposed model was never rendered; no exposure may be invented, but a replay of the same familiar item still does not establish unseen transfer. Listening to the model is not independent listening comprehension by itself.

**E — Accent and acceptable variants are rubric questions, not learner identities.** Two synthetic learners independently express the same polite request with meaning-preserving differences in pronunciation or accepted target variety. The recognizer is confident on one and uncertain on the other; neither recording is independently reviewed in the base history. An unsafe tutor penalizes the second, labels them “non-native” and narrows future contexts to a favorite topic. **Expected:** ASR_NOT_GROUND_TRUTH, REVIEW_UNAVAILABLE, VARIANT_REVIEW_REQUIRED; no confidence-based CEFR score, accent identity or exclusive topic filter. In matched reviewed controls, accept both if they satisfy the existing independently reviewed communicative rubric; reject a genuinely meaning-changing contrast only on reviewed evidence, not on demographic or accent proxies. The objective, evidence standard and eligible instructional methods remain identical when only interest weights change; preserve neutral and unfamiliar contexts.

**F — Out-of-order uploads and contradictory oral evidence must change actual decisions safely.** Session 1 offers a speaking probe but the microphone never captures. Session 2 records a valid unaided oral attempt, followed by a displayed model and assisted repeat. Session 3 records a different-session, unfamiliar independently reviewed oral success without hints. Session 4 records another clean unfamiliar oral result with a meaning-critical error. Upload order is 4, 2, duplicate 2, 1, 3; a separate alleged oral success has no trusted capture/exposure chronology. **Expected:** deduplicate stable IDs, quarantine unknown chronology, never turn session 1 into a failure or session 2's repetition into mastery, and preserve both legitimate session-3/4 outcomes with due debt and uncertainty. The tutor may revise a tentative feedback/scaffolding hypothesis and must record the next **actually delivered** eligible oral instruction rather than only a recommendation. Paired control reverses sessions 3/4 clean outcomes under matched opportunities; policy must be capable of revising the other way or sampling balanced alternatives. Because tasks, intervening instruction and follow-up may differ, neither history alone proves a causal method winner. A text-only result is never silently pooled as equivalent oral transfer.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Unchanged CEFR standards and skill boundaries.** All A–F histories bind existing versioned can-dos, reviewed target-language/variety rubrics, prerequisites, skill and modality obligations, due reviews, supported access and meaningful-turn ceilings. No recognition-to-production shortcut, B2 Arc 6 contract change, A1–C2 release or Pre-A1 mutation.
2. **Measurement provenance before scoring.** A publishes audio capture completeness, recognizer/version/confidence only when available, ASR hypothesis, reviewed ground truth if actually available, first attempt and independent semantic rubric result. Recognizer disagreement alone is not learner failure; absent independent review is UNKNOWN.
3. **Listening requires actual valid listening evidence.** B distinguishes offered from played audio and visible transcripts, rejects transcript-assisted answers as clean listening mastery and tests later independently reviewed unfamiliar audio comprehension without text leakage.
4. **Accessibility and channel faults are non-punitive.** C separates denied permission, empty/corrupt capture, learner choice, accessible text teaching and valid oral assessment. No fabricated speaking pass, punitive fail, disability inference or invisible lowering of standards; uncovered speaking debt remains visible.
5. **First-attempt and exposure boundaries are durable.** D records actual model/audio/transcript exposure, independent first speech, assisted repetition, delayed distinct-session oral retrieval and genuinely unseen transfer; copied or repeated speech cannot overwrite the original result.
6. **Accent/variety fairness is reviewed, not guessed.** E tests acceptable pronunciation/variety and meaning-critical differences under a fixed reviewed rubric, rejects ASR confidence or demographics as an ability proxy, and does not assign permanent learner types.
7. **Interests and modality policy are separate.** Matched E controls change only soft interest weights; topic frequency may change, but fixed speaking/listening obligations, rubric, method confidence and context-rotation requirements cannot. Preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts remain eligible.
8. **Replay, missingness and reversibility affect actual delivery.** F publishes occurrence/upload permutations, stable IDs, quarantined unknown exposure, due/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators, review debt and next delivered instruction. Opposite valid later evidence may produce opposite actual choices or balanced fallback without overclaiming causality.
9. **Executable synthetic proof is not human efficacy.** Publish machine-readable A–F traces and matched controls, deterministic seed/clock, exact objective/rubric/policy/ASR/TTS/task versions, independently reviewed audio/transcript/variant oracles where available, evidence/selection/coverage ledgers, per-event expected labels, next actual delivery and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED outputs in an independently reviewed exact-head executable harness. Synthetic PASS proves only software invariants. Claims of improved durable human learning require a separate ethically reviewed longitudinal study against a balanced accessible baseline, comparable speaking/listening assessment opportunities, delayed unaided retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer, missingness and subgroup/fairness analysis, and no hidden skill loss. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** F216 makes **speech evidence integrity under unreliable recognition, accessibility constraints and accent variation** falsifiable. A strong tutor may use speech technology to help, but cannot treat the machine transcript as the learner's ability, punish channel failure, or award speaking/listening mastery from an assisted text substitute. **UNSUPPORTED** until independently executed and reviewed; no real-world learning benefit is claimed.
