# F204 — Multi-speaker reference, ellipsis and independent communicative transfer

**Status:** design-only synthetic longitudinal curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until independently reviewed target-language/variety-specific reference-resolution and dialogue oracles, actual utterance delivery, first-authorship provenance, executable exact-head checks and genuinely delayed first-unaided unseen transfer bind to existing versioned CEFR can-dos. Not a new CEFR objective, a universal pronoun rule, shipped dialogue engine, automatic truth adjudicator or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F193 distinguishes who received a message; F198 integrates claims; F199 repairs misunderstanding; F201 attributes conflicting sources; F202 tailors summaries; F203 chooses whether and what to clarify. **Distinct construct:** across successive utterances by different speakers, can the learner track *who or what an expression refers to*, distinguish recoverable ellipsis from unresolved reference, and respond without silently inventing an antecedent? A grammatical pronoun can still be ambiguous. This is language-mediated discourse comprehension and production, not an authority to decide a real-world fact.

## Fixed objective and independently reviewed discourse oracles

Bind only **existing versioned** CEFR can-dos where following a short exchange, identifying participants or referents, maintaining coherence, checking a reference, relaying an utterance or responding to conversational ellipsis is genuinely relevant. No new graduation gate. Preserve the unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix disagreement; B2 stays unavailable.

An independently reviewed **turn-and-reference oracle** freezes the exact ordered speaker turns, quotation boundaries, speaker/recipient roles, antecedent candidates, grammatical features relevant *in the target language*, discourse prominence, temporal order, referent continuity, demonstratives, omitted material, corrections and what the learner actually received. Distinguish a uniquely recoverable antecedent, a plausible but underdetermined antecedent, a misleading nearest noun, and an ellipsis recoverable only from the immediately relevant conversational context. English gender or number agreement cannot be generalized to other languages; avoid assigning identities or gender from names or pronouns beyond what the utterance explicitly supports. The oracle must accept natural paraphrases and target-variety-appropriate referent checks rather than one magic wording.

A separate **communicative-response oracle** fixes the actual task: identify a referent, continue a conversation, relay a quoted statement, or ask a targeted question if ambiguity materially affects the response. It scores correct reference and pragmatic adequacy, not just surface grammar. When a referent is unresolved, accept explicitly preserving uncertainty or asking “Which folder do you mean?”; reject confident selection of one candidate. Do not reward unnecessary questions when the referent is clear. A clarifying answer changes the referent state only if actually delivered before the learner response. Quoted “I” is anchored to the quoted speaker, not automatically the current reporter. A statement about a plan or offer never by itself proves acceptance, receipt or completion.

The **actual-delivery and first-authorship ledger** records pseudonymous learner, stable session-qualified event/replay IDs, fixed can-do/discourse/oracle/rubric versions, speaker-turn order and exact text/audio actually rendered, quotation boundaries and corrections as delivered, candidate referents and whether the response required disambiguation, first eligible unaided interpretation or reply, hints/models and answer-bearing referent highlights actually rendered, retries and assisted repairs, optional reliable latency, recurring error type, instructional choice/dose/confidence, accessible modality route, and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed probes. Unopened help is not exposure; rendered answer-bearing referent arrows are. Failed audio earns no listening credit; a separately bound visible-text route may count as reading. Duplicate replays, copied repairs and calendar midnight cannot create independent mastery or delayed retention.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Recover a clear antecedent without needless clarification.** A volunteer says, “Maya put the blue folder on the desk. Nora picked it up.” Asked “What did Nora pick up?”, accept “The blue folder.” Reject “Maya” and an unnecessary “Which folder?” when no other folder has been introduced. A fluent pronoun replacement is also acceptable. A learner who correctly answers only after a rendered antecedent arrow receives assisted, not independent, evidence.

**B — A plausible pronoun is not always a resolved reference.** The notice says, “Maya spoke to Nora after she finished the poster.” No preceding utterance establishes who finished it. Asked who completed the poster, accept “It isn't clear whether Maya or Nora did” or “Who finished the poster?” Reject either name stated as certain. If a later delivered reply explicitly says “Nora finished it,” a subsequent response may name Nora; a merely scheduled or unrendered reply cannot resolve the ambiguity. The oracle must be reviewed for the actual target language and variety rather than treating English ambiguity as universal.

**C — Nearest noun and ellipsis traps in a multi-turn exchange.** Alex says, “The library has a printer, but the community center doesn't.” Jo replies, “Then let's use the one at the library.” Asked which printer Jo means, accept the library printer; reject inventing a printer at the community center. In a separate continuation Alex says, “I can cover the morning shift,” and Jo says, “Me too.” Accept that Jo also offers to cover the morning shift; reject that the schedule has been officially assigned or that Jo volunteered for the evening. The oracle freezes which of these two short dialogues was actually shown, not a stitched composite.

**D — Quotation, speaker perspective and a later correction.** A coordinator reports, “Lena told Amir, ‘I'll email the draft tomorrow.’” Asked who offered to email it, accept Lena; reject Amir, the coordinator, or a claim that the draft was already sent. Later the coordinator says, “Correction: Lena said Amir will email it.” Only if that correction was actually delivered before the response should the learner relay Amir as the planned sender. The corrected plan is still not evidence of a completed email. Distinguish the quote speaker from the reporter and the update from an earlier version.

**E — First authorship, answer exposure, audio failure and replay.** At 10:01 the learner receives B and independently writes “Maya finished the poster.” Record FIRST_UNAIDED_RESPONSE and REFERENT_MISASSIGNED or unsupported certainty. At 10:02 a complete model explains both antecedent candidates; at 10:03 the learner copies “We don't know who finished it.” Record ANSWER_EXPOSURE and ASSISTED_REPAIR, not independent mastery. At 10:04 a replay of B with the same stable event identity is deduplicated. An unopened hint does not count as exposure. If the audio fails but a transcript is actually rendered, no listening credit is awarded; a separately bound reading route may count. A later probe needs a genuine interval and a different speaker, antecedent and ellipsis/quotation structure, not renamed Maya and Nora.

**F — Reversible methods, contrary outcomes and soft interests.** Learner A often picks the nearest noun in multi-speaker dialogue. Across comparable completed sessions, SPEAKER_TURN_MAP, ANTECEDENT_CANDIDATE_CONTRAST and BALANCED_DEFAULT are actually delivered and sampled. Tentatively favor one only if later first-unaided unseen reference tracking improves with matched speaker count, ambiguity, grammar burden, help, channel, topic and delay. Learner B initially performs well after quote-color highlighting but later fails a new unaided quoted-“I” case; lower confidence, resume balanced exploration and test QUOTE_VS_REPORT_CONTRAST. A shorter dialogue, clearer morphology, familiar gaming example, extra prompt or shorter follow-up delay is a confound, not a causal win. Missing delayed follow-up remains unknown. Gaming may softly weight one context; adjacent sports, neutral libraries and unfamiliar community events remain in rotation, without excluding any required objective or modality.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Shared CEFR standard remains fixed.** A–F bind only applicable existing versioned can-dos with independently reviewed discourse and response oracles, preserving prerequisites, language burden, turn ceilings, thresholds, accessibility, skill balance and exit gates; otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE/UNSUPPORTED.
2. **Clear antecedents do not trigger needless questions.** A resolves “it” to the blue folder and rejects unrelated entities without inventing ambiguity.
3. **Underdetermined reference retains uncertainty.** B permits both plausible antecedents until a relevant answer is actually delivered; no fabricated certainty or target-language generalization.
4. **Ellipsis and nearest-noun reasoning preserve the dialogue.** C recovers the library printer and the morning-shift offer without inventing a community-center printer or an approved schedule.
5. **Quotation perspective and delivered correction are distinct.** D anchors quoted “I” to Lena, uses the later correction only after actual delivery and never treats a plan as completed action.
6. **First authorship, help and modality integrity.** E distinguishes initial unaided error, rendered answer-bearing help, assisted copying, unopened hints, failed audio, possible text route and replay deduplication; no phantom independent or listening credit.
7. **Matched reversible instructional adaptation.** F compares actually delivered teaching options against later first-unaided reference performance; reference complexity, morphology, help, topic, channel, register and delay confounds lower confidence and block causal-winner claims.
8. **Delayed unseen transfer and nonexclusive interests.** Require distinct genuine sessions, changed speaker/antecedent/quotation structures, first-unaided output, explicit missing follow-up, no script leakage, balanced receptive/productive skills and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar rotation; never skip objectives for interests.
9. **Honest evidence and protected scope.** Synthetic histories and generic CI are not executable reference-resolution QA, a shipped adaptive tutor or proof of improved human retention. Require exact-head executable delivery/provenance/response checks, observed reversible instructional decisions and ethically reviewed delayed independent outcomes against a balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge, Pre-A1 changes, A1–C2 enablement or weakened B2 boundaries.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic design histories, not observed learner behavior or executable tests. They specify how to preserve referents, speaker perspective, ellipsis and justified uncertainty across dialogue. Remain UNSUPPORTED until independently implemented and verified.
