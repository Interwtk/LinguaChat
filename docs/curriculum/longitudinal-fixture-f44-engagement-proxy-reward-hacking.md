# F44 — Engagement and completion proxies cannot replace durable independent learning

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a reproduced defect, shipped adaptive tutor, causal finding or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F24 guards against biased instructional assignment; F25 protects skill/review coverage; F35 protects scaffold fading; F40 exposes learner-level regression; F42 respects learner choice; F43 separates assessment-induced learning. F44 isolates a different risk: an adaptive tutor can optimize **engagement proxies** (clicks, minutes, streaks, completed cards, replay counts, praise, XP, self-reported enjoyment) that rise even as genuinely delayed unaided retrieval or unfamiliar-context transfer deteriorates. Engagement can inform usability and access, but is not a substitute for demonstrated CEFR ability. Neither lower engagement nor an early exit proves low ability.

## Controlled setup

Resolve `goal-1` to one **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, supported skill, frozen rubric, prerequisites and independent mastery/exit gate. Compare eligible A (`concise_explanation`), B (`worked_example`) and a shared balanced comparator across distinct actual sessions, with equivalent target demand, opportunity, independent scoring, due reviews, novelty and receptive/productive coverage. Inject a deterministic clock and seed, a versioned `engagement_proxy_guardrail_policy`, and an immutable synthetic ledger.

Record stable learner/objective/skill/session/run/event IDs; actual option eligibility, recommendation, assignment mechanism and known probability (unknown stays unknown); verified delivery and duration when observed; task difficulty and context family; card impressions versus started and completed activities; observed engagement events (streak, replay, click, XP, self-report) with their source and timestamp; first unaided attempts, retries, hints, models and scaffold dependence; due-review and coverage debt; actual elapsed delay, intervening practice, calibrated independent retrieval and unseen transfer; follow-up eligible/completed/missing denominators and reasons; rubric/policy versions; event versus upload time; and data-use lineage. **Do not infer attention, motivation, fatigue, disability, willingness or mastery from dwell time, silence, an early exit or an unobserved interaction.** Avoid unnecessary raw chat or sensitive profiling.

Maintain separate ledgers for (1) engagement/experience, (2) assigned versus delivered instruction, (3) valid independent CEFR evidence, and (4) uncertain learner/objective method-effect hypotheses. Declare `PROXY_ONLY`, `ENGAGEMENT_OUTCOME_DIVERGENCE`, `ASSISTED_COMPLETION`, `UNDELIVERED`, `OPPORTUNITY_IMBALANCE`, `FOLLOWUP_UNKNOWN`, `NOT_COMPARABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. Predeclare how proxies may influence *optional experience decisions* without entering mastery thresholds or method-superiority estimates.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — More clicks, worse delayed transfer.** B's gamified cards generate more starts, XP and immediate assisted correct completions than A, but comparable verified B exposures produce weaker genuinely delayed unaided retrieval and unseen-context free production. Preserve positive engagement reporting; flag `ENGAGEMENT_OUTCOME_DIVERGENCE` and refuse to promote B as the better teaching method on engagement alone. Do not call B harmful without credible attribution.
- **B — Low engagement, strong independent learning.** A learner skips optional celebrations and finishes quickly after A, yet repeatedly demonstrates calibrated delayed unaided retrieval and transfer. Another stays longer with B but relies on hints. Low clicks/time must not be counted as an A failure or lack of ability; independent evidence retains priority. The learner may still request more engaging presentation.
- **C — Scaffolded completion inflation.** A tutor raises its completion rate by revealing model answers, reducing challenge and allowing unlimited same-item retries. Completed-card and satisfaction totals rise; first attempts and unfamiliar production remain weak. Log support exposure, distinguish assisted practice from independent mastery and preserve the frozen can-do gate. Do not turn easy recognition into evidence of productive transfer.
- **D — Retention-based, reversible choice.** Across interleaved comparable A/B occasions, B initially earns more engagement while A yields stronger delayed unaided transfer. Later independent observations reverse the learning pattern. Update only a provisional objective-specific instructional hypothesis based on valid comparable learning evidence, with explicit uncertainty and feasible alternative sampling; engagement may still guide optional presentation.
- **E — Missing follow-up and opportunity distortion.** Learners assigned a longer B activity have more recorded minutes but fewer completed independent follow-ups because sessions end; A users receive more timely probes. Show eligible/started/completed/delivered/followed-up denominators and due-review debt. Missing delayed evidence is neither success nor failure, and exposure time alone is not a learning effect. Offer an accessible later opportunity without coercing completion.
- **F — Replay, offline XP and topic clickbait.** A duplicated offline completion awards XP twice; a stale displayed B card was never delivered; familiar-interest examples draw clicks while neutral/unfamiliar transfer remains untested; a model answer appeared before a supposedly independent response. Deduplicate by stable event ID, preserve trusted occurrence versus upload order, classify answer-exposed probes as assisted/contaminated, and keep interest sampling soft. Replay order must not change valid mastery, exposure or method-hypothesis conclusions.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Separated metrics and provenance.** For A–F publish the versioned can-do/rubric/skill, eligible/offered/assigned/verified-delivered instruction, engagement metrics, support/first attempts, independent delayed and unseen transfer, follow-up denominators and uncertain method-effect conclusions separately. A displayed card, time-on-screen, click or XP event is not a verified teaching exposure or learning outcome.
2. **Proxy divergence blocks false promotion.** In A, higher clicks, streaks, assisted completion or satisfaction cannot outweigh repeated contrary calibrated independent delayed/transfer evidence or trigger a claim that B teaches better. Preserve the legitimate descriptive engagement difference.
3. **No disengagement-as-failure shortcut.** In B, low time, no replay, skipped rewards or an early exit cannot automatically mark an incorrect can-do attempt, low motivation or a learner type. Preserve actual independent success and learner agency.
4. **Assistance and task demand remain visible.** In C, models, hints, retries and easier item families cannot be reclassified as independent first-attempt mastery; unchanged CEFR requirements and genuinely novel production still gate exit.
5. **Revisable, uncertainty-aware method policy.** In D, only genuinely comparable verified instructional exposures and later independent retrieval/transfer may provisionally adjust the learner/objective method hypothesis. Contradictory later outcomes can reverse it; keep nonzero feasible alternatives and balanced skills.
6. **Missingness and unequal opportunity.** In E, report offered/started/completed/actually delivered instruction and eligible/completed/missing independent probes by option and observed opportunity. No fabricated zero outcomes, unobserved dwell time, unsupported propensity or causal counterfactual.
7. **Replay-safe evidence, interest separation.** In F, deduplication, event/upload chronology, original rubric/policy versions, support exposure and holdout lineage yield invariant counts across replay orders. Interests affect example probability only; neutral, adjacent and unfamiliar contexts remain reachable.
8. **CEFR, accessibility and autonomy boundary.** Across A–F preserve shared objective/prerequisites/rubric, independent exit gates, due reviews, receptive/productive and context coverage. Unsupported listening/speaking stays `UNAVAILABLE`; user choices and deferrals are respected without treating them as efficacy proof or inferred protected traits.
9. **Conservative evidence and release claim.** If calibrated delayed independent retrieval, unseen transfer, adequate follow-up, exposure comparability or a credible balanced comparator is absent, mark method superiority `UNSUPPORTED`/`NOT_COMPARABLE`. Do not erase valid can-do evidence, conflate product engagement with learning or claim human efficacy from synthetic PASS.

## Required evidence and release gate

For A–F publish the **exact tested engine HEAD**, executable command, deterministic seed/clock, full immutable synthetic ledger, real supported versioned CEFR can-do and frozen rubric, engagement-event definitions and source, offered versus delivered instruction, first attempts/assistance, actual delayed independent retrieval and novel-context transfer, follow-up/opportunity denominators, policy/rubric versions, interest-context rotation, replay invariance, review/skill debt, uncertainty and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion.

A future claim that adaptive instruction improves durable independent learning requires separately reviewed longitudinal learner evidence with a credible balanced comparator, sufficiently delayed unaided retrieval, unfamiliar-context transfer, uncertainty and heterogeneous outcomes, and an ethically sound pilot. **Engagement is useful product evidence, not a CEFR mastery score.** Documentation PASS, if achieved, would establish only a software acceptance invariant.
