## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Decimal locale and prefix preservation in a recipient handoff.** A reviewed fictional supply label explicitly uses a Spanish decimal convention: **“1,5 L” = 1.5 litres = 1500 mL**. Learner A independently tells an English-speaking recipient “fifteen litres”; learner B independently says “one point five litres, or fifteen hundred millilitres.” Counterfactual: a bare “1,500” in a forwarded note with no trusted locale or unit is ambiguous; neither learner should invent whether it means 1.5 or 1500, nor assume litres. Exact SI prefix conversion does not establish the original measurement's precision or tolerance.

**B — Same dimension, different scale, and measured versus nominal.** A reviewed parcel note says **0.75 kg**, exactly **750 g** as a unit conversion of the stated quantity. Learner A independently relays “0.75 grams”; learner B independently says “seven hundred and fifty grams.” A second synthetic label explicitly says **12 inches**, equivalent by the exact international-inch definition to **30.48 cm**; this does not mean the physical object was measured to 0.01 cm accuracy. Counterfactual: if the source says “about 12 inches,” preserve “about” after converting and avoid presenting 30.48 cm as newly verified physical precision. An independently reviewed source correction supersedes the old label only within its stated scope.

**C — Dimensions, axes and fit restraint.** A synthetic board is labeled **30 cm long × 20 cm wide**; a faithful alternate representation is **300 mm × 200 mm** with axes preserved. Learner A independently sends “300 × 200” without units and promises it will fit a **25 cm**-wide opening. Learner B independently supplies both units and says that fit cannot be guaranteed without knowing permitted orientation, thickness, clearance and the opening's other dimensions. Counterfactual: if an authoritative specification supplies orientation, full opening geometry and tolerance, the recipient-oriented message may make only the resulting supported claim. Changing a 20 cm width into a 30 cm width is a reference error, not merely a numeric typo.

**D — Convention-dependent recipe volume.** A fictional recipe says “add **two cups**” but does not identify the cup convention. Learner A independently converts to “exactly 500 mL” as though every cup were metric. Learner B independently asks which cup system is intended. With a reviewed explicit **metric cup = 250 mL**, two cups are **500 mL**; with a reviewed **US customary cup ≈ 236.588 mL**, two cups are approximately **473 mL**. Neither is universally implied by the original wording, and a particular household cup's capacity is not known. The target-language task is to preserve or clarify the convention, not to certify a recipe's suitability or infer a serving size.

**E — Channel corruption, accessible equivalence and first-authorship.** An authoritative low-stakes maker note says “cut a **15 mm** strip, not a **50 mm** strip.” ASR renders “fifty millimetres”; captions replace “mm” with “cm”; a translation converts “1,5” to “15”; and a screen reader omits the superscript in a separate area label “m².” Preserve the **actually delivered** stimulus and mark altered constructs unscorable rather than penalizing the learner for a corrupted channel. If a learner independently asks “Did you mean fifteen or fifty millimetres?” at 10:00, and an answer-bearing conversion tooltip renders at 10:01, preserve the first unaided clarification but mark a subsequent copied answer ASSISTED. A readable text alternative can preserve unit-communication evidence, but it cannot prove unheard audio discrimination. Replaying one session-qualified event cannot manufacture delayed retrieval.

**F — Longitudinal outcome-sensitive method selection without fixed styles.** Fictional learner A immediately succeeds after PREFIX_ANCHOR_MINIMAL_PAIRS but later independently confuses kilograms and grams in a new parcel handoff; comparable actually delivered QUANTITY_UNIT_RECIPIENT_READBACK opportunities precede better delayed first-unaided communication on a distinct source and recipient. Only repeated matched observations may cautiously raise confidence in readback, while alternatives continue to be sampled. Fictional learner B initially succeeds with CONVERSION_WITH_SCAFFOLD_FADE but later overstates precision in an unseen craft message; confidence falls and balanced alternatives resume. If readback tasks contain labeled simple SI prefixes while the alternative receives unlabeled customary cups, or one method reveals the converted answer, mark confounding and infer **no winner**. Swapping “parcel” for “music equipment” in the same numeric template is not unseen transfer. Missing later opportunities remain missing, not failures. Topic preferences affect example probabilities only; neutral, unfamiliar, work, community and travel settings stay reachable under the same CEFR objective and rubric.

2. **Locale and unit-prefix exactness.** A preserves “1,5 L” as 1.5 L = 1500 mL under the explicitly supplied locale and SI definition. An unlabeled or locale-ambiguous numeral remains UNKNOWN rather than silently normalized.
3. **Dimension and precision integrity.** B preserves mass versus length, kg versus g, inches versus centimetres, and exact **conversion factors** versus uncertain **physical measurements**. Approximate source values remain approximate after conversion.
4. **Axis, geometry and recipient restraint.** C preserves each measured axis and unit and rejects a fit guarantee unsupported by orientation, clearance, tolerance or complete dimensions. A bare “300 × 200” is not an actionable unit-preserving handoff.
5. **Unit-system ambiguity discipline.** D never treats an unspecified “cup” as universally metric or customary. A correct clarification is positive communicative evidence; only a reviewed explicit convention licenses its conversion and precision.
6. **Actually delivered, accessible, chronological, idempotent evidence.** E reconciles authoritative and rendered ASR/captions/translations/screen readers/converter previews, quarantines construct-changing corruption, preserves first authorship before actual help exposure, separates accessible equivalent from different construct and deduplicates immutable session-qualified events. Reading cannot certify unheard listening.
7. **Controlled, uncertain and reversible adaptation.** F compares feasible actually delivered methods under matched unit complexity, source completeness, numeric burden, modality, register, topic, help, interval and follow-up opportunity. Sparse, contradictory or confounded evidence yields no winner; delayed regression reduces confidence and restores alternatives.
