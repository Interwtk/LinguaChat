## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Bundle quantity and unit binding.** Two fictional stationery offers state: Paper Fox sells **two pens for $4**; Blue Finch sells **three pens for $5**. A classmate needs six identical pens, and each offer may be repeated without limits or other fees. Learner A says Paper Fox is cheaper because “four is less than five”; learner B explains that three Paper Fox bundles cost $12, whereas two Blue Finch bundles cost $10, so Blue Finch is cheaper for six. A different, valid answer is possible if the task asks for only two pens. Counterfactual: change Blue Finch's bundle from three pens to two without changing its $5 price; the six-pen conclusion reverses. Preserve language evidence separately from arithmetic proficiency: if the learner correctly paraphrases “three for five” but miscalculates, do not mislabel the error as a receptive-language failure.

**B — Discount scope and exclusions.** A fictional sign says “Buy two notebooks: **20% off the second notebook only**; discount applies to the lower-priced notebook; cannot combine with coupons.” Two identical $10 notebooks would cost $18 before tax, not $16. Learner A announces “everything is 20% off”; learner B identifies which notebook receives the reduction and communicates the no-coupon rule. Counterfactual: change the source to “20% off **each** of two notebooks”; then $16 becomes source-valid. A fluent but wrong claim about coupon stacking fails the target condition binding. If prices differ, the lower-priced item is the discount base; do not assume the higher-priced item.

**C — Strict thresholds and unknown charges.** A fictional offer reads “Free delivery on orders **over $30 before tax**, within the city limits; otherwise delivery cost is not specified.” A $30 basket does **not** qualify; a $32 pre-tax basket qualifies only if the delivery address is within the city limits. Learner A says a $30 order has free worldwide shipping; learner B preserves the strict threshold and location restriction, and requests the missing delivery fee when comparing total costs outside eligibility. Counterfactual: change “over” to “at least”; exactly $30 then qualifies. Missing tax or delivery fee is not zero and must not be silently invented.

**D — Time-limited claim and stock uncertainty.** A fictional museum shop announces “Members may reserve the guidebook for $8 **through Friday 18:00 shop-local time, while supplies last**.” Learner A reports the price as guaranteed for everyone all weekend; learner B reports membership, deadline, local-time anchor and stock caveat, then asks for availability before promising a purchase. Counterfactual: move the source-effective end time to Saturday 18:00, or change membership eligibility; the supported answer changes. A screenshot from an expired offer version must not override a newer authoritative source without recorded version precedence. Treat “while supplies last” as uncertainty, not guaranteed failure or guaranteed success.

**E — Multilingual/accessibility fidelity and actual help chronology.** A validated target-language audio announcement includes “second item only”; a caption erroneously omits “only,” an auto-translation turns “within the city” into “anywhere,” and a screen reader announces a footnote exclusion before the offer it modifies without sufficient binding. Record the authoritative source and **actually delivered** versions; quarantine the affected scope/eligibility comparisons as UNSUPPORTED while retaining independently valid partial language evidence. A learner's accurate first explanation at 10:00 remains FIRST_UNAIDED if a queued worked example first renders at 10:01; a later answer copied from the rendered example is ASSISTED. Offline replay of the same event must not duplicate evidence, while identical words in a different genuine turn remain a distinct event. Do not infer weak comprehension from defective captions.

**F — Longitudinal method comparison and unseen recipient transfer.** Fictional learner A can repeat a tutor's worked discount example immediately but later misstates which item qualifies in an unaided conversation. After actually delivered CONTRASTIVE_DISCOUNT_SCOPE instruction on comparable tasks, A later independently explains an unseen cinema-ticket offer to a classmate, preserving eligible person, discount scope, date and exclusions. Learner B instead benefits from RECIPIENT_EXPLANATION_ROLEPLAY on comparable tasks and independently handles an unfamiliar library-fee waiver. Compare baseline proficiency, CEFR objective, target language/variety, numeracy burden, number of offer clauses, exception nesting, modalities, accessibility, topic familiarity, help exposure, review interval and follow-up opportunity. If one method always gets one simple clause and the other gets nested exclusions, mark METHOD_OFFER_COMPLEXITY_CONFOUNDED and infer **no winner**. Replacing store names while keeping the same quantities, clauses and answer path is not unseen transfer. Record eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-ups; missing is neither success nor failure. Repeated comparable delayed unaided outcomes may warrant **actually different next instruction** with uncertainty; contradictory evidence must lower confidence and permit reversal. Keep alternative sampling, skill balance, due reviews and nonexclusive topic rotation.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real CEFR and source linkage.** A–F resolve versioned descriptor/blueprint/prerequisites, source-authored offer/item/condition IDs, target language/variety, rubric/evaluator, first-unaided threshold, accommodations, protected skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. No hidden advanced quantitative prerequisite for beginners.
2. **Item, quantity, unit and benefit binding.** A/B preserve the correct item and quantity to which each price or discount applies. Changing only authoritative quantity or discount scope changes the expected interpretation; cosmetic branding does not.
3. **Eligibility, exclusions, strict boundaries and unknowns.** B/C/D preserve “only,” “over” versus “at least,” coupon stacking, membership, location and deadline rules. Unspecified shipping, tax or stock is not fabricated; warranted clarification can be credited.
4. **Source-effective time and version precedence.** D records local-time context, expiration and stock caveats; an expired screenshot or later conflicting version cannot silently overwrite the task's frozen effective source. Historical reporting remains distinguishable from current availability.
5. **First-unaided evidence and actual help chronology.** E captures first authored answer, retries and models **actually rendered before** it; queued, failed or post-answer help is not retroactive. Assisted copying never earns independent retention, and event replay is idempotent.
6. **Multilingual and accessible delivered-stimulus fidelity.** B/C/E independently validate discount/negation/eligibility scope in target-language variants, audio, captions, translations, reading order and accessible alternatives. A corrupt or unvalidated stimulus quarantines only affected construct evidence, not all unrelated language evidence.
7. **Delayed retention and genuinely unseen communicative transfer.** A–F require separated learning opportunities and noncosmetic unseen source-authored offers with changed condition structures and recipients, without answer-bearing help. Publish eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; a renamed identical price list is not unseen transfer.
8. **Reversible adaptation, confound control and soft interests.** F records feasible/assigned/**actually delivered** methods, task/numeracy/topic comparability, uncertainty, exploration, contrary outcomes, protected receptive/productive skills, due reviews and exit gates. Interest categories increase example probability but never filter out neutral/unfamiliar contexts or skip required CEFR objectives. No fixed learning-style labels.
9. **Exact-head deterministic reproducibility and bounded claims.** A–F emit deterministic seed/clock, named engine HEAD and runnable command, descriptor/source/offer/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, source-effective and actually rendered accessible artifacts, first-attempt/help timeline, condition and arithmetic oracle, provenance/replay, method delivery, confounds, delayed/unseen denominators and pass/fail/UNSUPPORTED reasons.
