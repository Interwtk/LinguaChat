# F64 — Fluency and accuracy must be balanced against independent communicative success

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a reproduced defect, shipped fluency scheduler, validated speech evaluator, CEFR certification or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F27 guards latency-observation bias; F49 guards construct-preserving accessibility; F51 separates self-initiated repair from prompted correction; F53 protects context-appropriate register and legitimate varieties; F54 concerns breakdown repair; F56 concerns feedback timing; F58 concerns initiative; F59 calibrates challenge; F62 separates recognition from independent production. F64 isolates **fluency–accuracy calibration**: a strong tutor may vary fluency-oriented free interaction, form-focused practice, rehearsal, reflection and corrective feedback according to later independent performance, but must not reward fast, long or smooth output that fails the communicative task, nor punish thoughtful, accessible or slower output merely for not looking fast. Accuracy and fluency are **task-specific dimensions**, not permanent learner types or interchangeable CEFR credits.

## Controlled setup

Resolve an **actual versioned** CEFR can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, source-defined communicative function, task, modality, evaluator/rubric, required language form and meaning, permissible accommodations, independent evidence, meaningful delayed retrieval, unseen-context transfer, due reviews and exit gate. Freeze the source's actual criteria for intelligibility, semantic completion, grammatical/lexical accuracy, interactional continuity, appropriate timing, register and repair **only where assessed**. A source task requiring spontaneous oral interaction may legitimately evaluate fluency; a reflective untimed writing task may not. Do not invent a universal words-per-minute threshold, pause penalty, ideal error rate or generic fluency score. Missing audio capture or a validated oral evaluator means `UNAVAILABLE`, not simulated speaking credit from text.

Predeclare feasible instructional candidates `FLUENCY_ORIENTED_INTERACTION`, `FORM_FOCUSED_MICROPRACTICE`, `UNTIMED_REFORMULATION`, `DELAYED_FEEDBACK_REFLECTION`, `MIXED_BALANCED_PRACTICE` and `SCAFFOLD_FADE`, with source-valid task mappings. Separate **instructional practice** from **independent assessment**. For comparable histories record versioned objective/rubric, skill and task construct, topic and audience, item novelty and language burden, candidate/selected/**actually delivered** method, practice dosage, target exposure, prior models and corrective feedback, first unaided semantic attempt, meaning-critical errors, optional measured response latency and its measurement quality, speech continuity only when validly captured, self-repair, retries, assistance, final response, and independent delayed/unseen transfer. Capture eligible/offered/rendered/attempted/completed/missing opportunities; immutable session-qualified event IDs; trusted occurrence and cue-delivery chronology versus upload order; and accessibility conditions only as necessary to establish construct equivalence, without unnecessary personal or health data.

These describe observations and task evidence, not fixed attributes of learners.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — Smooth speech with a meaning-critical omission.** Learner A produces a rapid, continuous request but omits a condition essential to the source-defined communicative goal; learner B pauses, independently reformulates and conveys the complete request accurately. A's surface fluency cannot compensate for failed task completion. B's slower production cannot be penalized absent a source-valid timing criterion. Record first attempts, any spontaneous repair, actual modality and separate accuracy, intelligibility, task-completion and fluency evidence; do not infer competence from speed alone.
- **B — Accuracy drill versus independent interaction.** Two learners have comparable prerequisite and first-attempt errors for a shared source-valid objective. A improves immediate form accuracy after repeated sentence corrections but still fails to use the form independently in a new conversation. B makes more local errors during varied free interaction but later conveys the intended meaning accurately and independently in unseen dialogue. Keep practice success and later independent communicative transfer distinct. Do not claim B's method superior unless item novelty, task construct, dosage, feedback and opportunities are comparable.
- **C — The apparent fluency gain is a measurement artifact.** One learner's microphone path clips silences, another speaks over a slow connection, and an asynchronous written response has no trustworthy response-start time. A generated transcript removes hesitations and an automatic speech metric reports higher fluency, despite unchanged learner-authored content. Mark unavailable or confounded timing as `FLUENCY_METRIC_UNAVAILABLE`/`LATENCY_CONFOUNDED`; never fabricate timing or convert a transcript into verified speaking performance. Retain independently scored communicative evidence where the source construct permits it.
- **D — Accessibility, variety and spontaneous repair.** A learner uses a source-permitted accessible input route that changes observable timing but preserves the assessed meaning construct; another uses a legitimate target-language variety not matching the tutor's preferred wording; a third pauses and self-corrects a meaning-critical error before receiving help. Apply the actual source-specific criteria, preserve first-attempt and self-repair chronology, and do not penalize accommodation or a valid variant. If the task requires a genuinely oral timing construct that the accessible route cannot establish equivalently, mark that specific gate `UNSUPPORTED`/`UNAVAILABLE` rather than awarding or denying it by guess.
- **E — Reversal and balanced instructional allocation.** Early source-comparable, independently completed delayed probes favor form-focused practice for learner A and fluency-oriented interaction for learner B. Later unseen transfer reverses A's apparent advantage as spontaneous communication breaks down, while mixed practice improves both required form and meaning. Reduce method confidence, revise A's objective-specific hypothesis, sample feasible alternatives and preserve due reviews, required reading/listening/writing/speaking opportunities and unfamiliar topics. A learner's high engagement with sports role-play is a soft context signal, never an exclusive topic filter or an excuse to omit formal requests.
- **F — Offline feedback leakage, duplicate timing and rubric change.** An interaction prompt is assigned but fails to render; an answer-bearing model is delivered before a revision but uploads later; the first independent response syncs twice; two learning sessions reuse a local attempt ID; a network clock skew makes a response look instantaneous; and a rubric revision changes whether a specific pragmatic omission is meaning-critical. Replay trusted occurrence and **actual delivery** chronology using session-qualified immutable IDs and frozen rubric versions. Deduplicate, quarantine ambiguous exposure and timing, preserve the original first attempt, and reject unvalidated cross-rubric comparisons. Both receipt orders must yield the same evidence ledger and provisional next choice.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed source and noncompensatory construct gates.** For A–F emit actual versioned CEFR can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, task, modality, evaluator/rubric, source-required accuracy/fluency/meaning/interaction criteria, permitted support, delayed retrieval, unseen transfer, reviews and exit gate. Unknown source mappings are `UNSUPPORTED`; unavailable oral capture/evaluation is `UNAVAILABLE`. Neither speed nor polished grammar substitutes for source-required communicative evidence.
2. **Meaning and first unaided quality precede surface fluency.** In A/B, score the learner's independent first semantic attempt and task completion separately from continuity, local form accuracy, intelligibility and assisted revisions. A smooth but meaning-incomplete utterance cannot receive full communicative mastery; a deliberate but successful utterance is not automatically a fluency failure.
3. **Fluency observations must be valid for the modality.** In C/F, log whether pauses, starts, interruptions and latency were actually observable, clock-aligned, source-relevant and unaffected by clipping, network or transcript processing. No invented speech timing, speed threshold or oral credit from text; uncertain measurement stays `LATENCY_CONFOUNDED` or `FLUENCY_METRIC_UNAVAILABLE`.
4. **Instructional choice is not instructional delivery.** In B/F, distinguish candidate, selected, assigned, rendered/heard, actually experienced, attempted and completed fluency/form/feedback options, including model exposure before each response. Failed renders and late uploads cannot become delivered practice or unaided assessment by assumption.
5. **Compare methods on durable independent outcomes.** In B/E, compare source-equivalent opportunities with documented prerequisite readiness, item difficulty, task construct, topic familiarity, target exposure, practice dosage, feedback, order and novelty. Otherwise label `COMPARISON_CONFOUNDED`. Require meaningful delay, intervening material and unseen unaided communicative transfer, with eligible/offered/completed/missing follow-up denominators; immediate corrected grammar is not sufficient.
6. **Self-repair, accessibility and variation are source-specific.** In D, separate learner-initiated repair from tutor-prompted correction; protect validated accessible routes and legitimate source-valid varieties. Apply an explicit oral timing criterion only to the actual assessed task. Never infer intelligence, disability, anxiety, motivation or a fixed learning style from pauses, accent or accommodation.
7. **No tradeoff may waive required CEFR objectives.** Across A–E, instructional mix may shift, but source-required accuracy, intelligibility, communicative function, modality and interaction gates remain intact. A high fluency score cannot erase recurring meaning-critical errors; isolated grammar drills cannot replace independent free production; missing modalities remain open obligations.
8. **Reversible policy, soft interests and replay safety.** In E/F, report alternatives, selected/delivered practice, independent later outcomes, uncertainty and next reversible decision. Contradictory delayed results can reverse a method hypothesis; duplicates, late feedback and receipt permutations cannot. Keep interests separate, rotate adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topics, protect due reviews and required skills, and avoid permanent learner labels.
9. **No documentation-to-efficacy inflation.** For each history output exact source/rubric IDs, dimension-specific scoring reasons, method assignment/delivery, first/final attempts, feedback and exposure chronology, measured versus unavailable timing, opportunity and missingness denominators, independent delayed/unseen outcomes, skill/topic coverage, review/exit debt, policy uncertainty, replay comparison and assertion-level `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED`. Documentation CI does not prove a shipped fluency evaluator, CEFR certification or improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, injected seed/clock, immutable session-qualified synthetic histories and upload-order permutations, real source-resolved CEFR task/rubric versions, validated modality-specific fluency and accuracy measures where required, independent first-attempt semantic evidence, actually delivered instructional/feedback chronology, matched opportunities or explicit confounds, meaningful independently completed delayed retrieval and unseen-context communication with eligible/offered/completed/missing denominators, protected review and skill coverage, reversible policy trace and per-assertion verdicts. Unverified fluency metrics, inaccessible modality, unknown cue exposure, incompatible rubric versions and missing follow-ups remain unknown or unsupported.

Any claim that balancing form-focused and fluency-oriented instruction improves **durable independent communicative retention and transfer** requires separately reviewed longitudinal real-learner evidence against a credible balanced comparator, source-valid task measures, adequate delayed unaided observations, checks for sparse-data and accessibility-related harm, and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor teaches both accurate language and usable communication; neither quickness nor polished practice answers alone demonstrate what the learner can do independently.**
