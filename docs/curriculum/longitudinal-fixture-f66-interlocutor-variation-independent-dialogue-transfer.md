# F66 — Interlocutor variation must earn independent conversational transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a reproduced production defect, implemented partner sampler, validated speaking evaluator, CEFR certification or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 guards item exposure and unseen transfer; F37 separates assigned from delivered instruction; F49 protects accessibility and construct equivalence; F52 distinguishes collaborative practice from independent evidence; F53 protects context-appropriate register and legitimate varieties; F54 tests clarification and breakdown repair; F58 tests learner initiative; F62 separates receptive recognition from independent production; F64 guards fluency/accuracy proxies. **F66 isolates interlocutor overfitting:** repeated success with a predictable tutor persona, prompt template or turn-taking pattern must not be mistaken for the ability to communicate with a genuinely different partner. The tutor may vary partner prompts, response styles, interaction demands and rehearsal structure, but must preserve the source-defined CEFR communicative objective and independent mastery gate.

## Controlled setup

Resolve an **actual versioned** CEFR can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, source-defined dialogue function and participant roles, task, modality, evaluator/rubric, required semantic and interactional criteria, permitted assistance, independent evidence, meaningful delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen partner/context transfer, due reviews and exit gate. Freeze those requirements before comparing methods. Never invent universal partner-count requirements, accent comprehensibility thresholds, conversational speed cutoffs or proof of oral proficiency from text. Where audio capture, speech evaluation or a partner modality is unavailable, mark that gate `UNAVAILABLE`/`UNSUPPORTED` and do not simulate it with a text transcript.

Predeclare source-valid instructional candidates `FAMILIAR_TUTOR_REHEARSAL`, `ALTERNATING_PARTNER_PROMPTS`, `UNPREDICTABLE_FOLLOWUP_PRACTICE`, `ROLE_AND_REGISTER_VARIATION`, `SCAFFOLDED_PARTNER_SWITCH` and `BALANCED_DEFAULT`. These are reversible **instructional choices**, not permanent learner types. The actual independent assessment must remain distinct from the practice that prepared it. An unseen partner is not established by merely changing an avatar, voice label, cosmetic name or interest topic while preserving the same answer-bearing prompt template.

For each comparable learner/objective history, record the versioned objective/rubric, task construct, partner/prompt generator and version, source-valid partner variation dimensions, semantic task equivalence, assigned/actually delivered turn sequence, rendered prompt and reply content hashes, dialogue role and turn ownership, topic familiarity, item and dialogue-template exposure, first unaided learner-authored semantic attempt, partner clarification requests, tutor hints/models/cues with **delivery chronology**, retries, spontaneous versus prompted repair, error recurrence, assistance dependence, valid modality observations, independent delayed retrieval, unseen-partner/context transfer, and eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators. Use session-qualified immutable event IDs and trusted event occurrence/delivery order rather than upload order. Do not collect partner identity or sensitive personal attributes when a task-valid prompt variant identifier suffices.

Predeclare observation labels `FAMILIAR_PARTNER_SUCCESS`, `PARTNER_VARIANT_DELIVERED`, `PARTNER_VARIANT_NOT_RENDERED`, `TEMPLATE_REUSED`, `PROMPT_SEMANTIC_EQUIVALENCE_UNVERIFIED`, `INDEPENDENT_FIRST_ATTEMPT`, `PARTNER_CUED_REVISION`, `SELF_INITIATED_REPAIR`, `INDEPENDENT_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL`, `INDEPENDENT_UNSEEN_PARTNER_TRANSFER`, `SOURCE_VALID_VARIETY`, `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE`, `FOLLOWUP_MISSING`, `COMPARISON_CONFOUNDED`, `EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED`, `RUBRIC_MISMATCH`, `UNAVAILABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. Labels describe task evidence, not fixed characteristics of a person.

## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — The familiar tutor script does not transfer.** Learner A completes several apparently fluent requests with a familiar tutor whose identical opening question, answer order and follow-up are rehearsed. In a later independently completed, source-equivalent dialogue with a genuinely different prompt/turn pattern, A omits a required condition and cannot recover without a cue. Learner B has less immediate success in varied-partner practice but later independently completes the same communicative function with an unseen partner variant. Distinguish template memorization, actual prompt novelty, first unaided meaning and transfer. Do not favor B's method unless exposure, prerequisite, difficulty, modality, feedback and opportunity are comparable.
- **B — Avatar novelty is not conversational novelty.** A test alternates three partner names, portraits and synthesized voice labels but all deliver the same answer-bearing script. A second test varies legitimate follow-up intent, clarification wording and turn-taking while preserving the fixed CEFR function. The first does not earn `INDEPENDENT_UNSEEN_PARTNER_TRANSFER`; the second can only earn it after verifying semantic/task equivalence and independent learner response. Record both visible presentation and actual rendered dialogue content; novelty must be evidenced, not asserted by a generator seed alone.
- **C — Legitimate variety, accessibility and modality limits.** A learner successfully interprets a source-valid unfamiliar phrasing or target-language variety; another uses a permitted accessible interaction route that changes timing; a third has only text input while the source task requires assessed oral interaction. Score meaning and source-valid pragmatic appropriateness without penalizing accent, register or accessibility differences that are not part of the construct. Do not fabricate oral fluency, partner-hearing evidence or reliable latency from a transcript or asynchronous text. Mark unmet modality-specific evidence `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE`.
- **D — Partner support masquerades as independent dialogue.** A partner's follow-up contains the missing answer, a tutor silently supplies a sentence starter, and a learner repeats it correctly. Another learner independently asks for clarification and repairs the request without answer-bearing assistance. Preserve first attempt, actual delivered cue chronology, who initiated the repair, whether the clarification revealed required content, and source-defined interaction criteria. Partner-supported success remains practice evidence; it cannot replace an unaided transfer gate.
- **E — Reversal, soft interests and review obligations.** Early matched delayed probes favor familiar-tutor rehearsal for learner A; later independently completed unseen-partner dialogues show repeated breakdowns, while varied-prompt practice improves A's source-valid completion. Reduce confidence, revise the objective-specific method hypothesis and sample feasible alternatives. A's preference for game-related partners is a soft context weight, not an exclusive filter: rotate adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts, maintain required registers, due reviews and receptive/productive skill coverage, and never skip a can-do because its context is unfamiliar.
- **F — Offline replay, prompt mutation and rubric drift.** A variant is assigned but never renders; a model answer is delivered before a learner revision but syncs later; an independent first attempt uploads twice; two sessions reuse a local turn ID; a generator update reuses a variant ID with changed content; and the evaluator changes the required pragmatic condition between sessions. Rebuild trusted rendered dialogue and cue exposure from session-qualified immutable IDs, content hashes and frozen prompt/rubric versions. Deduplicate replays, quarantine uncertain chronology, and refuse cross-version equivalence without explicit source-valid mapping. Both receipt-order permutations must yield the same independent-evidence ledger and reversible next choice.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Frozen communicative construct and noncompensatory CEFR gate.** For A–F emit the actual versioned can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, participant roles, task, modality, evaluator/rubric, meaning/interaction/register requirements, allowed assistance, delayed retrieval, unseen partner/context transfer, due reviews and exit gate. Missing source mappings remain `UNSUPPORTED`; a novel partner cannot waive required accuracy, modality or independent evidence.
2. **Real interlocutor variation, not cosmetic substitution.** In A/B/F distinguish displayed partner persona from actually delivered dialogue-turn content, generator/template versions, follow-up intents and answer leakage. An unchanged answer-bearing script with a new avatar, voice label or random seed does not count as unseen-partner transfer. Missing content provenance means novelty is unverified.
3. **Unaided first attempt and cue chronology are preserved.** In A/D/F score the first learner-authored semantic response separately from later hints, partner-leading questions, model answers, prompted repairs and final success. Preserve actual cue delivery before/after each response; upload order cannot retroactively make an assisted answer independent.
4. **Variation preserves source-equivalent task demand.** In A/B/C/E document matched communicative intent, language burden, difficulty, partner role, modality, prerequisite readiness, target exposure and rubric. If one partner supplies required content, changes task demand or makes a source-inappropriate register demand, mark `PROMPT_SEMANTIC_EQUIVALENCE_UNVERIFIED`/`COMPARISON_CONFOUNDED`, not a learning advantage.
5. **Later independently completed partner transfer governs adaptation.** In A/E require meaningful elapsed/intervening practice, unseen source-valid dialogue content and no answer-bearing assistance, with eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up counts. Familiar-script completion, immediate role-play success or a changed cosmetic persona cannot establish durable independent conversational transfer.
6. **Accessibility, legitimate varieties and modality truth.** In C protect source-valid language varieties, context-appropriate register and permitted accommodations without assuming learner deficits from response timing, accent or assistive interaction. When the actual task requires validated oral evidence, missing audio capture/evaluation stays `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE`; text is not phantom speech credit.
7. **Delivered partner turns and replay-safe evidence.** In D/F separate candidate, assigned, rendered, heard/read, attempted and completed partner turns. Reconstruct session-qualified immutable IDs, trusted prompt content hashes, versioned rubrics and cue chronology; duplicates and receipt-order permutations must not change first-attempt credit, unseen-variant classification or next-policy choice. Ambiguity fails closed.
8. **Reversible instructional hypotheses with protected breadth.** In A/E/F report feasible alternatives, selected versus delivered method, matched independent outcomes, uncertainty and next reversible choice. Contradictory delayed evidence lowers confidence and may reverse a choice. Sample alternatives, preserve due reviews and required skill/role coverage, and rotate interest, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts without treating any preference as proof of learning.
9. **Assertion-level audit and no efficacy inflation.** For A–F emit exact source/rubric and generator/template IDs, rendered prompt/response provenance, variation and semantic-equivalence checks, first/final attempts, assistance chronology, independent delayed/unseen outcomes, follow-up denominators, modality gaps, review/skill/topic debt, confounds, confidence, replay equivalence and per-assertion `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED`. Documentation CI cannot establish that an interlocutor sampler exists or improves human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, deterministic seeded multi-session histories and clock, real source-resolved CEFR tasks and rubrics, actual rendered partner-turn content and version hashes, source-valid variation/equivalence checks, session-qualified immutable IDs, first unaided semantic scoring and cue delivery chronology, matched instructional opportunities or explicit confounds, meaningful independently completed delayed retrieval and unseen-partner/context transfer with eligible/offered/completed/missing denominators, protected review/skill/topic coverage, receipt-order replay permutations, reversible policy traces and assertion-level verdicts. Missing partner rendering, oral evaluator, equivalence mapping or follow-up remains unknown, not fabricated success.

A claim that partner-varied practice **improves durable independent conversational competence** requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, genuinely unseen and source-equivalent partners/dialogues, adequate delayed unaided outcomes, checks for sparse data, accessibility and individual harms, and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor prepares learners to communicate beyond the tutor's own predictable prompts, not merely to rehearse a familiar chat script.**
