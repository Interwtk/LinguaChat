# F41 — Multiple comparisons and selective reporting cannot manufacture a winning teaching method

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. This is not a reproduced defect, a shipped adaptive tutor, a statistically validated effect, a causal estimate or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F24 guards against biased method assignment, F32 against selective follow-up, F37 against assigned-versus-delivered confusion, F39 against external-learning confounding and F40 against pooled improvements hiding individual regressions. F41 isolates another failure: **testing many teaching options, endpoints, subgroups, windows or analysis cuts and reporting only the favorable result can create an apparently superior method even when the complete longitudinal evidence does not support it**. Exploratory signals can guide further balanced sampling; they cannot silently become confirmatory evidence or loosen CEFR gates.

## Controlled setup

Resolve `goal-1` to a real versioned CEFR can-do, supported skill, frozen rubric, prerequisites and independent mastery/exit gates. Compare feasible A (`concise_explanation`), B (`worked_example`) and a shared balanced curriculum comparator; do not invent unsupported audio/speaking. Keep objective, independent item novelty, task difficulty, assessment calibration, actual retrieval delay, due reviews and neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar context opportunities comparable. Use a deterministic seed/clock, a versioned `multiple_comparison_reporting_policy` and an immutable synthetic ledger across genuinely distinct learning sessions and days.

Before inspecting outcomes, declare a **versioned evaluation manifest** containing eligible learner/objective/skill cohorts, method eligibility and actual-delivery criteria, baseline comparator, primary *independent delayed retrieval plus unfamiliar transfer* endpoints, meaningful actual-delay windows, minimum observation/opportunity criteria, follow-up handling, secondary outcomes (first-attempt quality, retries, hints, pacing, engagement), planned analysis families/subgroups, uncertainty method, multiplicity strategy where applicable, stopping rule and the criteria for a provisional versus unsupported decision. There is no universal significance threshold or invented sample size: a confirmatory statistical claim requires a justified design and error-control procedure appropriate to dependent repeated learner observations; otherwise report descriptive estimates and uncertainty only.

Record stable learner/objective/skill/session/run/event/probe IDs, assignment and known propensity (unknown stays unknown), verified instruction delivery, eligible options, item-family and rubric versions, independent first attempts, answer-bearing support, retries, actual delayed and unseen-context outcomes, due-review/skill debt, context family, observed external practice, follow-up eligibility/completion and missingness, trusted event versus upload times. Preserve **every eligible tested option, endpoint, horizon, cohort and analysis version**, including unfavorable and inconclusive ones; show denominators, exclusions and exploratory decisions. Never treat repeated probes from one learner as independent people or manufacture an unobserved counterfactual.

Predeclare `PREDECLARED_COMPARISON`, `EXPLORATORY_ONLY`, `MULTIPLICITY_UNRESOLVED`, `SELECTIVE_REPORTING_DETECTED`, `OPTIONAL_STOPPING_RISK`, `HORIZON_SWITCH`, `HOLDOUT_NOT_INDEPENDENT`, `CONFIRMATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED`, `NOT_COMPARABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. Keep (1) valid learner mastery, (2) uncertain learner/objective method hypotheses, (3) complete descriptive outcomes and (4) any defensible confirmatory or causal claim separate. An inconclusive comparison does not erase a valid independent can-do demonstration.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — Endpoint cherry-picking.** B improves immediate assisted correctness and one recognition metric, while its preregistered independent delayed retrieval and unfamiliar free-production transfer do not beat the balanced comparator. An analyst selects only the best of many endpoints and labels B the winner. Preserve all endpoints and the original manifest, flag `SELECTIVE_REPORTING_DETECTED`, and withhold superiority. Assisted repair cannot replace the primary independent outcomes.
- **B — Repeated peeking and favorable stopping.** Across successive learning days, noisy B-versus-A estimates fluctuate around no stable advantage. A report is frozen on the first favorable interim look although the declared stopping rule required later probes; subsequent complete follow-up is inconclusive or contrary. Retain all interim looks and actual collection windows, flag `OPTIONAL_STOPPING_RISK`, and do not treat the chosen peak as independent confirmation.
- **C — Search across learners, skills and topics.** An analyst explores many objective, learner-activity, context-interest and skill slices and discovers one apparently strong B result among small, correlated subgroups. No predeclared subgroup contrast or independent replication exists. Report the full searched family, subgroup opportunity denominators, uncertainty and overlap; mark the discovery `EXPLORATORY_ONLY`. Do not assign a permanent learner type, infer protected characteristics or generalize from recognition to productive ability.
- **D — Post-hoc delay-window switch.** The manifest calls for a calibrated later independent probe, but B looks best at an early near-immediate check and weak at the actual delayed horizon. A report renames the early check as 'retention' and drops later missed/negative outcomes. Preserve real elapsed times, eligible/completed/missing denominators and the declared window; flag `HORIZON_SWITCH` and `NOT_COMPARABLE` where applicable. Crossing a calendar-day boundary alone cannot create delayed evidence.
- **E — Honest exploratory discovery and fresh confirmation.** An early exploratory pattern favors B for one learner's written-request objective. The policy provisionally samples B more often but maintains feasible A/balanced exposure, due reviews and productive/receptive coverage. A genuinely later, separately reserved set of independent unfamiliar probes either corroborates or reverses the pattern. Label exploratory versus confirmation data distinctly; allow an uncertain, objective-specific hypothesis update only to the degree supported, without claiming a causal winner or a validated learning gain.
- **F — Replay, manifest revision and holdout leakage.** Duplicate offline probes arrive out of order; a rubric version changes without a calibration anchor; an analyst edits the manifest after seeing results; a 'holdout' probe was already used to choose B or exposed an answer-bearing model. Stable-ID deduplication, trusted occurrence ordering, immutable manifest versions and data-use lineage prevent double counting or laundering reused evidence into confirmation. Quarantine ambiguous chronology, incompatible rubrics and leaked/assisted probes while retaining narrower valid learner observations.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Complete comparison registry.** Across A–F emit the exact objective/rubric/skill, eligible/assigned/verified-delivered methods, comparator, versioned prespecified manifest, all tried options/endpoints/horizons/cohorts/looks, independent versus assisted outcomes, eligible/completed follow-up denominators, review/context obligations, exclusions and uncertainty. Never report only the best-performing cut.
2. **Primary outcomes cannot be swapped for easy wins.** In A, immediate assisted correctness, easy recognition or engagement cannot displace preregistered delayed unaided retrieval and unfamiliar transfer. Mark `SELECTIVE_REPORTING_DETECTED` and withhold superiority while preserving valid individual mastery evidence.
3. **Stopping and repeated looks are auditable.** In B, the number/timing of looks, original stopping rule and full follow-up remain visible. A post-hoc favorable stop cannot become a confirmatory success without a defensible predeclared sequential/error-control design; do not fabricate independent samples.
4. **Search multiplicity and subgroup overlap are explicit.** In C, disclose the full analysis family, correlated repeated measures, learner versus opportunity denominators, subgroup overlap and uncertainty. Unplanned discoveries remain `EXPLORATORY_ONLY`; do not apply arbitrary significance claims or fixed learner labels.
5. **Real retrieval horizons are binding.** In D, report actual elapsed time and intervening practice for every probe, including missing follow-up. An early response or calendar rollover cannot substitute for the declared delayed horizon, and missing outcomes cannot be imputed as automatic success/failure.
6. **Exploration and confirmation are distinguishable.** In E, record which evidence influenced policy selection and which genuinely later, unused, comparable independent probes test it. A result reused for both is `HOLDOUT_NOT_INDEPENDENT`; a corroborated pattern may adjust a revisable objective-specific hypothesis, not certify human efficacy.
7. **Immutable provenance survives replay and revision.** In F, deduplicate stable event IDs, retain trusted occurrence/upload chronology and original analysis/manifest versions, flag incompatible rubrics and answer exposure, and make counts/classifications invariant to replay order. Post-outcome manifest edits cannot silently overwrite the declared primary outcome.
8. **CEFR, skills, interests and privacy remain independent.** Across all histories preserve the same required can-do, prerequisites, rubrics, independent mastery gates, due reviews and feasible receptive/productive balance. Interests remain a soft context probability with adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar sampling; no protected-trait inference or sensitive-data collection is needed to find subgroups. Unsupported routes stay `UNAVAILABLE`.
9. **Conservative method claim and release gate.** Without a complete analysis family, comparable verified exposure, valid predeclared or explicitly exploratory analysis, calibrated independent delayed/transfer probes, appropriate multiplicity/uncertainty handling and adequate follow-up, mark method superiority `UNSUPPORTED`/`CONFIRMATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED`. Retain legitimate learner mastery, normal scaffolds and a balanced curriculum; neither a synthetic PASS nor a favorable selected estimate proves durable learning benefit.

## Required evidence and release gate

For A–F publish the **exact tested engine HEAD**, executable command, deterministic seed/clock, full immutable synthetic ledger, real supported can-do and frozen rubric, complete versioned evaluation manifest, eligible methods and verified exposure, full analysis/search family and stopping history, independent first attempts versus assisted repairs, actual calibrated delayed retrieval and unfamiliar transfer, baseline comparator, learner/opportunity and follow-up denominators, event and data-use lineage, replay invariance, review/skill/context coverage, uncertainty and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED for each assertion. State explicitly whether the evidence is descriptive, exploratory or eligible for a justified confirmatory comparison. Do not invent p-values, propensities, independent holdouts, counterfactual outcomes or statistical power.

A deterministic fixture PASS proves only a software acceptance invariant. Claims that adaptive instruction preserves or improves durable independent learning require separately reviewed longitudinal learner evidence, an ethically sound and comparable balanced baseline, genuinely independent delayed and novel-context probes, calibrated uncertainty, transparent reporting of all tested outcomes and a check for individual regressions and sparse-data harm. A single attractive result selected from many analyses is not that evidence.
