# F61 — Blocked versus interleaved practice must be judged by delayed independent discrimination

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a reproduced defect, shipped activity-order selector, CEFR certification, validated desirable-difficulty score, or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F29 treats instructional carryover; F33 guards unseen item exposure; F36 guards cross-skill interference and review opportunity cost; F59 calibrates practice challenge; F60 isolates explanation strategy. F61 isolates **within-objective practice ordering and discrimination**: a tutor may provisionally alternate similar source-valid forms, meanings, functions or contexts (interleaving), or temporarily group practice by target (blocking), based on what the learner later distinguishes and produces **without help**. The tutor must not assume interleaving is universally better, mistake momentary switching errors for inability, or reward blocked repetition merely because immediate accuracy looks higher. Ordering is an uncertain, reversible instructional decision, not a learner identity.

## Controlled setup

Resolve an **actual versioned** CEFR can-do, curriculum blueprint, prerequisite graph, language and skill/modality, task and evaluator/rubric, target meaning/form/use, permissible aids, independent mastery evidence, meaningful delayed retrieval, unseen-context transfer, due reviews and exit gates. Define source-valid **contrast sets** (e.g., two grammatical forms whose meaning or pragmatic use must be distinguished) and predeclare which targets genuinely belong to the same can-do, which are prerequisites or separate objectives, and which are not construct-equivalent. A switch between different CEFR objectives is not automatically an interleaving trial for one objective. Keep required coverage, assessment construct, mastery threshold and supported modality unchanged.

For a comparable trial, predeclare matched **total opportunities per target**, target difficulty, answer visibility, feedback timing, explanation content, scaffold availability, prior item exposure, spacing and time-on-task when observable. Record exact item order and which responses were actually seen, heard or completed; an assigned activity that never renders is not practice. Separate order effects from extra exposure, recency, novelty, topic familiarity, answer-key leakage, task format, missing prerequisite and fatigue that cannot be reliably inferred. Interleaving may create desirable discrimination, but repeated nonproductive failure may warrant a reversible blocked bridge followed by mixed unaided tests. No universal optimal alternation ratio, latency threshold or required number of switches is invented.

Use deterministic pseudonymous synthetic learner histories, injected clock/seed, distinct real sessions, immutable session-qualified event IDs, and privacy-minimized logs. Record source/rubric versions, eligible order variants, selection and **actual delivery**, item/answer exposure, target identity and contrast relation, first unaided semantic attempt, error category (including **confusion between contrast members**), optional measured latency, retries, hints/models/scaffolds, self-correction, independent free production, meaningful intervening material/time, unseen-context discrimination and transfer, and eligible/offered/completed/missing delayed probes. Keep **interest-topic weighting** independent from order selection and rotate neutral, adjacent and unfamiliar contexts. Do not infer intelligence, disability, effort, personality, fixed “blocked/interleaved learner” type, or mastery from speed, stated preference or coached final answers.

Predeclare `BLOCKED_ORDER_DELIVERED`, `INTERLEAVED_ORDER_DELIVERED`, `ORDER_ASSIGNED_NOT_DELIVERED`, `CONTRAST_CONFUSION_FIRST_ATTEMPT`, `SCaffold_EXPOSED`, `ASSISTED_PRACTICE_ONLY`, `INDEPENDENT_DELAYED_DISCRIMINATION`, `INDEPENDENT_UNSEEN_TRANSFER`, `ORDER_COMPARISON_CONFOUNDED`, `FOLLOWUP_UNKNOWN`, `RUBRIC_MISMATCH`, `MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE`, `EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED`, `UNAVAILABLE` and `UNSUPPORTED`. These are provenance states, not stable learner labels.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — Blocked fluency masks contrast confusion.** On the same source-mapped contrast, learner A completes a block of form X followed by a block of form Y with near-perfect final accuracy, partly by inferring the answer from block position. Learner B makes more first-attempt mistakes on counterbalanced mixed practice but later independently chooses X versus Y appropriately in unseen contexts. The tutor must not award A durable discrimination from immediate block accuracy, or treat B's initial switching errors as conclusive failure. Match exposure and independent probes; preserve both first attempts and coached outcomes.
- **B — Mixed practice too early, blocked bridge helps.** Learner A has the prerequisites and improves delayed independent discrimination after mixed contrasts. Learner B lacks a source-defined prerequisite and repeatedly guesses under the same alternation; a short, source-valid blocked bridge plus scaffold fading and subsequent mixed unaided testing yields better later independent transfer. Do not permanently classify B as a blocked learner, infer inability from early errors, or lower the shared mastery threshold. If prerequisite instruction differs between arms, report the direct ordering comparison as confounded.
- **C — False interleaving advantage from recency and answer leakage.** A mixed sequence gives more examples of X, ends with X, repeats familiar music-themed items and exposes a model before the transfer probe; the blocked comparison uses unseen neutral contexts. An apparent interleaving win disappears under matched per-target exposure, counterbalanced order/recency, novel items and neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar contexts. Distinguish topic and answer exposure from ordering; a recycled item is not unseen transfer.
- **D — Switch cost is not a mastery or accessibility diagnosis.** A learner takes longer to switch formats but independently conveys correct meaning; another uses a source-permitted accessible display or requests a pause; a third cannot access the required audio contrast because the evaluator is unavailable. Optional measured latency and pauses are not failure proxies, accessibility aids are not automatically answer-revealing, and text-only discrimination cannot certify a required listening or speaking objective. Offer valid practice or a pause, retain due reviews and return `UNAVAILABLE` where the construct cannot be evaluated.
- **E — Reversal after genuinely delayed probes and coverage pressure.** A provisional policy favors interleaving because early unaided mixed probes look stronger, but later delayed retrieval reveals repeated X/Y confusions; a blocked bridge followed by independently mixed transfer improves retention. A second learner shows the opposite trajectory. Revise per-objective order hypotheses and uncertainty, continue feasible alternatives, and do not crowd out due reviews, weaker can-dos, receptive/productive skill coverage or unfamiliar topics to maximize an easy discrimination score. Missing delayed probes lower confidence rather than counting as failure or success.
- **F — Offline delivery, duplicate events and rubric drift.** An interleaved sequence is assigned but the UI only renders its first item; a model for the second target is displayed before a retry but syncs after it; a first independent response uploads twice; a new session reuses a local item ID; and a later rubric version changes acceptable contrast use. Reconstruct trusted occurrence/**actual exposure** chronology using session-qualified IDs, immutable source versions and replay permutations. Count only delivered opportunities, preserve first-attempt independence where warranted, mark exposed revisions assisted, quarantine ambiguous timing and reject unsupported cross-rubric comparisons. Receipt order must not change the evidence or policy ledger.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source and standard stay fixed.** For A–F emit actual versioned can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, contrast-set mapping, skill/modality, evaluator/rubric, permissible supports, independent mastery requirement, delayed and unseen transfer, due reviews and exit gate. If a contrast is not source-equivalent, do not combine it into a fabricated mastery score; missing mappings are `UNSUPPORTED` and missing modalities/evaluators are `UNAVAILABLE`.
2. **Ordering is not exposure quantity.** In A/C, record exact per-target eligible, assigned, delivered, attempted and completed opportunities; item order, recency, topic, answer visibility, feedback and scaffold exposure. Do not credit blocked or interleaved practice when content was not actually experienced or when exposure imbalance explains the difference.
3. **First unaided discrimination outranks position-cued success.** In A/B, distinguish semantic use of each contrast member before hints from success after model, predictable block position or revealing prompts. Preserve assisted practice as useful instruction but not independent mastery evidence.
4. **Order comparisons require construct-equivalent tasks.** In B/C, hold prerequisites, difficulty, target count, modality, evaluator, explanation, spacing and independent assessment comparable or explicitly mark `ORDER_COMPARISON_CONFOUNDED`. Do not turn an untested contrast, added prerequisite lesson or switched CEFR objective into a causal ordering claim.
5. **Delayed unseen discrimination decides provisional value.** In A/E, require meaningful intervening time/material, source-valid independently completed mixed discrimination and unseen-context free production where required. Report eligible/offered/completed/missing probe denominators; immediate blocked accuracy, assisted revisions and calendar rollover alone are insufficient.
6. **Switching cost and accessibility do not define the learner.** In D, measured latency is contextual, not a timed gate; supported accommodations and opt-outs are not automatic failures. Missing oral/listening evaluation stays unavailable; no psychological, disability or permanent “interleaving type” inference is permitted.
7. **Policy remains uncertain, reversible and balanced.** In B/E, expose available order options, selected versus delivered choice, later independent outcomes, uncertainty and a reversible next decision. Contradictory delayed evidence must be able to reverse an earlier preference. Maintain feasible exploration, due reviews, skill balance and soft interest rotation without excluding unfamiliar contexts.
8. **Replay and rubric provenance are stable.** In F, duplicate uploads, reused local IDs, late model sync, partial rendering, receipt-order permutations and rubric changes must converge on the same first-attempt, independent/assisted, exposure, review and policy ledgers. Unknown exposure remains unknown; do not invent cross-version equivalence.
9. **No documentation-to-efficacy inflation.** For each history output source/rubric IDs, contrast map, per-target order and delivery ledger, first/final semantic attempts, errors, cues, independent delayed/transfer outcomes with missingness, topic/skill coverage, provisional order hypothesis/uncertainty and per-assertion `PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNSUPPORTED`. Green documentation CI does not mean an executable evaluator, shipped adaptive tutor, CEFR certification or improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, deterministic seed/clock, immutable synthetic events with receipt-order permutations, source-resolved contrast and rubric versions, per-target assigned/**delivered** opportunity counts and item chronology, matched practice comparisons or explicit confound labels, first-attempt semantic outcomes, actual hint/model exposure, independently completed meaningfully delayed mixed discrimination and unseen-context transfer with eligible/offered/completed/missing denominators, skill/topic balance, reversible policy decisions and assertion-level verdicts. Unmeasured delivery, inaccessible modality, uncertain construct equivalence and missing follow-ups remain explicitly unknown or unsupported.

A future claim that individualized ordering improves **durable independent discrimination and transfer** requires separately reviewed longitudinal real-learner evidence against a credible balanced comparator, sufficient delayed unaided outcomes, subgroup harm checks for sparse-data and accessibility-supported learners, and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor varies practice order to help the learner distinguish meaning independently later, and changes course when evidence contradicts the initial choice.**
