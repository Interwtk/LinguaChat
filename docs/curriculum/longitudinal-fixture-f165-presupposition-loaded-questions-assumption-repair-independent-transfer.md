# F165 — Presuppositions, loaded questions, assumption repair and independent communicative transfer

**Status:** design-only longitudinal curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until exact-head executable tests bind independently reviewed pragmatic/source-world oracles to existing versioned CEFR communicative objectives, actually delivered accessible tasks and later first-unaided genuinely unseen recipient-facing transfer. This is **not** a shipped presupposition detector, a new CEFR requirement, outcome-responsive adaptation or proof of improved human learning. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F83 covers conditional commitments; F103 critical-detail readback; F118/F157 negation and quantifier scope; F159 reported speech and epistemic stance; F161 degree thresholds; F162 reference/clarification; F164 corrections and exceptions. **Distinct construct:** can a learner independently recognize when a question or utterance **takes an unverified premise for granted**, distinguish asserted content from a presupposed or merely suggested premise, and repair it politely without accidentally accepting it? This is practical comprehension and clarification, not a compulsory formal pragmatics course for beginners.

Do not score an ordinary request as malicious merely because it carries a presupposition. **Even** may convey an expectation or surprise, but does not prove the speaker's social judgment. ## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — A question cannot force a false prior event.** Fictional library desk asks **“When did you stop volunteering here?”** Source-world record explicitly says the learner has **never volunteered**. A valid recipient reply is **“I haven't volunteered here, so there's no date when I stopped.”** Inventing a stop date fails. Counterfactual: a separate reviewed record says the learner volunteered until May; **“I stopped in May”** becomes licensed. The question's form alone is not evidence that the activity happened.

**B — Again versus first occurrence.** Fictional club message: **“Did you send the invitation again?”** Reviewed record shows the invitation has **not been sent before**. **“No, this would be my first time sending it”** repairs the premise; **“Yes, I resent it”** invents a repeat. Counterfactual: a timestamped first send exists and a second send is requested; now **again** is supported but whether the second send happened still requires evidence. A question does not itself confirm an action.

**C — Still and continuation are defeasible.** Fictional classmate asks **“Are you still waiting outside?”** Available record shows the learner **arrived only moments ago** and has not begun waiting. **“I just arrived; I haven't been waiting”** is a valid repair. If the earlier record instead shows a wait beginning at 14:00, **“Yes, I'm still waiting”** may be supported by a contemporaneous learner report. Counterfactual **“Are you waiting outside?”** removes the prior-wait premise; do not score these as equivalent stimuli.

**D — Addition and surprise are not verified history.** Fictional event organizer says **“Nora is coming too”** after a discussion in which no other attendee was confirmed. **“Who else is coming?”** is justified; the word **too** does not name another attendee. In a separate source-world, **“Even Nora joined”** can suggest surprise or an expectation, but it does **not** prove Nora was unwilling, late or excluded. **“Nora joined”** is the stated event; guesses about her motivation fail. Counterfactual **“Nora and Leo joined”** explicitly names both participants. Competent pragmatic review must allow contextual variation rather than impose one universal implicature.

**E — Actual delivery, first authorship and event identity.** Original fictional audio says **“Did you send the invitation again?”** ASR drops **again**; a translated caption inserts **already**; a screen reader moves a later correction ahead of the question. Quarantine construct-changing delivery rather than penalizing the learner for a missing or added premise. At 10:00 the learner independently writes **“This is my first invitation; do you mean send it now?”**; at 10:01 a tutor answer-bearing model is actually rendered; at 10:02 the learner repeats the model. Preserve the 10:00 FIRST_UNAIDED clarification; the 10:02 response is ASSISTED. Queued but unrendered help cannot retroactively contaminate authorship; replayed event IDs cannot create extra independent trials. Corrected text cannot certify unheard listening.

**F — Reversible instructional adaptation, real transfer and interests.** Fictional learner A correctly labels **again/still** after FACT_VS_ASSUMPTION_SOURCE_MARKUP_WITH_FADE but later independently accepts false premises in new conversations. Several matched, actually delivered POLITE_PREMISE_REPAIR_ROLEPLAY opportunities precede better delayed first-unaided corrections with new speakers, different triggers and unfamiliar contexts. Repeated comparable evidence may cautiously favor roleplay; alternative methods remain sampled. Learner B initially benefits from roleplay but later invents prior events: lower confidence, advance review and reopen exploration. If one method has a single short question and the other contains nested negation, three speakers and an unfamiliar register, infer **no method winner**. A music-to-games noun swap with identical question structure is not unseen transfer. Missing follow-up is MISSING, not failure; neutral and unfamiliar contexts remain eligible regardless of interests.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Existing CEFR objective and reviewed pragmatic oracle.** A–F bind versioned can-dos, prerequisites, recipient/information gap, source-world and pragmatic oracle, language/variety/register, actually delivered accessible task, evaluator/rubric, first-unaided threshold, due review, delayed/unseen probes and unchanged exit gate. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED; no invented advanced pragmatics requirement.
2. **False-premise repair without fabricated history.** A rejects an unsupported prior volunteering event, allows a natural correction, and licenses a stop date only when the independently reviewed source-world supplies it.
3. **Repeat presupposition versus completed action.** B distinguishes a prior invitation, a requested resend and an actually completed resend; a question alone cannot establish any send.
4. **Continuation and additive/surprise distinctions.** C–D preserve **still/too/even** and their contextual, defeasible implications; no invented earlier waiting, unnamed attendee, motive or social judgment is promoted into verified fact.
5. **Pragmatic response quality and uncertainty.** A–D accept brief polite, recipient-actionable corrections or targeted clarification, with context-sensitive English/Spanish variety review; they do not require a formal linguistics explanation or penalize valid alternative phrasing.
6. **Actual delivery, chronology and deduplication.** E compares original and rendered ASR/captions/translation/screen-reader order, quarantines construct-changing drift, protects pre-help authorship, records actual answer exposure and deduplicates stable events. Accessible reading cannot certify unheard listening.
7. **Controlled uncertain reversible adaptation.** F compares feasible actually delivered methods under matched trigger/negation load, language, modality, topic, source completeness, help, spacing and follow-up. Sparse, contradictory, selective or confounded evidence yields no winner; delayed regression lowers confidence and reopens exploration.
8. **Delayed first-unaided genuinely unseen transfer and nonexclusive interests.** Assisted trigger highlighting, same-session repeats, copied replies and topic-only substitutions cannot earn delayed/unseen mastery. Require a later distinct eligible session, changed recipient/context **and changed presupposition trigger or premise-repair structure**, reviewed oracle, first-unaided actionable repair or clarification and explicit missingness. Preserve shared objectives, balanced modalities and neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar topic rotation.
9. **Honest implementation and efficacy boundary.** Passing documentation CI does not ship a pragmatic evaluator, adaptive tutor or proof of improved human retention. Require exact-head executable assertions against real objectives, actually delivered instruction changes driven by longitudinal outcomes, later independent transfer against a balanced baseline and separately reviewed learner evidence before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, level enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic design/probe histories, not observed learner outcomes or executable QA. Preserve premise uncertainty, natural clarification, accessible delivery and explicit UNSUPPORTED gates until implementation and longitudinal evidence exist.
