Do not turn everyday “unless” into a universal biconditional: the source may specify a defeater without establishing that no other restrictions exist. A satisfied necessary condition is not sufficient; a conditional promise cannot be reversed; a permission is not proof of actual action. ## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — “Only if” names a necessary condition, not guaranteed access.** A reviewed library notice states: “You may borrow the recording kit **only if** you have a current library card. Staff approval is also required.” Learner A tells a friend “You have a card, so you can definitely borrow the kit”; learner B independently says “A valid card is required, but you still need staff approval.” Counterfactual: if staff approval status is unknown, ask; do not invent a denial. The notice does not imply everyone without a card is physically unable to enter the library or that having both requirements guarantees stock availability.

**B — “If” promise is not its converse; permission is not obligation.** A reviewed workshop message says “**If** your booking is confirmed, we will email a code. Confirmed participants **may** attend the optional practice session.” Learner A concludes “I received a code, so my booking must be confirmed, and I must attend practice”; learner B independently relays “A confirmed booking triggers a code email; the practice session is optional. Receiving a code alone does not prove confirmation under this notice.” Counterfactual: an independently verified source could explicitly establish that codes are sent **only** to confirmed participants; without that evidence, the converse remains unsupported. Do not treat a conditional promise as proof the email system worked.

**C — “Unless” exception and rule priority.** A reviewed club notice says “Guests cannot enter the equipment room **unless accompanied by a staff member**. Even accompanied guests must wear safety glasses.” Learner A says “With staff you can enter without glasses”; learner B independently explains “Accompaniment removes one barrier, but safety glasses are still required; the notice does not establish that every accompanied guest is automatically admitted.” Counterfactual: if a later authorized notice exempts supervised safety demonstrations from the glasses rule, bind its exact scope, authority and effective time. An ambiguous “except during demonstrations” without a named referent requires clarification rather than an invented exception.

**D — Conjunction, disjunction, threshold and effective time.** A synthetic registration notice, independently reviewed for its intended meaning, states: “From **Monday 09:00 local time**, members with **at least 10 credits** **and** a completed form may request a seat. A supervisor or coordinator may approve requests. Requests are not guaranteed places.” Learner A treats 10 as excluded, interprets supervisor **or** coordinator as both required, ignores the start time and promises a seat; learner B independently says “Ten credits meet the minimum, but the form and opening time also matter; either named role can approve a request, and approval is not a guaranteed seat.” Counterfactual: if “or” is explicitly exclusive in an amended rule, follow that reviewed definition; if the timezone, form status or policy version is unknown, do not invent eligibility. The oracle must distinguish permission to **request** from entitlement to **receive**.

**E — Accessible channels, delivered help and chronology.** Authoritative audio says “You can enter **only if** a host signs you in, **unless** the room is reserved for a private event.” ASR drops “only,” captions omit “unless,” a translation turns “can” into “must,” and a screen reader reads the exception after a prewritten answer summary. Record what was actually rendered, not just the canonical text. If the learner first independently asks “Is the host sign-in sufficient, or are there other restrictions?” at 10:00, and a model answer first renders at 10:01, preserve the independent first move; the revised answer after exposure is ASSISTED. A readable transcript can demonstrate reading, not hearing an inaudible conditional. Quarantine affected constructs only, including when a modality changes the source-world meaning. Replaying one session-qualified event does not create delayed evidence.

**F — Outcome-sensitive, uncertain and reversible instruction.** Fictional learner A gives correct immediate “only if” answers after CONDITION_GRAPH_WITH_FADE but later independently reverses an unseen library requirement in a new recipient conversation. Comparable actually delivered IF_VS_ONLY_IF_MINIMAL_PAIRS trials precede better delayed first-unaided explanations of a different service's prerequisites; preference for the second method may increase **only** after repeated matched evidence, with alternatives still sampled. Fictional learner B initially succeeds after PERMISSION_VS_REQUIREMENT_ROLEPLAY but later mistakes an exception for blanket permission; confidence falls and balanced options resume. If one method uses complete one-clause notices and the other uses ambiguous multi-clause exceptions, or if one method shows a prewritten answer, flag confounding and infer **no winner**. Merely swapping “library card” for “club card” in the same rule template is not unseen transfer. Missing probes are missing, not wrong. A learner interested in gaming may more often see game-club entry notices; transport, community, workplace, neutral, adjacent and unfamiliar settings remain reachable under the same CEFR can-do and rubric.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real CEFR objective and reviewed rule oracle.** A–F bind existing versioned descriptors/can-dos, recipient/purpose, policy authority/version/time, condition direction, permission/obligation, exceptions/priority, reviewed natural-language realizations, accessible stimuli, evaluator/rubric, first-unaided threshold, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and unchanged exit gate. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED, never fabricated policy or CEFR mastery.
2. **Necessary/sufficient direction.** A does not convert a valid card into sufficient permission when approval remains required; unknown approval stays UNKNOWN rather than an invented approval or denial.
3. **Converse and modality integrity.** B rejects “code received therefore confirmed” without a reviewed converse, and preserves optional **may** rather than compulsory **must**. A promised email is not evidence of successful delivery.
4. **Exception scope and nonautomatic permission.** C preserves the accompaniment exception and separate glasses requirement; an exception to one prohibition does not cancel all other conditions. Ambiguous or later exceptions require reviewed authority, scope and time.
5. **Combined conditions, boundaries and timing.** D distinguishes **at least** from **more than**, **and** from source-reviewed **or**, request eligibility from guaranteed seat, and future effective time from current availability; unknown timezone/form status blocks a definitive claim.
6. **Actually delivered accessible, chronological and idempotent evidence.** E reconciles authoritative versus rendered audio/ASR/captions/translations/screen readers/autocomplete, quarantines only affected constructs, orders first authorship before actual model exposure and deduplicates session-qualified events. Reading cannot certify unheard listening.
7. **Controlled, uncertain and reversible adaptation.** F compares feasible actually delivered options with matched rule complexity, policy completeness, register, modality, topic, help, review and follow-up. Sparse, contradictory or confounded outcomes yield no winner and balanced exploration; delayed regression lowers confidence.
8. **Delayed first-unaided transfer and nonexclusive interests.** Assisted rule copying, immediate repeats, same-template noun swaps and hints cannot earn delayed/unseen mastery. Require a later distinct eligible session, new source/recipient/context and rule structure, reviewed oracle, first-unaided recipient-correct communication and explicit missingness. Preserve shared objectives, receptive/productive coverage and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation.
9. **Honest support, efficacy and release boundary.** Documentation or green CI is not executable policy interpretation, outcome-driven instruction or evidence of improved human retention. Require exact-head deterministic executable assertions against real objectives, logged actually delivered policy changes and delayed independent transfer versus a balanced baseline, then ethically reviewed learner evidence before efficacy claims.
