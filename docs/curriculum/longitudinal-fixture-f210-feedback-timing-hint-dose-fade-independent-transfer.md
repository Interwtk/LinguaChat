# F210 — Feedback timing, hint dose, scaffold fading and independent transfer

**Status:** design-only, synthetic *cross-cutting* longitudinal curriculum acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until an independently reviewed executable exact-head policy/assessment harness demonstrates these behaviors, and human-learning benefit remains unproven without an ethically reviewed longitudinal study. Not a new CEFR can-do, a prescribed delay, a frustration threshold, a shipped feedback engine, a new graduation gate or authorization to open A1–C2. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F207 specifies diagnosis and targeted remediation; F208 tests metamorphic evidence invariants; F209 triages review obligations under constrained opportunities. **Distinct question in F210:** for an existing objective and a real opportunity to respond, can the tutor choose *when* and *how much* feedback to deliver, then **fade or restore** assistance using subsequent *unaided* retention/transfer rather than treating easy assisted repairs as learning? The tutor must respond to explicit help/accessibility needs without manufacturing “productive struggle” from elapsed time or withholding necessary access.

## Fixed objective, feedback opportunity and provenance

Bind each history to an **existing versioned CEFR can-do** and prerequisites, target language/variety, communicative intent, independently reviewed meaning-critical error rubric, objective/assessment versions, supported response modalities, accessibility accommodations, any existing meaningful-turn ceiling, and unchanged independent mastery/exit requirements. The example English utterances below are illustrative; they do not establish that any named level or skill is released. Preserve the unresolved B2 Arc 6 recognition-versus-open-retrieval blueprint/reuse-matrix boundary as **UNSUPPORTED** rather than silently deciding it.

Use a deterministic, session-qualified **feedback opportunity ledger**: learner pseudonym; session/run/item-family and event IDs; trusted occurrence order versus upload order; objective/rubric/policy versions; chosen task, burden, context family and difficulty; supported and actually rendered modality; whether the learner saw/heard the prompt; first unaided response and independent semantic score; error category/severity; explicit help request; accessibility constraints; optional measured latency with quality flag; feedback candidates and policy uncertainty; feedback chosen, actually rendered, visible answer-bearing content, feedback timestamp if trusted; retries and authorship; completed/abandoned status; and subsequent due/offered/rendered/completed delayed or unseen probes. **Unopened help is not exposure; rendered answer-bearing help is exposure even when the learner claims not to have used it.** Unknown exposure order stays UNKNOWN and cannot be promoted to clean evidence.

Distinguish feedback forms **without asserting a universal ranking**: neutral acknowledgement; non-answer-bearing attention cue; targeted error-location cue; process/metalinguistic cue; partial scaffold; worked example or direct answer; and an explicit request for clarification or accessibility support. A feedback label is insufficient: store the **actual delivered text/audio**, whether it reveals the target answer, and whether the later response was independently authored. A learner may decline a hint; the tutor must not secretly force answer exposure to boost completion.

A policy decision records the *same required objective*, currently eligible feedback alternatives, selection reason/confidence, learner request and accessibility priority, intended timing relative to the **observed response** (not an invented countdown), selected dose, actual delivery, next independent probe, and whether future scaffolding is to be faded, maintained or restored. No fixed wait duration, latency penalty, psychological state diagnosis, one-size-fits-all feedback order, or automatic method winner is permitted. If evidence is sparse, choose a safe balanced default and keep alternatives eligible.

Expected labels: FIRST_UNAIDED, SEMANTIC_ERROR, NO_ANSWER_YET, HELP_REQUESTED, ACCESS_NEED, FEEDBACK_PLANNED, FEEDBACK_RENDERED, ANSWER_EXPOSED, PROCESS_CUE_ONLY, ASSISTED_REPAIR, INDEPENDENT_RETRY_UNVERIFIED, CLEAN_LATER_PROBE, DELAYED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_TRANSFER, FADE_WITH_REASON, RESTORE_WITH_REASON, NO_FORCED_DELAY, NOT_COMPARABLE, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE, TOPIC_ROTATION_PRESERVED and UNSUPPORTED. Labels describe evidence and decisions, **never permanent learner types**.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — A hint that reveals the answer cannot create first-attempt mastery.** The fixed can-do requires an independently phrased polite request. A learner first writes “You give me the file” where the reviewed rubric identifies a pragmatic mismatch. The tutor offers a process cue (“Could you make the request less direct?”), which the learner does not open; the original first attempt remains the only scored unaided answer. In a matched branch, a full model (“Could you send me the file, please?”) is **rendered before** the learner's next correct, nearly copied response. The model branch records answer exposure and ASSISTED_REPAIR, never an independent success. A later genuinely new request, without answer-bearing material, may supply clean evidence. A cue that only locates an error is not *automatically* non-answer-bearing: the reviewed actual wording decides contamination.

**B — Escalate support for a repeated meaning-critical error, then re-test unaided.** Across distinct completed sessions, the learner repeatedly says “I have sent it” when the task requires communicating that the document **has not** been sent. The error is independently judged meaning-critical, and the first unaided attempts remain failures. A generic “try again” cue was actually delivered in earlier sessions without improving later independent responses. The tutor may revise its uncertain hypothesis, choose a more targeted contrast or worked example, and log **why** the feedback dose increased. The immediate correct repair after the model is assisted. Only a later completed, unfamiliar unaided message can justify fading support or improving independent mastery evidence. A single typo or an uncompleted attempt in a matched control must not trigger the same confident escalation.

**C — Fade a helpful scaffold without losing the right to restore it.** In matched opportunities for the same versioned can-do, earlier targeted cues were actually delivered and followed by multiple *completed* independent delayed and unfamiliar-context successes. The tutor records a reasoned next choice to try a lighter cue or no cue **after a first unaided attempt**, preserving help-on-request. In a later independent transfer probe the learner makes a meaning-critical error. The tutor must update uncertainty and may restore a suitable scaffold or change explanation style; it cannot freeze the learner as a “minimal-hints” type. A control with only immediate correct repetitions after the cue is insufficient evidence for the same fade claim. Neither branch changes the CEFR rubric or exit requirements.

**D — Help and access outrank artificial struggle.** A learner explicitly requests clarification because an instruction is ambiguous, while a second learner cannot access the audio and asks for an accessible route. An unsafe policy withholds help until a fixed timer expires, interprets silence as low ability, or displays an audio transcript and awards listening mastery. The expected tutor offers timely clarification or a genuinely supported accessible activity, records the help/exposure actually delivered, and preserves any unavailable listening obligation as UNSUPPORTED. It may still invite independent production *after* access is restored, but it cannot retroactively classify an answer exposed by clarification as unaided. Optional latency is never a standalone mastery gate, a diagnosis of frustration or a justification to ignore a help request.

**E — Immediate assisted gains can lose to delayed independent outcomes.** Two eligible feedback strategies (short process cue versus detailed worked example) are actually delivered on independently reviewed **matched** tasks with the same can-do, difficulty, topic familiarity, modality, opportunity and first-attempt baseline. The worked example produces more correct immediate repairs; later completed unfamiliar independent probes favor the short cue. The tutor must use the delayed independent evidence to reconsider which feedback dose to sample next, preserving uncertainty and continued exploration; it must not optimize for assisted completion or proclaim causal superiority from synthetic outcomes. A second control removes most later worked-example follow-ups: the resulting completer-only advantage is NOT_COMPARABLE, not evidence that missing learners failed or succeeded. Selection and follow-up denominators remain explicit.

**F — Topic rotation, replay and unknown exposure order must not corrupt feedback policy.** A learner prefers game examples, but the fixed request can-do appears across game, adjacent community, neutral workplace and unfamiliar travel contexts. A paired transformation changes **only** interest weights while keeping independently reviewed equivalent tasks and delivered feedback histories fixed. The instructional evidence, rubric, method uncertainty and required can-do remain invariant; context sampling may change, but unfamiliar and neutral contexts remain eligible and must appear longitudinally. The device later uploads duplicated feedback/retry events out of order, and one event has unverifiable whether the worked example was rendered before a purported independent probe. Deduplicate by session-qualified identity; classify that probe UNKNOWN/quarantined rather than clean, and never use upload order to manufacture unassisted transfer. A planned feedback choice that never rendered cannot count as a delivered method trial.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Shared standards and eligibility remain fixed.** Every history binds existing versioned CEFR can-dos, prerequisite/semantic rubrics, supported modalities, accessibility, due-review and independent mastery/exit rules. Feedback timing/dose and interests cannot lower or substitute these requirements; unresolved B2 boundaries stay UNSUPPORTED.
2. **Record actual opportunity, response and exposure.** Publish deterministic event IDs, exact seed/clock, first unaided semantic response, feedback candidates, planned versus actually rendered feedback text/audio, trusted occurrence chronology, help requests, retries and completed-run state. A recommendation or unopened hint is not delivered exposure; a rendered answer-bearing example is.
3. **Assisted repair never becomes independent mastery.** A preserves the first unaided score and classifies a correct answer following an exposed model as assisted, regardless of confidence or copying distance. A later clean, independently reviewed unseen probe is required for independent transfer credit.
4. **Feedback dose responds to recurrent independent evidence.** B compares repeated meaning-critical independent errors and failed previously delivered cues against a single typo/abandonment control; escalation is justified and reversible, not a fixed error-count rule or permanent learner label.
5. **Fading and restoration are evidence-based and reversible.** C can fade after comparable delayed/unseen independent successes and reconsider after contradictory clean evidence. The next actually delivered choice, alternative eligibility, uncertainty and reasons must be auditable. Short-term assisted repetitions alone cannot justify durable mastery.
6. **Accessibility and explicit help are honored.** D forbids forced waiting, latency-only penalties and withholding needed clarification; any answer exposure is tracked. Failed audio or unavailable speech cannot be counted as listening/speaking mastery, and an accessible reading fallback does not erase required coverage debt.
7. **Optimize later independent learning, not assisted completion.** E separates immediate repairs from delayed retrieval and unfamiliar independent transfer; compare methods only on matched tasks, actual delivery and comparable follow-up opportunities. Missing follow-ups remain missing, never scored as failure or success.
8. **Interest and event provenance remain independent.** F permits soft context changes without altering method evidence, objective or CEFR status; adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar contexts remain sampled. Replay, upload permutation, untrusted exposure order and planned-but-undelivered feedback cannot create clean observations.
9. **Implementation and efficacy claims require independent proof.** Publish machine-readable histories, expected stepwise feedback and next-policy decisions, exact objective/rubric/policy versions, exposure-contamination oracle, actual-delivery assertions, delayed/unseen outcomes, missingness and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED in an independently reviewed executable exact-head harness. Claim *human* improvement only after an ethically reviewed longitudinal comparison against a feasible balanced feedback baseline, with equivalent opportunities, independent delayed retention/transfer, attrition analysis and no hidden CEFR skill loss. Do not mutate Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase, spend on paid Claude, touch EvoLabs, auto-merge, change Pre-A1 or release A1–C2.

**Fixture verdict:** F210 makes **feedback timing, amount and subsequent fading/restoration** falsifiable at the point of *actual delivery* and later independent performance. A tutor may help generously and adapt intelligently without confusing assisted repairs with retained, transferable ability. **UNSUPPORTED** until executed and independently reviewed; synthetic correctness is not evidence of real learner benefit.
