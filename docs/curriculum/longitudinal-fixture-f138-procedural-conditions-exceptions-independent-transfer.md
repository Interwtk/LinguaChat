F33 guards genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish actually rendered assistance and chronology; F49 protects accessible delivery; F50 protects nonexclusive interests; F104 guards method/difficulty confounding; F107 guards unreliable tutor feedback; F109 guards multilingual evidence; F117 guards variety/register; F118 guards negation/quantifier scope; F119 guards warranted clarification; F127 guards conditional offers; F129 guards event order; F132 guards unsupported causal inferences; F133 guards referents. **Distinct construct:** can a learner independently **interpret, explain and communicate a multi-step procedure with prerequisites, branching conditions, exceptions, stopping rules and clarification points** to a new interlocutor without silently turning conditional permission into an unconditional instruction? This is language-mediated procedural reasoning and communication, not autonomous task execution, professional certification or general logical aptitude.

## Authoritative instruction-language and evidence contract

Every fictional task must resolve a **real versioned** CEFR descriptor/can-do, blueprint, validated prerequisites, protected skill obligations, target language and independently reviewed variety/register-specific procedural wording. Freeze a **versioned source-effective procedure** with author/authority *within the fictional scenario*, audience, effective time, numbered and unnumbered clauses, ordered steps, preconditions, if/then/else branches, exceptions, negation and quantifier scope, stop/ask rules, required information, authorized outcomes, and an explicit state-to-licensed-next-step **decision-table oracle**. Include task/rubric/evaluator version, first-unaided threshold, accommodations, due reviews, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer and exit gate. Simple one-step imperatives may precede conditional procedures; complex nesting, modal nuance and multiple exceptions require validated language prerequisites. A correct diagram copied from a model is not independent language mastery.

Freeze a **procedure-provenance ledger**: descriptor/blueprint/source-effective version/procedure/decision-table/task/rubric/evaluator hashes; independently reviewed language/variety/register and acceptable paraphrases; authoritative versus **actually delivered** text, audio, captions, subtitles, ASR/TTS, translations, diagrams, numbered-list structure, visual hierarchy, screen-reader order and truncation; condition state and source-effective timing; learner's first independently authored next-step explanation, targeted clarification, reordered plan or free response and submission time; answer-bearing branch highlights, models, hints, feedback and corrections with **actual exposure times**; immutable session-qualified event IDs and replay; feasible/assigned/**actually delivered** instruction; baseline, number/depth of branches, exception count, temporal/negation burden, language load, ambiguity, modality/accessibility, topic familiarity and difficulty; eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing delayed follow-ups and source/decision-structure overlap. Use fictional, low-stakes, non-personal situations. Do not present learner-generated procedures as instructions for medical, legal, financial, security or hazardous activities.

Task-local evidence flags: VERSIONED_PROCEDURE_BOUND, STEP_ORDER_PRESERVED, PRECONDITION_RECOGNIZED, CONDITIONAL_BRANCH_SELECTED, ELSE_BRANCH_PRESERVED, EXCEPTION_OVERRIDES_DEFAULT, UNLESS_SCOPE_PRESERVED, ONLY_IF_NOT_REVERSED, MUST_VS_MAY_PRESERVED, STOP_RULE_OBSERVED, UNMET_PREREQUISITE_NOT_BYPASSED, DEADLINE_VS_SEQUENCE_DISTINGUISHED, INSTRUCTION_CONFLICT_IDENTIFIED, SOURCE_AUTHORITY_UNRESOLVED, TARGETED_CLARIFICATION, UNSUPPORTED_NEXT_STEP, CONDITIONAL_TO_UNCONDITIONAL_DRIFT, TRANSLATION_BRANCH_DRIFT, CAPTION_NEGATION_LOSS, DIAGRAM_ORDER_CONFLICT, SCREEN_READER_ORDER_CONFLICT, FIRST_UNAIDED, ACTUALLY_RENDERED_HELP, ASSISTED, DELAYED_UNAIDED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_UNAIDED_TRANSFER, METHOD_BRANCH_DEPTH_CONFOUNDED, METHOD_LANGUAGE_LOAD_CONFOUNDED, METHOD_EXCEPTION_COUNT_CONFOUNDED, TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, NOT_APPLICABLE and UNSUPPORTED. ## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Prerequisite versus mere order.** Fictional library kit notice: **“Check the label first. Seal the envelope only if the label matches the record. If it does not match, leave it unsealed and ask the librarian.”** Condition state 1: label matches; permitted plan is check, then seal. Condition state 2: label does not match; permitted plan is check, leave unsealed, ask. Learner A says “Seal it first, then check” or “Seal it anyway because checking came first”; learner B independently preserves the prerequisite and branch. Counterfactual: replace **“only if”** with **“after”**; the text still constrains sequence but no longer explicitly makes matching a sealing prerequisite, so the oracle must not invent that condition. Counterfactual 2: replace “does not match” with “matches”; the branch wording becomes contradictory and requires source resolution, not an invented repair. This evaluates communicated instructions, not physical envelope handling.

**B — Exceptions overriding a default rule.** Fictional reading-club notice: **“Put returned books on the blue shelf, except damaged books: take those to the help desk. If the help desk is closed, leave a note and wait; do not place damaged books on the blue shelf.”** For an undamaged return, blue shelf is licensed; for damaged with desk open, help desk; for damaged with desk closed, note and wait. Learner A follows the default for every book; learner B independently applies the exception and stop rule. Counterfactual: remove the final prohibition but retain the exception; the exception still displaces the default for damaged books. Counterfactual 2: remove the exception entirely; the default now applies unless another valid rule restricts it. A translated “except” rendered as “including” makes the delivered instruction non-equivalent and the affected branch **UNSUPPORTED**.

**C — Nested branches, scope and conditional permission.** Fictional club-message rule: **“If the host confirms the room, send the invitation. Otherwise, suggest two new times. Do not send an invitation unless the room is confirmed.”** If confirmed, sending is permitted; if unconfirmed, suggesting times is required and sending is prohibited. Learner A treats “unless confirmed” as “even if unconfirmed” or interprets “if confirmed, send” as proof that an invitation was already sent; learner B reports the right hypothetical next step without claiming completion. Counterfactual: source changes to **“If the room is confirmed, you may send the invitation”**; permission no longer imposes the same obligation as “send.” A source with a missing confirmation state licenses a targeted question, not a guessed action. Branch truth conditions are judged only against the independently reviewed target-language oracle, not English word-order assumptions applied universally.

**D — Versions, authority, deadlines and conflicting instructions.** Fictional event sheet v1 says **“Before Friday, post the schedule once the coordinator approves it.”** A later v2 explicitly supersedes v1 and says **“After approval, post it by Thursday 17:00.”** Learner A mixes the v1 deadline with the v2 sequence or treats “before Friday” as “on Friday”; learner B identifies the effective version and preserves approval-before-posting and the Thursday deadline. Counterfactual: remove the supersession notice and supply both as apparently active instructions with incompatible deadlines; the correct response identifies the conflict and requests authoritative clarification rather than inventing precedence. A missing timezone or unspecified “Thursday” in a cross-timezone scenario must remain source-relative or be clarified. No real posting or scheduling action is performed.

**E — Accessible delivery, actual help exposure, authorship chronology and replay.** Authoritative fictional text says **“Do not send the invitation unless approved.”** The **delivered** caption drops “not”; ASR turns “unless” into “when”; a diagram visually reverses two arrows; a screen reader reads the exception after the action; or translation changes “may” to “must.” Freeze source-effective and actually delivered stimuli. When the condition or order is unavailable in an accessible delivered channel, quarantine only that affected judgment as **UNSUPPORTED**, not learner failure. A learner's original plan authored at 10:00 remains FIRST_UNAIDED when a tutor branch diagram was queued at 09:59 but first **rendered** at 10:01; a later answer copied from the diagram is ASSISTED. Duplicate immutable session-qualified event replay cannot create another first attempt, distinct session or delayed retrieval. Equivalent accessible formats must preserve branches, order, negation, exception and permission/obligation distinctions.

**F — Reversible teaching choices and unseen procedural transfer.** Fictional learner A completes a highlighted if/else exercise immediately but later ignores a new exception in an independent spoken explanation. After **actually delivered** EXCEPTION_HIGHLIGHT_THEN_FADE and CONDITION_TO_DECISION_TABLE on matched branch depth and linguistic complexity, A later independently explains a **new** community-club sign-up procedure with an unfamiliar exception to a different recipient. Fictional learner B instead improves delayed oral branch explanations after AUDIO_INSTRUCTIONS_TO_SPOKEN_RECAP and LEARNER_REPAIRS_FAULTY_PLAN on comparable procedures. Freeze baseline, descriptor, branch depth, exceptions, negation/modals, instruction length, topic familiarity, modality/accessibility, help exposure, review interval and follow-up opportunity. If A sees a single two-step imperative and B a five-level nested rule with three exceptions, mark METHOD_BRANCH_DEPTH_CONFOUNDED / METHOD_EXCEPTION_COUNT_CONFOUNDED / METHOD_LANGUAGE_LOAD_CONFOUNDED and infer **no method winner**. Replacing “books” with “tickets” while retaining an identical decision table is **not** genuinely unseen transfer; require a changed branch/exception relation, communicative purpose or recipient demand. Track eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-ups; missing is neither success nor failure. Repeated comparable delayed first-unaided outcomes may justify an **observed change in subsequently delivered instruction**, with uncertainty, continued alternative sampling and reversal after contrary evidence. Interests in travel, games or music can bias example probability but cannot exclude neutral, adjacent or unfamiliar contexts or CEFR obligations.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real CEFR, language and procedural-source linkage.** A–F resolve versioned descriptor/blueprint/prerequisites, independently reviewed target-language/variety/register procedural expressions, source-effective procedure and decision-table oracle, rubric/evaluator, first-unaided threshold, accommodations, protected skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Nested conditions require validated prerequisites.
2. **Preconditions, order and branch fidelity.** A/C distinguish prerequisite from mere temporal order, only-if from if, required from permitted steps, unconfirmed from confirmed states and proposed action from completed event. State counterfactuals update only their licensed next steps.
3. **Exceptions, negation and stopping rules.** B/C preserve default versus exception, if/else/unless scope, prohibitions and stop/ask requirements. A missing prerequisite cannot be bypassed, and an exception cannot be silently erased by a general rule.
4. **Version, authority and temporal meaning.** D binds effective version, approval sequence and deadline. Unresolved conflicting instructions or timezone ambiguity require targeted clarification or qualified explanation; the learner must not fabricate authority or dates.
5. **Communicative meaning and independently reviewed alternatives.** A–D accept semantically faithful target-language paraphrases and audience-appropriate reports, while rejecting unconditionalization, unsupported action claims and modality-strength drift. No assumption that all languages lexicalize procedural scope identically.
6. **Actual delivery, accessibility, authorship and replay.** E compares authoritative and **actually delivered** captions, audio, translation, diagrams, list order, screen-reader and ASR/TTS channels. Only answer-bearing help **actually exposed before** first authorship contaminates independence; queued but unrendered help does not. Replay is idempotent; non-equivalent channels quarantine affected constructs.
7. **Delayed first-unaided retrieval and genuinely unseen transfer.** A–F require distinct real learning opportunities, new branch/exception structures or recipient purposes, independent first explanation, warranted clarification or free production, and complete eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Cosmetic topic swaps do not establish transfer.
8. **Reversible adaptation, comparable difficulty and soft interests.** F records feasible/assigned/**actually delivered** methods, matched branch/exception/modal/language load, uncertainty, alternatives, contrary evidence and **observed change in later instruction**. Interest contexts remain probabilistic; protected skills, due reviews, CEFR objectives, neutral/unfamiliar contexts and exit gates remain mandatory.
9. **Exact-head reproducibility and bounded claims.** A–F emit deterministic seed/clock, named exact engine HEAD and runnable command, descriptor/source/procedure/decision-table/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, delivered accessible artifacts, first/help/event chronology, independently reviewed language oracle, only-if/after/exception/unless/may/must/version/deadline/caption/translation/replay counterfactuals, confounds, delayed/unseen denominators and **PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion**. Documentation-only CI cannot prove a shipped procedure-language evaluator, a reproducible runtime defect or improved human learning.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and reproducible command; real versioned CEFR-linked objectives; independently reviewed target-language/variety/register procedural oracles; source-effective versioned instructions and state-to-licensed-next-step decision tables; accessible authoritative and **actually delivered** stimuli; independent first authorship and actual answer-bearing help chronology; delayed first-unaided retrieval and genuinely unseen branch/exception/recipient transfer; comparable or explicitly confounded instructional methods; protected skill coverage, due reviews and exit obligations; complete eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; and per-assertion verdicts for prerequisites, condition scope, exceptions, negation, stop rules, permissions, version/authority, timing, accessibility, translation and replay. Ambiguous authority, inaccessible conditions, conflicting delivered channels and missing follow-ups remain **UNSUPPORTED/unknown**, not learner failure.

A stronger claim that adaptation **improves durable independent procedural communication** requires separately reviewed, privacy-appropriate, multilingual and accessibility-validated real-learner longitudinal comparisons with attrition/subgroup-harm analysis and reproducible delayed outcomes. **A strong tutor helps learners understand what to do when conditions change, explain the exceptions and independently ask for missing information rather than confidently inventing the next step.**
