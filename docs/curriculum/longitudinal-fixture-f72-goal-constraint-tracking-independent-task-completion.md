# F72 — Goal and constraint tracking must transfer to independent multi-turn task completion

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. Not a shipped dialogue planner, external-action agent, reproduced production defect, validated multilingual task-success evaluator, CEFR certification or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F23 separates topic familiarity from method effects; F33 protects genuinely unseen transfer; F38 guards tutor-origin contamination; F49 protects accessibility construct equivalence; F50 protects topic breadth; F53 preserves source-valid register; F54 tests clarification and repair; F58 tests learner initiative; F62 protects independent production; F66 varies interlocutors; F69 prioritizes errors; F71 tests referent and discourse cohesion. **F72 isolates goal-directed constraint management:** a fluent, coherent conversation can still fail its source-defined communicative purpose if the learner forgets a required condition, accepts an incompatible option or relies on the tutor to resolve every trade-off. Conversely, a concise or noncanonical but source-valid negotiation may satisfy the task without following a prescribed dialogue script. F72 does not require transactional success for can-dos whose source construct does not include it.

## Controlled setup

Resolve a **real, versioned** CEFR can-do, curriculum blueprint, prerequisites, communicative function and task outcome, relevant skill/modality and interaction format, source-required constraints and permitted negotiation, register/variety, evaluator/rubric, assistance limits, independent evidence, meaningful delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer, due reviews and level exit gate. Freeze the goal and constraints before comparing treatments. If a source task requires only a basic exchange, do not invent a multi-condition planning gate; if it requires negotiation, do not award completion for grammatical sentences that leave a mandatory condition unmet. Do not impose a universal number of constraints, turns, concessions, explicit confirmations or English-centric phrases.

Predeclare feasible reversible instructional candidates GOAL_STATE_MAP, CONSTRAINT_REPHRASE, TRADEOFF_ROLEPLAY, OPEN_TASK_SIMULATION, REFLECTIVE_PLAN_REVIEW and BALANCED_DEFAULT. They may vary scaffolding, explanation, modality mix where actually available, activity order and review spacing without changing the source-defined objective or independent standard. A practice checklist, tutor reminder or worked negotiation is **assistance**, not proof that the learner can manage the task unaided. Preserve balanced skill exposure, alternative methods and soft interest-context rotation rather than assigning a permanent planning or conversational learner type.

For comparable learner/objective histories, record source/evaluator versions; frozen task goal, mandatory/optional constraints and acceptable outcomes; initial versus genuinely changed conditions; task, topic and interlocutor familiarity; negotiation burden and feasible alternatives; selected, assigned and **actually rendered** method; exact prompt, roleplay state, tutor suggestion and feedback hashes; first unaided proposals, questions, confirmations, concessions and final outcome; learner- versus tutor-origin constraint mentions; hints, recaps, answer-bearing cues, retries and revisions with trusted delivery chronology; practice dose and opportunity; modality/accessibility; independent delayed and genuinely unseen goal transfer; and eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators. Use immutable session-qualified event IDs, source/state versions and trusted occurrence/delivery order rather than upload order. Do not infer spoken negotiation from text transcripts or treat unavailable external transactions as failed language learning.

Evidence labels: GOAL_STATED, MANDATORY_CONSTRAINT_RETAINED, CONSTRAINT_DROPPED, CONSTRAINT_CONFLICT_DETECTED, SOURCE_VALID_TRADEOFF, INCOMPATIBLE_OPTION_ACCEPTED, GOAL_MET_UNAIDED, GOAL_UNMET, FIRST_INDEPENDENT_NEGOTIATION, TUTOR_STATE_RESCUE, ANSWER_BEARING_CUE_DELIVERED, ASSISTED_COMPLETION, INDEPENDENT_DELAYED_RETRIEVAL, INDEPENDENT_UNSEEN_GOAL_TRANSFER, SCRIPT_EXPOSURE, TOPIC_METHOD_CONFOUNDED, ACCESSIBILITY_CONFOUNDED, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, STATE_VERSION_MISMATCH, MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE and UNSUPPORTED. These are event/evidence labels, never permanent learner traits.

## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Fluent exchange, unmet goal.** In a source-defined simulated appointment request, a learner politely produces grammatical turns and correctly tracks referents but accepts a slot outside a mandatory availability window. Another learner uses shorter, less polished turns after constraint-rephrasing practice, asks a relevant question and independently secures a source-valid option in a genuinely new simulated context days later. Score source-required communicative outcome and first unaided constraint handling separately from grammar, fluency, tutor assistance and final assisted polish. No real booking or external action is performed.
- **B — A new constraint changes the feasible answer.** During an unseen simulated purchase or travel inquiry, the interlocutor introduces a legitimate source-equivalent condition that invalidates the learner's first proposal. One learner repeats a memorized script and accepts the now-incompatible option; another identifies the conflict, seeks source-permitted clarification and negotiates a valid alternative without receiving the answer. Distinguish a real mid-task state change from an unannounced evaluator change. Do not demand impossible completion when no valid option exists: a source-appropriate explanation, refusal or next step may itself satisfy the task.
- **C — Tutor rescue can manufacture apparent success.** A tutor recaps all constraints and supplies the only compatible option; the learner repeats it and receives a polished success score. Another learner independently checks a missing condition and proposes a feasible alternative after a non-answer-bearing clarification allowed by the frozen rubric. Preserve first attempt, exact tutor cue, cue delivery time, clarification content, support provenance and outcome. A tutor-completed plan is assisted practice, not independent task mastery.
- **D — Instructional advantage reverses across objectives and time.** Early matched delayed probes suggest GOAL_STATE_MAP helps a learner handle multi-condition requests better than OPEN_TASK_SIMULATION. Later unseen roleplays show independent simulation practice is more effective for a different source-required negotiation, and still later evidence reverses the initial advantage. Emit uncertain objective-specific hypotheses, contradictory observations, deliberate exploration and a **changed actually rendered next choice**. Do not assign a fixed “visual planner” or “roleplay learner” category.
- **E — Familiar topics and access routes confound success.** Trade-off roleplays occur only in favourite gaming-store scenarios, while open simulations use unfamiliar public-service contexts; one accessible text route lacks oral turn-taking and another modality lacks reliable timing. Match or disclose goal complexity, constraint count/type, feasible alternatives, interlocutor pressure, topic familiarity, prior script exposure, dose, opportunity and accessibility. Rotate adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts; protect due reviews and all required language skills. Mark missing oral evidence MODALITY_UNAVAILABLE, never substitute text completion for speaking mastery.
- **F — Offline replay, stale task state and missing follow-up.** A goal-state card is assigned but never rendered; a tutor supplies the compatible option before a learner revision but its upload arrives later; two offline sessions reuse a local turn ID; a roleplay reuses a scenario ID after the required time window changes; a delayed unseen probe is offered but not completed; and an evaluator version changes which concession is acceptable. Rebuild using session-qualified IDs, rendered content/state hashes, trusted delivery order and versioned source semantics. Deduplicate replay, quarantine irreconcilable chronology/state changes, separate missing follow-up from failure and ensure upload-order permutations do not change independence, constraint attribution or next-choice traces.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Frozen source-defined task outcome.** For A–F emit the actual versioned can-do, blueprint, prerequisites, task purpose, mandatory versus optional constraints, permissible outcomes, interaction/modality, rubric, assistance policy, delayed/unseen probes, reviews and exit gate. Missing source mapping is UNSUPPORTED; neither a generic checklist nor an invented transactional requirement may replace the CEFR-linked construct.
2. **Outcome is not confused with surface fluency.** In A/B distinguish coherent or grammatical turns from independently retaining mandatory conditions, detecting incompatibility and reaching a source-valid communicative result. Emit the violated condition and state transition, not merely a vague task-success score. Do not require an external purchase or booking to demonstrate a simulated language can-do.
3. **Constraint changes and impossible goals are handled validly.** In B/F evaluate the goal against the frozen task and the actual, versioned interlocutor changes. Credit source-valid negotiation, clarification, justified refusal or appropriate next step when no compatible option exists; do not penalize the learner for failing an impossible hidden constraint or reward acceptance of an incompatible option.
4. **First unaided negotiation is separated from tutor rescue.** In A/C/F retain first independent proposal, constraint checks, actual tutor reminders/models/recaps, answer-bearing cue chronology and revised completion. Tutor-provided compatible options and copied plans are assisted; source-permitted non-answer-bearing clarification is judged against the real interaction rubric.
5. **Durable unseen goal transfer governs adaptation.** In A/D/F require meaningful intervening time/practice and independent delayed, genuinely unseen goals or changed feasible-option structures with source-equivalent burden. Emit eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators; immediate script repetition, tutor-led completion and missing probes cannot prove durable task competence.
6. **Comparisons control constraint burden and access.** In A/D/E match or disclose mandatory-condition complexity, changes, negotiation alternatives, interlocutor pressure, script exposure, topic familiarity, dose, task opportunity, modality and accessibility. Preserve required receptive/productive skills, due reviews and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar contexts; text-only success cannot stand in for unmeasured oral interaction.
7. **Rendered state, cue chronology and replay invariance.** In C/F emit selected/assigned/rendered method, exact scenario/goal/constraint versions and content hashes, immutable session-qualified event IDs, trusted tutor-cue delivery order and offline deduplication. Upload-order permutations must yield identical independent labels, constraint-error attribution, final task result and next-choice trace. Unresolved state or evaluator equivalence fails closed.
8. **Uncertain, reversible learner-specific policy.** In A/D/E/F emit feasible options, chosen and actually rendered instruction, objective-specific delayed outcomes, confidence/uncertainty, contrary evidence, exploration and next reversible choice. Sparse or confounded data trigger BALANCED_DEFAULT; no permanent learning-style classification and no interest-driven change to objectives or mastery thresholds.
9. **Assertion-level audit without efficacy inflation.** For A–F emit source/rubric and scenario-state IDs, task outcome/constraint trace, first and final independent/assisted evidence, actual cue exposure, delayed/unseen denominators, confounds, access/topic/review debt, replay equivalence, next-choice rationale and per-assertion PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED. Documentation QA does not prove executable goal tracking or improved real-learner outcomes.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command; deterministic seeded multi-session histories and controlled clock; real source-resolved CEFR interaction tasks and validated language-aware goal/constraint evaluators; versioned scenario states with feasible and impossible branches; first unaided and assisted outcome traces; trusted rendered cue/content chronology; matched or disclosed burden and context exposure; independent delayed and genuinely unseen simulated task transfer with follow-up denominators; protected skill, review and topic breadth; offline replay-order permutations; reversible decisions; and assertion-level verdicts. Missing source mappings, feasible-state truth, chronology, modality or follow-up remains UNSUPPORTED/unknown, never invented competence.

A claim that adaptive goal/constraint instruction **improves durable independent communication** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, sufficiently delayed unseen independent tasks, multilingual pragmatic evaluator validation, privacy/accessibility review, subgroup harm checks and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor prepares learners to accomplish the language task themselves under changing conditions, not merely to sound fluent while the tutor manages the goal.**
