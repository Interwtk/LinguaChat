## Six synthetic longitudinal histories

- **A — Declining is not agreeing.** In a source-authored optional community invitation, an organizer asks “Can you help on Friday?” A participant replies “Thanks for asking, but I can't do Friday.” Learner A tells the organizer the participant confirmed Friday because of the friendly opening. Learner B independently communicates a Friday refusal without inventing a reason or a future commitment. In a separate validated target-language register, a concise direct refusal must also be accepted when appropriate. A later genuinely unseen invitation changes the social roles and surface phrasing; scoring requires the recipient to receive the correct participation status, not keyword recognition.
- **B — Offering Monday does not confirm Monday.** A colleague says “Friday doesn't work; I could help Monday if that suits you.” The organizer has not replied. Learner A schedules Monday as confirmed. Learner B reports Friday declined and Monday **proposed**, then asks for confirmation if the task requires booking. In a later source-authored message the organizer explicitly accepts Monday; B updates the ledger only then. An unseen task changes the alternative from a time to a location or task role and tests independent status preservation.
- **C — Partial acceptance, conditions and revision.** A volunteer says “I can prepare the slides, but I can't present; I can send them if you share the final outline.” Learner A reports full acceptance of both jobs and unconditional delivery. Learner B distinguishes the accepted preparation task, declined presentation task and conditional delivery. A later correction withdraws the offer to prepare slides; B updates the recipient without treating earlier wording as irrevocable. An unseen work-neutral task tests the same construct with different verbs and a different recipient.
- **D — Register, directness and power-sensitive boundaries.** Two source-validated target-language dialogues differ: peers coordinate an optional activity, and a learner responds to a superior's request in a context with documented institutional constraints. Learner A is marked wrong solely for a respectful direct refusal, while another learner is praised for a fluent but ambiguous answer that implies consent. Learner B preserves the boundary in both tasks, choosing a validated form or bounded clarification without inventing legal authority or making a promise. If the authority context is unsafe, social relation unspecified, required audio inaccessible or the evaluator lacks variety validation, mark UNSUPPORTED/confounded rather than scoring personality or culture.
- **E — Polished coached replies versus delayed independent outcomes.** In matched early sessions, ROLE_AND_REGISTER_DIALOGUE yields polished immediate refusal phrases after a visible answer-bearing model; REQUEST_COMMITMENT_LEDGER yields slower but independent first attempts. On later separate sessions with genuinely unseen requests, new roles and varied target-language wording, the ledger condition yields better unaided separation of refusal, offer and agreement for this synthetic learner. Match or disclose baseline proficiency, task difficulty, social-role familiarity, feedback, practice dose, register, modality/accessibility and topic exposure. Prefer completed delayed unseen recipient outcomes over assisted fluency, retain method exploration, express uncertainty and record the **actually rendered** next choice; never label a permanent “ledger learner.”
- **F — Interest overfit, late model and replay conflict.** A learner handles boundaries in favorite gaming chats but miscommunicates an optional neighborhood event and unfamiliar volunteer coordination. A tutor's refusal template is rendered before the learner reply but its telemetry arrives afterward; a second template was selected yet never rendered. Two offline sessions reuse a local event ID, an organizer's acceptance is later retracted in a source-version correction, and an eligible delayed probe was offered but not completed. Deduplicate only true session-qualified replays, quarantine unresolved source/rubric conflicts, reconstruct trusted render chronology and preserve unknown follow-up, review debt, topic rotation, reversible adaptation and stable verdicts under receipt-order permutations.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real source and CEFR applicability.** A–F emit versioned CEFR can-do, blueprint/prerequisites, target language/variety, source-authored task/provenance, interlocutor and recipient roles, validated rubric/evaluator, assistance policy, threshold, required skills, reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Missing real mapping or unvalidated pragmatic norms yields NOT_APPLICABLE/UNSUPPORTED, never fabricated proficiency.
2. **Refusal and request scope.** A/C/D identify exactly which requested action is refused, accepted, conditionally accepted or still open. Gratitude, apology, fluency, friendly tone and an indirect phrase must not override the source-supported participation status. Accept validated direct and indirect refusals.
3. **Offer versus agreement and conditionality.** B/C/F distinguish a proposed alternative from an explicitly accepted one, preserve conditions and recipient authority, and apply verified revisions or withdrawals without retroactively inventing commitments. No agreement is inferred from silence.
4. **Recipient agency, register and safe scoring.** A/D/F preserve the recipient's actionable understanding and the speaker's boundary; evaluate pragmatic appropriateness only against validated target-language variety/register and context. Do not penalize a direct refusal, private-reason nondisclosure, disability-related communication differences or opting out of unsafe scenarios.
5. **First independent move and actual render chronology.** A/D/F score the first learner-authored recipient-facing refusal/status message against trusted render chronology. Answer-bearing tutor templates, highlighted ledgers or translations rendered before that move make the affected move assisted; selected-but-unrendered cues do not. Separate legitimate source-authored prompts from interlocutor coaching leaks.
6. **Delayed genuinely unseen independent transfer.** A–F require distinct real sessions, new source-authored requests, roles, offers and response states, first unaided recipient-facing outputs and observable recipient outcomes. Emit eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; copied models, assisted repairs and missing follow-up cannot prove durable mastery.
7. **Comparable methods, accessibility and soft interests.** D/E/F match or disclose task stakes, social roles, language/variety, pragmatic ambiguity, baseline ability, practice dose, feedback, modality/accessibility and preferred/adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic exposure. Preserve shared CEFR objectives, noncompensatory required skills, reviews and exit gates; missing captions or culturally disputed politeness judgments are not deficits.
8. **Reversible adaptation and receipt-order invariance.** E/F log feasible alternatives, assigned/selected/**actually rendered** method, later independent outcomes, uncertainty, confounds, exploration and next-choice rationale. Sparse/conflicted evidence falls back to BALANCED_DEFAULT. Session-qualified replay deduplication, frozen versions and trusted render chronology must produce identical assistance labels, commitment verdicts, review debt and next method across receipt permutations; quarantine conflicts.
9. **Assertion-level audit without efficacy inflation.** A–F emit exact source/task/rubric/evaluator IDs, frozen request/response/role/condition/version fields, first and final learner-authored messages, rendered cues, recipient outcomes, delayed/unseen denominators, confounds, replay permutations and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion. Documentation CI is not an executable evaluator PASS, CEFR award or evidence of real learning gains.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and command, deterministic seed/clock, source-resolved CEFR-linked negotiation tasks, validated target-language/variety pragmatic rubrics, frozen requests/roles/commitment states and revisions, trusted cue-render chronology, first unaided recipient-facing moves, observable recipient outcomes, completed delayed genuinely unseen transfer with denominators, comparable method/topic/modality/access exposure, protected skills/reviews, receipt-order permutations, reversible next-method traces and assertion-level verdicts. Missing valid source, safe accessible channel, independently completed follow-up, validated social context or trusted chronology remains UNSUPPORTED/unknown.

A claim that adaptation **improves durable independent refusal and negotiation communication** additionally requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence against a credible balanced comparator, delayed unseen recipient-facing tasks, multilingual/variety and accessibility validation, human adjudication, privacy/subgroup-harm review and an ethically reviewed pilot. **A strong tutor helps learners decline, propose alternatives and clarify commitments faithfully, while respecting both speakers' agency.**
