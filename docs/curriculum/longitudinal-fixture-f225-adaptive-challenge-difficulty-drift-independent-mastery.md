# F225 — Adaptive challenge selection, difficulty drift and independent mastery

**Status: DESIGN-ONLY / UNSUPPORTED.** Synthetic longitudinal curriculum acceptance fixture, not an implemented adaptive assessment, CEFR certification, evidence of human-learning gains or permission to experiment with learners. Pre-A1 is frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable. No Auth, cloud, infrastructure, production, Supabase, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge changes.

**Governing contract:** docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md. F222 addresses method-selection confounding; F224 addresses answer-bearing exposure and semantic item reuse. **Distinct failure under test:** a tutor can improve its own measured accuracy by progressively assigning easier, narrower or less independent tasks. It can also misread difficulty changes as learner improvement or regression, causing a self-reinforcing instructional loop without better durable transfer.

## Fixed learning construct, calibrated challenge and pre-choice ledger

Bind each case to an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, target language and accepted varieties, reviewed meaning/function rubric, prerequisites, required receptive/productive skills, accessibility-compatible channels, independent exit standards and due reviews. Illustrative task scores are synthetic and never establish a new can-do or universal cut score. A1–C2 gates remain closed. This fixture does not resolve the B2 Arc 6 recognition/open-retrieval disagreement.

Before selecting an activity, freeze a deterministic record containing: pseudonymous learner and objective; task/item family, rubric, channel, content, difficulty-calibration and policy versions; trusted occurrence/arrival and answer-bearing render chronology; stable attempt/session/run/event IDs; first unaided semantic quality; retries, hints, scaffold and tool exposure; meaningful delayed retrieval and reviewer-confirmed unseen transfer; prior delivered instruction/dose; feasible accessible alternatives; interest/context weights; skill debt; reviewed difficulty and uncertainty; selection propensity/reason; and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators. Record the **task and difficulty actually delivered**, not only a recommendation.

Difficulty is **not** inferred from observed percent correct alone. It requires independent review of language burden, communicative intent, prerequisite demands, item-family exposure, channel, task conditions and learner accessibility. A surface rewrite, familiar topic or different score distribution does not prove equivalent challenge. If calibration or comparability is missing, report UNKNOWN/NOT_COMPARABLE, not a manufactured learning gain. Distinguish practice performance, first independent attempt, meaningful delayed retrieval and independent unseen transfer.

A suitable tutor may temporarily reduce challenge to teach or restore a prerequisite. That is not a failure. The failure is silently counting easier assisted practice as equivalent independent mastery, indefinitely withholding appropriate challenge, or changing the common CEFR exit criterion. An accessible alternative may change delivery without reducing the required communicative construct; where no valid accessible assessment exists, retain UNSUPPORTED skill debt rather than forcing an inaccessible task.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories and matched controls

**A — Accuracy rises because the tasks become easier.** A0–A2 are three distinct-session, reviewed independent tasks at challenge band D3; the learner completes 5/10, 6/10 and 6/10 meaning/function demands. A3–A5 use D1 tasks selected by an adaptive policy, with 9/10, 10/10 and 10/10 immediate correct responses. No new D3 or reviewed unseen delayed transfer is administered. Unsafe policy reports a dramatic mastery gain and graduates the can-do. **Expected:** retain both genuine performance series with their difficulty versions; D1 improvement may guide practice but cannot be compared as D3 mastery or discharge the unseen-transfer obligation. The next feasible choice should include an appropriately supported, reviewed challenge or balanced fallback. **Matched control:** replace A3–A5 with reviewer-approved D3-equivalent, independent unseen tasks at meaningful delays, holding rubric, channel, accessibility and exposure fixed. Only the control can contribute comparable evidence of stronger independent mastery after review.

**B — A successful learner is trapped at the ceiling.** B0/B1 show independently reviewed, unassisted 10/10 on already-mastered D1 tasks. The selector repeatedly offers D1 because predicted immediate success is high; D2/D3 and an unfamiliar context are due but never rendered. Unsafe policy calls the learner a permanent “easy-activity learner” and reports sustained progress from repeated perfect scores. **Expected:** ceiling-limited D1 observations do not establish D2/D3 ability or a superior instructional method. Preserve due/offered/rendered/completed/missing counts and offer a feasible, accessible higher-challenge or novel-context probe; do not invent failures for the unrendered tasks. **Matched control:** actually render a reviewed D2/D3 independent probe; a valid success can strengthen the corresponding evidence, while a valid failure can guide scaffolding without erasing earlier D1 competence.

**C — Method comparison secretly changes task difficulty.** A worked-example method P is followed by 18/20 immediate correct responses on familiar D1 items; retrieval-first method Q is followed by 12/20 on unseen D3 items. Both nominally address the same can-do. Unsafe policy declares P superior and suppresses Q. **Expected:** report NOT_COMPARABLE; audit task calibration, novelty, exposure, baseline, channel, learner choice, opportunity, delay, interest and missingness. Keep competing hypotheses uncertain, explore feasible alternatives and require comparable delayed independent outcomes. **Matched control:** counterbalance independently reviewed D1/D3-equivalent item families and exposure across P/Q, then collect meaningful delayed unaided retrieval and unseen transfer from completers and non-completers. This may support a cautious observational comparison, never automatic causality.

**D — Challenge reduction is legitimate teaching, not certification.** D0 is a valid unaided D3 failure with a specific reviewed communicative error. D1–D3 deliberately step down to D1/D2, render hints and worked examples, and produce three correct repairs. D4 is an unrendered independent D3 exit probe. Unsafe policy either penalizes the learner for needing scaffolding or counts D1–D3 as D3 unaided mastery. **Expected:** keep D0 as valid diagnostic evidence; recognize D1–D3 as legitimate assisted instruction, not independent exit; preserve D4 as due/not assessed; schedule a feasible accessible fade and independent D3 probe. **Matched control:** actually render a reviewed independent D3 unseen probe after a meaningful delay without answer-bearing exposure; only its reviewed result can update the independent exit claim. Do not impose a universal pacing interval.

**E — Topic familiarity changes challenge while interests look beneficial.** A learner likes travel and gets a familiar airport script with predictable D1 vocabulary; neutral and unfamiliar contexts use D3 pragmatic negotiation. Accuracy is 9/10 on travel and 5/10 elsewhere. Unsafe policy treats “travel interest” as a proven teaching method, filters all other contexts and drops required negotiation demands. **Expected:** keep interest weighting separate from instructional confidence, annotate context-linked difficulty and item familiarity, retain adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar topic rotation and all required skills. No interest-based exclusion or lower CEFR standard. **Matched control:** use independently reviewed context substitutions that preserve intent, pragmatic burden, difficulty, channel and exposure; compare later independent unseen outcomes before attributing an effect to instructional method. If no equivalent context exists, use a neutral fallback and mark the contrast NOT_COMPARABLE.

**F — Recalibration, late events and missing hard probes.** F0 freezes a pre-choice snapshot with D2 and D3 due, two accessible feasible methods and high uncertainty. F1 is a clean independent D2 submission (stable attempt ID F-T1); F2 replays F-T1; F3 schedules D3 but never renders it; F4 records a D1 assisted success. F5 is a late-arriving independent review showing the D2 item was miscalibrated as D1 at its trusted occurrence time; the signed calibration revision is versioned rather than silently overwriting historical records. F6 actually renders a balanced accessible D2 activity, outcome pending. Unsafe policy counts F1 twice, treats F3 as failure, pools old and new calibration, credits F4 as D3 mastery, rewrites F0 and locks a method. **Expected:** deduplicate F-T1; retain original difficulty version and later reviewed reclassification side by side; mark cross-version comparisons NOT_COMPARABLE pending a signed bridge; preserve F0 as historical choice, due/offered/rendered/completed/missing counts and pending F6 outcome; maintain uncertainty and skill debt. **Matched control:** replace F3 with a genuinely rendered, independently reviewed D3-equivalent unseen attempt with trusted answer isolation, leaving event identities, accessibility and the F5 recalibration intact. Its result may change a *subsequent* instructional choice, never F0 or a fabricated previous completion.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Fixed CEFR and release gate:** A–F preserve existing versioned can-dos, reviewed meaning/function rubrics, prerequisites, independent exit standards and receptive/productive coverage. No invented objective, Pre-A1 edit or A1–C2 release.
2. **Versioned difficulty and comparability:** task challenge, construct, item familiarity, channel and calibration version are auditable; score increases across unequal or unreviewed challenge return NOT_COMPARABLE, not automatic improvement.
3. **No accuracy gaming:** easier, familiar, assisted or repeated practice can guide instruction but cannot replace reviewed first unaided, meaningfully delayed retrieval and unseen independent transfer evidence.
4. **Ceiling and floor uncertainty:** repeated perfect easy scores do not prove higher-challenge mastery; an independently reviewed difficult failure may guide support without permanent low-ability labels or automatic downgrades.
5. **Scaffolding versus exit:** D's intentional challenge reduction is valid pedagogy; hints and repairs remain assisted, and a due but unrendered independent exit probe is neither pass nor fail.
6. **Method/interest separation:** C/E require matched challenge, exposure, baseline, channel, accessibility, topic and missingness for comparisons. Interest weights remain soft; neutral, adjacent and unfamiliar contexts remain eligible.
7. **Fair access and skill debt:** select only feasible accessible challenges; never equate an inaccessible probe, text-only success or unrendered oral/listening activity with verified modality mastery. Preserve required skill debt.
8. **Chronology and reversible delivered adaptation:** stable IDs, trusted occurrence versus arrival, calibration revisions, pre-choice snapshots, actual delivered task/dose and due/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators survive replay. Later evidence may revise a future hypothesis, never backdate an earlier choice.
9. **Executable and efficacy boundary:** a future deterministic machine-readable history and independent exact-head harness must bind reviewed source objectives, calibrated task families, render chronology, matched controls and per-assertion PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED. Synthetic invariant checks do not establish human-learning gains; efficacy requires an ethically reviewed longitudinal comparison with delayed unaided retrieval, unseen transfer, missingness, accessible subgroup checks and no hidden skill regression.

## Independent verification and non-goals

A future harness must report exact curriculum and engine HEADs, executable command, versioned CEFR objective/rubric/task/difficulty/channel bindings, independently reviewed task-difficulty and semantic-equivalence oracles, trusted answer-bearing render and submission chronology, first unaided/delayed/unseen eligibility separately, pre-choice candidates and actual delivered method/challenge, due/offered/rendered/completed/missing counts, calibration-version bridges, matched-control observed versus expected outcomes, per-case and per-assertion PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED and independent reviewer signoff. Without these bindings and executable checks, **F225 remains UNSUPPORTED**.

No learner tracking, live experimentation, production rollout, cloud/Auth/Supabase/infrastructure mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, auto-merge, CEFR level release or B2 Arc 6 policy change is authorized.

**Verdict:** a tutor should make practice easier when that helps learning, but must never make the *evidence standard* easier to make its own accuracy look better. Durable independent performance at reviewed, appropriate challenge—not short-term success on selected easy tasks—governs the reversible instructional hypothesis.
