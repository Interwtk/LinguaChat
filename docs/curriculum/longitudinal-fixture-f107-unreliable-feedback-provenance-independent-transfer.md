# F107 — Unreliable interlocutor feedback, source-grounded correction and independent unseen transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD with versioned CEFR-linked source tasks, trusted interaction provenance and independently adjudicated longitudinal evidence. This is not shipped feedback adjudication, a verified production defect, a CEFR award, or proof of human learning gains. Pre-A1 frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 protects genuinely unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish selected/rendered help and tutor-origin error; F39 external instruction confounding; F52 collaborative practice versus independent evidence; F53 variety/register; F54 communicative repair; F74 source attribution; F89 correction fidelity; F103 critical-detail read-back; F104 confound-aware instructional choice; F105 autonomous help seeking; F106 temporal regression/recovery. **Distinct construct:** when a conversation partner, peer, automated transcript or even a tutor gives **incorrect, ambiguous, socially motivated or inconsistent feedback**, can LinguaChat avoid treating that feedback as ground truth, protect valid independent learner evidence, and teach **source-grounded, respectful verification and correction** that later transfers unaided? This fixture tests the *epistemic reliability and authority of feedback*, not merely whether the learner accepts or rejects a correction. It does not authorize indiscriminate distrust of real interlocutors.

## Source, authority, interaction and adaptation contract

Resolve the actual versioned CEFR descriptor/can-do, level blueprint/prerequisites, source-authored communicative task, permitted answers and ambiguities, target language/variety/register, speaker roles, authoritative source facts, partner knowledge/authority limits, rubric/evaluator version, accessible channel, first-unaided threshold, assistance rules, protected listening/reading/writing/speaking/free-production requirements as applicable, due reviews, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer and exit gate. Predeclare which feedback can legitimately update a task fact (e.g. an authorized organizer announcing a new time), which is merely a speaker's belief, which is an automated hypothesis, and which is **answer-bearing tutor scaffolding**. If authority or ground truth cannot be established from the frozen task, record DISPUTED/UNKNOWN/UNSUPPORTED, not a learner error or a fabricated correct answer.

Keep an immutable, session-qualified event trace of learner-authored first attempt, interlocutor statement, source fact/version, actual speaker identity, claimed authority, feedback origin and confidence if available, accessibility/transcription quality, evaluator/rubric, task difficulty, topic familiarity, delivered versus selected tutor cues, repair chronology, follow-up opportunity and final recipient-relevant outcome. An ASR transcript is an observation of speech, **not** automatically the speech itself; preserve raw-audio evidence only when privacy and consent allow and provide source-authorized non-audio alternatives when needed. Never demand personal disclosures or privileged records to resolve a classroom scenario.

Possible **task-bounded** labels: SOURCE_VERIFIED, AUTHORIZED_FACT_UPDATE, PARTNER_BELIEF_ONLY, PARTNER_FEEDBACK_INCORRECT, PARTNER_FEEDBACK_AMBIGUOUS, AUTOMATED_TRANSCRIPT_UNCERTAIN, TUTOR_CUE_RENDERED, FIRST_UNAIDED_SOURCE_VALID, FIRST_UNAIDED_SOURCE_INVALID, LEGITIMATE_CLARIFICATION, RESPECTFUL_EVIDENCE_BASED_REPAIR, UNJUSTIFIED_ACQUIESCENCE, UNJUSTIFIED_DISMISSAL, SOCIAL_POLITENESS_WITHOUT_FACTUAL_CONCESSION, FACT_CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED, DELAYED_UNAIDED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_UNAIDED_TRANSFER, FEEDBACK_PROVENANCE_MISSING, SOURCE_VERSION_CONFLICT, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, REPLAY_DEDUPED, BALANCED_DEFAULT, NOT_APPLICABLE and UNSUPPORTED. These are evidence labels, not traits, diagnoses, permanent skepticism levels or fixed learning styles.

Eligible **reversible** instructional choices may include BALANCED_DEFAULT, SOURCE_VS_OPINION_CONTRAST, POLITE_VERIFICATION_MODEL_THEN_FADE, SPEAKER_AUTHORITY_MAPPING, TRANSCRIPT_UNCERTAINTY_REPAIR, INDEPENDENT_FACT_CHECK_PROBE and SOURCE_VALID_OTHER when actually supported. Adjust explanation, supported modality mix, scaffold dose, ordering, pacing, examples and review spacing using longitudinal **later independent** results, not short-term agreement with a tutor. Sample feasible alternatives, record uncertainty, protect CEFR objectives and noncompensatory evidence, and rotate preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar topics. Do not teach learners that every disagreement is a linguistic mistake or that disagreeing is always the desired answer.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Confident peer correction is wrong.** A source-authored invitation states that the meeting is *proposed* for Tuesday, not confirmed. A learner accurately conveys the provisional status unaided; a peer confidently insists it is confirmed. Another learner adopts the peer's assertion without checking; a third politely says the invitation only proposes Tuesday and asks the authorized organizer. In a matched world the organizer **really confirms Tuesday** after the original invitation: accepting that authorized update is now correct. Judge the utterance against source/version and speaker authority at response time, not the peer's confidence, politeness or the expected phrase.

**B — Automated transcript contradicts actual speech.** An accessible, consented listening task contains the spoken distinction between “fifteen” and “fifty” in a source-valid context. ASR renders the wrong numeral with high confidence, and an automated partner marks the learner's accurate unaided read-back incorrect. A second learner really mishears the numeral. A matched task has no reliable recording or accessible alternative. Treat ASR as uncertain evidence; use permitted independent adjudication rather than silently converting a transcript mismatch into a learner error. If original speech is unavailable, mark the listening verdict UNSUPPORTED rather than awarding either success or failure. Do not use this task to force inaccessible audio.

**C — Social agreement versus semantic concession.** In a group-planning exchange, one participant mistakenly says that everybody agreed to Friday; the authoritative log records agreement from only one participant. A learner replies politely (“I see why it sounded that way, but only Maya confirmed”) without accepting the false claim. Another says “Sure” as a conversational backchannel and then explicitly corrects the attendance claim; a third confirms all participants without evidence. Disambiguate discourse markers from factual commitments using full source-valid context, language variety/register and recipient outcome. In a matched world all participants subsequently confirm Friday, so the correct interpretation changes.

**D — Tutor-origin correction must not contaminate mastery.** The tutor displays a wrong worked answer before a learner responds; another tutor cue is assigned but never rendered. In one history the learner repeats the wrong displayed answer, while in another the learner independently detects the conflict with the source before any cue. A legitimate partner's reply to a source-authorized clarification is task stimulus, not automatically a tutor hint. Reconstruct trusted **rendered** cue/response chronology even when telemetry arrives out of order. Never attribute a tutor-injected error entirely to the learner, count coached correction as first-unaided mastery, or ignore a truly independent contradiction.

**E — Feedback-source overfit and delayed unseen transfer.** After several sessions, SOURCE_VS_OPINION_CONTRAST yields high immediate accuracy on favorite-game scheduling tasks. At a later independently adjudicated neutral health-service *fictional appointment* task and an unfamiliar community notice, the learner still follows a misleading unofficial comment instead of a versioned official update. Another learner verifies sources unaided across preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts and different recipient roles. Compare difficulty, variety/register, assistance, task stakes and topic familiarity. Do not conclude that one instructional method caused transfer or that the learner “learns by reading”; revise a capability-local hypothesis and preserve balanced skills, due reviews and exploration.

**F — Offline replay, source correction and conflicting authority.** Two genuine offline sessions reuse a local event ID; one packet is a true replay; a partner message arrives late; a task-source correction changes the official deadline; an evaluator migration changes an accepted regional form. A learner first correctly rejects an unsupported deadline, then later correctly accepts the versioned authorized correction. Another learner blindly repeats an obsolete deadline. Deduplicate only session-qualified identical replays, reconstruct trusted event-time source and cue order, quarantine irreconcilable source/rubric versions, and distinguish a real changed fact from a changed score. Reordering packet receipt must not alter first-unaided status, correct task-world meaning, due-review debt, next-method rationale or uncertainty. An unresolved authority conflict must remain UNKNOWN/UNSUPPORTED.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Real source, authority and CEFR applicability.** A–F require versioned CEFR can-do, level blueprint/prerequisites, source-authored task and answer space, speaker roles and authority, language/variety/register, rubric/evaluator, accessibility, independence rules, protected skills, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. A scenario with no adjudicable feedback conflict is NOT_APPLICABLE; missing source or authority is UNSUPPORTED, never a made-up correct answer.

2. **Ground truth is not feedback popularity.** A/B/C/F adjudicate source facts and authorized updates at the learner's response time. Peer confidence, number of agreeing participants, ASR confidence, social status and tutor fluency do not override versioned task truth. A genuine authorized update **must** be recognized; blanket distrust fails.

3. **Communicative pragmatics and legitimate uncertainty.** A/C distinguish politeness, backchannels, genuine assent, justified challenge, evidence-based repair and appropriate clarification without imposing one culture's preferred directness. A learner may appropriately say “I don't know yet” where facts are unresolved. An unauthorized contradiction is not automatically virtuous; source-valid agreement is not automatically gullibility.

4. **Independent evidence and feedback chronology.** B/D/F separate learner first-unaided response from tutor-provided examples, retries, ASR artifacts and source-authorized interlocutor stimuli. Only **actually rendered** answer-bearing tutor cues before the assessed response contaminate independence; assigned-but-unrendered cues do not. Late packet receipt cannot retroactively erase trusted event-time exposure or promote coached success to mastery.

5. **Source correction, rubric drift and access.** B/F retain source/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, valid correction effective times, transcription uncertainty and accessible delivery. Conflicting versions fail closed pending adjudication; a rubric or ASR error is not learner regression. Do not award listening/speaking evidence from text-only proxies unless the real CEFR-linked task authorizes equivalent evidence.

6. **Reversible, uncertainty-aware instructional adaptation.** D/E/F log feasible candidates, selected/assigned/rendered method, baseline independent evidence, feedback reliability, observed recurring error, decision confidence, safe fallback, alternative sampling and next-action rationale. Weak or confounded evidence selects BALANCED_DEFAULT or an independent diagnostic probe; later contradictory unaided evidence can reverse the instructional hypothesis without assigning fixed learner types.

7. **Delayed independent transfer and noncompensatory breadth.** A–F require distinct time-separated sessions, meaningful intervening material, new source-authored surface/context and independently adjudicated first-unaided verification/repair with a recipient-relevant outcome. Repeated prompts, coached agreement, same-session correction and absent follow-up do not establish retention. Preserve required receptive/productive skills, reviews, prerequisites and exit gates; report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators.

8. **Soft interests, fair comparators and event invariance.** E/F rotate preferred, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts; hold or disclose task difficulty, practice dose, partner authority, language variety/register, modality/accessibility, tutor exposure and source version. No interest category is an exclusion filter. Replays, reordered network receipt and evaluator migrations must not change independent evidence, feedback authority, review debt or next-choice rationale without a genuine event/source change.

9. **Assertion-level audit and bounded claims.** A–F emit source/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, speaker/authority and feedback provenance, frozen task-world timeline, learner first attempt, rendered cue chronology, adjudicated source-valid outcome, missingness, later unseen transfer, skill/review coverage, candidate/selected method, uncertainty, receipt permutations and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED for **each** assertion. Documentation CI or synthetic PASS cannot establish shipped feedback reliability, causal learning gains, CEFR mastery, a production defect or human robustness to misinformation.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD and reproducible command, frozen real CEFR-linked source tasks and source-authorized updates, multilingual/variety-aware human adjudication, trustworthy speaker/source/cue chronology, consent-respecting accessible media where applicable, actual first-unaided and assisted event separation, time-separated delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen independent transfer, valid task/method/topic comparisons, protected skill/review/exit requirements, follow-up denominators, replay/version permutations and assertion-level verdicts. Unresolvable authority, absent raw evidence for disputed ASR, inaccessible delivery, source/rubric conflict or missing independent follow-up remain UNSUPPORTED/unknown.

A stronger claim that adapting to feedback reliability **improves durable independent learning** requires separately reviewed real-learner longitudinal evidence with a credible balanced comparator or ethically reviewed experiment, independent adjudication, multilingual/variety/accessibility checks, attrition and subgroup-harm analysis, privacy safeguards and monitoring. **A strong tutor teaches learners to verify claims against reliable evidence, communicate disagreement respectfully and update when trustworthy information genuinely changes—without confusing a partner's confident mistake with the learner's lack of ability.**
