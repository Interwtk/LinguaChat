These relations often co-occur but are not equivalent. Unknown is not false. A room may exist but be unavailable; an item may be available without its exact location being known; possession does not prove legal ownership. “There are no rooms available” does not establish that the building has no rooms. A missing inventory entry does not prove nonexistence unless the source is explicitly complete for that time/domain.

English “there is no one at reception” may be natural where another language chooses a different existential, negative or location structure. ## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Existence is not availability.** A reviewed community-center register lists three meeting rooms, all reserved until 17:00. Learner A tells a visitor “There are no meeting rooms in the center”; learner B independently says “There are three rooms, but none is available before 17:00.” Counterfactual: a reviewed complete architectural inventory establishing zero rooms would license the nonexistence claim. An incomplete public booking page listing zero free slots proves neither zero rooms nor permanent unavailability. A cancellation at 16:00 changes availability only when the updated source is authoritative for that time. The recipient may reasonably ask about a later slot.

**B — Location, custody and ownership diverge.** A reviewed equipment handoff says that a projector belongs to the library, Maya borrowed it and placed it in room 204 at 09:00. Learner A reports “Maya owns the projector and it is available in the library”; learner B says “The library owns it; Maya has borrowed it, and it was last recorded in room 204 at 09:00. I would check whether it is free now.” Counterfactual: a later verified return to the library changes custody/location but not ownership. The fact that a device exists somewhere does not authorize a recipient to take or use it.

**C — Negative scope, quantity and complete domains.** A verified appointment desk says “No interpreters are available this afternoon,” while two interpreters are employed but on leave. Learner A reports “There are no interpreters at this hospital”; learner B independently states “The hospital has interpreters, but none can take an appointment this afternoon.” Counterfactual: “not all three rooms are available” does not entail that none is available; “no room on floor two” does not rule out rooms elsewhere. If the inventory is incomplete, an absent entry remains UNKNOWN. Countability, classifier choice and target-language negation require independent review, not English word-order copying.

**D — Discourse referents, animacy and natural target-language realization.** A recipient asks “Is there a nurse on duty, and where is that nurse?” A reviewed roster shows one on-duty nurse at the west desk. Learner A responds with a bare locative that presupposes an unidentified referent, or applies an inanimate existential template indiscriminately to a person in Japanese. Learner B first establishes that a nurse is on duty and then identifies the west desk, using a reviewed natural target-language/variety/register construction. Counterfactual: after the nurse has been introduced in common ground, a concise locative answer may be fully appropriate. A natural Spanish or Japanese paraphrase satisfying the same information gap earns credit without mirroring English there is.

**E — Actually rendered channels, authorship and replay.** Authoritative audio says “There are **no** accessible rooms available **today**,” while ASR omits “no,” captions drop “today,” translation changes “available” to “located,” or a map auto-highlights the room before the first response. Reconcile authoritative and **actually delivered** evidence; quarantine only the affected relation/negation/time or listening construct. Accessible text may show reading comprehension, not unheard listening. At 10:00 the learner independently asks “Do you mean none exists, or none is free today?” A model queued at 09:59 but first rendered at 10:01 does not retroactively assist that move; an edited response after model exposure is ASSISTED. Screen-reader ordering, autocomplete and suggested replies must be treated as exposure if they reveal the relation. Replay of session-qualified IDs cannot create a second independent trial or a new delayed session.

**F — Longitudinal method comparison, reversible selection and genuinely unseen transfer.** Fictional learner A initially succeeds after RELATION_AND_TIME_GRID_THEN_FADE but later says that a fully booked service does not exist. Comparable actually delivered INFORMATION_GAP_LOCATION_DIALOGUE trials precede a later unaided exchange about finding an available wheelchair at a different site, with a new recipient, source, domain and purpose. Learner B improves delayed first-unaided relation explanations after EXISTENCE_LOCATION_AVAILABILITY_CONTRAST_PAIRS, then regresses when location is unknown; confidence decreases and balanced alternatives resume. If the grid method always receives complete inventories and familiar vocabulary while dialogue receives partial records, ambiguous sources or a different register, mark the relevant confounds and infer **no winner**. Replacing room numbers in the same booking template is not unseen transfer. Track eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing separately; missing is not incorrect. Only repeated comparable delayed independent outcomes can justify a logged change in **actually delivered** instruction. A learner interested in gaming may see game-server availability examples more often, but transport, healthcare, workplace, community, adjacent, neutral and unfamiliar contexts, and all required objectives, remain reachable.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Existing CEFR-linked objective and reviewed relation oracle.** A–F bind real versioned descriptors/can-dos, prerequisites, recipient/purpose, target language/variety/register, reviewed source-world relations and temporal scope, accessible stimuli, evaluator/rubric, first-unaided threshold, due reviews, delayed/unseen probes and unchanged exit gate. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED, never invented CEFR certification.
2. **Existence versus purpose-specific availability.** A preserves the existence of three rooms while denying availability for a stated interval, handles updates only with source provenance, and refuses to infer nonexistence from an empty booking page.
3. **Location, custody and ownership.** B distinguishes the last known location, current custody, legal ownership and permission to use; it never upgrades a timestamped observation to a timeless location or a borrower into an owner.
4. **Negative/quantifier scope and source completeness.** C distinguishes “none available” from “none exists,” “not all” from “none,” and floor/time/domain restrictions from global claims; unknown inventory entries do not become negative evidence.
5. **Discourse and independently reviewed language-specific naturalness.** D credits recipient-appropriate introduction and later reference, animacy/number/politeness/register, natural paraphrases and justified clarification, without projecting English existential syntax into Spanish, Japanese or other languages.
6. **Rendered, accessible, idempotent evidence.** E checks actual ASR/TTS/captions/translation/visual/screen-reader/autocomplete exposure, quarantines only affected constructs, orders learner authorship against **rendered** help, and deduplicates session-qualified events. Reading does not certify unheard listening.
7. **Uncertain reversible adaptation under controlled confounds.** F compares feasible accessible delivered methods under matched source completeness, temporal scope, lexical burden, variety, register, modality, topic, prior assistance, interval and follow-up. Sparse, contradictory or confounded evidence yields no winner and a balanced fallback; later regression reduces confidence.
8. **Delayed first-unaided transfer and nonexclusive interests.** Assisted model copying, highlighted room maps, same-session drills and room-number-only swaps cannot earn delayed/unseen mastery. Require distinct eligible later sessions, new source/domain/recipient/purpose, reviewed oracle, first-unaided recipient-correct production and explicit missingness. Preserve skill-family balance and neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar topic rotation.
9. **Honest support and release boundary.** Documentation/CI success alone is not executable engine support, demonstrated outcome-driven teaching changes or improved human retention. Require exact-head executable assertions against real objectives, longitudinal delayed/transfer comparisons against a balanced baseline and later ethically reviewed learner evidence before efficacy claims.
