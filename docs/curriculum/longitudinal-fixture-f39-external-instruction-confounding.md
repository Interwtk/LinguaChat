# F39 — Outside instruction must not be misattributed to LinguaChat's teaching method

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD. This is not a reproduced defect, shipped adaptive tutor, causal estimate or evidence of improved human learning. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 remain unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F32 separates selective follow-up from outcome quality; F37 separates assigned from actually delivered instruction; F38 isolates tutor-origin content errors. F39 isolates a different source of misleading longitudinal attribution: **learners may study the same objective with teachers, friends, other apps, books or independent practice between LinguaChat lessons and delayed probes**. A valid independent answer is evidence about current learner ability, but does not by itself establish that LinguaChat's selected teaching method caused the improvement. Equally, outside practice must not erase legitimate mastery evidence or become a reason to deny instruction.

## Controlled setup

Resolve `goal-1` to an **existing versioned CEFR can-do**, real supported skill route, prerequisite, rubric and independent mastery/exit gate. Compare eligible LinguaChat methods A (`concise_explanation`) and B (`worked_example`) against a feasible shared balanced baseline. Keep objective, task demand, independent scoring, item novelty, due reviews, access constraints and neutral/adjacent/unfamiliar topic opportunities comparable. Use a deterministic clock, seed, versioned `outside_learning_attribution_policy` and immutable synthetic event ledger spanning multiple real sessions and learning days.

Record learner, objective/version, skill, session, method assignment and **verified actual delivery**, activity and item-family IDs, original independent first attempts, help/answer exposure, follow-up eligibility and completion, calibrated delayed retrieval and unseen-context transfer, elapsed time, intervening in-app practice, review debt, scoring/rubric version, and event versus upload time. Where voluntarily reported or otherwise legitimately observed, additionally record **outside-learning source category**, relevant objective/skill, claimed versus verified exposure, approximate occurrence interval and duration *only if available and reliable*, overlap with the tested construct, report provenance, and uncertainty/missingness. Do not infer external lessons from fast improvement, collect sensitive teacher identities, demand personal diaries, track learners outside the product or invent hours of practice. A missing report means `EXTERNAL_EXPOSURE_UNKNOWN`, not `NONE`.

Predeclare `EXTERNAL_VERIFIED_RELEVANT`, `EXTERNAL_SELF_REPORTED_RELEVANT`, `EXTERNAL_IRRELEVANT`, `EXTERNAL_EXPOSURE_UNKNOWN`, `TIMING_UNRESOLVED`, `INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE_VALID`, `METHOD_ATTRIBUTION_CONFOUNDED` and `NOT_COMPARABLE`. Preserve separate questions: (1) **Can the learner now perform the can-do independently?** (2) **What evidence supports attributing improvement to a LinguaChat instructional choice?** Outside instruction can affect the second answer without invalidating the first.

## Six synthetic histories

- **A — Outside tutoring between lesson and delayed probe.** A is verified delivered. A learner then receives documented relevant one-to-one tutoring before a clean, delayed unfamiliar independent probe and improves. Preserve the valid learner outcome and CEFR evidence, but flag method attribution as confounded; do not claim A caused the gain or fabricate how the learner would have performed without tutoring.
- **B — Self-reported practice with uncertain timing.** After B, a learner casually reports using another language app and watching videos, but the objective overlap, timing and dose are unknown. Preserve the report as lower-certainty provenance; do not turn it into verified exposure, infer no exposure, discard a clean probe or penalize the learner. Method ranking remains uncertain where material.
- **C — External activity unrelated to the objective.** After A, a learner reports a verified music-listening activity unrelated to the assessed written request can-do. The later independently scored writing probe is valid. Record the outside activity and its construct mismatch; do not mechanically quarantine all evidence or automatically infer it improved writing. Comparable outcomes can inform a provisional hypothesis, with ordinary residual-confounding caveats.
- **D — Unequal outside instruction reverses naive ranking.** Learners or repeated occasions assigned A receive substantially more relevant outside tutoring than those assigned B; raw completed-only delayed scores favor A. Under matched objective, baseline readiness, probe opportunity and observed outside exposure, the apparent advantage is unsupported or reverses. Report full eligible/assigned/delivered/followed-up denominators, observed confounder imbalance and sensitivity to unmeasured exposure; never declare a causal winner from a naive aggregate or a completed-only subset.
- **E — Valid mastery with attribution withheld and later revision.** An externally supported learner passes calibrated unaided delayed retrieval and novel-context transfer under the frozen rubric, while evidence is insufficient to credit A. Retain legitimate independent mastery if all shared gates are met; with later comparable, verified A/B delivery and independently assessed outcomes under less-confounded conditions, allow an uncertain objective-specific method hypothesis to change. Do not force redundant remediation merely to make product attribution cleaner, or freeze a learning-style label.
- **F — Offline, duplicate and post-probe external exposure.** An outside-lesson report describing an occurrence before the probe uploads afterward; one report is replayed; two reports conflict on timing; a separate verified lesson occurs only after a completed independent probe. Deduplicate stable IDs, retain both occurrence and report/upload timestamps, and quarantine unresolved chronology. The post-probe lesson cannot explain the earlier probe; unknown timing must not be silently sorted by upload order.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Two distinct evidence tracks.** For A–F report objective/version, rubric, supported skill, assigned versus verified delivered method, eligible/completed follow-up denominators, first attempts, assistance, actual delayed retrieval, unseen transfer, review obligations, outside-exposure provenance, confidence, missingness and separate learner-mastery versus method-attribution classifications.
2. **External tutoring blocks unjustified method credit, not learner achievement.** In A, independently valid delayed unfamiliar performance remains a learner observation. Relevant verified tutoring between instruction and probe yields `METHOD_ATTRIBUTION_CONFOUNDED` unless defensible comparison isolates effects; do not silently credit A or erase the probe.
3. **Unverified outside practice remains uncertain.** In B, voluntary self-report is not verified instruction; absent or ambiguous reports are not evidence of zero exposure. Preserve usable learner evidence, mark unresolved method attribution, and avoid fabricated practice dose or timing.
4. **Relevance and temporal order matter.** In C, an unrelated external activity is not automatically a confounder for every can-do. Document construct overlap and uncertainty; preserve comparable clean outcomes without asserting that unrelated activity caused or could not possibly influence them.
5. **No naive winner under exposure imbalance.** In D, show outside-exposure, readiness, assignment/delivery, completion and follow-up imbalance with opportunity denominators and sensitivity analysis. Completed-only correlations or unadjusted averages cannot establish A's superiority; no fabricated counterfactual outcomes.
6. **Mastery and adaptation are independently revisable.** In E, retain valid CEFR mastery when its independent evidence gates are satisfied even if instructional attribution is `UNSUPPORTED`. Update only objective/skill-specific teaching hypotheses from later sufficiently comparable outcomes, with uncertainty, nonzero alternative sampling and balanced review coverage.
7. **Replay-safe chronology and source quality.** In F, stable-ID deduplication and trusted occurrence-time ordering preserve first-attempt, exposure and probe lineage across upload orders. Contradictory times remain `TIMING_UNRESOLVED`; exposure after the probe never explains earlier performance.
8. **Privacy, CEFR, availability and interests remain independent.** No compulsory surveillance or sensitive outside-teacher identification. Every history keeps unchanged can-do, prerequisites, calibrated rubric, due reviews, supported receptive/productive obligations and independent exit gates. Interest-weighted topics cannot become exclusive; neutral, adjacent and unfamiliar contexts persist. Unsupported listening/speaking remain `UNAVAILABLE`.
9. **Conservative attribution/release gate.** When verified LinguaChat delivery, relevant outside-exposure provenance, comparable opportunities, clean delayed retrieval, novel transfer or sufficient follow-up are missing, classify the affected method comparison `UNSUPPORTED`/`NOT_COMPARABLE`, preserve narrower valid learner observations and use the shared balanced curriculum with normal supports. Neither clean mastery alone nor self-reported external activity proves causal instructional efficacy.

## Required evidence and release gate

For A–F publish the **exact tested engine HEAD**, executable command, deterministic seed/clock, versioned attribution policy, complete synthetic event ledger, existing CEFR objective/skill mapping and immutable rubric, assigned and actually delivered in-app method, outside-exposure relevance and provenance (including unknowns), original first attempts and assistance, clean calibrated delayed unfamiliar probes, actual elapsed time and intervening practice, full eligible/assignment/delivery/follow-up denominators, baseline and opportunity comparability, replay invariance, uncertainty/reason codes and PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED for all nine assertions. Include a feasible fixed balanced comparator and explicit sensitivity to unobserved outside learning; no invented control outcomes or causal inference from observational differences alone.

A deterministic fixture PASS establishes only a software acceptance invariant. A claim that instructional adaptation improves durable independent learning requires separately reviewed longitudinal learner evidence, credible comparable exposure/baseline, delayed independent retrieval and unfamiliar transfer, uncertainty calibration and unchanged CEFR coverage. Valid learner mastery and evidence for LinguaChat's *causal contribution* must be reported separately.
