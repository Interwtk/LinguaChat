# F116 — External text assistance, autocorrect provenance, authorship and independent transfer

**Status:** design-only curricular acceptance fixture; **UNSUPPORTED** until reproducible executable assertions run against a named exact engine HEAD, real versioned CEFR-linked tasks, privacy-respecting evidence of tool exposure, and longitudinal independent outcomes. This document does **not** ship an input-method detector, establish whether any particular learner used AI, diagnose proficiency, or prove durable learning gains. Pre-A1 remains frozen; A1–C2 unavailable.

**Contract:** `docs/curriculum/longitudinal-instructional-adaptation.md`. F33 protects unseen transfer; F37/F38 distinguish assigned help from actually rendered assistance and tutor-origin faults; F49 protects accessibility and construct fidelity; F50 protects soft interest breadth; F63 covers first-language instructional bridges; F68 addresses metacognitive calibration; F104 controls method confounds; F111 addresses ASR corruption; F112 addresses TTS corruption; F115 addresses latency provenance. **Distinct construct:** when the learner writes, types, composes or sends a target-language message, can the tutor distinguish **learner-authored language** from externally supplied, transformed or silently corrected text—without presuming that a keyboard, assistive tool, fluent answer or missing telemetry proves misconduct? The fixture separates communicative task success from evidence admissible for **independent productive mastery**.

## Source, authorship and evidence contract

For every scenario resolve a **real versioned** CEFR descriptor/can-do, blueprint/prerequisites, target language and validated variety/register, source-authored communicative goal and recipient, task/rubric/evaluator, first-unaided threshold, protected reading/listening/writing/speaking/free-production skills, assistance policy, approved accommodations, due reviews, delayed retrieval, genuinely unseen transfer and exit gate. The task author must specify whether the assessed construct is unaided **composition**, effective communication with permitted tools, editing/revision, or a distinct combination. A polished message may complete the recipient-facing goal while failing to demonstrate independent production if substantial language was supplied externally; the reverse can also occur.

Keep a privacy-minimized **task-local provenance ledger**, only where consent and platform observability permit: immutable task/source/prompt version; learner-controlled draft before and after *observed* transformation; input channel and explicitly permitted assistive route; actual tutor model/hint exposure; known user-requested translation/grammar/predictive-completion actions; app-owned suggestion displayed/accepted/dismissed; externally pasted text only when a paste event is actually observable; first submitted answer; revision after feedback; recipient-facing decision; later independent probes. Do **not** log raw keystrokes, clipboard contents, third-party app activity or sensitive disability indicators. OS keyboards, dictation, spellcheck, grammar correction, accessibility tools and external translators often cannot be observed reliably; label **UNKNOWN** rather than inferring their use from fluency, speed, punctuation, accent, a paste event or an unusual answer.

Task-local labels: SOURCE_VALID_TASK, COMPOSITION_CONSTRUCT, TOOL_PERMITTED_COMMUNICATION, EDITING_CONSTRUCT, FIRST_UNAIDED, FIRST_ATTEMPT_VALID, ACTUAL_TUTOR_HELP_RENDERED, EXTERNAL_LANGUAGE_SUPPLIED_OBSERVED, TRANSFORMATION_OBSERVED, MECHANICAL_CORRECTION_ONLY, SEMANTIC_REWRITE_OBSERVED, USER_AUTHORED_REVISION_OBSERVED, ASSISTANCE_UNKNOWN, AUTHORSHIP_UNDETERMINED, ACCOMMODATION_VALIDATED, ACCESS_EQUIVALENCE_UNKNOWN, RECIPIENT_ACTION_VALID, ASSISTED_TASK_SUCCESS, INDEPENDENT_PRODUCTIVE_EVIDENCE, DELAYED_UNAIDED_RETRIEVAL, UNSEEN_UNAIDED_TRANSFER, CHANNEL_CONFOUNDED, TOPIC_CONFOUNDED, FOLLOWUP_MISSING, EVENT_REPLAY_DEDUPED, VERSION_CONFLICT_QUARANTINED, NOT_APPLICABLE and UNSUPPORTED. These are **observations about a task**, not permanent learner labels or allegations of cheating.

Feasible reversible instructional candidates: BALANCED_DEFAULT, COMPOSE_THEN_CHECK, MINIMAL_FEEDBACK_THEN_REWRITE, TARGETED_FORM_EXPLANATION, CONTRASTIVE_SELF_EDIT, SOURCE_VALID_DICTATION_PRACTICE, TOOL_LITERACY_THEN_UNAIDED_PROBE, INDEPENDENT_FREE_PRODUCTION and SOURCE_VALID_OTHER. Only use implemented, accessible, source-validated methods. Keep CEFR objectives, independent thresholds, all protected skills, due reviews, exit gates and soft-but-nonexclusive topic rotation unchanged. Select methods from repeated later **independent** retention/transfer with uncertainty and alternative sampling, not apparent fluency of tool-assisted output.

## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Autocorrect changes the assessed meaning.** A learner independently intends to send “I can't attend on Friday” but a known app-owned completion changes the submitted sentence to “I can attend on Friday.” The recipient incorrectly expects attendance. The ledger shows an actual suggestion acceptance, original learner-authored negation, changed final meaning and recipient outcome. Score the submitted communication against its consequence, but do **not** attribute the semantic substitution to the learner's language knowledge without a separate probe. In a matched counterfactual, only punctuation or an obvious typo is corrected with no change in meaning; do not erase otherwise valid independent evidence merely because a mechanical correction occurred. An OS correction without observable provenance remains UNKNOWN, not a fabricated semantic rewrite.

**B — Translation gives success without unaided composition.** In a source-valid writing task, a learner drafts a request in their first language, knowingly invokes an available translator, and sends the translated target-language message successfully. The recipient accepts the request; communicative task success is recorded, but the externally supplied target-language structures cannot satisfy an **unaided composition** gate. A later genuinely unseen request with no translated model reveals whether the learner can independently produce the required forms. If the source task explicitly evaluates appropriate use of permitted tools, credit that *different* construct without laundering it into independent writing mastery.

**C — Dictation and accessibility without discriminatory assumptions.** A learner with an approved access route dictates an original target-language message using speech-to-text; another types the same independently authored message. Where the source-valid writing construct assesses message formulation rather than physical typing, and access equivalence has been validated, both may supply independent composition evidence. Where spelling or orthographic control is explicitly the assessed construct, a separate accessible equivalent must be source-validated before that subskill is credited. Neither dictation nor screen-reader use is evidence of AI authorship, disability, dishonesty or lack of proficiency. If ASR mishears a negation, attribute that error to the channel only when independently established, following F111.

**D — Predictive completion and model leakage.** Two learners face the same source-authored, unseen recipient request. One receives a tutor-provided full answer that is actually displayed before submitting a near-copy; the other sees only a non-answer navigation affordance and composes independently. The first may succeed communicatively but is ASSISTED for the relevant productive construct; the second retains first-unaided eligibility. An app-owned suggestion *queued but never rendered* cannot contaminate the answer. A third learner accepts a short predicted function word: score independence according to the source-defined construct and the material contribution of the observed suggestion, not an all-or-nothing keyboard ban.

**E — Reversible method choice, later unaided transfer and confounds.** Across fictional matched sessions, COMPOSE_THEN_CHECK produces polished assisted drafts but weak later first-unaided transfer for learner A; CONTRASTIVE_SELF_EDIT produces better delayed independent recipient-valid messages. Learner B shows the opposite pattern under otherwise comparable exposure. Record baseline, task difficulty, objective, topic, input method, assistive route, observed suggestion exposure, feedback, first attempts, delayed intervals, attrition and independent probes. With sufficient repeated evidence, the tutor **actually delivers** different next instructional choices with confidence bounds, continues sampling alternatives and protects required listening/speaking/reading/writing. If the methods occurred exclusively on different keyboards or only in preferred topics, report CHANNEL_CONFOUNDED or TOPIC_CONFOUNDED rather than causal superiority. A later reversal must reduce confidence and permit a changed choice.

**F — Offline drafts, paste uncertainty and replay order.** An offline learner composes an original answer, submits it, then opens a grammar helper for revision; queued events arrive server-side in reverse order and the submission is retried twice. Another learner pastes a sentence from an unknown source; a third switches devices and an old app-owned suggestion event is replayed under a reused local identifier. Reconstruct **session-qualified**, immutable task and exposure chronology, deduplicate true retries and quarantine irreconcilable versions. Do not retroactively mark an earlier independent submission assisted because a later revision or its packet arrived first. A paste proves only a paste, **not** that an external model generated the content. Where authorship cannot be resolved, retain communicative evidence, mark independent-production status UNKNOWN and offer a fair new unaided opportunity rather than punitive failure.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Source and construct specificity.** A–F resolve real versioned CEFR can-do, blueprint, source task, communicative goal, rubric/evaluator, protected skills, accommodation, assistance policy, independent threshold, delayed/unseen probes and exit gate. Separate unaided composition, tool-permitted communication and editing; no silent equivalence between them.
2. **Observed versus inferred external help.** A/B/D/F distinguish actually observed language supply, accepted app-owned suggestions, mechanical correction, semantic transformation, tutor help rendered, queued but unseen suggestions, unknown OS/external actions and mere paste. No fabricated AI-use or authorship diagnosis.
3. **Meaning and recipient consequence.** A/B/C/D score source-valid semantic intent, submitted message and actual recipient action separately. A successful assisted message may satisfy a permitted communication task without proving independent target-language production; a channel-corrupted negation cannot be misattributed as a learner error.
4. **Construct-valid accessibility parity.** C/F allow validated dictation/assistive routes without a default penalty, protect orthography when genuinely required, and use source-validated equivalents or UNSUPPORTED when equivalence is unknown. No inference of disability, attention, integrity or ability from input method.
5. **Chronological assistance and authorship.** D/F preserve first draft, first submission, actual pre-answer help exposure and later revision boundaries using stable session-qualified event identities. Queued, never rendered or post-answer assistance cannot retroactively contaminate first-unaided evidence; unresolved chronology is quarantined.
6. **Privacy, consent and fair unknown handling.** A–F prohibit raw keystroke/clipboard surveillance, third-party app inspection, hidden profiling and punitive guessing from polished text or paste. Minimize and version event data, support consent/opt-out, and preserve fair assessment opportunities where assistance provenance is unobservable.
7. **Delayed independent retrieval and unseen transfer.** B/E/F require meaningfully separated sessions and genuinely unseen, source-authored prompts/recipients with first-unaided language and valid recipient decisions. Report eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing follow-up denominators; assisted rewriting or near-copying cannot pass an independent exit gate.
8. **Reversible adaptation and balanced coverage.** E and cross-case counterfactuals log feasible, assigned and **actually delivered** methods, uncertainty, baseline, device/channel, task difficulty, topic, access and help exposure. Replaying events or swapping an unobserved keyboard must not manufacture mastery or a method winner. Sample alternatives; preserve all required skill families, due reviews and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar contexts despite interests.
9. **Exact-head reproducibility and bounded claims.** A–F emit deterministic seed/clock, named engine HEAD and runnable command, source/task/rubric/evaluator hashes, event/exposure ledger, original and final answers, recipient decisions, independent delayed/unseen outcomes, confound/replay permutations and **PASS/FAIL/UNSUPPORTED per assertion**. A documentation-only CI pass is **not** executable validation or proof of real learner improvement.

## Required evidence and release gate

An **executable fixture PASS** requires a named exact engine HEAD; reproducible command and deterministic clock; real versioned CEFR-linked tasks; consent-appropriate observed provenance or explicit UNKNOWN; source-valid rubric and construct-specific adjudication; independently verified recipient actions; valid accommodations; first-unaided evidence; meaningfully delayed retrieval and genuinely unseen transfer; complete eligible/offered/rendered/completed/missing denominators; alternative method sampling and confound checks; stable replay/version behavior; and assertion-level verdicts. When external tool use cannot be observed, report **AUTHORSHIP_UNDETERMINED** rather than asserting either unaided mastery or wrongdoing. This does not erase independently established communicative outcomes.

Any claim that authorship-aware adaptation **improves durable independent language production** requires a separate ethically reviewed, multilingual and accessibility-validated real-learner longitudinal study with a credible comparator, attrition analysis, privacy review and subgroup-harm checks. **A strong tutor helps learners use tools wisely while still checking what they can express on their own.**

**Boundary:** curricular acceptance documentation only. No Pre-A1 edits, A1–C2 release, engine, Auth, cloud, infrastructure, production, Supabase, paid Claude, EvoLabs or auto-merge changes. Integration/watchdog alone aggregates owner actions.
