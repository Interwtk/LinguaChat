## Six deterministic synthetic longitudinal histories

**A — Same words, reporting versus checking.** In a club meeting, **"You're joining us tomorrow."** may report a known plan, whereas **"You're joining us tomorrow?"** may seek confirmation. Punctuation here is *editorial*, not audible. Reviewed delivered recordings plus preceding turns can support different intentions; the listener independently acknowledges a report or confirms/corrects a question. If both remain viable, **"Are you asking me to confirm, or telling me the plan?"** is a valid first-unaided reply. A rising-equals-question rule fails when the speaker's variety uses rising statements.

**B — Tag questions without mind-reading.** At a study room, **"The room is ready, isn't it?"** can seek information or invite agreement depending on independently reviewed delivered prosody and discourse. **"I haven't checked yet—should I?"** is appropriate when the listener cannot confirm. A contour distinction may inform a *reviewed recording in its variety* but is not a universal rule. Do not assume the speaker is confident, sarcastic or impatient because of a tag or pitch.

**C — Ambiguous short response.** A volunteer says **"Really?"** after hearing that an event moved to Thursday. The utterance may request confirmation or serve as acknowledgment; it does not license a claim that the speaker is angry or delighted. With supporting context, **"Yes, Thursday; the notice was updated"** can be appropriate. If unresolved, **"Would you like me to check the notice?"** is acceptable. Reward recipient-appropriate uptake and justified uncertainty, not emotion labeling.

**D — Nonfinal prosody and unfinished condition.** A librarian says **"If the room is free..."**, pauses, then continues **"...we can book it for the group."** The first fragment is neither confirmed permission nor a completed booking. The learner waits for or asks about the missing condition before relaying the plan. A different reviewed speaker may pause longer or use a final contour for a genuine handoff. Do not impose one timing threshold across varieties, accessibility needs or network quality. This probes *acoustic continuation interpreted with syntax/context*, not F77's general turn-taking policy.

**E — TTS/caption drift and first authorship.** Source-world intent is a confirmation request **"You're coming tomorrow?"**, but unreviewed TTS delivers a declarative contour while captions insert a question mark. Quarantine TTS_PROSODY_DRIFT and CAPTION_FORCE_LEAKAGE; do not grade against invisible intended punctuation. At 09:02, before captions or hints, the learner asks **"Are you checking whether I'm coming?"**; preserve FIRST_UNAIDED_LISTENING. At 09:03 a force hint and question-mark caption are actually shown; at 09:04 the learner repeats the hint. The 09:04 reply is ASSISTED, not a replacement for the earlier answer. Deduplicate replayed event IDs; a hint selected but never rendered cannot contaminate 09:02 retroactively. A screen-reader text route is legitimate access but may measure reading rather than acoustic force.

**F — Outcome-responsive reversible instruction and new voice.** Learner A identifies transcript question marks immediately but fails later first-unaided replies to new speakers. After matched **actually delivered** CONTEXT_BEFORE_PROSODY_PREDICTION trials, later independent recipient-appropriate replies and clarifications improve in neutral/unfamiliar contexts; cautiously increase that method's probability while retaining balanced listening and alternatives. Learner B initially improves with CONTRASTIVE_RECORDED_CONTOURS, then overgeneralizes rising pitch as a question with a new reviewed variety; reduce confidence and reopen syntax/context triangulation. If one method used a familiar studio voice and another a noisy new voice, or one revealed punctuation, **no method winner** is inferable. Same-clip replay or topic-only rewrite is not unseen auditory transfer. Missing follow-up is missing, not failure or gain.

## Nine acceptance assertions

1. **Versioned objective and genuine reviewed audio.** A–F require existing relevant CEFR can-do, source-world/prosody oracle, actually accessible audio, first-unaided rubric, due review, delayed/unseen probe and unchanged exit gates. Otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE or UNSUPPORTED.
2. **Question versus assertion without punctuation leakage.** A accepts only context/audio-supported interpretation or targeted clarification; editorial punctuation and universal pitch heuristics cannot decide.
3. **Tag question and recipient uptake.** B preserves reviewed information-seeking versus alignment possibilities and does not infer private confidence from pitch.
4. **Short-turn ambiguity.** C accepts appropriate confirmation or clarification and rejects unsupported emotion/sarcasm claims.
5. **Continuation and access variation.** D rejects completed commitments from unfinished conditionals; respects reviewed timing/variety and accessibility differences.
6. **Delivery, drift and chronology.** E audits original/rendered audio, TTS/ASR/captions/translations, logs help/replay/answer exposure, preserves pre-hint authorship, deduplicates events and distinguishes reading from independent listening.
7. **Controlled uncertain reversible policy.** F compares only actually delivered feasible methods under matched objective, speaker, variety, signal, ambiguity, lexical load, topic, prior exposure, help, spacing and follow-up. Sparse/confounded/contradictory evidence yields no winner; regression reduces confidence and restores exploration.
8. **Delayed genuinely unseen independent transfer and soft interests.** No same-clip replay, transcript-punctuation recognition, immediate repair or topic-only substitution earns delayed/unseen mastery. Require a later distinct eligible session with **new reviewed recording/voice or materially new prosodic/contextual pattern**, first-unaided recipient response or justified clarification, and explicit missingness. Preserve shared CEFR gates, skill balance and adjacent/neutral/unfamiliar contexts.
9. **Honest implementation and efficacy boundary.** Documentation CI is not executable acoustic QA, a shipped adaptive tutor or proof of improved human retention. Require exact-head executable assertions against delivered assets, demonstrated longitudinal outcome-driven instructional changes and independently reviewed later unaided transfer versus balanced baseline before efficacy claims. No Auth/cloud/infra/production/Supabase mutation, paid Claude, EvoLabs, threshold weakening, level enablement or auto-merge.

**Fixture verdict:** A–F are synthetic design/probe histories, not observed outcomes or executable QA. Preserve acoustic/context ambiguity, accessible actual delivery, first-authorship provenance, reversible adaptation and UNSUPPORTED gates until independent implementation and longitudinal evidence exist.
